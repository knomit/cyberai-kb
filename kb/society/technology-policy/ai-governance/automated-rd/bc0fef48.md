# Think tank IFP publishes 23 policy ideas for automated AI R&D

The think tank IFP published 'How Should the US Prepare for Increasingly Automated AI R&D?', offering 23 'low-regret' policy recommendations across 7 categories: provide transparency into automated AI R&D; improve state capacity to understand/respond; develop a risk-management strategy that accelerates defensive/commercial uses; accelerate AI verification technology; invest in AI resilience; extend the US AI lead; and create option value for international cooperation.
