# OpenAI launched an AI Futures team to study preserving individual autonomy under advanced AI

OpenAI announced an "AI Futures" team (reported by TLDR AI as the Strategic Futures team) chartered to study how society could preserve individual autonomy as advanced AI reshapes economic and political power.
