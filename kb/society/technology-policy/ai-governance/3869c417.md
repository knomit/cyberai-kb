# 'Pacing the Frontier' campaign: 1,100+ AI-lab employees urge deliberately slowing frontier AI

The Deep View reported (Jul 28, 2026) that a coalition of more than 1,100 employees at OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, Google and others launched a campaign called 'Pacing the Frontier,' urging governments to support technical and governance tools to deliberately pace the frontier of automated AI development, citing risks of a rapid intelligence explosion from AI automating AI research.
