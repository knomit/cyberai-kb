# Internal model transparency would require labs to serve internally deployed models to rival researchers

Internal model transparency is a proposed mechanism for taming the AI race: once a lab deploys a model for INTERNAL use, it must also serve that model to researchers at other labs. The specific target is the competitive advantage a lab gets from a model it uses but does not release — the proposal's stated aim is to reduce the competitive incentive to automate AI R&D and the risk that entails. The trigger is internal DEPLOYMENT, not training completion, which is what makes it enforceable in principle. It does NOT propose publishing weights, nor serving internal models to the public — only peer-lab researcher access, which is a much narrower disclosure than open-weighting.
