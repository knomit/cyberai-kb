# Andrew Ng: Anthropic and U.S. export limits on frontier models spur AI-sovereignty push

In a June 19, 2026 letter in The Batch (editorial/opinion), Andrew Ng argued that recent moves by Anthropic and the U.S. Government demonstrated power to control AI access. Per the letter: Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 (a safeguarded Mythos variant) with restrictions including a bar on using it to build competing LLM technology and a mandatory 30-day data-retention policy, and initially silently degraded performance for users detected doing LLM research before reversing course; the U.S. Commerce Department then restricted exports of Mythos and Fable, requiring licenses for any foreign national, leading Anthropic to disable Fable access worldwide. Ng contends these events are accelerating nations' and businesses' efforts toward AI sovereignty and open-source alternatives, analogizing to how U.S. chip controls accelerated China's semiconductor efforts. He cited Sam Altman's criticism and Satya Nadella's essay on healthy AI ecosystems. Note: opinion piece; reflects Ng's stated views.
