# AI pioneers Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, and Ng argue for keeping AI open

AI researchers Geoffrey Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, and Andrew Ng publicly argued for keeping AI open (open-source/open-weight) to prevent a few large firms from monopolizing AI advancements, amid mounting AI safety concerns.
