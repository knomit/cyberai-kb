# US public sentiment toward AI continues to sour (Pew, 2026)

Per Pew Research (cited by The Deep View, June 2026), the US population leads polled countries in negative sentiment toward AI, with more US adults reporting they are more concerned than excited about the technology, up from prior years, and many worried about impacts on creativity, relationships, and jobs. The Deep View also cited a Gallup poll indicating a majority of Americans oppose data-center construction in their area on environmental, quality-of-life, and anti-AI grounds.
