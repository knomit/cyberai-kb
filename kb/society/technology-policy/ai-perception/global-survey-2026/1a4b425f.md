# Global opinion shifting toward China as the AI leader (2026 survey)

A 2026 international survey reported by Politico found global perception of the AI race shifting toward China over the US. Respondents in France, the United Kingdom, and Canada largely saw China as the AI frontrunner, while respondents in Vietnam, India, and Japan still saw the US as leading. Germany's respondents were the least confident in a US lead. The data was highlighted in The Deep View's June 16, 2026 edition.
