# Under 10% of Chinese public worried about AI destroying jobs (UCL survey)

A University College London survey reported in May 2026 (via South China Morning Post) found AI uptake going far better in China than the US, with less than 10% of the Chinese public worried that the technology will take jobs — a markedly more positive public attitude toward AI than in the US.
