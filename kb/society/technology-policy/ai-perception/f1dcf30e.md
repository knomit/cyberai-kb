# 2026: US–China AI divergence spans both public sentiment and open-model competitiveness

Multiple mid-2026 signals point the same direction: global and domestic opinion is tilting toward China in the AI race while US public sentiment sours. International polling (Politico) shows France, UK, and Canada now seeing China as the AI frontrunner; a UCL survey finds under 10% of the Chinese public worried about AI-driven job loss versus rising US concern (Pew/Gallup, including majority US opposition to local data centers). This perception shift tracks a capability shift: Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index reports China's open, cost-efficient models substantially closing the gap with US proprietary models. Sentiment and capability trends are mutually reinforcing China's momentum.
