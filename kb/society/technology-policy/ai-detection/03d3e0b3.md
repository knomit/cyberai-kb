# AI text-detector accusations: Orélien, Shy Girl, Adelphi (2026)

Per The Batch (Oct 30, 2026): Pangram and GPTZero flagged Thélyson Orélien's prize-winning novel; AFP found other detectors (incl. InVID-WeVerify) judged passages probably human, and the Prix Goncourt dropped the book. Pangram's CEO admitted no independent study confirms its false-positive rate in French. Hachette pulled Shy Girl in March after NYT reporting; Jerry Falade lost a $2M book deal partly over a Pangram finding. A New York court annulled Adelphi University's AI-cheating finding based on a Turnitin score in January; Turnitin says its indicator should not be the sole basis for action. Under the EU AI Act, providers must mark outputs as machine-detectable; Anthropic (August) and OpenAI (October, EU) said they would watermark text, and OpenAI's tests show replacing 25% of words with synonyms cuts detection of 400-token passages from ~92% to ~17%. Implication: detector scores are one piece of evidence, not proof.
