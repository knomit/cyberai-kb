# Trump said he will name an official AI czar while rejecting AI safety risks as a hoax

President Trump said, as reported 2026-09-19, that he will name an official AI czar, while simultaneously rejecting AI safety risks as a hoax. The pairing matters more than either half: a centralized federal AI coordination role created by an administration that publicly dismisses safety risk is likely to be oriented toward acceleration and deregulation rather than risk mitigation. This does NOT establish who the appointee is, what statutory authority the role will carry, or that any appointment has been made — only that the intention was stated.
