# Over 1,100 employees at OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and Google signed a letter urging deliberate pacing of the AI frontier

A coalition of more than 1,100 employees across OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and Google signed an open letter urging government and industry to work together to 'deliberately pace the frontier of automated AI development.' Anthropic has separately argued in an essay on recursive self-improvement that any meaningful pause would have to be both industry-wide and international to prevent one lab overtaking the others.
