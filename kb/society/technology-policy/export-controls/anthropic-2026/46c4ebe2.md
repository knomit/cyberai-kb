# US Commerce Department imposes export control on Anthropic's Fable and Mythos models

As reported in June 2026, the US Commerce Department imposed an export control on Anthropic's two latest models (referred to as Fable and Mythos), citing national-security and cybersecurity concerns. The directive requires Anthropic to restrict access to the two models for all foreign nationals — both outside and inside the US — including foreign-national employees of Anthropic. The Deep View framed this as part of a recurring standoff between Anthropic's safety posture and the US administration.
