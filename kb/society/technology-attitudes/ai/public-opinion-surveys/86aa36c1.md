# Under-30 Americans split evenly on whether chatbots help or hurt their creativity

A Pew Research Center chart published June 17, 2026 found that, unlike older Americans, U.S. adults under 30 are just as likely to think AI chatbots hurt their creativity as help it. Older age cohorts skew more positive about chatbots' effect on creativity. This is notable because under-30s are the heaviest chatbot users, so familiarity does not translate into a more favorable view of creative impact.
