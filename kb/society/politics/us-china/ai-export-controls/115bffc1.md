# US and China move to restrict access to frontier AI models

The Deep View (Jul 9, 2026) reported the US restricted international access to Anthropic's 'Mythos' and OpenAI's 'GPT-5.6' models, and per Reuters, China's Ministry of Commerce met in June with labs including Z.ai, Alibaba, and ByteDance to discuss curbing overseas access to China's top AI models and limiting foreign investment in Chinese AI startups.
