# EPA moving to cut the public-comment requirement on industrial air permits

The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency plans to eliminate a federal requirement that states solicit public input on air pollution permits for industrial facilities, data centers included. Analysts quoted by The Deep View argue this could worsen rather than defuse the backlash, since removing the avenue for constituent input leads people to assume the worst and removes a channel through which local officials learn of overlooked concerns.
