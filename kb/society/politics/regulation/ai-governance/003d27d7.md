# China bans companion chatbots and criminalizes minors' virtual romantic relationships

On Wednesday July 15, 2026, China enacted legislation and regulations that: make virtual romantic relationships with chatbots illegal for minors; require AI companies to alert a minor's emergency contact if an emotional crisis is detected; and ban companies from building chatbots designed to be virtual companions. Alibaba and ByteDance notified users the same day that some features would be disabled. Motivations reported are a mix of AI-safety concern and demographic policy — China's population shrank for a fourth consecutive year in 2025 with a record-low birthrate, and the government wants to encourage real-life relationships and family formation. Chinese regulators had warned in a September 2025 policy document that AI could transform 'traditional views on employment, fertility, and education.' By contrast, New York and California have enacted limits on companion chatbots but not full bans.
