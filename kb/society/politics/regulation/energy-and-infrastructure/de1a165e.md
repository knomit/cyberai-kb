# New York enacts first-in-nation moratorium on hyperscale AI data centers

New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed an executive order on Tuesday July 14, 2026 banning for up to one year the construction of 'hyperscale' AI data centers — defined as those using more than 50 megawatts of power. It is the first moratorium of its kind in the United States. The order also requires data centers to either produce their own energy or pay a premium for grid power, and states data centers will never be exempt from local zoning and approvals. New York is additionally exploring requiring data center operators to pay into a fund supporting the grid. Hochul cited rising power costs, water supply impacts, and noise pollution. Context: a Gallup poll from May 2026 found roughly 7 in 10 Americans oppose data center construction in their area, with nearly 50% strongly opposed.
