# EU orders Google to open Android system access to rival AI assistants under DMA

On 16 July 2026 the European Commission adopted two binding specification decisions under the Digital Markets Act. The first requires Google to give rival AI assistants the same reach into Android that Gemini has: camera, microphone, on-screen content, a wake word that fires with the display off, and the ability to drive other apps in the background by imitating taps and typing. Google must ship this in the next major Android release (Android 18), by 1 August 2027 at the latest. Eleven Android features are opened in total; access is gated on rivals meeting security and privacy requirements. Android carries roughly 60% of European mobile users. Proceedings opened 27 January 2026. These are specification decisions, not fines - the Commission's separate power to open a non-compliance case remains untouched.
