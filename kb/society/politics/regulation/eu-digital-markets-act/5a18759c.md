# EU compels Google to share anonymised Search data with rival search engines and AI chatbots

The second DMA specification decision adopted 16 July 2026 requires Google to hand anonymised Search query, click, and ranking data to rival search engines - and to AI chatbots that offer search functionality - for a cost-based fee, aiming for a 'more level playing field with Google Search'. Google may assess whether sharing specific data poses cybersecurity or data-protection risks. Teresa Ribera, Commission executive VP, framed it as helping smaller firms compete and giving users choice. Google lawyer Kent Walker told Reuters the rulings risk undermining 'vital privacy and security guardrails' and 'discount extensive evidence of user harm'.
