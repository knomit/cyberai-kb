# White House NEC Director Hassett: 'no sign in the data' that AI has cost jobs

National Economic Council Director Kevin Hassett told CNBC there is 'no sign in the data' that AI has cost anyone their job yet, adding that companies adopting AI tend to see rapid revenue growth and a bump in employment, and that the White House has a task force studying AI's future workforce impact. Reported in The Deep View.
