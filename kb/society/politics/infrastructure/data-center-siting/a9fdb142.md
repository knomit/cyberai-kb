# U.S. public opposition to data centers has become bipartisan and is blocking buildout

Opposition to AI data centers now spans the U.S. political spectrum: Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez announced an AI Data Center Moratorium Act, while Republican Gov. Greg Abbott ordered a pause on Texas data center approvals in early August 2026. New York Gov. Kathy Hochul instituted the first statewide moratorium in July 2026 on hyperscale data centers of 50MW or more, for one year. A Gallup poll published in May 2026 found an average of 7 in 10 Americans oppose data center construction in their area, including 48% who strongly oppose local facilities.
