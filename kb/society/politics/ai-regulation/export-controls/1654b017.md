# US lifts export controls on Anthropic's Mythos and Fable AI models

The U.S. Commerce Department lifted its export controls on Anthropic's Mythos and Fable models, ending a roughly three-week standoff and restoring Claude users' access to the frontier lab's most powerful AI. In a letter reported by Reuters, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick said Anthropic agreed to proactively detect and address security risks, work with the U.S. government on protocols and release standards for Mythos, Fable and future models, and inform the government of malicious activity. The models had been restricted since June 12, 2026, under controls that made it illegal for foreign nationals inside or outside the U.S. to access the technology. Reported by The Deep View, July 2, 2026.
