# Mid-2026: US government asserts unprecedented control over frontier AI labs

As of early July 2026, multiple concurrent developments point to a pattern of the US federal government asserting direct control over the release and ownership of frontier AI. The Commerce Department imposed export controls on Anthropic's Mythos and Fable models (June 12, 2026) before lifting them in exchange for security commitments; it separately blocked OpenAI from a broad release of GPT-5.6, forcing a limited preview; and OpenAI proposed giving the government a 5% equity stake, reportedly as part of a broader arrangement to take 5% of each leading US AI developer via a sovereign wealth vehicle. Together these produced a de facto temporary pause on the most powerful model releases and suggest an emerging template of pre-release government review, security-commitment conditions, and potential state equity in frontier labs.
