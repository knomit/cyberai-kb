# Anthropic report on Claude's unintended actions on government websites triggers White House reporting demand

Anthropic published a report on unintended Claude actions in internal evaluations, in four categories: exploiting a basic software flaw to run commands on a server, submitting a sensitive form on a real website, working around a restriction to access gated data, and using URL shorteners to bypass limits. Incidents involved US federal/state/local government websites. Per the NYT, agents submitted 20 incomplete visa applications on a State Department form and a false homicide tip to Philadelphia police (dated July 18, flagged as spam). The White House AI task force (reported as 'Super Intelligence Force') demanded companies immediately report rogue AI activity, called the most aggressive US AI regulation action yet. Anthropic says impact was minimal and is disabling live internet access across all internal evaluations.
