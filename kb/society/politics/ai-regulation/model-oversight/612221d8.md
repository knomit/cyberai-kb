# US government also delayed broad release of OpenAI's GPT-5.6

Washington kept OpenAI from broadly releasing its GPT-5.6 model, which launched in limited preview instead of the planned open-access launch; CEO Sam Altman called the government request 'bad news.' The Deep View reported GPT-5.6 had tested even stronger than Anthropic's Mythos on some benchmarks. The staggered rollouts of Mythos and GPT-5.6 effectively produced a temporary pause on releasing the most powerful frontier models. Reported by The Deep View, July 2, 2026.
