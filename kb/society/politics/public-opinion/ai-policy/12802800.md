# 56,000 Americans favour labor-market AI remedies over redistribution

The Center for Shared AI Prosperity polled 56,000 Americans on 79 AI policy ideas. The most popular were expanding apprenticeships (+66), requiring severance for automated-away jobs (+63) and sector-based job training (+60); the least popular were a US sovereign wealth fund (-51), a tax on distributed profits (-33) and universal basic income (-33). The pattern: policies that preserve the link between work and income poll well, direct redistribution polls badly.
