# Trump White House memo lets private firms hack foreign crime groups

A new White House memo signed by U.S. President Donald Trump instructed the National Coordination Center (NCC) to establish a program allowing private-sector companies to use their capabilities to hack and disrupt foreign crime groups (reported by The Hacker News, Aug 14, 2026).
