# Data centers a major factor in Nov 2026 US midterms; local 'restrict' measures won 38 of 39 counted

Per The Deep View citing NBC News exit polls, 56% of voters said data centers were a crucial or major factor in their congressional vote (13% said no role). Redbud Consulting's 'Data Centers on the Ballot 2026' tracker: 'Restrict' outcomes won 38 of 39 local measures with complete counts, 'Allow' won one. New York imposed the first statewide moratorium (hyperscale >=50 MW, one year) in July 2026. Reports say OpenAI and Anthropic are seeking smaller 20-30 MW facilities; Google is investing $15B in Malaysia data centers. Implication: regulatory risk to frontier-lab compute buildout.
