# Anthropic released two AI policy frameworks in June 2026

On Wednesday in June 2026 Anthropic published two policy proposals to steer AI development. The Advanced AI Framework outlines four areas of AI risk (biological weapons, cybersecurity, loss of control, and automated R&D amplifying those risks) and proposes four rules for frontier developers: transparency in safety, independent evaluations, security protection of model weights, and government enforcement against catastrophic risks. The Economic Policy Framework gives recommendations tiered to 5%, 10%, and unprecedented unemployment, including workforce-training grants, wage insurance, basic-needs relief, and wealth-distribution measures such as basic income or equity sharing.
