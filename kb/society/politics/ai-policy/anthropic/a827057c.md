# Dario Amodei essay 'Policy on Exponential AI' calls for AI regulation

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay titled 'Policy on Exponential AI' advocating increased AI regulation, including public safety testing and auditing, preempting economic disruption, protecting against autocratic misuse, and accelerating positive impacts. He wrote: 'in the several years that it can take Congress to act, AI can go from an amusing toy to the full country of geniuses,' underscoring a timescale mismatch between AI progress and policymaking.
