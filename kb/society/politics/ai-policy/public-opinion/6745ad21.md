# CSAIP poll: 61% of Americans say voluntary AI-company commitments are 'not enough'

Center for Shared AI Prosperity polling (n=2,498) found 61% of Americans say the voluntary AI self-policing agreements are 'not enough' (incl. 53% of Trump voters), and 54% say government should set and enforce AI rules (61% Harris, 48% Trump voters). Reported via Import AI #475; primary data posted on X by CSAIP.
