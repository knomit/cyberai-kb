# White House to review a voluntary framework for testing AI model cybersecurity

The White House planned to meet with AI companies to discuss a new voluntary framework, ordered by President Trump, for reviewing cybersecurity risks in AI models. The framework lets AI developers share models with the government for cybersecurity evaluation; companies including OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic were set to attend, with the assessment criteria remaining classified. Reported via TLDR AI / CNBC, Aug 3, 2026.
