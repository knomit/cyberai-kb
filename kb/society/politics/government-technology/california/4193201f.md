# California signs deal for Claude at 50% discount for state agencies

California signed a first-of-its-kind agreement with Anthropic giving state agencies, cities and counties access to Claude at a 50% discount, plus free workforce training and technical assistance. Governor Gavin Newsom announced it on June 29, 2026. Some agencies already use Claude (e.g., the DMV for customer service and the Dept. of Health Care Services for internal workflows); the Dept. of Technology and Office of Emergency Services are using it for cyber defense and code patching.
