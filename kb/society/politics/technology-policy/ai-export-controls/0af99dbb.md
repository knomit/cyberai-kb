# US imposes export controls on Anthropic's Mythos 5 and Fable 5 models

The US government imposed export controls on Anthropic's latest AI models, Mythos 5 and Fable 5, restricting their use outside the country and by foreign nationals, citing cybersecurity and national security concerns. The move triggered concern from G7 leaders that US ability to restrict powerful models could ricochet back on US AI companies.
