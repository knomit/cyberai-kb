# OpenAI backs the bipartisan FRONTIER act mandating embedded third-party evaluators at frontier labs

Politico reported on 2026-09-15 that OpenAI is backing the FRONTIER act, a bipartisan US House proposal that would require frontier AI labs to embed outside evaluators into their development processes to verify model safety. This is one of several converging 2026 proposals for external evaluation: Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called for giving 'employee-like access' to teams that can verify adherence to safety practices and commitments, and Elon Musk called for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and leading Chinese labs to test each other's models before public release.
