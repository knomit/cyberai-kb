# June 2026 White House executive order sets frontier-model security guidance

In early June 2026 the White House issued an executive order providing guidance for companies building frontier AI models, promoting AI development while addressing security. It mandates ramping up defensive cybersecurity efforts and sets up a framework for frontier labs to voluntarily share models with the government and collaborate on cybersecurity. Andrew Ng characterized it as a reasonable compromise. The regulatory push was driven largely by cybersecurity concerns, notably Anthropic's 'Mythos' system, a significant advance in automatically finding code vulnerabilities. Ng credited David Sacks (co-chair of PCAST) and AI policy advisor Sriram Krishnan, while cautioning against overregulation.
