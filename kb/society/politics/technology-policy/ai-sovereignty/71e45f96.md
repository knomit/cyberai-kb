# Forrester forecast: most nations will stay dependent on US and Chinese AI through 2030

Forrester's Global Sovereignty Forecast predicts tech sovereignty — a country's ability to develop, run, and secure new technologies independently of foreign influence — will grow only slowly over the next five years. Forrester's index scores China at 82% and the US at 79%, the two highest. The other 12 assessed countries (Australia, Brazil, Canada, France, Germany, India, Italy, Japan, Mexico, South Korea, Spain, UK) averaged 39% and are expected to rise only to about 40% by 2030. Sovereignty is measured across eight dimensions: government AI investment, cloud, workforce, AI model development, data-center capacity/autonomy, chip production, software creation, and rare-earths processing. The biggest area of growth is chip production, led by South Korea, Japan, and India.
