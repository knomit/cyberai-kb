# Aidan Gomez argues against a few Silicon Valley firms setting global AI rules

Cohere CEO Aidan Gomez published an argument against allowing a small number of dominant Silicon Valley firms to set global AI regulation, drawing parallels to historical monopolies that limited competition under safety pretexts. He proposes a diverse, international, evidence-based governance framework emphasizing transparency, mandatory testing and independent assurance mechanisms, warning that current proposals could entrench market leaders. This is a position piece from an interested party — Cohere is a competitor to the firms whose proposals he criticizes — and should be weighed as an argument, not a neutral assessment.
