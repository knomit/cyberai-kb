# AI leaders push US-led coalition for AI governance at 2026 G7

At the G7 Summit in Évian-les-Bains, France (June 17, 2026), Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and Google DeepMind founder Demis Hassabis called for a US-led international coalition to set standards for releasing advanced AI models, warning of bioterrorism and cybersecurity risks if no alliance forms. OpenAI's Sam Altman and Canadian PM Mark Carney supported it; France's Macron and India's Modi warned against US 'kill switch' control and non-cooperation among democracies.
