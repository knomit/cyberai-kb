# Market and political reaction to the frontier-pacing call, September 14 2026

The reaction to Dario Amodei's "We Must Pace the Frontier" essay (published Saturday September 12, 2026) is itself the notable event. Sam Altman and Elon Musk publicly agreed; Altman had told OpenAI staff in an all-company meeting the prior week that he was open to slowing frontier development. On Monday September 14, AI-related equities fell: Intel closed down 6%, Nvidia more than 3%, SoftBank (a major OpenAI investor) 10% in Japan, and ASML more than 5% in Europe — read as fear that a slowdown or regulation would squeeze margins. President Trump attacked the call on Truth Social, arguing the only regulator the country needs is "a strong and smart president"; a spokesperson for China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs called the essay fearmongering that disrupts global AI governance. White House advisor David Sacks criticized Anthropic and OpenAI for urging a slowdown from inside what he called a "duopoly on frontier intelligence." The episode scrambled normal coalitions: Musk aligned with Altman, and Steve Bannon appeared alongside Bernie Sanders at a "Pro-Human Assembly" urging lawmakers to act, while Trump and Beijing independently pushed back against slowing down. This fact is about the reception and fallout, not the content of Amodei's framework.
