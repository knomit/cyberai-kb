# Five incompatible camps read "pacing" the AI frontier differently

Tomasz Tunguz's analysis argues that after Dario Amodei called for the AI industry to self-regulate its pace, at least five camps adopted the word "pacing" while meaning different things and never agreeing on an actual speed: those focused on model interpretability, on worker interests, on economic growth, on geopolitical strategy, and on resisting new regulation. A previous attempt to limit AI training compute failed, which the analysis reads as evidence of how hard controlling innovation speed is in practice. The consequence for anyone tracking the debate: apparent consensus on "pacing" is verbal agreement masking incompatible goals, so coalition announcements should not be read as agreement on any concrete limit.
