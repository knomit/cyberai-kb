# DIA report: China launched 93 space missions in 2025; Russia and China developing counterspace weapons

The Defense Intelligence Agency report 'Challenges to Security in Space' (summarized in Import AI 479) says China more than tripled launches per year in a decade (22 in 2016, 93 in 2025); China and Russia are developing capabilities from kinetic destruction to jamming and cyber; China may field space-based directed energy systems by the late 2020s; Russia's Peresvet ground laser dazzles satellite optics; Russia is developing co-orbital ASAT (Burevestnik) and experimenting with nuclear weapons in space (EMP risk to satellites).
