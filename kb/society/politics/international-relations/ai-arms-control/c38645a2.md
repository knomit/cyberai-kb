# Amodei ranks four levels of feasible global AI pacing agreements by difficulty

Amodei's September 2026 essay sets out four escalating levels of possible international AI agreement: Level 1, prohibiting narrow obviously dangerous uses such as AI-assisted bioweapon production (he calls this probably possible); Level 2, both sides testing models before release for acute cyber, bio and alignment risks, potentially via a global standards body (likely feasible to create, hard to give teeth, with the difficulty being verification that neither side holds untested secret models); Level 3, a 'speed limit' on the rate of recursive self-improvement, analogous to the SALT treaties (difficult but 'just on the edge of being possible'); Level 4, a full pacing or pause agreement (he supports floating it but thinks it unlikely soon, because evading monitoring could radically shift global power). He argues any agreement must either have ironclad verifiability or be limited enough that defection would not be militarily existential.
