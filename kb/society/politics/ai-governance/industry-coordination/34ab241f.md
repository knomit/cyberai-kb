# 'Pacing the Frontier': 1,000+ frontier-AI employees ask government to help slow automated AI development

More than a thousand employees from frontier AI companies (spanning OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, Google and others; The Deep View cited more than 1,100) signed a public statement/campaign called 'Pacing the Frontier,' asking the US government to support an international effort to develop the technical and governance tools needed to deliberately pace the frontier of automated AI development. Signatories warn that as labs near the ability to automate AI research, capability development risks accelerating beyond researchers' ability to understand or control it, and that slowing safely requires industry-wide coordination.
