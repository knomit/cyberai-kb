# Anti-AI extremism: US agencies monitoring rising violent sentiment over job displacement and data centers

WIRED report (May 27, 2026): DHS, FBI, and other US agencies are monitoring anti-technology extremism linked to AI-driven job displacement and data center construction. NY Intelligence and Counterterrorism Bureau warns AI could cause 'large-scale protests that devolve into civil unrest and anti-tech violent extremist activity.' Agency in Western Pennsylvania warns adversaries may target US data centers. Two Gallup polls: (1) May 2026 — 7 in 10 Americans oppose AI infrastructure construction in their region (environmental/quality-of-life concerns). (2) April 2026 — youth (ages 14–29) excitement about AI dropped 14 percentage points since 2025; many say they don't want to use AI but feel forced to for job security. Deep View analysis: AI adoption requires voluntary engagement; enterprises blaming AI for layoffs or forcing adoption are fueling resentment that now has a violent extremist fringe.
