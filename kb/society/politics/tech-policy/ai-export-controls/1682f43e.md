# US Treasury threatened sanctions over claim that Moonshot distilled Anthropic's Fable model

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent reiterated that sanctions remain on the table for Chinese AI companies after the White House accused Moonshot of improperly distilling Anthropic's Fable model. Model distillation is a common technique for training a smaller/cheaper model on outputs of a larger one; the accusation frames it as illicit appropriation of a US lab's model.
