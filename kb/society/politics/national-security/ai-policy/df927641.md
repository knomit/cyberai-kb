# Five Eyes 2026 ministerial statement commits to frontier model access for national security

The 2026 Five Country Ministerial statement (Australia, Canada, New Zealand, UK, US) devotes three paragraphs to AI, committing to 'deepen collaboration with industry on shared national security priorities and public safety, including enabling timely access to frontier models to support secure innovation and strengthen cyber security', and to discuss which model characteristics may require additional government scrutiny. Prior Five Eyes statements treated AI mainly as a crime-adjacent concern; the shift to practical model access is new.
