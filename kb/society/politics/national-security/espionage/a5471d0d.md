# Five Eyes warns China uses LinkedIn to recruit cleared personnel

A Five Eyes advisory warns that Chinese military-intelligence services use LinkedIn and sites like Indeed and Upwork to recruit people with access to government, military, foreign-policy, or sensitive economic information. Officers or affiliates pose as employees of consultancies, think tanks, or HR firms and post job ads for foreign-policy and defense analysts, offering payment (via PayPal, Zelle, Wise, Western Union, or cryptocurrency) for increasingly privileged information. Reported targets include cleared Five Eyes nationals, military personnel in the Asia-Pacific, journalists, academics, and think-tank staff.
