# Microsoft study: only ~16% of people use AI day-to-day

The Deep View (July 3, 2026) cited a Microsoft study finding that just over 16% of people actually use AI on a day-to-day basis, illustrating a large 'capability overhang' between what AI models can do and how much they are actually used.
