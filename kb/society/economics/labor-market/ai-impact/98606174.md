# Indeed data: UK graduate hiring weak but AI-skilled roles surging

New data from Indeed's Hiring Lab found UK graduate job postings have fallen to their lowest share on record, while occupations most exposed to AI (software development, IT, engineering) now see stronger-than-average hiring growth. Job postings that explicitly mention AI continue to surge even in HR, management, marketing, and finance as overall postings in those fields decline. Senior economist Jack Kennedy said employers are prioritizing experience and AI fluency over volume hiring of junior talent, valuing skills like prompt engineering, code review, and quality assurance.
