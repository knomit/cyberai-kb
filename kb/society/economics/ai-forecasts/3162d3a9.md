# Anthropic modeling: US GDP could reach $44 trillion by 2030 under rapid-AI-diffusion scenario

Jack Clark's IMF Per Jacobsson lecture (Oct 17, 2026, Bangkok; Import AI 477) cites Anthropic modeling: if models autonomously complete a broad range of tasks and diffuse quickly, US GDP could reach 44 trillion by 2030. It is a scenario under singularity-like assumptions, not a forecast. He also says Anthropic researchers produce almost 10x the code they did in the company's first four years.
