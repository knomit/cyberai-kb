# 2026 AI labor-disruption concern backed by both expert petition and layoff data

By mid-2026 concern over AI's economic disruption is supported from two directions simultaneously: expert consensus and measured outcomes. The 'We Must Act Now' petition drew ~200 economists and tech leaders, including 16 Nobel laureates and the chief economists of OpenAI and Anthropic, warning of large-scale job displacement. Concurrently, Founders Reports' tracker attributed 101,743 U.S. job cuts to AI through June 2026 — nearly double the full-year 2025 figure of 54,836 — with Oracle, Meta, Cisco, Cloudflare and Coinbase citing AI. The convergence of elite warning and rising quantified layoffs marks AI labor impact as an active, not merely speculative, policy problem.
