# 'We Must Act Now' AI economics petition signed by ~200 economists and 16 Nobel laureates

In July 2026 a petition titled 'We Must Act Now' was published urging policymakers to prepare for AI's economic disruption before it arrives. Nearly 200 economists and tech leaders signed it, including the chief economists of OpenAI and Anthropic and 16 Nobel laureates. Its three points: (1) AI may become radically more powerful over the next 10 years; (2) this could drive an economic transformation larger than the Industrial Revolution but far faster, with risks like large-scale job displacement; (3) economists, policymakers and tech leaders must act now to build incentives, guardrails and institutions to steer AI to complement humans.
