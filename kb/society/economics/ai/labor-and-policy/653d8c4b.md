# AI cited in 101,743 US job cuts through June 2026 per Founders Reports tracker

Founders Reports' AI layoff tracker reported that through June 2026, AI was cited in 101,743 U.S. job cuts — nearly double the 54,836 attributed to AI in all of 2025. Companies including Oracle, Meta, Cisco, Cloudflare and Coinbase blamed layoffs on AI in 2026. Reported by The Deep View newsletter.
