# Economists and hedge fund leaders warn AI is widening wealth and geographic inequality in the US

Reporting in The Washington Post (2026-08-20) documents warnings from economists and hedge fund leaders that AI is increasing inequality in the United States along both wealth and geographic lines — concentrating gains in particular income strata and regions.
