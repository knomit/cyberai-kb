# Bill Gates proposes 'Human Reserved' work domains in response to AI-driven displacement

In a 2026 essay, Bill Gates argues AI 'demands an unprecedented global response' and will displace large amounts of human labor in law, customer service, medicine, software, and manufacturing over roughly a decade rather than generations, with entry- and mid-level jobs most at risk. He proposes a category he calls 'Human Reserved' — roles deliberately set aside for people, either for economic reasons (avoiding mass displacement of workers who cannot easily retrain) or for non-economic ones (e.g. a human, not a robot, delivering an incurable-disease diagnosis).
