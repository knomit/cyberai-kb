# Pew 2026 global survey: 46% expect AI to reduce jobs vs 9% expecting growth

A Pew Research Center study published 17 September 2026, surveying more than 42,000 people across 36 countries, found that 46% of respondents believe AI will lead to fewer jobs, while only 9% said it would lead to more jobs; 13% expected minimal impact and 25% were unsure. Australia and South Korea were most pessimistic at roughly 76% expecting fewer jobs, with the US at 71%. About 46% of US respondents said AI would widen the gap between rich and poor, and this belief was age-stratified in the US: 56% of young people held it versus 38% of those aged 50 and older. Operational consequence: AI vendors and enterprise buyers should not assume public sentiment tracks enterprise adoption — expect employee and public resistance framed around job loss even where deployment is going well. Note what this does NOT mean: the survey measures expectation, not realized job loss, and says nothing about actual AI-caused employment changes.
