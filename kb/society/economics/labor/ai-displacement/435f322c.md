# Bill Gates shifted from AI optimist to warning of AI-driven injustice and unemployment, and proposed a 'Human Reserved' category of work

MERGED from two records of the same position shift: one recording the shift itself, one recording the essay and the specific proposal. Both are preserved.

THE SHIFT. Bill Gates, PREVIOUSLY AN AI OPTIMIST, has publicly shifted to warning that AI could become A MAJOR SOURCE OF INJUSTICE and cause WIDESPREAD UNEMPLOYMENT.

THE ESSAY. In a 2026 essay, Gates argues AI 'demands an unprecedented global response' and will displace large amounts of human labor in LAW, CUSTOMER SERVICE, MEDICINE, SOFTWARE AND MANUFACTURING over ROUGHLY A DECADE rather than generations, with ENTRY- AND MID-LEVEL JOBS MOST AT RISK.

THE PROPOSAL. He proposes a category he calls 'HUMAN RESERVED' — roles DELIBERATELY SET ASIDE FOR PEOPLE, on either of two distinct grounds that must both be carried:
- ECONOMIC: avoiding mass displacement of workers who cannot easily retrain.
- NON-ECONOMIC: cases where a human rather than a machine should perform the role regardless of efficiency — his example is a human, not a robot, delivering an incurable-disease diagnosis.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this is one prominent individual's stated position and proposal, not a measured labor-market finding, not a policy in force anywhere, and not evidence about the displacement timeline he asserts. Both input records were single-sourced. The decade-scale timeline and the list of affected sectors are Gates's claims.
