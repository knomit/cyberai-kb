# Anthropic economics modelling finds gains flow to capital over labor

Anthropic's Economics team published a model of AI's potential impact on the US economy, finding that in rapid AI-driven growth scenarios knowledge workers could face greater unemployment and wage stagnation while the gains flow disproportionately to capital rather than labor.
