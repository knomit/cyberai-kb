# Indeed's metro AI-exposure index puts software and knowledge-work cities at the top

Indeed built a metric measuring how exposed each US metro's job composition is to AI, combining the jobs each metro advertises with the AI exposure of the underlying skills, drawing on the Indeed Hiring Lab 2025 AI at Work Report and its GenAI Skill Transformation Index. Across US metro areas the average exposure score was 44, with a range of 40 to 60. The five most-exposed metros were San Jose CA (59), Seattle WA (57), Washington DC (54), San Francisco CA (53) and Austin TX (52). Metros whose economies lean on hands-on physical work scored lower. Indeed Hiring Lab economist An Nguyen stresses the score measures potential transformation, not replacement: high exposure flags where work is likely to be reshaped or augmented, and where adapting early pays off.
