# Anthropic's economic scenarios model shows AI gains accruing to capital over labor

Anthropic's Economics team published (2026-09) a scenario model of AI's potential impact on the US economy, spanning moderate growth through extreme transformation. Its central asymmetry: under rapid AI-driven growth scenarios, KNOWLEDGE workers face higher unemployment and wage stagnation while NON-knowledge sectors may see wages rise — inverting the usual expectation that automation hits manual work first. Across scenarios, more of the gain flows to capital than to labor, widening inequality.

WHAT THIS IS NOT: this is an explicitly scenario-based model from an interested party (a frontier lab), not an empirical forecast, and not a measurement of what has already happened.
