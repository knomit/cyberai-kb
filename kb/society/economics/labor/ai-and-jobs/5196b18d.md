# Conflicting research on AI's effect on work (MIT, Gartner, Harvard)

The Deep View summarized conflicting findings on AI's labor impact: an MIT study suggests more than 11% of US work hours can already be automated; a Gartner forecast finds 50% of those laid off due to AI will be rehired; and a Harvard study found AI actually increases the hours and scope of work rather than reducing them.
