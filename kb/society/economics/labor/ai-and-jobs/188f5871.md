# Challenger Gray & Christmas: AI cited in over a quarter of April 2026 US layoffs

A Challenger, Gray & Christmas report found that more than a quarter of US layoffs in April 2026 were attributable to AI, with more than 21,000 cuts announced, and AI was the leading stated rationale for job cuts for the second month in a row. CRO Andy Challenger said: 'Regardless of whether individual jobs are being replaced by AI, the money for those roles is.' Reported in The Deep View.
