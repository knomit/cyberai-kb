# LinkedIn data: hiring down sharply, 70% of job skills to change by 2030

At VivaTech (Paris, June 2026), LinkedIn's head of global policy Sue Duke cited internal data showing European hiring down 25% from pandemic-era highs and 15% year over year, while U.S. hiring remained 24% below pre-pandemic levels (down 6% YoY as of March). LinkedIn estimates 70% of the skills used in most jobs will change by 2030, with AI as a major catalyst. Executives (including ManpowerGroup's Ruth Harper) emphasized that employers increasingly want workers who combine technical skills with AI fluency, adaptability, judgment, and communication. Reported by The Deep View.
