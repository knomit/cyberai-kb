# KPMG: 68% employee adoption of AI agents, low worker pushback

Per new KPMG data reported by The Deep View (June 25, 2026), employee adoption of AI agents has reached 68%, and only 2% of tech leaders report significant worker pushback. Among leaders who did report resistance, 78% attributed it to skills gaps and job-security fears. A separate KPMG study found 63% of organizations require human review of AI agent outputs, with deployments still limited to low-risk tasks (e.g., triaging IT tickets, answering HR questions). Average AI investment is projected to reach $269 million over the next 12 months, often without full cost visibility.
