# Ramp/Revelio study: high-intensity AI adopters grew headcount 10.2%

A joint study by Ramp and Revelio Labs analyzing firm-level generative AI investments across more than 21,000 U.S. companies found that companies with high-intensity AI spending grew overall headcount by 10.2 percent and entry-level positions by 12 percent over the two years following adoption. Reported via TLDR AI, July 2, 2026.
