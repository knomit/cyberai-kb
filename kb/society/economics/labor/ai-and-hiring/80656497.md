# LinkedIn data shows hiring down sharply and 70% of job skills changing by 2030

Per LinkedIn data cited at VivaTech (Paris) by Sue Duke, LinkedIn's head of global policy: hiring across Europe is down 25% from pandemic-era highs and 15% year over year; in the U.S., hiring remains 24% below pre-pandemic levels and was down 6% year over year as of March 2026. LinkedIn estimates that 70% of the skills used in most jobs will change by 2030, with AI as a major catalyst, raising demand for adaptability plus combined AI/technical/human skills.
