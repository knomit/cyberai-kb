# Heavy AI adopters grew headcount, per Ramp/Revelio study

A study by Ramp and Revelio Labs analyzing AI spending and workforce data from more than 21,000 U.S. companies found that companies investing most heavily in AI grew headcount by 10% over two years, with entry-level hiring up 12% among the heaviest adopters. Growth was strongest at larger, engineering-intensive, venture-backed firms in information, finance and insurance, and professional and technical services. Ramp lead economist Ara Kharazian noted employers appear to be selecting for AI skills; the authors caution the correlation should not be generalized to the broader economy.
