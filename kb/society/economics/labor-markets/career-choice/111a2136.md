# LinkedIn 2026 Davos data: most Gen Z in four Western markets say trades offer more meaning than office jobs

A LinkedIn report released around Davos 2026 found that nearly 6 in 10 Gen Z respondents say technical trades offer more meaning than an office job — 65% in France, 60% in the US, 57% in Germany, and 55% in the UK. The finding accompanies broader evidence of AI-driven anxiety reshaping early career choices; Geoffrey Hinton has publicly advised people to "train to be a plumber."
