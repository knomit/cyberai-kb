# US accounted for 82% of nearly 160,000 tech layoffs in 2026, about half tied to AI restructuring

Data reported by The Deep View shows the US made up 82% of the nearly 160,000 tech layoffs recorded in 2026, and almost half of those were tied to companies restructuring around AI and automation. Countervailing evidence complicates the picture: some estimates find companies investing in AI grew headcount by as much as 10% with entry-level hiring rising 12%, and other research finds AI increases the hours and scope of work rather than narrowing it.
