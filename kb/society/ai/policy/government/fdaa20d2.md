# White House asks OpenAI to delay release of next-gen frontier model

According to TechCrunch (June 25, 2026), the White House issued an official administrative request asking OpenAI to delay the public deployment of its next-generation frontier model over national security and structural safety concerns, pushing for an extended red-teaming window to audit the system's advanced cyber-capability limits and automated social-manipulation vulnerabilities.
