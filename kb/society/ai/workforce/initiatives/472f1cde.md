# $500M 'Raise Us' coalition launched to help US workers adapt to AI

On June 25, 2026, a bipartisan group led by former governors Gina Raimondo (Democrat) and Eric Holcomb (Republican) launched Raise Us, a $500 million nonprofit initiative to help the American workforce adapt to AI. Backers include OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon, Microsoft, and Google, leveraging private and philanthropic capital. The nonprofit aims to retain and redeploy employees and measures success by avoiding layoffs, operating across an employer coalition, state workforce partnerships, and education/training programs.
