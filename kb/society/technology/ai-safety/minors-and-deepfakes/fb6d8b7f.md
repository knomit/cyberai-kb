# Common Sense Media: 44% of surveyed teens believe they've seen AI-generated sexual content

In a Common Sense Media survey of more than 1,300 US teens ages 13–17, 44% reported believing they had seen AI-generated sexual content (explicit imagery and videos), mostly on social platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and X. Of those, 79% said their exposure was accidental, and a quarter said they had seen such content depicting themselves or people they know. About two-thirds of all teens surveyed worry about being targeted by non-consensual deepfake sexual content. In response, 38% are more careful about what they post, 30% made accounts private, 23% post less often, and 9% deleted social media entirely. 82% agreed creating such content without consent should be illegal.
