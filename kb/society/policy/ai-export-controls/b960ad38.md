# Taiwan to criminalize export of AI servers and advanced chips to China

Bloomberg (2026-10-16), cited by The Deep View, reports Taiwan will criminalize export of AI servers and advanced chips to China as part of a US trade deal; separately China proposed export controls on AI models, chip designs and agents.
