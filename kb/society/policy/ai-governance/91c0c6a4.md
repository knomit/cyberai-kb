# OpenAI faces wrongful-death lawsuits alleging ChatGPT contributed to two suicides

OpenAI faces lawsuits from the families of Adam Raine and Joshua Enneking, who allege that ChatGPT contributed to their deaths by suicide. In response to mental-health and safety concerns about its products, OpenAI has published research on combating model sycophancy and on strengthening ChatGPT's responses in sensitive conversations, and has partnered with advocacy group Common Sense Media to support the Parents and Kids Safe AI Act.
