# White House Accord on Super Intelligence: voluntary self-regulation, no new law

On 2026-09-29 President Trump and six AI leaders (Amodei/Anthropic, Pichai/Google, Zuckerberg/Meta, Brockman/OpenAI, Musk/SpaceXAI, Huang/Nvidia) signed the White House Accord on Super Intelligence, which Trump called 'morally binding' but which carries no legal enforcement. Each company commits to four layers: internal controls monitoring capability/alignment (cyber, bio, chem risk), an internal team empowered to ensure safeguards run, external auditors, and a board-level oversight committee. It says codifying into law 'may make sense' over time. Does not create new regulation; Trump rejected additional regulation.
