# CISA published a Logging Reference Architecture for federal agencies

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency published a Logging Reference Architecture for federal agencies to establish logging, visibility, and operational standards within an Agency Logging Plan. The guidance implements a practical, risk-based, prioritized logging approach intended to improve agency network monitoring. CISA Acting Executive Assistant Director for Cybersecurity Chris Butera said it guides agencies away from fragmented practices toward a mature enterprise capability that maximizes the operational value of log data.
