# FCC banned foreign-made advanced robotic devices over 4.4 pounds

The Deep View reported the FCC decided this week to ban foreign-made advanced robotic devices weighing over 4.4 pounds, a category that includes humanoid robots, robot vacuums, and robot lawn mowers, against a backdrop of Chinese dominance in robotics.
