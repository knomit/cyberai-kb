# Chainalysis: ~$30M stolen in violent crypto 'wrench attacks' in 2026

According to Chainalysis, an estimated $30 million was stolen in violent 'wrench attacks' (physical coercion to steal crypto) in 2026. Home invasions accounted for 37% of incidents in 2026, up from 26% in 2023, while kidnappings' share stayed relatively stable. Annual value stolen in such attacks peaked at $58 million in 2025.
