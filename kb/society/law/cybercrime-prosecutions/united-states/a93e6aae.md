# DoJ charged extradited Russian national over a 2016-2017 Excel malware campaign on a freelance platform

The U.S. Department of Justice charged Searzhudin Tamirlanovich Aktulaev, 40, a Russian national extradited from Cyprus on 2026-08-28, with using roughly 255 fake accounts on a freelance employment platform to send malware-laced Excel attachments to about 80,000 of its users in 2016 and 2017. He was arrested in Cyprus in May 2025, made his initial appearance in federal court in San Francisco on 2026-08-31, and was remanded to federal custody. The indictment was filed 2021-06-01 and unsealed the day of his appearance; it describes the platform only as a well-known freelance employment technology company in the Northern District of California. Thousands of computers infected with TVRAT, one of two malware families named, called back to a U.S.-hosted C2 domain, with roughly half the victims in the U.S.
