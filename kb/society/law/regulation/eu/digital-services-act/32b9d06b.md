# ChatGPT, Reddit and Roblox crossed the EU DSA 45M-user threshold for VLOP obligations

ChatGPT, Reddit and Roblox will face the EU Digital Services Act's strictest obligations after surpassing 45 million EU users, the threshold at which a service is designated a Very Large Online Platform and subject to the DSA's toughest online-safety requirements. Reported August 31, 2026.
