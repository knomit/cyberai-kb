# EU Cyber Resilience Act reporting duties start Sept 11, 2026 — 15 months before its engineering rules

The EU Cyber Resilience Act's reporting obligations take effect on September 11, 2026, requiring notification of actively exploited vulnerabilities roughly 15 months before the Act's substantive engineering and product-security requirements become applicable. The practical effect is that manufacturers must disclose exploited flaws under a regime that does not yet compel them to have built the products securely.
