# EU orders Google to open Android sensors and Search data to rivals under the DMA

On July 16, 2026 the European Commission adopted two binding specification decisions under the Digital Markets Act. The first orders Google to give rival AI assistants the same reach into Android that Gemini has: access to the camera, microphone, whatever is on screen, a wake word that fires with the display off, and the ability to drive other apps in the background by imitating taps and typing. Google must ship this in the next major release, Android 18, by August 1, 2027 at the latest. The second decision requires Google to hand anonymised Search query, click, and ranking data to rival search engines and to AI chatbots that do search, for a cost-based fee. Neither decision is a fine; the proceedings were opened January 27, 2026. Android carries around 60% of European mobile users.
