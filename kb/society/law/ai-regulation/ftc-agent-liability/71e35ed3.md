# FTC chair Ferguson: AI developers should be liable for their agents' actions

FTC Chairman Andrew Ferguson said on 2026-09-25 (Reuters event) that AI developers should be liable for the actions of their agents rather than treating agents as independent actors. The FTC separately confirmed an investigation into OpenAI, Anthropic and other labs, reportedly begun before the Hugging Face incident.
