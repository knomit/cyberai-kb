# German court holds Google liable for false AI Overviews claims

In June 2026 a regional court in Munich issued a temporary injunction finding Google directly liable for false statements generated by its AI Overviews (AIO). The case involved two Munich-based publishers whom AI Overviews incorrectly linked to scams, describing them as 'known for dubious business practices.' The court rejected Google's argument that users understand AI output may be wrong, reasoning that AIO does not merely surface links but synthesizes content 'in its own words and according to its own structure,' making the output 'the defendant's own statements.' It is among the first times a court has held an AI firm liable for the speech of its models.
