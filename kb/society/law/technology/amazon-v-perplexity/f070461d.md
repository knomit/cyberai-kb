# Amazon sues Perplexity over Comet agentic browser

Amazon is suing Perplexity, alleging its Comet browser breaks the Amazon Store's Conditions of Use by circumventing the requirement to clearly identify itself as an agent and instead identifying itself as Chrome. The case raises questions about agentic browsing and user control on the open Web.
