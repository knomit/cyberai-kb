# Regulatory pressure on AI child safety: $942M New Mexico ruling against Meta, California SB-243, FTC chatbot inquiry

AI risk-management attorney Lily Li cited three concurrent pressures shaping AI companies' teen-safety products as of Aug 2026: a $942 million New Mexico court ruling ordering Meta to pay and overhaul child protections on Facebook and Instagram; California's SB-243 regulating companion chatbots including safety protocols for minors; and an FTC inquiry into AI chatbots acting as companions and their impact on children and teens. Li also noted California's AB 1043 will require operating systems to send age signals, and argued OS-based age signals are likely a more reliable age indicator than model-side age estimation.
