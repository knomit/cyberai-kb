# Texas AG sues Meta over WhatsApp encryption claims

Texas Attorney General sued Meta (May 2026) alleging WhatsApp does not provide the end-to-end encryption it claims. Lawsuit based on Bloomberg report that US Commerce Dept Bureau of Industry and Security closed an investigation into allegations Meta could access encrypted WhatsApp messages. Preliminary findings: 'there is no limit to the type of WhatsApp message that can be viewed by Meta.' Meta called allegations 'baseless.'
