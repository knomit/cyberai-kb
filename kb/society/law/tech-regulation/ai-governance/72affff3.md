# Pope Leo XIV's 'Magnifica Humanitas': most comprehensive AI governance framework to date (May 2026)

Pope Leo XIV released a 44,000-word encyclical 'Magnifica Humanitas' (Magnificent Humanity) on May 26, 2026, in 8 languages. Key recommendations: (1) Ban AI for autonomous lethal force — life-or-death decisions must never be delegated to algorithms. (2) Prevent power concentration — private tech giants controlling data/algorithms/infrastructure threaten democracy; calls for transparency, oversight, and treating data as shared resource. (3) Protect truth, children, and education — deepfakes, disinformation, algorithm-driven outrage eroding democracy and youth mental health; calls for age limits and platform accountability. (4) Human dignity over efficiency — technology should strengthen, not weaken, humanity. (5) Humanity faces a choice — technology for profit/dominance vs. for common good. Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah (self-described atheist) attended and addressed the audience, calling for informed critics and moral voices outside financial incentives. Leo XIV is the first American pope (mathematics degree, Villanova), first pope known to use a smartphone/Apple Watch. Chose name to invoke Leo XIII's Rerum Novarum (1891 labor encyclical). Jason Hiner calls it 'clearer and more comprehensive than anything from AI labs, governments, or nonprofits.'
