# Former MEP Stelios Kouloglou repeatedly hacked with Pegasus while investigating spyware

A Citizen Lab report revealed that former Member of the European Parliament Stelios Kouloglou had his mobile device repeatedly hacked with NSO Group's Pegasus spyware while he served on a committee investigating spyware abuse. Reported by The Hacker News, July 3, 2026.
