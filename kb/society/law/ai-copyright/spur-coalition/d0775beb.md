# SPUR coalition grows to 80 publisher members; NYT, Atlantic, Economist, AFP join

The Standards for Publisher Usage Rights (SPUR) coalition, formed February 2026 (initially UK-based publications) to combat unauthorized AI scraping, welcomed 40+ new members on 2026-10-13 including The New York Times, AFP, The Economist, The Atlantic and The Observer, reaching 80 members across 20+ countries. Goal: publisher-set standards with licensing as the default way AI systems use copyrighted media. SPUR published the Content Telemetry Standard v1.0 so AI labs can record which media is used for training and report it to publishers.
