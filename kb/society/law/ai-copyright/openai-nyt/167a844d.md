# OpenAI accused of concealing ability to search ChatGPT logs in copyright case

Per TLDR AI (citing Ars Technica), news organizations suing OpenAI accuse it of misleading the court for two years about the cost and ability to search large anonymized samples of ChatGPT logs. OpenAI privacy engineer Vincent Monaco reportedly revealed that OpenAI had already conducted such searches before litigation; the discovery may lead to sanctions.
