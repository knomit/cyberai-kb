# INTERPOL Operation Jackal IV produced 58 arrests and identified 263 suspects across 22 countries

INTERPOL's Operation Jackal IV, an eight-month operation running November 2025 to June 2026 across 22 countries on six continents, targeted West African organized crime groups such as Black Axe. It produced 58 arrests and identified 263 suspects. Findings included a crime-as-a-service network of 196 identified individuals that supplied website domains and money-laundering support (17 arrests). In Johannesburg, raids on seven locations tied to romance and investment scams targeting retirees in English-speaking countries led to 39 arrests, $2.67 million confiscated and 257 bank accounts blocked; the syndicate assigned members 'conversion' or 'retention' agent roles like a legitimate business. A Romanian call-center investment scam promising high stock and crypto returns was estimated to have stolen and laundered €143 million (~$166 million) globally; 11 arrests were made with ~€330,000 ($379,000) in cash and crypto, six real-estate properties and luxury watches seized. A pan-European laundering network using shell companies and remittance services was traced through a single account that moved €845,000 (~$736,000) across 560 transactions using 20 financial instruments.
