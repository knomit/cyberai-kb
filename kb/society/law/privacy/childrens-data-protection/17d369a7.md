# TikTok agreed to a $400 million DOJ settlement over U.S. child privacy violations

The U.S. Department of Justice announced that ByteDance-owned TikTok will pay $400 million to settle a 2024 lawsuit accusing the company of violating U.S. child privacy laws. TikTok pays $300 million immediately and an additional $100 million upon entry of an order vacating a prior consent decree against TikTok's predecessor, Musical.ly. The original complaint, filed in August 2024 alongside the Federal Trade Commission, alleged 'massive-scale invasions of children's privacy' by knowingly allowing children under 13 to create accounts and unlawfully collecting data from those using 'Kids Mode', and alleged TikTok and ByteDance failed to comply with parents' requests to delete their children's accounts and information.
