# Brazil's ANPD fined ByteDance R$153.8M for unlawful processing of teenagers' data

Brazil's data protection authority ANPD fined TikTok owner ByteDance 153.8 million reais (about $29.81 million) for violating Brazil's General Data Protection Law, finding that ByteDance's local unit processed personal data of teenagers aged 13–18 without a valid legal basis. The regulator estimated TikTok may have processed the data of at least 8 million children during the review period.
