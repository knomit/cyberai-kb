# US seized two NightmareStresser domains tied to hundreds of thousands of DDoS attacks since 2022

A U.S. court-authorized operation seized two domains associated with NightmareStresser, a distributed denial-of-service (DDoS)-for-hire service. The U.S. Justice Department assesses NightmareStresser was used to launch hundreds of thousands of actual or attempted DDoS attacks worldwide since 2022, targeting educational institutions, government agencies, gaming platforms, and millions of people. Domain seizure disrupts the service's front end; it is not the same as arresting operators or dismantling the attack infrastructure, so it should not be read as the service being permanently offline.
