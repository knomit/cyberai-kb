# Zurich court sentenced a Ukrainian IT specialist to 12 years 9 months as lead developer of Lockergoga, MegaCortex and Nefilim

A Zurich court sentenced a Ukrainian IT specialist to 12 years and nine months in prison for developing ransomware used in extortion attacks on companies including Stadler Rail. The court identified him as the LEAD DEVELOPER behind three ransomware families — Lockergoga, MegaCortex, and Nefilim — with estimated losses of $123 million. His defence was that he only worked as a consultant for an unknown client in IT security and was unaware his software was used for ransomware; the court did not accept it. The significance: a developer, not an operator or affiliate, drew a near-13-year sentence, and the 'I only wrote the code' defence failed.
