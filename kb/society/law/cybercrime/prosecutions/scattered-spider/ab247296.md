# Two Scattered Spider hackers sentenced to 5.5 years each for TfL hack

Owen Flowers, 18, and Thalha Jubair, 20, were each sentenced to five and a half years at Woolwich Crown Court on 16 July 2026 for the 2024 hack of Transport for London. The attack left 148 TfL systems inoperable and forced all 27,000 TfL employees into an office for in-person password resets. The NCA and CPS put TfL's losses and recovery costs at GBP 29 million. Both pleaded guilty on 22 June 2026 under Section 3ZA of the Computer Misuse Act 1990 - the Act's most serious charge - on the basis they were reckless as to whether they created a significant risk of serious damage to human welfare. The CPS says they are believed to be the first hackers successfully prosecuted under Section 3ZA.
