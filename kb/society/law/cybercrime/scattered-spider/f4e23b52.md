# 19-year-old Scattered Spider suspect extradited from Finland to face U.S. charges

A teenager accused of belonging to the hacking group Scattered Spider was extradited from Finland to the United States to face charges of conspiracy, computer intrusion, and fraud, according to a U.S. Department of Justice announcement. Reported by The Hacker News, July 2, 2026.
