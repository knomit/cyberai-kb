# Ukraine dismantled 94 fraudulent call centers in a nationwide operation

Ukrainian police disrupted 94 fraudulent call centers in a nationwide operation involving more than 400 searches. Schemes included callers impersonating bank employees, fraudulent investment and cryptocurrency platforms, and attempts to obtain remote access to victims' devices. Seizures included 3,336 pieces of computer equipment, 1,346 phones, over 5,200 SIM cards, 90 bank cards, access to 20 crypto wallets, 22 cars, about $2 million, €64,000, cash in hryvnias, a kilogram of bank gold and jewelry.
