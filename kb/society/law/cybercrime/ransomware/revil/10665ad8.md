# Armenia detains Russian tourist on US REvil warrant; lawyers claim mistaken identity

Armenia has held a Russian tourist named Aleksandr Ermakov in detention since 28 June 2026 on a US extradition request for a REvil ransomware suspect of the same name. The suspect the US wants is Aleksandr Gennadievich Ermakov, sanctioned by Australia, the US and the UK in January 2024 for stealing 9.7 million records from Medibank Private, one of Australia's largest private health insurers, and leaking some on the dark web; he is also reportedly serving a two-year Russian sentence barring him from leaving the country. His lawyers say the man detained in Armenia is Aleksandr Yuryevich Ermakov from Omsk, a former prison-service lawyer - i.e. the wrong man.
