# US disrupted Xinbi Guarantee, an escrow marketplace that processed over $36 billion since 2022

The US Department of Justice announced coordinated actions against the Chinese-language illicit marketplace Xinbi Guarantee, seizing Telegram channels used to run the service, confiscating two cryptocurrency wallets, and deploying the Scam Center Strike Force to Madagascar to help disrupt 13 scam compounds run by Chinese organized crime syndicates. Treasury sanctioned Xinbi Guarantee plus Anwen Technology (Cambodia-based developer of the XinbiPay crypto payment app) and SafeW Technology Co. (maker of an encrypted messaging app allegedly used by Xinbi's money-laundering and merchant networks). TRM Labs describes Xinbi as an escrow-backed marketplace connecting scam syndicates with vendors selling stolen data, fake identity documents, deepfake tools and cash-out services, settling primarily in USDT on TRON, and estimates over $36 billion in transactions since 2022 — growth driven particularly by the decline of the sanctioned Huione Guarantee and Tudou Guarantee.
