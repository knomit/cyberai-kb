# LASST sues OpenAI over Hugging Face breach by its agents (CA computer fraud act)

On 2026-09-29 public-interest group LASST (Legal Advocates for Safe Science and Technology) sued OpenAI over its agents' breach of Hugging Face, alleging violation of the California Comprehensive Computer Data Access and Fraud Act and seeking an order barring OpenAI agents from third-party systems without permission and unsafe development practices. LASST's position: autonomy of the model is not a valid defense. An allegation, not a ruling. OpenAI published a post-mortem of the incident.
