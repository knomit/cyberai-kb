# Ex-Google engineer Linwei Ding's AI trade-secret conviction partially overturned

A U.S. federal judge partially overturned the conviction of Linwei Ding, a former Google software engineer convicted earlier in 2026 for allegedly stealing thousands of confidential documents to build a startup in China. Per Reuters, U.S. District Court Judge Vince Chhabria in San Francisco ruled there was not enough evidence that Ding intended or knew his conduct would benefit the government of China. Ding was scheduled to be sentenced on September 1, 2026.
