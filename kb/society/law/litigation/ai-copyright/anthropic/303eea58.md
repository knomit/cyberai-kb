# Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell sued Anthropic over training data, alleging a torrenting campaign from July 2021 with named founders involved — allegations in active litigation, denied by Anthropic

MERGED from two records of the same lawsuit: one recording the filing and parties, one recording the specific allegations. Both are preserved.

## THE FILING
SONY MUSIC PUBLISHING and WARNER CHAPPELL filed a MULTI-BILLION-DOLLAR lawsuit against Anthropic alleging INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY THEFT, characterized in the complaint as AMONG THE LARGEST ONGOING THEFTS OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. This adds MUSIC PUBLISHERS to the set of rightsholders litigating against frontier AI labs over training data.

## THE ALLEGATIONS
Plaintiffs allege that Anthropic's MASS ILLEGAL TORRENTING BEGAN IN JULY 2021; that co-founder BENJAMIN MANN PERSONALLY USED BITTORRENT to download and upload MILLIONS OF PIRATED BOOKS from LIBRARY GENESIS; and that co-founder and CEO DARIO AMODEI APPROVED THE TORRENTING. Internal staff chats INCLUDING THE PHRASE 'zlibrary my beloved' are cited.

## THE DENIAL
ANTHROPIC DENIES using any pirated material to train its COMMERCIAL MODELS. Publishers say they can prove otherwise.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN — load-bearing
THESE ARE ALLEGATIONS IN ACTIVE LITIGATION, NOT ESTABLISHED FINDINGS. Nothing here is a court determination. Note also the precise shape of Anthropic's denial: it is scoped to TRAINING COMMERCIAL MODELS, which is narrower than a denial that any pirated material was ever downloaded — the two are not the same claim and the distinction should not be dropped when citing either side.
