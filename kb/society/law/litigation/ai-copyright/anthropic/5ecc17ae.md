# Sony suit cites Anthropic staff chats to allege a torrenting campaign beginning July 2021

In a suit brought by Sony, plaintiffs allege Anthropic's mass illegal torrenting began in July 2021, that co-founder Benjamin Mann personally used BitTorrent to download and upload millions of pirated books from Library Genesis, and that co-founder and CEO Dario Amodei approved the torrenting; internal staff chats including the phrase 'zlibrary my beloved' are cited. Anthropic denies using any pirated material to train its commercial models; publishers say they can prove otherwise. These are allegations in active litigation, not established findings.
