# Russia's FSB charged Telegram founder Pavel Durov with aiding terrorist activity

Russia's Federal Security Service (FSB) said it charged Telegram founder Pavel Durov for allegedly facilitating terrorist activities and for failing to remove prohibited information, as reported on July 29, 2026.
