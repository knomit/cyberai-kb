# US OFAC issues first sanctions on a VPN service and malware cryptor seller over ransomware

The U.S. Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) designated two individuals and a VPN service provider for enabling ransomware actors and other cybercriminals — described as the first time a VPN service and a malware cryptor seller were sanctioned over ransomware support. Reported by The Hacker News.
