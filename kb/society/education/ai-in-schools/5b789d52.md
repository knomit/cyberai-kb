# PISA 2025: OECD scores at record lows; AI users for schoolwork scored ~20 points lower

The OECD's PISA 2025 results (Volume I) reported OECD-average performance declining to the lowest levels ever observed in science, reading and mathematics, and that students reporting AI use for schoolwork scored on average 20 points lower than non-users (about one year of schooling). Correlational: it does not by itself show AI use caused the gap.
