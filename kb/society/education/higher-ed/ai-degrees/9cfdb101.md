# US universities rapidly expand AI undergraduate degree programs

As of April 2026 there were at least 1,000 AI programs across nearly 584 US colleges and universities, including 78 majors and 103 minors, per Northeastern University's Center for Inclusive Computing. In 2021 only five schools offered AI majors (per The New York Times). Carnegie Mellon University became the first US university to offer a bachelor's degree in AI, in 2018. Programs vary widely from math-intensive (CMU) to applied (Univ. of Oklahoma Polytechnic) to interdisciplinary humanities-oriented (Drake University).
