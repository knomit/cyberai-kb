# Common Sense Media survey: 70% of teens use AI for schoolwork; only 30% have had a teacher discuss safe AI use

A Common Sense Media survey published Monday Aug 17, 2026 found 70% of teens use AI for schoolwork, yet only 39% of respondents feel they are missing out on learning when they use AI, and only 30% of teens say a teacher has ever talked with them about how to use AI safely. Robbie Torney, Common Sense Media's head of AI and digital assessments, said the organization had not yet tested ChatGPT for Teens and had unresolved questions about the features' safety and efficacy, noting that social media showed parental controls fail most families because people do not understand or adopt them.
