# Research finds teens increasingly turn to AI chatbots for emotional support

A report from Hopelab and the Center for Digital Thriving (based on interviews with 30 people aged 14–22) found teens often turn to chatbots for emotional topics when they feel unable or unwilling to rely on people around them, reporting they feel more understood, validated, and less judged by AI. Corroborating data: a Pew Research Center survey found 12% of US teenagers have used generative AI for emotional support or advice, and research from Surgo Health and The Jed Foundation found about one in eight young people who reported mental-health struggles had discussed those concerns with chatbots. (Note: sensitive topic — youth mental health.)
