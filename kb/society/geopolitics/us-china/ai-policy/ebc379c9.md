# US proposed an AI-incident 'notification mechanism' with China ahead of Xi's Sept 2026 White House visit

Ahead of Chinese President Xi Jinping's White House visit the week of Sept 21, 2026, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the US had proposed a 'notification mechanism' for AI incidents that could affect national security, seeking a 'shared vision of common goals and common threats' between the two leading AI powers. This is a proposal, not an agreed mechanism.
