# China opens a cybersecurity review of Palo Alto Networks

China's Cyberspace Administration (CAC) announced a review of Palo Alto Networks' products, citing protection of critical information infrastructure, prevention of network security risks, and national security under China's National Security Law and cybersecurity laws.
