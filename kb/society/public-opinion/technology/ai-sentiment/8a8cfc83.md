# Pew 2026: majority of US adults under 30 now more concerned than excited about AI

A Pew Research Center survey published August 18, 2026 found 52% of Americans say they are more concerned than excited about the increased use of AI, up from 37% in 2021.

For the first time, a majority of adults under 30 (55%) say they are more concerned than excited — putting young adults on par with all other age groups, reversing the historical pattern in which younger cohorts are the most enthusiastic early adopters of new technology.

71% of adults think AI will lead to fewer jobs in the US over the next two decades, up from 64% the prior year, and the youngest adults are now as likely as those aged 30–64 to hold that view.

Related: separate LinkedIn research indicates Gen Z is increasingly open to careers in the skilled trades, consistent with the finding that physically hands-on work is the least AI-exposed category. That is a different dataset, not part of the Pew survey.
