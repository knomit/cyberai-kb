# Pew: majority of US adults under 30 are now more concerned than excited about AI

Pew Research Center found that, for the first time, a majority of US adults under 30 (55%) say they are more concerned than excited about AI. Separate LinkedIn research indicates Gen Z is increasingly open to careers in the skilled trades, consistent with the finding that physically hands-on work is the least AI-exposed category.
