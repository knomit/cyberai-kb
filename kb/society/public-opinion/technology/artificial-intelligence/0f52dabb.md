# US public opinion on AI has turned broadly negative

As of mid-2026, public sentiment in the US toward AI is broadly negative: one poll cited found 55% of Americans believe AI will do more harm than good, and a May 2026 Gallup poll found about 7 in 10 Americans oppose data center construction in their local area (nearly 50% strongly opposed). Data center opposition is framed as the most physical, visible manifestation of broader AI discontent — tied to utility bills, pollution, and water supply. Separately, Pew Research data indicates that for the first time more people globally trust China than the US to do good in the world.
