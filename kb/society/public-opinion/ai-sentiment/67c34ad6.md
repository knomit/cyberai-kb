# Quinnipiac poll: 55% Americans negative on AI, 70% expect job loss

March 2026 Quinnipiac University poll: 55% of Americans hold a negative view of AI (up from ~40% in 2024), 70% believe AI will cause significant job loss in their lifetime, 38% say AI makes them feel 'anxious or worried' vs 18% 'excited or hopeful'. 63% support federal regulation of AI. Poll of 1,437 adults, margin of error ±2.6%.
