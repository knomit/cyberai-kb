# Oxford survey: many UK adults use LLMs for emotional and relationship support

An Oxford Internet Institute survey of 2,000 UK adults (reported by The Deep View, Jul 9, 2026; lead author Florence Enock) found 31% of regular LLM users use them for emotional support, 38% for personal relationship advice, and about two-thirds for health information, with 75% enthusiastic about chatbots' potential benefits.
