# Survey: 9 in 10 US workers report mental strain, partly from AI

At a VivaTech (Paris) panel on AI and wellness, leaders and a psychiatrist argued AI is increasing workplace stress by adding demands, fueling uncertainty, and challenging workers' sense of purpose. A Headspace study found 9 of 10 U.S. workers are experiencing cognitive and mental strain, with more than a third saying it worsened over the past year, in part due to AI. Panelists noted AI doesn't automatically reduce stress — reviewing AI outputs adds distractions and time saved is often refilled with more meetings, while eroding critical-thinking skills.
