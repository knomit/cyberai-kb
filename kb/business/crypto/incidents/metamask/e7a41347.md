# MetaMask infrastructure incident; affected Ethereum validators exited

MetaMask disclosed an ongoing security incident in part of its infrastructure, said it found no immediate threat to MetaMask wallets, and proactively exited affected validators in its non-custodial staking operations; it does not manage clients' withdrawal keys. Investigation ongoing.
