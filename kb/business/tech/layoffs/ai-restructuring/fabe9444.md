# US firms drove 82% of 156,975 global tech layoffs in H1 2026

A report from UK research firm TradingPlatforms found that about 82% of the 156,975 tech jobs eliminated globally in the first half of 2026 came from US companies, with nearly half of the cuts tied to US firms restructuring around AI and automation (led by Oracle, Meta, Block, Cisco, and PayPal). The analysis drew on TrueUp.io, Layoffs.fyi, TechCrunch, and state WARN filings. Cloud/SaaS companies recorded the most layoffs globally; e-commerce/marketplace firms (Amazon, eBay) were second. Specific 2026 cuts: Microsoft ~4,800 jobs on July 6 (1,600 in Xbox); Oracle ~21,000 over the prior year; Meta ~8,000; Cloudflare ~1,100. Notably, Ford rehired 350+ veteran engineers after its AI-powered quality systems couldn't perform on their own.
