# Google/MIT study: enterprises expose only ~45% of their data to AI, and access level tracks agent accuracy

A Google Cloud and MIT Technology Review study of 300 global data and technology executives found more than half say legacy data systems prevent them from scaling AI agents, and that companies give AI access to just 45% of their enterprise data on average. Among organizations granting AI access to more than 70% of enterprise data, every respondent described agent outputs as mostly or consistently accurate; among those granting 30% or less, only 22% trusted their agents' decisions.
