# Deloitte: agent ambition far outpaces agent deployment at scale

A Deloitte survey of more than 500 U.S. business and IT leaders found nearly three-quarters expect half of their businesses to be rebuilt or designed around AI agents within four years, but while 42% have tested or deployed agents, only 15% have scaled multi-agent systems across the business. Just 21% said their business processes are prepared for agentic AI and only one in five said their organization is ready to redesign processes for agents.
