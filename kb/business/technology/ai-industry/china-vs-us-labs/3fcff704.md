# Chinese labs are targeting enterprise AI on price while trust remains the obstacle

Chinese model firms (DeepSeek, Alibaba, Z.ai) are pushing into the enterprise market with cheaper, more customizable open-weight models, using coding as the entry point, at a time when Claude Code and Codex bills are straining enterprise budgets. The counterweight is trust: OpenAI, Anthropic and Google have accused these providers of model distillation, and the models carry weaker guardrails and jailbreak risk.
