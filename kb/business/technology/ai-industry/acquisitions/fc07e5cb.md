# Stripe agreed to acquire OpenRouter for over $7 billion

Stripe reportedly agreed to acquire AI model-routing startup OpenRouter for more than $7 billion. OpenRouter, which routes developer requests across models based on capability and price, had been valued at roughly $1.3 billion after its May 2026 funding round.
