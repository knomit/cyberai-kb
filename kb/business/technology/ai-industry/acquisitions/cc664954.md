# SpaceX acquired Cursor

SpaceX acquired the AI coding company Cursor, with the stated aim of enhancing AI model training using SpaceX's GPU resources and developing stronger, more cost-effective models. Grok 4.6 is cited as an early demonstration of the collaboration.
