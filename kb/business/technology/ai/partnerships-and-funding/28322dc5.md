# June 2026 AI industry deals: SpaceX-Reflection, Micron-Anthropic, Google-A24, Groq, OpenAI-Getty

The Deep View's June 23, 2026 links roundup noted several AI-industry deals: SpaceX and Reflection signed a computing deal worth up to $6.3 billion; Micron and Anthropic inked a strategic partnership to scale AI infrastructure; Google invested $75 million in film studio A24 and forged an AI film-tools partnership; chip firm Groq raised $650 million to expand data-center capacity; and OpenAI and Getty Images announced a licensing deal for images in ChatGPT.
