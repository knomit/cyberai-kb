# OpenAI's Nvidia-backed Ohio data centre campus: reported at 10 GW, with Nvidia's first-phase backstop cut from $250B to under $120B over investor risk concerns

OpenAI was reported to be in advanced talks to lease a 10 gigawatt data centre in Ohio, with Nvidia backing. By the later reporting the two were close to closing a deal for the large-scale campus, with Nvidia providing a financial backstop for the FIRST PHASE only — roughly five gigawatts of power.

THE BACKSTOP WAS REDUCED. Nvidia originally planned to commit $250 billion but lowered its guarantee to less than $120 billion, to address investor concerns about the chipmaker's risk exposure. OpenAI must decide later how to finance the remainder.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the 10 GW figure describes the campus as reported in the earlier, thinner account; the financially backstopped commitment is the ~5 GW first phase, and the two should not be conflated. Both records are reported/'in talks' rather than confirmed closed, and the earlier account carried no named source. Nothing here establishes that the remaining capacity beyond phase one is financed.
