# OpenAI's Nvidia-backed Ohio data centre campus: reported at 10 GW, with Nvidia's first-phase backstop cut from $250B to under $120B (reported as 'up to $105B' on 2026-08-17) over investor risk concerns

MERGED from two records of the same Nvidia-financed OpenAI Ohio campus, reported at different moments with different figures. Both figures are carried below rather than reconciled, because the records do not state that they describe the same disclosure.

THE CAMPUS. OpenAI was reported to be in advanced talks to lease a 10 GIGAWATT data centre in Ohio, with Nvidia backing. By the later reporting the two were close to closing a deal for the large-scale campus, with Nvidia providing a financial backstop for the FIRST PHASE only — roughly FIVE GIGAWATTS of power. The lease was also backed by SB Energy.

THE BACKSTOP WAS REDUCED. Nvidia originally planned to commit $250 BILLION but lowered its guarantee to LESS THAN $120 BILLION, to address investor concerns about the chipmaker's risk exposure. OpenAI must decide later how to finance the remainder.

THE 2026-08-17 FIGURE. On 2026-08-17 news broke that Nvidia would provide UP TO $105 BILLION in financing for the Ohio data centre. That figure sits inside the reduced 'less than $120 billion' band, but no source states that the two numbers describe the same commitment or disclosure, and they should not be silently collapsed into one.

NVIDIA'S FRAMING. In its accompanying blog post Nvidia framed AI factories as the "defining infrastructure" of the AI era and said "compute is revenue," positioning itself as responsible for helping secure those resources for labs facing compute constraints.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN — load-bearing, carried from both inputs:
- The 10 GW figure describes the campus as reported in the earlier, thinner account; the financially backstopped commitment is the ~5 GW FIRST PHASE, and the two must not be conflated.
- Both records are reported / 'in talks' rather than confirmed closed, and the earlier account carried no named source.
- Nothing here establishes that the remaining capacity beyond phase one is financed.
- Nvidia's framing is Nvidia's own marketing language, not an independent characterization of the deal's economics. A chipmaker financing a lab that buys its chips is the entangled structure described in kb/business/markets/ai-infrastructure/buildout-capital-2026.md; the circularity is what makes headline figures less informative about independent demand.
