# Nvidia cut its OpenAI Ohio data center backstop from $250B to under $120B

Nvidia and OpenAI are close to closing a deal for a large-scale data-center campus in Ohio in which Nvidia provides a financial backstop for the first phase, roughly five gigawatts of power. Nvidia originally planned to commit $250 billion but lowered its guarantee to less than $120 billion to address investor concerns about the chipmaker's risk exposure. OpenAI must decide later how to finance the remainder.
