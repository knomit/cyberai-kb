# AI music disclosure scandals: HAVEN 'I Run' (Suno) and The Velvet Sundown

Per The Deep View (Oct 2026): the song 'I Run' by HAVEN went viral in Nov 2025 before it emerged that producers used Suno to transform Harrison Walker's vocals into a feminine voice; backlash led to it being banned from streaming services. The Velvet Sundown gained over a million listeners across streaming platforms before admitting it was 'a synthetic music project guided by human creative direction'. Lesson: undisclosed AI use triggers stronger backlash than AI use itself.
