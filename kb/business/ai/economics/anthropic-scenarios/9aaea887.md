# Anthropic economics scenarios for 2030: GDP +1.6% / +8.3% / +32.4% with rising knowledge-worker displacement

The Deep View (2026-09-28) summarizing Anthropic's economics research: modest scenario (internet-like) GDP $34.1T (+1.6%), 0.3% of knowledge workers displaced, all reallocated; substantial (railroad-like) +8.3% to $36.3T, ~2.5% displaced, 1.8% re-employed by 2030; extreme (likely via recursive self-improvement) +32.4% to $44.4T, 13.5% displaced, 5.2% re-employed, 8.3% unemployed. Labor share currently ~60% vs 40% capital could flip in extreme cases. Appcast economist Julius Probst argues the modest scenario is likeliest. These are scenarios, not predictions.
