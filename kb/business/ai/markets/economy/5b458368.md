# Generative AI economy at ~$110B trailing sales, $175B+ run rate

An Exponential View analysis estimates the generative AI economy generated about $110 billion in sales over the trailing 12 months as of mid-2026, with an annualized revenue run rate exceeding $175 billion. The piece notes the supply side is well understood but demand-side measurement (enterprise plus consumer spend, revenue vs. investment expense) is harder, and considers how falling token prices and improving token quality affect the trajectory.
