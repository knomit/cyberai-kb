# OpenAI reportedly in talks to raise $30B at ~$1.4T valuation; 2026 IPO ruled out

TechCrunch (2026-09-29) reports OpenAI is in talks to raise at least $30B at roughly a $1.4T valuation, after raising $122B in March; it has ruled out a 2026 public debut, and the new round is a bridge to an IPO. Reported, not confirmed by OpenAI.
