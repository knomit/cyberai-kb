# KPMG: employee adoption of AI agents reaches 68%

KPMG data cited by The Deep View (week of June 26, 2026) found that employee adoption of AI agents has reached 68%, while only 2% of leaders report significant pushback on AI deployments, indicating workers are gravitating toward agentic tools with limited internal resistance.
