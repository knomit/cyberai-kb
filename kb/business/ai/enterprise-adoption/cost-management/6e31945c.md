# Uber burned through its annual AI budget in four months amid 'tokenmaxxing'

Uber incentivized employee AI use with internal leaderboards ranking workers by AI tokens consumed, and reportedly burned through its annual AI budget in the first four months of 2026 — an example of the 'tokenmaxxing' trend and subsequent cost backlash described by The Deep View (citing Fortune).
