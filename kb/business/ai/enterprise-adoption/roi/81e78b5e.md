# Gartner Oct 2026: AI spend rising while few executives see clear returns

At Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo (week of 2026-10-19), Gartner reported survey data: 81% of CIOs are increasing AI spend; 44% of CEOs struggle to demonstrate AI-driven financial returns to stakeholders; 13% of CIOs say their organizations have realized significant or transformational value from AI; 11% of CFOs see clear returns; 4% of CIOs have plans to redesign roles for a human-agent workforce; 58% of CIOs face increased pressure to deliver cost improvements from AI. Gartner analysts argue the value problem is not solved by adding more AI to existing ways of working; workflow and organizational redesign is required. Does not mean AI spending is falling: spend is rising while demonstrated ROI lags.
