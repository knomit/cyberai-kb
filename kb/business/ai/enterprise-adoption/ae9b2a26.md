# Mid-2026 enterprise AI: token-cost backlash coincides with Anthropic dominance

As of mid-2026, several data points cohere into one picture: enterprise 'tokenmaxxing' drove runaway inference costs (e.g., Uber burned its annual AI budget in four months), triggering a cost-control backlash; Anthropic simultaneously dominated enterprise frontier-LLM adoption (77% of adopting orgs per Ramp, up 40pts YoY) and saw its revenue run rate rocket from $9B to $47B, while shipping Claude Enterprise spend-alert/analytics/model-default features to help customers rein in that very spend, and locking in massive compute (a reported $19B TeraWulf infrastructure lease). The through-line: surging agentic token consumption is both Anthropic's growth engine and its customers' budget crisis, and vendor-side cost governance has become a competitive necessity.
