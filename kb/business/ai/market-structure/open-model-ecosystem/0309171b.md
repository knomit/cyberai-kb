# Nvidia commits that its compute will not be required to use Hugging Face

In his blog post announcing the $12.9 billion Hugging Face acquisition on 2026-09-03, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang made an explicit non-tying commitment: 'Hugging Face will remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem. Developers will choose the models they want, the frameworks they want, the clouds and inference service providers they want and the computing platforms they want. NVIDIA compute will not be required to build on or deploy through Hugging Face.' Clem Delangue reinforced the grassroots framing in the press briefing, saying the platform lets AI 'be more distributed throughout the world and avoid too much concentration of power.' This is a stated commitment from an acquirer, not a binding structural or regulatory safeguard — there is no enforcement mechanism named, and the standing concern is that commercial gravity pulls an acquired platform toward being a revenue driver for the parent over time, producing choices it would not make as an independent open ecosystem.
