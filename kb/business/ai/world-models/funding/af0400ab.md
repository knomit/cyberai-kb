# World-model startups raised billions in 2025-2026

As of mid-2026, world-model startups (AI models that understand physical environments) attracted large early-stage funding: AMI Labs (Advanced Machine Intelligence), founded by Yann LeCun after leaving Meta, raised a $1B seed at a $3.5B pre-money valuation in March 2026; World Labs, founded by Fei-Fei Li, raised $1B at a $5B valuation in February; Runway raised $315M Series E at $5.3B; Luma raised $900M Series C at $4B in November; and General Intuition raised $320M Series A at a $2.3B valuation in June. Reported by The Deep View, July 3, 2026.
