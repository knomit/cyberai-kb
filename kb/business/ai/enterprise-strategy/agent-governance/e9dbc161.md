# Dataiku CEO: as AI creation cost falls, value moves to judgment and agent management

In a Deep View Conversations interview published 2026-09-03, Dataiku CEO and co-founder Florian Douetteau argued that as the cost of creating software with AI falls toward zero, the scarce and valuable work shifts to deciding what to build, proving it works, and maintaining it over time. His specific predictions: enterprise AI models are commoditizing; companies will buy roughly 90% of their agents and build only the 10% that differentiates their business; and 'agent management' will emerge as a necessary discipline. He framed the CEO dilemma as two-sided — move too slowly and competitors gain a structural cost advantage, move too fast without control and one major AI failure creates a crisis. These are a vendor CEO's forward-looking claims, and Dataiku sells governance and agent-management tooling, so the 90/10 split is a strategic thesis rather than a measured market figure.
