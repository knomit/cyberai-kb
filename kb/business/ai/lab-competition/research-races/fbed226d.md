# OpenAI redirected all compute to Navier-Stokes after rumors Anthropic was ahead

OpenAI technical staff member Sebastien Bubeck said in the 2026-09-08 press briefing that OpenAI did not pick the Navier-Stokes problem on merit alone: the company saw rumors that rival Anthropic had solved two Millennium Prize Problems including Navier-Stokes, where Anthropic had made more progress, and so decided to 'pull together all of our compute and to put it all on that question' — a decision Bubeck said cost millions of dollars. A credit dispute followed: mathematician Tristan Buckmaster, who had been working with Anthropic employee Levent Alpöge on the equation for the past month, alleged OpenAI took their work and finished it with a single prompt; OpenAI chief research officer Mark Chen denied this, saying 'that would be a huge breach of user trust.' The allegation is unresolved as of 2026-09-09. The mechanism worth carrying forward: competitive rivalry, not scientific prioritization, determined where an enormous compute budget was pointed.
