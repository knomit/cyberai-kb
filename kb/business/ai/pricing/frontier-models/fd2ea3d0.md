# Gemini 4 Argon pricing vs Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol

Per a Google representative quoted by The Deep View, Gemini 4 Argon launches at an introductory $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens (cached input 95% cheaper), matching Claude Sonnet 5.5 and OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol. After the introductory period the price doubles to $4 input / $20 output per million tokens. Deep View's analysis: with labs leapfrogging each other, price is starting to matter as much as capability.
