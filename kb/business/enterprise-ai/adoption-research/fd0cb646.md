# Deloitte 2026 State of AI in the Enterprise: 81% speed-up vs 25% transformation

Deloitte's State of AI in the Enterprise report (3,500 global business leaders) finds 81% of enterprises used AI to speed up decision-making but only 25% say it meaningfully transformed the business. 60% have redesigned jobs around AI but only 15% extensively or completely; 15% report a significant shift in decision-making authority; revenue gains from AI rose to 28% from 20% in the January report. Wharton's fourth annual report finds ~40% measure AI returns by hours saved and 27% track net-new revenue. Takeaway: ROI depends on redesigning work, not just faster existing work.
