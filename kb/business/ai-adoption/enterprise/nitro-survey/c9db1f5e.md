# Nitro survey shows AI adoption gap between executives and managers

Per The Deep View, a Nitro survey of 1,300+ executives, managers, and directors across the US, UK, and Canada found 85% of C-suite executives report deploying AI but only 54% of managers say AI is a top priority. Among teams that adopted AI, 37% rely on standalone tools and only 12% say AI is fully embedded in document workflows; 62% of managers say employees spend 6+ hours per week on manual document tasks, and only 43% report clear, enforced AI policies.
