# AI cost pressure is driving vertical integration across the stack in 2026

In mid-2026 large AI players are moving to own more of their stack to cut the cost of running AI at scale. Microsoft is swapping licensed OpenAI/Anthropic models for cheaper in-house MAI models in Excel and Outlook (a 'good-enough AI' bet on its Office 365 lock-in) while cutting 4,800 jobs and reallocating to AI. China's DeepSeek is designing its own data-center inference chips to reduce reliance on Nvidia and Huawei under US export controls. The shared driver is margin: incumbents accept slightly lower capability or upfront investment to escape third-party model/hardware costs.
