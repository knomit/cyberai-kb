# Apple sues OpenAI over hardware trade secrets (July 2026)

On July 10, 2026, Apple filed suit against OpenAI in US District Court, alleging former Apple employees systematically took trade secrets to build OpenAI's hardware devices (designed by Jony Ive). The suit names OpenAI's chief hardware engineer (24 years at Apple) and Chang Liu (8 years at Apple, joined OpenAI's hardware team in January 2026), and states OpenAI has hired over 400 former Apple employees. Apple requested a jury trial. Apple's incoming CEO is John Ternus.
