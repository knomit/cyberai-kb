# Apple sends legal letters to former employees now at OpenAI over trade-secret suit

Apple has sent legal letters to dozens of former Apple employees now working at OpenAI, instructing them to preserve documents relevant to Apple's trade-secret lawsuit against OpenAI. Apple claims OpenAI recruited key engineers and benefited from proprietary designs and manufacturing processes. More than 400 former Apple employees now work at OpenAI. OpenAI denied the allegations.
