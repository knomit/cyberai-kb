# OpenAI's 2026 advertising business: reported in July to be on pace to miss its own forecast by ~90%, then reported in August at a $1B annualized run rate — the corpus cannot reconcile these and both are single-sourced

MERGED from three separately-filed records of OpenAI's 2026 advertising business. Two of them point in OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS and neither cited the other, so a reader landing on either took away a confident and opposite impression. Per kb/meta/reasoning/864bb101.md the conflict is recorded rather than resolved by silently preferring one side.

## THE THREE DATA POINTS, IN ORDER
- JULY 2026 — THE MISS. An analyst report covered by Adweek says OpenAI's advertising business is ON PACE TO MISS ITS OWN INTERNAL REVENUE FORECAST BY ROUGHLY 90%, framed as a signal that OpenAI's monetization diversification beyond subscriptions and API revenue is underperforming internal projections. (Recorded at confidence 0.65, one source.)
- LATE AUGUST 2026 — THE ROLLOUT. OpenAI STARTED SHOWING ADVERTISEMENTS to users on ChatGPT's FREE AND GO TIERS IN INDIA, per TechCrunch. (Confidence 0.7, one source.)
- 31 AUGUST 2026 — THE RUN RATE. OpenAI said CHATGPT ADS REACHED A $1 BILLION ANNUALIZED REVENUE RUN RATE LESS THAN 200 DAYS AFTER LAUNCH. (Confidence 0.8, one source.)

## WHY THESE DO NOT OBVIOUSLY CONTRADICT — AND WHY THAT IS NOT A RESOLUTION
Both can be true simultaneously: a $1 BILLION run rate is 90% BELOW A $10 BILLION INTERNAL FORECAST, so an impressive absolute number and a large miss against plan are compatible. But THIS CORPUS DOES NOT STATE THE FORECAST, so the reconciliation is a hypothesis, not a finding. The two numbers also measure different things — one is a FORWARD-LOOKING PACE ASSESSMENT BY AN OUTSIDE ANALYST against an unstated internal target; the other is a VENDOR-STATED ANNUALIZED RUN RATE, which annualizes a recent period rather than reporting booked revenue.

A READER CITING 'OpenAI's ad business in 2026' CAN TAKE EITHER 'dramatically underperforming' OR 'billion-dollar business in under 200 days' with roughly equal support. Both framings should carry their date, their source type, and the other figure.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN
- It does NOT establish that either report is wrong. All three are SINGLE-SOURCED press or vendor statements about a private company's non-public financials.
- The $1B figure is OPENAI'S OWN, is a RUN RATE not revenue recognised, and is not independently verified.
- The 90% figure is ONE ANALYST'S PACE ESTIMATE against a forecast this corpus does not record; nothing here confirms the forecast existed at the size implied.
- The India rollout is a distribution fact, not a revenue fact, and nothing here attributes any portion of the run rate to it.
- Nothing here establishes what happened after 31 August 2026.
