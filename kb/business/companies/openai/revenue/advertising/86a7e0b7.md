# OpenAI ChatGPT ad stack uses an LLM orchestrator to place ads; reached $1B annualized run rate

OpenAI published a deep dive on its ChatGPT ad stack (as of Nov 2026). A second LLM acts as an 'orchestrator' in an agentic loop: it reads the conversation, judges commercial intent, calls tools to source ads, filter eligible candidates, evaluate relevance, auction the placement and serve ads labeled 'sponsored'. OpenAI's stated priority rubric is user trust, then user value, then advertiser value, then revenue. Per The Deep View, ChatGPT Ads reached $1 billion annualized revenue run rate less than 200 days after launch with more than 100,000 advertisers, built by a small team relying heavily on Codex. Placement integrity (not showing ads in sensitive/intimate conversations) is described as a major decision. This does not mean ads are shown in every conversation.
