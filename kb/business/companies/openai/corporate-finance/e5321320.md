# OpenAI ruled out a 2026 IPO, citing AI safety concerns

On 2026-09-12 Sam Altman said OpenAI would not go public in 2026, arguing that current AI safety concerns made an IPO ill-advised. The company had previously filed confidentially and was reportedly considering a 2027 listing instead.
