# Apple–OpenAI dispute escalates with injunction motion and public rebuttal

In early August 2026 the legal dispute between Apple and OpenAI escalated. Apple filed a motion for a preliminary injunction seeking to bar OpenAI and two former Apple employees (including a former chief hardware engineer and Chang Liu, an eight-year Apple veteran) from accessing or using alleged confidential Apple information, plus a concurrent motion for expedited discovery. OpenAI responded publicly the next day, posting text messages and emails it presented as evidence rebutting Apple's accusations. OpenAI's first AI hardware device is rumored to resemble an Amazon Echo smart speaker.
