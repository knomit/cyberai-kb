# OpenAI research shows internal shift from chatbots to Codex agents

OpenAI published economic research ('how agents are transforming work', evidence from Codex) showing a rapid workplace shift from chatbots to agents. Inside OpenAI, every department now uses Codex as its primary AI tool, with the average worker generating about 85% of their output tokens in Codex rather than ChatGPT. OpenAI chief economist Ronnie Chatterji framed it as the first economic study of agents, with more papers on other parts of the AI stack to follow.
