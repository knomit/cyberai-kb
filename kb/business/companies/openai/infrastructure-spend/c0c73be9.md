# OpenAI's projected compute and infrastructure spend through 2030 rose from $600B to $856B while projected cash burn fell

OpenAI's projected compute and infrastructure spending through 2030 rose from $600 billion to $856 billion, a $256 billion increase, even as its projected cash burn over the same horizon fell. These two movements are only reconcilable through higher projected revenue or a shift in how the spend is financed (leases, partner-funded capacity, vendor financing), so the fact must never be compressed to 'OpenAI's costs went up' or 'OpenAI's burn improved' alone — both moved, in opposite directions, and the combination is the finding. This is a projection, not reported actuals.
