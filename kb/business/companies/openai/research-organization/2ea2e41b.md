# OpenAI launched an AI Futures / Strategic Futures team on preserving individual autonomy

OpenAI announced a team (published as "Introducing AI Futures", referred to in TLDR AI as the Strategic Futures team) to study how society can preserve individual autonomy as advanced AI reshapes economic and political power.
