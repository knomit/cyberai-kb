# OpenAI Grid Protectors Program: flexible data-center load; Georgia Power deal frees 1,000 MW

On 2 Nov 2026 OpenAI announced the Grid Protectors Program: building campuses that can reduce electricity use when the grid is under strain, working with utilities/regulators and with batteries/on-site power. For a planned Georgia campus it struck a deal with Georgia Power freeing 1,000 MW of flexible demand response (about 700,000 households' demand) and committed to pay infrastructure and electric-service costs in full. Axios reported OpenAI is rebranding data centers as 'AI compute campuses' ahead of a post-midterm PR push.
