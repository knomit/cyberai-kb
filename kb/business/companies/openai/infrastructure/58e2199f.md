# OpenAI expects $750B AI-infrastructure spend by 2030; plans 3.2 GW Georgia data center

Per Wall Street Journal reporting, OpenAI expects to spend about $750 billion on AI infrastructure by 2030, a 25% increase over earlier estimates. OpenAI also announced plans for a 3.2 gigawatt data center in Georgia costing an estimated $20-30 billion. Its earlier $500 billion Stargate project is reportedly stalled amid partner disputes.
