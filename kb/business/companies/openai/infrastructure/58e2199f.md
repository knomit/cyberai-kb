# OpenAI raised planned infrastructure spending through 2030 to $750 billion

OpenAI reportedly increased its planned infrastructure spending through 2030 to $750 billion. Its first major project under this plan was a $20 billion, 3.2-gigawatt data center campus in Georgia.
