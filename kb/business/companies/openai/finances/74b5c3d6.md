# OpenAI reportedly expects negative free cash flow of $278 billion through 2030

The Financial Times reported in September 2026 that OpenAI internally expects cumulative negative free cash flow of $278 billion by 2030. This is a projection attributed to sources rather than an audited or company-published figure. Read alongside separately reported compute-commitment figures, it indicates OpenAI is planning for a decade-scale cash-burn position financed by capital raises rather than operations. It does NOT state that OpenAI expects to lose $278 billion in net income, nor that the company expects to be unprofitable in every year through 2030 — free cash flow and profitability are distinct measures, and heavy capex can drive deeply negative FCF alongside improving operating margins.
