# OpenAI paused new $200/month Pro signups under Astra demand

TechCrunch reported on 2026-09-10 that OpenAI halted new subscriptions to its $200-per-month ChatGPT Pro plan while rolling GPT-6 Astra out to Pro, Plus, Enterprise and Business accounts. OpenAI framed Astra as a major step in reasoning, coding and computer use and characterized it as the beginning of the AGI era.

The operationally meaningful fact is the demand signal: a frontier lab turning away paying customers at the top tier indicates serving capacity, not willingness to pay, is the binding constraint.
