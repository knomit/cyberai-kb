# OpenAI parts ways with three safety researchers over information handling

Per the Wall Street Journal (reported by The Hacker News on 2026-10-03), OpenAI parted ways with three safety team members (Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, Mikita Balesni) for violating policies on accessing and handling sensitive company information; they reportedly shared confidential information with an undisclosed third-party AI-safety organization. All three had previously expressed concerns about the pace of AI development. Reported via sources; OpenAI's statement confirms the departures and policy violation only.
