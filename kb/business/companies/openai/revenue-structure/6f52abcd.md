# OpenAI's revenue is broadly distributed across enterprise customers, not concentrated

As of mid-2026, OpenAI's enterprise business exceeds 40% of revenue and is reportedly on track to reach parity with consumer revenue by end of 2026. The base is wide, not concentrated:

- 5,000+ enterprise customers, 4M+ enterprise seats crossed by Q1 2026, average contract value ~$800,000
- 1M+ business customers use OpenAI tools directly; 9M+ paying business users rely on ChatGPT for work
- No public disclosure names any OpenAI API customer exceeding 10% of revenue
- Usage spans ~200 organizations processing >1T tokens and 9,000+ organizations exceeding 10B tokens

IMPLICATION: 5,000 enterprise customers at ~$800k ACV implies roughly $4B enterprise revenue; with enterprise at >40% of the total, top-20 accounts representing >50% of TOTAL revenue is close to arithmetically impossible. Analyses predicting extreme revenue concentration at OpenAI are running against the available evidence. The concentration risk narrative may fit other AI vendors but does not currently fit OpenAI.

SOURCE QUALITY: these figures are largely company-stated or aggregator-compiled rather than audited. A public S-1 would be the first authoritative disclosure.
