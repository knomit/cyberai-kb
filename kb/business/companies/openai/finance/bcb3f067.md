# OpenAI annualized revenue approaching $50B, $20B below earlier reported figure

Per TechCrunch (2026-10-08), OpenAI told investors annualized revenue is approaching $50 billion, $20B less than a figure reported about a week earlier, which investors had devised to compare with Anthropic. Anthropic counts sales made by cloud partners; OpenAI does not, so the two calculate annualized revenue differently.
