# Kalshi and OpenAI partnered to surface prediction market data in ChatGPT search results

Prediction market Kalshi and OpenAI struck a partnership to use Kalshi's prediction market data in some soccer tournament search results within ChatGPT, reported 2026-07-13 — an example of OpenAI sourcing live third-party market data into its search surface.
