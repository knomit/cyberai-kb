# OpenAI and IBM strike enterprise consulting partnership

OpenAI announced a partnership embedding its technology into IBM's consulting arm. IBM joins OpenAI's 'elite partner tier' and is launching a dedicated OpenAI Practice with thousands of OpenAI-certified consultants and engineers trained via the OpenAI Partner Network. Three focus areas: modernizing legacy operations (finance, procurement, customer ops, HR) with frontier models including GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work; modernizing legacy applications/software development; and expanding IBM's role in 'Project Daybreak' by combining OpenAI's cyber capabilities with IBM Autonomous Security.
