# OpenAI is piloting outcome-based pricing with select enterprise accounts

OpenAI has started testing an arrangement with a limited number of select major accounts in which the customer pays only when the AI actually completes the job. Terms, customers and prices are undisclosed and OpenAI has not announced the test publicly. The driver is that token-based pricing makes cost accounting difficult for buyers, pushing the industry toward outcome-based pricing as an emerging standard.
