# OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Sol prices more than 20% in an explicitly three-month promotion

In late August 2026 OpenAI announced it would cut developer API prices for GPT-5.6 Sol, its highest-performing model, by more than 20%. The reduction was STATED AS LASTING THREE MONTHS — it is a promotion, not a permanent list-price change, and the temporary/permanent distinction is frequently dropped when this figure is cited.

Standard short-context pricing moved to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, down from $5 and $30. For comparison at the time, Anthropic's Fable 5 ran $10/M input and $50/M output, and Opus 5 ran $5/M input and $25/M output, making the discounted Sol cheaper than both.

A month earlier OpenAI had cut prices for its mid-tier GPT-5.6 Terra by 20% and its lightweight GPT-5.6 Luna by 80%. VC Tomasz Tunguz observed that Luna is now price-competitive with DeepSeek's latest model on intelligence-per-dollar, and benchmark platform Arena reported Sol's price decrease improved its standing on agentic coding and work benchmarks. The move fits a broader mid-2026 pattern of aggressive frontier-model price competition driven partly by open-weight alternatives.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN. A headline per-token cut does not predict what a user pays: GPT-5.6 Sol at its highest reasoning setting consumes roughly twice the tokens per session of GPT-5.5 in Codex workflows and adds a cache-write charge GPT-5.5 did not have. Sol received the smallest and the only explicitly temporary cut of the three tiers, and is the tier whose per-session consumption rose.
