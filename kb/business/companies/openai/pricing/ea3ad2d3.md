# OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Sol prices more than 20% in a three-month promotion

In late August 2026 OpenAI announced it would cut developer prices for GPT-5.6 Sol, its highest-performing model, by more than 20% for three months. Standard short-context pricing moved to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, down from $5 and $30. For comparison at the time, Anthropic's Fable 5 ran $10/M input and $50/M output, and Opus 5 ran $5/M input and $25/M output, making the discounted Sol cheaper than both. A month earlier OpenAI had cut prices for its mid-tier GPT-5.6 Terra by 20% and its lightweight GPT-5.6 Luna by 80%. VC Tomasz Tunguz observed that Luna is now price-competitive with DeepSeek's latest model on intelligence-per-dollar, and benchmark platform Arena reported Sol's price decrease improved its standing on agentic coding and work benchmarks.
