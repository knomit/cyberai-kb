# OpenAI's $110B round, and the PE deployment arm that accompanied it

OpenAI raised a historic $110B funding round: roughly $30B from SoftBank, $30B from Nvidia, and $50B from Amazon. The valuation exceeds the GDP of 168 countries. The raise was framed as funding to 'scale AI for everyone.' It was reported in March 2026 and again by The Deep View on 10 July 2026 — the same round, reported twice, not two raises.

SEPARATELY, THE PE DEPLOYMENT ARM: OpenAI was in talks with private equity firms (TPG as anchor, plus Bain, Brookfield, Advent) for a joint venture at a $10B pre-money valuation, with PE committing $4B in exchange for equity stakes, board seats, and early access to enterprise tooling. Fidji Simo (CEO of Applications, OpenAI) confirmed this, framing it as a 'deployment arm' focused on embedding OpenAI engineers within enterprise clients.

ENTERPRISE TRACTION CITED AT THE TIME: roughly 1M businesses on OpenAI products; API usage up 20% in the week after the GPT-5.4 launch; the Frontier platform (managing 'AI coworkers') facing more demand than supply.

PARALLEL MOVE: Anthropic was simultaneously in talks with Blackstone and other PE firms for a similar arrangement (per The Information). Both companies were racing to capture enterprise clients via PE portfolio companies.

EPISTEMIC STATUS: both source records were newsletter-reported with no independent sources recorded, and the PE joint venture was in talks rather than closed at time of reporting.
