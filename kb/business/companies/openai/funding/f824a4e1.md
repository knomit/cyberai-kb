# OpenAI reported to raise $110B round

The Deep View (Jul 10, 2026) reported OpenAI raised a historic $110 billion funding round, with roughly $30B from SoftBank, $30B from Nvidia, and $50B from Amazon, framed as funding to 'scale AI for everyone.'
