# Every OpenAI financial quantity in this corpus is a single-sourced point estimate against a baseline the corpus does not hold — so a figure quoted without its date, source type and baseline supports opposite conclusions

MECHANISM: a private company's financial quantities reach a corpus only as point estimates, each from one source, each framed against a baseline the corpus does not itself record — a prior estimate, an internal forecast, an annualization window — so the meaning of the number lives in the date, the source type and the baseline, none of which travel with the number when it is quoted.

The corpus records two distinct failures of this kind for the same company, and they are DIFFERENT failures, not one:

- FIGURES IN THE SAME SERIES MOVE, so a value without its date is a point on a trajectory misread as a level. A forward capital-commitment figure was revised upward more than once inside a single year, each revision framed as an increase over the prior estimate rather than as a new plan. Cite such a figure with its date or not at all, and treat the series slope rather than the level as the informative quantity.
- FIGURES ABOUT THE SAME SUBJECT CONFLICT IN DIRECTION, and the conflict is recorded rather than resolved. A forward-looking pace assessment by an outside analyst, measured against an internal forecast the corpus does not state, and a vendor-stated annualized run rate, can both be true simultaneously while supporting opposite impressions; a reader can take either 'dramatically underperforming' or 'billion-dollar business' with roughly equal support. Both framings should carry their date, their source type, and the other figure.

The source-type distinction is load-bearing across both: a VENDOR-STATED RUN RATE annualizes a recent period rather than reporting booked revenue; an OUTSIDE ANALYST'S PACE ESTIMATE is measured against a target the corpus may not hold; a PROJECTION is not reported actuals. These are three different kinds of claim and none of them is audited revenue.

WHAT FOLLOWS: when recording or citing a financial quantity for a private company, carry the date, the source type, and the baseline it is measured against as part of the claim, and where two figures conflict, record the conflict rather than silently preferring one.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this does not establish that any particular report is wrong — all of these are single-sourced press or vendor statements about a private company's non-public financials, and a rising series is not evidence the spending will be incurred, nor that any trend continues. It does not claim the figures are irreconcilable in fact; in the conflicting case a reconciliation is available but is a HYPOTHESIS rather than a finding, because the corpus does not state the baseline that would settle it. Nothing here establishes what happened after the dates its members record. Provenance — the specific figures, outlets, dates, projects and product lines — stays in the member facts cited below.
