# OpenAI Startup Fund has offered portfolio companies early model access since 2021 — a standing perk, not a post-IPO development

The OpenAI Startup Fund, founded 2021, is OpenAI's venture arm. Portfolio companies receive capital, EARLY ACCESS TO FUTURE OPENAI SYSTEMS, strategic support from the fund team, and Azure credits. As of November 2025 the fund had made 44 total investments across its main fund and multiple SPVs, with 4 unicorns (Ambience, Speak, Harvey) and 2 acquisitions.

IMPORTANT QUALIFIER: the fund reportedly does NOT require portfolio companies to build on OpenAI's APIs, supporting 'any company pushing the boundaries of applied AI.' Early model access is therefore a conventional corporate-VC perk rather than an exclusivity or channel-capture clause.

DATING — load-bearing: preferential model access for portfolio companies has been a standing feature since 2021. Any analysis treating it as a NEW signal of post-IPO commercial capture is citing a five-year-old arrangement as evidence of a recent shift. The count (44 investments) has also exceeded 20 for some time.
