# OpenAI employee arrested for planting hidden recorder in executive conference room

A Saudi national and OpenAI employee (named Abdulrahman Alfozan per TLDR) was arrested after OpenAI found a hidden recording device in an executive conference room at its San Francisco offices; OpenAI alerted the FBI. Prosecutors allege covert interception of conversations. OpenAI said it found no evidence model weights, source code or customer data were accessed. Allegation stage.
