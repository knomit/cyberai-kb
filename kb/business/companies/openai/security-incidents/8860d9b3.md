# Alfozan pleads not guilty to intercepting oral communications at OpenAI; next hearing Nov 17, 2026

Former OpenAI engineer Abdulrahman Alfozan, 35, pleaded not guilty in San Francisco federal court to a single count of interception of oral communications for allegedly planting a recording device in an OpenAI executive conference room. Held without bail since arrest on Oct 9, 2026; next hearing Nov 17, 2026; maximum penalty five years and $250,000 fine.
