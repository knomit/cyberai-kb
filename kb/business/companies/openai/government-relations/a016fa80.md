# OpenAI proposes 5% equity stake to U.S. government via sovereign wealth vehicle

OpenAI proposed giving the Trump administration a 5% stake to ease political pressure in Washington, as part of a larger arrangement under which the U.S. government would hold 5% of each leading U.S. AI developer through a sovereign wealth fund vehicle. Reported by CNBC via TLDR AI, July 2, 2026.
