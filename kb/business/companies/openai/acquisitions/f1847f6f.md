# OpenAI acquired camera startup Glass Imaging at a valuation over $300 million

OpenAI quietly acquired Glass Imaging, a startup developing smartphone camera technology that uses AI to reach image quality comparable to a DSLR, in a deal valuing Glass Imaging at over $300 million, per Wall Street Journal reporting. Glass Imaging was founded in 2019 by two former Apple employees who previously worked on camera technology there. OpenAI's plans for the company were not disclosed, but OpenAI has been developing a secretive hardware device with Jony Ive, the former Apple design chief behind the iPhone — the camera-hardware connection is inference, not a stated plan.
