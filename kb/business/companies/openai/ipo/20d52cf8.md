# OpenAI confidentially filed a draft S-1 in May 2026 but is leaning toward a 2027 IPO

OpenAI confidentially submitted a draft S-1 registration statement to the SEC (sent in late May, reportedly May 22, 2026; publicly confirmed by OpenAI June 8–9, 2026). Targeted valuation reported between $852B and $1 trillion. Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan are leading. A confidential filing means the public prospectus would not appear until roughly 60–90 days after submission — earliest late July or August 2026.

TIMING REVERSED WITHIN WEEKS: on June 25, 2026, NYT (reported by Bloomberg) and Reuters reported OpenAI is leaning toward postponing until 2027. CFO Sarah Friar has told some associates the company is eyeing a 2027 listing. Advisers cautioned that a listing may not draw sufficient enthusiasm given the volatile public tech market, reportedly informed by SpaceX's rocky debut. Offered a choice between waiting until 2027 for a $1T debut or accepting a lower valuation sooner, Altman called any cut to the trillion-dollar figure a 'nonstarter'. OpenAI's own statement: it has not decided timing; there are things it wants to do that are easier as a private company; the filing preserves the OPTION to go public sooner.

EPISTEMIC NOTE: a confidential S-1 filing is an option, not a commitment. Reading it as evidence of imminent listing was contradicted 15–17 days later by the delay reporting. Filing and listing should be tracked as separate events.
