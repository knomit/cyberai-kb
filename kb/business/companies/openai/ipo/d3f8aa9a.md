# OpenAI and Anthropic Both Confidentially File for IPO in 2026

Both OpenAI and Anthropic confidentially filed for IPOs as of early June 2026. This creates a structural tension: both companies have publicly committed to safety-first principles (OpenAI calling for decentralized AI power, Anthropic urging slower development toward autonomous systems), but IPO status subjects them to shareholder-value pressures prioritizing growth and profit margins. The Deep View editorial explicitly flags this as a watch item — whether actions match stated safety commitments post-IPO filing.
