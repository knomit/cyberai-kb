# OpenAI leadership transition: Fidji Simo steps back, Greg Brockman takes over key projects (2026)

OpenAI's Fidji Simo planned to step down from her full-time executive role after an extended medical leave (reported by TLDR AI citing The Wall Street Journal). Co-founder Greg Brockman subsequently took oversight of key OpenAI projects, tasked with driving revenue amid intense competition and ahead of a prospective IPO.
