# OpenAI CRO Denise Dresser departs ahead of IPO

In August 2026, OpenAI Chief Revenue Officer Denise Dresser left the company just ahead of its anticipated IPO — the second major executive departure that week. Dresser, who joined from Salesforce less than a year earlier, is being replaced by Dali Rajic from Wiz. Executive Brad Lightcap also announced his departure to start a new venture.
