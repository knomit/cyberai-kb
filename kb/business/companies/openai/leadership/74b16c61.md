# Brad Lightcap departing as OpenAI COO amid pre-IPO exec shakeup

Brad Lightcap, OpenAI's longtime Chief Operating Officer, is leaving to start a new venture, hinting it will advance OpenAI's mission from a different vantage point. His departure is part of a wider top-executive shakeup at OpenAI ahead of a planned IPO; Fidji Simo, Bill Peebles, and Kevin Weil have also recently left the company.
