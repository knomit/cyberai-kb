# OpenAI shut down its Sora video platform to free up compute

Amid an industry-wide compute crunch, OpenAI axed its AI video platform Sora to free up compute for its flagship models, according to The Deep View, July 3, 2026. The piece framed world models as far more compute- and data-intensive than language-only models.
