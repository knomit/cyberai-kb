# OpenAI: ChatGPT handles about one fifth of Google's query volume; 1.2B users

Per OpenAI's ChatGPT consumer product lead Adam Fry (Nov 2026): ChatGPT handles roughly 20% of Google's query volume (Google ~90% of global search, Bing ~5%); users grew from 1B to 1.2B in two months; voice mode has 250M weekly actives (about 20% of users rely on it); image generation produces 1.5B images/week; OpenAI plans to merge the Chat and Work tabs. Figures are OpenAI-internal, not independently verified.
