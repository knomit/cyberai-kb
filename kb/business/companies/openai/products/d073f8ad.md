# OpenAI paused new ChatGPT Pro subscriptions during Astra rollout

OpenAI paused new subscriptions to its $200 per month ChatGPT Pro plan as the GPT-6 Astra model rolled out to Pro, Plus, Enterprise and Business accounts, indicating capacity rather than demand was the binding constraint.
