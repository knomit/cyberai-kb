# Tuesday Labs unveils Voxel Series-1 home robot

Per The Deep View (2026-10-28): Tuesday Labs (YC and FCVC-backed, founded by ex-Boston Dynamics roboticist Ros Shinkle) introduced Voxel Series-1, a 'droid butler' with 7-DOF arms and 1-DOF grippers. Reservations cost $200; estimated final price $20,000; shipping not before 2028. The live demo was puppeteered.
