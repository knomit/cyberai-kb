# Anthropic S-1 enterprise metrics: 11,900 customers over $100K, NRR 900%

As of Sept 30, 2026 Anthropic reported 11,900 customers with over $100,000 trailing-twelve-month revenue, ~1,900 over $1M and ~250 over $10M; net revenue retention of 900% (customers spend about nine times what they did a year earlier). About 25% of revenue comes from its five largest customers (concentration risk). The filing also has a risk section on 'catastrophic or existential risks to humanity'. Source: S-1 as summarized by The Deep View.
