# Anthropic's mid-2026 posture: massive capital, tighter data policy, outward partnerships

By June 2026 Anthropic combined aggressive capitalization with strategic positioning: it raised a $65B Series H at a ~$965B valuation while preparing for an IPO, shipped its most capable models (Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5), shifted from Zero Data Retention to a 30-day retention policy for high-capability models, and pursued external collaborations such as a reported joint model-distillation effort with Alibaba. Together these suggest a company balancing commercial scale-up and public-company optics with continued model and partnership expansion. Most underlying items are single-source newsletter reporting.
