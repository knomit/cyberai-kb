# Anthropic IPO prospectus: $42B 2025 net loss, $518B infrastructure commitments

Per reporting on Anthropic's IPO prospectus (2026-09-28): Anthropic targets a roughly $2 trillion valuation, reported a $42 billion net loss in 2025, and plans $518 billion of cloud/compute/infrastructure obligations in the coming year. Nearly a quarter of last year's revenue came from two customers, and many large clients lack long-term contracts.
