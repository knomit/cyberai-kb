# Anthropic IPO could exceed $2 trillion valuation

As of August 2026, investors were reported to anticipate Anthropic's IPO could exceed a $2 trillion valuation, potentially the largest in history. The Financial Times reported investors were in talks around a $2 trillion valuation.

**QUALIFIER ADDED 2026-08-25 — the revenue figure below is a self-reported projection, not a result.** This fact previously stated flatly that "the company projects annualized revenue of $100-120 billion by the end of 2026." Read as written, that lands as a corpus-endorsed number. It is not one. It is ANTHROPIC'S OWN forward-looking projection, circulated while the company was raising at, and being valued on, that trajectory — the most interested possible source for the most load-bearing possible number.

CHECK IT AGAINST THIS CORPUS BEFORE USING IT. `kb/business/companies/anthropic/financials/revenue-run-rate-trajectory.md` records the measured path: $9B annualized (end 2025), $47B (mid 2026), $65B+ (August 2026). Reaching $100–120B by end of 2026 requires roughly a doubling in the final four months of the year, on top of a base that itself grew ~7x in eight months. The projection is not absurd against that growth rate, but it is an extrapolation of an extraordinary curve, published by the party it benefits, and nothing in this corpus independently corroborates it.

Both figures here — the $2T valuation and the $100–120B projection — are anticipations reported ahead of an IPO that had not occurred. A later record showing the actual outcome should supersede this fact, not sit beside it.
