# Anthropic S-1 analysis: first profitable quarter, risk in locked-in compute commitments

A third-party analysis of Anthropic's S-1 (Tilo Mitra, via TLDR AI) says Anthropic is growing very fast and had its first profitable quarter; the main risk is years of locked-in compute bills against customers who can cut back any month, plus regulatory issues, copyright suits and heavy reliance on large cloud companies.
