# Anthropic launches Mythos-class models Fable 5 and Mythos 5

As reported by The Deep View (June 10, 2026 edition), Anthropic launched two 'Mythos-class' models on Tuesday: Fable 5, claimed safe for general use, and Mythos 5, which has some safeguards lifted. Both are claimed state-of-the-art on nearly all tested benchmarks and able to work autonomously for longer periods. Fable 5 routes queries on certain sensitive topics to Claude Opus 4.8 (released end of May 2026). Mythos 5 has no such safeguards, is available only to a small group of cyberdefenders and infrastructure providers via Project Glasswing, and is claimed to have the strongest cybersecurity capabilities of any model. Single-source newsletter reporting, unverified.
