# Anthropic releases Claude Opus 5 targeting cost efficiency

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, described as a 'proactive' model approaching Claude Fable 5's frontier intelligence at lower cost (about half the cost of Fable per The Deep View). Opus 5 hit state-of-the-art on coding and knowledge-work evaluations but lags Mythos 5 on cybersecurity. Pricing remained $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens. Anthropic said Opus 5 is its most aligned model, with the lowest rates of deception. Early testers cited include Cognition, Cursor, Lovable, Zapier, and Box.
