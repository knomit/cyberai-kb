# Anthropic's Fable 5 access was rationed and repeatedly extended through July 2026 — and the corpus's two records give the recurring '50%' figure two incompatible meanings

Merged from two records of the same rationing episode, four weeks apart. The sequence is compatible; the '50%' figure is NOT, and that ambiguity is preserved here rather than resolved.

THE SEQUENCE.
- 2 JULY 2026 (TLDR AI): Anthropic REDEPLOYED Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5. Fable 5 was to be 'included for up to 50% of weekly usage limits' until 7 July 2026, after which it would be available via USAGE CREDITS. Mythos 5 was restored for SOME U.S. ORGANIZATIONS, with Anthropic continuing to coordinate with the U.S. government to expand Mythos access to the broader Glasswing program.
- THROUGH 19 JULY 2026: Anthropic EXTENDED access to Fable 5 and RAISED Claude Code usage limits, amid uncertainty about the model's long-term availability.

So the shape is: restricted redeployment with a hard stop, then an extension past it. Both records describe rationing of a model Anthropic could not supply freely.

THE UNRESOLVED '50%'. Three records use the number and at least two of them cannot both be right:
- This record's 2 July source: Fable 5 'included for up to 50% OF weekly usage limits' — i.e. a CAP on how much of a user's allowance Fable may consume.
- kb/business/companies/ai-labs/pricing-competition/a6b06fc8.md: Anthropic 'kept Claude Code weekly rate limits 50% HIGHER' — i.e. an INCREASE in the allowance itself.
A cap on Fable's share and a 50% increase in the overall limit are opposite in direction and effect. A reader taking either as 'Anthropic's 50% Claude Code change' gets a different picture of whether access was loosened or constrained. Neither source is strong: the 2 July item carries confidence 0.6 with zero recorded sources; a6b06fc8 carries 0.7 with zero recorded sources. The 19 July extension record is the best-sourced of the three at 2 sources.

WHY THE RATIONING HAPPENED, from elsewhere in the corpus and not established by these two records: kb/business/companies/anthropic/infrastructure/3b9c4553.md states compute constraints forced Anthropic to taper Fable 5 access to Max and Team Premium only at 50% of standard weekly limits — a THIRD use of the same figure, again with a different scope (which plans, not which share).

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN:
- It does NOT establish why access was constrained. Compute scarcity is recorded elsewhere as the reason but is not stated in either merged source.
- It does NOT mean Mythos 5 became generally available. Restoration was to SOME U.S. organizations only, under a government-coordinated program.
- 'Available via usage credits' after 7 July is a change in billing mechanism, not a removal — and no record here states what happened after 19 July.
- The dates are as-reported by newsletters; neither merged record cites primary Anthropic communications.
