# Anthropic enterprise demand softening ahead of reported IPO (Oct 2026)

Per press reports cited by The Deep View (Oct 7 2026): the Pentagon has ceased using Anthropic products after the supply-chain-risk designation; The Information reports Microsoft cut its expected >$1B 2026 internal Anthropic spend by over a third and Meta halved Claude Code seats from ~60,000 to ~30,000. Bloomberg reports Anthropic targets an IPO possibly the week of Nov 9 2026; Reuters (prospectus) reports a $42B 2025 loss and planned $518B compute spend. These are press reports, not confirmed by Anthropic.
