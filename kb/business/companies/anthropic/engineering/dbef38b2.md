# Anthropic says 65% of its code is created by an internal version of Claude

In its Claude Tag announcement, Anthropic stated that 65% of its code is created by an internal version of the product (an evolution of Claude Code), and that tagging Claude is now used internally well beyond engineering — for chasing product metrics and data, working support tickets, and root-causing bugs.
