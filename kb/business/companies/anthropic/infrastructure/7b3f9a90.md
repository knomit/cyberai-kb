# Anthropic signs reported $10B cloud deal with Volta

Anthropic reportedly agreed to buy six years of cloud capacity from AI infrastructure startup Volta in a deal reported at around $10 billion. The plan centers on a 133-megawatt data center in Norway, developed with Bitdeer and powered by NVIDIA Vera Rubin systems.
