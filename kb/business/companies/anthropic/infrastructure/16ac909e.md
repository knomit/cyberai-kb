# Anthropic signed a ~$45 billion cloud compute deal with Nscale

Anthropic agreed to a roughly $45 billion cloud deal with Nscale, a UK-based AI infrastructure company, renting approximately 460 megawatts of compute capacity at an Nscale data center development in West Virginia. The facility is expected to come online at the end of 2027 and will use NVIDIA's Vera Rubin chips.
