# Anthropic to invest $50B with Fluidstack on US data centers

Anthropic announced a $50 billion investment with Fluidstack to build data center facilities in Texas and New York, marking a shift toward owning infrastructure. Anthropic has otherwise largely leased compute from SpaceX, Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta, and AMD, and has committed far less to owned infrastructure than rival OpenAI. Compute constraints have forced Anthropic to taper access to its flagship Fable 5 model (Max and Team Premium only, 50% of standard weekly limits).
