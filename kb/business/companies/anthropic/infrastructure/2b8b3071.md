# Anthropic's 2026 compute buildout is spread across at least four separately-contracted non-hyperscaler providers, and most of the capacity was contracted years before it comes online — these are commitments, not delivered compute

This corpus records Anthropic's 2026 compute procurement as a series of SEPARATE deals with SEPARATE providers, each filed as its own record with no cross-reference. Assembled, the shape is distribution across many mid-tier and 'neocloud' suppliers rather than concentration in one hyperscaler — and, critically, most of the named capacity had NOT COME ONLINE at the time of reporting.

## THE CONTRACTS, WITH THEIR SOURCE STRENGTH STATED

- NSCALE — roughly $45 BILLION, renting approximately 460 MEGAWATTS at an Nscale data centre development in WEST VIRGINIA, using NVIDIA's Vera Rubin chips. The facility is EXPECTED TO COME ONLINE AT THE END OF 2027. Nscale is a UK-based AI infrastructure company. (Source record confidence 0.8, 1 source.)
- VOLTA — REPORTEDLY around $10 BILLION for SIX YEARS of cloud capacity, centred on a 133-MEGAWATT data centre in NORWAY, developed with Bitdeer and powered by NVIDIA Vera Rubin systems. (Confidence 0.75, 1 source; 'reportedly' is the source's own framing.)
- TERAWULF — a REPORTED $19 BILLION infrastructure LEASE; initial capacity expected to come online in the SECOND HALF OF THE FOLLOWING YEAR. TeraWulf's shares surged on the report. (Confidence 0.65, ZERO recorded sources — the weakest figure here.)
- AKAMAI — REPORTEDLY a 7-YEAR, $1.8 BILLION cloud deal.
- MICRON — a strategic partnership to scale AI infrastructure (no figure recorded).

PROVENANCE WARNING ON THE LAST TWO: the Akamai and Micron items appear only as single lines inside newsletter LINK-DIGEST records carrying confidence 0.5. Per kb/meta/reasoning/3c229162.md, digest records should be treated as POINTERS, NOT EVIDENCE, and never cited for a load-bearing claim. They are listed here to show the breadth of counterparties, not to support any figure.

## WHAT THE PATTERN ACTUALLY IS

At least four distinct counterparties, on four different commercial structures (build-out rental, multi-year capacity purchase, lease, partnership), across at least three geographies (West Virginia, Norway, and unstated for TeraWulf/Akamai). Two of the four name NVIDIA VERA RUBIN as the silicon generation, which places both deliveries in a future hardware cycle rather than current inventory.

## WHY THE DELIVERY DATES ARE THE LOAD-BEARING DETAIL

kb/meta/reasoning/bae0b862.md establishes that a claim about realized position cannot be settled by observing that deals FORMED — formation and delivery are different variables, and the second was in that case settled by observing the first. That applies directly here. The Nscale facility is expected online at the END OF 2027; TeraWulf's initial capacity in the SECOND HALF OF THE FOLLOWING YEAR. So the dollar totals describe CONTRACTED FUTURE CAPACITY, and cannot be read as compute Anthropic had available in 2026.

The corpus supplies the contemporaneous counter-evidence directly. kb/business/companies/anthropic/infrastructure/3b9c4553.md (outside this cluster) records that Anthropic has LARGELY LEASED compute from SpaceX, Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta and AMD and has COMMITTED FAR LESS TO OWNED INFRASTRUCTURE THAN RIVAL OPENAI — and that COMPUTE CONSTRAINTS FORCED ANTHROPIC TO TAPER ACCESS to its flagship Fable 5 model (Max and Team Premium only, 50% of standard weekly limits). A company signing tens of billions in forward capacity while rationing its flagship model is exactly the situation in which contracted and available compute must not be conflated.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN — load-bearing

- DO NOT SUM THESE FIGURES. They come from records of sharply differing reliability (0.8, 0.75, 0.65, and two digest lines at 0.5), several are explicitly 'reported' or 'reportedly' rather than confirmed closings, and this corpus separately records a further ~$50 BILLION Anthropic/Fluidstack commitment for Texas and New York that is not in this cluster. A total assembled across these would be a number no source supports.
- IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH A STRATEGY. Four deals with four counterparties is consistent with deliberate supplier diversification, with opportunistic capacity buying, or with a constrained buyer taking whatever is available. No source states Anthropic's reasoning, and this record does not infer it.
- IT DOES NOT CLAIM ANTHROPIC IS MOVING AWAY FROM HYPERSCALERS. 3b9c4553 records the hyperscaler leases as continuing, and separate corpus records state Anthropic's own position that chips from Google, Amazon and Nvidia REMAIN CENTRAL to its hardware strategy. These neocloud contracts are recorded as ADDITIVE, not substitutive.
- IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH THAT ANY DEAL CLOSED, that any facility was built, or that any capacity was delivered. The TeraWulf record in particular reports a share-price reaction to a report — a market response, not a confirmation.
- INDIVIDUAL SCOPE CONDITIONS MUST SURVIVE CITATION: the megawatt figures, the six-year and seven-year terms, the Bitdeer co-development, the Vera Rubin dependency, and the specific online dates. See the source facts.
