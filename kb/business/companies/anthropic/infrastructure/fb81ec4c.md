# Anthropic signed its first Australian data centre agreement, for 2.16 GW

Reuters reported on 16 September 2026 that Anthropic signed a data centre lease agreement in Australia for 2.16 GW — its first Australian data centre agreement. Both components matter: the scale (2.16 GW) and the fact that it is a first-in-country commitment, marking geographic expansion of compute capacity rather than an incremental addition to an existing Australian footprint.
