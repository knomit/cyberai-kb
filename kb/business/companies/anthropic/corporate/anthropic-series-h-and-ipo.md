# Anthropic raised $65B at a $965B valuation (Series H) and confidentially filed for a US IPO — the two records of that week disagree on which model shipped alongside it

Merged from two records of the same event. Both state the funding identically; they DISAGREE on the product launch they place in the same week, and that disagreement is preserved here rather than resolved.

THE FUNDING, AGREED BY BOTH. Anthropic raised $65 BILLION at a $965 BILLION valuation, recorded as its SERIES H, while preparing for a large IPO.

THE IPO. Anthropic CONFIDENTIALLY FILED to go public with the SEC. The filing is confidential and TERMS HAVE NOT BEEN DISCLOSED.

ALSO ANNOUNCED IN THAT PERIOD. Anthropic said it would expand Mythos-class model access to 15 MORE COUNTRIES and 150 MORE ORGANIZATIONS, and is giving the EU CYBERSECURITY BODY access to Mythos.

UNRESOLVED CONFLICT — WHICH MODEL SHIPPED THAT WEEK.
- kb/business/companies/anthropic/corporate/332f32ed.md (confidence 0.9, 2 sources) states: 'Earlier in the same week, Anthropic launched CLAUDE OPUS 4.8 and raised $65B at a $965B valuation (Series H).'
- kb/business/companies/anthropic/funding/6d775d6f.md (confidence 0.7, 0 recorded sources, The Deep View) states the policy proposals 'came on the heels of releasing CLAUDE FABLE 5 AND MYTHOS 5, described as its most powerful models to date.'
These name different models for the same week. The better-sourced record says Opus 4.8; that is NOT sufficient to discard the other, because the two may be describing different events days apart, or one may have mis-dated a launch. A reader who needs the model-launch date must go to primary reporting. Note that a third record, kb/business/companies/anthropic/c44c32b3.md, independently associates the $65B raise with Fable 5 and Mythos 5, which counts in favour of that pairing but shares the Deep View provenance rather than corroborating it independently.

FRAMING CARRIED FROM THE WEAKER SOURCE. The Deep View characterized Anthropic's accompanying policy and economic commitments as PARTLY PUBLIC RELATIONS AND DAMAGE CONTROL, given its for-profit, soon-to-be-public status. That is an editorial judgement by a newsletter, not an established fact, and is recorded here as such.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN:
- A confidential S-1 filing is NOT a public offering, a priced deal, or a commitment to a timetable. Nothing here establishes when or whether Anthropic lists.
- The $965B valuation is the SERIES H post-money figure, and must not be conflated with the ~$2 trillion IPO valuation discussed by investors in August 2026 (kb/business/companies/anthropic/finance/9680bcce.md) or with the $1 trillion SECONDARY-MARKET price recorded for late April 2026 (kb/technology/ai/industry/funding/3cfbc794.md). Three different valuation types, three different dates.
- Expanded Mythos access to countries and organizations is an access-policy announcement, not a deployment or revenue figure.
