# Anthropic committed $350M to AI economic-impact programs

Alongside its June 2026 policy proposals, Anthropic committed $350 million across two programs: $150 million to a national fellowship for early-career people to 'extend the benefits of AI to communities across America,' and $200 million to its Economic Futures Research Fund.
