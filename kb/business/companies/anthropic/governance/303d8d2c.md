# Anthropic to allocate initial 5% of equity to a Public Benefit Fund

Anthropic announced (reported in TLDR AI, 2026-10-19) plans to allocate an initial 5% of its equity to a new Public Benefit Fund, with additional contributions tied to valuation growth. The fund is meant to support initiatives such as addressing AI's economic and labor impacts. The 5% is an initial allocation, not a cap.
