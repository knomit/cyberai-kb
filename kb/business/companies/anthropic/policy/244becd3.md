# Anthropic loosened its Responsible Scaling Policy

Per a TIME interview with chief science officer Jared Kaplan (cited by The Deep View, Jul 2026), Anthropic revised its Responsible Scaling Policy, dropping some unilateral commitments to hold back models absent guaranteed risk mitigations, with Kaplan reasoning that unilateral restraint 'wouldn't actually help anyone' if competitors race ahead.
