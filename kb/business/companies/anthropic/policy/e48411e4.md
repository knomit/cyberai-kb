# Anthropic plans to let enterprises hold required 30-day data retention on their own cloud

Reuters reported (2026-08-20, sourced to a person familiar) that Anthropic planned to change its enterprise data retention policy so customers can keep the required 30-day retained data on their own cloud infrastructure rather than Anthropic's.
