# Anthropic shifts from Zero Data Retention to 30-day retention for high-capability models

Per The Deep View (June 10, 2026), Anthropic announced that for Fable, Mythos and all future releases of similar or higher capability it will require 30-day data retention for all usage across first- and third-party services — a shift from its previous Zero Data Retention policy. Anthropic says it will not train models on this data, using it only to defend against 'complex and novel attacks.' Single-source newsletter reporting.
