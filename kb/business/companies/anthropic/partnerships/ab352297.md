# Salesforce and Anthropic launched 'Claudeforce' expanded partnership

On August 26, 2026, Salesforce and Anthropic announced 'Claudeforce,' an expanded strategic partnership. It launches with 'Salesforce in Claude,' a plugin containing 37 pre-built sales skills built jointly by both companies, letting Claude reason over live Salesforce revenue context and automate pipeline updates. Admins connect Salesforce to Claude once, with authentication and permissions managed centrally, rather than per-user. It is available to select pilot customers with open beta in September 2026. Claude also serves inside Salesforce as a reasoning model for the Agentforce Atlas Reasoning Engine and powers Agentforce Vibes and Agentforce Coworker by default, and is accessible within the Salesforce Trust Boundary via Amazon Bedrock. Claude becomes the default model for Slack, powering Slackbot and the @Claude tag, and is a founding partner for Slack Code. The agreement is reciprocal, with each company becoming a customer of the other.
