# Anthropic raised $65B at a $965B valuation around its IPO

Per The Deep View, Anthropic raised $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation (Series H) and is preparing for a large IPO. The policy proposals came on the heels of releasing Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5, described as its most powerful models to date. The newsletter framed the policy/economic commitments as partly public relations and damage control given Anthropic's for-profit, soon-to-be-public status.
