# Anthropic signed up to $517B / 14.8GW of compute contracts in 11 months

Reporting on 2026-09-07 put Anthropic's compute-capacity contracts signed since October 2025 at up to $517 billion covering 14.8 gigawatts across more than a dozen providers — an escalation from a previously projected ~$180 billion in server leasing through 2029. Amazon and Google account for the largest share, supplying roughly 11 GW valued above $300 billion. Other reported deals: Microsoft Azure ~$30 billion for 1 GW, SpaceX ~$45 billion, Lambda ~$35 billion, and nScale ~$45 billion (including ~460 MW from nScale's West Virginia campus on a six-year term). Anthropic filed confidentially for an IPO with the SEC in June 2026.

Important caveat: $517 billion is NOT an Anthropic-disclosed budget and is not cash spent or an unconditional present liability. It is an upper-bound estimate aggregated by journalists from cloud commitments, chip capacity, data-center leases and long-duration infrastructure agreements, most of which phase in over many years. The TLDR blurb attributes deals to Akamai and Fluidstack; broader reporting names Microsoft, SpaceX and Lambda instead, so the counterparty list should be treated as unsettled.
