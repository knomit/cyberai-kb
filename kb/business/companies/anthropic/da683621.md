# Anthropic's June 2026 Mythos-class launch bundled new models, pricing, and a data-retention shift

Per The Deep View (June 10, 2026), Anthropic's launch of its Mythos-class models was a coordinated event: it released Fable 5 (safe for general use, routing sensitive queries to Claude Opus 4.8) and Mythos 5 (some safeguards lifted, restricted to cyberdefenders via Project Glasswing); priced both at $10/M input and $50/M output tokens (half the earlier Mythos preview), with Fable 5 free on Claude Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise until June 22, 2026; and simultaneously shifted from Zero Data Retention to 30-day retention for these and future high-capability models. This coincided with a $65B Series H at a ~$965B valuation ahead of an IPO. All single-source newsletter reporting, unverified.
