# Anthropic reports Claude leads 26% of its own AI research work with tens of thousands of internal agents

Anthropic published measurements stating that Claude now leads 26% of the company's AI research work and that Anthropic oversees tens of thousands of active internal agents. The accompanying framework tracks three things: how much AI contributes to building the next generation of models, whether humans can still meaningfully oversee those agents, and the compute driving the work. Operational consequence: this is a self-reported internal metric from the model vendor, published as part of a measurement framework rather than independently audited — treat the 26% figure as Anthropic's own operational definition of 'leads research work,' not a verified external benchmark. The oversight-capacity question is the load-bearing part of the framework, not the percentage.
