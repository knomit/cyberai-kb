# Anthropic hired ex-Google TPU chief Amir Salek for an in-house chip effort

Bloomberg reported on 2026-08-21 that Anthropic hired Amir Salek, a Google chip veteran who led Google's TPU work, as part of a push into in-house hardware. This is part of a broader pattern of frontier labs vertically integrating into custom silicon.
