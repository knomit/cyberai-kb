# Anthropic amended S-1 (Nov 2026): ~$895B compute commitments, Q3 revenue $17B, Q3 net loss $23.3B

Per The Deep View (3 Nov 2026) citing Anthropic's amended SEC S-1/A filed Monday 2 Nov 2026: Anthropic expects to spend more than $895B in coming years, mostly on infrastructure, with about $448B of commitments incurred before end of 2030. Quarterly revenue as of 30 Sep 2026 was $17B vs $1.4B a year earlier (~12x); Q3 net loss was $23.3B vs $12.1B in Q3 2025, driven largely by compute costs. The filing warns revenue is mostly consumption-based and not under long-term contract while compute commitments run multi-year, so obligations may rest on revenue expectations that are not realized. TLDR (citing CNBC) reports nine-month 2026 revenue of $33.24B (+1,200%) and a nine-month net loss of $104B, commitments ~ $895.4B. Does NOT mean Anthropic is unprofitable-forever; the filing says it 'may not achieve or sustain profitability'.
