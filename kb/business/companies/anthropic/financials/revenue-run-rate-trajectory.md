# Anthropic annualized revenue run rate trajectory: $9B (end 2025) to $47B (mid 2026) to $65B+ (Aug 2026)

Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate rose from approximately $9 billion at the end of 2025 to $47 billion by mid-2026, as cited by The Deep View referencing New York Times reporting. In August 2026 Anthropic was reported via Bloomberg to be on track to exceed $65 billion in annualized revenue based on then-current performance, described as more than seven times its pace at the end of the previous year — a figure consistent with the ~$9B end-2025 baseline. All three figures are annualized run-rate estimates reported by press rather than audited financial statements, and the Aug 2026 figure is a forward-looking projection of current performance rather than realized revenue.
