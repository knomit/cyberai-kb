# Anthropic S-1 financials: H1 2026 revenue $16.3B, operating loss $693M

Per Anthropic's S-1 as summarized by The Deep View: revenue was $16.3B in the first six months of 2026 vs about $1.2B in the same period of 2025 (about 15x). Reported net loss was $80.7B, but about $72.4B of it is a non-cash 'change in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs'; operating loss was $693M. Compute spend was almost $9B in H1 2026 vs $7.3B for all of 2025, with ~$266B of future compute commitments as of June 30 (terms 1-20 years) to Amazon, Google, Microsoft and neoclouds such as Nscale and Lambda. Cloud-provider related deals were 48% of H1 revenue. Do not read the $80.7B loss as operating losses.
