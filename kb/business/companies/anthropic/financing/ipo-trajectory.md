# Anthropic's 2026 IPO trajectory: confidential filing, $65B round at $965B valuation, bankers convening investor meetings

TIMELINE:

- **Early June 2026** — Anthropic confidentially filed for an IPO, alongside OpenAI's own confidential draft S-1 (the OpenAI side is tracked in detail at kb/business/companies/openai/ipo/20d52cf8.md, which supersedes the thinner joint-filing framing).
- **~July 2026** — Anthropic closed a $65 billion funding round at a $965 billion valuation, surpassing OpenAI's reported $852 billion valuation.
- **2026-07-15 (CNBC)** — Bankers are lining up investor meetings ahead of a potential IPO later in 2026. Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan Chase are leading the effort — the same syndicate reported on OpenAI's filing.

STRUCTURAL TENSION (flagged editorially by The Deep View, worth tracking rather than asserting): both Anthropic and OpenAI have publicly committed to safety-first principles — OpenAI calling for decentralized AI power, Anthropic urging slower development toward autonomous systems. Public-market status subjects both to shareholder-value pressures favoring growth and margin. Whether post-filing conduct matches stated safety commitments is the watch item.

EPISTEMIC NOTES: (1) A confidential filing is an option, not a commitment — OpenAI's own timing reversed within weeks of its filing, so filing and listing must be tracked as separate events. The same caution applies to Anthropic. (2) Valuation figures are from reporting, not audited disclosure; an effective S-1 would be the first authoritative source. (3) 'Surpassing OpenAI' compares a closed round to a reported target range ($852B–$1T for OpenAI) and should not be read as a settled ranking.
