# Anthropic's 2026 IPO trajectory: confidential filing, $65B round at $965B valuation, bankers convening investor meetings, offering sized to rival SpaceX, ~$100B raise reported

TIMELINE:

- **Early June 2026** — Anthropic confidentially filed for an IPO with the SEC, alongside OpenAI's own confidential draft S-1. The OpenAI side is tracked separately at kb/business/companies/openai/ipo/20d52cf8.md, which supersedes any thinner joint-filing framing.
- **~July 2026** — Anthropic closed a $65 billion Series H at a $965 billion valuation, surpassing OpenAI's reported $852 billion valuation.
- **2026-07-15 (CNBC)** — Bankers were lining up investor meetings ahead of a potential IPO later in 2026. Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan Chase led the effort — the same syndicate reported on OpenAI's filing.
- **August 2026 (FT / Ars Technica)** — Investors were reported in talks around a valuation above $2 trillion, potentially the largest IPO in history.
- **2026-08-20 (Bloomberg)** — Anthropic PBC (a public benefit corporation) expects its planned initial public offering to match or exceed the size of SpaceX's record offering.
- **2026-08-21 (New York Times)** — Anthropic could raise as much as $100 billion in the IPO, which would rank among the largest IPOs ever attempted. This figure is consistent with, and gives a concrete number to, the Bloomberg 'SpaceX-scale or larger' expectation.

STRUCTURAL TENSION (flagged editorially by The Deep View — worth tracking rather than asserting): both Anthropic and OpenAI have publicly committed to safety-first principles, OpenAI calling for decentralized AI power and Anthropic urging slower development toward autonomous systems. Public-market status subjects both to shareholder-value pressures favoring growth and margin. Whether post-filing conduct matches stated safety commitments is the watch item. Related: Anthropic plans supervoting founder stock (kb/business/companies/anthropic/corporate-governance/920121bb.md), which is the governance mechanism intended to blunt exactly that pressure.

EPISTEMIC NOTES: (1) A confidential filing is an option, not a commitment — OpenAI's own timing reversed within weeks of its filing, so filing and listing must be tracked as separate events. The same caution applies to Anthropic. (2) Valuation and raise-size figures are from reporting, not audited disclosure; an effective S-1 would be the first authoritative source. (3) 'Surpassing OpenAI' compares a closed round to a reported target range ($852B–$1T for OpenAI) and should not be read as a settled ranking. (4) The SpaceX-scale offering size is an expectation attributed to Anthropic via Bloomberg reporting, not a priced or filed figure. (5) The $2T valuation figure is investor talk reported secondhand and is not consistent with the $965B round price — treat the spread between them as unresolved. (6) The $100B raise figure (NYT) is a raise size, not a valuation, and should not be conflated with the $965B or $2T valuation numbers.
