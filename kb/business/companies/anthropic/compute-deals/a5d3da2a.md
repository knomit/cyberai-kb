# Reported: Nscale and Anthropic in talks for another $45B compute deal

TechCrunch (2026-10-19) reports Anthropic and Nscale are reportedly in talks for another $45B compute deal, a little over a month after announcing a first $45B deal. Both are pursuing IPOs; Anthropic expected to go public this year, while Nscale faces investor pushback on a proposed $30B valuation. Reported, not confirmed.
