# Mythos-class model pricing and Claude plan inclusion

Per The Deep View (June 10, 2026), Anthropic priced both Fable 5 and Mythos 5 at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens — half the cost of the earlier Mythos preview. Until June 22, 2026, Fable 5 is included on Claude Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans at no extra cost; afterward it requires usage credits. Single-source newsletter reporting.
