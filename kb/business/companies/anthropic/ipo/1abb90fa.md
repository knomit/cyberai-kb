# Anthropic files S-1 for Nasdaq IPO under ticker ANT

On Monday 2026-10-26 Anthropic publicly filed an S-1 for its IPO, planning to list on the Nasdaq under ticker "ANT", with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading. Share count, price range and raise size were not disclosed in the filing as reported by The Deep View (2026-10-27). Does not mean the listing date is fixed; the newsletter says only 'likely in the next month'.
