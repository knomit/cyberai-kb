# Anthropic accused DeepSeek, Moonshot, and MiniMax of industrial-scale distillation

In February 2026, Anthropic accused three Chinese AI labs — DeepSeek, Moonshot, and MiniMax — of systematically extracting Claude's outputs to train their own models, an effort it called 'industrial-scale' distillation, detecting over 16 million exchanges from 24,000 fraudulent accounts. Anthropic argued illicitly distilled models lack safeguards and create national security risks. Reactions were mixed: some critics called it hypocritical given that AI developers commonly train on copyrighted material as presumed fair use, while others saw it as Anthropic protecting competitive advantage. In April 2026 the White House issued a memo acknowledging industrial-scale distillation as an adversarial threat.
