# Anthropic adds Claude Enterprise token cost-control features

Anthropic rolled out Claude Enterprise features to control token spend: spend alerts (admins notified at 75% and 90% of the org limit; employees at 75% and 95% of personal limits), costs-vs-outputs analytics for ROI analysis, and admin-set default models for new conversations/tasks across Claude, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork. Reported by The Deep View.
