# Anthropic Public Benefit Fund: nonprofit arm to receive 5% of Anthropic shares (subject to stockholder approval)

Announced Oct 18, 2026: the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, a nonprofit arm that, subject to stockholder approval, receives 5% of Anthropic's shares plus an additional 2% at the board's discretion for every 50% increase in company value above a set baseline. Reuters estimates contributions of roughly $75-100B. Complements a $200M Economic Futures Research Fund, a co-founder pledge to donate 80% of equity, and an employee equity-donation match (about half of employees participate). The Deep View notes Anthropic could IPO as early as November.
