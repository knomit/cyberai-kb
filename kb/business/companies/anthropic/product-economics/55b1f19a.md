# Anthropic's cheaper Opus 5 overtook Fable 5 in corporate model spending within a month of launch

Opus 5 overtook Fable 5 in corporate model spending within a month of its launch. The analysis attributes this to low switching costs, which let businesses route routine work to cheaper models while reserving premium systems for tasks requiring sustained autonomy. Opus 5 costs roughly half of Fable 5's rates, but the cheaper system may need more attempts, longer prompts, or more human review, so cost per successful task can converge. Fable remains positioned for long autonomous projects that must stay coherent across many connected steps.
