# Character.ai files voluntary Chapter 11 to pursue court-supervised sale

Character.ai began a voluntary Chapter 11 process to pursue a court-supervised sale after legal, regulatory and operational pressures. Platform can continue under a successful bid but may be wound down without a viable buyer.
