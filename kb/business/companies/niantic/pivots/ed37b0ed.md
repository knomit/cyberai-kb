# Niantic sold games to Scopely for $3.5B and spun out Niantic Spatial

Niantic, maker of Pokemon Go, sold its suite of mobile games to Scopely for $3.5 billion and spun out a lab (in March) called Niantic Spatial, focused on building a 'Large Geospatial Model' — a world model that enhances spatial reasoning. Reported by The Deep View, July 3, 2026.
