# Thinking Machines Lab in talks to raise $6B at $40B valuation

Thinking Machines Lab was reported in September 2026 to be in talks to raise $6 billion at a $40 billion valuation. A separate report described Accel in talks to lead a $1 billion round at the same $40 billion valuation, below the $50 billion the company had previously sought.
