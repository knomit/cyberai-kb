# AMD to acquire World Labs for about $8.2B in all-stock deal

AMD agreed (2026-09) to acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs in an all-stock deal valued at roughly $8.2 billion; Li becomes AMD's chief scientist and World Labs' spatial-intelligence team joins AMD.
