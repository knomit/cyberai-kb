# Salesforce Dreamforce 2026 product slate: AIforce, Claudeforce skills and Headless 360

Alongside the Koa model, Salesforce announced three products at Dreamforce 2026 on 2026-09-15. AIforce is a new interface replacing the traditional Salesforce UI, letting users ask questions, run complex queries, assign tasks and generate dashboards by interrogating their Salesforce instance directly. Claudeforce ships with 37 pre-built sales skills at launch covering deal review, pipeline hygiene, prospect research and account management. Headless 360 exposes all platform elements through APIs, MCPs, plugins and skills so customers can reach their Salesforce data from other platforms without visiting salesforce.com or using Salesforce's own tools. CEO Marc Benioff framed the strategy as 'the SaaS-pocalypse was not about the end of software, but it may be about the end of software that makes humans do all the work.'
