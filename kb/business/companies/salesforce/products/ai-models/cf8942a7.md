# Salesforce announced Koa, a domain-specific reasoning model post-trained from Nvidia Nemotron 3 Super

At Dreamforce 2026 in San Francisco on 2026-09-15, Salesforce announced Koa, a domain-specific reasoning model purpose-built to let agents handle business tasks while keeping customer data private. Salesforce reported that 'Koa already matches or exceeds leading model performance on CRM actions with 3x fewer errors,' measured on the LLM benchmark for CRM created by Salesforce AI Research. Koa — named for the Hawaiian tree used to make canoes and ukuleles — was built by post-training Nvidia's open model Nemotron 3 Super on synthetic data drawn from nearly three decades of business knowledge, and tuned for knowledge work. Rohan Kumar, Salesforce chief platform and engineering officer, said Koa 'is designed to support long-running agents that execute multiple tasks and complete complex outcomes.'
