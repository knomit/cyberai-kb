# Ford rehires ~350 veteran engineers after AI quality systems fell short

Ford rehired more than 350 veteran 'gray beard' engineers over three years after over-relying on automated AI quality systems failed to deliver desired results and cost billions. COO Kumar Galhotra said the company had been relying more on automated quality systems without the desired results. Ford is keeping AI but pairing it with human oversight; its 2026 J.D. Power Initial Quality Study placed Ford first for the first time in 16 years.
