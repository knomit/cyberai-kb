# Chinese AI startup Z.ai nearing $1bn in sales while releasing top models for free

Z.ai is about to hit $1bn in sales while giving its best models away for free. A large share of its income comes from on-premises deployments for state-owned enterprises and financial institutions, plus a fast-growing cloud business.
