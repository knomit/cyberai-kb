# Groq raised $350 million at a $3.5 billion valuation after Nvidia licensed its technology

Groq raised $350 million at a $3.5 billion valuation after Nvidia licensed its technology and hired senior members of its team. The remaining company was rebuilding around an inference cloud that combines Groq LPUs with Nvidia systems.
