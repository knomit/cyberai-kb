# AMD and Anthropic signed a multi-billion-dollar chips-and-investment deal

AMD and Anthropic signed a deal covering tens of billions of dollars' worth of AI servers. Anthropic will purchase up to 2 gigawatts of AMD's Instinct MI450 chips beginning in the first half of 2027. AMD will invest up to $5 billion in Anthropic as certain deployment milestones are met, and the companies are jointly identifying data centers to host the chips.
