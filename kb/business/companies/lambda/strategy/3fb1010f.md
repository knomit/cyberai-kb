Lambda's CCO Robert Brooks IV (Deep View Conversations podcast, May 2026) argues compute is a strategic resource, not a commodity. Lambda's mission is building supercomputers for superintelligence. Key topics: why 'one GPU per person' is achievable, hidden complexity behind modern AI data centers, why compute demand keeps surprising the industry, a $40K robot experiment and lessons about the future of work, how AI is changing how leaders spend their time.
