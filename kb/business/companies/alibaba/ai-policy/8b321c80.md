# Alibaba reportedly restricted employee use of Claude Code

Alibaba reportedly planned to prohibit employee use of Anthropic's Claude Code starting July 10, 2026, after classifying it as high-risk software, and directed employees toward its own Qoder tool. The move came amid Anthropic's efforts to prevent unauthorized access and model distillation.
