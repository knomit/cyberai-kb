# Alibaba is shifting Qwen from open to closed weights

Alibaba is increasingly closing its top-tier Qwen models. Qwen3.7-Max, Qwen3.6-Max-Preview, and Qwen3.6-Plus have closed weights, while lower-tier Qwen3.6-27B and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B remain freely available. Alibaba also began charging for its Qwen Code command-line coding tool. The Batch (DeepLearning.AI) reports these changes follow leadership turnover in the Qwen team and suggest Alibaba aims to monetize its best models rather than maximize reach.
