# Stripe acquired OpenRouter, which routes over 10 trillion tokens per day

Stripe acquired OpenRouter. Analysis argues the strategic rationale is AI security and alignment rather than payments: OpenRouter manages over 10 trillion tokens per day across the model ecosystem, giving it cross-network behavioral data on model usage that no individual provider or lab possesses. The acquisition positions Stripe as a neutral party combining its security infrastructure with OpenRouter's unique cross-provider data assets.
