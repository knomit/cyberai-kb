# Replit makes OpenAI its preferred provider for Replit Agent, replacing Anthropic

TechCrunch (2026-10-17) reports Replit replaced Anthropic with OpenAI as its preferred provider for Replit Agent, citing cost, performance and partnership considerations. CEO Amjad Masad clarified users can still select Claude and other supported models, so this is a default change, not removal of Claude.
