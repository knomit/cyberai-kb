# Databricks launches open agentic AI stack at Data + AI Summit

Databricks, valued at about $134 billion with over 60% of Fortune 500 companies as customers, launched an open stack for agentic AI at its Data + AI Summit. Announcements included OpenSharing (an open-source Linux Foundation project to share AI models, agents, and apps across clouds), Omnigent (an open-source 'harness for harnesses' to manage agents), DBRX (an open-source LLM), the Genie enterprise agent ecosystem, and Unity AI Gateway for token cost visibility and model routing.
