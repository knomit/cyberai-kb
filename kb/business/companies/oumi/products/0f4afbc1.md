# Oumi launches platform for enterprises to build and own custom AI models

Oumi, a startup led by former Google Gemini lead Manos Koukoumidas (CEO), released new capabilities letting enterprises deploy specialized custom AI models with one-click auto-scaling production inference, continuously retrain them on live production data, and manage the full model lifecycle from one console, plus a publicly accessible CLI operable by agents. The pitch is to let organizations 'own' rather than 'rent' their intelligence in hours rather than months.
