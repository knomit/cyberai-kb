# Thinking Machines reportedly raising $1B at a $40B valuation, below its $50B target

TechCrunch reported on 2026-09-03 that Accel is in talks to lead a $1 billion round for Thinking Machines at a $40 billion valuation. That figure is below the roughly $50 billion valuation the company reportedly sought in late 2025 — a down-round relative to its own ask, though still an increase in absolute terms over prior rounds. As-reported, not confirmed by the company.
