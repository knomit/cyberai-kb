# Thinking Machines reportedly raising at a $40B valuation, below its $50B target

Two September 2026 reports describe Thinking Machines Lab raising at a $40 billion valuation, with differing round sizes.

TechCrunch reported on 2026-09-03 that Accel is in talks to lead a $1 billion round at a $40 billion valuation. The Information separately reported the company in talks to raise $6 billion at the same $40 billion valuation. The two figures may describe the lead's cheque versus the full round, or successive versions of the same deal; the reports do not reconcile them.

The $40 billion figure is below the roughly $50 billion valuation the company reportedly sought in late 2025 — a down-round relative to its own ask, though still an increase in absolute terms over prior rounds.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: as-reported, not confirmed by the company, and a valuation below the ask is not the same as a valuation below the previous round.
