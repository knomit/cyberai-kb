# Apple acquired Israeli audio-AI startup Q.ai in January 2026

Reuters reported on 2026-01-29 that Apple quietly acquired Israeli audio-AI startup Q.ai. The Deep View cites this as a possible route for Apple to close the gap with Google's Rambler and Wispr Flow on AI-quality voice dictation, where Siri dictation currently lags.
