# Apple's Siri reboot at WWDC 2026 leans on third-party models

Apple's WWDC 2026 keynote (Monday, June 8, 2026) was set to reveal a new AI vision, including a long-awaited Siri overhaul, after a disappointing AI reveal at WWDC 2024. Apple has enlisted Google Gemini to help power the Apple Foundation Models running Siri, and is partnering with Google and OpenAI (with Anthropic models seen as likely to join), positioning Apple to orchestrate across multiple labs rather than build its own frontier models. The Information reported Apple plans an AI agent marketplace on the App Store with strict privacy/security adherence. CEO Tim Cook told staff in August 2025 that AI is 'as big or bigger' than the internet and smartphones.
