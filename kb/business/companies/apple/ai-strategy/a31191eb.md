# Apple's rebuilt Siri runs on APPLE FOUNDATION MODELS co-developed with Google, on-device and via Private Cloud Compute — NOT on Google-served Gemini, which is what the pre-WWDC preview wrongly predicted

MERGED TO PUT A WRONG FORECAST NEXT TO THE OUTCOME THAT REFUTED IT. A pre-WWDC preview record predicted 'Gemini-powered Siri' and was left standing at reduced confidence (0.45) with the wrong claim in its title. A confidence downgrade does not correct a wrong body, so the prediction is preserved here AS A PREDICTION THAT DID NOT HOLD, alongside what the post-event record establishes.

## WHAT ACTUALLY SHIPPED — the post-WWDC account
Apple unveiled a FULLY REBUILT SIRI at WWDC 2026, based on APPLE FOUNDATION MODELS CO-DEVELOPED WITH GOOGLE. The architecture runs BOTH ON-DEVICE AND VIA APPLE'S PRIVATE CLOUD COMPUTE SERVERS.

New Siri capabilities: PERSONAL CONTEXT (reads the user's messages, emails and photos to act on them); ONSCREEN AWARENESS; VISUAL INTELLIGENCE baked into the iPhone camera app (and Mac/iPad via screenshot or keyboard shortcut); a STANDALONE SIRI APP for conversation history; improved DICTATION with auto-formatting; and WRITING TOOLS with auto-proofreading usable anywhere text is typed. Siri can now CHAIN CONTEXT ACROSS APPS (e.g. 'find that hotel Jason emailed me and add it to my calendar').

Mike Rockwell, who led the rebuild, said Apple achieved a smarter Siri A YEAR AGO but went back to REBUILD THE TECHNICAL INFRASTRUCTURE FROM SCRATCH. This was TIM COOK'S FINAL WWDC AS CEO.

## THE PREDICTION THAT FAILED — preserved as history
A pre-WWDC preview (Deep View, citing Mark Gurman) stated that Apple's WWDC keynote WOULD reveal: (1) 'GEMINI-POWERED SIRI — iOS 27 will use GOOGLE GEMINI MODELS UNDER THE HOOD for Siri', with Apple crafting the UX, potentially including a standalone Siri app and a ChatGPT-competitor chatbot; and (2) 'AI EXTENSIONS IN APP STORE' — a new dedicated section letting users download agents and AI tools from frontier labs and integrate them into Siri, turning it into an AI superapp. It gave the keynote date as JUNE 8, 2026 and said features would launch in beta in summer 2026 with official rollout in fall software updates.

WHY CLAIM (1) IS WRONG AND THE DISTINCTION IS LOAD-BEARING. Apple runs ITS OWN WEIGHTS on its own silicon and its own Private Cloud Compute. A further corpus record establishes that AFM 3 is Apple's OWN ~20B mixture-of-experts model, co-developed with Google and DISTILLED FROM Gemini. 'Apple's Siri runs on Google's models' and 'Apple's Siri runs on models Apple distilled from Google's, with Google's help' are MATERIALLY DIFFERENT CLAIMS — the first implies Google could withdraw inference and break Siri, which does not follow from the second. See kb/business/companies/apple/b65fd0a6.md, which records this correction and states it deliberately does NOT rely on the AI Extensions prediction either, noting the two records also disagree on the keynote's date.

WHAT SURVIVES FROM THE PREVIEW: the standalone Siri app did ship, and Apple's stated hardware edge — Apple silicon with neural processors, widely used by frontier-lab developers, with Mac minis popular for running personal AI agents — is separately corroborated elsewhere in this corpus.
WHAT DOES NOT: the Gemini-under-the-hood claim, and the AI Extensions App Store section, for which no post-event confirmation exists here.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN
- Do NOT cite this record, or any record, for the claim that Siri runs on Google-served Gemini inference.
- The post-WWDC account carried confidence 0.93 with ZERO recorded sources; confidence here is set to 0.85 to reflect that. The preview carried 0.45 with two sources and is retained only as a superseded forecast.
- 'Co-developed with Google' is Apple's characterization as reported; the division of labour between the two companies is not established here.
