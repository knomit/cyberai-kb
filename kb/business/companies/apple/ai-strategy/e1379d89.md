# Apple WWDC 2026: New Siri Architecture Built on Apple Foundation Models + Google, On-Device + PCC

Apple unveiled a fully rebuilt Siri at WWDC 2026, based on Apple Foundation Models co-developed with Google. The architecture runs both on-device and via Apple's Private Cloud Compute servers. New Siri capabilities: personal context (reads user's messages, emails, photos to act on them), onscreen awareness, Visual Intelligence baked into the iPhone camera app (and Mac/iPad via screenshot/keyboard shortcut), standalone Siri app for conversation history, improved dictation with auto-formatting, Writing Tools with auto-proofreading usable anywhere text is typed. Siri can now chain context across apps (e.g., 'find that hotel Jason emailed me and add it to my calendar'). Mike Rockwell, who led the rebuild, said Apple achieved a smarter Siri a year ago but went back to rebuild the technical infrastructure from scratch. This was Tim Cook's final WWDC as CEO.
