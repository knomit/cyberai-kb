# Apple to spend $30B on US-made Broadcom chips

Apple reached a deal to spend $30 billion on US-made chips from Broadcom (reported by The Deep View citing the Wall Street Journal, Jul 8, 2026). (Note: The Deep View's news blurb mis-stated the figure as '$30 million'; the WSJ headline states $30 billion.)
