# Apple's WWDC 2026 Apple Intelligence and Siri reboot

Per The Deep View (June 10, 2026), at WWDC 2026 Apple rebooted Apple Intelligence and Siri. The standout unique feature is 'Spatial Reframing' (recomposing photos by shifting the subject's perspective and generating content to fill gaps). Other updates: Photos 'Extend' and an upgraded 'Clean Up'; Safari AI tab grouping and 'Notify Me' natural-language website-change alerts plus describe-to-build extensions; Passwords auto-fixing compromised passwords; Image Playground photorealistic generation via Private Cloud Compute; and Shortcuts describe-to-automate. Apple's Foundation Models reportedly get 'help from Gemini.' The framing emphasizes Apple's 'Personal Context' as its key trust-based advantage versus Google. Single-source newsletter reporting.
