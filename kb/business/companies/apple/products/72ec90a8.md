# Apple HomeView ($299) smart home display brings context-aware Siri to home

On 2026-10-13 Apple launched HomeView, its first smart home display ($299, 7-inch Liquid Retina, wall-mounted variant also available), recognizing up to six users faces and using Apple Intelligence plus personal app context (Messages, Calendar, Find My) for multistep Siri requests. It works with the new Apple Home Collection (cameras, thermostats, lock made with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric).
