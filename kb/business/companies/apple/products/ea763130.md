# Gurman: Apple smart-home hub and Siri AI devices could launch Oct 13

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reported (2026-09-30, 10-06) Apple's smart-home launch could come as soon as October 13: a 6-inch display hub with FaceTime camera, upgraded HomePod mini and Apple TV for Siri AI, plus an LG co-developed doorbell, thermostat, deadbolt and cameras. Rumor-level, unconfirmed by Apple.
