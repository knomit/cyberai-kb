# AI now powers 40%+ of the top iPhone apps and drives outsized spending

Apple announced its App Store facilitated over $1.4 trillion in developer billings and sales in 2025. More than 40 of the top 100 App Store apps featured consumer-facing AI capabilities, and those apps saw four times the billings growth of the other top 100 apps. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini dominate the iOS charts, but non-AI-first apps (photo editing, shopping, health) with AI features also benefit. Apple said developers paid no commission on more than 90% of App Store billings and sales. The Deep View frames AI as directly transactional in apps (paying unlocks immediate capability), unlike game tokens.
