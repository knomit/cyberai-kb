# Figma Config 2026: AI tools aimed at team collaboration

At Figma's Config event (June 2026, San Francisco), CEO Dylan Field and CDO Loredana Crisan announced tools positioning AI as a team capability rather than solo productivity: Code Layers (work with repos and sync design/production code in Figma), Motion and Shader tools (animations, 3D, advanced visuals), Figma Weave (bringing 20+ AI tools into the canvas as reusable building blocks), and agent skills for automating repetitive tasks. Field said he does not expect differentiating creative breakthroughs to come from AI any time soon. The Deep View notes Figma is used by ~95% of Fortune 500 companies.
