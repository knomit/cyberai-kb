# Cognition raised $2B at a $48B valuation against ~$800M annual burn

TechCrunch reported on 2026-09-08 that Cognition raised $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation, led by Andreessen Horowitz with Accel, Founders Fund, General Catalyst and Avenir. Cognition leases an Nvidia server cluster that could push total 2026 cash burn to $800 million, and is expected to reach $4-5 billion annualized revenue by end of 2026. The framing in coverage is that investors continuing to fund multiple AI-coding companies at this scale signals they do not expect the AI coding market to be winner-take-all.
