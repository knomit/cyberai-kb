# Cognition set to raise ~$1B at a ~$47B valuation on >$900M annualized revenue

Bloomberg reported on 2026-09-02 that Cognition, maker of the Devin AI engineering agent, is set to close a funding round of around $1 billion at a valuation of about $47 billion, with the final size possibly larger given roughly $10 billion of investor interest. Cognition now brings in more than $900 million in annualized revenue. Three months earlier it had raised $1 billion at a $26 billion valuation — an ~81% valuation increase in one quarter. Terms were not final at the time of reporting.
