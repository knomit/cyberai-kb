# Cognition's 2026 round progressed from reported talks at $40B (12 Aug) to ~$47B (2 Sep) to a closed $2B at $48B (8 Sep) — three snapshots of ONE round, not three rounds

MERGED from three separately-filed records of the SAME evolving funding round. None referenced the others, so a reader landing on the earliest would take $40 billion as the current valuation. The progression is preserved with its dates; THIS IS ONE ROUND OBSERVED AT THREE MOMENTS, NOT THREE RAISES.

## THE PROGRESSION
- 12 AUGUST 2026 (Bloomberg): AI coding startup Cognition reported to be IN NEW FUNDING TALKS to raise about $1 BILLION at a $40 BILLION valuation. (Recorded at confidence 0.7.)
- 2 SEPTEMBER 2026 (Bloomberg): Cognition, maker of the DEVIN AI engineering agent, SET TO CLOSE a round of around $1 BILLION at a valuation of about $47 BILLION, with the final size POSSIBLY LARGER given roughly $10 BILLION OF INVESTOR INTEREST. Cognition then brought in MORE THAN $900 MILLION IN ANNUALIZED REVENUE. TERMS WERE NOT FINAL at the time of that reporting. Context noted: three months earlier it had raised $1 BILLION AT A $26 BILLION VALUATION — an ~81% VALUATION INCREASE IN ONE QUARTER.
- 8 SEPTEMBER 2026 (TechCrunch): Cognition RAISED $2 BILLION at a $48 BILLION valuation — DOUBLE the $1B repeatedly reported in the talks stage, consistent with the noted $10B of investor interest. Led by ANDREESSEN HOROWITZ with ACCEL, FOUNDERS FUND, GENERAL CATALYST and AVENIR.

## THE COST SIDE
Cognition LEASES AN NVIDIA SERVER CLUSTER that could push TOTAL 2026 CASH BURN TO $800 MILLION, and is expected to reach $4–5 BILLION ANNUALIZED REVENUE BY END OF 2026.

## THE FRAMING IN COVERAGE
That investors continue to fund MULTIPLE AI-coding companies at this scale signals THEY DO NOT EXPECT THE AI CODING MARKET TO BE WINNER-TAKE-ALL.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN
- The $40B, $47B and $48B figures are NOT three separate valuations to be compared or averaged; the first two are reporting on talks that had not closed, and the third is the closed number. Cite $48B / $2B with the 8 September date.
- The >$900M annualized revenue (early September) and the $4–5B end-2026 expectation are DIFFERENT KINDS OF NUMBER: one measured-as-reported, one a forward expectation. The end-2026 figure is an expectation reported in coverage, not a result.
- The $800M burn figure is a POTENTIAL total contingent on the leased cluster, not a booked expense.
- The winner-take-all reading is COVERAGE FRAMING, not a finding about the market.
- All three inputs were single-sourced press reports; Cognition is not recorded here as confirming any of them.
