# Poetiq pursues recursive self-improvement via optimizers outside the base LLM

Poetiq, founded by former Google DeepMind engineers Ian Fischer and Shumeet Baluja, builds recursive self-improvement (RSI) technology using 'self-optimizing optimizers' — harnesses that sit outside existing LLMs and improve themselves on narrowly-scoped tasks rather than modifying the base model. In August 2026 it debuted Augur, a system that uses customized, domain-specific benchmarks to quantitatively compare model performance (open-source or proprietary) and help match the right model to a task. Poetiq keeps its RSI tech internal-only, citing danger if released.
