# Amazon launches Alexa Tablets built around Alexa+

Amazon launched Alexa Tablet 12 Pro, 11 and 8 (2026-10-08) centered on Alexa+. The 12 Pro is 6.5mm thick, 3:2 aspect ratio, 4nm 8-core chip (claimed ~3x CPU, 9x graphics vs prior Amazon tablets), Nanomatte display, $549.99 with nanomatte, and a 'Kindle Sanctuary' reading mode.
