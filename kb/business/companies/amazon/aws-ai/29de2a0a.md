# AWS invests $1 billion in forward deployed engineers for enterprise AI

Amazon Web Services announced a $1 billion investment to embed forward deployed engineers (FDEs) within its customer network, working directly with customers' business, engineering, and security teams to deploy AI agents into their AWS environments. Francessca Vasquez, VP of frontier AI engineering and services at AWS, described the goal as making customers 'self-sufficient with AI.' The Deep View noted FDE job postings were up roughly 700% year over year (per Business Insider), and that OpenAI launched its own OpenAI Deployment Company for FDEs in May 2026. Reported July 2, 2026.
