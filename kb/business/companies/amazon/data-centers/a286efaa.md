# Amazon 'Built Together': $1B over five years for data-center communities

On 2026-10-02 Amazon announced 'Built Together', a $1 billion five-year investment framework for communities hosting its data centers: education/workforce (free community-college associate degrees for electricians, HVAC, fiber, IT etc.), energy and water affordability grants (heat pumps, HVAC, insulation, solar; up to 40% energy-cost reduction), and community-led local priorities. AWS CEO Matt Garman rejected state data-center moratoria, saying the US 'could be writing its own losing ticket' in the AI race. The Deep View cites a Gallup poll that on average 7 in 10 Americans oppose data centers being built in their communities.
