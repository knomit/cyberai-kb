# Amazon's new Kindles omit AI features; Kindle read 720B pages in 2025

Amazon's new Kindle, Paperwhite and Colorsoft (announced ~Oct 2026) emphasize thinner/lighter design, edge-to-edge display and ~30% faster book opening with no AI features. Amazon says Kindle had double-digit global growth three consecutive years and over 720 billion pages read in 2025.
