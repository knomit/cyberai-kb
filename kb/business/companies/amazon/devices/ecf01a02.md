# Amazon launches Bee Frontier ($74.99) and Bee Eclipse ($99.99) AI wearables

Amazon announced Bee Frontier (pin/wristband, 7-day battery, IP67, two mics; $74.99) and Bee Eclipse (magnetic phone-back device, 21+ day battery; $99.99, available soon) on 2026-10-15. Summaries and 14-day memory are included; a $11.99 Bee Pro subscription adds Amazon.com and Alexa+ integration. Competitor Plaud Pin is $179, Plaud Note $159. Amazon says Bee Private Compute processes conversations in an isolated environment, audio is not stored.
