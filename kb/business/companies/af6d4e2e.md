# Frontier AI labs treat compute buildout as a strategic moat in 2026, with divergent build-vs-lease strategies

By mid-2026 access to compute is a primary competitive lever among frontier labs. OpenAI expects ~$750B of AI infrastructure spend by 2030 (a 25% increase) and a 3.2 GW, $20-30B Georgia data center, favoring owned/vertically-integrated capacity (its earlier $500B Stargate is reportedly stalled). Anthropic has leaned on leased compute (SpaceX, Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta, AMD) and only recently moved to owning infrastructure via a $50B Fluidstack deal in Texas and New York; compute constraints forced it to taper access to its flagship Fable 5 model (Max/Team Premium only, 50% of weekly limits) and contributed to releasing the cheaper, efficiency-focused Claude Opus 5. Caveat: spending figures are press-reported estimates, not audited commitments.
