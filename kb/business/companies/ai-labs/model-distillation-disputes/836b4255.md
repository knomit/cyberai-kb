# Anthropic says it disrupted unauthorized distillation of Claude by Alibaba, Moonshot and DeepSeek

Anthropic said it detected and disrupted unauthorized large-scale efforts by China-based AI labs including Alibaba, Moonshot and DeepSeek to train their models using Claude. Per Anthropic, operators affiliated with Alibaba used Claude outputs to help train its Qwen models, while Moonshot relayed some Kimi user requests to Claude and used the resulting exchanges to train its own models. China dismissed the US allegations as 'groundless.' Anthropic's report covered seven China-based AI companies in total.
