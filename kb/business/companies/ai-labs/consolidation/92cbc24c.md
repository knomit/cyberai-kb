# Cohere and Aleph Alpha combining into one enterprise AI company (Sept 2026 follow-on)

Reuters reported on 16 September 2026 that Cohere and Aleph Alpha are combining into a single company targeting the enterprise AI market. This is a FOLLOW-ON to the April 2026 acquisition agreement recorded separately in this base, not a second, independent transaction — do not count it as a distinct deal. Both were enterprise-focused model vendors positioned outside the US hyperscaler orbit (Cohere in Canada, Aleph Alpha in Germany), so the combination concentrates the non-hyperscaler enterprise AI segment rather than adding a new entrant to it.
