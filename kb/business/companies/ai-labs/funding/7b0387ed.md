# Arcee AI raised $150 million at a $1 billion valuation

Fortune reported on 16 September 2026 that open-source model company Arcee AI raised $150 million at a $1 billion valuation, having trained four models for roughly $20 million. The two figures are the point taken together: a ~$20M cumulative training spend against a $1B valuation is the efficiency claim underpinning the round, so quoting the valuation alone loses what the round was priced on.
