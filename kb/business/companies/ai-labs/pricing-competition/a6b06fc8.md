# Summer 2026 AI price war: Anthropic extends Fable 5 access, OpenAI ships GPT-5.6

In summer 2026 Anthropic and OpenAI competed for power users by loosening access to their most capable models. Anthropic made Claude Fable 5 ('Mythos-class') available to paid users and, under pressure, extended promotional access to all paid subscribers through July 19 and kept Claude Code weekly rate limits 50% higher. OpenAI released GPT-5.6 (mid-tier 'Terra' to free users; flagship 'Sol' and lightweight 'Luna' reserved for paid tiers) and, celebrating 7 million active users of Codex and ChatGPT, added a 'banked reset' letting users reset usage caps.
