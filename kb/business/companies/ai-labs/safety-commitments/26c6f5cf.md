# Altman and Hassabis responded favorably to Amodei's embedded-evaluator proposal

After Amodei published his pacing essay on 2026-09-12, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said he agrees that 'committing to having independent evaluators with employee-like access is a great idea' and that OpenAI will follow suit. Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis said 'the direction is correct for meeting this critical moment.' Both endorsements were specifically of the embedded-evaluator step, not of the broader industry-wide or global pacing steps.
