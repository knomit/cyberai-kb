# Rokt appoints first Chief AI Officer; draws on 10B+ transactions per year

Rokt, an ecommerce technology company focused on optimizing the checkout experience, named Claire Southey as its first Chief AI Officer. Southey said the company's decisioning models draw on data from more than 10 billion transactions per year, and she frames internal AI adoption as 'a movement, not a mandate' — making tools available and encouraging experimentation rather than mandating use. Reported by The Deep View, August 2026.
