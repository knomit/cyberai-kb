# Rokt collapsed its engineering career ladder into a single 'builder' title, per CTO Sam Dozor

Sam Dozor, CTO of ecommerce technology company Rokt, told The Deep View (published 2026-09-19) that everyone on Rokt's engineering team holds the single title 'builder', with no separately differentiated product managers, engineers, or designers. He frames this as an AI-enabled collapse of the career ladder into fewer rungs, on the reasoning that AI handles much of the tedious technical execution, so people can be differentiated by what they are interested in working on rather than by the technical skills they were trained on, and training can go directly to systems design and overall product strategy. IMPORTANT SOURCING CAVEAT that must not be dropped when citing this: the edition was a special weekend edition presented in partnership with Rokt — sponsored content — and the claims are the assertions of a single executive about his own company, not independently verified reporting or measured outcomes. Treat the org-structure fact (Rokt uses one 'builder' title) as reported, and the causal claim (AI makes flat ladders viable) as one practitioner's position. This does NOT establish that flattening engineering titles is an industry trend or that it produced measurable results at Rokt.
