# Microsoft replacing OpenAI/Anthropic models with in-house MAI in Office apps

On Tuesday (early July 2026), Microsoft announced it is replacing OpenAI and Anthropic models with its own internally built MAI models in products like Excel and Outlook, per Bloomberg. Tens of thousands of AI prompts in those apps are now handled each week by MAI models (spanning reasoning, image, voice, and coding). The stated motivation is cost: in-house models are far cheaper than licensing third-party models, and AI chief Mustafa Suleyman had signaled intent to wean off Anthropic models. The move tests a 'good-enough AI' thesis — that enterprises locked into Office 365 may accept models 80–90% as capable as frontier models if they are cheaper and seen as safer.
