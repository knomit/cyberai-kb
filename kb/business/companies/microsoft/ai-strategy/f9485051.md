# Microsoft posts biggest market leap since 2008, winning AI on efficiency not best model

The Deep View reported Microsoft's latest quarterly results drove its biggest market leap since 2008, arguing its AI advantage lies in efficiency, enterprise trust (Outlook/Teams ubiquity), and translating tokens into measurable business outcomes rather than owning the best frontier model.
