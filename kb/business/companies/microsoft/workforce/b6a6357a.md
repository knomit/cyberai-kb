# Microsoft cut 4,800 jobs in early July 2026

Microsoft eliminated 4,800 jobs (2.1% of its workforce) on Monday in early July 2026 amid AI-related cost pressures. EVP and Chief People Officer Amy Coleman told employees the eliminated roles are not being replaced by AI, while the company shifts resources to expand its AI division.
