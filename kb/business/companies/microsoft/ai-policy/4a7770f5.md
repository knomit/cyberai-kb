# Microsoft published a 37-page humanist AI code of conduct

Microsoft published a 37-page document it calls a "humanist AI code of conduct" at microsoft.ai/code-of-conduct, released during the September 2026 period when frontier-pacing and AI governance were under active public dispute. It is a company-authored voluntary framework, not a regulatory instrument and not binding on anyone else.
