# Open-weight models were 29% of Vercel AI Gateway token volume, under 4% of spend (June 2026)

Vercel reported that open-weight models handled 29% of AI Gateway token volume in June 2026 while accounting for less than 4% of spending, indicating open-weight models are used heavily for high-volume, low-cost workloads.
