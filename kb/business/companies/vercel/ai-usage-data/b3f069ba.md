# Vercel AI Gateway index (Sept 2026): open-weight models 71.5% of tokens, 23% of spend

Vercel's AI Gateway Production Index (October 2026 edition, September data): open-weight models process 71.5% of gateway tokens and nearly 1 in 4 dollars (23%); spend share more than quadrupled since June. Almost one in three teams changed their most-used lab in September (index record). Jev was the fastest-adopted model in gateway history but only about 0.27% of spend. Gateway usage only, not whole-market.
