# Valar Atomics raised $1B Series B led by Sequoia at a reported $6B valuation

Valar Atomics, a startup building small modular nuclear reactors, announced a $1 billion Series B funding round led by Sequoia, with Bloomberg reporting a $6 billion valuation. The company has previously partnered with Nvidia and is developing a waterless 30-megawatt AI factory. The round reflects AI investors moving capital deeper into the stack toward energy. Reported by The Deep View / Bloomberg, Aug 3, 2026.
