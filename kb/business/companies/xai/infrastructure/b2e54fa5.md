# Elon Musk acquired APR Energy, a ~$1B gas turbine company, to power Grok

Elon Musk acquired APR Energy, a Jacksonville-based company operating a fleet of mobile and diesel turbines generating more than 1 GW of capacity, reportedly for around $1 billion, to supply power for Grok/xAI compute.
