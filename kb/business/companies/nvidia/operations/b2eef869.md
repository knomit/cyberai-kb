# Nvidia CEO says company will spend $150 billion per year in Taiwan

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated that Nvidia plans to spend $150 billion per year in Taiwan, describing Taiwan as the 'epicentre of the AI revolution.' This underscores Taiwan's critical role in the global AI hardware supply chain.
