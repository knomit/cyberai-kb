# Nvidia board opens independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang foreign-intelligence targeting

Around 2026-11-08/09 Nvidia's board announced an independent investigation, led by independent directors with outside counsel, after reporting that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort; the board said it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised and Huang continues as CEO. NYT first reported the US intelligence probe in late October; China denied spying allegations; Nvidia stock fell about 5% per CNBC.
