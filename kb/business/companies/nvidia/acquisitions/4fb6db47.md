# Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion — roughly 80x forward revenue, and defensive against customers' custom silicon

Per an August 2026 report by The Information (cited by The Deep View), Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face — the dominant repository of open-source AI models — for $12.9 billion.

THE MULTIPLE. At the time of the agreement Hugging Face had generated only about $150 million in ANNUALIZED revenue, making the purchase price roughly 80 times forward revenue.

THE RATIONALE. The move diversifies Nvidia beyond chipsets and gives it ownership of what IDC's Ashish Nadkarni called the 'front door for open AI innovation.' It is also defensive: Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are each moving toward custom or co-designed AI silicon, and owning the model distribution layer keeps developers tied to Nvidia's ecosystem regardless of who builds the chips.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this is reported by The Information and relayed through newsletter coverage, not a confirmed or closed transaction, and the revenue figure is likewise reported rather than disclosed by Hugging Face. See kb/business/companies/hugging-face/corporate-actions/02ccba86.md for the earlier, vaguer '~$13 billion acquisition offers' stage of the same story.
