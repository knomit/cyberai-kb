# Nvidia acquires Hugging Face for $12.9 billion

Nvidia and Hugging Face finalized Nvidia's acquisition of the open-source AI platform on Thursday 2026-09-03 for $12.9 billion, confirmed by Nvidia's own blog post and by CEO Clem Delangue on X, and independently reported by CNBC and Tom's Hardware. It is Nvidia's second-largest acquisition ever, after the $20 billion purchase of Groq in December 2025. Hugging Face has about 200,000 companies on the platform and over 18 million developers, researchers and creators sharing more than 3 million models, 500,000 datasets and 1 million applications; Delangue's stated goal is 5x growth in users over the next few years. The two stated rationales are compute access for training/fine-tuning/customizing open models, and scale. Notably, Hugging Face had reportedly turned down a $500 million Nvidia investment at a $7 billion valuation in late 2025 specifically to avoid one investor having outsized sway — so the acquisition represents a reversal driven by the need for a scaling partner.
