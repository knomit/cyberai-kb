# Nvidia acquired Hugging Face for $12.9B

In early September 2026 Nvidia acquired Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, its second-largest acquisition ever after the $20B Groq purchase in December 2025. Hugging Face had roughly 200,000 companies on the platform at the time and Nvidia stated an aim to grow that user base fivefold. Nvidia says the platform remains open and Nvidia compute is not required to use it.
