# Nvidia acquired Hugging Face for $12.9 billion on 2026-09-03, after a reported ~$13 billion offer round and an earlier refused $500M investment at a $7B valuation

MERGED from the completed-deal record and an earlier, undated record of the rumour stage ('reportedly fielding acquisition offers valuing the company at around $13 billion'). The rumour record is preserved here as the run-up rather than left standing as an open 'in talks' claim that the deal has since resolved.

## THE DEAL
Nvidia and Hugging Face finalized Nvidia's acquisition of the open-source AI platform on Thursday 2026-09-03 for $12.9 BILLION, confirmed by Nvidia's own blog post and by CEO Clem Delangue on X, and independently reported by CNBC and Tom's Hardware. It is NVIDIA'S SECOND-LARGEST ACQUISITION EVER, after the $20 billion purchase of Groq in December 2025.

## THE RUN-UP
Before the deal closed, Hugging Face was reported to be fielding acquisition offers valuing the company at AROUND $13 BILLION — close to, and slightly above, the $12.9 billion final price. Earlier still, Hugging Face had reportedly TURNED DOWN a $500 MILLION Nvidia investment at a $7 BILLION VALUATION in late 2025, SPECIFICALLY TO AVOID ONE INVESTOR HAVING OUTSIZED SWAY. The acquisition therefore represents a reversal driven by the need for a scaling partner.

## SCALE AT ACQUISITION
Hugging Face has about 200,000 companies on the platform and over 18 MILLION developers, researchers and creators sharing more than 3 MILLION MODELS, 500,000 DATASETS and 1 MILLION APPLICATIONS; Delangue's stated goal is 5X GROWTH IN USERS over the next few years.

## STATED RATIONALE
Two: COMPUTE ACCESS for training, fine-tuning and customizing open models, and SCALE.

## ADJACENT NVIDIA MOVE, carried from the merged record
Separately, Nvidia announced a $6 BILLION INVESTMENT aimed at bolstering the OPEN-SOURCE AI ECOSYSTEM IN THE US. That is recorded in its own right at kb/business/companies/nvidia/investments/241f03f2.md, which adds that the stated purpose is to build a US alternative to Chinese open models; it is noted here only because the two items travelled together in the rumour-stage reporting, NOT because any source links them causally to the acquisition.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN
- The ~$13 billion rumour figure and the $12.9 billion final price are close but are NOT the same number and come from different reporting moments; do not cite the rumour figure as the deal price.
- The 5x user-growth target is Delangue's stated goal, not a projection anyone has validated.
- Nothing here establishes what the acquisition does to Hugging Face's governance, model-hosting neutrality, or the independence concern that reportedly motivated refusing the 2025 investment.
