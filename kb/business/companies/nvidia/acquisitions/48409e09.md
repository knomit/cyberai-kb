# Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion

Per an August 2026 report by The Information (cited by The Deep View), Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face — the dominant repository of open-source AI models — for $12.9 billion. The move diversifies Nvidia beyond chipsets and gives it ownership of what IDC's Ashish Nadkarni called the "front door for open AI innovation." It is also defensive: Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are each moving toward custom or co-designed AI silicon, and owning the model distribution layer keeps developers tied to Nvidia's ecosystem regardless of who builds the chips.
