# Nvidia guided to roughly 70% revenue growth for FY 2028

Nvidia guided for approximately 70% revenue growth in FY 2028, implying revenue approaching $700 billion. A year earlier, average sell-side estimates for Nvidia's FY 2028 revenue were around $310 billion; even after repeated upward revisions through the year, analysts remained roughly $125 billion short. Analyst commentary suggests ~70% growth could be the floor rather than the ceiling.
