# NVIDIA on track to exceed $100 billion in quarterly revenue

Following NVIDIA's Q2 FY27 earnings, analysis indicates NVIDIA will cross $100 billion in quarterly revenue next quarter (roughly a $108B quarter), implying approximately $432 billion in annual revenue and making it about the sixth largest company in the world. The business is described as more diversified than before, though custom silicon from Big Tech customers is flagged as a potential future demand headwind.
