# Nvidia targeting ~$1T data center chip revenue through 2027

The Deep View (Jul 2026) reported Jensen Huang said Nvidia is on track for roughly $1 trillion in data center revenue through 2027 from Grace Blackwell and Vera Rubin chip sales, a figure he suggested the final result would likely exceed.
