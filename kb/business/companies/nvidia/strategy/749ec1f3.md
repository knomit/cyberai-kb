# Nvidia invested $105 billion in an Ohio data center supporting OpenAI as its moat shifts from chips to capital

As of Aug 2026, Nvidia is leveraging capital strength rather than chip performance alone to defend AI leadership against gains by AMD and Google. It committed a $105 billion investment in an Ohio data center supporting OpenAI, and is forming partnerships with Wall Street financiers for GPU investments to widen market influence and diversify revenue.
