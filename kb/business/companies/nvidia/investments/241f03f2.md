# Nvidia investing $6 billion in a US open-source AI ecosystem and eyeing Perplexity

Reported in August 2026: Nvidia is spending $6 billion to build up a US open-source AI ecosystem as an alternative to Chinese open models (Wall Street Journal), and is separately in talks to invest in Perplexity at a valuation above $30 billion, with a technology licensing deal also under consideration (The Information).
