# Lawsuit alleges Nvidia's $20B Groq deal shortchanged stockholders (no Delaware vote)

A stockholder lawsuit alleges Nvidia's $20 billion Groq deal was made without the stockholder vote Delaware law requires and without a process to maximize value (CNBC, 2026-10-05). Allegation, not a ruling.
