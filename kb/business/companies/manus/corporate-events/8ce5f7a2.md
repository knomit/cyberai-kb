# Manus resumed independent operations after a data-access interruption

Manus announced it has resumed independent operations, with its founding team continuing to drive product development on general AI agents. Some users experienced temporary data-access interruptions requiring backup and restoration. Manus says it plans to deepen workflow integration and expand complex task management capabilities.
