# Temporal raised a $550M Series E at a $12.55B valuation

Temporal, a durable workflow orchestration platform, announced a $550 million Series E round at a $12.55 billion valuation, positioning the raise around AI workloads. Workflow orchestration is infrastructure that long-running agentic systems depend on for retries and state durability, which is the stated rationale for the AI framing.
