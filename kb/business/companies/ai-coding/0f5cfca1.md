# Mid-August 2026 AI funding clustered on coding/agentic tooling at revenue multiples of roughly 25-40x

Across two independent reports covering the same window (week of 2026-08-12, reported in The Deep View's 2026-08-18 edition), private AI capital concentrated heavily on code-generation and agentic-workflow companies rather than foundation-model labs. Lovable raised $400M at a $13.3B valuation against ~$500M annualized run-rate revenue (~27x ARR), roughly doubling its $6.6B December valuation. Cognition, operator of the autonomous software engineer Devin, was reported in talks for $1B at $40B against a revenue run rate reportedly approaching $1B (~40x), after raising $1B at $26B earlier in the year. CodeRabbit, an AI code review platform focused on security and governance, raised a $143M Series C at $1.5B; its revenue was not disclosed, so no multiple can be computed for it. Adjacent-category rounds in the same window were an order of magnitude smaller or in different segments: River AI at $1.1B raised (user-owned AI), Wispr at a $2B valuation (beyond dictation).

Scope and caveats carried from sources: the Cognition round is reported as in-talks, not closed, and its revenue figure is 'reportedly approaching' $1B rather than confirmed. Lovable's ARR figure is company-reported. CodeRabbit's multiple is unknown, not low. These are private-market marks, not realized outcomes.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this does not establish that AI coding valuations are a bubble, nor that the multiples are unjustified — high multiples on rapidly compounding revenue can be rational. It also does not claim these were the only or largest AI rounds in the period; the two source facts describe one newsletter's coverage of one week, and neither is a systematic market survey.
