# AI coding startup valuations surged in August 2026: CodeRabbit, Lovable, and Cognition

In the week of 2026-08-12, three AI coding startups announced or were reported in large funding rounds. CodeRabbit, an AI code review platform focused on security and governance, raised a $143 million Series C at a $1.5 billion valuation. Lovable, a European vibe-coding platform, raised a $400 million Series C at a $13.3 billion valuation, roughly doubling its $6.6 billion December valuation, following $500 million in annualized run-rate revenue. Cognition, which operates the autonomous software engineer Devin, was reported in talks to raise $1 billion at a $40 billion valuation, after raising $1 billion at $26 billion earlier in the year and reportedly approaching a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate.
