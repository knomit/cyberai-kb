# DeepSeek in talks to raise $1.5B at ~$71B valuation ahead of IPO

As of July 2026, DeepSeek (Chinese LLM developer, founded 2023) is reportedly in talks to raise roughly $1.5 billion in new funds at about a $71 billion valuation, and is preparing for an IPO debut in 2027 that could come as early as the end of 2026. This follows a $7 billion raise about a month earlier (June 2026) at roughly a $50 billion valuation, which was its first-ever outside funding round. Investors include Tencent and Beijing's National Artificial Intelligence Industry Investment Fund. Originally reported by Bloomberg; DeepSeek did not comment.
