# DeepSeek's 2026 fundraising is recorded with THREE different round/valuation pairs that this corpus cannot reconcile — $7B at ~$50B, $1.5B at ~$71B, and $7.4B at ~$74B

MERGED from two records whose figures do not line up. Neither is demonstrably wrong and neither cites the other; per kb/meta/reasoning/864bb101.md the conflict is recorded rather than resolved by silently preferring the better-sourced side.

THE DATED SEQUENCE (TechCrunch via Bloomberg, July 2026 — the better-specified account):
- JUNE 2026: a $7 BILLION raise at roughly a $50 BILLION valuation — DeepSeek's FIRST-EVER outside funding round. Investors include Tencent and Beijing's National Artificial Intelligence Industry Investment Fund.
- JULY 2026: reportedly IN TALKS to raise roughly $1.5 BILLION at about a $71 BILLION valuation, while preparing for an IPO debut in 2027 that could come as early as the end of 2026. DeepSeek did not comment.

THE UNDATED THIRD FIGURE: a separate record states DeepSeek 'is raising $7.4 BILLION at a reported $74 BILLION valuation' to fund research and development and the buildout of computing infrastructure. It carries no date and confidence 0.65.

WHY THIS CANNOT BE RECONCILED HERE. $7.4B/$74B matches NEITHER the June round ($7B/$50B) nor the July talks ($1.5B/$71B). It could be (a) a later, fourth round; (b) a revised report of the July talks with a different amount; (c) a garbled restatement of the June round at an updated valuation; or (d) an aggregate. No source in this corpus distinguishes these. A reader citing 'DeepSeek's 2026 raise' can take any of three amounts and any of three valuations with roughly equal support.

COMPANY CONTEXT. DeepSeek is a Chinese LLM developer founded in 2023. Its cloud service runs on Huawei-made chips.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this does NOT claim any of the three figures is false. All are single-sourced press reports of private, in-progress fundraising, where amounts and valuations legitimately move between reports. It does NOT establish how many rounds occurred in 2026. And 'in talks' is not a closed round — nothing here confirms the July raise completed or that an IPO was filed.
