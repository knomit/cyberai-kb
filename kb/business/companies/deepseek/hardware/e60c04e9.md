# DeepSeek plans to develop its own inference chips

China's DeepSeek is planning to enter the silicon business, focusing on data-center chips for inference to reduce reliance on both Huawei and Nvidia amid US export controls. The company has spent the past year meeting potential hardware/silicon partners and hiring engineers for the project. Reported in TLDR AI, July 8 2026.
