# Google AI leadership shakeup: Hassabis to chairman, Jeff Dean departs

Around mid-August 2026, Google announced a leadership shift in which Demis Hassabis moved from CEO to chairman of DeepMind and chief scientist of Alphabet, while Jeff Dean left the company to launch a new startup called Discovery Loop.
