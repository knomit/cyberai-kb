# Google DeepMind leadership reshuffle (August 2026)

On or about Wednesday 5 August 2026, Google announced major AI leadership changes.

- Demis Hassabis moved from CEO of DeepMind to chairman of DeepMind and chief scientist of Alphabet, focusing on AGI and scientific discovery.
- Koray Kavukcuoglu — previously DeepMind's CTO and Google's chief AI architect — was elevated to SVP and head of Google DeepMind, reporting directly to CEO Sundar Pichai and overseeing Gemini model development, frontier AI research, and the Gemini app and developer teams.
- Separately, Jeff Dean left Google entirely to launch a new startup called Discovery Loop.

Context and reading: the changes came amid Google falling behind OpenAI and Anthropic in advanced models and agents, having struggled to launch Gemini 3.5 Pro and lagged on agentic coding tools in 2026. Commentators read the moves as Google prioritizing execution over deep research in that race — an interpretation, not a stated rationale.
