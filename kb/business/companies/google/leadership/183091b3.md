# Koray Kavukcuoglu named head of Google DeepMind

Koray Kavukcuoglu is becoming head of Google DeepMind, reporting directly to CEO Sundar Pichai and overseeing Gemini model development, frontier AI research, and the Gemini app and developer teams. He was previously DeepMind's CTO and Google's chief AI architect. Commentators read the move as Google prioritizing execution over deep research in its race with OpenAI and Anthropic.
