# Google DeepMind leadership reshuffle (August 2026)

In August 2026, Google reshuffled its AI leadership. Demis Hassabis moved from CEO to chairman of DeepMind and chief scientist of Alphabet. Koray Kavukcuoglu — previously DeepMind's CTO and Google's chief AI architect — became head of Google DeepMind, reporting directly to CEO Sundar Pichai and overseeing Gemini model development, frontier AI research, and the Gemini app and developer teams. Separately, Jeff Dean left Google to launch a new startup called Discovery Loop. Commentators read the moves as Google prioritizing execution over deep research in its race with OpenAI and Anthropic.
