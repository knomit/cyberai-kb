# Google DeepMind invests $75M in film studio A24

Google DeepMind announced a partnership and a $75 million investment in film studio A24 (maker of Hereditary, Midsommar, Backrooms), a move that drew criticism from fans and Hollywood professionals. A24 framed it as wanting a voice in shaping AI tools built for artists.
