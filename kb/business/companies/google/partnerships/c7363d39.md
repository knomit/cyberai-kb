# Google Cloud and Accenture formed a Gemini Enterprise business group

Google Cloud and Accenture formed the "Accenture Gemini Enterprise Business Group," with Google training up to 1,000 Accenture forward-deployed engineers to build custom AI applications on the Gemini Enterprise platform.
