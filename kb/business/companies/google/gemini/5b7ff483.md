# Google Gemini app surpasses 1 billion monthly active users

Google reported the Gemini app surpassed 1 billion monthly active users, making it Google's 14th product to reach that milestone. Google also cited heavy voice usage, more than 150 million images generated daily, and over 100 million active Gemini users on iOS. Announced by CEO Sundar Pichai.
