# Google reportedly in advanced talks to acquire Mechanize for ~$1.5 billion

Google is reportedly in advanced talks for a roughly $1.5 billion deal with AI coding startup Mechanize, which builds virtual environments, benchmarks, and training data used to teach AI agents to handle more complex tasks.
