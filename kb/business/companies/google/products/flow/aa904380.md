# Google Flow Sessions is a six-week creative partner program for AI filmmaking

Google runs 'Flow Sessions', a creative partner program in which invited artists spend six weeks producing short films using Flow, Google's AI video generator. The fourth cohort's films were screened at a Google Flow film festival in New York City in August 2026. Films ranged from roughly 1 to 3 minutes; some were generated entirely with Flow while others combined practical sets and live actors with AI-generated elements.
