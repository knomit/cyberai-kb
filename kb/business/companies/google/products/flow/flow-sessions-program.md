# Google Flow Sessions is a six-week creative partner program whose filmmakers found hybrid live-action/AI production most effective

Google runs 'Flow Sessions', a creative partner program in which invited artists spend six weeks producing short films using Flow, Google's AI video generator. The fourth cohort's films were screened at a Google Flow film festival in New York City in August 2026.

Films ranged from roughly 1 to 3 minutes. Some were generated entirely with Flow; others combined practical sets and live actors with AI-generated elements.

Filmmakers reported that the most effective use was hybrid: keeping real actors and practical sets while using AI for elements with no real-world counterpart (aliens, monsters, impossible scenarios). Example: Andrew Nethery's 'Nothin' Beats a Cheeseburger' used 20 live-action shots, 32 hybrid shots and five fully AI-generated shots.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the hybrid finding is the filmmakers' reported preference, not a claim that every film in the cohort was hybrid — some were generated entirely with Flow.
