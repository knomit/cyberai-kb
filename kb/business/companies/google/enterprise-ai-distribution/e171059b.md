# Google and Accenture formed a joint unit to field 1,000 forward-deployed engineers

TechCrunch reported (2026-09-08) that Google Cloud and Accenture created the Accenture Gemini Enterprise Business Group, a joint unit placing engineers inside enterprises to drive adoption of Google's AI tools. Google will train up to 1,000 Accenture forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) to build custom applications on the Gemini Enterprise platform. The unit lives under Accenture, not Google — Google is buying distribution and services capacity rather than building an FDE org. This mirrors the FDE model popularized by Palantir and adopted by OpenAI and Anthropic.
