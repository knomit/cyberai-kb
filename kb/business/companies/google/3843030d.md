# Google and Anthropic are investor, supplier and talent-rival simultaneously — and each hedges the channel it controls while deepening it

Between April and July 2026 three distinct relationships ran concurrently between Google and Anthropic, in different directions, and both parties took a hedging step on the channel they control.

THE THREE CHANNELS.
- CAPITAL, Google to Anthropic: an investment of up to $40 billion announced around 27 April 2026, following prior Google investment.
- COMPUTE, Google to Anthropic: Anthropic stated that chips from Google, Amazon and Nvidia 'remain central to its hardware strategy'. Google's own silicon line expanded with TPU 8t and TPU 8i, unveiled at Google Cloud Next 2026 and optimized for training and inference respectively.
- TALENT, Google to Anthropic: Gemini researchers Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel left Google for Anthropic, part of a broader departure wave that also included Noam Shazeer and John Jumper.

The recipient of Google's capital and compute is a direct competitor to Gemini, and is also drawing Gemini's researchers.

BOTH HEDGE THE CHANNEL THEY CONTROL. Anthropic, the compute buyer, reportedly discussed a custom AI chip collaboration with Samsung to diversify its compute stack — while affirming the Google/Amazon/Nvidia dependency in the same breath. Google, the compute seller, reportedly tapped AMD to co-design a next-generation TPU pairing its proprietary accelerator with on-package general-purpose CPU cores for CPU-heavy workloads such as reinforcement learning. Neither hedge exits the relationship; each adds a second supplier inside a dependency both are simultaneously deepening.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN:
- It does NOT claim the $40B is a compute prepayment, or that any portion returns to Google as TPU revenue. That structure is COMMON in such deals but is NOT stated by any source here, and must not be inferred from the co-occurrence of the investment and the supply relationship.
- It does NOT claim the researcher departures were caused by, or are connected to, the investment.
- It does NOT claim Google has lost the talent competition. Two named Gemini departures plus a 'broader wave' is what is reported; no net-flow figure appears.
- The 'up to' in 'up to $40 billion' is a ceiling, not a committed or deployed amount.

SOURCING IS WEAK ON THREE OF FIVE INPUTS AND THE READER SHOULD NOT TREAT THIS AS FIRM. The Samsung talks are reported at confidence 0.55 with no recorded sources; the AMD hybrid TPU at 0.6 from one source; and the talent claim comes from a mid-2026 roundup-shaped synthesis at confidence 0.5. Both hedges are REPORTEDLY-framed and neither is confirmed by the companies. Only the investment and the TPU 8t/8i launch are firmly sourced.
