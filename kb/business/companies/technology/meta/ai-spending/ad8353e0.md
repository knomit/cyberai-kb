# Meta has quietly become one of Microsoft's largest AI customers, spending hundreds of millions a year on Azure

Per Bloomberg reporting dated 2026-08-20, Meta spends hundreds of millions of dollars per year with Microsoft Azure for access to AI models, making it one of Microsoft's largest AI customers — notable given Meta operates its own large-scale AI training infrastructure and open-weights model program.
