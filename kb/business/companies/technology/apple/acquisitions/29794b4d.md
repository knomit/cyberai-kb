# Apple acquired Israeli audio AI startup Q.ai in January 2026

In January 2026 Apple quietly acquired Q.ai, an Israeli audio AI startup. The acquisition is read as a possible route for Apple to close the gap with AI-native dictation systems such as Google's Rambler and the Wispr Flow app, since Siri's dictation has lagged both.
