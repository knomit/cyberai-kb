# SambaNova raises $1B at $11B valuation

AI inference-chip maker SambaNova raised $1 billion at an $11 billion valuation (reported by TLDR AI and The Deep View, Jul 8, 2026), positioning it as a challenger to Nvidia; the company is reportedly considering an IPO in 2027.
