# South Korea's Kakao is spinning its chat platform business into a new KakaoAI unit

Reuters reported on 2026-08-21 that South Korea's Kakao Corp will spin off its chat-app-based platform business into a new unit called KakaoAI, with a relisting planned for 2027.
