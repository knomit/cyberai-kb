# Gemini 3.5 Pro reportedly delayed; Alphabet shares fell 4%

Alphabet shares fell 4% on 16 July 2026 after a report said Google had delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro while working to improve its performance, particularly for coding. Google said the model remained in partner testing alongside an upgraded Flash model and other releases.
