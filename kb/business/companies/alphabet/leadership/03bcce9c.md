# Google DeepMind leadership reshuffle: Hassabis elevated, Jeff Dean departs

In August 2026 Demis Hassabis moved to chair of Google DeepMind and chief scientist of Alphabet. Jeff Dean departed Google after 27 years to launch a venture called Discovery Loop. Alphabet shares fell more than 5% following the announcement.
