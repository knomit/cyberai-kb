# Hugging Face reportedly explored a sale at a $13 billion valuation, nearly triple its 2023 mark

Hugging Face reportedly worked with a bank to gauge buyer interest at a valuation of $13 billion or more — close to triple its 2023 valuation. The potential price is attributed to the value of its model hub, developer ecosystem, and AI infrastructure. No agreement had been reached as of reporting.
