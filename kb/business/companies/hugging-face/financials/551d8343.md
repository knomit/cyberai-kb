# Hugging Face's ~$150M annualized revenue implies an ~80x forward multiple in the Nvidia deal

At the time of Nvidia's reported $12.9 billion agreement to acquire it, Hugging Face had generated only about $150 million in annualized revenue, making the purchase price roughly 80 times forward revenue, according to The Information's reporting relayed by The Deep View.
