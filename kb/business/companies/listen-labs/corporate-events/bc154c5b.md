# Listen Labs scrubbed a $125M round for Salesforce acquisition talks

Listen Labs, a voice-AI market-research startup with roughly $30 million ARR, scrubbed a signed $125 million Series C term sheet at a $1.5 billion valuation amid acquisition talks with Salesforce reportedly worth about $2 billion.
