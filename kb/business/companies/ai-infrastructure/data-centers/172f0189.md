# Nvidia to provide up to $105 billion in financing for OpenAI's Ohio data center

On 2026-08-17 news broke that Nvidia would provide up to $105 billion in financing for OpenAI's data center in Ohio, with the lease also backed by SB Energy. In its accompanying blog post Nvidia framed AI factories as the "defining infrastructure" of the AI era and said "compute is revenue," positioning itself as responsible for helping secure those resources for labs facing compute constraints.
