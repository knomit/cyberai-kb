# Crusoe positions vertical integration and Managed Intelligence for enterprise AI inference

In a sponsored August 22, 2026 edition of The Deep View, Crusoe described a strategy of owning every layer of its stack — energy, data centers and cloud infrastructure — to make high-performance inference cheaper and faster. Michael Yen-Chi Ho, director of technical product marketing, claimed Crusoe's MemoryAlloy technology delivers 9.9x faster time-to-first-token and 5x greater throughput than vLLM on OpenAI's GPT-OSS-120B (vendor claim, not independently verified). Crusoe's Managed Intelligence portfolio, centered on its Intelligence Foundry, comprises Serverless Inference, Tailored Deployments, Self-serve Deployments and Serverless Fine-Tuning. The company also says it built an open-source high-performance tokenizer called 'fastokens' that has been integrated into vLLM, SGLang and NVIDIA Dynamo.
