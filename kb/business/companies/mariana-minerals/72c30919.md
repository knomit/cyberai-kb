# Mariana Minerals raised $310M Series B led by Khosla Ventures for critical minerals

Mariana Minerals, which mines critical minerals for 'modern energy, AI, and defense,' raised a $310 million Series B led by Khosla Ventures (valuation undisclosed). Its software, MarianaOS, uses AI and machine learning to cut mining execution timelines in half. Khosla Ventures founder Vinod Khosla framed critical minerals as deciding 'whether America builds its own future or keeps depending on China.' Reported by The Deep View / PR Newswire, Aug 2026.
