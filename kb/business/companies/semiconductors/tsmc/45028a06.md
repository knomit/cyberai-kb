# TSMC committed an additional $100B to Arizona, raising its US pipeline to $265B

TSMC is accelerating its Arizona factory build-out, citing a multi-year AI demand 'megatrend.' Per its CFO, TSMC is committing an additional $100 billion to expand its US chipmaking footprint, raising its total Arizona investment pipeline to $265 billion.
