# Sphinx.ai launches Sphinx 2.0 enterprise AI accuracy layer

Sphinx.ai, a seed-stage startup backed by Lightspeed and Bessemer Venture Partners and co-founded/led by CEO Rohan Kodialam, unveiled Sphinx 2.0 (reported in a sponsored June 2026 edition of The Deep View). The product is an 'institutional knowledge layer' that sits between an enterprise's AI tools and its data, filtering every AI query for accuracy and continuously monitoring/refining outputs to reflect business context, and surfacing detected discrepancies for human review. It reframes Sphinx's earlier approach (an agent that fixed organizational data to make it AI-ready) toward getting 'your AI ready for your data,' aiming to reduce model hallucination on business-specific queries without costly data migration. Note: source is a sponsored newsletter feature.
