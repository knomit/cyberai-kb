# Anthropic planned to let enterprise customers hold required 30-day data retention on their own cloud

Per Reuters reporting dated 2026-08-20 (sourced to a person familiar with the matter), Anthropic planned to change its enterprise data retention policy so that enterprise customers could keep the required 30-day data retention on their own cloud infrastructure rather than Anthropic's.
