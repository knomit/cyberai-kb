# IDC: Global AI infrastructure investment to hit $1T by 2030; ~1B agents by 2029

IDC (presented at IDC CIO Summit, New York, May 2026) projects global AI infrastructure investment could reach $1 trillion by 2030, including a fivefold increase in enterprise spending. By 2029, IDC projects AI will be in production across most business functions with nearly one billion AI agents operating worldwide. Current bottleneck: capacity shortage keeping inference costs high. Meredith Whalen (IDC Chief Research & Product Officer): 'as soon as they start to adopt AI at scale, they've got to have the price per inference at a reasonable level, or the business case won't be there.' Key enterprise blockers identified: (1) Rethinking business processes and workflows for an agentic future. (2) Change management and people — organizations further ahead spent significant effort here. IDC characterizes the next phase of AI agents as 'fundamentally different' from prior years — actual workflow reshaping rather than just productivity assistance.
