# Poolside AI struck a $6 billion non-exclusive licensing deal with Nvidia

AI coding startup Poolside AI agreed a non-exclusive licensing deal with Nvidia valued at $6 billion. As part of the arrangement, 109 Poolside employees received offers to leave the startup and join Nvidia. Reported by journalist Eric Newcomer on 2026-08-20.
