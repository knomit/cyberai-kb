# SoftBank raised an upsized $11.9 billion bank loan to continue funding OpenAI

In September 2026 SoftBank borrowed nearly $12 billion from roughly 20 banks to keep funding OpenAI, exceeding the $10 billion it originally sought. Masayoshi Son was still targeting close to $65 billion into OpenAI by October 2026 even after Altman shelved a 2026 IPO. SoftBank shares fell as much as 13% on Monday 2026-09-14 on concern over the debt pile.
