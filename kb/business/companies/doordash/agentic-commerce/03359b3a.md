# DoorDash has received only 'several hundred' AI-agent orders versus 3M daily deliveries

DoorDash CEO Tony Xu said the company has received 'several hundred' orders from AI agents while processing about 3 million deliveries daily; most orders still come from the DoorDash app. DoorDash is building its own agents and considering advertising within agent platforms (TechCrunch, Oct 30, 2026).
