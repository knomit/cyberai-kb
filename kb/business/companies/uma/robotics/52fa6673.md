# UMA unveils first humanoid robot, building physical AI in Europe

UMA, a physical-AI startup founded in 2025 by CEO Remi Cadène (a former Tesla Optimus staff scientist), unveiled the design of its first humanoid robot in early July 2026. UMA is developing two systems: a dual-arm mobile industrial robot for warehouses/assembly lines, and a compact humanoid for human-centric spaces. Its differentiator is a 'Real-Time Learning' architecture letting robots learn by demonstration rather than manual programming, improving over time. Unusually, UMA builds in Europe (vs. US/China), and its team includes people from NYU, Google DeepMind, and LeRobot, with backing from figures including Yann LeCun and Olivier Pommel.
