# Altman: OpenAI accepts more risk than Anthropic; rejects 'catastrophic loss of control' framing

In a Politico Decoded interview (published 2026-10-04) Sam Altman said OpenAI has a higher tolerance for risk than Anthropic, arguing the benefits of AI justify some adverse impacts, favors a 'lighter-touch regulatory stance', and rejects the notion that AI presents 'really catastrophic risks' including serious loss of control. Anthropic's Dario Amodei essay 'We Must Pace the Frontier' argues capabilities could outrun ability to understand and control systems. Both companies nonetheless signed the Trump administration's voluntary superintelligence accord the prior week. Misreading to avoid: the two CEOs agree on some governance calls to action but differ materially on catastrophic-risk beliefs.
