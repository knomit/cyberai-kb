# Meta's Muse personal AI agent topped the US App Store charts ahead of ChatGPT

Meta's Muse personal AI agent reached the top of the US App Store charts in September 2026, ranking above ChatGPT. App-store rank measures recent download velocity, not installed base, revenue, or retention — so this does NOT establish that Muse has more users than ChatGPT or that it is displacing it. It does establish that Meta's distribution and launch push produced a step change in acquisition, which is the relevant signal for anyone tracking consumer AI-assistant market entry.
