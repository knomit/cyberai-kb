# Meta One is a subscription bundling AI features across Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp from $2.99/month

Meta announced Meta One in September 2026, a subscription service offering enhanced AI and self-expression features across Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. It includes single-product plans as well as bundled offerings for individuals, creators and businesses, starting at $2.99 per month.
