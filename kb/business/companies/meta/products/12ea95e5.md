# Meta launches Facebook Verified selfie-based authenticity badge

Meta announced 'Facebook Verified', a free badge confirming a profile belongs to a real person rather than an AI-generated fake. Users record a short video selfie checked against existing profile photos; accounts must meet trust and safety standards to qualify.
