# Ray-Ban Meta Audio: camera-free AI glasses at $349

Meta's Ray-Ban Meta Audio are its first AI glasses with open-ear audio only (no camera). Per The Deep View's hands-on: 8-9 g lighter than Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3, $100 cheaper at $349, claimed 12 hours battery plus 48 more from the case, two open-ear speakers, 6-mic array, Meta AI assistant. Trade-off: no camera removes the visual context that makes AI wearables more useful.
