# Meta launches Enterprise Platform; ex-MongoDB CEO CJ Desai to lead

On 2026-09-28 Meta launched its Meta Enterprise Platform packaging the Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API and Muse Code. Chirantan 'CJ' Desai, formerly CEO of MongoDB, joins as Chief Enterprise Platform Officer. The launch coincides with reports of Muse privacy/security problems, including a Reuters-reported 'human concierge' test that Meta said it rolled back.
