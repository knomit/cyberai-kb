# Meta's 'Watermelon' model reportedly matches GPT-5.5 on benchmarks

Meta's superintelligence chief Alexandr Wang said the company's upcoming model, codenamed Watermelon, has caught up with OpenAI's GPT-5.5 on closely followed AI benchmarks. The model is still in training and reportedly uses an order of magnitude more compute than Meta's Muse Spark; no release timeline was given. Reported via TLDR AI, July 3, 2026.
