# Meta planning a cloud business to sell surplus AI compute

Meta Platforms is developing an internal cloud infrastructure initiative to sell access to its surplus AI computing power and hosted models to external developers, aiming to create a new revenue stream from its multi-billion-dollar data center and chip investments and to challenge hyperscalers AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. Reported by TLDR AI, July 2, 2026.
