# Google DeepMind leadership shakeup (Aug 2026)

On Wednesday (~Aug 5, 2026), Google DeepMind announced major leadership changes amid falling behind OpenAI and Anthropic in advanced models and agents. Chief AI architect Koray Kavukcuoglu was elevated to SVP of Google DeepMind, reporting directly to CEO Sundar Pichai. Demis Hassabis shifted from CEO to chairman of DeepMind and chief scientist of Alphabet, focusing on AGI and scientific discovery. Jeff Dean is leaving Google entirely to launch a new startup called Discovery Loop. Context: Google struggled to launch Gemini 3.5 Pro and lagged on agentic coding tools in 2026.
