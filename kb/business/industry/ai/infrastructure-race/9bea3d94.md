# Amazon holds lead over Google in AI power and data center race

In the hyperscaler race for electricity to power AI, Amazon holds an incumbent advantage from two decades of data center construction and is expected to add the most US data center and power capacity through 2030, though Google is projected to significantly close the gap by that time.
