# Coursera unveils Project Helix, an AI-native enterprise learning platform

On Wednesday 2026-09-09 at its FWD 2026 event, Coursera unveiled Project Helix, an AI-native learning platform for enterprises built around real-time skill insights, AI-guided adaptive learning paths generated from natural language prompts by both leaders and learners, personalization based on goal, role and demonstrated capability, and continuous assessment via practical observation and recognized credentials to produce portable proof of skills. CEO Greg Hart told The Deep View that Helix 'uses AI not just to deliver more content, but to create deeply personalized, adaptive learning paths and portable proof of skills, so companies can actually close the gap between AI infrastructure and talent readiness.' Two things to keep straight: Helix is not broadly available — general enterprise availability is expected in the first half of 2027 — and it does not replace existing course formats, which remain available. Hart said Helix is only possible because of the Coursera–Udemy combination completed in May 2026, and gave the reason the fit works: before combining, Coursera's business was two-thirds consumer and one-third enterprise, while Udemy's was the exact inverse. The claim that Helix closes a talent-readiness gap is the CEO's framing of a product not yet in general release.
