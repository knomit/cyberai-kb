# Music industry and AI: labels settle and partner while litigating (Suno, Udio, 2026)

Per The Deep View (2026-10-15): Suno raised $400M at a $5.4B valuation in June 2026; Universal and Sony filed a second lawsuit in September over Suno's v6 model (alleging 62,000+ recordings); Warner, BMG and Believe partnered with Suno on v6 launch and BMG signed an alliance in August. Udio settled with Warner and Universal to build licensed platforms but remains in litigation with Sony (30,000+ recordings alleged); AFM sued Warner over the settlements. Deezer reported ~75,000 AI tracks uploaded daily (~44% of uploads) in April 2026; Tidal blocks royalties on 100% AI-generated tracks; YouTube announced AI-song labeling in mid-October 2026. Labels pursue both litigation and licensing at once.
