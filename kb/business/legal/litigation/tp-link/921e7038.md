# Five U.S. states now suing TP-Link Systems over router security and China ties

On 2026-10-06 Florida, Iowa, Montana and Nebraska sued TP-Link Systems, joining Texas (suit filed February 2026), for five states total. They allege the Irvine, California company misled buyers about router security and separation from China. TP-Link Systems was affiliated with Chinese company TP-Link Technologies until a 2024 restructuring; the suits do not name the Chinese company as defendant. The Florida, Montana and Nebraska complaints do not allege the Chinese government obtained customer data, framing it as a risk under Chinese law; they separately say state-backed hackers exploited TP-Link router flaws. TP-Link denies the claims and will fight in court.
