# US AI data center buildout implies $5T of mostly-debt financing and 55% revenue CAGR

Tom Tunguz's September 2026 analysis models US data center capacity expanding from 25 GW to 70 GW, requiring roughly $5 trillion, financed mostly by debt. That would represent about 34% growth in the entire US corporate bond market, raising the question of whether municipal bonds get pulled in. To service that debt, annual AI revenue would have to grow from roughly $150 billion to at least $1.2 trillion by 2030 — a sustained ~55% compound annual growth rate. This is a modeled projection with explicit assumptions, not a forecast of committed spend.
