# US AI data center buildout implies a $5T mostly-debt financing wave needing 55% annual AI revenue growth

Tom Tunguz's September 2026 analysis models US data center capacity expanding from 25 GW to 70 GW, requiring roughly $5 trillion in investment, financed mostly by debt. That would represent about 34% growth in the entire US corporate bond market, raising the question of whether municipal bonds get pulled in.

To service that debt, annual AI revenue would have to grow from roughly $150 billion to at least $1.2 trillion by 2030 — a sustained ~55% compound annual growth rate.

The load-bearing assumption is the financing structure, not the technology: the buildout's viability turns on debt service, so a revenue shortfall bites through the credit channel rather than through any technical failure.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this is a modeled projection with explicit assumptions, not a forecast of committed spend.
