# Ramp August 2026 AI spending index: Anthropic leads on adoption while enterprise token spend shifts away from the most capable models

ADOPTION SHARE. Ramp's August 2026 AI spending index found 43.5% of its U.S. business clients paid for Anthropic subscriptions or tokens versus 39.7% for OpenAI. xAI posted its fastest growth month since July 2025 (+0.94 pp month-over-month, roughly 4% of AI business spend), and open-source / Chinese model serving platforms such as Hugging Face reached 6.1% of Ramp AI customers.

TOKEN SPEND WITHIN VENDORS. Only about 6% of Anthropic token spend went to Fable 5, its most capable and most expensive model, while 25% of OpenAI tokens among Ramp's OpenAI customers went to GPT-5.6 Sol. Anthropic's less powerful and roughly half-priced Opus 5 has surpassed Fable 5 in business spending. Ramp lead economist Ara Kharazian said Fable 5 marked 'a new upper bound' for AI spend, a point at which more performance is not worth the price.

SUPPORTING CONTEXT. Gartner research separately projects that AI agents could raise inference costs up to fivefold. The overall pattern is credited with driving traction for small models, domain-specific models, open-source alternatives, and model routing.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN. Ramp's customer base SKEWS toward startups and small businesses, so these shares should not be read as the enterprise market as a whole. Low Fable 5 token share is attributed by Ramp to PRICE; it is not evidence about capability, and it does not show frontier models being displaced for work requiring sustained autonomy. Spend share is a budgeting fact, not a measure of delivered value or of cost per successful task, which no source in this cluster publishes.
