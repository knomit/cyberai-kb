# Ramp August 2026 index: Anthropic leads enterprise AI adoption but not on top models

Ramp's August 2026 AI spending index found 43.5% of its U.S. business clients paid for Anthropic subscriptions/tokens vs 39.7% for OpenAI. Only ~6% of Anthropic token spend went to Fable 5 (its most capable, most expensive model), which was less popular with businesses than OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol (25% of OpenAI tokens). Ramp's lead economist Ara Kharazian said Fable 5 marked 'a new upper bound' for AI spend where more performance is not worth the price. xAI posted its fastest growth month since July 2025 (+0.94 pp MoM, ~4% of AI business spend), and open-source/Chinese model serving platforms (e.g., Hugging Face) reached 6.1% of Ramp AI customers. Caveat: Ramp customers skew toward startups and small businesses.
