# Frontier AI access is segmenting into closed camps via rationing and whitelists

Tomasz Tunguz argues the frontier AI market is sorting itself into closed camps: rationing programs and export limits increasingly decide who may run the strongest models, enterprises are standardizing on one or two named vendors, and products ship with default models embedded. The consequence is that frontier AI is no longer a utility sold by the token — labs are developing whitelists and blacklists. This is an analyst thesis rather than a documented market structure.
