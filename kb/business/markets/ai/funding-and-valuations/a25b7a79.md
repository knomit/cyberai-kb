# AI funding datapoints, September 2026: OpenAI pre-IPO at $1.2T, Gates Foundation $1B, CADDi at $1.2B

Reported mid-September 2026: the Financial Times reported OpenAI is considering a pre-IPO funding round at a $1.2 trillion valuation; the New York Times reported the Gates Foundation pledged $1 billion to expand global AI access; and Fortune reported manufacturing AI firm CADDi raised $114 million at a $1.2 billion valuation in a Series D. Separately, Bloomberg reported Google DeepMind staffer Bilal Chughtai resigned over AI safety concerns, and Agility Robotics unveiled Digit 5, a humanoid designed to work safely alongside people.
