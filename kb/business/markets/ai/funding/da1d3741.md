# Arena (AI leaderboard) raises $200M at $3.1B valuation; Isomorphic in talks at ~$50B

Bloomberg reported (2026-10-08) AI model evaluator Arena raised $200M at a $3.1B valuation. Separately Isomorphic Labs was reported in funding talks at a valuation of at least $40B (Deep View headline says $50B); Manus parent Butterfly Effect raised $500M per CNBC.
