# Chinese AI firms saw heavy capital-raising and consolidation under regulatory scrutiny (July 2026)

In early-to-mid July 2026, Chinese AI companies showed a burst of capital and consolidation activity shaped by domestic regulators. MiniMax sought ~$1.9B via a share sale and convertible bond, while Tencent entered talks to take a large stake in AI agent startup Manus (~$2B valuation) after Chinese regulators struck down Meta's earlier acquisition of Manus and forced it to be unwound. Pattern: Chinese authorities are actively steering ownership of strategically important agentic-AI assets toward domestic players.
