# AI infrastructure supply chain is showing a classic bullwhip effect

Analysis of the 2026 AI infrastructure buildout describes a sequence of bottlenecks moving from GPU scarcity to storage constraints, raising costs across the supply chain. As demand for AI components surged, GPU prices spiked, server shipments declined, and memory manufacturers shifted production toward High Bandwidth Memory. The resulting supply-demand mismatch inflated hardware prices and data center construction costs, a pattern characteristic of the bullwhip effect.
