# Enterprise LLM spend is shifting away from the most powerful frontier models

Evidence in mid-2026 suggests enterprises are trading peak capability for efficiency. Data from payments platform Ramp indicated that Anthropic's most powerful model, Fable 5, accounted for only 6% of Anthropic token spend among Ramp customers, while OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol accounted for 25% of tokens used by OpenAI Ramp customers; Anthropic's less powerful Opus 5 has surpassed Fable 5 in business spending. Gartner research separately projects that AI agents could raise inference costs up to fivefold. This pattern is credited with driving traction for small models, domain-specific models, open-source alternatives, and model routing.
