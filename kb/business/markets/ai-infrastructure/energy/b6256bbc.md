# AI investment moved 'down the stack' into energy and critical minerals, with large nuclear, fusion and mining rounds (Aug 2026)

The Deep View reported on 4 August 2026 that AI investors are increasingly funding the ENERGY and MATERIALS layers of the AI stack rather than only models and chips.

NUCLEAR AND FUSION.
- Valar Atomics, building small modular nuclear reactors, raised a $1 billion Series B (Bloomberg reported a ~$6 billion valuation).
- Antares raised $470 million to build reactors for the US military.
- Commonwealth Fusion Systems raised $1 billion for small nuclear/fusion buildout.

CRITICAL MINERALS AND MATERIALS.
- Mariana Minerals, mining critical minerals for energy, AI and defense, raised a $310 million Series B.
- Minerals startups such as Terra AI (raised $20M) and EnergyX are attracting investor attention.

INVESTORS. Prominent firms including Sequoia, a16z, and Khosla Ventures are participating across such deals.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: these are single-outlet newsletter-reported round figures, not audited or confirmed closings, and the valuations cited are as-reported.
