# AI investment is shifting into energy and minerals, with large nuclear and fusion rounds

The Deep View reported that AI investors are increasingly funding the energy and materials layers of the AI stack rather than only models and chips. Recent examples: nuclear startup Antares raised $470 million to build reactors for the US military; Commonwealth Fusion Systems raised $1 billion for small nuclear/fusion buildout; and minerals startups such as Terra AI (raised $20M) and EnergyX are attracting investor attention. Prominent firms including Sequoia, a16z, and Khosla Ventures are participating. Aug 4, 2026.
