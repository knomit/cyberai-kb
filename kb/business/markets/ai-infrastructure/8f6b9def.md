# AI capital is moving down the stack into energy, nuclear, and critical-minerals startups

As of Aug 2026, top venture firms (Sequoia, a16z, Khosla Ventures) are increasingly funding the physical substrate layers of the AI stack — power generation and raw materials — rather than only models and chips. Concrete Aug 2026 rounds: Valar Atomics (small modular nuclear reactors) raised a $1B Series B led by Sequoia at a reported $6B valuation, and is building a waterless 30MW AI factory with Nvidia; Mariana Minerals (critical minerals, MarianaOS AI mining software) raised a $310M Series B led by Khosla Ventures; Antares raised $470M for reactors for the US military; Commonwealth Fusion Systems raised $1B; and minerals startups Terra AI ($20M) and EnergyX drew investment. The pattern: AI compute demand is pulling investment into energy and materials, framed partly as US strategic independence from China. This does NOT mean model/chip investment has declined — only that capital is broadening deeper into the stack.
