# AI data-center buildout implies a $5T debt wave needing 55% annual revenue growth

US data-center capacity is projected to expand from 25 to 70 gigawatts, requiring roughly $5 trillion in investment, mostly debt-financed. Servicing that debt would require annual AI revenue to grow from $150 billion to at least $1.2 trillion by 2030 — a 55% compound annual growth rate. The financing structure, not the technology, is the load-bearing assumption.
