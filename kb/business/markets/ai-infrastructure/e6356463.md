# Mid-2026 AI compute buildout is drawing hundred-billion-dollar commitments across labs and chipmakers

As of late July 2026, multiple large capital commitments point to a coordinated surge in AI compute infrastructure: OpenAI raised planned infrastructure spending through 2030 to $750 billion (starting with a $20B, 3.2 GW Georgia data-center campus); AMD and Anthropic signed a deal for tens of billions in AI servers, with Anthropic buying up to 2 GW of AMD Instinct MI450 chips from H1 2027 and AMD investing up to $5B in Anthropic on milestones; and TSMC committed an additional $100B to its Arizona fabs (total US pipeline $265B), explicitly citing a multi-year AI demand 'megatrend.' The common thread is gigawatt-scale capacity planning and vertical entanglement between model developers, chipmakers, and data-center siting.
