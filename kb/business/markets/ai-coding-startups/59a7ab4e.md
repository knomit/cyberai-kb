# AI coding startups command soaring valuations in mid-2026

In August 2026, AI-assisted 'vibe coding' and AI coding-agent startups were attracting outsized private valuations. Lovable reached a $13B valuation (raising ~$400M, on track for a ~$600M revenue run rate by end of August 2026), while Cognition was reported in talks to raise ~$1B at a $40B valuation. Together these signal intense investor demand for AI software-development tools, though the specific figures are as-reported and, in Cognition's case, reflect in-progress talks rather than a closed round.
