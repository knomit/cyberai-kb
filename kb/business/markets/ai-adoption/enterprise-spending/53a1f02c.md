# Ramp AI Index shows enterprises trading down from frontier models

Ramp's monthly AI Index for August 2026 found US business AI adoption at 56.1% with growth slowing, per-employee AI spend down about 10% to $7,205, the effective price per million tokens down 41% to $0.68 from a March 2026 peak of $1.15, and frontier models' share of token usage falling to 45% from a 53% peak. Falling spend here reflects substitution toward cheaper older models, not falling usage.
