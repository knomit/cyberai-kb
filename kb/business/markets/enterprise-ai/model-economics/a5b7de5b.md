# Model routing is emerging as a distinct enterprise AI product category

Model routing became a distinct product category in mid-to-late 2026: Snowflake shipped dynamic model routing in its Cortex AI Gateway and in Snowflake CoCo and CoWork in mid-August 2026, and Replit made Intelligent Model Routing its default at end of August. Both target the same shift — enterprises choosing the right model for each task rather than the most powerful model available — alongside growth in niche, domain-specific, and small models. The open strategic question is what commoditization of frontier labs does to an industry whose economics assume perpetually growing AI spend.
