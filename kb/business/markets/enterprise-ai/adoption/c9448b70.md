# Wharton/GBK 2026 AI report: only 7% of organizations succeed on all three AI success markers

Wharton's fourth annual AI adoption report (with GBK Collective; 1,416 US enterprise leaders and employees) names three markers of AI success: proving value, transforming workflows, aligning the organization. Only 7% succeed on all three. 62% use AI daily, 91% multiple times a week, 73% report significantly changed workflows. About 40% judge ROI by hours saved; 27% track net-new revenue. 35% of departments tracking at least one growth measure report strongly positive returns vs 13% tracking efficiency only; 58% of managers with a newly designated Chief AI Officer report strongly positive ROI.
