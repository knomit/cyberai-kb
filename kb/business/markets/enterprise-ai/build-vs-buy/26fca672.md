# Enterprises are building their own domain-specific models rather than outsourcing the intelligence layer

As of September 2026, Salesforce (Koa), CrowdStrike and Thomson Reuters have each built their own AI models rather than relying solely on frontier lab APIs. The Deep View's analysis of the pattern: as models get smarter, the intelligence layer holds the greatest value inside an organization, so outsourcing it means losing control of the company's most important asset and potentially sending proprietary business information to a vendor that may also serve competitors. The economically viable path is not becoming a frontier lab but post-training an open model (Salesforce used Nvidia Nemotron 3 Super), which preserves ownership while avoiding pretraining cost. Domain-specific models typically reduce token costs and hallucinations and increase task performance because their expertise is narrower.
