# Menlo Ventures: enterprise AI spend hit $177B, 5x YoY; Anthropic 55% enterprise share

Menlo Ventures report (Nov 3, 2026; ~400 US enterprise decision makers): enterprise AI spending reached $177B, 5x YoY; deployment spending exceeds model development ($102.5B). OpenAI, Anthropic and Google hold 69% of the market (50% prior year); Anthropic 55% (from 40%), OpenAI 31% (from 27%), Google 9% (from 21%). Open-source models handle 19% of tokens (11% prior) but 4% of model spend. Agents account for 80% of OpenRouter tokens. Spend measures deployment, not realized ROI.
