# Kalshi is building prediction-market forward curves for AI compute prices

Kalshi has created a tool that plots the future price of computing power, using prediction markets to build a forward curve for GPU rental prices. The curve is based on weekly and monthly event contracts related to compute costs and extends up to a year into the future. The approach mirrors forward curves used for interest rates, and reflects compute becoming a commodity in its own right as demand grows.
