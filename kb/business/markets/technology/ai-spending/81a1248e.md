# Gartner projects 2026 AI spending near $2.6 trillion

Gartner projects global AI spending will reach nearly $2.6 trillion in 2026, up about 47% from the prior year. The Deep View cites this alongside enterprise 'AI sticker shock' as driving a shift away from 'tokenmaxxing' toward cost efficiency in model design.
