# Surging AI spend forecasts are pushing the industry toward cost efficiency

Independent analyst forecasts frame why 2026 AI strategy is turning from raw capability toward cost efficiency. Gartner projects global AI spending near $2.6 trillion in 2026 (up ~47% year over year), and IDC projects AI infrastructure investment could reach $1 trillion by 2030 with nearly one billion AI agents operating by 2029, while a capacity shortage keeps inference costs high. IDC's Meredith Whalen notes the enterprise business case collapses unless price-per-inference falls to reasonable levels. This macro pressure (enterprise 'AI sticker shock') is the backdrop for concrete cost moves such as Microsoft substituting cheaper in-house MAI models for licensed frontier models and DeepSeek designing its own inference chips.
