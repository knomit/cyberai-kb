# OpenAI's July-August 2026 model discounts produced a Jevons-paradox usage surge

OpenRouter data covering OpenAI discounts between July 27 and August 14, 2026 showed Luna token usage jumping 13.8x and Terra token usage rising 5.6x, while Sol — which stayed at list price — rose only 1.1x. Most of the share OpenAI gained came from other labs rather than cannibalizing its own models, and nearly a third of users who tried a discounted OpenAI model kept using it after the discounts expired.
