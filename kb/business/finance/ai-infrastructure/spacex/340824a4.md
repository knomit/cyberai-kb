# SpaceX closes $40B special-purpose-vehicle debt financing for GPUs, funding arrives Q1-Q2 2027

CNBC (via TLDR AI, 2026-10-26): SpaceX closed $40 billion of debt financing to buy GPUs/AI chips, mainly capitalizable debt, expected to arrive in Q1 and Q2 2027, structured through a special purpose vehicle to keep debt off SpaceX's balance sheet and preserve investment-grade ratings.
