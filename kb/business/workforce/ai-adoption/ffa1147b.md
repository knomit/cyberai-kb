# Workers exaggerate AI skills while quietly resisting adoption (GCheck study, 2026)

A GCheck study of 1,500 workers (May 2026) found 63% exaggerate their AI skills, rising to 80% among Gen Z. Despite the bravado, 81% admitted they discourage or limit AI use at work. GCheck CEO Houman Akhavan coined this 'double distortion' — overstating abilities while undermining adoption. Only 38% feel prepared to use AI tools effectively. 40% observed AI already partly doing their jobs. More than half of those who exaggerate never received any AI training. Companies using AI as a scapegoat for layoffs are feeding the problem. Enterprises don't get an accurate picture of deployment performance when workers' words don't reflect their actions.
