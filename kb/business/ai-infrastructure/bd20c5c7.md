# Mid-2026 AI buildout marked by trillion-dollar-scale capital and compute concentration

Mid-2026 reporting (The Deep View, TLDR AI) shows an AI infrastructure capital surge concentrated among a few players: OpenAI's reported $110B round (backed by SoftBank, Nvidia, Amazon), Nvidia targeting ~$1T in data-center revenue through 2027, SambaNova raising $1B at an $11B valuation as a Nvidia challenger, and Apple committing $30B to US-made Broadcom chips. The pattern is escalating capital intensity and vertical entanglement (chipmakers funding model labs that buy their chips).
