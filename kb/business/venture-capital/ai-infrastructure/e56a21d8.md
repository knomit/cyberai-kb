# AI investment moves 'down the stack' into nuclear and critical minerals

The Deep View reported (Aug 4, 2026) large funding rounds for AI-adjacent infrastructure: Valar Atomics, building small modular nuclear reactors, raised a $1 billion Series B (Bloomberg reported a ~$6 billion valuation); Mariana Minerals, mining critical minerals for energy/AI/defense, raised a $310 million Series B. Investors involved across such deals include Sequoia, a16z, and Khosla Ventures. Related nuclear raises cited: Antares ($470M) and Commonwealth Fusion Systems ($1B).
