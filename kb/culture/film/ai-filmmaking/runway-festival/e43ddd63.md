# Runway's 4th annual AI Film Festival showcased AI-generated short films

Runway held its fourth annual AI Film Festival (2026), showing the ten best AI-generated films selected from hundreds of submissions, spanning animation, stop-motion, and realistic styles. Runway's chief creative officer Jamie Umpherson told The Deep View the goal of AI is to 'raise the floor,' giving people without prior access to production tools the ability to create. Screenwriter Roger Avary, on a panel with Runway CEO Cristobal Valenzuela, argued the only people likely to lose jobs are 'the gatekeepers.'
