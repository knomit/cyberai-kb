# AI music backlash: imperfection and live performance gaining value as human signals

The Deep View (Oct 2026) reports musicians and listeners increasingly treat imperfection (sour notes, odd time signatures, no click tracks) and live performance as proof of human authorship, as AI-generated music is perceived as overly polished with tells like over-repetition and 'high-end hiss'. Examples: Angine de Poitrine's KEXP live set (~18M YouTube views), Armin van Buuren recording an electronic album as live piano. Experts quoted: Robert Rowe (NYU), Carmine-Emanuele Cella (UC Berkeley), David Das (SCL). Trend interpretation is from interviews, not measured data.
