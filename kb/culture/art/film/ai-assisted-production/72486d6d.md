# Audience acceptance of AI-generated imagery tracks whether a real-world referent exists

Reporting on the Google Flow film festival, The Deep View's Sabrina Ortiz observed a consistent pattern in her own and audiences' reactions: AI-generated oceans, people, and everyday objects were immediately off-putting because viewers have a real-world counterpart to compare against, whereas AI-generated monsters, aliens, and animations were accepted without friction. The practical heuristic is that generative video is most safely deployed on subjects with no real-world referent, and riskiest on the mundane.
