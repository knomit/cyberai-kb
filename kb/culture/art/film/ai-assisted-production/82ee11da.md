# Hybrid shot composition in an AI-assisted short film: 20 live-action, 32 hybrid, 5 fully generated

Filmmaker and creative director Andrew Nethery's Google Flow Sessions short 'Nothin' Beats a Cheeseburger' (an alien searching Earth for the best cheeseburger) used a real diner set and two live actors. Its final shot breakdown was 20 purely live-action shots, 32 hybrid shots containing AI-generated elements, and 5 fully AI-generated shots with no live action. This ratio is a concrete data point on how AI video generation is actually being integrated into professional production: predominantly as an augmentation layer over practical photography rather than as a wholesale replacement.
