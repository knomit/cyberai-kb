# Old Models Foundation lets users run historical image-generation models

Import AI 474 notes the Old Models Foundation site offers access to historical image models from Efros-Leung texture synthesis (1999) through VAEs, DRAW, DeepDream, DCGAN, pix2pix, BigGAN, StyleGAN, VQGAN+CLIP, Stable Diffusion, ControlNet and SDXL 1.0 (2023), limited to 25 generations per account per day.
