# Stanford HAI: AI therapy chatbots can reinforce stigma and give dangerous responses

Research from Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute (cited by The Deep View, Jul 2026) found AI therapy chatbots can contribute to harmful stigma and dangerous responses, making them less effective than human therapists.
