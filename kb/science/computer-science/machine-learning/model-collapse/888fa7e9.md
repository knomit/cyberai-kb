# Study: training LLMs on AI-generated data reduces performance vs human data

A study by US and China university researchers published in Management Science (May 2026) found that training a large language model on AI-generated data reduced performance compared to authentic human data; using outputs from multiple models and only the highest-quality synthetic data helped but did not fully close the gap.
