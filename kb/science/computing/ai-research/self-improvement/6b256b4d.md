# Google RRSI regularizes recursive self-improvement of agent harnesses to reduce benchmark overfitting

Google published RRSI (regularized recursive self-improvement), a method that constrains how AI agent harnesses recursively modify themselves to reduce benchmark overfitting and favor changes that transfer to new tasks. Across eight benchmarks it improved out-of-distribution performance while using fewer policy tokens.
