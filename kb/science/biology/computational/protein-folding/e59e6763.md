# ESMFold2 predicts molecular shapes by treating molecules as language

A team at Biohub and EvolutionaryScale released ESMFold2 (6.2 billion parameters), which infers shapes of proteins, DNA, RNA, and binding molecules. Unlike AlphaFold 3, it can embed individual molecules directly via a transformer (the ESMC embedding model, trained to fill masked tokens across ~2.8 billion sequences) rather than requiring a multiple sequence alignment (MSA). Without MSAs it outperforms competitors like Chai-1 (0.85 vs 0.81 lDDT on protein shape); with MSAs it matches AlphaFold3 (0.89 lDDT). Weights are available on Hugging Face and via a free website, with API access through Biohub.
