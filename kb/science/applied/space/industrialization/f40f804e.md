# 2016 NASA KSC paper: bootstrapping lunar industry needs 12-41 MT launched, ~$1.5-4.2B via Starship

A 2016 paper from NASA Kennedy Space Center researchers (Affordable, Rapid Bootstrapping of the Space Industry and Solar System Civilization, arXiv) proposes bootstrapping a self-sustaining robotic lunar industry across six manufacturing generations. Baseline max rate needs 41 MT launched to the Moon; a reduced-rate case needs 12 MT with industry two orders of magnitude smaller and benefits delayed ~8 years. Import AI 480 estimates 12-41 MT could reach the Moon via multiple Starship launches and refuelings for roughly $1.5bn-$4.2bn at list prices (Jack Clark's napkin math, not the paper's).
