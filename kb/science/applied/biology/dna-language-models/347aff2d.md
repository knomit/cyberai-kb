# Radical Numerics launches with $50M and Omnii genome language model

Radical Numerics, an AI research lab spun out of Stanford pursuing 'general biological intelligence,' emerged from stealth in June 2026 with $50 million in seed funding and announced Omnii, a proprietary 'genome language model' for researchers in research preview. CEO/co-founder Eric Nguyen said its earlier open-source models Evo and Evo 2 were the first AI models able to both read and write DNA at scale, and were used to design novel CRISPR systems and create the first complete AI-designed genome (a bacteriophage). Omnii was kept proprietary, unlike Evo, to control access amid bioweapon-misuse concerns.
