# US DOE and Arcee AI announced Genesis-Science-1 (GS1), an open-weight scientific model

The US Department of Energy, in collaboration with Arcee AI, announced Genesis-Science-1 (GS1), an open-weight AI model aimed at enhancing scientific computing workflows while maintaining reproducible records. Arcee AI leads model development while DOE scientists provide domain expertise, data, and research tasks. The initiative solicits outside contributions for pretraining, post-training, and evaluation; the first contribution window closes August 6.
