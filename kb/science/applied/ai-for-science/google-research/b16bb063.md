# Google Research uses AI agents (ERA) for scientific discovery

Lizzie Dorfman leads science AI at Google Research, where the team built ERA (Empirical Research Assistants) — coding agents that use a tree-search methodology to explore hundreds of thousands of computational approaches to a problem. In one epidemiological forecasting example they generated 200,000 candidate models (top-scoring in CDC competitions), most discarded automatically. Dorfman says human expertise and physical-validity verification remain essential, and that the team has produced roughly 45 Nature and Science papers over four years.
