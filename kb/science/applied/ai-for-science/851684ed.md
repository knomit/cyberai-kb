# MIT CSAIL: AI cost of solving theory research questions falling

MIT CSAIL paper 'AI Disruption and Falling Costs in Theory Research' compared models released Dec 2024 to Apr 2026 on questions drawn from 254 recent arXiv papers across seven domains (math, physics, economics) with human ground-truth answers. Share of problems reaching the authors' conclusion rose from 3% (o1) to 35% (GPT-5.5); about $5 per attempt, ~$15 per success for GPT-5.5; compute per full-marks answer fell from ~$170 to ~$15 (excluding human labor and verification); AI cost estimated under 0.3% of a human expert's. Researchers expect scientists to shift to complementary tasks like verification.
