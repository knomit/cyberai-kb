# Google outlines vision for AI-driven scientific discovery

In a Deep View Conversations interview, Yossi Matias, head of Google Research, described Google's vision for AI-driven science. Google is building AI systems to amplify (not replace) scientists, including Gemini for Science, AI Co-Scientist, AlphaEvolve, and an Empirical Research Assistant, to search papers, generate hypotheses, and accelerate experiments. Google is partnering with universities including Stanford and Imperial College.
