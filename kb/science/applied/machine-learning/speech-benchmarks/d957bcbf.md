# TURNBENCH: 30-hour six-domain benchmark for turn-taking in spoken dialogue

TURNBENCH is a benchmark for turn-taking dynamics in spoken dialogue, covering end-of-turn detection and interruption detection across six domains with 30 hours of evaluation data, accompanied by a 104-hour training set. It targets a capability that text benchmarks cannot measure — when a speech system should start and stop talking — which is distinct from transcription or generation quality.
