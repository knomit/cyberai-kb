# Mode-hopping: abrupt response-pattern switches during LM pre-training

A paper terms MODE-HOPPING the abrupt switching of response patterns during pre-training; observed in OLMo3-32B on arithmetic tasks as sudden shifts between pattern-following and correct task inference. Controlled experiments suggest competing shallow and generalizable circuits; longer training does not always guarantee better generalization.
