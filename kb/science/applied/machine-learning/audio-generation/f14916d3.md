# StepAudio 3 Gen unifies speech, music and sound effects in one autoregressive model

The StepAudio 3 technical report describes StepAudio 3 Gen, which uses discrete autoregressive modeling over shared RVQ (residual vector quantization) audio tokens to generate speech, voices, vocals, sound effects, music and mixed audio within a single model. It reports state-of-the-art results on text-to-speech and voice design while retaining broad general-audio generation capability — the notable claim being that the specialization did not cost it generality.
