# Sakana AI 'Smart Cellular Bricks' show collective intelligence in physical hardware

Sakana AI extended its collective-intelligence research into physical hardware with 'Smart Cellular Bricks.' The bricks use local communication and neural networks to autonomously classify and reconstruct 3D shapes without centralized control. In experiments the system achieved high accuracy in shape classification and was robust against noise and module failures.
