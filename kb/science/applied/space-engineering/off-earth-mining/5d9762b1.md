# Six-stage framework for off-Earth mining with space robots

A multi-institution paper (Chinese Academy of Sciences, TU Munich, Beihang, Wuhan University, University of Würzburg, Shenzhen University, China University of Mining and Technology, and others) lays out six stages for off-Earth mining: (1) orbital/telescopic prospecting, (2) in-situ robotic exploration, (3) single-robot small-scale sampling, (4) multi-robot large-scale excavation, (5) autonomous extraction and refinement of volatiles/metals/water ice, and (6) integration into in-situ resource utilization or terrestrial transport. The paper's notable claim is that hardware is largely on a derisking path while data and software are the binding constraints.
