# Space-robotics datasets are the binding constraint on autonomous off-Earth mining

The off-Earth mining survey finds space robot datasets are 'characterized by extreme scarcity and frequent quality discontinuities', making data — not hardware — the main obstacle. Identified needs: momentum-aware multimodal simulators that decouple surface interaction from gravitational dependence; foundation models providing 'embodied geological intelligence' with world models merging geometric-semantic mapping and predictive material response; and closed-loop validation linking simulation, terrestrial emulation, and extraterrestrial deployment, since microgravity, vacuum, thermal cycling, radiation, and regolith behaviors are rarely reproduced together.
