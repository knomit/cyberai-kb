# Outer Biosciences trains AI models on living donated human skin tissue

Outer Biosciences, a startup associated with Michael Polansky, keeps donated human skin tissue alive, measures how it responds to applied compounds, and trains models on the resulting biological response data — an approach to generating proprietary wet-lab training data rather than relying on public datasets.
