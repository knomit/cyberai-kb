# Google open-sources WeatherNext cyclone forecasting models

Google's WeatherNext is an AI weather model achieving state-of-the-art accuracy in predicting a cyclone's track, intensity, and wind structure, giving forecasters on average an extra day of predictive accuracy versus current methods. Google open-sourced WeatherNext 2 and WeatherNext Cyclones models. WeatherNext predicted Hurricane Melissa's rapid intensification and landfall in Jamaica during the 2025 hurricane season, enabling earlier warnings.
