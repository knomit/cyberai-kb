# METR finds AI acceleration of science is uneven across cyber, math, and AI research

An August 2026 METR research note examined whether LLMs have accelerated discovery in three domains. Findings: (1) Cyber vulnerabilities show major acceleration — the rate of reported vulnerabilities accelerated dramatically in 2026 vs 2025 both for specific projects (cURL, OpenSSL, Firefox, Microsoft) and aggregate databases (US NVD, OSV). (2) Mathematics research shows minor but hard-to-measure acceleration — arXiv submissions doubled in some areas in under 12 months, and problems such as the Jacobian conjecture from Smale's list, Problem 44 from Green's list (the halving sieve), and the sofic half of Green's Problem 100 have been solved. (3) Optimization of AI research shows no measurable acceleration across seven benchmark problem areas (CIFAR-10, Hutter compression, Gurobi MIP, MIPLIB, nanoGPT, Stockfish, matrix-multiplication exponent), with only nanoGPT and CIFAR-10 showing LLM-attributable contributions. The pattern suggests 'lumpy' differential acceleration rather than uniform speedup.
