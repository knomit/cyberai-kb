# DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas covering all single-nucleotide variants

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas, a predictive map of the regulatory effect of every possible single-DNA-letter change in the human genome — a 1-petabyte database covering all 9 billion possible single-nucleotide variants.
