# AlphaGenome Atlas precomputes effects for all 9 billion human SNVs

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas on 2026-09-08, a roughly 1-petabyte database of predicted molecular and regulatory effects for all 9 billion possible single-nucleotide variants in the human genome (three possible substitutions at each of ~3 billion positions). It is over 30 times larger than the AlphaFold Database. The Atlas introduces the AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score, a single combined metric spanning both coding and non-coding regions, letting researchers rank candidate variants quickly. It is free for academic research through a web portal. These are model PREDICTIONS, not experimentally measured effects — the Atlas prioritizes hypotheses rather than establishing causation.
