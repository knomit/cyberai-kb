# OpenAI says an internal model solved the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize problem; formed math advisory group

OpenAI stated (Sept 2026) that a new internal model solved the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize problem and over 100 other open mathematical problems, and in response established an independent advisory group of mathematicians to assess, advise on and communicate math-related AI capabilities. This is OpenAI's claim as reported; independent verification/acceptance by the mathematical community (e.g., Clay Institute) was not reported at the time.
