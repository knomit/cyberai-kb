# OpenAI internal model produces finite-time singularity proof for Navier-Stokes

On Tuesday 2026-09-08, OpenAI announced that an unreleased internal model 'significantly more capable than GPT-6 Astra' produced a proof that Navier-Stokes fluid dynamics can develop a singularity in finite time — a fluid's speed growing without bound — meaning viscosity does not always keep fluid motion well behaved. Roughly 10,000 agents were used in a coordinated run of about 88 hours.

TWO QUALIFICATIONS MATTER AND ARE EASY TO LOSE: OpenAI says the result establishes statements C and D of the Millennium Prize formulation and OpenAI will not claim the $1 million prize; and the Clay Mathematics Institute has not verified or accepted the result, so it is a developing claim rather than a settled theorem — though the proof was formally checked in Lean, which gives mathematicians substantial confidence in its correctness.

OpenAI mathematician Ven Chandrasekaran noted in the press briefing that these equations underlie aerodynamics and weather forecasting.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the Navier-Stokes Millennium Problem is not closed in the prize's full sense.
