# PSI research system with a non-public Anthropic model proves Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture

Physical Superintelligence (PSI), an AI science startup, reports that an internally developed research system running a non-public Anthropic model found a proof of the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture (open since 2008), which concerns when quantum computers can be exponentially faster than classical ones. The system had a complete proof on day three of the run; the Lean proof assistant accepted it, and the system improved the result twice more on related open problems, with Lean accepting the final version. TLDR AI summarizes the result as showing quantum computers are not exponentially faster on random inputs. Jack Clark (Import AI 480) frames it as an example of automated researchers extending the frontier of knowledge. Claim is from PSI's own blog.
