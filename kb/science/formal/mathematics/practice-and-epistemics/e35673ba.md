# Bryna Kra argues AI has broken mathematics' use of scarce deep theorems as a proxy for deep understanding

In a September 2026 guest post on Terence Tao's blog, Bryna Kra argues that mathematics long relied on an implicit signal — deep theorems were scarce and difficult, so producing one demonstrated deep thought — and that AI has broken this mechanism, because models now produce polished proofs faster than experts can digest them. The consequence is that the discipline needs a different way to identify genuine understanding.
