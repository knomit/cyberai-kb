# The Foundation for Science and AI Research (SAIR) launched an open math model initiative independent of major AI companies

The Foundation for Science and AI Research (SAIR) was founded with private donors as a non-profit to support responsible uses of AI in mathematics and other sciences INDEPENDENTLY of the major AI companies — independence from industry funding is the organizing premise, not an incidental detail. Its Open Math Model Initiative plans to develop open models with the mathematical community, with participation open across institutions, regions, and career stages. The initiative is in early stages and is collecting expressions of interest, and is seeking partners for funding, compute, expertise, or community-building. Announced by Terence Tao on 2026-09-18. Collecting expressions of interest is NOT the same as having models, compute, or funding secured.
