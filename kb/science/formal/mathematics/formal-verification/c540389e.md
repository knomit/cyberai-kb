# Axiom Math completes computer-checked proof of Classification of Finite Simple Groups in Lean

Per TLDR AI (2026-10-28), Axiom Math released the first complete computer-checked proof of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups (CFSG), whose original proof spans roughly 15,000 pages in 500+ articles by ~100 mathematicians. Dozens of AI agents were directed over 24 days, producing about 43.1 million lines of Lean code.
