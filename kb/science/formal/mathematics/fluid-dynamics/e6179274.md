# OpenAI internal model claimed a Navier-Stokes finite-time singularity proof

OpenAI said an unreleased internal model, running roughly 10,000 agents, proved that the Navier-Stokes equations can develop a fluid-speed singularity in finite time — a result bearing on one of the Millennium Prize Problems. The claim is OpenAI's own and was reported via newsletter coverage rather than a peer-reviewed publication.
