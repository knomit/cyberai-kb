# Claude improves Riemann hypothesis zero-density lower bound

Anthropic reported that its Claude model improved the lower bound on the proportion of nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function satisfying the Riemann hypothesis from 41.6% to 67.2%. Claude attempted ~650 ideas and coordinated multiple subagents to run numerical checks and re-prove the finding; two mathematicians plus a formal validation confirmed the result.
