# Study: retrieval is the weakest link for web-search LLMs answering news

Mirac Suzgun and colleagues at Stanford University and Together AI tested six popular web-search-equipped LLMs on daily news questions (Feb 9-22, 2026) in six languages. Top models exceeded 90% accuracy on well-formed English questions but underperformed in other languages (worst in Hindi, ~79.3%). Most errors stemmed from retrieval rather than reasoning: 38.8% from retrieval failures and 32.7% from retrieving topically relevant but 'smart but wrong' sources. The authors conclude improving retrieval may yield larger gains than scaling model parameters for timely queries.
