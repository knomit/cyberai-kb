# Melanie Mitchell argues AI should be studied as "alien intelligence"

In a Quanta Magazine piece (2026-08-20), Melanie Mitchell argues that AI intelligence differs fundamentally from human reasoning and is better classified as "alien intelligence." She proposes adapting psychological methods developed for infants and animals to evaluate AI cognition, and offers six principles for refining AI assessment — emphasizing robust experimental design and cautioning against anthropomorphism.
