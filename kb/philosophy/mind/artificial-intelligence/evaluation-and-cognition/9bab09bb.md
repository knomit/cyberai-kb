# Melanie Mitchell argues AI is an "alien intelligence" requiring animal/infant-style evaluation methods

In an August 2026 Quanta Magazine piece, Melanie Mitchell argues that AI's intelligence differs fundamentally from human reasoning and is better classified as "alien intelligence." She proposes adapting the psychological methods used to study infants and animals in order to evaluate AI cognition, and offers six principles for refining AI assessment — emphasizing robust experimental design and cautioning against anthropomorphism.
