# Zuckerberg's 'The Future is for Everyone' frames proliferation as the safety strategy

Mark Zuckerberg published an essay, 'The Future is for Everyone', laying out Meta's approach to superintelligence: individual empowerment as the source of prosperity, invention as the primary purpose of superintelligence, and balance of power as the foundation of safety. The strategy is to proliferate AI capabilities as widely as possible to avoid concentration of power. Jack Clark's critique in Import AI is that the essay is silent on whether a system capable of superhuman invention would in fact work solely on behalf of individuals less capable at invention than itself.
