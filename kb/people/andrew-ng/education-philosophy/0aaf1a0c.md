# Andrew Ng on Harvard grade inflation: education should aim to help 100% succeed, not judge

Andrew Ng (The Batch, May 22 2026) opposes Harvard's decision to cap A grades at 20% of class. His philosophy: education should relentlessly support 100% of learners succeeding rather than standing in judgment. He prefers 'Practice Problems' (designed to build skill) over 'Assessment Problems' (designed to judge skill). Permits unlimited assignment retries in his online courses. GPA is not an important hiring signal — screening/interviewing processes are far more accurate. 'Eliteness' should be defined by helping as many people as possible excel, not by limiting who succeeds.
