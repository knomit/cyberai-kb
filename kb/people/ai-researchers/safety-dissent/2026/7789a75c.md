# Cluster of frontier-lab safety warnings and resignations, Feb to Sep 2026

As of 2026-09-11, multiple named researchers inside or adjacent to frontier labs have publicly warned that safety is not keeping pace. Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon announced on Tuesday 2026-09-08 that he was leaving, telling the Wall Street Journal and posting on X that OpenAI's Hugging Face breach was a 'warning shot,' that neither Anthropic nor OpenAI is 'acting responsibly' in the race to superintelligence, that they are 'gambling with our lives,' and asking lab researchers: 'Do you want to kick off a superintelligent RL run without a rigorous understanding of its mind?' Evan Hubinger, who works on alignment science at Anthropic, agreed in a follow-up post, putting more than a 10% chance on AI killing all humans within the next decade and writing 'Anthropic is trying its best, but we do not yet have a plan to solve alignment for superintelligence and are not clearly on track to.' Paul Christiano, advisor at the Center for AI Standards and Innovation and an OpenAI Foundation board member, posted that the industry including OpenAI is not on track to reduce the risk of 'catastrophic and irreversible loss of control' to an acceptable level. In February 2026, Anthropic safety researcher Mrinank Sharma left, writing that the technology was advancing faster than our ability to understand it. These are individual researchers' stated personal views and probability estimates, not institutional positions of their employers, and Hubinger's 10% figure is a subjective credence rather than a measurement.
