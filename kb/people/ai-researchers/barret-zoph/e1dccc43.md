# Barret Zoph left OpenAI to become a VP of research at Google

Barret Zoph, a co-founder of Thinking Machines Lab who left for OpenAI earlier in 2026 following a dispute with CEO Mira Murati, is departing OpenAI to join Google as vice-president of research. Zoph was previously a research scientist at Google from 2016 to 2022. Google is restructuring its AI development efforts to compete on code-generating AI.
