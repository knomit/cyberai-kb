# Fields Medalist Jacob Tsimerman joined OpenAI

Jacob Tsimerman, a recent Fields Medal winner, took a position at OpenAI. Tsimerman had previously co-authored a paper categorizing ways AI might pose catastrophic risk, and is reported to be deeply concerned about AI dangers despite joining the lab.
