# Julian Togelius published a 2025 'crisis of faith' essay on AI research and human meaning

AI researcher Julian Togelius made public an essay, 'Losing my religion', written during a 2025 crisis of faith about the implications of successful AI research for human meaning. He wrote of waking at 3am from dread of a future where human talent, knowledge, and genius do not matter, warning that greater technological capability could produce 'abundance, but at the price of redundance'. Import AI's Jack Clark situates this alongside similar career pivots by Turing Award winners Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio, who moved from research toward public policy advocacy about AI's impacts.
