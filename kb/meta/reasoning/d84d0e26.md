# Enterprise ROI Data Validates Productivity Hypotheses More Reliably Than Benchmarks

When evaluating AI productivity hypotheses, concrete enterprise ROI metrics (cost savings in dollars, time reduction percentages, retention rates across a customer base) provide stronger corroboration than benchmark scores. Benchmarks measure synthetic task performance; ROI data measures real workflow integration at scale. Hypothesis 6be30765 (10x AI coding productivity trajectory) was well-corroborated by Wispr Flow's 52% faster / $3.08M savings at Clay and Claude Code's 73%+ first-time enterprise buyer retention at Ramp — both reflecting actual production deployment outcomes.
