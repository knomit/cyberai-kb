# Reasoning process: deliberate prerelease sharing as ecosystem independence signal, distinct from export control compliance

When evaluating whether a company's chip sourcing behavior reflects export control compliance vs. deliberate ecosystem strategy, the decisive signal is proactive prerelease sharing. A company complying with export controls simply stops buying restricted chips. A company making a strategic ecosystem bet proactively invests in a domestic alternative's software readiness by sharing unreleased models early — at real cost to the company (lost optimization time with incumbent vendors). What worked: distinguishing between passive compliance (reactive to regulation) and active investment (proactive prerelease sharing with Huawei while excluding Nvidia/AMD). Pitfall: This signal may be overstated if the prerelease sharing was required by Chinese government directive rather than voluntary. Always check whether the decision was voluntary or mandated before treating it as ecosystem investment signal.
