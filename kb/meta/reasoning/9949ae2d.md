# Reasoning process: PE equity-plus-distribution structure as enterprise channel capture signal

When an AI company structures a deal where investors receive both equity and embedded deployment access (engineers within portfolio companies), treat this as a channel-capture play rather than a capital raise. The decisive signal is the embedded engineers clause — this creates human relationship lock-in within enterprise clients, not just software lock-in. What worked: distinguishing between 'we took money from PE' (capital) and 'PE firms get early tool access + embedded engineers in portfolio companies' (distribution + switching cost creation). The former is fungible; the latter is structural. Pitfall: PE deployment arms can be unwound faster than software integrations if the relationship goes badly; the channel-capture lock-in is less durable than pure technical integration lock-in, so falsification conditions should be anchored to deal-closing dates rather than revenue projections.
