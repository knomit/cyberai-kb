# Reasoning process: a channel-dominance claim settled on deal-closing dates measures formation, not dominance — and the PE deployment-arm confirmation shows the corpus doing it

COMPANION TO kb/meta/reasoning/755ff139.md (every settlement signal must be diagnostic and forward-looking) and kb/meta/reasoning/2f78043b.md (a criterion must name an instrument that exists and covers the subject). This one records a worked example inside this corpus, and a contradiction the corpus carries about the same mechanism.

THE CLAIM AND ITS SETTLEMENT. A prediction held that PE deployment arms would become the DOMINANT AI enterprise sales channel by end of 2027. kb/technology/ai/economics/synthesis/pe_deployment_arms_confirmed.md declares it confirmed '18+ months ahead of the falsification date', and the entire evidentiary basis is that two deals CLOSED simultaneously on 5 May 2026: OpenAI's 'The Deployment Company' ($4B+ from TPG, Brookfield, Bain Capital at $10B) and Anthropic's AI-native enterprise services firm (Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, Goldman Sachs, ~$1.5B, expanding a prior $200B deal).

WHY THAT DOES NOT SETTLE IT. 'Dominant channel' is a share claim. Settling it requires some measure of how much enterprise AI business flows through this channel versus direct sales, cloud marketplaces, systems integrators, or self-serve. The confirmation record contains no such measure — no channel share, no retention figure, no revenue attribution, no comparison to any alternative channel. Two vehicles being CAPITALIZED is evidence that two vendors are betting on the channel. It is not evidence the bet paid, and it cannot be, eighteen months before the horizon. Formation and dominance are different variables, and the second was settled by observing the first.

THE CONTRADICTION THE CORPUS CARRIES. Two records make opposite claims about the durability of this exact mechanism:
- kb/meta/reasoning/9949ae2d.md: embedded-engineer lock-in 'is less durable than pure technical integration lock-in' and PE deployment arms 'can be unwound faster than software integrations if the relationship goes badly'.
- pe_deployment_arms_confirmed.md: 'Once engineers are embedded and workflows built on a specific AI stack, switching costs compound quarterly.'
Both describe PE-embedded engineers inside portfolio companies. One says this is the weak form of lock-in; the other says it compounds. Neither cites the other. A reader can take either from the corpus with equal support.

HOW THE ANCHOR ADVICE GOT INVERTED. 9949ae2d recommends anchoring falsification 'to deal-closing dates rather than revenue projections'. That advice is sound BECAUSE the channel is weak and revenue projections about it would be unreliable — closing dates are what can be observed honestly. The confirmation used that anchor and read it in the opposite spirit: as affirmative evidence of dominance. An anchor chosen for its observability was treated as though it were chosen for its diagnosticity.

RULE. Before recording a claim as confirmed, restate the claim's variable and the settling observation's variable side by side. If they are not the same variable, the confirmation is invalid however well-sourced the observation is. Structural claims (dominance, displacement, standardization) are settled by SHARE OR FLOW measurements; announcement and closing dates settle only that a thing was attempted. When only the second kind of evidence exists, the honest record is 'the bet has been placed', not 'the prediction is confirmed'.

COROLLARY — check whether the confirming record contradicts the methodology it invokes. pe_deployment_arms_confirmed.md cites the mechanism 9949ae2d analyzes while asserting the opposite durability conclusion. A confirmation that quietly reverses its own methodology's finding is a signal that the evidence was fitted to the claim.

COUNTERVAILING PRESSURE ALREADY ON THE RECORD, which the dominance claim has to survive and has not yet been tested against: open-source models rose from 28% to 62% of token share at Vercel over roughly two months in mid-2026, at the expense of OpenAI and Anthropic. The confirmation itself names this as a known gap — 'open-source alternatives could still commoditize the model layer and reduce PE distribution advantage' — and then confirms anyway.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this does not claim PE deployment arms are unimportant, will fail, or are not becoming a major channel — they may well be, and kb/technology/ai/society/power/2d62146d.md gives a coherent structural argument for why PE fiduciary duty to LPs would deepen adoption inside portfolio companies regardless of competitive alternatives. It does not claim the underlying deal facts are wrong; they are well-sourced. It claims only that the recorded CONFIRMATION does not follow from the recorded evidence, and that the corpus should carry the prediction as open rather than settled. The Vercel token-share figure is one platform's reported number and is itself held at low confidence — it is cited here as untested countervailing pressure, not as disproof.
