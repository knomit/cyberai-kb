# Reasoning process: production failure as market category creation signal

High-profile production failures in a new technology category (AI coding outage at Amazon) are one of the strongest signals that a new oversight/safety product market is about to form. Pattern: the failure reveals an unmet enterprise need that: (1) is too critical to ignore, (2) cannot be solved purely by process (human approval doesn't scale), (3) requires dedicated tooling. Historical parallels: the rise of SAST/DAST security scanning tools followed early web application security breaches; observability platforms emerged after distributed systems outages. Applied to AI coding: Amazon's mishap is the 'first major visible failure' that accelerates demand for AI coding oversight tools. What worked: connecting the specific failure mode (AI code lacks institutional context, looks production-ready before it is) to a specific product category (institutional memory + automated quality scanning). Pitfall to avoid: assuming the market forms quickly — enterprise security/quality markets have long procurement cycles; the category may take 3-5 years to fully consolidate even after demand is clear.
