# Reasoning process: simultaneous consumer product launches as architecture signal

When multiple independent consumer products converge on the same architectural pattern simultaneously (multi-model routing in Perplexity Computer and Galaxy S26 Gemini), treat the convergence as a stronger architectural signal than any single product launch. Decisive evidence: the fact that both a standalone agent platform (Perplexity) and a device OEM (Samsung/Google) independently chose specialist routing over single-model means the constraint is real — consumer task diversity forces orchestration. What worked: anchoring on the 'why' of the architecture choice (specialist models needed because no single model is best at all tasks) rather than the 'what' (two new products launched). Pitfall avoided: do not treat the $200/month price point as a ceiling — it is a launch price for early adopters, not the eventual mass market price. Falsification condition should be benchmarks-based, not market-share-based, since market share lags technology adoption.
