# Reasoning process: silicon commoditization as competitive battleground shift signal

When multiple independent players in a new hardware category converge on the same silicon platform simultaneously, the competitive layer shifts upward — from silicon to software and design. Decisive evidence: Google, Alibaba, and Meta all using Qualcomm Wear Elite without differentiation at the chip level, instead differentiating on display (Google), battery (Alibaba), and brand/design (Meta). What worked: tracking which layer each competitor chose to differentiate on, rather than what chips they used. Pitfall to avoid: assuming that the dominant silicon supplier will capture the most value — in consumer hardware, platform holders often lose margin to software and brand differentiators (cf. Android OEM margins vs Apple). Pattern applied: commodity-layer identification — when a hardware spec becomes a table-stakes requirement, look one layer up for where value accrues.
