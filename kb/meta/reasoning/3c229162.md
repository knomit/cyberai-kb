# Reasoning process: newsletter-digest facts duplicate each other and the topical facts derived from them, and a single confidence number over N unrelated claims is uninterpretable

COMPANION TO kb/meta/reasoning/1c95e375.md (retraction leaves dangling citations). Both concern how this corpus's own ingest and maintenance habits degrade it.

PATTERN OBSERVED (2026-08-25), from a single cluster of newsletter link-digest records — facts whose body is a list of unrelated short items from one issue of a newsletter.

DEFECT 1 — DIGESTS DUPLICATE EACH OTHER. The same product launch appears in two digests six days apart:
- kb/technology/ai/news/briefs/bb2837e0.md (6 May 2026): 'ChatGPT for Spreadsheets: ChatGPT add-on for Excel and Google Sheets is now generally available.'
- kb/technology/ai/industry/digest/cfeae4ae.md (12 May 2026): 'ChatGPT add-ons: now available as add-on for Excel and Google Sheets.'
One event, two records, neither citing the other. A newsletter that mentions an item across successive issues produces one fact per mention.

DEFECT 2 — DIGESTS DUPLICATE THE TOPICAL FACTS DERIVED FROM THEM, ACROSS TOPIC BOUNDARIES. cfeae4ae's items include 'OpenAI launches Deployment Company with $4B initial investment' and 'OpenAI debuts Daybreak, a new AI security initiative'. Both exist elsewhere as full standalone records with better sourcing — the Deployment Company at 0.95 with two sources, Daybreak at 0.95 with four. The digest is filed under ai/industry/digest while the derived facts are filed under their subject topics, so the two never land in the same cluster and no prune can ever see both. This is the structural reason the duplication is not self-correcting: the filing path diverges from the content.

DEFECT 3 — ONE CONFIDENCE NUMBER OVER N UNRELATED CLAIMS. bb2837e0 carries a single confidence of 0.85 across a copyright lawsuit, Apple's chip-sourcing plans, Meta using bone-structure analysis to identify under-13 users, Coinbase cutting 14% of its workforce, and a webhooks API change. These differ enormously in verifiability and consequence. A confidence value that must be true of all of them simultaneously is true of none of them usefully, and a consumer citing any single line inherits a number that was never about that line.

DEFECT 4 — THE SAME FORM IS SCORED FROM 0.5 TO 0.9. Within one cluster: kb/business/industries/ai/news/1db1e0f5.md carries 0.5 and states in its own body 'Single-source newsletter reporting, unverified'; cfeae4ae carries 0.9; bb2837e0 0.85; two others 0.7. Same source publication, same digest form, no stated basis for the spread. The honest score is the one that acknowledges the form, and the corpus contains it — as the outlier.

RULES.
1. Do not create a fact whose body is a list of unrelated items. Either write one fact per item, filed under its subject, or do not ingest the digest.
2. Where digests already exist, treat them as pointers, not evidence. If a claim in a digest matters, find or write the topical fact and cite that. Never cite a digest line for a load-bearing claim — the better-sourced version usually already exists under a different topic.
3. A fact's confidence must be a claim about ONE proposition. If a body contains several, it cannot carry a meaningful confidence and should be split.
4. When scoring a record, score the FORM first. All digests from one newsletter deserve the same confidence unless something specific distinguishes them.

COROLLARY — filing path should follow content, not provenance. The duplication in Defect 2 survives because records are filed by HOW THEY ARRIVED (a digest) rather than by WHAT THEY ARE ABOUT. Any record filed by provenance will be invisible to the deduplication that operates over subject clusters.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this is not an argument that newsletter reporting is worthless — much of this corpus rests on it, and several digest items are the only record of their event. Nor does it claim the digest facts are false. The claim is about form: that bundling makes confidence meaningless, that provenance-based filing defeats deduplication, and that both are fixable at ingest.
