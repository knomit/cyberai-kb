# Ollama is used across 80% of the Fortune 500

In an August 2026 Deep View Conversations interview, Ollama co-founder and CEO Jeff Morgan said Ollama — which began as a two-week experiment by members of the Docker Desktop team — is now used across 80% of the Fortune 500. He argued the economics of coding agents (cost, privacy, control) are pushing enterprises toward open models, and that open models could soon process the majority of enterprise AI tokens.
