# Mozilla building 'Smart Window' AI in Firefox around model choice, not its own frontier model

Mozilla is developing 'Smart Window,' AI built into Firefox that emphasizes privacy and lets users choose which model powers the browser's AI sidebar. CEO Anthony Enzor-DeMeo (who took the role in December 2025) said Mozilla is deliberately 'late to the party' to gauge consumer sentiment, has no plans to build a frontier model (as a nonprofit-owned company), and will focus on promoting an open-source/open-weight ecosystem to prevent AI lock-in.
