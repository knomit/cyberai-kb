# Anthropic opened a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard

Anthropic released a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard, a model-agnostic specification that lets AI agents operate scientific and manufacturing equipment. It is analogous in ambition to MCP but targets physical laboratory and factory instrumentation rather than software tools.
