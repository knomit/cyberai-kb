# OpenClaw 2.0 landed 16,000+ pull requests in its largest release

OpenClaw released version 2.0, its largest update to date. What began as an effort to simplify installation and rebuild the browser experience expanded into a broader overhaul incorporating more than 16,000 pull requests spanning memory, skills, models, automations, apps, plugins, and security.
