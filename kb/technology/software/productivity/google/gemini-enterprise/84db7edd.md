# Google is prototyping 'Rooms' as a shared objective workspace in Gemini Enterprise

Google is prototyping a feature called 'Rooms' for Gemini Enterprise: a workspace that lets a team collaborate with Gemini against a specific objective rather than in individual one-off chats.
