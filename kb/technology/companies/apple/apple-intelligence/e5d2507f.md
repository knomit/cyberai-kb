# Private iOS 27 frameworks show Apple built Siri to delegate to third-party models

Private frameworks in iOS 27 and macOS 27 reveal that Apple built Siri so a third-party model such as Claude or GPT-5.6 can act as its brain. Two distinct mechanisms appear: "Model Delegation", which lets an external model handle a natural-language request and then hand control of Reminders or Messages back to Siri's own tooling; and an inference-provider path that can replace Apple's server-side Siri model entirely while keeping Siri's UI and voice unchanged to the user. A demonstration showed ChatGPT finding emails, summarizing action items, and texting a contact through Siri's tools. Important qualifier: these hooks are not user-facing in the shipping release — their presence is architectural readiness for substitution, not an announced feature, and it does not contradict that shipping Siri runs on Apple's own foundation models. Their arrival with iOS 27 follows EU Digital Markets Act pressure to open Siri to rival assistants.
