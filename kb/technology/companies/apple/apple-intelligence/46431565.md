# iOS 27 Siri AI feature set and the iPhone 15 Pro hardware floor

The OS 27 release wave (iOS/iPadOS/macOS/watchOS/visionOS 27) carries a specific set of AI features beyond the headline Siri upgrade: Photo expand (outpainting to reframe portrait/landscape or repair a cropped subject), Spatial reframe (post-capture adjustment of the shooting angle), a standalone Siri AI app for text chat, customizable expressive Siri voices with pace control, and materially improved system dictation. Siri's personal-context ability means multi-step action across Apple apps — answering "When is my marathon?" from Mail, then adding it to Calendar on follow-up, or using Visual Intelligence to scan a flyer, create the event, and share it to a group chat. The hardware floor matters for planning: the Siri AI upgrade requires an Apple Intelligence-capable chipset and the oldest eligible model is the iPhone 15 Pro, so installing iOS 27 on an older device does not deliver it. Non-AI improvements in the same release include up to 80% faster AirDrop transfers.
