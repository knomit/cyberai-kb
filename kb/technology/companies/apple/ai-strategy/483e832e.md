# Apple delivered Siri / Apple Intelligence overhaul at WWDC 2026

At WWDC 2026, Apple finally delivered the long-promised Siri and Apple Intelligence overhaul after roughly two years of delays. Apple's approach emphasizes bringing AI to everyday users through deep product integration and on-device features rather than forcing them into standalone chatbots or new apps — a strategy that differs from OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic.
