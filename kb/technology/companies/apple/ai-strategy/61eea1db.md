# Apple's WWDC 2026 AI strategy: integration and privacy over standalone chatbots

Apple's WWDC 2026 AI push coheres around a distinct strategy: rather than competing on standalone chatbots like OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic, Apple embeds AI into existing products and the OS. The overhauled Siri/Apple Intelligence (delivered after ~2 years of delays) is powered by a mix of Apple's own Foundation Models and Google Gemini, with privacy protected via Private Cloud Compute and 'Personal Context' as the central differentiator. This integration-first approach manifested in concrete features like Spatial Reframing and AI upgrades across Photos, Safari, Messages, Writing Tools, and Mac, spanning iPhone, Mac, and Vision Pro.
