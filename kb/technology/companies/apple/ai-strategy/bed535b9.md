# Apple Intelligence uses Apple Foundation Models plus Google Gemini

As discussed in Apple's WWDC 2026 announcements, Apple Intelligence operates using a combination of Apple's own Foundation Models and Google Gemini. Apple's privacy approach for cloud-based AI processing relies on Private Cloud Compute, and 'Personal Context' is positioned as Apple's biggest AI differentiator.
