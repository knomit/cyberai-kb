# Apple released public betas of iOS 27 / macOS 27 / iPadOS 27 / watchOS 27 with new Siri AI

In July 2026 Apple released the public beta versions of iOS 27, macOS 27, iPadOS 27 and watchOS 27, opening its revamped 'Siri AI' assistant to public testers. The software was announced June 8 2026 at WWDC; the final release is expected in fall 2026. The Deep View's Jason Hiner assessed the new Siri as far more functional and reliable than the old Siri but not frontier-level, comparing its usefulness to Meta smartglasses running Muse Spark models.
