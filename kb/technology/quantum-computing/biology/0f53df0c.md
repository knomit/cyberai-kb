# IBM + Cleveland Clinic + Riken: Quantum-AI Simulates 12,635-Atom Protein Complexes

IBM, the Cleveland Clinic, and Riken demonstrated a biological research milestone: using two IBM quantum computers and two of the world's most powerful supercomputers, they simulated protein complexes spanning up to 12,635 atoms. This compares to just 10 atoms achievable in October 2024 — an extraordinary leap in scale.

The significance: proteins are the 'workhorse of the cell,' and understanding their 3D structure and motion is critical for drug development (how drug candidates bind to proteins). Classical computers can only approximate these solutions; quantum makes more accurate simulation feasible.

Additional use case highlighted: using quantum + machine learning to rapidly identify which treatments a harmful microbe is sensitive to — potentially life-saving given that infections remain a leading cause of death globally.

IBM CEO Arvind Krishna stated that in the next three years, quantum will NOT replace AI or classical CPUs but will solve problems neither can solve — serving as a complementary compute tier.
