# Agility Robotics targets manufacturing 'islands of automation' as the humanoid beachhead

Agility Robotics CTO Pras Velagapudi told The Deep View the company targets manufacturing first because large parts of it remain unautomated — what he calls 'islands of automation', pockets of existing automation separated by manual processes that are hard to automate because they have the wrong structure for a robot arm or must occur in many places in a facility. Agility focuses on 'dull, dirty, dangerous' bulk material handling (e.g. 50-pound totes, machine loading/unloading) in hot or hazardous environments, and has robots deployed with Toyota, Amazon, and Mercado Libre. Household and care-facility deployment is a stated long-term goal but on a longer safety timeline.
