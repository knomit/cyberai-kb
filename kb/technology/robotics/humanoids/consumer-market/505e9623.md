# Humanoid robots at IFA 2026 are purchasable but priced at $13.5K to $100K

Deep View reporting from IFA 2026, published 2026-09-09, found humanoid robots that box, dance, climb stairs and play soccer — and, unlike prior years, many were actually available for purchase. Prices cited: the Xiaomi CyberOne at $99,999 and the Unitree G1 at $13,500. The reporter's judgment is that the hardware looks ready while the price does not, and explicitly predicts humanoid robots will NOT be commonplace in households within 5 or even 10 years — so 'nearing the mainstream' means the spectacle-to-product transition, not imminent household adoption. IFA Berlin CEO Leif Lindner identified two gaps from visits to Shenzhen robotics camps: dependence on vast training and operational data, and consumer trust in how that data is used. The argued reason both gaps are narrowing: accurate simulated data is far easier to obtain now (Nvidia Omniverse and Cosmos, Fei-Fei Li's World Labs), and consumers have shown increasing willingness to share personal data and real-time feeds through devices like smart glasses in exchange for smarter AI features.
