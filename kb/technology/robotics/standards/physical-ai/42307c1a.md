# Arm launches Robotics Capability Framework to standardize robot autonomy levels

On Monday 2026-09-07 at Arm Everywhere China, Arm announced two physical-AI initiatives aimed at reducing robotics-industry fragmentation. First, it extended Arm Total Design — its chip-development ecosystem program — into physical AI, covering more than 80 companies across AI hardware, software, sensors and robotics, including AWS, Hugging Face and Unitree. Second, it unveiled the Robotics Capability Framework, inviting industry experts to build a standardized vocabulary for robotics and define levels of increasing sophistication for robotic systems, explicitly modeled on the SAE levels of driving automation (Level 0 through Level 5). Drew Henry, EVP of Arm's physical AI unit, said the market is roughly $25 billion today but that Arm views it as becoming one of the largest TAMs in the history of computing — a vendor's forward-looking market framing, not an independent forecast. Henry also said Arm has shipped two billion units into physical AI use cases in the past 12 months, from microcontrollers and sensors up to compute platforms for autonomous vehicles and robotics; that is Arm's own reported figure. The framework is at the invitation stage: no levels have been published or adopted.
