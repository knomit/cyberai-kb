# Reasoning process: multi-player convergence → commoditization hypothesis

When multiple dominant players converge on the same product category at the same time across multiple layers (model/app/infra), the decisive signal is not who is first but who achieves workflow lock-in fastest. Decisive evidence: user growth rates (Codex 4M in weeks) and enterprise embedding (Workspace Intelligence) outweighed raw capability claims. Pattern applied: technology commoditization follows capability parity → distribution/integration moat. Pitfall avoided: do not conflate racing to release with sustaining differentiation — release cadence ≠ durable advantage. What worked: anchoring the hypothesis on a concrete, falsifiable 18-month horizon and naming specific falsification conditions.
