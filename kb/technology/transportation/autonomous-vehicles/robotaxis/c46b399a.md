# Pony AI and Uber plan 2,000 robotaxis in Europe while Nevada granted Tesla only 10

Uber and China's Pony AI plan to deploy 2,000 robotaxis across Europe. Separately, Nevada granted Tesla a robotaxi permit covering just 10 vehicles, despite Tesla having sought a permit for 5,000 in Las Vegas.
