# Google Flow Sessions program produced AI-assisted short films

Google's 'Flow Sessions' invited a fourth cohort of artists to spend six weeks making short films with Google's Flow AI video generator, screened at an event in NYC. Filmmakers reported the most effective use was hybrid: keeping real actors and practical sets while using AI for elements with no real-world counterpart (aliens, monsters, impossible scenarios). Example: Andrew Nethery's 'Nothin' Beats a Cheeseburger' used 20 live-action shots, 32 hybrid shots and five fully AI-generated shots.
