# Apple's photo philosophy is enhancement not remixing, and iOS 27 tests that line

Apple's stated position, articulated repeatedly by VP of camera software engineering Jon McCormack, is that a photograph is a celebration of a moment that happened and deserves respect as such — which is why Apple has been a relative purist about what generative AI may add to an image, in contrast to Samsung, Google and Chinese manufacturers like Huawei, which use generative AI to insert people into group shots, swap skies, or restore a partially eaten cupcake. As of the 2026-09-08 Deep View analysis, iOS 27 pushes that boundary: Spatial Reframing shifts the perspective a photo was taken from, Extend uses generative AI to expand content at a photo's edges, and an upgraded Clean Up removes larger objects and fills the scene with generated content. Apple's own framing is that all of these still enhance the shot you took rather than remix it into something closer to artwork than a photograph. Note this is a philosophical line Apple draws and moves, not a technical constraint or a disclosure standard — nothing here says generated content in an iOS 27 photo is labeled as such.
