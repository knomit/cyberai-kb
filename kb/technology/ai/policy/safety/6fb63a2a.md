# OpenAI partners with the American Psychological Association on teen AI mental-health safeguards

The Deep View reported (Aug 6, 2026) that OpenAI announced a partnership with the American Psychological Association (APA) to develop mental-health safeguards for young AI users, including family-facing resources, clinician guidance on AI overreliance, and convening teens/families/clinicians. Sara Johansen leads OpenAI's mental health and well-being product policy. Context cited: a Pew Research Center study found 12% of US teenagers have used generative AI for emotional support or advice.
