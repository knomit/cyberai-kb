# Argument: verifiable US-China AI slowdown needs treaty with compute monitoring

LessWrong post argues a unilateral US pause fails because rivals would catch up within about a year, so a slowdown needs a treaty that catches defection early; it surveys governing compute via remote sensing, on-site inspections, and hardware mechanisms like chip licensing and location verification. Advocacy/analysis post, not adopted policy.
