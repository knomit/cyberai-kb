# California Governor Newsom AI Workforce Executive Order: Preparing Workers for Economic Disruption (May 2026)

On May 22, 2026, California Governor Gavin Newsom issued an executive order directing the state to prepare workers and businesses for 'economic disruption' from AI. One of the first pieces of state AI regulation explicitly confronting potential widespread workforce impact.

Key measures in the order:
- AI/job training programs at small businesses and higher education institutions; enhanced 'worker ownership models' and collective bargaining agreements
- Public dashboard showing AI's impact on the workforce; report with recommendations and best practices
- Worker safety net policy exploration, 'employment stability payments' awareness, 'AI playbook' for modernizing job training programs
- Academic + private sector partnerships to develop policies ensuring AI advances the public good

Context: Issued as layoffs continue mounting across tech (Meta, Block, Atlassian, Oracle, Amazon) with AI cited as justification. Newsom quote: 'This moment demands that we reimagine the entire system — how we work, how we govern, how we prepare people for the future.'

Analyst note (Jason Hiner, The Deep View): California has historically led tech regulation but regulation perpetually lags the actual impacts. The executive order may serve as 'triage to salve the wounds' rather than preventing the disruption.
