# Hollywood AI Awards Split: Oscars Ban AI Acting/Writing, Golden Globes Take Lenient Stance (May 2026)

Two major awards institutions diverged on AI rules: (1) Oscars (Academy): Updated rules ban AI-generated acting ('must be demonstrably performed by humans') and writing ('must be human-authored') from awards eligibility. AI tools in other production areas neither help nor harm nomination chances. (2) Golden Globes: More lenient — AI doesn't auto-disqualify, but 'human creative direction, artistic judgment, and authorship must remain primary throughout production.' AI can enhance but not replace core creative contributions; must be authorized by the performer. Background: 'Stealing Isn't Innovation' petition by Human Artistry Campaign (signatories include Scarlett Johansson, Cate Blanchett). MPA head Charles Rivkin shifted from opposing Sora to saying AI can 'bolster storytelling.'
