# Hollywood AI Policy Landscape May 2026: Consent Standard, Awards Splits, and Industry Fractures

Hollywood's AI governance in May 2026 spans two distinct but connected fronts:

**Likeness & Consent:** RSL Media (nonprofit co-founded by Cate Blanchett) launched the 'Human Consent Standard' — a protocol embedded in digital works that lets creators set terms for AI use of their likeness. AI/web crawlers must check a registry (launching June 2026) against these terms. Supported by Viola Davis, Kristen Stewart, Tom Hanks, Meryl Streep, George Clooney. WIRED counterpoint: Hollywood screenwriters are increasingly doing AI training data work to make ends meet, fracturing the movement.

**Awards Eligibility:** Oscars updated rules to ban AI-generated acting and writing from awards eligibility. Golden Globes took a more lenient stance — AI doesn't auto-disqualify, but human creative direction must remain primary; AI can enhance but not replace. MPA head Charles Rivkin shifted from opposing Sora to saying AI can 'bolster storytelling.'

**Shared context:** Both fronts trace to the 'Stealing Isn't Innovation' petition by the Human Artistry Campaign (signatories include Scarlett Johansson, Cate Blanchett). The industry is fragmented — consent infrastructure is being built, awards bodies are diverging, and economic pressure is pulling individual creators toward AI work despite collective resistance.
