# Hollywood Human Consent Standard: RSL Media, Cate Blanchett, AI Likeness Registry (May 2026)

RSL Media, a nonprofit co-founded by actress Cate Blanchett, launched the 'Human Consent Standard' — a protocol embedded in digital works that lets creators set terms for AI use of their likeness, from full restriction to unlimited permissions. AI/web crawlers must check a registry (launching June 2026) against these terms. Supported by Viola Davis, Kristen Stewart, Tom Hanks, Meryl Streep, George Clooney. Standard builds on prior movements: 'Stealing Isn't Innovation' petition and Creators Coalition on AI. Counterpoint: WIRED found Hollywood screenwriters are increasingly doing AI training work to make ends meet.
