# US National AI Policy Framework (March 2026): preempts state laws, minimally burdensome regulation

The White House released its National AI Policy Framework (March 21, 2026), authored under Michael Kratsios (OSTP director). Key provisions: (1) Aims to preempt state AI laws, calling state regulations 'undue burdens'; states should not regulate AI development as it is an 'inherently interstate phenomenon'; (2) States cannot penalize AI developers for third-party unlawful use of their models; (3) Seeks 'minimally burdensome' federal regulation; (4) Key risk areas addressed: age-assurance tools for children, IP 'balanced approach' (training on copyrighted materials ok + creator rights), regulatory sandboxes, AI-ready workforce training. Critics: Samir Jain (VP Policy, Center for Democracy and Technology): 'internal contradictions and failure to grapple with key tensions' — framework says no coercion on content yet 'woke AI' Executive Order does exactly that. Microsoft also launched MAI-Image-2 text-to-image model (Mar 2026): ranked #3 on Arena.ai behind Gemini 3.1 Flash (#1) and GPT-Image-1.5 High Fidelity (#2).
