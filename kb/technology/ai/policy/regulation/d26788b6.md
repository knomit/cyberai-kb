# OpenAI calls for mandatory civilian AI safety evals (CAISI/NIST) vs Trump EO's voluntary NSA-run framework

OpenAI released a policy document (June 2026) calling for stricter AI oversight than the Trump Administration's executive order. The EO requested a voluntary window for government review of powerful new models, administered by the NSA (a defense agency). OpenAI's policy paper differs in two key areas: (1) Agency: OpenAI wants evaluations run by the Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI) under NIST/Commerce Department — civilian agencies, not the NSA; (2) Mandatory: OpenAI calls for these pre-deployment safety checks to be mandatory, not voluntary. Simultaneously, CEO Sam Altman said the EO 'gets the balance right,' while Anthropic, IBM, and Amazon all publicly supported the EO. Analysts interpreted OpenAI's position as a strategic play for public opinion — appearing to go 'above and beyond' peers in safety advocacy while remaining commercially aligned.
