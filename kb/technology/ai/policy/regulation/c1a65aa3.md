# US AI regulatory patchwork: 40+ states enacting own laws as Trump threatens withholding federal funds

By April 2026, 40+ US states are actively passing their own AI regulations despite the Trump Administration's effort to discourage state-by-state legislation. More than 1,500 state bills under consideration, plus 100+ existing laws already enacted by 40 states. Key state laws: California — Aug 2026 invisible watermarks on AI-generated content; March 2026 EO on privacy/civil rights/bias; Jan 2026 laws on catastrophic risk assessment and chatbot restrictions for minors; Colorado — July 2026 bias protection for 'high-risk AI systems' in education/employment/finance/healthcare; New York — Jan 2027: companies >$500M revenue must block bioweapon/hacking tool creation and audit annually; Minnesota, Ohio, Utah also passing laws. Trump Administration: Dec 2025 executive order discourages state legislation; threatens to withhold federal funds from states passing 'onerous' AI laws; March 2026 guidelines support only children protection and electricity price controls. Result: a compliance minefield where an AI model may need to pass bias audit (Colorado), provide watermarks (California), and meet reporting thresholds (NY) — simultaneously.
