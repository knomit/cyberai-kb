# US government repeatedly gated frontier-model access in mid-2026

Through June-July 2026 the US government repeatedly acted as a gatekeeper on frontier-model access, via both pre-release approval and export controls. (1) Export-control arc: Commerce imposed export controls on Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 on 12-13 June 2026 over an Amazon-found jailbreak that produced exploit code, requiring cutoff for all foreign nationals; Anthropic shut both models down globally; Commerce lifted the controls on 30 June and Fable 5 access was restored 1 July. (2) Pre-release gating (reported by The Batch ~3 July 2026): OpenAI initially limited the GPT-5.6 launch to ~20 undisclosed US government-approved organizations (called temporary), and the same day the US government let Anthropic offer Claude Mythos 5 to ~100 companies and agencies, two weeks after forcing its suspension. Caveats preserved: the June export controls are NO LONGER in force (do not read as current); the White House military-use ban (March 2026) and the Mythos Preview expansion block (May 2026) are separate, still-distinct actions not covered here.
