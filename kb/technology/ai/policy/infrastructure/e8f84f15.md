# Anti-data-center revolt intensifies: $64B in projects blocked, violence, state moratoriums

Resistance to AI data centers is escalating in the US. ~$64B worth of projects blocked or delayed May 2024–Mar 2025. Maine passed a bill placing a moratorium on new data centers requiring 20+ MW until 2027 (first statewide ban, awaiting governor signature). 12+ other states filed data center moratorium bills in 2026. Violence: molotov cocktail thrown at Sam Altman's home in SF (same man threatened OpenAI HQ); 13 gunshots fired at Indianapolis city councilor who approved a $500M data center. Wisconsin: Port Washington referendum requires voter approval for data center tax incentives. Oracle/OpenAI 1.3 GW data center in Port Washington expected online 2028.
