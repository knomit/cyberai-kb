# U.S. orders Anthropic to suspend Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for foreign nationals

On Friday June 13, 2026, Anthropic said it would abruptly disable its most advanced AI models, Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5, for all users after the U.S. government ordered it to suspend access to those models for foreign nationals (inside or outside the U.S.) citing national security concerns. Anthropic said it received the order at 5:21 p.m. ET, believed there was a 'misunderstanding,' and was working to restore access. The directive was framed as an export-control measure; access to Anthropic's other models was not affected. The government reportedly believed it had become aware of a method of jailbreaking Fable 5.
