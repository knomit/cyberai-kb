# US Commerce Dept imposes export control on Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5

On June 13, 2026 (effective Friday), the US Commerce Department imposed an export control on Anthropic's latest Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models, citing cybersecurity and national security concerns. The directive requires Anthropic to restrict access to the two new models for all foreign nationals, whether outside or inside the US, including foreign nationals employed by Anthropic such as recent hire Andrej Karpathy. The stated concern is that the models give even low-level attackers the ability to penetrate enterprise cybersecurity defenses.
