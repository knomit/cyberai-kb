# OpenAI Endorses Kids Online Safety Act and Illinois SB 315 Frontier AI Bill (May 2026)

OpenAI announced endorsement of two AI safety bills: (1) Kids Online Safety Act (KOSA) — stronger protections for minors using AI/social media; (2) Illinois SB 315 — requires safety, transparency, incident reporting, and accountability for frontier AI models. Chief Global Affairs Officer Chris Lehane stated 'We can't repeat the mistakes made during the rise of social media.' OpenAI also opened a Washington DC AI workshop to foster conversations among elected officials, regulators, civil servants, educators, workers, and community leaders. The company articulated a vision of intelligence as a 'global utility' akin to electricity.
