# Apple bans vibe-coded Anything app: enforces no-AI-generated-apps policy

Apple removed 'Anything' — a viral vibe-coded app built entirely with AI (Claude + Cursor) that reached #4 in App Store productivity in late March 2026 — citing App Store Review Guideline 4.3 (spam/copycat apps) and a newly enforced interpretation that apps built primarily by AI without meaningful human review constitute 'automated submissions.' Apple issued a developer advisory clarifying that apps must demonstrate 'human curation and decision-making in the development process.' The Anything app developer (Solo founder, ~28K MRR at peak) appealed; Apple upheld the decision. Sparked industry debate on what constitutes authorship in AI-assisted development.
