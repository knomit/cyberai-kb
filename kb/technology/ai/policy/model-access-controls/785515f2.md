# GPT-5.6 launch restricted to ~20 US government-approved organizations

OpenAI initially limited the GPT-5.6 launch to around 20 U.S. government-approved organizations (names undisclosed), previewing the models to the U.S. government before release; OpenAI called the restriction temporary. On the same day, the U.S. government granted Anthropic permission to offer Claude Mythos 5 to roughly 100 companies and federal agencies, two weeks after forcing Anthropic to suspend Claude Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5; Anthropic later restored Claude Fable 5. Reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
