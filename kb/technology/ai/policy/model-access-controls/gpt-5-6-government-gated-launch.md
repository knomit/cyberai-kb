# GPT-5.6 launched in government-gated limited preview to ~20 approved organizations instead of open access

OpenAI initially limited the GPT-5.6 launch to around 20 U.S. government-approved organizations (names undisclosed), previewing the models to the U.S. government before release. OpenAI called the restriction temporary. Washington kept OpenAI from releasing GPT-5.6 broadly, so it launched in limited preview instead of the planned open-access launch; CEO Sam Altman called the government request 'bad news.'

SAME-DAY PARALLEL AT ANTHROPIC: the U.S. government granted Anthropic permission to offer Claude Mythos 5 to roughly 100 companies and federal agencies — two weeks after forcing Anthropic to suspend Claude Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5. Anthropic later restored Claude Fable 5.

NET EFFECT: the staggered rollouts of Mythos and GPT-5.6 effectively produced a temporary pause on releasing the most powerful frontier models.

The Deep View reported that GPT-5.6 had tested even stronger than Anthropic's Mythos on some benchmarks.

EPISTEMIC STATUS: one event reported twice — The Deep View on 2 July 2026 and The Batch on 3 July 2026 — not two separate restrictions. Neither source record carried independent sources.
