# Anthropic's position on open-weight models

Anthropic said it had not advocated banning open-weight AI models and argued that less-capable open releases are a public good. Instead it supported tighter chip export controls, targeted action against industrial-scale model distillation, and mandatory safety testing for sufficiently capable open and closed models.
