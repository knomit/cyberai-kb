# Open letter: Open Weights and American AI Leadership

On Friday (late July 2026), a group of 25 leading American tech companies led by Microsoft and Nvidia published an open letter titled 'Open Weights and American AI Leadership.' It calls on US policymakers to avoid blanket bans on open-weight AI models, take targeted legal and commercial steps against large-scale model distillation (while avoiding sweeping restrictions on legitimate distillation), and expand access to compute and other AI resources for researchers and startups. Its theme is 'keeping the frontier plural' by avoiding premature restrictions on open models.
