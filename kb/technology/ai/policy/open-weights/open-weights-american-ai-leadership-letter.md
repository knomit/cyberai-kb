# 'Open Weights and American AI Leadership': 25 US tech companies led by Microsoft and Nvidia urged policymakers not to restrict open-weight models

In late July 2026, a group of 25 leading American technology companies, led by Microsoft and Nvidia, published an open letter titled 'Open Weights and American AI Leadership.' Its overall argument is that supporting open-weight AI models fosters innovation and American competitiveness, under the theme of 'keeping the frontier plural' by avoiding premature restrictions on open models.

Three specific asks:
1. Avoid blanket bans on open-weight AI models.
2. Take targeted legal and commercial steps against large-scale model distillation — while avoiding sweeping restrictions on legitimate distillation.
3. Expand access to compute and other AI resources for researchers and startups.

ATTRIBUTION NOTE: one of the merged source records described this as a paper by Nvidia alone. It is a 25-company open letter co-led by Microsoft and Nvidia; attributing it to a single company understates the coalition behind it.
