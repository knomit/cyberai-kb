# World ID upgraded to full-stack proof-of-humanity for agents, businesses, and consumers

Sam Altman's World (founded 2019) upgraded World ID at its Lift Off event in April 2026 to a full-stack proof-of-humanity protocol. Partnerships: Tinder/Match Group (verified human badges, expanding from Japan to US), Zoom (Deep Face technology to confirm non-deepfake speakers), DocuSign (verified human document signing), Reddit (considering World ID for bot prevention), Razer and Mythical Games (gaming). For agents: AgentKit enables agent delegation, human-in-the-loop, and agentic commerce — Vercel Workflow SDK, Browserbase, and Exa integrations. 18 million users already iris-verified.
