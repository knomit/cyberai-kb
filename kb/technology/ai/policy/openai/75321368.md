# OpenAI 'AI New Deal' document proposes wealth-sharing mechanisms

OpenAI has proposed an 'AI New Deal' document that includes mechanisms for sharing the economic gains of AI broadly, including approaches involving taxes and dividends tied to observable economic indicators. This is referenced by the OpenAI Foundation's $250M economic futures initiative.
