# OpenAI Foundation commits $250M for AI economic preparedness

On May 28, 2026, the OpenAI Foundation announced a $250 million commitment to grants and partnerships aimed at preparing the economy for AI disruption. The goal is 'building secure and abundant economic futures.' Work spans three areas: (1) independent measurement/forecasting of AI's economic impact; (2) supporting workers through near-term disruption including retraining and giving workers 'agency over AI deployment'; (3) developing 'post-AI political economies' and equitable sharing of AI economic gains, including research into shifting taxes and dividends in response to 'observable indicators.'
