# EU Postpones High-Risk AI Restrictions Until End of 2027

EU regulators reached a deal to postpone restrictions on high-risk AI applications until the end of 2027 (reported May 8, 2026). This extends the compliance window for AI systems classified as high-risk under the EU AI Act. The delay follows ongoing industry pressure and implementation complexity concerns. Combined with the US National AI Policy Framework's 'minimally burdensome' posture and federal preemption push, both major Western regulatory blocs are now deprioritizing near-term enforcement of the most stringent AI rules. The regulatory window for deployment of high-risk AI systems is materially wider than the AI Act's original timeline suggested.
