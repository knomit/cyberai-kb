# Anthropic usage policy update prohibits sustained needless cruelty toward Claude (effective Nov 12)

Anthropic updated its usage policy to prohibit sustained, needless abusive or cruel behavior toward its models; users have until November 12 (2026) before enforcement. Common frustration, pushback, dark creative themes and model testing/research are exempt. Anthropic notes no established evidence AI models experience distress.
