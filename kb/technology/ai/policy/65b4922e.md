# White House 'Make America AI-Ready': Free 7-Day SMS AI Literacy Course (Mar 2026)

US Department of Labor announced 'Make America AI-Ready' initiative (March 25, 2026): free AI literacy course delivered entirely via text message. Access: text 'READY' to 20202. Duration: 7 days, 10 minutes per day. Built through public-private partnership with Arist (AI-powered learning platform). Content: 5 foundational areas from AI Literacy Framework (released mid-February 2026). Privacy: phone numbers not sold or shared, only used for course delivery. Secretary of Labor Lori Chavez-DeRemer announced. Designed to be accessible regardless of laptop or internet access. After completion, users offered advanced AI skill resources tailored to goals. Addresses growing AI skills gap; K-12 AI literacy also being pushed by many AI firms.
