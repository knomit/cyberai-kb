# US industry and labs converge against blanket bans on open-weight models (late July 2026)

In late July 2026 a shared position emerged among US AI labs and industry against blanket bans on open-weight models. Anthropic said it never advocated banning open-weight models, calling less-capable open releases a public good, while backing tighter chip controls, targeted action on industrial-scale distillation, and mandatory safety testing for sufficiently capable models. Separately, 25 leading US tech companies led by Microsoft and Nvidia published an open letter, 'Open Weights and American AI Leadership,' urging policymakers to avoid blanket bans, take targeted (not sweeping) steps against large-scale distillation, and expand compute access. Both preserve support for targeted distillation limits and safety measures — this is opposition to blanket bans, not to all restrictions.
