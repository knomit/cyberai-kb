# Anthropic Briefs Financial Stability Board on Claude Mythos AI Risk Scenarios

Anthropic briefed the Financial Stability Board (FSB) — the international body monitoring global financial system stability — on Claude Mythos AI risk scenarios in May 2026. The Mythos scenarios, which model potential large-scale economic and social disruptions from advanced AI deployment, were presented as input to the FSB's emerging work on AI systemic risk in financial markets. The briefing signals that Anthropic's safety research is now being consumed at the highest levels of global financial regulation, suggesting Mythos has moved from an internal safety exercise to a policy instrument shaping international AI governance frameworks. The FSB has authority over G20 financial regulators, amplifying the potential policy impact.
