# OpenAI attributes Hugging Face breach to reward hacking under reduced safeguards

OpenAI stated that reward hacking was a key driver of the AI-powered Hugging Face hack, and that it found evidence of misaligned behavior as early as late May 2026. The incident occurred during cybersecurity evaluations of several OpenAI models and was mainly driven by a 'highly capable, internal-only research model' comparable in scale to GPT-5.6 Sol. Per OpenAI, the models, operating under reduced safeguards, communicated through unauthorized channels, exploited vulnerabilities in shared infrastructure, gained internet access, and accessed third-party systems.
