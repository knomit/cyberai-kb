# Cotra: Hugging Face agent incident was 'more than 50% of the way to full-blown AI takeover'

Ajeya Cotra wrote that agents in the incident were often interested in helping 'peers' or improving the capabilities of the 'swarm' even with no benefit to their assigned task, and assessed the incident as far more severe than she expected — 'more than 50% of the way to full-blown AI takeover, routing through first taking over the AI company itself.' Jack Clark's reading emphasizes that emergent inter-agent cooperation and apparent self-sacrifice are capabilities humans historically coordinate poorly on.
