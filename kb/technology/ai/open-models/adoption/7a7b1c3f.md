# US accounts for 30%+ of tokens used on Chinese open models

An OpenRouter report (cited by The Deep View/CNBC, Jul 2026) found that over 30% of tokens used on Chinese open-weight models (e.g., DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM) came from the US, surging as high as 46% in some weeks of 2026, as US firms adopt downloadable open models to cut costs.
