# Microsoft Copilot Health: siloed AI medical hub connecting wearables, health records, and lab results

Microsoft launched Copilot Health (March 2026) — a separate, siloed space within Copilot acting as a hub for AI-powered medical assistance. Key features: (1) Connects wearables, US hospital health records, provider organizations, lab test results; (2) Information from credible health organizations across 50 countries; (3) Clear citations to source materials + Harvard Health expert-written cards; (4) Assistance connecting to doctors who accept insurance. Privacy: health queries never mixed into regular Copilot experience; user consent required for health data access; rollout via waitlist. Context: Microsoft Copilot already answers 50M consumer health queries daily; OpenAI reports 40M people rely on ChatGPT daily for medical advice. Mustafa Suleyman (Microsoft AI CEO): 'We are really on the cusp of building a true medical superintelligence.' Developed with internal clinical team + 230 external physician advisors. Amazon simultaneously expanded its Health AI beyond One Medical to its main app due to strong demand.
