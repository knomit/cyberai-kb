# Anthropic Claude Cowork usage breakdown from 1.2M sessions

Anthropic released a report on Claude Cowork usage: from a sample of 1.2 million anonymized, aggregated Cowork sessions, about a third (33.4%) involved business operations, followed by content creation and copywriting (16.4%), software development (8.7%), and DevOps and infrastructure (7.0%). Reported by The Deep View, July 8 2026.
