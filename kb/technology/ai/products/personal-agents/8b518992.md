# Google is piloting a family agent with its own cloud computer and Google account for up to six people

Google announced an experimental family agent that runs on its own dedicated cloud computer and its own Google account, turning shared household emails, files, and calendars into daily briefings and updated plans. It can fill out forms and coordinate activities for up to six people, and asks permission before acting outside the group. The experiment is US-only with a waitlist. Operational consequence: the notable architectural choice is that the agent has its own identity and compute rather than acting as a delegate of one user's account — which is what makes multi-person shared-context coordination possible, and also what makes the permission boundary at the group edge the critical control.
