# Google Android 'Gemini Intelligence' Launch: Rambler, Task Automation, Custom Widgets (May 2026)

Google announced Gemini Intelligence for Android, rebranding Android AI as a suite of proactive features rolling out to Samsung Galaxy and Pixel phones starting summer 2026. Key features: (1) Rambler — built into Gboard, filters 'ums' and self-corrections from voice dictation, switches languages seamlessly (similar to Wispr Flow but natively integrated); (2) Task Automation expansion — takes screen/image context, now works across more apps; (3) Autofill — adapts to fill online forms using Personal Intelligence; (4) Custom Widgets — build widgets from natural language. VP of Product Mindy Brooks: 'Android fundamentally evolving from an operating system to an intelligence system.' New Material 3 Expressive design language.
