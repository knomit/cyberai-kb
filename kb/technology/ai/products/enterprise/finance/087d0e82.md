# AI Finance Vertical Wave: Claude, OpenAI+PwC, Perplexity Simultaneous Finance Launches (May 2026)

Three major AI platforms launched simultaneous finance-vertical products in May 2026, signaling coordinated race for the financial services market ahead of IPOs: (1) Anthropic — Claude financial service agents: ready-to-run templates for pitch books, KYC screening, valuations, closing books; Claude add-ins now work across Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and soon Outlook (refs: https://www.anthropic.com/news/finance-agents); (2) OpenAI + PwC — 'Native Finance Function': collaboration to build agents around core finance rhythms (planning, forecasting, payments, treasury, taxes, accounting); procurement agent already in production inside OpenAI's own finance org (refs: https://www.pwc.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/pwc-openai-native-finance-function.html); (3) Perplexity — Computer for Financial Services: 35 dedicated analyst workflows with licensed data from Morningstar and Pitchbook. Key risk identified by Terra Higginson (Info-Tech Research Group): if all institutions use same models on same data and reach same conclusions, 'markets could become more crowded, more reactive, and potentially more volatile.' The edge is sharpening human judgment on when NOT to trust the model. Finance is highly regulated, high-stakes — human-in-loop non-negotiable.
