# The May 2026 finance-agent wave put three vendors' agents on the same licensed data for the same workflows — the homogenization the convergence records celebrate is the mechanism behind the volatility risk one of them names

This corpus records agentic AI convergence as a competitive story: every major vendor shipping agents into the same surfaces, on the same shapes, at the same time. One record in the same cluster names the consequence of that convergence for the buyer's side, and the two are nowhere connected.

THE CONVERGENCE, IN ONE SECTOR AND ONE MONTH. Three major platforms launched finance-vertical products simultaneously in May 2026:
- Anthropic: Claude financial-service agents with ready-to-run templates for pitch books, KYC screening, valuations and closing books, with Claude add-ins across Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint and Word (Outlook to follow).
- OpenAI + PwC: 'Native Finance Function', building agents around core finance rhythms — planning, forecasting, payments, treasury, taxes, accounting — with a procurement agent already in production inside OpenAI's own finance organisation.
- Perplexity: Computer for Financial Services, 35 dedicated analyst workflows running on LICENSED DATA FROM MORNINGSTAR AND PITCHBOOK.
Same sector, same month, overlapping workflows, and in at least one case the same commercial data vendors any competing analyst would license.

THE RISK, NAMED IN THE SAME RECORD. Terra Higginson (Info-Tech Research Group): if all institutions use the same models on the same data and reach the same conclusions, 'markets could become more crowded, more reactive, and potentially more volatile.' The stated edge shifts to sharpening human judgement about WHEN NOT TO TRUST THE MODEL. Finance being highly regulated and high-stakes, the record treats human-in-the-loop as non-negotiable.

THE CONNECTION. Correlated conclusions are not a side effect of vendor convergence — they are what convergence looks like from the buyer's position. Every property the competitive records treat as evidence of a healthy race (all vendors shipping the same capability, into the same surfaces, at the same time, drawing on the same licensed sources) is simultaneously a property that reduces variance in what institutions conclude. The convergence records describe the mechanism; this record supplies the consequence they omit.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN — load-bearing:
- SCOPE IS FINANCE. The evidence for shared data sources and overlapping workflows is specific to the May 2026 finance wave. This record does NOT generalize the correlation risk to agent adoption at large; no source supports that, and sectors differ in whether institutions draw on common licensed data.
- It does NOT claim the volatility has occurred or been measured. Higginson's statement is a forward-looking risk assessment by a named analyst, not an observation. No source here reports correlated behaviour, crowding, or a volatility change.
- It does NOT claim the three products are equivalent. Their workflows overlap but are not identical, institutions can and do use several vendors, and human-in-the-loop review — which the record treats as non-negotiable in this sector — is precisely the mechanism that would decorrelate outputs.
- It does NOT criticize the convergence records. Their claims about competitive dynamics stand on their own evidence; the point is that they and the risk record describe one phenomenon from two sides and neither cites the other.
- The convergence records are same-week or same-month vendor-announcement clusters, explicitly not measured adoption — none reports usage, retention, or outcome data.
