# OpenAI Guaranteed Capacity: 1-3 Year Compute Commitments for Enterprise Mission-Critical AI

On May 20, 2026, OpenAI unveiled Guaranteed Capacity — long-term compute access commitments for enterprise customers:

- Commitment durations: 1 to 3 years, with discounts based on duration
- Guarantees certainty of access to compute at specific 'spend levels'
- Usable across OpenAI products and supported cloud providers and model plans
- Available until OpenAI sells out its allocated capacity (with future rounds planned)

Sam Altman (X): 'Customers are increasingly asking us for certainty on capacity. As models get better, we expect that the world will be capacity-constrained for some time.'

Strategic rationale: OpenAI is targeting large, regulated enterprises (healthcare, finance) that require guaranteed uptime and predictability before embedding AI in mission-critical infrastructure. Follows Multipath Reliable Connection (MRC) networking protocol announcement — together these signal OpenAI's ambition to become part of the foundational AI compute stack, not just a model provider.

Context: OpenAI also partnered with 1Password for secure Codex authentication and with Dell to bring Codex to hybrid/on-premise enterprises — all aimed at proving trustworthiness to 'the big kids' in regulated industries.
