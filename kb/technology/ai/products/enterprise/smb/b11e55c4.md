# Anthropic Claude for Small Business Launch — 15 Workflows, 15 Skills, SMB-Focused Agents (May 14 2026)

Anthropic launched 'Claude for Small Business' targeting the SMB market, which accounts for 44% of US GDP but has lagged enterprise in AI adoption. Includes: 15 ready-to-run agentic workflows (finance, operations, sales, HR) and 15 skills built on repeatable SMB tasks. Connected apps at launch: Intuit QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365. One-click plugin install. Partnered with PayPal to release free 'AI Fluency for Small Business' course. Also supporting Workday Foundation Solopreneurship Accelerator (15 solo entrepreneurs) and partnering with Accion Opportunity Fund, Community Reinvestment Fund USA, and Pacific Community Ventures. Co-founder Daniela Amodei: 'AI is the first technology that can finally close that gap.' Separately, Anthropic's revenue run rate reportedly on track to hit $50B by June 2026.
