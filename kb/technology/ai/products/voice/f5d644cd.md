# OpenAI launches GPT-Live full-duplex voice model

OpenAI launched GPT-Live (reported by TLDR AI and The Deep View, Jul 2026), a voice model with a full-duplex architecture that listens and speaks simultaneously, uses conversational backchannel cues, and offloads complex reasoning to GPT-5.5 to keep dialogue flowing. It ships as GPT-Live-1 (for paid ChatGPT tiers) and GPT-Live-1 Mini (default for free users).
