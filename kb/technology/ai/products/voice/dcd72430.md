# Wispr Flow: AI voice-to-text with reasoning and personalization; 270 Fortune 500 companies

Wispr Flow (founded 2021 by Tanay Kothari) is a voice-to-text tool that layers AI reasoning over voice input rather than simple transcription. Differentiators: uses screen context, conversation history, and user corrections to resolve ambiguities (mumbled names, noisy rooms, rapid speech). 'Correct the same mistake twice, and you never have to correct it again.' Wispr pivoted mid-2024 from a neural signal wearable (B2C wasn't ready) to a B2B voice tool after observing employees using $10 microphones. Company went from 40 to 5 people. Now used by 270+ Fortune 500 companies. ROI case: Clay (sales intelligence) — half the GTM org adopted within one month with no mandate; 52% faster customer response times; estimated $3.08M annual cost savings. Roadmap: voice-to-action (triggers real workflows: drafting, scheduling, document updates), developer ecosystem, wearable hardware. Kothari: 'Each phase has to earn the next.'
