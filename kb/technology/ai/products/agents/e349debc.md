# Perplexity Personal Computer expands to enterprise with $2.8B labor-equivalent work claim

Perplexity launched enterprise upgrades for its Personal Computer agent at Ask NYC event. New features: 50+ prebuilt workflow templates (shareable, schedulable, asynchronous), Databricks and Snowflake connectors for enterprise data, 1Password integration that allows Computer to take actions inside password-protected tools while keeping credentials private from AI models. New Computer for Financial Services offering lets users bring their own licenses for Carbon Arc, Daloopa, Morningstar, and PitchBook. Equity Research Council workflow recreates panel-style research review. Previously Max-tier only, now available to Pro subscribers on Mac and via Microsoft Teams/Microsoft Marketplace. Computer also available as native side panel in Excel (beta). Perplexity claims Computer has performed more than $2.8B in labor-equivalent work since launch; in its first four internal weeks on Slack, it performed $1.6M worth of work.
