# OpenAI shipped an Apple Messages plugin for ChatGPT on macOS

On Thursday 2026-08-20 OpenAI launched an Apple Messages plugin that lets ChatGPT find specific messages, summarize conversations, and compose and send messages. It works with iMessage, SMS, and RCS.

Distribution at launch: the ChatGPT desktop app for macOS. The Deep View reported it as limited to ChatGPT on Mac via ChatGPT Work and Codex; 9to5Mac reported it as available across all plans in the macOS desktop app.

Demo use cases shown include asking ChatGPT which threads from the previous day warrant follow-up (returning a bulleted list with suggested replies the user reviews before sending), and asking it to check the calendar and compose a text proposing free times.

The plugin grants ChatGPT broad access to message history, which carries privacy and control risks — notably around granting persistent approval. It also lands ahead of Apple's iOS 27 Siri overhaul, previewed at WWDC in May 2026 and expected to ship around the September iPhone event, which promises comparable capabilities across the whole Apple device ecosystem rather than Mac only.
