# OpenAI GPT-Rosalind: first frontier reasoning model purpose-built for life sciences

OpenAI released GPT-Rosalind (named after Rosalind Franklin, DNA structure discoverer) in mid-April 2026 — its first frontier reasoning model designed exclusively for life sciences. Capabilities: in-depth understanding of genomics, chemistry, protein engineering; designed for drug discovery, translational medicine, biology research. On RNA sequence function prediction, GPT-Rosalind outperformed 95% of human experts. Bundled with a 'Life Sciences research plugin' connecting to 50+ scientific databases and tools for protein structures, DNA sequences, scientific papers. Access restricted to approved organizations due to dual-use risk: launch partners include Amgen, Moderna, Allen Institute, and Thermo Fisher Scientific. OpenAI target: 'every scientist to become their own research team.' Signals shift from general-purpose to domain-specific restricted frontier models.
