# OpenAI merges Codex into ChatGPT and launches ChatGPT Work and Sites

Alongside GPT-5.6, OpenAI moved Codex into the ChatGPT app and launched ChatGPT Work (a GPT-5.6-powered agent workspace that gathers context from team tools and acts across files, with a Chat/Work toggle on web and mobile) and ChatGPT Sites (build and deploy a website from a natural-language description). OpenAI cited ~1 billion ChatGPT users and 5M+ weekly Codex users. OpenClaw founder Peter Steinberger joined OpenAI's Codex team.
