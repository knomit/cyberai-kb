# OpenAI launches ChatGPT Workspace Agents for team workflows

OpenAI launched ChatGPT 'Workspace agents' on April 23, 2026 — a new tab in ChatGPT's left navbar powered by Codex that lets teams build, share, and deploy agents across organizations. Agents can gather context from internal docs, messaging platforms, and databases; coordinate multi-step workflows with human-in-the-loop approvals; and operate with persistent context about how a team works. Key risks: data access/governance challenges, accuracy and oversight requirements, and potential misuse of business-critical data access.
