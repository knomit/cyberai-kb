# OpenAI launches ChatGPT Images 2.0 with thinking and web-browsing capabilities

OpenAI released ChatGPT Images 2.0 (model: gpt-image-2) on April 22, 2026. Key features: thinking capabilities (searches web, double-checks outputs), generates up to 8 distinct images per prompt with character/object continuity, improved multilingual text rendering (Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Bengali), knowledge cutoff of December 2025, wider aspect ratio support (up to 3:1 and 1:3), and stronger style fidelity. Available to all ChatGPT and Codex users; thinking features for Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise only. Also available via API. ChatGPT has generated over 1 billion images per week.
