# OpenAI launches ChatGPT 'Dots' always-on agents at DevDay (Sep 29, 2026)

At its fourth DevDay (Sep 29, 2026, San Francisco) OpenAI launched Dots: always-on agents with their own cloud computer, powered by GPT-6 Astra, working across apps such as Slack, Messages and Teams (TLDR: 4,000+ app integrations). Users set Custom Rules for what Dots may do alone, must ask about, or must never do; sensitive actions like changing a password or deleting data need explicit permission. Business/Enterprise/Edu Dots are not used for model training by default. Launch is limited to Pro and Business Premium users; one Dot per user initially, teams of Dots planned. OpenAI cites 35M+ weekly users across ChatGPT Work and Codex.
