# OpenAI folded Codex into ChatGPT alongside GPT-5.6, launching ChatGPT Work and ChatGPT Sites and opening Codex's agent capabilities to non-coders

MERGED from two newsletter records of the same product move, each carrying details the other omitted. Neither record dates the announcement precisely; both place it alongside the GPT-5.6 release.

THE MOVE. OpenAI collapsed the wall between ChatGPT and Codex, putting ALL of Codex's agent capabilities inside ChatGPT so users no longer toggle between apps, and opening it BEYOND SOFTWARE DEVELOPERS. The same Codex features are available on computer and phone within ChatGPT. Alex Embiricos (head of enterprise product) framed this as a SECOND PHASE: turning useful agents into capabilities for everyone.

WHAT SHIPPED ALONGSIDE.
- CHATGPT WORK — a GPT-5.6-powered agent workspace that gathers context from team tools and acts across files, with a Chat/Work toggle on web and mobile.
- CHATGPT SITES — build and deploy a website from a natural-language description.
- SIX ROLE-SPECIFIC PLUGINS targeting creative production, sales, and public-equity investing.

NON-CODER USE CASES DEMONSTRATED. Drafting and sending messages via connected email or Slack; calendar briefings via a plugin; scheduled daily automations such as an 8 AM meeting-and-inbox summary.

SCALE FIGURES CITED BY OPENAI. Approximately 1 BILLION ChatGPT users and 5M+ WEEKLY CODEX USERS.

PERSONNEL. OpenClaw founder Peter Steinberger joined OpenAI's Codex team.

COMPETITION. Codex's main rival in this space is Anthropic's Claude Code.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: both source records are single-newsletter reporting at low confidence (0.65), one of them with ZERO recorded independent sources. The user and weekly-active figures are OpenAI's own. Nothing here reports adoption of the new non-coder surfaces, retention, or whether the plugins were used. This record does not establish the announcement date beyond its association with GPT-5.6.
