# Anthropic merged Claude Cowork and Claude chat into one Claude, adding Docs and Slides

On 16 September 2026 Anthropic announced that Claude Cowork (its work agent) and the Claude chatbot are merging into a single 'One Claude' experience, so users can ask questions and assign tasks in one interface instead of context switching between two. This SUPERSEDES the earlier arrangement in which Cowork and Claude Design were separate surfaces. Anthropic's stated reason: 'We built Cowork as a separate place for bigger work, and Design for visual work. People used both, and told us the frustrating part was deciding where a task belonged.' Alongside the merge it launched Claude Docs and Claude Slides and integrated Claude Design into the platform. Slide decks can be edited and presented directly in Claude or exported as PowerPoint or PDF; one-page visual and graphic designs can be requested in the chat; documents support collaborative editing, section drafting, questions and comments, and start private before colleagues can collaborate; users can check on Claude's progress on assignments and Claude can check in with users before taking actions, leaving users the final say. The rollout is staged, and the stages must be stated together: Pro and Max plans over the following few weeks, with Team and Free plans following, and enterprise admins hearing from Anthropic at least 30 days before anything changes for their organizations. This does NOT mean the merged experience was generally available to all plans on the announcement date.
