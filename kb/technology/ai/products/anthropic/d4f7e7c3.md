# Anthropic launches Claude Design for visual creation and design workflows

Anthropic launched Claude Design (an Anthropic Labs product) on April 19-20, 2026 — the day after releasing Claude Opus 4.7. Claude Design lets users create visual projects (flyers, designs, slides, wireframes, pitch decks, marketing assets) from text descriptions with iterative prompt-based refinement. Teams can feed brand guidelines and import company styles. Export options: PDF, PPTX, standalone HTML, or direct to Canva. Can be handed off to Claude Code for development. Available to Claude Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise subscribers.
