# Neurometric SLM Marketplace: 115 Task-Specific Models <20B Params, 25/75 Frontier Split

Neurometric (CEO Rob May) launched SLM Marketplace (March 26, 2026): 115 task-specific small language models, each under 20B parameters, spanning 14 categories (accounting/finance, developer tools, etc.). Free to download; free hosting with 100M tokens/month; $2/month after. Neurometric's cost for 100M tokens: $0.40. Key insight from May: 25% of enterprise AI workflow tasks require frontier reasoning; 75% are structured, repetitive tasks (classification, extraction, formatting, routing, scoring, summarization) that SLMs handle. Auto-SLM Creator: generates custom models from plain-language description. Target: 5,000 models by year-end. Emerging discipline: 'harness engineering' using 'micro harnesses' (checkpoint prompts) to keep SLMs on track — necessary because SLMs lack frontier models' natural course-correction. Theme: multi-model world where right model matches right task at right cost.
