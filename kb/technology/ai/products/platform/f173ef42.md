# Claude Marketplace: use Claude subscription budget on Claude-powered third-party solutions

Anthropic launched Claude Marketplace, enabling users to spend their existing Claude subscription budget on Claude-powered third-party solutions including Lovable, Harvey, and GitLab. This extends the value of a Claude subscription beyond claude.ai itself into the broader Claude-powered product ecosystem.
