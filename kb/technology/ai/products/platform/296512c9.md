# Anthropic Claude for Open Source - 6 months free Claude Max 20x for maintainers

Anthropic launched 'Claude for Open Source' program: qualifying open-source maintainers receive 6 months free access to Claude Max 20x plan ($200/month value). Eligibility: 5,000+ GitHub stars OR 1M+ monthly npm downloads, with recent activity. Boris Cherny (Head of Claude Code): 'So much of what makes Claude Code great came from feedback from OSS developers.' Claude Opus 4.6 found 500+ previously undetected bugs in production open-source projects. Context: Anthropic banned Chinese-controlled companies from Claude since September 2025 — mainland Chinese developers likely ineligible. Background: Anthropic accused DeepSeek, Moonshot, and MiniMax of 'industrial-scale' model distillation from Claude.
