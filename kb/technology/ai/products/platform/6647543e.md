# OpenAI Scraps Sora, Ends $1B Disney Deal; EU AI Act Parts Delayed; Meta Rebrand to 'AI Builders'

Three notable March 2026 developments: (1) OpenAI discontinued Sora generative video app and ended its $1 billion 3-year Disney licensing partnership to refocus on enterprise products and IPO preparation. xAI announced it would 'double down' on Grok Imagine to fill the gap. (2) EU voted to delay key parts of the EU AI Act, reducing near-term regulatory pressure on AI companies in Europe. (3) Meta issued internal memo disclosing plans to rebrand employees as 'AI builders', signaling company-wide AI-native identity shift. Andrew Bosworth to lead Meta's initiative to become AI-native.
