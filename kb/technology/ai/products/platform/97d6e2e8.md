# OpenAI Sora shutdown: losing $1M/day, DAU peaked ~1M fell to <500K, redirecting to Spud and world models

OpenAI announced Sora shutdown (April 2026, web/app access ends Apr 26, API Sep 24). Economics: losing ~$1M/day. DAU peaked ~1M shortly after mobile app launch (Sep 2025), then fell to <500K. Sora 2 Pro ranked 19th on Artificial Analysis video leaderboard at shutdown — far behind ByteDance, Kling, xAI, Google. Disney partnership effectively ended ($1B, 3-year licensing deal). Compute redirected to Sora team's Spud model for coding/enterprise products. Sora team itself redirected to world models and robotics. Fidji Simo (CEO of Apps): 'We cannot miss this moment because we are distracted by side quests.' Implication: video generation is too compute-intensive to justify when enterprise AI is more profitable. OpenAI consolidating ChatGPT, Codex, Atlas into single desktop superapp.
