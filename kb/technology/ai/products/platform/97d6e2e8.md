# OpenAI Sora shutdown (April 2026): economics, market exit, compute redirect to Spud and world models

OpenAI discontinued Sora generative video app (April 2026; web/app access ends Apr 26, API ends Sep 24). Economics: losing ~$1M/day. DAU peaked ~1M shortly after mobile app launch (Sep 2025), then fell to <500K. Sora 2 Pro ranked 19th on Artificial Analysis video leaderboard at shutdown — far behind ByteDance Kling, xAI Grok Imagine, Google Veo. $1B Disney 3-year licensing partnership ended. Compute redirected to Spud model (coding/enterprise). Sora team itself redirected to world models and robotics. Fidji Simo (CEO of Apps): 'We cannot miss this moment because we are distracted by side quests.' OpenAI consolidating ChatGPT, Codex, Atlas into single desktop superapp. xAI announced it would 'double down' on Grok Imagine to fill the gap. Additional Mar–Apr 2026 context: EU voted to delay key parts of EU AI Act, reducing near-term regulatory pressure on AI companies in Europe. Meta issued internal memo disclosing plans to rebrand employees as 'AI builders'; Andrew Bosworth to lead Meta AI-native initiative.
