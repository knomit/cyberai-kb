# Mistral Forge: custom model training platform on proprietary data (Mar 2026)

Mistral launched Forge (March 18, 2026) — a platform enabling customers to build AI models using their proprietary data: codebases, internal documentation, compliance policies, operational processes. Supports pre-training, post-training, and RL phases for domain-specific training and behavior alignment. Key differentiator per Elisa Salamanca (Head of Product, Mistral AI): open-source foundation avoids vendor lock-in and model deprecation risk. 'AI is changing a lot of things, but customizing models and making sure that you are able to own that AI is what's going to get you the differentiation.' Relevant context: OpenAI's GPT-4o retirement backlash shows model deprecation is a real enterprise risk for fine-tuned models. GPT-5.4 mini and nano also launched same day — mini 2x faster than GPT-5 mini, within 3% on SWE-Bench Pro; nano smallest/cheapest OpenAI model; mini available API/Codez/ChatGPT, nano API only.
