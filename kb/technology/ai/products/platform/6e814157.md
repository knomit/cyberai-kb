# Claude usage surge + peak-hour limits: uptime below 99%, surge pricing behavior (Mar 2026)

By late March 2026, Claude's rapid user growth created infrastructure strain. Outages dropped uptime below 99% standard for most applications. Anthropic implemented peak-hour usage limits (8am-2pm ET weekdays): 1 prompt = 1.5-2x usage units during peak vs. 1:1 off-peak. ~7% of users affected. Paid users skyrocketed in Jan-Feb 2026 (Indagari/TechCrunch data). Claude estimated 30M-180M monthly active users vs ChatGPT at 900M — ChatGPT is still 5x-20x larger. ChatGPT still signs up twice as many new subscribers per week. However, most intensive AI users (Jason Hiner's circle) have switched to Claude citing higher quality. Key dynamic: surge pricing behavior (like Uber) without price changes — spreading load rather than charging more.
