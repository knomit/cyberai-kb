# OpenAI $110B round + PE deployment arm + enterprise expansion (Mar 2026)

OpenAI raised a historic $110B funding round: $30B SoftBank, $30B Nvidia, $50B Amazon. Valuation exceeds GDP of 168 countries. Separately, OpenAI in talks with PE firms (TPG anchor, Bain, Brookfield, Advent) for a joint venture at $10B pre-money valuation; PE commits $4B for equity stakes, board seats, early enterprise tool access. Fidji Simo (CEO of Applications, OpenAI) confirmed, framing as 'deployment arm' focused on embedding OpenAI engineers within enterprise clients. ~1M businesses on OpenAI products; API usage +20% week after GPT-5.4 launch; Frontier platform (manage AI coworkers) facing more demand than supply. Anthropic simultaneously in talks with Blackstone and other PE firms for similar deal (The Information report). Both companies racing to capture enterprise clients via PE portfolio companies.
