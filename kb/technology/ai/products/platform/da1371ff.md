# Apple Siri 'Extensions' (iOS 27): Opens to Rival AI Models, App Store Subscription Cut

Apple plans 'Extensions' system for Siri in iOS 27 (Bloomberg/Mark Gurman, March 2026): allows users to route queries to any installed AI model (ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, etc.). Separate from existing OpenAI partnership and Google Gemini deal for Siri's underlying rebuild. Extensions will allow agents from installed apps to work with Siri and on-device features. Apple may take a cut on AI premium subscriptions sold through the App Store. Also reported: revamped Siri chatbot app similar to ChatGPT/Claude. WWDC June 8-12 with major AI announcements. Strategy assessment: Apple avoids betting on one AI winner, instead becomes the best interface layer — models become backend commodities. Revenue model: platform cut on every AI subscription without frontier model investment.
