# DoorDash Tasks App: Pays Users for Real-World AI Training Data, Biometric Privacy Concerns

DoorDash launched Tasks (March 2026): pays Dashers to provide AI training data — photographing restaurants, filming everyday tasks, recording speech in other languages. Also piloting standalone app for broader public. Data use: training AI and robotic systems to understand physical world. Privacy concerns per Andrew Shimshock (CTO, Mill Pond Research): once biometric/spatial data baked into deployed multimodal AI, 'cannot be unlearned or deleted'; bad actors don't need server breach — just query the AI. People incidentally captured in background have no consent. Precedent: Pokémon Go imagery (hundreds of millions of players) repurposed as Visual Positioning System (VPS) to train delivery robots. Analysis: DoorDash likely profits far more reselling data upstream than paying individual contributors.
