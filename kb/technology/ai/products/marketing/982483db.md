# AEO and GEO emerging as new marketing disciplines in zero-click AI search world

Adobe SVP Pat Brown (global marketing, growth, analytics, platform) described a shift from SEO to new disciplines: AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) as AI search creates a 'zero-click' environment. AI is reshaping media workflows end-to-end — from audience measurement to agents that automate entire processes. The marketing playbook is being rebuilt in real time. Discussion from The Deep View Conversations podcast ep. 40.
