# Google Workspace Intelligence launched at Google Cloud Next 2026

Google launched Workspace Intelligence at Google Cloud Next 2026. It understands real-time semantic relationships across Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Drive to power agentic workflows. Features include: AI briefings and task execution in Google Chat, natural-language spreadsheet editing in Sheets, infographic generation in Docs, full slide deck creation in Slides, AI Inbox summaries, AI Overviews in Drive, and Drive Projects for organizing files and emails. Also launched: Rapid Enterprise Migration offering that makes migrating from Microsoft 365 five times faster.
