# Google AI glasses at MWC 2026 - Warby Parker and Gentle Monster collaboration with in-lens Gemini display

Google demoed AI smart glasses prototypes at MWC 2026. Key features: in-lens display (primary differentiator vs Meta Ray-Bans audio-only approach), real-time Gemini integration with live transcription and navigation, Nano Banana photo modification (~15s processing). Final product to be produced with Warby Parker and Gentle Monster (premium eyewear brands for design credibility). Monochromatic HUD in prototype; full-color expected in final. Design goal: indistinguishable from regular glasses at a glance. Snapdragon Wear Elite to power the device. Google re-entering smart glasses after Meta Ray-Bans success.
