# Qualcomm Body Area Network vision: multiple AI wearables as interconnected device ecosystem

Alex Katouzian, Qualcomm GM of Mobile, Compute & XR, articulated the company's vision at MWC 2026: AI wearables will evolve into a Body Area Network — multiple devices working together rather than a single wearable. Key principles: (1) Device ecosystem serves personal preferences, distributes computing needs, expands per-device capabilities; (2) Hierarchy of computation: immediate needs addressed on the wearable, demanding tasks offloaded to phone or puck — without compromising user experience (notify when answer is ready); (3) More devices = more input and context = more assistance opportunities. Example use case: riding a bike, asking glasses to find an address in a WhatsApp conversation on your phone — address and directions auto-populate on glasses. The biggest obstacle to adoption: human comfort and willingness to wear multiple devices simultaneously (smartwatches took years to take off). Secondary obstacle: paying for connectivity per device.
