# Alibaba Qwen Glasses S1 and G1 - swappable battery differentiator at MWC 2026

Alibaba unveiled Qwen AI smartglasses at MWC 2026: Qwen Glasses S1 (dual in-lens display, monochromatic green HUD) and G1 (no display, like Meta Ray-Bans). Key specs: Snapdragon AR1 chip, 12MP POV camera, 3K video, 109° ultra-wide FOV, HDR, IMX681 sensor, 5P lens; bone conduction mic + 5-mic array; 8mm ultra-slim temples; dual-battery system with swappable 272 mAh packs. On-device assistant 'Qweenie' powered by Qwen. Features: AI assistance, photo/video capture, real-time translation, notifications. Key differentiator: swappable battery solves all-day wear problem that plagues smartglasses. Limitation: monochromatic display vs full-color competitors. Context: Qwen tech lead Lin Junyang stepped down around same time. The swappable battery approach is considered the most practically innovative hardware feature at MWC 2026 wearables.
