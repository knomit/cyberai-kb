# Google Search 'Biggest Upgrade in 25 Years': Multimodal Input, Background Information Agents, Agentic Coding Integration

At Google I/O 2026, Google unveiled what it called 'the biggest upgrade to our Search box in 25 years,' deeply infusing AI into the core Search experience:

**Multimodal search box**: Expanded input accepting text, images, files, videos, and Chrome tabs — rolling out immediately in all AI Mode countries and languages.

**Information agents**: Background agents that reason across web, blogs, news, and social posts to surface relevant information proactively (e.g., new apartments matching saved criteria, sending updates as results are found). Rolling out to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, summer 2026.

**Agentic booking**: Expanding to local experiences and services.

**Agentic coding in Search**: Integrates Antigravity and Gemini 3.5 Flash's coding capabilities directly into Search to build custom responses including visual tools, simulations, tables, and graphs. Summer 2026.

**Personal Intelligence in AI Mode**: Expanding to nearly 200 countries and 98 languages. Users can connect Gmail, Google Photos (and soon Google Calendar) for personalized responses.

Strategic significance: Google is using its unparalleled scale (billions of daily queries) to embed AI into an already-trusted product. Risk: AI-generated results embedded in trusted Search blurs the line between authoritative information and potentially unreliable AI outputs (hallucinations).
