# Samsung Galaxy S26 - first agentic AI phone with Gemini task automation

Samsung launched Galaxy S26 lineup (S26 $899.99, S26+ $1,099.99, S26 Ultra $1,299.99; available March 11 2026). Key AI feature: Gemini task automation (beta) — first truly agentic feature to ship on a consumer device. How it works: user gives natural language goal (e.g., 'Call me an Uber to SFO in 15 minutes'), Gemini executes multi-step workflow in background sandbox, optional 'View Progress' button shows steps in real time, user gives final confirmation before action executes. Safeguards: sandbox environment + mandatory final confirmation prevent agent going rogue. Other AI features: Now Nudge (proactive real-time suggestions in any messaging app via keyboard, pulls contact info/calendar dates), improved Document Scan. TM Roh (Samsung President/CEO Device eXperience): 'first true agentic AI phones.' Feature also shipping to Google Pixel 10 and Pixel 10 Pro; potential extension to iPhones via Google-Apple Siri partnership. Historical context: Rabbit R1 previously failed to deliver similar agentic smartphone feature; this marks first successful consumer agentic deployment.
