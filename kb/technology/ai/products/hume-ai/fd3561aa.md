# Hume AI launches Audio Decision API built on an audio-native decision model

Hume AI unveiled its Hume Audio Decision API on 2 Nov 2026 (per The Deep View). Rather than predicting next tokens, the decision model answers many structured questions about audio in parallel with confidence levels (e.g. speaker count, language, tone, gender, background music; developer-defined question formats yes/no, choice, scale, span). Hume reports it matched a human rating panel's collective judgment (70.7%) more closely than any individual rater (56.5%) and averaged 82% over 10 benchmarks vs Gemini 3.8 Flash 78%, Qwen 3.8-Omni-Flash 76%, Inkling-Small 67% (vendor-reported, not independently verified). A bulk mode analyzes existing recordings.
