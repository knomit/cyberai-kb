# Meta launches Muse Image generation model

Meta launched Muse Image, an AI model for generating high-quality visuals, available in Meta AI and integrated with Instagram and WhatsApp. Users can generate images from prompts, apply AI effects on Instagram, or redesign rooms with real products via Facebook Marketplace. It is free for general use with more advanced options in Meta's subscription plans. Reported in TLDR AI, July 8 2026.
