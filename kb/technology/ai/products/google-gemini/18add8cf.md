# Google's Gemini Notebook adds ebooks via 'Expert Intelligence', limited to Google Play purchases

Google introduced Expert Intelligence in Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM), letting users load ebooks alongside notes, PDFs, slides, and web pages, then query them or generate flashcards, slide decks, infographics, and audio overviews. The significant limitation is that only previously purchased Google Play Store books can be inserted — excluding Kindle and Apple Books, where most ebook readers buy. Google is buying one book per person in the U.S. while supplies last; eligibility is shown by a Gemini Notebook badge under 'Tools' on a Play Books detail page.
