# Adobe launches CX Enterprise agentic AI for full customer lifecycle management

Adobe launched Adobe CX Enterprise at Adobe Summit 2026 — an agentic AI system for marketing professionals to build workflows managing the full customer lifecycle. Features: new agents for Adobe and third-party app tasks, agent skills catalog with reusable instructions, Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker (conversational AI agent coordinator), no-code Agent Designer. Supports MCP and integrates with AWS, Anthropic, IBM, OpenAI. Built on Adobe Experience Platform. Open and interoperable. Demo showed VP of Marketing role automatically redesigning a landing page to fix a conversion rate issue.
