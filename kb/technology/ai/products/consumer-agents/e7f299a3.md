# Meta launched Muse, a consumer personal AI agent

Meta launched Muse, a personal AI agent for US consumers that can browse the web, fill forms, make purchases and create documents. It is free for up to 100 million tokens per week, runs on "Muse Spark" inside a secure "Muse Secure VM" with a Sentinel oversight agent, and is available on iOS, Android and muse.ai.
