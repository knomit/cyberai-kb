# Meta Connect 2026 confirmed shipped/upcoming Muse features: voice mode, realtime avatar, connectors, AI pendant

At Meta Connect 2026 (Menlo Park), Meta confirmed a slate of Muse agent features distinct from the earlier-rumored 'Shared Agents': a Voice Mode with customizable talking speed/style/accent; a Realtime Avatar (interactive, customizable avatars demoed on-stage by Mark Zuckerberg); partner connectors including GitHub, Box, Granola, Notion, PayPal, Instacart, Walmart, Best Buy, Gap, Dick's Sporting Goods, Wayfair, and Michael Kors; Computer Use on its Mac desktop app (no Windows/Linux yet); a dedicated Muse email address for autonomous tasks; hands-free voice activation from Meta AI glasses (with camera access on camera-equipped glasses); and a square AI pendant called Charm with a 2-inch OLED screen, 5G connectivity, two cameras, a fingerprint sensor, and mics/speakers, sized about like an AirPods case.
