# Google launches Sheets canvas for Gemini mini-apps

Google launched Sheets canvas, a Gemini-powered feature that turns spreadsheet data into custom interactive mini-apps inside Google Sheets, letting users organize, edit, and navigate information without formulas or a separate app; the visual layer updates with the underlying spreadsheet. It is available globally in English to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.
