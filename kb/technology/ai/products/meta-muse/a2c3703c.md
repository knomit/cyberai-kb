# Meta launches Muse Image; can use public Instagram photos by default

Meta launched Muse Image (reported by TLDR AI and The Hacker News, Jul 2026), an image-generation/editing model available in the Meta AI app, Instagram Stories, and WhatsApp. It supports multi-reference composition and room redesigns, adds invisible watermarks, and can draw from public Instagram photos when a user @-mentions an account — a capability enabled by default, raising privacy concerns.
