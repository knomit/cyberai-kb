# OpenAI launched Presence, an enterprise product for controlled AI agents

OpenAI introduced OpenAI Presence, an enterprise product for deploying controlled AI agents across customer support and internal operations. It combines model reasoning with permissions, policies, evaluations, escalation rules, and tooling for improving agents after deployment.
