# OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens with automatic age-based enrollment and default safeguards

OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens on Tuesday Aug 18, 2026. Users who self-report ages 13–17, or whose age OpenAI's estimation system places in that range, are automatically enrolled. Features: homework reminders that trigger when ChatGPT detects a teen shortcutting an assignment, redirecting to Study Mode (launched summer 2025, modeled on Socratic questioning); quizzes and learning visualizations; 'study hours' where parent and teen schedule Study Mode on by default; an AI-literacy partnership with CodeAI; proactive cues warning against uploading sensitive content and reminding users to take breaks. Default protections block romantic or sexualized roleplay, discourage emotional dependence, and prevent the model implying it has feelings or consciousness. Linked parent accounts can set quiet hours, manage settings, and receive high-risk notifications, now including eating disorders.
