# Apple shipped Siri AI in beta on 2026-09-14 with capacity-driven daily usage caps

Apple's long-delayed Siri AI launched in beta alongside the OS 27 releases (including iOS 27) on 2026-09-14 and remains labeled beta. It carries daily usage caps that vary by feature, request complexity, system demand and policy, plus language and regional restrictions. Paid access for expanded use is planned for the future. Apple's stated reason for rationing is regulating demand against its available server capacity — the binding constraint is inference infrastructure, not model readiness per se. As-reported via AppleInsider ahead of launch.
