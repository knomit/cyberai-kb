# Apple shipped Siri AI in beta on 2026-09-14 with capacity-driven usage caps

Apple's long-delayed Siri AI launched with the OS 27 releases on 2026-09-14 and remains labeled beta. It carries daily usage caps that vary by feature, request complexity, system demand and policy, plus language and regional restrictions; expanded access is expected to require a fee in future. Apple's stated reason for rationing is regulating demand against its available server capacity — the constraint is inference infrastructure, not model readiness per se. As-reported via AppleInsider ahead of launch.
