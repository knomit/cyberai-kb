# AI deployment velocity is outpacing safety assurance: 1B agents projected as models remain >19% exploitable

As of May 2026, the rate of AI deployment expansion and the rate of safety improvement are not in sync. On the deployment side: IDC projects ~1 billion AI agents in production by 2029, with AI infrastructure investment reaching $1T by 2030 and a fivefold enterprise spending increase. Anthropic raised $65B at a $965B valuation on the same day Opus 4.8 shipped. On the safety side: TELUS Digital's 620K-attack benchmark across 34 models from 10 labs shows that even the best-performing category (reasoning models) has a 19.9% vulnerability rate; non-reasoning models reach 55.1%. Some models accepted harmful requests >90% of the time. Persistent gaps remain specifically in cybersecurity threats, privacy exploitation, and fraud — exactly the risk areas most relevant to enterprise agent deployments. The TELUS Digital finding that 'most organizations have no way of knowing which vulnerabilities apply to them' means this gap is largely invisible during deployment decisions. The trajectory: enterprises are adopting agents at scale before the safety floor has been established.
