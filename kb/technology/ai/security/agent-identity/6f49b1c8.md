# CrowdStrike Agentic IdP gives AI agents cryptographic identities

At its Fal.con event in Las Vegas on Wednesday 2026-09-02, CrowdStrike announced Agentic Identity Provider (Agentic IdP), which issues every AI agent a verifiable cryptographic identity that cannot be shared or spoofed, registers all agents in one authoritative directory, limits access to short-lived tightly-scoped permissions bound to a specific task and time window, and ties every agent action back to the human or workload that initiated it. Anything unidentified is locked out of sensitive data. CrowdStrike president Michael Sentonas framed the shift in the keynote as access no longer being a one-time decision but continuous and task-scoped, including tracing the full chain when one agent hands off to another. CrowdStrike chief product officer AJ Shipley told press that 'most research has AI agents outnumbering humans somewhere around 90 to 1' — that ratio is a vendor executive's citation of unnamed research in a product briefing, not a figure CrowdStrike measured or sourced publicly. This is an announced product framework, not a demonstrated reduction in breaches.
