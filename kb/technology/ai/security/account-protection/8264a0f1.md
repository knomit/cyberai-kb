# OpenAI Advanced Account Security: opt-in ChatGPT protections with YubiKey partnership

OpenAI launched 'Advanced Account Security' (May 2026), opt-in protections for ChatGPT users 'designed for people at increased risk of digital attacks.' Controls: stronger sign-in protections, tightened account recovery, reduced exposure from compromised sessions, more visibility into account activity. Partnered with Yubico to link YubiKey C Nano and YubiKey C NFC physical security keys to ChatGPT accounts. FIDO-compliant security keys and software passkeys also supported.
