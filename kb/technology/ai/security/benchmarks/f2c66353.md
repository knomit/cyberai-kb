# TELUS Digital 2026 AI safety benchmark: 620K attacks on 34 models — reasoning models 3x safer

TELUS Digital released one of the most comprehensive AI safety benchmarks to date (May 2026): 620,000 attack simulations across 34 models from 10 global AI labs including Claude, GPT, Gemini, LLaMA, Mistral, Qwen (Alibaba), ERNIE (Baidu), Seed (ByteDance), GLM (Z.ai). Key findings: (1) Reasoning models significantly safer: 19.9% vulnerability rate vs. 55.1% for non-reasoning models. (2) SLMs easier to crack than larger models. (3) Open models generally more vulnerable than proprietary ones, but GLM 4.7 (Z.ai, open-source) outperformed many proprietary models. (4) 'Some models engaged with harmful requests more than 90% of the time.' (5) Persistent risk areas even for top-performing models: cybersecurity threats, privacy exploitation, and fraud. (6) Progress since November 2025 benchmark in: political manipulation. Bret Kinsella (SVP, TELUS Digital): 'The real risk isn't that AI models have vulnerabilities. It's that most organizations have no way of knowing which vulnerabilities apply to them.'
