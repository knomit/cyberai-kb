# Cisco research: no frontier AI model is iteratively safe

Cisco research released May 28, 2026 concludes that no frontier AI model is iteratively safe. The study evaluated 15 closed/proprietary models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Amazon, and xAI using both single-turn attacks (standard benchmarks) and multi-turn iterative attacks (closer to real-world adversarial behavior). Key findings: multi-turn ASR (attack success rate) ranged 7.89%–88.30% vs single-turn 2.19%–64.91%. Amazon Nova 2 Lite had the lowest multi-turn ASR at 7.89%. Anthropic Claude models had the lowest single-turn ASR (2.19%–3.64%) but reached 11.16%–16.20% under multi-turn attack. OpenAI GPT-5.4 saw a 9x increase from 2.74% single-turn to 24.68% multi-turn. A previous Cisco red-teaming study found vulnerability rates rose 71% after five-turn conversations vs single-turn. Quote from Amy Chang (head of AI threat and security research at Cisco): 'No base model is iteratively safe, which means defense-in-depth is the price of deploying AI securely.'
