# Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber gated to defenders via DeepMind Fairwind program

Alongside Gemini 3.8 Flash, Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber on 2026-09-02, which it calls its most capable cybersecurity model yet. It is made available specifically to cyber defenders through DeepMind's recently launched Fairwind program rather than being generally released. Google claims 'frontier-level performance' in vulnerability detection and automated patching; that phrase is the vendor's own characterization and was not accompanied by independent third-party evaluation at launch. The gated distribution is the notable design choice: a dual-use capability (finding and patching vulnerabilities) shipped through an access program rather than a public API.
