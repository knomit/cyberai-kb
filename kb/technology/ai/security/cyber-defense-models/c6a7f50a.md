# Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber shipped alongside Gemini 3.8 Flash as a dual-use security model gated to defenders through DeepMind's Fairwind program, not the general API

MERGED from two records of the same release. Both describe Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber; one carried the launch-alongside-3.8-Flash framing and the restricted-distribution point, the other carried the Fairwind program name and the vendor-claim caveat. Both are retained in full.

THE LAUNCH. Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash (dated 2026-09-02 in one source record and 2026-09-03 in the other; the discrepancy is preserved rather than resolved), claiming improved coding, agentic and multi-step reasoning performance over Gemini 3.7 Flash while HOLDING THE SAME INTRODUCTORY PRICING. Those benchmark gains are GOOGLE-REPORTED.

THE CYBER VARIANT. Alongside it Google released GEMINI 3.8 FLASH CYBER, a specialized variant for VULNERABILITY DETECTION AND AUTOMATED PATCHING, which Google calls its most capable cybersecurity model yet. It is distributed ONLY through a restricted defender program — specifically DeepMind's recently launched FAIRWIND PROGRAM, made available to cyber defenders — rather than being generally released via the public API.

THE VENDOR CLAIM. Google claims 'frontier-level performance' in vulnerability detection and automated patching. That phrase is the VENDOR'S OWN CHARACTERIZATION and was NOT accompanied by independent third-party evaluation at launch.

WHY THE DISTRIBUTION CHOICE IS THE NOTABLE PART. A dual-use capability — the same model that finds vulnerabilities can be pointed at systems the operator does not own — shipped through an access program rather than a public API. The gating is the design decision worth recording, separately from any capability claim.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: nothing here establishes that the gating is effective, who qualifies for Fairwind, or that the capability claims hold up; all performance figures are Google-reported and unaudited at launch. Both input records carried a single source. This record covers the CYBER VARIANT and its gating; Gemini 3.8 Flash's own pricing, eval-table placement against Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, and the 'most intelligent workhorse model' marketing-continuity caveat are recorded separately at kb/technology/ai/models/frontier-releases/c2dffd45.md and are not restated here.
