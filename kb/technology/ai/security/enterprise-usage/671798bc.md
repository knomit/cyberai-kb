# LayerX 2026 AI Usage Report: top 5% of employees generate 12x more AI conversations, 9x deeper prompts

LayerX Security's State of AI Usage Report 2026 reveals that enterprise AI risk is not evenly distributed — it is heavily concentrated among a small group of 'AI power users'. Key findings: ~50% of enterprise users interacted with AI tools over the past year, but only 18.24% do so weekly (30.47% monthly, 40.57% quarterly, 47.67% less than quarterly). Median user had ≤12 AI conversations; top 5% generated at least 144 conversations (12x more). Top 5% averaged 18 prompts per conversation vs. the overall average of 2 (9x deeper). These power users interact across multiple AI platforms and generate the majority of enterprise AI activity and sensitive data exposure. Simultaneously, AI usage is fragmenting across personal accounts, browser extensions, embedded copilots, AI connectors, and secondary tools operating outside traditional enterprise visibility — compounding the governance challenge.
