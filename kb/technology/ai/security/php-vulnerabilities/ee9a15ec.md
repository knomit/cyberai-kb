# OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol helped find critical PHP vulnerabilities CVE-2026-109618/109619; PHP patched 2026-10-21

OpenAI said researchers and security experts used GPT-5.6 Sol (and Wiz independently) to find critical PHP RCE vulnerabilities CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, after two months of coordination with PHP maintainers, Wiz, Cloudflare and government agencies under its Patch the Planet initiative. PHP released patches 2026-10-21 and urged immediate updates; the FBI Cyber Division urged patching or taking systems offline. PHP is used by an estimated ~70% of sites where the server-side language is identifiable. OpenAI also released a PHP vulnerability checker and proposed a coordination framework ('Defender's Window') for AI-discovered flaws. Third-quarter 2026 CVE publications reached nearly 38,000, up 81% quarter over quarter (ThreatCluster, via Deep View).
