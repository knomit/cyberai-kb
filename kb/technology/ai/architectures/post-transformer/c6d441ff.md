# Pathway BDH-CQ post-transformer reasoning model

Pathway (an AI 'neolab') unveiled BDH-CQ on 2026-08-11, a 150-million-parameter small reasoning model built on a post-transformer architecture. On the ARC-AGI-1 benchmark it scored 29.5% pass@2 accuracy at a computed inference cost of ~$0.0007 per task. By comparison, OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna (Low) scored 34.5% on the same benchmark but cost ~11x more per task. The model needs no chain-of-thought and less training data thanks to improved memory. Pathway's underlying theory was published in its Oct 2025 paper 'The Dragon Hatchling: The Missing Link between the Transformer and Models of the Brain' (arXiv:2509.26507); the team claims the approach can scale to 600B-parameter frontier-competitive models. CEO Zuzanna Stamirowska; advisor Lukasz Kaiser (Transformer co-author) independently verified the ARC-AGI-1 result.
