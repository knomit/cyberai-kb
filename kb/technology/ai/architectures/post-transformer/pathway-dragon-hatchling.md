# Pathway 'Dragon Hatchling' post-transformer architecture and BDH-CQ model

Pathway, an AI 'neolab' led by CEO Zuzanna Stamirowska, is developing 'Dragon Hatchling' (BDH), a post-transformer AI architecture intended to give models native memory, continual learning, and a different approach to reasoning — aiming to move beyond transformer limits on memory while reducing hallucinations and compute costs. Its theory was published in the Oct 2025 paper 'The Dragon Hatchling: The Missing Link between the Transformer and Models of the Brain' (arXiv:2509.26507). On 2026-08-11 Pathway unveiled BDH-CQ, a 150-million-parameter small reasoning model on this architecture: it scored 29.5% pass@2 on the ARC-AGI-1 benchmark at a computed inference cost of ~$0.0007 per task, versus OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna (Low) at 34.5% but ~11x the cost per task. The model needs no chain-of-thought and less training data thanks to improved memory; Pathway claims the approach can scale to 600B-parameter frontier-competitive models. Advisor Lukasz Kaiser (a Transformer co-author) independently verified the ARC-AGI-1 result.
