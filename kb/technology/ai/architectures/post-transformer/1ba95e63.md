# Pathway is building 'Dragon Hatchling', a post-transformer architecture

Pathway, led by CEO Zuzanna Stamirowska, is developing 'Dragon Hatchling', a new AI architecture intended to give models native memory, continual learning, and a different approach to reasoning, aiming to move beyond transformer limits on memory and to reduce hallucinations and compute costs.
