# AI-powered cyber attacks surge: EY study — 96% threat perception, budget to quintuple (2026)

EY published a study (March 2026) of 500 senior corporate security leaders: (1) 96% say AI-enabled cybersecurity attacks are a significant threat; (2) 48% attribute at least 25% of their cybersecurity incidents in the past year to AI; (3) <50% strongly confident in ability to defend against a major AI-enabled breach; (4) 99% believe AI will transform proactive and defensive cybersecurity strategies. Budget impact: 85% say current budgets are insufficient; organizations spending 25%+ of cybersecurity budget on AI will quintuple in two years — AI's share growing from 9% to 48% of total cybersecurity spend. Ganesh Devarajan (EY Americas Consulting Cyber Risk Practice Leader): 'The velocity and sophistication of attacks have scaled in a way that traditional manual defenses struggle to match.' DJ Sampath (SVP Products AI, Cisco): defense costs must be viewed as insurance on expensive AI investments.
