# Andrew Ng: Coding agents accelerate frontend > backend > infrastructure > research

Andrew Ng's analysis (Apr 2026): Coding agents accelerate software work unevenly. Most accelerated to least: (1) Frontend — dramatically faster, agents fluent in TypeScript/JS/React/Angular and can close the loop by browsing; (2) Backend — much faster but more human steering needed for corner cases, security, and database migrations; (3) Infrastructure — agents have limited knowledge of complex trade-offs, infrastructure bugs are hard to detect; (4) Research — least accelerated, research involves hypothesis formation, experiment interpretation, and non-coding work agents barely help with. Key implication: frontend teams should be expected to ship dramatically faster; research team timelines are largely unchanged.
