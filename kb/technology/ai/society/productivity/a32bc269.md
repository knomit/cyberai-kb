# AI 'workslop' epidemic: 92% execs see productivity gains but 40% workers see none

A study found white-collar workers face an explosion of AI-generated 'workslop' — documents with poor human direction from chatbots that must be cleaned up. 92% of executives say AI makes workers more productive, while 40% of workers say it saves them no time. OpenClaw founder Peter Steinberger warned: 'If you don't navigate them well, if you don't have a vision of what you're going to build, it's still going to be slop.' In his April 16 TED Talk he stated: 'The bottleneck is no longer typing. It's thinking.'
