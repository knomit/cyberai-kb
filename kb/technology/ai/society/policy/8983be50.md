# Anthropic-Pentagon standoff: supply chain risk designation, Claude #1 App Store, OpenAI takes contract

Following Anthropic's refusal to remove guardrails, the Pentagon designated Anthropic a 'supply chain risk' (designation normally reserved for adversaries). Trump directed every government organization to immediately cease using Anthropic's technology. Secretary of War Pete Hegseth: 'The Terms of Service of Anthropic’s defective altruism will never outweigh the safety, the readiness, or the lives of American troops on the battlefield.' In response: Claude app shot to #1 on Apple App Store (dethroning ChatGPT), community chalked 'GOD LOVES ANTHROPIC' outside SF HQ, Ilya Sutskever commended Anthropic, celebrities like Katy Perry publicly admired the stance. CEO Dario Amodei on CBS: disagreeing with the government is 'the most American thing in the world.' Anthropic vowed to take the Pentagon to court, calling designation 'legally unsound.' OpenAI signed Pentagon contract under standard 'all lawful purposes' language. Sam Altman: 'I do not believe unelected leaders of private companies should have as much power as our democratically elected government.' Open letter 'We Will Not Be Divided' garnered 537 Google employee signatures + 89 OpenAI employee signatures. Anthropic ARR: ~$19B vs OpenAI $25B. Three key essays: 'Clawed' (Dean W. Ball, Hyperdimensional), 'Anthropic and Alignment' (Ben Thompson, Stratechery), 'A Few Observations' (Sarah Shoker). 78.8% of Deep View readers: Anthropic should NOT have removed safety restrictions.
