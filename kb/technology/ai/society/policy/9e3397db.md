# AI actors/writers ineligible for Oscars — Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

The Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences ruled that AI actors and writers will be ineligible for Oscar nominations (May 2026). Reflects broader creative industry pushback against AI-generated content in awards contexts.
