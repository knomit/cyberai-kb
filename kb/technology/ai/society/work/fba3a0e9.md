# Andrew Ng: AI-native teams push eng:PM ratios to 1:1, creating bottlenecks in every non-engineering function

Andrew Ng's April 17, 2026 letter in The Batch describes structural changes in AI-native software engineering teams: (1) Coding 10x-100x faster creates downstream bottlenecks everywhere else — product management, design, marketing, legal compliance; (2) Teams pushing engineer:PM ratios from 8:1 down to as low as 1:1; (3) Fastest-moving teams have engineers who can also make product decisions; PMs who can also build software; (4) Small in-person teams of 2-10 people are optimal for speed; remote can perform well but in-person maximizes velocity; (5) Generalists now outperform specialists in small AI-native teams; (6) Marketing bottleneck example: teams built features so fast that marketing scrambled to communicate them; legal bottleneck: teams build in a day what legal needs a week to review. Implication: 'agentic coding isn't just changing software engineering workflows, it's changing all the teams around it.' Ng calls this 'the golden age of learning and building.'
