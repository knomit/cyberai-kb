# AI Glasses Public Opinion: 58% Would Consider Wearing, 7% Already Do (Deep View Poll, May 2026)

A Deep View reader poll (May 2026) on AI smart glasses found: 7% already wear AI glasses, 58% would consider it, 23% unsure, 10% would not, 2% other. The 65% combined yes/considering rate is notably high for a consumer hardware category that hasn't yet launched widely (Google's AI glasses are fall 2026; Meta Ray-Bans are the only mainstream offering). The strong consideration rate suggests consumer receptivity is ahead of product availability — a favorable market condition for the Google/Samsung glasses launch.
