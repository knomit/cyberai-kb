# Gallup Poll: 70% of Americans Oppose Local Data Center Construction, Bipartisan (May 2026)

A Gallup poll published May 2026 found that an average of 7 in 10 Americans (70%) oppose data center construction in their area, with 48% strongly opposed. Only ~25% favor local construction, with just 7% strongly in favor.

Opposition breakdown by party:
- Democratic: 75% opposed
- Independent: 74% opposed
- Republican: 63% opposed

Top concerns among opponents:
- Environmental/resource impacts: 50%
- Excess water usage: 18%
- Power grid constraints: 18%
- Pollution: 16%
- Quality-of-life (property values, traffic, housing): 22%
- Cost-of-living increases/higher utility bills: 20%
- Negative views of AI broadly: 14%

Among supporters: 66% anticipated local economic benefits (jobs, tax revenue, improved infrastructure).

Gallup's assessment: 'Overcoming this opposition stands as a major hurdle in the expansion of AI computing. The intensity of opposition means that proposed data centers are likely to spur grassroots activism from local residents as well as legal challenges.'

Additional context: Only 16% of people globally use AI regularly, per a Microsoft study. AI infrastructure faces an 'existential paradox' — without broader adoption, labs struggle to deliver on promised returns and transformative impact, but the narrative of job losses and environmental harm is actively repelling the user base needed for that adoption.
