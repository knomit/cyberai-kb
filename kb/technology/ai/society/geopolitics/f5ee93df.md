# China blocks Meta's $2.5B acquisition of Manus AI; Meta prepares to unwind

China blocked Meta's $2.5 billion acquisition of Manus AI. Meta is reportedly preparing to undo the acquisition following the ban. This is a significant instance of Chinese regulatory intervention in a US tech acquisition of a Chinese-origin AI company.
