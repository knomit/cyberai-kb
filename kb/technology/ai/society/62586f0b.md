# Anti-data-center revolt: $64B projects blocked, Maine moratorium bill, violence at Altman home and Indianapolis councilor (2026)

Resistance to AI data centers is mounting in the US across democratic and violent channels (reported April 24, 2026). Scale: ~$64B worth of data center projects blocked/delayed amid local opposition between May 2024-March 2025. Legislative actions: Maine passed a bill for moratorium on new data centers >20MW until 2027 (first statewide ban, awaiting governor signature); 12+ other states filed data center moratorium bills in 2026; Port Washington, WI: referendum requiring voter approval for tax incentives for large projects (passed 2:1, under legal challenge); Festus, MO: voters ousted all council members who approved $6B data center; Ohio: ballot measure to amend state constitution to prohibit data centers >25MW (needs 400K signatures by July 1); Boulder City, NV: postponed data center hearing after protests. Violence: Molotov cocktail thrown at OpenAI CEO Sam Altman's San Francisco home (man wrote about AI risks to humanity); 13 gunshots fired at Indianapolis councilor who supported $500M data center (note 'no data centers' left at scene). Primary objections: electricity prices, energy/water consumption, noise, residential proximity.
