# Vibe coding boosts software engineering jobs 11% YoY - supply-creates-demand dynamic

Citadel Securities rebutted the 'Citrini Research 2028 Global Intelligence Crisis' worst-case AI job market collapse with current data: US unemployment rate 4.28%, software engineering job postings up 11% year-over-year. Mechanism (Aaron Levie, Box CEO): 'If you lower the cost of something that was previously supply-constrained, demand for that thing goes up. Software engineering is just one of the easiest examples to contemplate.' Pattern: vibe coding enables novices to launch apps, those apps require maintenance/scaling by real engineers, net demand for technical expertise increases. Secondary implication: enterprise AI enabling custom SaaS tooling may shift spending away from legacy SaaS (Salesforce, Workday) toward in-house developers + AI coding tools — 'SaaS-pocalypse' scenario.
