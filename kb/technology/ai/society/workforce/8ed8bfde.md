# Anthropic 80,000-user Claude study: desires, fears, and reality of AI (Dec 2025)

Anthropic published results of a December 2025 survey of 80,000+ Claude users from 159 countries, 70 languages (March 2026). Users conversed with an AI interviewer. Results: (1) What users WANT: 19% want routine work offloaded for focus on complex tasks; 13% life management; 13% personal transformation. (2) What users FEAR: 27% fear hallucinations/inaccuracy/domino effects; also job displacement, autonomy loss, cognitive atrophy. (3) Reality: 32% report significant productivity gains; 19% report AI 'hasn't delivered' on their vision. Anthropic's stated goal: 'AI helps them live better, not simply work faster.' Blind spot flagged by The Deep View: survey only captured Anthropic users; Pew Research shows broader public is more concerned than excited, believing AI will worsen critical thinking and harm meaningful relationships.
