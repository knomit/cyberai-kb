# AI skills required in 50% of $100K+ jobs - Ladders 2026 data

Ladders (US job site specializing in $100K+ white-collar jobs, 1.1M listings, 72M reviewed/year) found ~50% of all high-paying jobs ($100K+) now require AI skills (up from 20% in 2021 when requirements focused on ML, deep learning, automation, big data). By role: executive roles 45%, Data/Finance/Design/Product/Software Engineering/HR each 45-50%+. Marc Cenedella (CEO): requirement has shifted from 'familiarity prerequisite' to 'expected to have this knowledge within your purview.' Caution on AI resumes: 'AI will give you an exactly average, typical resume' — job seekers should demonstrate hands-on AI expertise to stand out.
