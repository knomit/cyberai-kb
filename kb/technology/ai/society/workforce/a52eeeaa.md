# AI Cognitive Offloading: Workers Losing Judgment and Soft Skills at Scale (May 2026)

Jayney Howson (Chief Learning Officer, ServiceNow) observed at ServiceNow Knowledge 2026 that workers are not just using AI tools — they are outsourcing their judgment to them, degrading the ability to read people, make calls under uncertainty, and trust their own instincts. 'I really do fear that we're sleepwalking into losing those [soft] skills.' This is distinct from AI replacing jobs — it is AI degrading the quality of human judgment even while people remain employed. Pattern most visible in sales: AI generates meeting briefs, customer summaries, call prep — reps lose in-the-moment customer reading ability. AI literacy (knowing how to use tools) is necessary but insufficient. Howson identifies three required skills: (1) core role competency, (2) AI tool proficiency, (3) judgment of when NOT to use AI — the third being the hardest to teach and easiest to lose. ServiceNow University (2M+ enrolled) explicitly includes the third skill in curriculum. Structural risk: if AI systems fail or become unavailable, over-reliant employees cannot fall back on their own judgment. 'You have to build that human ability just to be okay being a bit messy. If you don't have that confidence in yourself, you will not build those AI skills.'
