# AI Layoff Debate: 21K+ April Cuts Blamed on AI vs White House 'No Evidence' Claim (May 2026)

Contradictory data on AI-driven job losses: (1) Challenger, Gray & Christmas: 21,000+ layoffs in April 2026 attributed to AI — second consecutive month AI was the leading stated rationale; (2) White House NEC Director Kevin Hassett: 'no sign in the data' AI has cost anyone their job; companies adopting AI see rapid revenue growth and employment bumps; (3) Writer survey (April, thousands of C-suite execs): 60% of enterprises plan to lay off employees who won't use AI; (4) MIT study: 11%+ of US work hours already automatable; Gartner: 50% of AI-displaced workers will be rehired; Harvard: AI increases hours and scope of work rather than reducing them. Analyst view: pandemic overhiring and AI-washing (using AI as layoff scapegoat) likely explain many announced cuts at Block, Atlassian, Meta, Oracle, Amazon.
