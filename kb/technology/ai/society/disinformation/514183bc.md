# AI-generated video fakes flood US-Iran conflict: speed and scale as information warfare

During the March 2026 US-Iran conflict, AI-generated fake videos flooded social media at unprecedented speed and scale, forcing news organizations (BBC, NYT, AP, Financial Times, US News) into real-time debunking. Notable fakes: (1) US fighter jet 'shot down' in Iran — 70M views, turned out to be video game footage; (2) Burj Khalifa 'on fire' after Iranian missile strike — obvious AI fake; (3) missiles hitting Tel Aviv downtown — 20M views, AI-generated but Grok falsely validated it as real when users tried to verify. X/Twitter issued warning on March 3: creators in revenue-sharing program immediately suspended for 90 days if they post AI-generated conflict videos without disclosure; permanent ban on second offense. Melanie Smith, ISD: 'The volume of AI content is starting to just pollute the information environment in these kinds of crisis settings to a really terrifying degree.' Key shift vs. pre-AI propaganda: speed and scale are now distributed among the populace, not just state actors. Verification tools: AP Fact Check, BBC Verify, Compass from Blackbird AI.
