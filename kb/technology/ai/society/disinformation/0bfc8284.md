# HYPOTHESIS: AI disinformation democratization causes structural failure of centralized fact-checking by 2027, forcing platform-level cryptographic provenance responses

HYPOTHESIS: The democratization of propaganda capacity via AI will cause structural failure of centralized fact-checking infrastructure by 2027 — debunking cannot scale to match AI-generated fake production rates — forcing platform-level systemic responses (cryptographic media provenance, C2PA content credentials, or equivalent) rather than content-level responses.

Evidence chain: (1) March 2026 US-Iran conflict: 70M + 20M views on AI fakes outpaced any fact-checking response; (2) Grok falsely validated fakes when users sought verification — AI verification tools amplify AI fakes, closing the loop; (3) Asymmetric burden: fact-checkers must debunk faster than AI can generate, which is structurally impossible; (4) X monetization suspension as band-aid: economic incentives don't address geopolitical actors with non-monetary motivations; (5) Internet Watch Foundation 260x rise in AI CSAM further confirms AI-generated harmful content is proliferating faster than moderation infrastructure can respond.

Reasoning step: When the cost of generating false content drops below the cost of verifying it by orders of magnitude, content-level responses fail by definition. The only viable countermeasure operates at the authentication layer — cryptographic provenance or equivalent — because it shifts the problem from 'is this real?' (impossible to verify at scale) to 'was this signed by a trusted source?' (scalable to verify).

Known gaps: (1) C2PA adoption requires coordinated industry action that historically moves slowly; (2) Geopolitical actors can generate content from jurisdictions where signing requirements don't apply; (3) Deepfakes of existing signed content could still spread before verification.

Falsification condition: If centralized fact-checking achieves measurable reduction (>50%) in AI fake viral reach by Q2 2027 without platform-level provenance infrastructure, the structural failure thesis is wrong.
