# Google signs classified AI deal with DOD for Gemini models

Google signed a classified AI deal with the Pentagon for use of Gemini models, according to The Information. This follows Google's prior Project Maven involvement and comes amid ongoing defense sector AI adoption. Notably this occurred alongside employee opposition.
