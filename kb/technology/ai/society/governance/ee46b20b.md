# Altman publishes 'Our Principles' manifesto: AI democratization and anti-concentration pledge

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman published 'Our Principles' at openai.com/index/our-principles/, explicitly calling for AI democratization and decentralization: 'Power in the future can either be held by a small handful of companies using and controlling superintelligence, or it can be held in a decentralized way by people. We believe the latter is much better.' Pillars: empowerment, universal prosperity, resilience, adaptability, resistance to power consolidation. Followed OpenAI researchers' earlier policy document with 'ideas to keep people first.' Greg Brockman (OpenAI president) and Andrej Karpathy (former Tesla AI lead/OpenAI cofounder) also voiced democratization positions. Context: Deep View editor Jason Hiner noted that once OpenAI goes public, all financial incentives will favor centralization — making this pledge difficult to maintain.
