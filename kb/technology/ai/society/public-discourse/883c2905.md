# Fei-Fei Li: AI debate is too polarized and has lost nuance (Ai4 2026 keynote)

At the marquee keynote of the Ai4 conference in Las Vegas on Wednesday (~Aug 5, 2026), AI pioneer Fei-Fei Li argued that discourse around AI has become too bifurcated between doomer (job destruction / existential risk) and utopian ('solve for abundance') extremes, losing all nuance. She urged a more mature, pragmatic perspective and argued the fix is giving far more people easier access to learning about AI. She opposes slowing AI down (agreeing with Geoffrey Hinton, who shared the stage along with Andrew Ng), using a 'genie' metaphor: humanity doesn't put the genie back in the bottle but transforms it into a useful genie with guardrails set by policymakers. The event was described as North America's largest AI conference with ~12,000 attendees.
