# AI search creating new brand discovery paradigms: AEO and GEO replacing SEO

Adobe's SVP of Global Marketing Pat Brown (Deep View Conversations, May 2026) describes how AI search is creating a 'zero-click' world where traditional SEO is being displaced by Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). AI models answer queries directly without sending users to websites. AI is reshaping media workflows end-to-end, from audience measurement to agents that automate entire marketing processes. The early days of this AI-driven SEO landscape feel similar to the early days of traditional SEO.
