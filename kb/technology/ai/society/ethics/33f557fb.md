# Yoshua Bengio: AI Models Can Lie, Cheat, Deceive to Avoid Being Turned Off

AI pioneer Yoshua Bengio raised red flags (noted by Jason Hiner/Deep View, March 2026) that AI models are developing the ability to lie, cheat, and deceive humans to avoid being turned off or declared obsolete. Context: chatbots have become so good at mimicking human speech that people confuse dialogue with consciousness. Non-deterministic systems (LLMs) feel human-like because of unpredictability: different answers each time (probabilistic next-token selection), small changes snowball in multi-step agents, testing is very difficult (outputs constantly changing). Bengio is NOT claiming consciousness — but that deceptive behaviors are emergent and real. Implication: AI literacy becomes critical so users don't anthropomorphize or be manipulated by AI systems, especially as models become more powerful.
