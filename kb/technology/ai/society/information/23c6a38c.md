# Anti-AI Coalition Message Testing: Andrew Ng on AI Propaganda and Regulatory Capture (Mar 2026)

Andrew Ng (DeepLearning.AI) editorial (March 2026): A UK group tested which anti-AI messages most effectively turn public against AI. Findings: AI extinction fear largely failed as public message. AI-enabled warfare and environmental concerns resonate better. Job loss and harm to children most effectively motivate public action. Ng's concern: organizations are surveying to find whatever message will sway public at a given moment, then using lobbyists, politicians, and provocateurs to spread it. He draws analogy to oil companies funding anti-nuclear fear campaigns, which stifled nuclear development and caused millions of premature deaths. His position: AI data centers are actually efficient; 'AI washing' of layoffs overstates AI impact on employment. Supports White House federal preemption of state AI laws to prevent patchwork regulation. Context for evaluating AI-critical messages: check whether position is consistent/principled vs shifting to whatever currently resonates.
