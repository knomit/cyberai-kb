# 35% of newly published websites since ChatGPT launch are AI-generated

A study found that approximately 35% of newly published websites since the ChatGPT launch are AI-generated, per 404 Media. This represents a significant transformation of the web content ecosystem and has implications for SEO, content trust, and the information environment.
