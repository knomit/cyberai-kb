# Ray Kurzweil maintains AGI-by-2029 prediction at HumanX 2026: competition accelerates exponential growth

At HumanX mid-April 2026, Ray Kurzweil (inventor, futurist, 63 years in AI) reaffirmed his 1999 prediction that AGI would arrive by 2029. His updated framing: 'I predicted that there would be a several-yearfold increase where people would start to face AGI, not everybody would, and everybody would face AGI a few years later — so that's consistent with that prediction that we would start now.' Key arguments: (1) AI will transform every industry including housing and food; (2) Competition among AI labs fuels exponential growth and will make the next 10 years 'absolutely fantastic'; (3) AI represents knowledge expansion — 'AI that can expand our knowledge.' Panel included son Ethan Kurzweil (co-founder/managing partner at Chemistry VC) and Bloomberg AI reporter Shirin Ghaffary. Kurzweil maintains optimistic position: positive outcomes will outweigh negative.
