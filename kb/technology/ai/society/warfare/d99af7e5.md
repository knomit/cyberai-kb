# Iranian drones hit AWS data centers (March 1, 2026); Claude + Maven targeting system in US-Iran war

On March 1, 2026, Iranian drones damaged AWS data centers in Bahrain and two UAE facilities — the first time AI data centers have been targeted during warfare. Damage: structural, power disruptions, water damage from firefighting; Amazon recommended customers move workloads to US/Europe/Asia by March 3. At risk: trillions in Persian Gulf AI investment (Amazon, Google, OpenAI, Microsoft, xAI all have multibillion-dollar commitments in region); Gulf Cooperation Council has 2.0 GW data center capacity + 0.4 GW planned. Critical context: Claude is integrated with Maven Smart System (MSS), built by Palantir for targeting and logistics. MSS reduced targeting process from 12 hours to <1 minute; 20 staff accomplished what previously required 2,000. Claude/MSS played role in January 2026 capture of Venezuelan president Maduro; first 'major war operations' were in Iran. Claude was 'part of a system that helped select 1,000+ targets in initial 24 hours of US-Israel war on Iran' (Washington Post). Despite benefits, a bomb destroyed a school killing 170+ people mostly children during initial strikes; preliminary findings indicate out-of-date target data may have played a role.
