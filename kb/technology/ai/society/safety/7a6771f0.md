# Internet Watch Foundation: 260x Rise in AI-Generated Child Sexual Abuse Material (2026 Report)

The Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) released a report (March 2026) documenting a 260x rise in AI-generated child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This corroborates related xAI/Grok Imagine controversy where nonconsensual sexualized AI images of children persisted after xAI promised fixes (EU investigations, country bans, Common Sense Media deemed Grok a risk for under-18 users). OpenAI also put its planned 'Adult Mode' on hold indefinitely, citing related concerns.
