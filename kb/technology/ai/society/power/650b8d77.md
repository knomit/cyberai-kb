# AI power consolidation: utopian narrative justifies capital concentration in few hands (Mar 2026 analysis)

The Deep View (March 22, 2026) analysis by Jason Hiner: The AI industry's utopian promise ('AI will advance humanity') serves as the rationale for unprecedented capital and power concentration. OpenAI's $110B round was supported by Softbank ($30B), Nvidia ($30B), Amazon ($50B) — raised more in one round than GDP of 121 countries. Nvidia announced $1T data center revenue target through 2027. Anthropic loosened its Responsible Scaling Policy — CSO Jared Kaplan: 'We didn't really feel, with the rapid advance of AI, that it made sense for us to make unilateral commitments if competitors are blazing ahead.' Luis Lastras (IBM Director of Language Technologies): 'There's a tremendous amount of risk if very few people decide what this machine should be. We're definitely at risk of that happening because it's the way capitalist societies naturally work.' Stargate $500B project 'no longer on solid ground.' Data center demand predicted to nearly triple by 2030. Social media precedent: Facebook promised global connection, became ad empire that swayed elections/addicted children. Key question: 'If we accept that AI is too important to slow down, we should also ask: too important for whom?'
