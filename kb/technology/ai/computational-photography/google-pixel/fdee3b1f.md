# Google Pixel camera as a decade-long AI/ML proving ground

Isaac Reynolds, who has led Google's Pixel camera team for a decade, told The Deep View the team targets 'durable problems' in phone photography (dynamic range, low-light, zoom, sharpness, background blur, motion freezing, artifacts, timing). Pixel ML/AI features by generation include HDR+ (2016), single-lens Portrait Mode (2017), Night Sight/Super Res Zoom (2018), computational astrophotography (2019), Magic Eraser (2021), Magic Editor/Best Take (2023), and diffusion-based Pro Res Zoom (Pixel 10, 2025). Reynolds said progress accelerated sharply in the past two years due to generative AI and integration of Gemini-class models.
