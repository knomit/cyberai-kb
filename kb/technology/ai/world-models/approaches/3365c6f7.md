# Major players pursue divergent world-model approaches

The Deep View (July 3, 2026) surveyed competing world-model approaches: predictive video models (Nvidia's Cosmos, Google DeepMind's Genie/Project Genie powered by Gemini and Genie 3, and Runway's interactive-video GWM-1); 3D spatial-representation models (World Labs' 'Marble'); simulation models for robotics and self-driving (Tesla, Waymo World Model, Figure AI's Helix); and multimodal approaches (Luma). All face amplified compute and data constraints; their most commercially significant current use is generating synthetic data to train robotics and self-driving systems.
