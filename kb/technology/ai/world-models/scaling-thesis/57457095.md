# Runway thesis: video models scale like LLMs and generalize across domains

Runway co-CEO Cristóbal Valenzuela argues video models do next-frame prediction analogous to LLM next-token prediction, that the same scaling laws apply, and that scaled video/world models generalize from film to robotics. He says physical-AI data is not scarce; the bottleneck is time to gather, clean and annotate diverse data. He forecasts 12-18 months until fully live production deployments where policy models are world models (Praxis-1 is first generation). He also claims the US is 'LLM-pilled' while China is 'world-model-pilled' (~70% of China's token consumption for video models, his estimate). These are CEO claims in an interview, not independently verified.
