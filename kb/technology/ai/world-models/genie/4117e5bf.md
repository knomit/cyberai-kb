# Google DeepMind introduces Genie 4 world model

Per TLDR AI (2026-10-28), Google DeepMind introduced Genie 4, a world model generating photorealistic interactive environments with synchronized audio and real-time prompt-based editing. It maintains consistent simulations for up to two minutes (double Genie 3's duration) with improved physics and character realism.
