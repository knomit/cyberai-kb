# Parallelism strategies for transformer training are bottlenecked by hardware and communication patterns

An interactive guide by Edward Yang walks through data parallelism, FSDP (fully sharded data parallel), tensor parallelism, pipeline parallelism, and expert parallelism for training transformers. Its organizing point is that the choice among them is determined by hardware topology and communication patterns — each strategy has a regime where it becomes bottlenecked.
