# Pretraining efficiency gains came 3.24x more from data than architecture

Between 2019 and 2025, compute-efficiency gains in pretraining came 3.24x more from data improvements than from model or architecture improvements, and the two sources of gains were found to be largely independent — meaning architecture work and data work compound rather than substitute.
