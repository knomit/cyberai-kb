# Thinking Machines argues task expertise belongs in the RL training signal, not the scaffold

Thinking Machines Lab reported state-of-the-art text-to-SQL performance by putting task expertise into task-specific reinforcement learning rather than into scaffolding. Their argument: adding scaffolding around text-to-SQL models improves results only somewhat because it is bounded by the base model's capabilities; the task knowledge encoded in a scaffold is better placed in the training signal, and expert judgment should be part of the training process because that is what scales.
