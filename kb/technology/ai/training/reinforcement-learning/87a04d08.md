# Mercor and SkyRL lifted APEX-Agents Pass@1 by 70% post-training Qwen3.5-397B-A17B on 1,928 expert tasks

Mercor and SkyRL post-trained Qwen3.5-397B-A17B with reinforcement learning on 1,928 expert knowledge-work tasks, raising APEX-Agents Pass@1 by 70%. Their write-up argues that at frontier scale, robust environments, exact token accounting, asynchronous RL and harness design matter as much as the choice of RL algorithm.
