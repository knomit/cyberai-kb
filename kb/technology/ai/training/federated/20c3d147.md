# US national labs trained LLMs via cross-site federated training (NNSA tri-lab; ASCR 4 supercomputers)

Two US government projects: (1) NNSA tri-lab (Sandia, LLNL, LANL) federated training of LLaMA 3.x models (1B/8B/70B) across El Capitan (AMD MI300A) and H100 systems, keeping access-restricted data on-site, using torchtitan and evaluating NVFlare, Flower and APPFL; (2) ASCR federated fine-tuning of LLaMA-2 7B on chemistry set SMolInstruct across ~1,700 GPUs on Aurora, Polaris, Frontier and Perlmutter. Scale is roughly 100x below the frontier.
