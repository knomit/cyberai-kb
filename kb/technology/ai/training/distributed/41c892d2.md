# Core Automation trains 32B MoE across two clusters ~9,000 miles apart with different accelerators

Core Automation (startup founded by senior OpenAI and Google people) reports training a ~32B-total-parameter mixture-of-experts model (32x sparsity) across two clusters about 9,000 miles apart: 160 B300 GPUs in the US and 160 GB300 GPUs in Asia, with different parallelism schemes and batch sizes. Syncing happened every 50 steps, fragment by fragment staggered across each window, using a Distributed Shampoo variant. Not the largest distributed run (Covenant's 72B model is). The authors argue the bottleneck is power, grid equipment and land rather than chips, and float orbital training as a long-term idea. Source: Core Automation's post 'The Hitchhiker's Guide to Training on Compute Islands', via Import AI 480.
