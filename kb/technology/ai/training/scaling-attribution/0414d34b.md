# Most 2019-2025 pretraining efficiency gains came from data, not architecture

A September 2026 analysis published via Dwarkesh Patel finds that between 2019 and 2025, data improvements delivered 3.24x more compute-efficiency gain than model/architecture improvements. Gains from the two sources were largely independent and did not interact multiplicatively. The analysis characterizes most model research over that period as removing or pushing back constraints on scaling rather than improving per-FLOP learning directly.

IMPORTANT BOUNDARY CONDITION: data-quality gains are largest for small models and may matter less as model size grows, so the finding should not be extrapolated to frontier-scale training without qualification.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: 'largely independent' means the two gain sources did not interact multiplicatively — it is not a claim that architecture work is worthless, nor that the two compound into a larger combined gain than either alone.
