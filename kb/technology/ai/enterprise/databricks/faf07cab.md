# Databricks launches Genie agent ecosystem at 2026 Data + AI Summit

At the Databricks Data + AI Summit (June 2026), Databricks unveiled its Genie agent ecosystem — Genie One (querying/task planning), Genie Ontology (business context), Genie App Builder (vibe-coding solutions), Genie Agent (reusable templated agents), and Genie Code (engineering workflows). CEO Ali Ghodsi and chief AI scientist Jonathan Frankle framed the strategy around open ecosystems to avoid vendor lock-in amid enterprise AI agent sprawl.
