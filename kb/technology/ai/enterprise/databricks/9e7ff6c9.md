# Databricks launched an open agentic AI stack and the Genie agent ecosystem at its June 2026 Data + AI Summit

At its Data + AI Summit in June 2026, Databricks — valued at about $134 billion with over 60% of Fortune 500 companies as customers — launched an open stack for agentic AI.

Stack-level announcements: OpenSharing, an open-source Linux Foundation project to share AI models, agents and apps across clouds; Omnigent, an open-source 'harness for harnesses' to manage agents; DBRX, an open-source LLM; and Unity AI Gateway for token cost visibility and model routing.

The Genie enterprise agent ecosystem comprises Genie One (querying and task planning), Genie Ontology (business context), Genie App Builder (vibe-coding solutions), Genie Agent (reusable templated agents) and Genie Code (engineering workflows).

CEO Ali Ghodsi and chief AI scientist Jonathan Frankle framed the strategy around open ecosystems to avoid vendor lock-in amid enterprise AI agent sprawl.
