# Enterprise 'token panic' over AI inference costs drives model routing and hybrid compute

By mid-2026 enterprises faced an AI inference cost crisis ('token panic'), with coding agents and frontier-model overuse blowing past budgets. Databricks CEO Ali Ghodsi called it 'completely unsustainable.' Two emerging responses: smarter model selection (routing simple tasks to small, domain-specific, or open models) and hybrid/local compute. Databricks launched Unity AI Gateway (token visibility + model routing) and Omnigent (a 'harness for harnesses' for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor).
