# Mid-2026 enterprise AI shift: 'token panic' drives model routing, open ecosystems, and infra efficiency

By mid-2026 the dominant enterprise-AI narrative shifted from adoption push to cost control. Enterprises hit an inference cost crisis ('token panic'), with coding agents and indiscriminate frontier-model use blowing past budgets — Databricks CEO Ali Ghodsi called it 'completely unsustainable.' The responses cluster around three levers: (1) smarter model selection / routing of simple tasks to small, domain-specific, or open models (Databricks Unity AI Gateway, Omnigent), (2) hybrid and local compute to escape per-token pricing, and (3) open ecosystems positioned against vendor lock-in amid agent sprawl (Databricks Genie suite). Infrastructure efficiency is the same pressure one layer down: CoreWeave's 'Valvey'/'Racky' innovations around Nvidia's Vera Rubin NVL72 target downtime and manageability of GPU racks. Together these point to inference economics — not raw capability — becoming the gating constraint on enterprise AI in this period.
