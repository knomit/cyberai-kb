# Adobe CMO Rachel Thornton: AI Creates 'Agents as New Audience' for Marketers (May 2026)

Rachel Thornton, CMO of Adobe Enterprise, argues AI is reshaping brand discovery without changing marketing fundamentals. Key shifts: (1) Brand discovery now happens on ChatGPT and Perplexity alongside Google — marketers must ensure visibility with LLM agents, not just human searchers; (2) 'Agents are a new audience' — marketers must optimize for how AI agents encounter and represent their brands; (3) AI helps marketing teams move faster on content creation, customer data analysis, and personalization; (4) AI-assisted orchestration ensures campaigns work across channels. Core principle: storytelling and customer connection remain unchanged, AI accelerates the workflow around them.
