# ServiceNow: Hidden Management Cost of AI Agents — Token Costs, Governance, Oversight Burden (May 2026)

ServiceNow updated its AI Control Tower to include agent behavior observability and ROI tracking, and expanded its autonomous workforce with 'AI specialists' covering IT, CRM, and security. Key finding: deploying AI agents significantly increases manager workload. City of Raleigh, NC CIO Mark Wittenburg: when IT support desk agent launched, supervisor workload initially spiked because they had to train the agent, monitor behavior, and quality-check responses. ServiceNow CPO Jacqui Canney: 'Managers had a hard job before. Now they have a harder job.' CLO Jayney Howson: tracking token usage will be critical as combined human+agent usage can rack up AI bills and create data leakage risk. Key insight: AI agents are not digital employees — best treated as embedded parts of new workflows, not team members.
