# Gartner 2027 predictions: 25% of enterprise security breaches by 2028 will trace to mismanaged AI agent credentials

At Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo (Oct 2026) Gartner published its top strategic predictions for 2027. As summarized by The Deep View (2026-10-26): by 2028, 25% of enterprise security breaches will trace back to AI agents with mismanaged credentials; by 2029, 80% of all new applications will be intentionally disposable and used for less than a year; by 2030, 80% of companies with public-facing AI will experience a 'cost exhaustion' attack that drives excessive AI consumption to run up bills. Consequence: AI agent identity/credential governance and per-task cost controls are security issues, not only finance issues. Does NOT mean disposable apps make their underlying data, integrations or security disposable.
