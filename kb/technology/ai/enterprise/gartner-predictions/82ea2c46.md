# Gartner: AI-ready data lifts agentic accuracy up to 80% and cuts costs up to 60% by 2027; ROI discipline now expected

Per The Deep View's summary of Gartner's 2027 outlook: by 2027 companies that prioritize making data AI-ready will increase agentic accuracy by up to 80% and reduce costs by up to 60%. Gartner analyst Daryl Plummer advised measuring cost to finish useful work (including retries/mistakes) rather than price per token, and framing AI as augment-and-extend rather than replace workers. Cited survey figures: only 11% of CFOs see clear AI returns and 13% of CIOs report transformational value. These are Gartner predictions, not measured outcomes.
