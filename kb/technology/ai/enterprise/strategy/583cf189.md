# Capital One's open-source AI strategy: deep customization of open models for regulated financial services

At HumanX conference (mid-April 2026), Capital One SVP of AI Foundations Milind Naphade explained the company's approach to AI in a highly regulated financial industry. Capital One uses US-developed open-source models (not proprietary off-the-shelf frontier models) that are deeply customized 'to the point where it's almost unrecognizable.' Process: start with open-source, completely dissect architecture and trace lineage, heavily customize for compliance. Naphade: 'This is not your usual enterprise, where you just take something, do a little bit of fine-tuning.' Motivation: (1) Regulatory constraints require meeting 'must' and 'cannot' rules; (2) Open weights allow deep customization; (3) Reduces dependence on frontier labs. Capital One has been experimenting with AI for 2+ years; deployed first agent tool in January 2025. Key insight: customer expectations are now set by ChatGPT usage in daily life — enterprise AI must match that level of intelligence.
