# Trust and simplicity are the enterprise AI battlegrounds (Microsoft vs Snowflake)

At their flagship conferences (early June 2026), Microsoft and Snowflake both argued the winning enterprise-AI strategy comes from the scaffolding around models, not the models themselves — removing the barriers of trust and convenience. Snowflake introduced the Horizon Catalog (trust/data-governance layer giving agents auditable identities and continuous monitoring) with Horizon Context, plus updates to its Snowflake Cowork work agent (Cortex Sense, User Memory, Skills). Microsoft unveiled the open-source Agent Control Specification (controls within the agent loop), Project MDASH (agents that hunt exploitable bugs), and Microsoft Scout (a proactive personal work agent integrating with Teams and Outlook). Microsoft's Sarah Bird stressed not underestimating the investment needed to get the trust story right.
