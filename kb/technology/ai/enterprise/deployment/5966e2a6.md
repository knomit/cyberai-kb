# IBM CEO Arvind Krishna: 'Day Zero' of AI Revolution — Enterprises Running AI at the Margin

IBM CEO Arvind Krishna at IBM Think 2026 keynote: 'Most enterprises run AI at the margin.' Core processes — how enterprises make money — are 'largely untouched in the vast majority.' We are at 'day zero' of the AI revolution, meaning the revolution has arrived and companies must act now or face 'slow oblivion.' Fear drivers for hesitation: unknown risks, employee reactions, unclear ROI. Krishna's challenge to hesitant leaders: if so many companies are sharing AI success stories, 'why is your company so special that those use cases wouldn't work for you?' Strategic advice: do NOT run 100 experiments — pick 2-3 areas where AI could scale massively, specifically where the operational leadership is genuinely interested (not just mandated). Use early success as a confidence multiplier to accelerate further adoption. This framing — core process transformation vs. margin optimization — is the key diagnostic for enterprise AI maturity.
