# Celonis: 'There is no enterprise AI without process intelligence'

Alex Rinke, co-founder and co-CEO of Celonis (works with ~half of the world's 200 largest companies), argues that companies deploying AI agents without first mapping how work actually happens risk automating inefficiency at scale. Celonis builds 'digital twins' of enterprise processes — acting as an 'MRI for the enterprise.' Founded in Munich with $15K in 2009. Companies cannot see what to build until it is visible via prototyping, and by then time is lost.
