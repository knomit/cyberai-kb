# CEO AI survival fear: 81% risk job loss over failed AI deployments

A Harris Poll / Dataiku global survey of 900 CEOs (2026) finds 81% fear that failing AI deployments could cost them their jobs. 75% believe a fellow CEO will be ousted due to a bungled AI rollout. 62% report board pressure to deliver measurable AI-driven outcomes. 56% admit competitors have stronger AI strategies. 79% worry about legal risks, 57% about inability to trace AI outputs. 51% have delayed rollouts due to regulatory issues. Florian Douetteau, Dataiku CEO: 'Every enterprise now has access to powerful AI. The differentiator is whether they can turn that power into reliable business decisions.'
