# Intel's Olena Zhu: Cloud-Only AI Has Economic Limits, Future is Hybrid On-Device (May 2026)

Intel's AI lead for client computing Olena Zhu argues that cloud-only AI is economically and infrastructurally unsustainable at scale. Key positions: (1) On-device/edge AI will become critical as cloud costs and energy demands grow; (2) AI affordability, energy use, and data sovereignty are the industry's biggest unsolved challenges; (3) The shift to agentic AI feels qualitatively different from prior hype cycles; (4) Experimentation matters more than expertise for getting value from AI tools; (5) AI adoption at scale will depend on trust and accessibility, not just capability. Intel's vision: privacy-first AI systems running locally on phones, laptops, and personal devices.
