# Gartner estimate: AI agent inference costs projected to grow fivefold by 2028

As reported by The Deep View, a Gartner estimate projects that inference costs for running AI agents will grow roughly fivefold by 2028, driven by agentic workflows increasing token usage. The figure is cited in enterprise AI infrastructure marketing as motivation for vertically integrated inference providers.
