# Vast Data raises $1B Series F at $30B valuation for enterprise AI data infrastructure

Vast Data raised $1 billion in Series F funding (April 2026), tripling its valuation to $30 billion. Investors include Nvidia and Fidelity. Vast offers a DASE (Disaggregated Shared Everything) architecture AI operating system that consolidates enterprise data with AI systems using ultrafast links to reduce latency. Customers include Mistral, CoreWeave, JPMorgan Chase, Microsoft, and Google Cloud. The company generates over $100M in quarterly revenue and has tripled its staff in the past year. Founder Renen Hallak cites agentic AI's data generation as the key growth driver.
