# Google Project Suncatcher: Trillium TPUs tested for space, launching on Transporter-18

Per Import AI 474 (2026-09-28), Google updated Project Suncatcher (space-based AI compute). With partner Planet it is preparing to send TPUs to orbit on the SpaceX Transporter-18 rideshare mission. Google reports its Trillium TPUs survived radiation testing exceeding the total ionizing dose of a five-year space mission and were stress-tested for launch g-forces; cooling (radiating heat in vacuum) is the open challenge. Not a claim that orbital training is operational.
