# Google Project Suncatcher prototype ran Gemma 4 on a data-center-class TPU in orbit

Google's Project Suncatcher launched its first prototype satellite with SpaceX in early October 2026 and reported the first AI response from a data-center-class TPU in orbit: a ground-station pass uplinked a prompt to the onboard Gemma 4 open model, and the haiku was downlinked over Svalbard, Norway. Next steps are verifying TPU performance under thermal stress and radiation; TLDR AI reports a planned pair of interconnected satellites in 2027. Critics cite orbital congestion (~11,000 active Starlink satellites), Kessler syndrome debris risk, the need for hundreds of thousands to a million satellites for orbital data centers, and military-target risk (Bloomberg). A single haiku does not demonstrate scalable orbital compute.
