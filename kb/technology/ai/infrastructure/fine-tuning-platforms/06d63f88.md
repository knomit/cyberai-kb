# Crusoe fine-tuning benchmark: GLM 5.2 (753B) gains 19-61 points for $180

In Crusoe's Serverless Fine-Tuning GA benchmarks (Sept 2026), the largest model tested, GLM 5.2 (753 billion parameters), improved by 19 percentage points on an intent-classification benchmark and by 61 percentage points on a multi-turn conversational benchmark after fine-tuning. The fine-tuning run that produced these gains cost $180.
