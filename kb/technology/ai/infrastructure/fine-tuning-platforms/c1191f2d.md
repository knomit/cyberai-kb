# Crusoe launches Serverless Fine-Tuning (GA), covers 19 open models 2B-750B

Crusoe's Serverless Fine-Tuning service is now generally available (announced in The Deep View newsletter, Sept 26 2026). It covers 19 open models ranging from 2 billion to 750 billion parameters, each run through an identical benchmark evaluation harness before GA, with the exact configuration that produced the published results shipped so customers can reproduce the tests. Pricing is tiered by parameter count: training runs 40 cents per million tokens for models under 16B parameters, up to $10 per million tokens for models above 300B parameters, with no sales call required. Fine-tuned models can be deployed to dedicated endpoints via Self-Serve Deployments starting at $5.50/hour on Nvidia H100 chips.
