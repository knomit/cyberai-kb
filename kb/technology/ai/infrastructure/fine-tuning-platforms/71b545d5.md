# Crusoe benchmarks show fine-tuning regressions on theorem-proving for 2 models

In Crusoe's published Serverless Fine-Tuning benchmarks (Sept 2026), two models, Qwen3.5-9B and Gemma-4-31B, scored worse after fine-tuning on a theorem-proving benchmark. Crusoe's team (developer advocate Emmanuel Acheampong) flagged this regression publicly as a likely sign of pre-training data contamination rather than concealing it, as part of Crusoe's practice of publishing full benchmark results including regressions.
