# Fine-tuned Qwen3.5-2B beat Qwen3-235B on Banking77 at 1/15th the cost

In Crusoe's Serverless Fine-Tuning benchmark suite (Sept 2026), a fine-tuned Qwen3.5-2B model outperformed the much larger Qwen3-235B on Banking77 (a benchmark of online banking customer-service queries), while costing roughly one-fifteenth as much to train. This is cited as evidence that small, fine-tuned models can beat larger base models on narrowly scoped tasks.
