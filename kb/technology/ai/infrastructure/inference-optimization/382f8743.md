# TPU v7 megakernels hit 700+ tokens/sec on Kimi K3 with speculative decoding

The open-source project inferact/tpu-megakernels provides megakernels for TPU v7. Its Kimi K3 implementation delivers over 700 tokens per second with speculative decoding. Without speculative decoding, its megakernels for Kimi K3 and Qwen 3.8 27B deliver roughly 1.4 to 2x the decode throughput of an Nvidia GB200 baseline at batch sizes 1 through 8.
