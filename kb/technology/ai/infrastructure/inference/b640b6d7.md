# Baseten launches Grid, Sandboxes and Loops

Baseten announced Grid (model routing and governance), resumable VM-isolated Sandboxes for agent execution, and general availability of Loops (serverless supervised fine-tuning and RL). Customers retain ownership of trained weights.
