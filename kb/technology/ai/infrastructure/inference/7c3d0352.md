# Fireworks AI Forge launches: up to 1,500 tokens/s inference and Nexus router

At Forge, Fireworks AI announced ultra-fast inference up to 1,500 tokens per second, cheaper background processing, and Nexus, a model router working inside existing coding tools. Fireworks' claim (sponsor-promoted): Nexus Auto scored 89.3% vs 86.8% for Claude Opus 5.5 across 1,222 tasks at 17% lower cost; vendor claim.
