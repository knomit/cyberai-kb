# Push-based NIXL KV-transfer connector in vLLM improves TTFT/TPOT for disaggregated inference

A vLLM blog post (2026-10-14) introduces a push-based NIXL connector that moves post-prefill KV-transfer coordination and setup off the critical path in disaggregated prefill/decode inference, improving time-to-first-token and time-per-output-token, particularly at high request rates.
