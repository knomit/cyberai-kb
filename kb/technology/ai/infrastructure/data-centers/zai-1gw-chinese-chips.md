# Z.ai completes 1GW data center built entirely on Chinese-made chips

Chinese AI company Z.ai (z.AI) completed a 1-gigawatt-scale AI data center powered entirely by domestically produced Chinese chips and began partial operation, reported mid-July 2026 (via TLDR AI 2026-07-21 and The Deep View links roundup). It is an indicator of China's push toward hardware self-sufficiency for AI amid US export controls. The same Deep View roundup separately noted that Google is reportedly developing a specialized server chip targeting 2028.
