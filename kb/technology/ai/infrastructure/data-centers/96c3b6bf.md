# Nvidia and Siemens use digital twins to build AI factories ~40% faster to first token

Nvidia, with Siemens, uses digital twins (Omniverse-based virtual replicas) to simulate and rehearse AI data-center ('AI factory') builds. Siemens' Ales Alajbegovic said digital twins improve 'time-to-token' (groundbreaking to first useful AI query served) by roughly 40%. Nvidia estimates well over 100 AI-factory projects underway globally. Figures are interview claims from the vendors, not independent measurements.
