# Post-midterm backlash against AI data centers: Microsoft reportedly pulls back from neocloud deals

After the November 2026 US midterms, where voters backed candidates and ballot measures restricting data center construction, data center and chip stocks (IREN, AMD, Nvidia, CoreWeave, Core Scientific) fell. Bloomberg reported (Nov 5, 2026) Microsoft may be backing away from neocloud deals with Nebius, IREN and Nscale; Reuters reported Microsoft declined an option to more than double its Nscale agreement. OpenAI published a blog pledging responsible data center deals (no NDAs, resident input, paying for its own electricity) plus a policy blueprint. Analyst Valence Howden (Infotech) expects 'a slowdown with higher costs' rather than a wholesale retreat, with state-level moratoria (e.g. Texas Gov. Gina Hinojosa) the biggest factor. The Microsoft claim is 'may', per Bloomberg.
