# Nvidia open-sources full Run:ai platform under DSX AI Factory

Nvidia made the full NVIDIA Run:ai platform open source as part of the NVIDIA DSX AI factory platform, including KAI Scheduler, GPU Fractioning Operator, KAI Resource Management and Workload Map. Partners can adopt it whole, build on it, or integrate individual components.
