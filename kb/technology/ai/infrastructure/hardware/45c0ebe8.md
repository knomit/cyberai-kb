# Apple M5 MacBook Pro - dominant AI builder hardware with 40-90B local model support

Apple launched MacBook Pro with M5 Pro and M5 Max chips (March 2026). Key AI improvements: (1) Comfortably runs local models up to ~40B parameters; can stretch to ~90B with limitations; (2) 4x peak GPU performance for AI workloads vs M4 Pro/Max; (3) Neural Accelerators added to every GPU core; (4) New Fusion Architecture combining two silicon dies into single SoC (CPU+GPU+Neural Engine+memory controller+Thunderbolt 5); (5) Optimized for sustained AI workloads like local training. Pricing (up $200-$400): M5 Pro starts $2,199 (14"), $2,699 (16"); M5 Max starts $3,599 (14"), $3,899 (16"). Pre-orders March 4 and March 11. MacBook Pro is dominant hardware choice in AI builder community — both ChatGPT Atlas and Claude Cowork launched Mac-only. Apple still lagging on AI software features despite hardware leadership.
