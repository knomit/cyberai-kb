# Qualcomm Snapdragon Wear Elite: specs powering 2026 AI wearables ecosystem

Qualcomm launched Snapdragon Wear Elite at MWC 2026 for AI wearables (pins, watches, earbuds, pendants, glasses). Key specs: Hexagon NPU supports up to 2B-parameter models on-device; 5x single-core CPU performance; up to 7x faster GPU at max FPS; multi-day battery life (30% longer than previous gen); rapid charging (50% in 10 minutes); multi-mode connectivity (5G RedCap, Micro-Power Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 6.0, UWB, GNSS, NB-NTN). First chipset to support WearOS by Google, Android, and Linux simultaneously. Global partners: Google, Motorola, Samsung. First devices expected within months. Meta Ray-Bans (Qualcomm-powered) sold millions of units in past year. Part of broader Qualcomm body area network vision: multiple wearable devices working together, hierarchy of computation (wearable for immediate tasks, phone/puck for demanding tasks).
