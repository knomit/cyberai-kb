# Nvidia Vera Rubin Space Module: on-orbit real-time AI inference (GTC 2026)

At GTC 2026, Nvidia unveiled its first space computing solutions including the Vera Rubin Space Module: up to 25x more AI compute than Nvidia H100 for space-based inference. Key distinction: unlike Google/SpaceX orbital data centers that process data on the ground, Nvidia enables satellites and spacecraft to process AI workloads in real-time on-orbit without sending data back to Earth. Jensen Huang: 'Intelligence must live wherever data is generated.' Applications: disaster monitoring, environmental hazard tracking, autonomous space operations. Space companies already using Nvidia platforms: Aetherflux, Axiom Space, Kepler Communications, Planet, Sophia Space, Starcloud. Platform design accounts for SWaP constraints (size, weight, power). SpaceX, which acquired xAI, is pursuing orbital data centers as a major synergy.
