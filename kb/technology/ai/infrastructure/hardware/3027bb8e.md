# Edge AI inference descends to device: Apple M5 (developer track) and Qualcomm Wear Elite (wearables track) as dual frontiers

March 2026 shows two complementary hardware tracks bringing AI inference off the cloud and onto devices: (1) Developer/builder track — Apple M5 MacBook Pro comfortably runs 40B-parameter models locally, stretches to 90B, at 4x GPU AI performance vs M4; dominant hardware choice in AI builder community (ChatGPT Atlas and Claude Cowork both Mac-only at launch); starts $2,199. (2) Ultra-low-power wearables track — Qualcomm Snapdragon Wear Elite runs 2B-parameter models on a chip powering smart glasses and earbuds with multi-day battery; first devices expected within months. Combined pattern: the inference frontier has bifurcated — large-model local inference for developers (40-90B params) and efficient on-device inference for wearables (2B params). The next 18 months will determine whether these tracks converge or remain distinct. Apple still lagging on AI software despite hardware leadership.
