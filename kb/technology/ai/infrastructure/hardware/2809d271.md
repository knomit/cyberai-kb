# Edge AI inference dual tracks will converge: wearables and developer tracks merge as on-device model sizes grow toward 10-20B params by 2027

Given that the developer track (Apple M5: 40-90B params) and wearables track (Qualcomm Wear Elite: 2B params) are separated by a 20-45x parameter gap in March 2026, continued hardware improvement will compress this gap toward a convergence zone of 10-20B params by 2027, enabling a single on-device model to serve both developer and wearables use cases without cloud offload. Evidence chain: (1) Apple M5 Fusion Architecture shows that combining CPU+GPU+Neural Engine+memory in a single die dramatically raises on-device inference ceiling; (2) Qualcomm's NPU roadmap historically doubles parameter capacity every 18-24 months; (3) Model distillation techniques (Qwen Small Series: 800M-9B) are producing capable smaller models that close the gap from the model side; (4) The wearables use cases (translation, navigation, transcription) require less reasoning depth than developer use cases (code generation, complex analysis) — convergence happens when distilled models reach wearables quality bar. Known gaps: thermal constraints in wearables form factors may limit NPU power budget even as peak parameter count rises; developer use cases may scale expectations faster than hardware improves. Falsification condition: if the parameter gap between the leading developer-track device and leading wearables-track device remains >10x by Q4 2027, convergence has failed to materialize on the predicted timeline.
