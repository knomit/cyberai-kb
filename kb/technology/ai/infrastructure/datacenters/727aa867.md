# China's z.AI completes 1GW data center built entirely on Chinese chips

The Deep View's links roundup reported that Chinese AI company z.AI completed a 1-gigawatt data center using only domestically produced Chinese chips, an indicator of China's push toward hardware self-sufficiency for AI amid US export controls. The same roundup noted Google is reportedly developing a specialized server chip targeting 2028.
