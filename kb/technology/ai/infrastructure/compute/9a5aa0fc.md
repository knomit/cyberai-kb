# Meta signs AWS Graviton deal for agentic AI compute at scale

Meta signed a deal with Amazon to deploy tens of millions of AWS Graviton cores, making Meta one of the world's largest Graviton customers. The ARM-based CPUs (Graviton5: 192 cores, large cache, Elastic Fabric Adapter support) support CPU-intensive agentic workloads: workflow state management, agent routing, and multi-step coordination. Graviton5 delivers 25% energy efficiency boost over previous generations. The deal signals the AI compute race extends well beyond Nvidia GPUs to full-stack infrastructure.
