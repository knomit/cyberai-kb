# AI compute crisis: token demand 2500x spike, GPU costs up 48%, Iran war disrupts helium supply

By April 2026, a perfect storm of factors has created a deepening AI compute crisis. Three drivers: (1) Token demand explosion: OpenAI's token demand jumped from 6 million per minute (October 2025) to 15 billion per minute (March 2026) — a 2,500x increase in five months. (2) GPU/memory/storage scarcity: Ornn Compute Price Index estimates Nvidia GPU compute cost increased 48% in recent months. Price hikes risk pricing out startups. (3) Iran war supply disruption: US-Israel-Iran conflict is disrupting helium supply (critical for semiconductor manufacturing) and driving inflation in tech logistics/shipping. Anthropic Mythos limited release attributed partly to insufficient compute to scale (not just safety reasons). OpenAI preparing 'Spud' model described as another capability step-change. The crisis validates the observation that AI progress is shifting from being limited by ideas to being limited by infrastructure.
