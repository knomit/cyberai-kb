# Microsoft Acquires Stargate's Orphaned 700MW Compute from Crusoe in Texas

Microsoft agreed to rent 700 megawatts of data center capacity from neocloud Crusoe Energy in Abilene, Texas — capacity originally built for Oracle and OpenAI Stargate but abandoned after both walked away. This follows OpenAI narrowing its compute spending target from $1.4T to $600B by 2030. Microsoft separately committed $50B in server farm leases in its most recent earnings. Andrew Sharp (Info-Tech Research Group): 'Unclaimed power capacity is the new beachfront property, and everyone from hyperscalers to independents is scrambling for whatever's left.' Oracle has a higher debt-to-earnings ratio than other cloud hyperscalers, limiting its ability to fund AI projects. AI-fueled data center demand is not slowing; power and grid capacity are increasingly the binding constraints.
