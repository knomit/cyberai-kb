# OpenAI + Helion Fusion Deal: 5GW by 2030, 50GW by 2035 as AI Energy Solution

OpenAI in talks to buy electricity from Helion Energy (fusion startup backed by Sam Altman). Starting at ~12.5% of Helion's production, targeting 5GW by 2030 and 50GW by 2035. Altman stepped down from Helion board and recused from deal discussions. Morgan Stanley analysts: AI appetite could leave US with 13GW power shortfall by 2028. 'Ratepayer protection pledge' signed with US government requiring AI companies to bear costs of new electricity generation. Google also betting on fusion (Commonwealth Fusion Systems deal: 200MW). xAI and Google pursuing orbital data centers for solar energy. OpenAI's fusion bet follows its compute spending cut from $1.4T to $600B.
