# Vals AI: model routers as a new AI-stack primitive

Vals AI argues routers have emerged as a new primitive of the AI stack: they handle model-management complexity so companies can adopt new models without revisiting model selection per application. Routers share one fundamental design and differ in how they decide; the post gives an independent comparison of routers versus single models on quality and cost.
