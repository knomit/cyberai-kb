# Nvidia Computex 2026: Vera CPU (agent-native, 1.8x x86), Vera Rubin in production, full-stack AI push

At Computex 2026 (Taiwan), Nvidia declared itself a full-stack AI company and announced across the entire AI stack: (1) Vera CPU: specifically designed for agentic workloads (tool use, code, data processing); claims 1.8x faster than x86 CPUs for these tasks; customers: OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceXAI, CoreWeave, Lambda, Dell, HPE, Lenovo. Jensen Huang: 'AI agents will be the largest users of computing. Vera is the first CPU designed for that future.' (2) DSX software platform for AI factories: DSX MaxLPS (40% more GPUs on same power budget via power/cooling optimization), DSX OS (open-source AI factory OS). (3) DGX Station for Windows: runs models up to 1T parameters locally (targeting engineers, researchers, enterprises). (4) Vera Rubin: next-gen AI infrastructure (GPUs + CPUs + networking + storage + security), announced at CES, now in full production across 350 factories in 30 countries; delivers 10x higher agent throughput vs previous Grace Blackwell platform. (5) Alpamayo 2 Super: open reasoning model for robotaxis. (6) H2 Plus: open reference design for humanoid robots (hardware + software + onboard compute).
