# Nvidia Cosmos 3: open omnimodel for physical AI/robotics with mixture-of-transformers architecture

At Nvidia GTC Taipei / Computex, Nvidia unveiled Cosmos 3, a 'fully open omnimodel' world foundation model for physical AI and robotics. Architecture innovation: 'mixture-of-transformers' combining a reasoning transformer and a generation transformer — enables understanding object interactions, motion, and spatiotemporal relationships before generating video or action paths. Modalities: text, video, images, ambient sound, and action (all first-class citizens). Training data: 20 trillion tokens, 1 billion images, 400 million authentic and synthetic videos — one of the largest multimodal datasets for physical AI. Sizes: Super (high-quality physics and accuracy, available now), Nano (efficient quick generation, available now), Edge (real-time inference at edge, coming soon). Open-source, runs locally, customizable for data security. Key challenge addressed: generalization capability — Ming-Yu Liu (Nvidia VP Cosmos Labs): 'The key problem to solve in physical AI is the generalization capability of the agent. To be clear, [Cosmos] is not yet solving the problem, but I think this architecture provides a great foundation to solve what I think is the holy grail in robotics.' Related: Cosmos Coalition (Runway + Nvidia) to build frontier world models for physical AI.
