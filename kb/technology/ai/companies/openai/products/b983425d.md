# OpenAI Codex expands: 6 role-based plug-ins, Sites preview, Annotations; 20% non-developers growing 3x

OpenAI expanded Codex beyond software development with major product updates (Jun 3, 2026): (1) Six new role-specific plug-ins launching: data analytics, product design, creative production, sales, public equity investing, investment banking. More coming: corporate finance, marketing strategy, private equity, strategy consulting, legal. (2) Sites (preview for business/enterprise): Codex creates interactive hosted websites and apps users can share; partner ecosystem: Wix, Base44, Replit, Lovable, Figma, Webflow, Emergent. (3) Annotations: point to any user-created content (docs, spreadsheets, slides) and ask Codex to refine it. (4) Open plug-in ecosystem where partners can create and deploy their own plug-ins in Codex and ChatGPT. User metrics: non-developers now ~20% of Codex users, growing at more than 3x the rate of developers.
