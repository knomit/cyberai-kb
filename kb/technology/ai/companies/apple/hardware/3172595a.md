# Apple Silicon is the de facto enterprise AI developer platform: 90% of frontier lab devs, Mac mini 4-5mo backorder

Exclusive interview with Doug Brooks (Apple, Mac hardware product manager) reveals Apple's AI hardware position as of June 2026. Mac mini has become the canonical machine for running personal AI agents 24/7 in a secure, sandboxed local environment, at roughly the same price as a Chromebook. Demand driven by OpenClaw, Perplexity Personal Computer, Hermes Agent, and China's 'raising lobsters' phenomenon. Mac minis are backordered 4-5 months. Key facts on Apple's silent AI dominance: (1) 90% of employees at frontier AI labs (in SF Bay Area) run Macs, including most developers. (2) Frontier labs launch critical desktop apps Mac-first (past 6 months: Claude Cowork, OpenAI Codex, Perplexity Computer, Google Gemini desktop app). (3) MLX (launched 2023): Apple's open-source machine learning framework for Apple silicon — the equivalent of Nvidia CUDA for on-device processing and local inference; saves token costs and improves privacy. (4) Apple silicon architecture: CPU + GPU + Neural Engine + unified memory on one chip, first on iPhone (2010), then Mac (November 2020). (5) On-device AI poised to become a major story in H2 2026 as agents drive up token costs and regulated industries need privacy. Same architecture powers iPhone, positioning it equally for local AI. Doug Brooks: 'The performance, the unified memory, the power efficiency, the targeted acceleration...have put all of our systems in a position to be able to tap into this.'
