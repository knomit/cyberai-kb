# Mistral launched Mistral for Industrial Engineering with Airbus and BMW partnerships

On May 29, 2026, French AI lab Mistral launched 'Mistral for Industrial Engineering,' a fully integrated AI stack combining advanced models, engineering expertise, and robotics for industrial operations. Built partly via Mistral's acquisition of Emmi AI (an Austrian AI startup specializing in physical AI and engineering models). Use cases include design, production, quality inspection, validation, and agentic workflows in mission-critical environments. Infrastructure hosted on private bare-metal servers for data sovereignty. Launch partners: Airbus (implementing Mistral AI at the core of its operations) and BMW Group (using Mistral as central partner for its 'Large Industry Model' initiative). Mistral's CEO Arthur Mensch called industrial engineering 'the heart of the next AI revolution.'
