# Anthropic Institute: 4 Research Areas + 80x Growth Trajectory (May 2026)

Anthropic announced four primary research areas for The Anthropic Institute (May 8, 2026): (1) Economic diffusion — how AI alters the economy, productivity, job market, paid labor; (2) Threats & resilience — double-edged AI in health/education, geopolitics, surveillance, cybersecurity/bioweapons offense-defense balance; (3) AI systems in the wild — impact on critical thinking, behavior, values, AI agent accountability frameworks; (4) AI-driven R&D — implications of automating research with less human oversight, governing AI acceleration, models building themselves. Simultaneous: CEO Dario Amodei at developer day (May 6) disclosed Anthropic had planned 10x growth in 2026 but is now tracking 80x growth — 'too hard to handle.' Anthropic partnered with SpaceX for Colossus 1 data center compute capacity; agreement void if Anthropic's AI 'engages in actions that harm humanity' (Elon Musk). Revenue caveat: The Information reports Anthropic may overcount revenue from cloud partnerships (AWS, Google, Microsoft).
