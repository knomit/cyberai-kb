# JD's Oxygen AI Item Center manages item understanding at country-scale on Huawei Ascend NPUs

JD (the 'Amazon of China', ~700 million users, millions of merchants, catalog of tens of billions of SKUs) published a paper on Oxygen AI Item Center (Oxygen AIIC) V1, an LLM/VLM-centric system for item understanding, management, and applications. It covers tens of thousands of JD categories and processes hundreds of millions of item updates per day, running on Huawei Ascend NPUs — an instance of China's push toward technology sovereignty in compute. Four key architectural elements: (1) ontology engineering via human-AI collaboration, where experts distill industry knowledge and algorithms learn from it to scale ontology construction; (2) a 'semantic search then discrimination' design in which the evolving ontology is externalized into a separate knowledge base so it can update without model retraining, and the model only judges whether an item matches retrieved ontology entries — reducing task complexity, mitigating hallucination, and improving generalization to ontology evolution; (3) self-evolving item-understanding LLMs/VLMs using incremental learning with lightweight 'expert modules' dynamically integrated into an expert pool to mitigate catastrophic forgetting; (4) a 'unified item tunnel' interface supporting daily-, minute-, and second-level production and distribution pipelines while preserving data consistency.
