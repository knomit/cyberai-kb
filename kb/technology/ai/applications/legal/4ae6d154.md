# Harvey post-trained a Kimi K3 base for long-horizon legal work

Legal AI company Harvey post-trained a Kimi K3 base model using asynchronous reinforcement learning in realistic long-horizon legal environments, then trained separate capabilities for diligence, review tables, and firm knowledge that the main system routes to as tools or subagents. The central claim is that a professional-services model improves by learning the workflow and harness rather than merely ingesting more legal text. Caveat: all reported results are vendor-authored and task-specific.
