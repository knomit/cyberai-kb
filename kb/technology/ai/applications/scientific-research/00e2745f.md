# OpenAI launches Rosalind Workbench for life-science research

OpenAI released Rosalind Workbench in research preview inside the ChatGPT app. It gives life-science users a central environment combining their existing science tools, specialized biology models, and reusable data-analysis workflows, with a guided experience over frontier models and scientific tooling.
