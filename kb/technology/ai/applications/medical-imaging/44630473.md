# Alibaba's Damo Academy open-sourced Damo Radar, a model detecting nearly 150 abdominal conditions

Alibaba's research arm, Damo Academy, open-sourced an AI model called Damo Radar that identifies nearly 150 abdominal conditions, including cancers, from medical scans. The significance is the open-sourcing: a broad multi-condition diagnostic model released with open weights lowers the barrier for downstream clinical research and for deployment in settings without access to proprietary diagnostic services. Open-sourcing the model does NOT constitute regulatory clearance for clinical use in any jurisdiction, and detection breadth (the number of conditions covered) says nothing on its own about per-condition sensitivity or specificity.
