# Google says its AI now supports 300+ languages covering about 86% of the global population

In a blog post on 2026-09-15, Google announced its technology now supports more than 300 languages, spoken by 7 billion people — roughly 86% of the global population. The post, by James Manyika (SVP of research, labs, technology and society), framed Google's applied-AI strategy around 'making disease detectable, treatable, and preventable, predicting natural disasters, expanding learning, and unlocking economic opportunities for more people.' Named accompanying releases: AlphaGenome Atlas, a map of all 9 billion possible single-letter genetic changes across the human genome; WeatherNext 3, Google's most accurate global weather model to date; and the Planetary Prediction Engine for forecasting 'planetary crises' such as disease outbreaks. Other initiatives cited include AI study of breast cancer, tuberculosis and diabetes, wearables detecting cardiovascular disease, insulin resistance and hypertension, and prediction of monsoons, wildfires, earthquakes and floods.
