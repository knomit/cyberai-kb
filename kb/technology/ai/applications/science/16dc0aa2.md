# Google Gemini for Science: multi-agent research tools compressing months of work into days

Google launched Gemini for Science at Google I/O 2026 (May 27, 2026 Deep View). Four components: (1) Co-Scientist — multi-agent system built on Gemini; agents for idea generation, critique, ranking, improvement, and review, with a lead 'manager' agent. Published in Nature. Stanford used it to find a drug reducing liver scarring in lab-grown tissue; Calico Life Sciences (aging), U Edinburgh (liver disease), U Cambridge (viral spillover flu/COVID). Imperial College researchers had spent years on a bacterial hypothesis that Co-Scientist reached in days. (2) Computational Discovery — built on AlphaEvolve and ERA; generates/scores 200,000+ candidate models in parallel (solar forecasting, epidemiology). (3) Literature Insights — NotebookLM-powered; structures findings across papers into searchable tables, reports, slides, audio. (4) Science Skills — 30+ life science databases (UniProt, AlphaFold, AlphaGenome API) plugged into agentic tools like Antigravity. Enterprise partners: BASF, Klarna, Bayer Crop Science, US National Labs (DOE Genesis Mission). Key quote from Lizzie Dorfman (science product lead): 'I input some ideas, and then I went to sleep, and then I woke up and I had all these cool results.' Competing: OpenAI's GPT-Rosalind (life sciences, launched ~April 2026).
