# Vals AI: AI agents designed candidate room-temperature antiferromagnetic semiconductors

A Vals AI post describes a team of AI agents designing two candidate room-temperature antiferromagnetic semiconductors with zero net magnetism that still sort electrons by spin, targeted at next-generation computer memory. Candidates, not validated materials.
