# Anthropic launches Claude Science workbench

Anthropic announced Claude Science, an 'AI workbench for scientists' launched in beta for Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans, running locally on Mac or Linux or on a remote machine via SSH/HPC login. It uses existing Anthropic models rather than a new science-specific model, natively rendering 3D protein structures, genome browser tracks and chemical structures. Anthropic also launched an 'AI for Science Program' supporting up to 50 projects with $30,000 in Claude Science credits each, with applications due July 15, 2026.
