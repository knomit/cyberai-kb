# Claude-driven optimization gave biomolecular models a 4x speedup plus a single-GPU low-memory mode

Anthropic reported that Claude was used to optimize more than 30 biomolecular models, achieving a 4x speed increase and creating a low-memory mode that allows prediction of larger molecular systems on a single NVIDIA GPU. The optimizations were open-sourced. Anthropic co-sponsored a protein design competition with Adaptyv Bio offering up to $1 million in Claude credits. Operational consequence: the claimed advance is in inference efficiency and memory footprint for existing structure-prediction and protein-design models, NOT a new model or a new scientific result — the significance is that a general coding model produced domain-specific performance engineering. Note what this does NOT mean: a 4x speedup on optimized models does not imply 4x faster drug discovery end to end, since wet-lab validation remains the bottleneck.
