# Runway Project Luxo: AI video reaches 'story-over-tech' inflection point (May 2026)

Runway announced Project Luxo (May 27, 2026), a research initiative debuting three short AI-generated films (46s to 10 min). Each film had one staff member and cost ~$4,000. Shortest (animated pigeons) took 4 hours; longest (boy lost at sea, realistic) took 3 weeks. When screened for Hollywood executives/directors/producers, critiques focused on the stories themselves, not the AI technology — signaling an inflection point. An AI-generated watch ad got 100M+ Instagram views with no AI disclosure. Named after Luxo Jr. (1986 SIGGRAPH CGI short), which was the inflection point for CGI as an artistic medium. CCO Jamie Umpherson: 'The technology is good enough to no longer have to be the story. The story can be the story.' Broader context: ~50% of Deep View readers can't distinguish AI from real images in a daily challenge. The $200B film industry will look 'very different' as entry barriers collapse.
