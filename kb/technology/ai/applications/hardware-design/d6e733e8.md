# OpenAI used GPT-6 Astra to design a Codex voice device end to end; hardware-team token usage up 23x

OpenAI blog (Nov 4-5 2026): GPT-6 Astra designed every component of a test device for talking to Codex (requirements, enclosure, PCB/electronics, UI, software) without direct human editing. Two team members built it as a side project in under a week; a contract manufacturer built prototypes in under a month. Manufacturing readiness still needed human review rounds because Astra ignored the manufacturer's production limits. Since February, model usage on OpenAI hardware teams grew 23-fold to 212 million tokens per employee per day. On BenchCAD 2.0 a newer internal model scored 0.875 vs 0.594 for GPT-6 Astra at highest reasoning. Collaborations: expanded with Chip Ganassi Racing, new research with Mazda.
