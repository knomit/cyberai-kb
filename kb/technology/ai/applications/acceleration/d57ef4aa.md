# AI Removes Specialist Bottlenecks in Science and Creative Production (May 2026)

Two May 2026 announcements illustrate AI compressing timelines in historically slow, expensive domains. Google Gemini for Science / Co-Scientist (launched with two Nature papers) identified drug-repurposing candidates in weeks rather than years — a liver fibrosis candidate blocked 91% of a scarring-linked response in lab tests. Runway Project Luxo demonstrated that production-quality short films can be made by a single person in 4 hours to 3 weeks at ~$4,000, versus traditional crew budgets 100–1000x higher. The common pattern: AI removes the specialist bottleneck, collapsing the time and capital required to reach a 'good enough' threshold. This is distinct from AI replacing workers — it eliminates entire coordination layers (lab teams, production crews), making what required institutions achievable by individuals.
