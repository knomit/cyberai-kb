# Anthropic 'global workspace'/J-space research on Claude's internal reasoning

An Anthropic research paper introduces the concept of a 'J-space' in language models: internal neural patterns that emerge during training (not by explicit design) and let Claude internally reason, modulate thoughts, and solve multi-step problems, distinguishing deliberate from automatic processing. Anthropic frames this as a way to monitor internal thoughts for AI misbehavior. Reported via TLDR AI (2026-07-07).
