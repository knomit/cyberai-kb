# OpenAI launched Sponsored Agents and AI-assisted ad creation in ChatGPT

On 16 September 2026 OpenAI announced Sponsored Agents, which let users start conversations with business-sponsored agents after clicking an ad in ChatGPT. Alongside it OpenAI introduced AI-assisted ad creation in ChatGPT Work, new creative tools in Ads Manager, and integrations with HubSpot and Shopify. The structural change is that the ad click becomes the entry point to an ongoing agent conversation rather than a destination link, so the advertiser's surface inside ChatGPT is conversational and persistent.
