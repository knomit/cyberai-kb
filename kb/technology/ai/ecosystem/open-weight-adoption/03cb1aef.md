# Open-source models grew from 28% to 62% of token share at Vercel in two months

Reported data from Vercel shows open-source models rising from 28% of token share to 62% of token share over a roughly two-month period in mid-2026, at the expense of OpenAI and Anthropic. This is cited as evidence of open-weight models reaching a commercial tipping point alongside aggressive pricing moves.
