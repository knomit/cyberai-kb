# Mid-2026: open-weight and on-device models absorbed mainstream AI workloads while closed models kept the frontier

As of mid-2026 several independent data points converge on a pattern: the practical center of gravity for AI inference moved to open-weight and on-device models, while closed models retained the frontier. (a) A majority of production tokens route through open-weight models and the five highest-volume models on OpenRouter are all open, though closed models still lead at the frontier — the qualifier matters: the claim is about workload volume, not capability leadership. (b) DeepSeek, an open-weight Chinese developer, took nearly 23% of the tens of trillions of tokens on Vercel's enterprise AI gateway in June 2026 versus Anthropic's 32%, while running its cloud service on Huawei chips despite U.S. export controls. (c) Capable models are compressing onto consumer hardware: Google's Gemma 4 E2B runs natively on the Pixel 10 TPU, and PrismML's Bonsai 27B fits a 27B-class model on a phone at 3.9 GB (1-bit) / 5.9 GB (ternary) with multi-step reasoning and tool use intact. WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: it does not mean open models match frontier closed models on capability, nor that closed-model revenue or share is declining — only that most workloads do not require the frontier.
