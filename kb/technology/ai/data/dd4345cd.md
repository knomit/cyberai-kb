# Meta installing employee tracking software (mouse, clicks, keystrokes) to train AI models

Meta is installing tracking software on US employee systems to capture mouse movements, clicks, and keystrokes (Reuters report, April 2026). Stated purpose: train AI models. Company position: data will not be used for employee performance reviews. In a parallel development, GitHub announced its CLI now collects anonymous usage telemetry by default (users must opt out). Both cases raise questions about the scope of behavioral data collection for AI training and the blurring line between product telemetry and workplace surveillance.
