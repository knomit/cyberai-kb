# Reddit and Wikipedia clash with AI scrapers over content use

Late March 2026: Reddit updated its Data API terms to explicitly prohibit training AI models without a paid licensing agreement; existing Reddit Data API costs $12M+/year for large scrapers. Wikipedia's Wikimedia Foundation announced a 'machine-readable attribution' standard requiring AI systems to cite Wikipedia as a source when using its content. Both platforms cite 'content theft at scale' as primary concern. Wikipedia traffic from AI referrals (where AI answers replace clicks) down ~23% since 2024.
