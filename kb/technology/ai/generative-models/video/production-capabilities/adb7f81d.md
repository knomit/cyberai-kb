# AI video generation outperformed traditional VFX on face-shape retention and reflections in a live-action alien transformation

Andrew Nethery used Google Flow to transform a stand-in live actor into an alien, reporting that the model preserved the actor's face shape and correctly handled reflections on a window — tasks he described as notoriously difficult for conventional VFX and CGI. He estimated the traditional route (motion capture of the kind used on Avatar) would have taken weeks or months, versus a far shorter AI turnaround. He also used AI to insert set elements such as a TV and a cow that would otherwise have been time-consuming to stage.
