# ByteDance to launch Seedance 2.5 video model

ByteDance is reportedly launching its Dreamina Seedance 2.5 AI video model on July 9, 2026, with availability expected across Dreamina, CapCut, and other ByteDance partner platforms. The model can output videos up to 180 seconds (3 minutes) long, though its ability to keep character identity, motion, camera logic, and prompt intent stable is unconfirmed.
