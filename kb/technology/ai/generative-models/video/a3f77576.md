# Meta's Muse Video model entered closed beta with native audio and 10-second clips

Meta's Muse Video model entered closed beta as of August 2026. The model generates video with native audio, and early outputs show fine detail, world understanding, and temporal consistency. It currently produces videos 10 seconds long. Sample generations were published by TestingCatalog.
