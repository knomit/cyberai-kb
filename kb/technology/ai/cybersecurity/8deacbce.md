# AI is a two-sided force in cybersecurity offense and defense (mid-2026)

Mid-2026 developments show AI operating on both sides of the security equation. On defense, Microsoft launched MAI-Cyber-1-Flash inside its MDASH harness, which with GPT-5.4 scored 95.95% on CyberGym at ~50% lower cost than its prior model combination. On offense, STAR Labs researcher Lee Jia Jie said AI helped find the Linux kernel use-after-free CVE-2026-53264 and speed up development of a working local root exploit. Caveat: these are separate specific cases (each with its own conditions and limits documented in the source facts), not a general claim that AI dominates either side.
