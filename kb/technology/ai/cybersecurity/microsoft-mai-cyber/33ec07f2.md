# Microsoft launched MAI-Cyber-1-Flash inside MDASH, its agentic bug-hunting harness

Microsoft introduced MAI-Cyber-1-Flash, its first cybersecurity-specific model, in late July 2026 (The Deep View reported 2026-07-27). It runs inside MDASH, Microsoft's multi-model agentic harness for identifying and remediating vulnerabilities.

Benchmark and cost: MDASH running MAI-Cyber-1-Flash with GPT-5.4 scored 95.95% on CyberGym while costing about 50% less than Microsoft's prior best combination (GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, GPT-5.3 Codex). The routing design has MAI-Cyber-1-Flash handle up to 90% of MDASH tasks, with GPT-5.4 reserved for the hardest 10%.

Availability: MAI-Cyber-1-Flash is available only inside MDASH via an Azure AI Foundry private preview, not as a standalone public model.

Microsoft also launched 'Perception', an agentic security system for continuous monitoring and patching. Quotes in the reporting are attributed to Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman and EVP Hayete Gallot.

Scope caveat: the cost and benchmark figures are Microsoft's own claims as reported, not independently verified.
