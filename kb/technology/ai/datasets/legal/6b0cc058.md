# UC Berkeley releases LOCUS corpus of US local ordinances for legal AI

UC Berkeley researchers assembled the Local Ordinance Corpus for the United States (LOCUS), a comprehensive corpus and county-harmonized access layer for U.S. municipal and county ordinance codes. LOCUS-v1 contains about 2.2 million rows, each a specific piece of information about a local ordinance, classified by function (rule, enforcement, context, or process) and topic (buildings, businesses, zoning, nuisances, other). It is released with coverage metadata to support reproducibility and downstream legal AI research, and is framed as an access layer rather than a final theory of local legal authority.
