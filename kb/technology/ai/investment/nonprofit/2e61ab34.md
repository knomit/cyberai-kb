# Anthropic + Gates Foundation Pledge $200 Million for AI in Health and Education (May 2026)

Anthropic and the Gates Foundation announced a $200 million partnership in May 2026 focused on applying AI to health and education. This represents a significant philanthropic commitment to ensuring AI benefits extend beyond commercial use cases, and signals Anthropic's strategic interest in being seen as a force for global public good alongside its commercial AI work.
