# NVIDIA releases NeMo AutoModel for MoE fine-tuning

NVIDIA launched NeMo AutoModel on Hugging Face to optimize fine-tuning pipelines of large Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures such as Qwen3 and DeepSeek V3. Reported results show up to 3.7x increase in training throughput and a 32% reduction in peak GPU memory usage compared to native Transformers v5 libraries.
