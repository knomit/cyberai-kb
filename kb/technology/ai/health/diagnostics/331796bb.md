# Google AI Mammogram System: 25% Better Sensitivity, 40% Human Effort Reduction in NHS Trials

Two new studies evaluated Google's breast-cancer detection AI system (developed 2020, licensed to iCAD in 2022) under real-world UK NHS conditions.

Retrospective study (116,000 mammograms, 5 hospitals, 2016 data, ages 50-70):
- AI sensitivity: 0.541 vs. first human reader: 0.437 (significantly better)
- AI specificity: 0.943 vs. human: 0.952 (lower but statistically equivalent)
- AI identified 25% of cancers that humans initially missed but became apparent up to 3 years later
- Simulated replacement of second human reader: ~40% reduction in human effort, despite sending 1,800 more cases (absolute +4pp) to arbitration panels

Live test (9,250 fresh scans, 12 NHS clinics, 2023-2024, ages 50-70):
- AI median processing time: 17.7 minutes from screen to interpretation
- Human first read: >2 days
- Confirmed better sensitivity than first human evaluation; lower but equivalent specificity
- Patients diagnosed normally by doctors (AI test was parallel/blind, did not affect care)
- Some doctors reported distrust in AI output

System architecture: Three CNNs trained on a mammography database — one for embeddings, one for potential cancerous region detection, one for cancer probability classification.

Context: ~2.3 million breast cancer diagnoses annually worldwide; 760,000 deaths. UK consultant breast radiologists have ~4 hours/week for 5,000 annual scans. The system remains under regulatory review for deployment. Google-iCAD partnership extended to 20-year worldwide commercialization in 2023.
