# Meta's SAM 3.1 does promptable detect-segment-track on the Meta Model API at $2.50 per 1,000 images

Segment Anything Model (SAM) 3.1 detects, segments, and tracks objects in images and video and is served on the Meta Model API on inference infrastructure built specifically for its architecture. Users supply text prompts to identify, segment, and follow any object. Pricing is $2.50 per 1,000 images or $0.20 per 1,000 frames of video. Note the two rates are NOT interchangeable units: a second of 30fps video is 30 frames, so video at $0.20/1,000 frames costs roughly $0.006 per second of footage — budget video workloads on frame count, not clip count.
