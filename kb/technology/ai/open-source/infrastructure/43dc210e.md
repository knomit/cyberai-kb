# Ai2 and Hugging Face expand partnership to scale 'fully open' AI

The Deep View reported (Aug 6, 2026) that the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2) deepened its partnership with Hugging Face: HF will roughly triple Ai2's storage and exempt Ai2 traffic from standard rate limits so its largest datasets and multi-checkpoint models download at full throughput. Ai2's models have been downloaded more than 50 million times from the Hugging Face Hub since 2024, making it the most active model provider on the platform. Peter Clark is interim CEO of Ai2; Clem Delangue is co-founder/CEO of Hugging Face.
