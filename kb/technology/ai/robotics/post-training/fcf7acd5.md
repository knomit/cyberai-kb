# Dong and Finn: robotics needs a universal post-training recipe; EXPO(-FT)

Per Import AI 474, Stanford's Perry Dong and Chelsea Finn argue robotics is where language modeling was before a shared post-training recipe emerged (strong pretrained model, defined environments/rewards, RL against a reference model, watching for reward hacking). They say robotics needs a stable algorithm for fine-tuning billion-parameter models from small amounts of real experience plus defaults for success definition, scene reset and human feedback, and describe their early-stage EXPO(-FT) algorithm as one candidate.
