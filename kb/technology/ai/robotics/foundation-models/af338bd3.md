# Anthropic Project Fetch: Opus 4.7 completes robot tasks 20x faster than prior human record

In Anthropic's 'Project Fetch: Phase Two', Claude Opus 4.7 acting autonomously completed all but one quadruped-robot task in 9 minutes 35 seconds (May 2026). In August 2025, Claude Opus 4.1 was completely unable to do the tasks, and humans assisted by the model took 181 minutes. Anthropic said the gains were not from a robotics-specific effort but emerged from general model scaling.
