# Sunday robotics ACT-2 model reaches 99.1% laundry-folding success via pretraining-first recipe

Robotics startup Sunday introduced ACT-2, a robot foundation model achieving a 99.1% success rate over 778 successful folds across 9 garment types. Its recipe: scale pretraining, then hill-climb with a minimal amount of high-quality in-house data; a stronger base model closes the generalization gap so gains transfer to unseen real home environments. Sunday plans to deploy 'Memo' to families via a beta program.
