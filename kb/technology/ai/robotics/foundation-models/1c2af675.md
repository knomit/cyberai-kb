# Google released Gemini Robotics ER 2

Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics ER 2 (referred to as Gemini Robotics 2), improving robotic reasoning and body control. The Deep View noted the new generalist robotic models create a feedback loop that could accelerate both robotics and AI, part of an industry trend toward generalist models that let robots handle many tasks without significant task-specific training.
