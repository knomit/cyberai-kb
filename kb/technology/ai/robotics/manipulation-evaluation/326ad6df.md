# GPT-6 Astra succeeded 19/20 on block-in-bowl but only 2/20 on precise puzzle insertion

Researchers gave GPT-6 Astra direct control of YAM robot arms under an Inspect Robots agent policy on two manipulation tasks. On picking up a red block and placing it in a bowl, Astra succeeded in 19 of 20 trials. On the harder task — grasping a round blue puzzle piece by its center knob and seating it in the matching circular groove — it succeeded only 2 of 20 times. Astra also completed the bowl task far more often than Fable at roughly half the cost per run. The 95%-vs-10% spread across two superficially similar pick-and-place tasks is the load-bearing finding: coarse placement is largely solved for VLM-driven manipulation while precise aligned insertion is not, so a single headline success rate for 'robotic manipulation' is meaningless without the task.
