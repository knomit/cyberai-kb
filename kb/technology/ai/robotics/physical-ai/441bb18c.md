# Physical AI commercialization threshold crossed: humanoid cost parity with entry-level labor, full-stack platform in place (2026)

A structural transition in physical AI is confirmed across three converging signals in H1 2026: (1) Cost parity reached — Agility Robotics Digit deployed at Schaeffler factory at $10-25/hr vs $20/hr for entry-level human labor; the displaced human worker was promoted to supervisor, validating the co-existence rather than pure replacement model; (2) Capital formation scale — $40B+ in humanoid valuations (Figure AI $39B, Skild AI $14B, Apptronik $5B, Sunday Robotics $1.15B), with Nvidia as backer across multiple companies, signals venture capital has moved from exploration to conviction; (3) Full-stack platform in place — Nvidia's GTC 2026 announcements (GR00T humanoid models, Isaac simulation, Physical AI Data Factory Blueprint, Alpamayo 1.5 for autonomous vehicles, Halos OS) represent a complete training-to-deployment stack that physical AI companies can build on without starting from scratch. The 100,000-year data gap (Ken Goldberg, UC Berkeley) remains the key constraint — physical robots lack the training data depth of LLMs — but Nvidia's Blueprint approach (synthetic data via Cosmos world models) is the industry's bet on closing that gap. The 2040 projection of 5M humanoids in factories (McKinsey) now has a credible 2026 starting point at ~200 deployed.
