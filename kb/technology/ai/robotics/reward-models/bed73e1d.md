# RoboReward vision-language reward models for robot RL

Researchers at Stanford and UC Berkeley (Tony Lee, Andrew Wagenmaker, Karl Pertsch, and colleagues) built RoboReward, a family of vision-language reward models (4B and 8B parameters) that reward diverse tasks across robot types, plus a dataset and benchmark (RoboRewardBench). Key insight: negative training examples were generated by relabeling successful-action videos with mismatched commands and by truncating successful videos into partial-progress examples. Models were fine-tuned from Qwen3-VL 4B/8B to predict a 1–5 progress score used as reward; GPT-5 mini and Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 helped generate/label data. Reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
