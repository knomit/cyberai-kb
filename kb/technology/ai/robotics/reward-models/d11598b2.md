# RoboReward outperforms generalist VLMs on reward estimation

On RoboRewardBench (evaluated by mean absolute error, lower better), RoboReward 8B (0.665 MAE) outperformed 21 other models including GPT-5 mini (0.691), GPT-5 (0.811), and Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5 (0.906); RoboReward 4B (0.845) placed 4th, beating Gemini 3 Pro (0.851). In real-world WidowX robot-arm demos, a diffusion transformer trained on RoboReward 8B rewards beat one trained on Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5 (e.g., 50% vs 10% on pick-and-place; 80% vs 45% on drawer-opening) but underperformed human-assigned rewards. Reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
