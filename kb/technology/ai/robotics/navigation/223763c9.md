# Mistral releases Robostral Navigate, an 8B single-camera robot navigation model

Mistral released Robostral Navigate (reported by TLDR AI and The Deep View, Jul 2026), an 8B-parameter AI model that lets robots autonomously navigate environments using a single RGB camera, part of Mistral's physical-AI push.
