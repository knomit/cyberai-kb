# Physical AGI is a heating-up platform race with an open-vs-closed tension

Robotics startups are racing toward 'physical AGI' — a generalist robot brain that can do any task a human asks. Nvidia-backed Generalist raised $400 million, reaching a $2 billion valuation, joining Physical Intelligence, Rhoda AI, and Skild AI. Key obstacles are a massive real-world data gap and far higher compute needs than language models. Luma (a video AI / world-model startup pursuing 'multimodal AGI') announced an Open Physical AI Lab to solve generalization collaboratively; CEO Amit Jain argues generalized robots would become 'the means of production' and warns that keeping the work closed risks dangerous centralization across a few companies. OpenAI is also building a robotics division.
