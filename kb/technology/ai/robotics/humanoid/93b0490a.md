# Humanoid robotics funding wave 2025-2026: $40B+ in valuations across Figure, Skild, Apptronik, Sunday

By early 2026, humanoid robotics saw a major investment surge: Figure AI raised $1B+ Series C (backers: Nvidia, Intel, Salesforce, LG), valuation $39B; Skild AI raised $1.4B at $14B valuation (Nvidia-backed, building general-purpose robot brain); Apptronik raised $935M across two Series A rounds, valuation $5B (retail/manufacturing/logistics focus); Sunday Robotics raised $165M Series B at $1.15B valuation (household humanoid). Google DeepMind partnered with Agile Robots to integrate Gemini into humanoids (mid-March). IntBot deploying social-intelligence humanoids in NYC/Las Vegas hotels and San Jose airport. Key thesis from Nvidia: (1) Amit Goel: humanoid enables platform shift like smartphones — millions of developers build unified apps; (2) Deepu Talla: world built for humans makes humanoid 'largest opportunity.' Key challenge: UC Berkeley's Ken Goldberg coined the '100,000-year data gap' — usable robot training data is extremely sparse vs language models. RLWRLD founder Jung-hee Ryu: 'For AI to achieve human-level capabilities, it needs a body.'
