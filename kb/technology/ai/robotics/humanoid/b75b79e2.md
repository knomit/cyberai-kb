# Agility Robotics Digit deployed at Schaeffler factory: $10-25/hr vs $20/hr human labor, 200 humanoids in factories today

Agility Robotics is supplying Digit humanoid robots to Schaeffler (German automotive parts manufacturer) in the first operational deployment of humanoid robots in industry (reported April 24, 2026). Deployment details: Schaeffler factory in South Carolina; Digit carries 25-lb bins from stamping press to conveyor belt (~1 min/traverse); two 4-hour shifts per day with charging break in between; operates behind plexiglass barrier (no human-detection capability yet, planned for next year). Cost: $10-25/hr per robot vs $20/hr for entry-level human labor. Hardware: 5'9", 143 lbs, inverted knees for lifting, four-fingered grippers, torso houses processing/batteries/sensors, LED 'eyes'; sensors include RGB depth cameras, LiDAR, IMU, joint encoders; based on Cassie bipedal platform (Oregon State University, ~2016). Plans: hundreds of Digit robots at Schaeffler US/Europe plants by 2030. Industry context: ~200 humanoid robots working in factories globally today (McKinsey); projected 5M by 2040. The human worker was promoted to a supervisory position.
