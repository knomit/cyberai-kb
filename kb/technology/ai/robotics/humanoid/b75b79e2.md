# Agility Robotics Digit humanoid deployed in Schaeffler factory — $10-25/hr vs $20/hr human labor

Agility Robotics' Digit humanoid robots are working operational shifts at Schaeffler (German auto parts maker) factory in South Carolina — the first real industrial humanoid deployment beyond pilots. Digit carries 25-lb bins from stamping press to conveyor (1-min cycle), operates two 4-hour shifts per day behind plexiglass (no human-detection yet). Cost: $10-25/hr vs $20/hr entry-level human. Schaeffler plans hundreds of Digits by 2030. Digit specs: 5'9", 143 lbs, inverted-knee legs, 4-fingered grippers, RGB/LiDAR/IMU sensors. ~200 humanoids in factories globally today; McKinsey projects 5 million by 2040.
