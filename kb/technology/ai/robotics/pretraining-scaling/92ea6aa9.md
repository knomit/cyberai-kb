# Web-video pretraining scale transfers to real robot task performance, predicted by DINO FD

A 2026-09 study on Direct Video-Action models found that larger video models and more pretraining compute measurably improve real-robot task performance, including complex real-world industrial unpacking. The mechanism identified is that gains flow through better prediction of HELD-OUT web videos rather than through robot-specific data. Crucially, pretraining quality measured by DINO Frechet Distance predicts downstream robot efficiency — giving a cheap proxy metric for selecting a video backbone without running robot trials, which is the practically useful part for scaling real deployments.
