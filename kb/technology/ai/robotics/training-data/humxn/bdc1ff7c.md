# HUMXN subsidizes home repairs in 3 US cities to collect robot-training video

AI lab HUMXN offers free plumbing, electrical, HVAC, air-duct and carpet cleaning in Minneapolis, Chicago and Miami; workers wear cameras recording work inside real homes to gather data for training AI robots (Tom's Hardware).
