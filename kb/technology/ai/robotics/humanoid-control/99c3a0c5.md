# Figure's Helix 2.5 performed household tasks zero-shot across 30 unseen Bay Area homes

Figure introduced Helix 2.5, a humanoid control model pretrained on the company's Index dataset and tested zero-shot across 30 Bay Area homes. Without collecting any training data in those specific homes, the robots performed tasks including tidying rooms, folding towels, and making beds. Operational consequence: the claim is about generalization to unseen home environments from a pretrained dataset, which is the hard part of household robotics — but the evaluation is vendor-run across a single geographic region, with no published success-rate metrics in the summary, so treat it as a demonstration of transfer rather than a measured reliability result.
