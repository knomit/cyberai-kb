# DeepSeek V4-Pro was the first DeepSeek model built around Ascend from the outset; Huawei-led team post-trained 1.6T params on 1,000+ Ascend 910C

DeepSeek-V4-Pro, released April 2026, was the first DeepSeek model built around Huawei Ascend from the outset. A research group including Huawei Technologies completed full-parameter POST-TRAINING of the 1.6-trillion-parameter V4-Pro model using a cluster of at least 1,000 Huawei Ascend 910C chips.

SCOPE — important: this is post-training (fine-tuning/alignment on an existing base model), NOT pretraining from scratch. It is meaningfully less compute-intensive and less sensitive to interconnect stability and long-run numerical stability than pretraining, which is where DeepSeek's earlier Ascend attempts failed. This fact should not be cited as evidence that Ascend can pretrain frontier models.

Separately, Huawei's Atlas 950 SuperNode (8,192 Ascend 950 cards over the LingQu fabric, 16 EFLOPS FP4 aggregate per Huawei) has been cited as part of the training compute infrastructure behind some DeepSeek-family models.
