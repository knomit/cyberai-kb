# DeepSeek reportedly plans to pretrain V5 on Huawei Ascend chips (Nov 2026 report)

Report (Nov 2026): DeepSeek plans to pretrain DeepSeek V5 on Huawei Ascend chips, previously used mainly for inference. A test of whether Chinese accelerators can support frontier pretraining; success would reduce dependence on Nvidia. Report-based, not confirmed by DeepSeek; complements earlier entries on R2 failure and V4-Pro.
