# DeepSeek's R2 training on Huawei Ascend failed; training reverted to Nvidia while Ascend handles inference

DeepSeek attempted to train its R2 model on Huawei Ascend hardware after Chinese authorities urged use of domestic silicon. The attempt failed: despite on-site assistance from Huawei engineers over weeks, the effort reportedly did not achieve a single successful training run. DeepSeek reverted to Nvidia accelerators for R2 training while retaining Huawei hardware for INFERENCE. V4 was ultimately also trained on Nvidia chips.

Reported causes: unstable performance, slower chip-to-chip connectivity, and limitations of Huawei's CANN software toolkit — i.e. a mix of hardware/interconnect and software factors, not software alone.

SIGNIFICANCE: this establishes an asymmetry that is easy to elide. Chinese domestic silicon is in production use for inference serving while simultaneously failing at frontier pretraining. Claims about a 'fully independent Chinese AI compute ecosystem' must specify which workload they mean; as of mid-2026 the answer differs sharply between training and inference.
