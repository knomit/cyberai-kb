# Iran-US War: Tech Firms Named Military Targets, Chip Supply Chain Under Threat (Mar 2026)

The Iran-US conflict directly threatens the AI/tech industry. Iran's official military news agency named Amazon, Microsoft, Palantir, and Oracle as 'enemy's technological infrastructure' and military targets. Qatar's Ras Laffan helium plant struck (Qatar supplies ~30% of world helium, essential for semiconductor cooling/manufacturing). Strait of Hormuz closure threatens full semiconductor supply chain disruption. Tech companies rethinking billions in UAE data center investments — OpenAI/Nvidia/Oracle/Cisco had been collaborating on a 5GW UAE facility. Potential redirect to India, Southeast Asia, or Northern Europe. Jason Hiner (Deep View): 'We may only be a week or two away from the conflict bringing the semiconductor industry to a halt.' Iranian civilians and tech workers in region now at risk. Multiple tech companies also operating in UAE (AWS buildings already attacked).
