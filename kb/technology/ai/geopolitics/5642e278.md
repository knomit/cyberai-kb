# DeepSeek withholds DeepSeek-V4 prerelease from Nvidia/AMD; shares with Huawei (Mar 2026)

DeepSeek (Chinese AI developer) departed from typical practice by not giving Nvidia and AMD early access to its upcoming DeepSeek-V4 model (in final development stages as of March 2026) for chip compatibility optimization. Instead, it shared a prerelease version with Huawei, giving the Chinese chip maker weeks to optimize software for its hardware. A senior Trump administration official reportedly said DeepSeek-V4 was trained on Nvidia's most advanced chips despite US export controls. Nvidia provided extensive technical assistance during DeepSeek-V3 training, achieving 'major training efficiency gains.' Context: US has tightened chip export restrictions since 2022 (25% charge on top-of-line AI chips); China government asked Chinese AI companies to buy foreign chips only when necessary; China mandated security review of Nvidia H20. DeepLearning.AI perspective: 'Export restrictions alone are not stopping international rivals from gaining access to US chips. The world would benefit more from negotiated limits, mutual cooperation, and free exchange.'
