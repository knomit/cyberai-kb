# DeepLearning.AI Launches 'Transformers in Practice' Course — Taught by AMD VP Sharon Zhou (May 2026)

DeepLearning.AI launched 'Transformers in Practice', a new course taught by Sharon Zhou, VP of Engineering & AI at AMD. The course focuses on the internals of transformer-based LLMs to help practitioners reason about model behavior, debug issues, and make better deployment decisions. Topics covered: (1) token-by-token text generation and how sampling affects outputs; (2) attention mechanisms, positional encoding, and transformer layer operations; (3) why hallucinations occur and mitigation techniques including RAG and constrained generation; (4) inference optimizations: quantization, KV caching, flash attention, and speculative decoding for GPU efficiency. Includes interactive visualizations throughout. Positioned for engineers who want model + systems-level understanding.
