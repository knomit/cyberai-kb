# Vals AI Vibe Code Bench: agent teams cost 1.8-5.1x with mostly no significant gain

Vals AI tested GPT-6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 on Vibe Code Bench as single agents vs teams at medium and maximum reasoning effort. Teams cost 1.8x to 5.1x as much. No statistically significant score difference except GPT-6 Sol at medium effort. Whether teams pay off depends on model, task and budget; higher reasoning effort may be a better alternative to multi-agent teams.
