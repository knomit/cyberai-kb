# Google Deep Review: agentic system for rigorous feedback on technical documents

Google Research's Deep Review is an agentic AI system giving rigorous feedback and technical verification on complex documents. Initially built for computer-science academic paper analysis, it has expanded to many domains. It initially accepts PDFs and plain text; multi-file input is promised soon. Google expects such tools to play a significant role in peer review by supporting authors and reviewers.
