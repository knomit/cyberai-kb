# Self-improving agent harnesses are the next step after AI agents

OpenAI forward-deployed engineers built 'Tax AI' with Thrive Holdings to automate preparing 1040 and 1041 tax returns. It cut tax-document prep time by a third while maintaining up to 97% accuracy, and notably kept improving itself during tax season. Field-completion accuracy rose from only ~25% of returns at 75%+ completion at launch to 86% within six weeks, eventually with 90% of returns reaching 100% correct field completion. What is 'self-improving' is the harness around the model (built on OpenAI's open-source Codex harness), not the underlying model: a three-part loop where a practitioner reveals/steers errors, corrections are converted into evals, and a Codex-driven loop builds on those evals. Leads (Arthur Fernandes Araujo, John de Wasseige) say the design is 'quite generalizable' to other domains like accounting, audit, and operational workflows.
