# Cognition SWE-2 hits 50.0% on FrontierCode 1.1 at a quarter of GPT-6 Astra's cost

Cognition released SWE-2 on 2026-09-11, reporting 50.0% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main1 while being 64% cheaper than its predecessor. Cognition's positioning is cost-per-capability rather than peak capability: SWE-2 is claimed to beat SWE-1.7 and Grok 4.6 on both score AND cost, match GPT-5.6 Sol and Fable 5/5.1 at a fraction of their price, and land within a few points of GPT-6 Astra at roughly a quarter of the cost. All figures are Cognition-reported — SWE-2 is explicitly NOT claimed to be the strongest coding model, only the best on the price-performance Pareto frontier.
