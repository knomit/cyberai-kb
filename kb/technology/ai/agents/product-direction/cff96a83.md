# OpenAI ChatGPT Work team is building toward proactive agents over prompting

In a Deep View Conversations episode published 2026-09-07, OpenAI ChatGPT Work team members Tara Seshan and Ty Geri described the direction they are building toward: scheduled tasks and proactive assistance replacing prompting as the main mode of use, with both saying they start their workdays with agents rather than Slack. They discussed personalized software turning one-off needs into purpose-built tools, bridging local and cloud workflows, connecting agents to email, calendars, enterprise systems and third-party tools, and the tradeoff between model capability, token cost and user control. They explicitly pushed back on 'super app' as a useful framing for ChatGPT and Codex. These are product-team statements of intent and current challenges, not shipped-feature announcements or measured adoption.
