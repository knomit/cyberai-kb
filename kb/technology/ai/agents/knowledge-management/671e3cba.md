# Meta's organizational second brain separates knowledge store from reasoning

Meta Engineering described (2026-09-02) an internal AI agent acting as an organizational 'second brain' that codifies expert knowledge for reuse across the org. Its design has two layers: a structured, auditable knowledge architecture that deliberately separates stored knowledge from the reasoning process that consumes it, and a self-improvement loop that folds in expert feedback without retraining the underlying model. The claimed benefit is consistent, high-quality output on large-scale assessments while freeing experts for harder work. This is an architecture pattern report, not a measured productivity study.
