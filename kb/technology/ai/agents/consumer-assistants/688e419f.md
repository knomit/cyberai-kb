# Meta launches Muse consumer agent with 100M free tokens per week

On Tuesday 2026-09-08, Meta launched Muse, a personal AI agent for consumers, available in the US. Meta says Muse handles busywork, answers questions, browses the web, fills out forms, makes purchases, creates documents, and watches user activity to proactively suggest help; it plans and coordinates against a stated goal and checks with the user before 'sensitive actions' like purchases or sending email. Mark Zuckerberg said on X that Muse is free up to 100 million tokens per week with paid plans above that, and Meta says it works out of the box with no technical experience required. Architecture claims: Muse runs on Muse Secure VM holding the agent and the user's data, with a separate 'sentinel' agent on the same VM isolated at the system level; a Muse Confidential VM encrypted with a user-held key 'so not even Meta can access it' is promised later in 2026. Meta says the model has no visibility into passwords or payment methods (credentials go to secure storage usable but not readable by the model) and that no user information is shared with its ad platform. All of the above is Meta's own description at launch, not independently audited. The catch users should register: the agent's usefulness depends on connecting it to calendars, email, payment platforms and other daily services.
