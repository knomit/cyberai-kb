# Meta launched Muse, a consumer personal agent isolated in a secure VM

Meta launched Muse on 2026-09-09, a consumer-facing personal AI agent powered by Muse Spark that automates tasks such as booking travel and sending email. Its security design is the differentiator: Muse runs inside a Muse Secure VM with a separate 'Sentinel' agent overseeing its actions, and Meta says encrypted execution via Muse Confidential VM is coming. Available on iOS, Android and muse.ai in the US at launch. The isolation and oversight-agent architecture are Meta's stated design, not independently audited.
