# Meta launched Muse, a consumer personal agent isolated in a secure VM

Meta launched Muse on 2026-09-09, a consumer-facing personal AI agent for US users, powered by Muse Spark. It can browse the web, fill forms, make purchases, create documents, and automate tasks such as booking travel and sending email. It is free for up to 100 million tokens per week and available on iOS, Android and muse.ai in the US at launch.

Its security design is the differentiator: Muse runs inside a Muse Secure VM with a separate 'Sentinel' agent overseeing its actions, and Meta says encrypted execution via Muse Confidential VM is coming.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the isolation and oversight-agent architecture are Meta's stated design, not independently audited. The security claims are the vendor's own.
