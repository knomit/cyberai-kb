# Apple WWDC 2026 Agents Gap: No Autonomous Agent Product, Only Shortcuts Upgrade

Apple's WWDC 2026 revealed a critical gap in its AI strategy: autonomous agents. While OpenAI (Codex), Anthropic (Claude Cowork), and Perplexity (Personal Computer) all offer agents that autonomously pursue multi-step goals using apps and context, Apple's closest equivalent was a Shortcuts app upgrade that generates automations from a natural language prompt. This is a goal-to-automation compiler, not a goal-pursuing agent. The Deep View editorial explicitly flags this as the fastest-advancing area of AI where Apple cannot afford to wait until WWDC 2027.
