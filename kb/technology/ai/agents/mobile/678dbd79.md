# Alibaba's Qwen Intelligence launches three personal AI agents; Mobile-Use agent self-reports 90% end-to-end success on Qwen's own benchmark

Alibaba's Qwen Intelligence launched three personal intelligence agents, announced around September 23-24, 2026: one for planning, one for mobile use / cross-app execution (Mobile-Use), and one for creative / rapid content-creation tasks. Alongside the launch it released open benchmarks covering planning, real-device performance, and safety, and reported top benchmark results including a 90% end-to-end task success rate for its Mobile-Use agent. Reported in TLDR AI's September 24, 2026 edition.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the 90% figure is self-reported by Qwen on a benchmark Qwen itself released; it is not an independent or third-party result and should not be compared as-is against other vendors' numbers on different benchmarks.
