# Qwen Intelligence's Mobile-Use agent reports 90% end-to-end task success on its own benchmark

Alongside launching three mobile AI agents (for planning, cross-app execution, and rapid content creation), Alibaba's Qwen Intelligence released open benchmarks for planning, real-device performance, and safety, and reports top benchmark results with a 90% end-to-end success rate for its Mobile-Use agent; these figures are self-reported by Qwen on its own released benchmark. Reported in TLDR AI's September 24, 2026 edition.
