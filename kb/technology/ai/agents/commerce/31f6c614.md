# Visa Agentic Commerce Benchmark: 22 models averaged 57.2% pass rate on shopping tasks

Visa launched its Agentic Commerce Benchmark in early November 2026, testing 22 AI models from 12 labs (model names undisclosed) on 15 realistic shopping challenges such as buying a TV, laptop or personal care appliance. Average pass rate was 57.2%; best models passed 82.2% of attempts, worst 28.9%. Cost did not predict quality: the five most expensive models passed 67.1% of runs vs 50.2% for the five cheapest. 54% of failed runs still ended with the agent making a purchase. Models that inferred 'hidden buying factors' through conversation averaged 70.2% but were inconsistent when repeating scenarios. Visa's Trust Index (Sept 2026) found 72% of US consumers used an AI assistant but only 23% trust AI to make payments. Does NOT mean agentic shopping is unusable; it indicates models are not yet reliably deciding when not to buy.
