# AI agents shift compute demand toward CPUs and consume far more tokens

Agentic AI workloads lean much more on CPUs than chatbot workloads do, using CPUs for orchestration, feeding GPUs, and powering the enterprise services and tools agents use, whereas LLM chatbots rely heavily on GPUs. Research cited by The Deep View indicates AI agents can consume 1,000x more tokens than standard chatbot queries, deepening the industry's compute crisis as agents surged in popularity in 2026.
