# Nvidia open Nemotron strategy and OpenShell agent controls (Boitano interview)

In a Deep View Conversations episode, Nvidia VP of enterprise AI Justin Boitano says Nvidia releases Nemotron datasets, training techniques and weights openly, arguing proprietary business knowledge becomes enterprise advantage via specialized models, that the agent harness (tools, memory, execution) matters as much as the model, that AI incidents need an aviation-style flight recorder, and that OpenShell moves agent controls (sandboxing, enforceable access limits) into the infrastructure layer. Nvidia also worked with CrowdStrike on cybersecurity models. These are statements by an Nvidia executive, not independent findings.
