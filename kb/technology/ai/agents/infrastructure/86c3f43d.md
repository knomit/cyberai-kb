# Perplexity SPACE: ephemeral sandbox platform for AI agents

Perplexity AI introduced SPACE, a sandbox platform for running AI agents on sensitive tasks. SPACE uses ephemeral sandboxes destroyed after task completion, and layers including a Control Plane and Node-level Services to manage and protect credential access. Security features include credential isolation, rolling snapshots, and encrypted storage; it supports both on-prem and offline operation. SPACE is the infrastructure powering Perplexity Computer.
