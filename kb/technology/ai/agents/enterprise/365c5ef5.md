# Google launches Gemini Agent, a universal work agent across Workspace

At Gemini at Work 2026 (2026-10-08), Google introduced a universal Gemini agent for knowledge work, media creation, coding and multi-step workflows across Workspace and other enterprise tools, with persistent context, multi-agent orchestration, model routing, governance and cost controls.
