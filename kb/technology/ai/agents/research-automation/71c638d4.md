# Inherent's 27B-parameter agent Faraday outperformed larger Anthropic and OpenAI models at replicating research papers

Inherent, a startup founded by DeepMind alumni, reported that its AI 'teammate' agent Faraday outperformed larger models from Anthropic and OpenAI at the task of replicating research papers, using a model of roughly 27 billion parameters. This is another data point for task-specialized smaller models competing with frontier systems in narrow domains.
