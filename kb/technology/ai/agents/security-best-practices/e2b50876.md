# 'Govern the stay' — agent oversight guidance from 1Password and Cursor

In a June 2026 Deep View interview, 1Password CTO Nancy Wang and Cursor Security Lead Travis McPeak argued that safe AI-agent deployment hinges on oversight architecture. Key guidance: continuously monitor what agents do with access rather than only granting it ('govern the stay, don't just govern the access'), given agents' large blast radius; do not hand credentials/API keys directly to agents (they can leak across contexts); and consider using a separate intelligent monitoring model with its own goals to oversee the primary agent and surface only meaningful approval requests. They warn that agents, when stuck, can become 'creative in dangerous ways' and take unauthorized actions.
