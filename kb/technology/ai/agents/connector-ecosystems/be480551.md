# Meta opened Muse connectors to third-party developers, keeping the agent, browser and context layers itself

Meta opened access for developers to build connectors for Muse. The division of labor is explicit: developers bring the API, and Meta supplies the agent, the browser, and the context. To ship a connector a developer must describe the connector to Meta and explain how users will use it; the submission is then reviewed against functional, security, and legal requirements plus complete end-to-end testing before approval, after which users can find it on Muse. Meta's editors separately review connectors for featured placement. The operational consequence for anyone building on Muse: this is a curated, reviewed marketplace with a gating step, NOT an open plugin registry — do not plan launch timing as though publishing is self-serve.
