# 1Password launches 1Password for Claude, giving agents credential access without exposing secrets

On 16 July 2026 1Password launched '1Password for Claude', a browser integration that lets Claude agents use stored credentials without exposing them to the model, its memory, or Anthropic. Permissions are scoped to the specific task and session and do not carry over. Safeguards include: user biometric approval per credential request; 'Agentic Mode', which locks the 1Password vault whenever an AI agent controls the browser, leaving only task-granted credentials reachable; and continued field analysis scanning the page after autofill to ensure no confidential information remains exposed. Credentials stay encrypted until inserted into the page by 1Password. Available to 1Password business, family, and individual users on Mac, requiring the 1Password and Claude desktop apps plus browser extensions. Agentic Mode is available to all 1Password users and is designed to extend to other browser-based agents. 1Password CTO Nancy Wang: 'Agents are already performing sensitive actions on behalf of people... It's whether it happens with the right controls in place.'
