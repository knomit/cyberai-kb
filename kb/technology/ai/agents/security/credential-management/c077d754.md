# 1Password for Agents lets AI agents sign in without seeing credentials

1Password launched 1Password for Agents (Thursday 2026-10-22): when an agent reaches a sign-in form it verifies its identity to 1Password with a unique digital key; the user sees which credential is requested and why and approves on their device; 1Password then fills the credential directly into the form so the agent never sees or stores it, and it is removed from the form once the site accepts it. Initial integrations include Meta's Muse, Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's Dots and SpaceXSI Grok Bot; partners must uphold shared security principles and pass a security review. Builds on the earlier 1Password for Claude. Separately WSJ reports 1Password passed $500M ARR driven by enterprise AI agent demand.
