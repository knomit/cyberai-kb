# By Aug 2026 the AI agent attack surface is being treated as first-class on both the offense and defense sides

Four concurrent August 2026 datapoints show AI assistants and agents moving from novel to routinely-attacked-and-defended infrastructure. Offense/disclosure: Varonis Threat Labs' CoSnitch (CVE-2026-24301) showed three Microsoft Copilot Personal flaws allowing one-click exfiltration from connected apps, reached in part via an undocumented URL parameter the assistant itself surfaced under repeated questioning. Defense at the runtime layer: an arXiv policy-algebra paper enforces agent permissions continuously across a whole task rather than only at task start, reporting 94.8% of rule-breaking actions stopped or corrected while still completing 86.9% of legitimate tasks. Defense at the code layer: Google Cloud's AVDH is a multi-agent system doing agentic source-code review for vulnerabilities. Defense at the isolation layer: Vercel offered up to $1 million in a two-week challenge for escaping its Firecracker-based Sandbox — the substrate agents execute in. SCOPE CAVEATS: CoSnitch scope is Copilot Personal at copilot.microsoft.com; the research does not state Microsoft 365 Copilot was affected, patches shipped Aug 18 2026, and researchers found no evidence of in-the-wild exploitation. The policy-algebra numbers are author-reported, not independently replicated. WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: no claim that agent exploitation is yet common in the wild.
