# OpenAI research shows shift from chatbots to agents via Codex

OpenAI economic research on its Codex app found that internally, the average OpenAI worker now generates about 85% of their output tokens in Codex rather than ChatGPT. Among sampled users, 70% made at least one Codex request equivalent to more than one hour of human work and 25% made a request equivalent to 8 hours. KPMG data published the same week found employee adoption of AI agents had reached 68%.
