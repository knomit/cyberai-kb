# Anthropic's Project SWAP found Claude agents' bargaining choices matched human preferences 61% of the time

In Anthropic's research project 'Project SWAP', after five-minute interviews, Claude agents negotiating trades of books on behalf of interviewed people produced preference rankings that matched the humans' own rankings on 61% of pairs.
