# Arena.ai HarnessTax: harness choice barely moves success rate but changes cost up to 5x

Arena.ai published 'HarnessTax' on 16 September 2026, evaluating 21 model–harness pairs spanning seven models and three harnesses — Claude Code, Codex CLI, and Pi — on SWE-bench Lite and Terminal-Bench 2.0. Method: 30 randomly sampled tasks per benchmark, each pair run three times per task to capture attempt-to-attempt variation, starting from each harness's native configuration at its high-effort setting. Three findings, and the first must be stated with both halves: harness choice has LITTLE effect on task success rate but CAN SIGNIFICANTLY AFFECT COST on these benchmarks, with the same model reaching similar success rates at up to 5x the cost depending on harness. Second, a simple harness can be competitive: Pi, a minimal open-source harness, was competitive on both cost and success rate. Third, models may perform better with harnesses other than their own vendor's. Operational consequence: when selecting a coding agent, treat harness choice as primarily a cost decision rather than a capability decision, and do not assume a vendor's own harness is optimal for its own model. Scope limit: these are two open-source benchmarks with 30 sampled tasks each, so this does NOT establish that harness is irrelevant to capability on all task types.
