# Google opened Model Context Protocol early access for Google Home

Google introduced early access to the Model Context Protocol (MCP) for Google Home, letting external AI agents such as ChatGPT control smart home devices. Three conditions bound availability and must be stated together: the user must set up a Google Cloud project and supply MCP connection details to their chosen AI agent, the integration covers all devices in the Google Home ecosystem, and it is initially limited to US subscribers of Google Home Premium Advanced. This does NOT mean any Google Home user can point an agent at their devices — the Cloud-project setup and the paid US-only tier are both required.
