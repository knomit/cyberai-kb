# ContextPilot-14B teaches agents to offload less useful context

ContextPilot-14B is the Qwen3-14B checkpoint of ContextPilot, a model trained to make agents plan, maintain long-term memory, and actively offload less useful context while continuing to reason and use tools — treating context management as a learned policy rather than a fixed window.
