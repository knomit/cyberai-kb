# DeepMind proposes an 'Automated Scientific Economy' with four market components

A DeepMind paper argues AI-scientist progress will be bottlenecked by physical resources and empirical validation, and proposes a market with proof of ideation, ex-ante evaluation (agents stake compute credits), brokerage/fractional licensing of ideas, and validation payouts/royalties. Proposal, not implemented.
