# Distilling expert trajectories into small models can hurt once the harness is optimized

Salesforce research (arXiv 2609.09134, September 2026) on co-evolving agents and their harnesses found that directly training smaller models on expert agent trajectories can DEGRADE performance after the harness has already been optimized. The implication is that harness improvements and model fine-tuning are not independent additive gains: trajectories collected under one harness encode behaviors that a better harness already handles or handles differently, so distillation can teach the model to fight its own scaffolding. Practical consequence: measure distillation gains against the CURRENT harness, not the one that generated the data.
