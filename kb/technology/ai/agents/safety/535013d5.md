# Anthropic research on multi-agent systemic failure modes

Anthropic published research examining how individually benign behaviors in frontier AI agents could compound into systemic failures when many agents interact in shared environments. Highlighted risks include confabulation, reward hacking, and unexpected dynamics emerging faster than human institutions can oversee them.
