# Agent harness capability and model capability have advanced in tandem, shifting the harness role toward attention management

An analysis of the evolution of the agent harness argues AI models improved most when model capabilities and agent harnesses advanced together. Early systems like ChatGPT relied solely on next-token prediction; newer harnesses let models interact with digital environments. As models absorbed more harness capabilities into themselves, the harness's purpose shifted toward optimizing human attention — acting as an interface that aids human interaction and decision-making rather than a crutch the model depends on.
