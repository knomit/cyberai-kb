# Apple Research: instruction tuning and RLHF push models toward overconfidence

Apple Research (cited by The Deep View, Jul 2026) identified that popular post-training methods — instruction tuning and RLHF — reward responses that sound helpful and confident, incentivizing models to express certainty even when guessing, which contributes to confidently-stated hallucinations.
