# Confidence 'strengtheners' make users trust AI answers more

Research by Maarten Sap and colleagues (cited by The Deep View, Jul 2026) found that when AI responses used epistemic 'strengtheners' (language expressing certainty), nearly 90% of users trusted the answer; when 'weakeners' (uncertainty language) were used, ~90% chose to verify it themselves.
