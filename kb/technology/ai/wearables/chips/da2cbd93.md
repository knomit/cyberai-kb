# Qualcomm announces Snapdragon Sound Elite Gen 2 chip for audio AI wearables

Qualcomm announced its Snapdragon Sound Elite Gen 2 chip on September 24, 2026, targeting next-generation audio- and voice-focused AI wearables (camera-less), timed alongside Meta's camera-less Ray-Ban Audio glasses launch.
