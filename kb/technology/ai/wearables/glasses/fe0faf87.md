# Meta launches camera-less Ray-Ban Audio AI glasses

Meta announced the Meta Ray-Ban Audio glasses on September 23, 2026 at Meta Connect 2026: a camera-free line of AI glasses with 6 microphones, 12 hours of battery life (vs 8 hours for Gen 2 and 4 hours for Gen 1 Ray-Ban Meta glasses), a weight of about 43 grams (10-20% lighter than most competing models), adjustable temple tips and nose pads, two styles (Clubmaster and Burbank), starting at $349, shipping October 13, 2026. Meta VP of wearables Ming Hua said the camera-less design was developed over roughly two years specifically to counter privacy backlash over Ray-Ban Meta's camera, and to cut weight, cost, and boost battery life.
