# Meta launches Hearing Enhancement software feature for AI glasses

Meta launched 'Hearing Enhancement', an FDA-cleared over-the-counter hearing aid software feature for its AI glasses, announced at Meta Connect 2026 alongside the camera-less Ray-Ban Audio glasses. After a 5-minute onboarding routine it boosts specific frequencies based on the user's hearing profile and can direction-boost audio from whoever the wearer is looking at. It costs a one-time $149 fee, versus $1,000-$8,000 for a typical prescription hearing aid, and starts shipping later in fall 2026.
