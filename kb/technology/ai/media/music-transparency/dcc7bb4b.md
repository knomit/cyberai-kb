# Deezer study: 97% of listeners can't distinguish AI-generated from human music

A study from music streamer Deezer found that 97% of listeners are unable to tell the difference between authentic (human-made) and fully AI-generated music. Deezer reports roughly 50,000 AI tracks flooding the platform daily. Cited by The Deep View as evidence of the growing 'AI slop' detection problem.
