# Spotify rolling out 'AI Personas' badges to flag non-human artists

On August 11, 2026 Spotify announced 'AI Personas,' badges that identify when an artist profile does not represent a real person — applied both to artists who self-disclose and to 'relevant profiles' that do not. Spotify will not recommend these artists in user algorithms unless the user follows them. Part of a broader industry transparency push; AI music platform Suno is separately adding watermarking and fingerprinting technology.
