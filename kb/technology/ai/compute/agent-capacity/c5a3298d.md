# Epoch AI: chips shipped through 2027 could run tens to hundreds of millions of concurrent agents

Epoch AI estimates AI chips shipped through 2027 could run tens to hundreds of millions of concurrent frontier-model agents, equal to about 140-720 million full-time employees of weekly hours; using 20% of central-estimate capacity implies $2.6-5.3 trillion/year of API-equivalent spending, requiring massive demand growth. Estimate, not measurement.
