# Adobe survey of 16,000 creators finds creative AI now central to most creators' work

An Adobe report surveying 16,000 creators across the US, UK, France, Germany, South Korea, Japan, India, and Australia found that 87% of respondents using creative AI said it accelerated the growth of their business or audience, and 75% described it as integrated or essential to how they work. About 93% said creative AI helps them produce content faster, though 57% said outputs typically require moderate or extensive editing before sharing. 35% said it gives them more freedom to experiment and 33% more confidence to pursue ambitious projects.
