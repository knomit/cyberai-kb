# Meta to use off-site business data for Feed and AI chatbot personalization

Meta announced (June 2026) it will use information that other businesses share about users' off-site activity (e.g., games played, purchases on other websites) to personalize users' Feed and AI chatbot responses, expanding beyond targeted ads. Meta says it is not collecting new data and that users retain controls over how the information is used for personalization.
