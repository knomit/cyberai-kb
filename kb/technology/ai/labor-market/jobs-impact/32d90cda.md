# IBM CHRO says AI's job-elimination impact is overstated

Nickle LaMoreaux, IBM's chief human resources officer, told The Deep View at New York City Tech Week (June 2026) that fears of AI eliminating jobs are overstated. She argues AI will eliminate relatively few roles overall: highly administrative and process-driven jobs are likely to disappear, but productivity gains from AI will create entirely new roles that don't yet exist, because as economies grow they still need humans to fill new work.
