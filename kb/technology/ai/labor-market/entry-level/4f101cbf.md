# Stanford research finds entry-level AI-exposed jobs in substantial decline

Stanford research cited by The Deep View finds that entry-level roles most exposed to AI - including customer service, data entry, and coding - are in 'substantial decline.' The finding comes from the Stanford Digital Economy Lab paper 'Canaries in the Coal Mine' (Nov 2025).
