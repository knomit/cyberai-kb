# Soft skills expected to resist AI automation

According to IBM CHRO Nickle LaMoreaux (speaking to The Deep View, June 2026), soft skills will remain difficult to automate. Specifically, communication, leadership, judgment, and conflict management 'will not be taken over' by AI, making them more valuable as routine tasks are automated.
