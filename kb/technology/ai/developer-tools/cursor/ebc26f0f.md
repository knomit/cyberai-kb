# Cursor preparing 'Cursor Review' code-review platform (formerly Origin)

Cursor appears to be preparing to launch its Origin platform beyond a closed partner beta under the name 'Cursor Review.' It adds two tabs: Codebase (syncing/managing GitHub repositories) and Review (an automated pull-request pipeline that notifies developers when human judgment is needed, letting humans and agents work through open PRs together). Rollout could land as early as the week of Aug 11, 2026.
