# Multi-layer agentic AI convergence will drive commoditization of model layer and shift competition to workflow integration

Given that all major AI/tech players (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Adobe, SpaceX/xAI) have simultaneously converged on agentic AI at model, application, and infrastructure layers, the model layer will commoditize within 12–18 months as capability parity is reached. Competitive differentiation will shift decisively to depth of enterprise workflow integration and proprietary data access. Evidence chain: (1) GPT-5.5 built via recursive self-improvement — capability is now recursive/self-accelerating, shortening gaps; (2) Google Workspace Intelligence and Gemini Enterprise embed agents into productivity tools with existing data access; (3) OpenAI Codex 4M users in weeks — distribution and workflow lock-in outpacing raw capability; (4) SpaceX/Cursor $60B deal shows infrastructure-layer players rushing to secure agentic application footholds before consolidation. Known gaps: pace of recursive self-improvement is uncertain; regulatory interventions could slow convergence; open-source (GLM-5.1) may disrupt commoditization timelines. Falsification condition: if model capability remains a primary purchase driver for enterprise AI contracts 18 months from now, and no major provider offers 'good enough' baseline parity, the hypothesis fails.
