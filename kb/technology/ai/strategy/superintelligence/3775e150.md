# Sam Altman prefers many slightly-superhuman AIs over one superintelligence

Per a Cheeky Pint interview (via TLDR AI 2026-10-30): Sam Altman said he would rather have a trillion AIs of slightly superhuman intelligence join society than one AI of 1 trillion IQ, arguing swarms of agents resemble a resilient society that keeps itself in check and that AI research should not be centralized.
