# vLLM hardware-agnostic model layers reach up to 96.6% of native efficiency on H100

vLLM introduced hardware-agnostic layers to support models across diverse accelerators; they achieve up to 96.6% of native-implementation efficiency on NVIDIA H100 GPUs while remaining torch-compilable and extensible.
