# Randomly sampled KV-cache eviction matches learned-importance methods

Salesforce AI Research released Random Attention, which retains a uniformly random sampled subset of generated KV-cache entries rather than selecting by learned importance signals or attention statistics. Across several reasoning benchmarks and model families it matched or exceeded more complex eviction methods while cutting eviction overhead, since random selection requires no scoring pass. The implication is that the sophistication of existing KV eviction heuristics may be delivering little over chance — a negative-result-shaped finding about the field, not just a new method.
