# Random KV-cache eviction matches learned-importance methods

Random Attention, a KV-cache eviction method that keeps a uniformly sampled subset of cache entries rather than using learned importance signals, matched or exceeded more complex eviction methods across several reasoning benchmarks and model families. This is evidence that the sophistication of existing importance heuristics may not be what produces their benefit.
