# Test-time training trades KV-cache growth for per-user model weights

Test-time training lets AI models adapt by updating their weights during use, analogous to a GPS learning a persistent traffic shortcut. The approach reduces memory needs by holding a fixed-size set of weights instead of a linearly growing KV-cache, but requires a separate model per user, raising computational demands. The trade-off is between efficient long-context handling for personalized services and the broader accessibility of standard shared models.
