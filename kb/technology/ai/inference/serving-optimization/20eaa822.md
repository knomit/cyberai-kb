# Cohere megakernel reaches 62% of speed-of-light decoding

Cohere's decode "megakernel" serving engine for North Mini Code reaches 292 tokens per second at batch size 1, which is 62% of theoretical speed-of-light and 1.58x faster than vLLM, with the margin holding out to 256K context and no measurable accuracy loss.
