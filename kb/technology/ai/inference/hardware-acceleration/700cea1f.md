# OpenAI previewed 'Ultrafast', a GPT-5.6 Sol API tier on Cerebras hardware at up to 750 output tokens/second — reported as both ~11x and ~14x faster depending on which baseline is used

MERGED from two records of the same August 2026 Ultrafast preview. They agree on the headline figure and DIFFER ON THE SPEEDUP MULTIPLIER because they use different baselines; both are preserved rather than reconciled.

WHAT IT IS. Ultrafast is a new OpenAI API SERVICE TIER that runs GPT-5.6 Sol ON CEREBRAS HARDWARE instead of OpenAI's usual infrastructure, aiming to deliver real-time performance without switching to a smaller model.

THE HEADLINE FIGURE, AGREED BY BOTH RECORDS: UP TO 750 OUTPUT TOKENS PER SECOND.

THE TWO MULTIPLIERS — NOTE WHICH BASELINE EACH USES.
- ~11x, computed against the 65 TOKENS/SECOND that Artificial Analysis measures for GPT-5.6 Sol at max reasoning on OpenAI's own API.
- UP TO 14x 'standard processing speed', as reported by 9to5Mac citing OpenAI.
These are not the same comparison and should not be cited interchangeably.

HOW. Cerebras achieves this by keeping the model's weights in 44 GB OF ON-CHIP SRAM, avoiding the external-memory round trips that bottleneck GPU inference.

END-TO-END MEASUREMENTS (Cerebras'). Across six quality-matched GDPVal tasks, Cerebras measured 83 SECONDS PER TASK versus 7.7 MINUTES for standard Sol — a 5.6x end-to-end speedup. Ultrafast completed all 2,500 Humanity's Last Exam questions in 11h11m versus 78h27m for Claude Fable 5 (~7x).

STATUS. It launched as a LIMITED PREVIEW with NO PUBLISHED PRICE and NO PUBLISHED LATENCY figure, and NO INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION of the benchmarks.

SEPARATE PRICING NOTE carried from one input: OpenAI's lowest-cost model GPT-5.6 Luna is priced at $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens. See kb/business/companies/openai/pricing/gpt-5-6-sol-price-cut.md for the full GPT-5.6 tier pricing picture and the temporary/permanent distinction on the Sol cut.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: throughput is not latency. Ultrafast targets TOKENS PER SECOND ONCE GENERATION STARTS; no time-to-first-token figure was published, and GPT-5.6 Sol at max reasoning posts 97.2 seconds to first token on standard infrastructure. See kb/technology/ai/inference/performance-concepts/1c5b7976.md — a system can be excellent on one axis and poor on the other. All benchmark figures here are Cerebras' or OpenAI's own.
