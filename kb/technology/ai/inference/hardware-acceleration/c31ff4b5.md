# OpenAI and Cerebras previewed Ultrafast, running GPT-5.6 Sol at up to 750 tokens/second

Ultrafast is a new OpenAI API service tier that runs GPT-5.6 Sol on Cerebras hardware instead of OpenAI's usual infrastructure, advertising up to 750 output tokens/second — roughly 11x the 65 tokens/second Artificial Analysis measures for GPT-5.6 Sol at max reasoning on OpenAI's own API. Cerebras achieves this by keeping the model's weights in 44 GB of on-chip SRAM, avoiding external-memory round trips that bottleneck GPU inference. Across six quality-matched GDPVal tasks, Cerebras measured 83 seconds per task versus 7.7 minutes for standard Sol (a 5.6x end-to-end speedup), and Ultrafast completed all 2,500 Humanity's Last Exam questions in 11h11m versus 78h27m for Claude Fable 5 (~7x). It launched as a limited preview with no published price or latency figure, and no independent confirmation of the benchmarks.
