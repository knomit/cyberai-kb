# SGLang Diffusion reached 1.95x lossless speedup on MiniMax-H3 video generation

An LMSYS benchmark of MiniMax-H3 video generation on 8x H200 GPUs found SGLang Diffusion achieving 1.95x lossless speedups, rising to as much as 6.24x when combined with step reuse and sparse attention.
