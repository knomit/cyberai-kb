# Decagon reports up to 2.5x inference throughput from self-improving AutoResearch agents

Decagon's AutoResearch agents diagnose inference bottlenecks, test optimizations against replays of production traffic and preserve findings across experiments. Decagon reports throughput gains of up to 2.5x within endpoint latency requirements via quantization, scheduling, engine patches and speculative decoding; production changes require human approval.
