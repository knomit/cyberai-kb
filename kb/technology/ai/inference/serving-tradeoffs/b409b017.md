# Inference engineering has an efficient frontier trading latency, throughput and quality

Baseten frames inference engineering as having its own efficient frontier, analogous to the model frontier of best intelligence per cost or size. The trade-off is most often stated as latency versus throughput, but quality can also be exchanged for throughput and intelligence for speed. Techniques divide into those that merely select a point on the existing frontier (e.g. batching choices) and those that push the frontier outward (e.g. quantization, better kernels).
