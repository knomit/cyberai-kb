# Epoch: frontier models cannot yet autonomously produce Epoch-quality work

Epoch AI's Automation Reports find frontier models perform reliably on well-defined tasks but fail on open-ended aspects; open-weight models lag further, even on well-defined tasks. Models struggled to absorb Epoch's standards even with ample reference material and lacked judgment to follow through on promising research directions.
