# OpenAI's 2026 voice capability claims are measured against its own predecessor; every rival-inclusive measurement in the corpus shows no category leader and an unsolved problem

Two kinds of voice-model measurement appear in this corpus and they support different conclusions. The difference is the choice of baseline, and it is stated in the sources themselves.

SELF-REFERENTIAL MEASUREMENT — the headline numbers. In OpenAI's internal tests, GPT-Live-1 at high reasoning scored 84.2% on GPQA (graduate-level science) versus 45.3% for its predecessor Advanced Voice Mode, and 75.2% on BrowseComp (agentic web search) versus 0.7% for AVM. Human raters preferred GPT-Live-1's conversational quality to AVM 75.7% of the time and GPT-Live-1 mini 69.2% of the time in matched 5–10 minute conversations. Safety flagging also improved against AVM: illicit behaviour 97% vs 74%, self-harm 96% vs 89%; on adversarial prompts, 84% of mental-health queries vs 57%, and 98% for self-harm vs 72%. THE SOURCE FACT STATES THE LIMIT EXPLICITLY: 'All comparisons were against OpenAI's own predecessor rather than rival voice models.' Note in particular that the BrowseComp baseline was 0.7% — a near-zero starting point produces a dramatic ratio regardless of where the new model sits against competitors.

RIVAL-INCLUSIVE MEASUREMENT — a different picture. Where OpenAI's voice models are scored against other vendors:
- τ-Voice agentic tasks: GPT-Realtime-2 led the airline domain (63%) but placed 3rd OVERALL (39.8%), behind Grok Voice Think Fast 1.0 (52.1%).
- Artificial Analysis Big Bench Audio at high reasoning: tied with Gemini 3.1 Flash Live Preview (96.6%), BEHIND Step-Audio R1.1 Realtime (97.6%) and Grok Voice Think Fast 1.0 (97.1%).
- Latency at comparable accuracy: Step-Audio R1.1 Realtime (1.51s) and Grok Voice Think Fast 1.0 (1.25s) are faster than GPT-Realtime-2 at its higher reasoning settings (up to 2.33s). Real-time interaction typically requires under 500ms, which even minimal-reasoning GPT-Realtime-2 at 1.12s does not achieve.
- Hume's Real World VoiceEQ — 40+ voice models, 15+ dimensions, 60+ metrics, grounded in 700,000 human judgments — found NO single best model. Category leaders split three ways: Google Gemini strongest at Text-to-Speech and Speech Understanding, OpenAI's GPT Realtime Mini strongest at Speech-to-Speech, ElevenLabs strongest at Automatic Speech Recognition. Hume CEO Andrew Ettinger: 'Our results show there isn't one best voice model.'

AND THE PROBLEM IS OPEN. GPT-Realtime-2's own record concludes that an overall pass rate below 50% even at xhigh reasoning 'indicates reliable multi-turn spoken dialogue remains unsolved'. Hume separately found current models are being fine-tuned to perform well on popular benchmarks without those capabilities necessarily translating to real-world use.

THE POINT. Generational self-comparison measures how much a vendor improved on itself. It cannot establish position against competitors, and where the prior generation was weak on a metric the improvement ratio is largest exactly where the absolute standing is least established. Both kinds of number are legitimate; conflating them is not.

RELATED FAILURE MODES ALREADY RECORDED, distinct from this one: kb/technology/ai/security/benchmarks/single-turn-ranking-inverts-under-multi-turn.md (AGGREGATION choice — compositing single- and multi-turn attacks changes which vendor leads) and kb/technology/ai/economics/cost-shift-evidence-is-four-different-metrics.md (UNIT conflation — spend share, token share, price per token and hit rate read as one trend). This record concerns BASELINE choice. The three are separate mechanisms with separate evidence.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN:
- It does NOT claim GPT-Live-1 is not a large improvement over AVM. On the evidence it plainly is, including on safety flagging, and OpenAI stated the limitation of its own comparison rather than concealing it.
- It does NOT claim any rival is better overall. Hume's finding is specifically that no such ranking exists — different systems have different strengths and trade-offs.
- The rival comparisons concern GPT-Realtime-2 and GPT Realtime Mini, which are OpenAI's DEVELOPER-API voice models; GPT-Live-1 is the consumer ChatGPT Voice model and shipped with no developer API. They are not the same product and no source scores GPT-Live-1 against rivals.
- All figures are as-measured by the named evaluators (Artificial Analysis, Scale AI, Hume) or as-reported by OpenAI, none independently replicated here, and voice leaderboards at this release cadence go stale within weeks.
