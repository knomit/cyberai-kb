# Hume's Real World VoiceEQ benchmark finds no single best voice AI model across categories

Hume built a new voice AI benchmark called 'Real World VoiceEQ', arguing traditional benchmarks measure technical correctness rather than actual human interaction quality. It evaluates more than 40 voice models across more than 15 evaluation dimensions and more than 60 metrics, grounded in 700,000 human judgments — the largest human evaluation of voice AI to date per the release. Evaluations run on Kairos, Hume's voice-native evaluation platform, and assess emotional cue understanding, natural communication, and reliability in real-world conversation. Category leaders: Google Gemini strongest at Text-to-Speech and Speech Understanding; OpenAI's GPT Realtime Mini strongest at Speech-to-Speech; ElevenLabs strongest overall at Automatic Speech Recognition. Hume CEO Andrew Ettinger: 'Our results show there isn't one best voice model. Different systems have different strengths and trade-offs.' Hume also found current models are being fine-tuned to perform well on popular benchmarks without those capabilities necessarily translating to real-world use.
