# Q2D-Web benchmarks first-stage web-search retrievers at scale

Q2D-Web is a large-scale benchmark and leaderboard for evaluating first-stage web-search retrievers, spanning 190 million documents and 69,721 queries across ten languages, with three independent sets of relevance judgments.
