# dig.bench measures whether agents can experiment to discover a game's unknown rules

dig.bench is a benchmark testing whether an AI agent can experiment its way to discovering a game's unknown rules. It contains 70 text-based games, 21 of which have been publicly released, and scores progress by whether a game can be beaten within a limited number of steps. Humans can make the discoveries necessary to solve even the hardest games, while the best models struggle to beat games in the top tier.
