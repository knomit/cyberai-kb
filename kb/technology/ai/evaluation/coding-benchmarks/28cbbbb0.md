# Cognition SWE-2 matches frontier coding models at a fraction of cost

Cognition's SWE-2 achieves 50.0% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main1 while being 64% cheaper than SWE-1.7. It beats Grok 4.6 on both score and cost, matches GPT-5.6 Sol and Fable 5/5.1 at a fraction of the price, and comes within a few points of GPT-6 Astra at a quarter of the cost.
