# Real-SWE benchmarks coding agents on private enterprise codebases; best model resolves 38.8%

Specific Labs released Real-SWE in September 2026, a benchmark built from tasks in private production codebases licensed from real companies, so the code and solutions are not on the public internet. Top result is Fable 5.1 on Claude Code at a 38.8% resolution rate (pass@1 averaged over eight runs per task), followed by GPT-6 Astra on Codex CLI at 33.8% and Gemini 3.8 Flash on Gemini CLI at 31.2%; GPT-5.6 Sol on Codex CLI was last at 16.2%. Tasks are harder than public suites structurally: the reference solution edits a median of 11 files versus 6 for FrontierCode and DeepSWE, and 6 of 10 sampled tasks have resolution rates below 15%. The dominant failure mode is missed requirements, followed by integration errors and unverified assumptions. Longer rollouts did not help — 71.4% of rollouts under 10 minutes failed versus 73.4% of longer ones — and higher cost per rollout did not guarantee a higher resolution rate. The benchmark evaluates model-and-harness combinations rather than models in isolation.
