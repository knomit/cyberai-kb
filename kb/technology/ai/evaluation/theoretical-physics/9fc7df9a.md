# TheoPhysEval: AI scored up to 98% (49/50) on unpublished theoretical physics problems after experts corrected errors

Import AI 477 reports TheoPhysEval, a paper with 50 unpublished theoretical physics problems compiled by physicists. Per Suvrat Raju, initial scores for GPT-6-Astra were in the late eighties followed by Opus 5.5; models insisted expert answers were wrong, experts found subtle errors, and after revisions Astra reached 98% (49/50). Figures come via Jack Clark's speech; verify against the paper.
