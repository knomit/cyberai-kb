# GPT-6 Astra is the first model at OpenAI's Critical cybersecurity level, scoring 100% on ExploitBench

OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra is the company's most capable broadly deployed model and the first to reach the "Critical" cybersecurity level under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, meaning it triggers the framework's highest tier of deployment safeguards for cyber capability.

Reported scores: 100% on ExploitBench, 98% on FrontierMath Tier 4, and 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the ARC-AGI-3 figure is harness-dependent and drops to 62.7% under the benchmark's own standard harness — see kb/technology/ai/evaluation/agentic-benchmarks/astra-arc-agi-3-harness.md. A 100% score on ExploitBench indicates the benchmark is saturated as much as it indicates the ceiling of the model's offensive capability.
