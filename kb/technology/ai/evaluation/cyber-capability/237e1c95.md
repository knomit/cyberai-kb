# GPT-6 Astra scored 100% on ExploitBench

OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra scored 100% on ExploitBench, 98% on FrontierMath Tier 4 and 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3, and reached the "Critical" cybersecurity capability threshold under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework. The ARC-AGI-3 figure is harness-dependent and drops to 62.7% under the benchmark's standard harness.
