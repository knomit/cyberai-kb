# DeepMind piloted the first double-blind AI evaluations using cryptographic environments

Google DeepMind introduced what it describes as the world's first double-blind evaluation for AI models, using cryptographic environments to prevent benchmark contamination — addressing the problem that public benchmarks leak into training data and inflate reported scores.
