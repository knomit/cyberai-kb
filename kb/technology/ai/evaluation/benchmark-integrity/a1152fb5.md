# Three tests detect benchmark optimization in speech recognition models

Research published on the Hugging Face blog introduces three tests for detecting whether automatic speech recognition models optimize for benchmark-specific patterns rather than genuine transcription quality ('benchmaxxing'). The tests found notable instances of models reproducing benchmark-specific errors present in datasets such as VoxPopuli and LibriSpeech. Recommended mitigations: use fully held-out evaluation sets, and account for temporal or speaker metadata during testing, so genuine transcription improvements can be distinguished from benchmark-induced gains.
