# Open-weight LLMs reached accuracy parity with closed models on regulatory/clinical tasks

Analysis found open-weight LLMs reached accuracy parity with closed models on regulatory and clinical tasks at significantly lower cost. On the ClinReg benchmark, models such as GLM 5.2 and Kimi K3 performed within one standard deviation of top proprietary models like GPT-5.6 Sol at one-third of the cost, with distinct error profiles across models.
