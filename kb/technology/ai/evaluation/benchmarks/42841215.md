# OpenAI LifeVal benchmark measures AI on everyday-life tasks

OpenAI introduced LifeVal (early Nov 2026), a benchmark evaluating AI on practical everyday tasks such as budgeting, finding childcare, and accessing government services (per TLDR AI and The Deep View).
