# Perplexity published WANDR, an open benchmark for wide-and-deep research agents

WANDR (Wide ANd Deep Research) is an open benchmark and evaluation harness from Perplexity Research, built around realistic data-collection tasks for knowledge work. It tests whether research agents can sustain broad discovery without sacrificing factual quality record-to-record, using a flexible, composable qualification key hierarchy.
