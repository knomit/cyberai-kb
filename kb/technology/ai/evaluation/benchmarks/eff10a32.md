# ARC Prize announced ARC-AGI-4 as a benchmark for autonomous open-ended innovation

On 2026-09-12, ARC Prize announced that ARC-AGI-4 will be a benchmark for autonomous open-ended innovation, framed as the meta-skill that unlocks progress across every field of technology. ARC Prize stated that despite rapid model progress humans still significantly outperform AI at open-ended invention. The announcement was made as a direct response to Amodei's pacing essay, arguing that the knowledge behind frontier AI — not just the technology — should be broadly distributed, and that any coordinated industry effort to reduce openness or concentrate access to frontier AI would undermine a positive-sum future.
