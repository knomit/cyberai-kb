# Humanity's Sixth Sense benchmark: best AI 53.6 vs human 93.1 on implied-visual reasoning

Elorian and Scale AI released Humanity's Sixth Sense (HSS): 522 questions over 288 images and 234 video clips (17.6 h) testing reasoning about what a scene implies but does not show (temporal/causal, physical/spatial, social, abstract). Human pass@1 is 93.1; best AI (GPT 6 Astra) 53.6; Chinese open-weight GLM-5.3-flash 26.8 and Kimi K3 25.5. Top failure causes: missing a decisive detail (21%) and misidentifying an object/person/role (20%). Source: Import AI 476 (newsletter summary; primary Elorian/Scale Labs links are redirects).
