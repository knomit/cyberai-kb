# Benchmark Map: ~90% of US working hours have little or no benchmark coverage

Mercor's Benchmark Map project, aimed at a continuously updated resource on AI capabilities across the economy, initially finds that 90% of American working hours are spent on tasks with little or no coverage from existing benchmarks, making it hard to know how good AI models are at work Americans actually do.
