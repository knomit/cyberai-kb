# Astra's ARC-AGI-3 result is 62.7% or 99.9% depending on harness

ARC Prize reported (2026-09) that GPT-6 Astra scored 62.7% on ARC-AGI-3 Semi-Private under the standard provider-neutral harness, and 99.9% when run with a provider-supplied adapter harness. The 62.7% figure is at max reasoning and compares against a prior best of 30.2% by Claude Opus 5; the 99.9% figure is at high reasoning under the Provider Adapter harness. On 96% of levels Astra used fewer actions than the median human.

ARC Prize's qualitative observation was that Astra converted unfamiliar environments into compact symbolic world models, encoding game mechanics as logical rules and inventing its own shorthand notation to track state and plan.

The headline '99.9% on ARC-AGI-3' circulating in press is the adapter-harness number, not the standardized one; quoting it without the harness caveat is the common misreading. The widely-quoted figure is therefore a property of the scaffolding as much as the model: the same weights through the benchmark's own software score 62.7%.
