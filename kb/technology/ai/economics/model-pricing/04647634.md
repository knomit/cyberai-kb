# Fable 5.1 cuts effective cost via cheaper cache reads, not list price

Fable 5.1's per-token list price is unchanged from Fable 5 at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. The savings Anthropic advertises — roughly 25% less than Fable 5 for typical token-billed workloads and roughly 45% less for agentic workloads — come from reducing the cost of cache reads (re-reading previously processed and stored input), not from a headline price cut. Consequence: a workload only realizes the advertised savings if it actually reuses cached context; low-cache-hit workloads still pay the same $10/$50 rates, which remain among the most expensive on the market.
