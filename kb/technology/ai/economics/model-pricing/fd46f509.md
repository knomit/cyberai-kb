# Log-scale intelligence-vs-cost plots understate frontier model price gaps

A 2026-09 critique of ArtificialAnalysis' intelligence-vs-cost chart argues the visualization misleads in two ways. First, the logarithmic cost axis visually compresses the gap between cheap and frontier models, so readers underrate how enormous the frontier premium is and simultaneously overrate the (actually trivial) price differences among cheap models. Second, open-weight models are plotted at hosted datacenter pricing, which is far above their cost when run on local hardware. The author's conclusion is that most users do not need frontier-level intelligence and would be served by cheap Chinese open-source models. This is an opinion piece, not a measurement.
