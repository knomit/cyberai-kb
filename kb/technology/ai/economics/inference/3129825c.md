# Gartner: 90% Inference Cost Drop by 2030, 100x More Efficient vs 2022 — But Token Demand Rises

Gartner (March 2026) predicts: performing inference on a 1-trillion-parameter LLM will cost AI providers 90% less than 2025 by 2030. LLMs in 2030 will be 100x more cost-efficient than 2022 models of similar size. Drivers: semiconductor efficiency, model design innovations, chip utilization, inference-specialized silicon, edge devices. Two scenarios: Frontier (leading-edge chips only: lower cost) vs Legacy Blend (mixed: higher cost). BUT: savings will not automatically benefit enterprises. Frontier models demand significantly more tokens than mainstream apps. Agentic AI (e.g., OpenClaw) drives token demand higher, not lower. Will Sommer (Senior Director Analyst, Gartner): 'Integrating the next largest, most capable foundation models into every new feature will be commercially insolvent at scale.' Recommendation: diversify model portfolio — SLMs for routine high-frequency tasks, frontier for complex high-margin work.
