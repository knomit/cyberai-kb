# OpenAI 'The Deployment Company' raises $4B+, valued at $10B

OpenAI raised more than $4 billion from TPG, Brookfield, and Bain Capital for a new entity called 'The Deployment Company' (May 5, 2026), focused on helping businesses deploy AI into their operations. Valued at $10B per Bloomberg. Announced simultaneously with Anthropic's Blackstone deal. Both OpenAI and Anthropic are targeting late 2026 IPOs, making enterprise contract capture critical.
