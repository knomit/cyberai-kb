# Anthropic + Blackstone/Goldman create AI-native enterprise services firm (~$1.5B)

Anthropic inked a partnership with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs (May 5, 2026) to form a new 'AI-native enterprise services firm' aimed at integrating Claude into companies' core operations. The standalone entity uses Anthropic engineering. Valued at ~$1.5B per WSJ, it expands a prior $200B deal struck in April. Announced within minutes of OpenAI's competing deployment announcement.
