# OpenAI Economic Research Exchange Launched June 2026

OpenAI launched the OpenAI Economic Research Exchange on June 9, 2026 — a platform to fund and coordinate external academic research on AI's economic effects across labor markets, productivity, inequality, regional economics, public finance, and education. Selected researchers gain privacy-protected access to OpenAI tooling for empirical work. The Exchange builds on OpenAI Signals (launched February 2026) and follows the OpenAI Foundation's $250M commitment (announced late May 2026) to build institutional preparedness for AI-driven economic shocks.
