# Average LLM token spend fell from $2.07 to $0.97 per million between late May and 2026-08-31

The LLM Token Expenditure Index from SiliconData reports that as of 2026-08-31 users spent an average of 97 cents per million tokens, down from a high of $2.07 per million in late May 2026 — a roughly 53% decline in average realized token price over about three months, attributed to open-source model uptake and frontier-lab price competition.
