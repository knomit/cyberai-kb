# Sierra AI raises $950M Series E; Cerebras targets $26.6B IPO valuation

Sierra, the AI startup founded by OpenAI chairman Greg Brockman, raised a $950 million Series E (May 2026). Separately, AI chipmaker Cerebras is targeting a $26.6 billion valuation in its IPO. SAP announced plans to acquire Prior Labs and Dremio to advance its AI capabilities. Nvidia's Jensen Huang stated he has 'zero percent market share in China' due to US export restrictions.
