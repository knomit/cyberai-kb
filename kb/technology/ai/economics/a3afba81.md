# Forward-deployed-engineer hiring, up ~700% year over year across at least three vendors, is the first non-formation evidence about the AI deployment channel — and still does not measure its share

kb/meta/reasoning/bae0b862.md records that this corpus declared PE deployment arms the 'dominant AI enterprise sales channel' on the basis of two deals CLOSING, and that dominance is a share-or-flow claim which deal formation cannot settle. This record notes the first flow-type evidence to appear, and its limits.

THE SIGNAL. Forward-deployed-engineer (FDE) job postings were up roughly 700% YEAR OVER YEAR (per Business Insider, cited in The Deep View, 2 July 2026). At least three vendors are building the channel directly rather than only capitalizing it:
- Amazon Web Services announced a $1 BILLION investment to embed FDEs within its customer network, working directly with customers' business, engineering and security teams to deploy AI agents into their AWS environments. Francessca Vasquez, VP of frontier AI engineering and services, framed the goal as making customers 'self-sufficient with AI.'
- OpenAI launched its own Deployment Company for FDEs in May 2026.
- Anthropic formed an AI-native enterprise services firm with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman and Goldman Sachs (recorded separately).

WHY THIS IS A DIFFERENT KIND OF EVIDENCE. Deal closings measure that a bet was placed. Hiring measures the channel being STAFFED — capacity actually being built, at a rate, across competitors. That is closer to a flow measure and it is the first such measure to appear in this corpus for this channel.

WHY IT STILL DOES NOT SETTLE DOMINANCE.
- Postings measure HIRING INTENT, not filled roles, not delivered engagements, and not revenue attributable to the channel.
- A percentage growth figure with no absolute base is uninterpretable for share. 700% growth from a small base is compatible with the channel remaining marginal.
- It says nothing about the ALTERNATIVE channels — direct sales, cloud marketplaces, systems integrators, self-serve — which is what a share claim requires. Note in particular that AWS's move is a CLOUD PROVIDER building its own FDE capacity, which is at least as consistent with the channel being contested as with any one lab's PE arm capturing it.
- The AWS/Vasquez framing is explicitly about making customers SELF-SUFFICIENT, which if delivered would reduce rather than deepen the ongoing embedded-engineer relationship that the lock-in thesis depends on.

WHAT WOULD ACTUALLY SETTLE IT, unchanged from bae0b862: revenue or engagement share attributable to deployment-arm channels versus alternatives, or retention of accounts past the first embedded-engineer year. Neither exists here.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this does not claim the channel is failing or unimportant — three well-capitalized vendors staffing it simultaneously is meaningful. It claims only that the FDE hiring figure should be cited as evidence the channel is being BUILT, never as evidence it has WON.
