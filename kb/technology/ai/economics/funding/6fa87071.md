# David Silver (AlphaGo) raises $1.1B seed round for new AI lab Ineffable Intelligence

David Silver, the DeepMind researcher behind AlphaGo, raised a record $1.1B seed round for a new AI lab called Ineffable Intelligence. Backers include Nvidia and Google. This is notable as one of the largest seed rounds in history and reflects continued mega-scale investment in frontier AI research.
