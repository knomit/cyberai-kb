# Gartner predicts AI inference cost per agentic workflow will rise more than fivefold through 2028

Research firm Gartner predicts that AI inference costs per agentic workflow will increase more than fivefold through 2028.

The driver is an 'inference paradox': efficiency gains let labs develop and deploy more powerful, more expensive models, and users find increasingly sophisticated applications such as agentic workflows, so token consumption keeps climbing even as unit economics improve. Per-token prices falling and per-workflow cost rising are not in tension — they are the same mechanism.

Enterprises are pushed toward agents over chatbots because agents are seen as more likely to deliver step-change returns, despite those returns being unpredictable. Scott Bickley, Advisory Fellow at Info-Tech Research Group, told The Deep View that 'the current environment has created a top-down fervor, in fact a mandate, for virtually all enterprises to aggressively adopt AI en masse' often without understanding total cost of ownership.

CAVEAT ON PROVENANCE: the fivefold figure is a Gartner projection, and it also circulates in enterprise AI infrastructure marketing as motivation for vertically integrated inference providers — treat citations of it accordingly.
