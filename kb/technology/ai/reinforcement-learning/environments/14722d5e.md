# Prime Intellect releases verifiers v1 environment stack for agentic RL

Prime Intellect released 'verifiers v1', an overhaul of its environment stack for agentic reinforcement learning and evaluations. It decomposes environments into a task set, a harness, and a runtime, enabling complex agentic tasks such as coding and computer use at scale in any harness.
