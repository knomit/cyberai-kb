# Expensive-loop problem: models trained in cheap-loop domains lack skills for scarce-feedback work

Cheap-loop domains have quick, deterministic, cheap experiments; expensive-loop domains are limited by real-world cost or constraints, and most high-value human labor is expensive-loop. Such tasks need theory-building, proxy management, budgeting time/compute and diagnosing failure under scarce feedback, which the essay argues models do not learn from cheap-loop training.
