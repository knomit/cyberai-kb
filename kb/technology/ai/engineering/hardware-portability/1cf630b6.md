# Google released Accelerator Agents for PyTorch-to-JAX migration

Google released Accelerator Agents, a Gemini-powered toolkit including MaxCode and MaxKernel, to help developers migrate PyTorch workloads to JAX and optimize custom kernels for Google Cloud TPUs — lowering the switching cost that keeps workloads on Nvidia.
