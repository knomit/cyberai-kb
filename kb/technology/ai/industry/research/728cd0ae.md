# Stanford HAI 2026 AI Index: adoption surges to 88% but trust, safety, and talent acquisition lag

Stanford University's Center for Human-Centered AI (HAI) released its 2026 AI Index in April. Key findings: (1) Capability/adoption surge: 88% organizational AI adoption; 80% of university students use generative AI; >90% of notable frontier models released in 2025; several meet/exceed human PhD-level baselines. (2) Safety lagging: documented AI incidents rose to 362 in 2025 (up from 233 in 2024); responsible AI reporting remains uneven. (3) US-China gap nearly closed: Anthropic's top model leads China's best by just 2.7%. (4) US losing AI talent: number of AI researchers/developers moving to the US fell 80% in the last year. (5) Public trust deficit: 73% of AI experts positive about AI's impact on jobs; only 23% of general public shares that view. (6) Educational policy gap: only 50% of middle/high schools have AI policies; only 6% of educators say policies are clear. (7) Generative AI has reached 53% global population over three years; US adoption ~28%.
