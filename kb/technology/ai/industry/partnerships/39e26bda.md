# Apple adopts Google Cloud and Gemini as foundation model partner for new Siri

Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian confirmed at Google Cloud Next 2026 that Google is Apple's preferred cloud provider for developing next-generation Apple foundation models based on Gemini technology. These will power future Apple Intelligence features including a more personalized Siri launching in iOS 27 (expected fall 2026). The new Siri will draw on personal data (messages, notes, emails) for context, feature a chatbot-like interface that sits in the Dynamic Island, and take actions within apps. iOS 27 is expected to be unveiled at WWDC on June 8, 2026.
