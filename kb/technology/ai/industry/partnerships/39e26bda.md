# Apple bets on hardware + Gemini AI partnership under new CEO John Ternus

Apple announced John Ternus (hardware engineering leader) as next CEO succeeding Tim Cook on September 1, 2026 — signaling a hardware-first AI strategy. In parallel, Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian confirmed at Google Cloud Next 2026 that Google is Apple's preferred cloud provider for next-gen Apple foundation models based on Gemini, powering a new personalized Siri in iOS 27 (expected WWDC June 8, fall release). New Siri draws on personal data, features a Dynamic Island chatbot interface, and takes in-app actions. Apple's AI strategy: partner for foundation models (Gemini), win on hardware/integration/privacy/trust. Apple was dethroned from No. 1 public company as Nvidia and Google surged ahead on AI. Mac mini/Mac Studio backordered until fall due to AI agent boom. Vision Pro was a wrong bet vs. AR glasses.
