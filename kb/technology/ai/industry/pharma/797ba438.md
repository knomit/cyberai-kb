# Eli Lilly commits $2.75B to Insilico Medicine for AI drug discovery: 18-month discovery vs 5-6 year baseline

Eli Lilly agreed to pay up to $2.75B to Hong Kong-based Insilico Medicine (founded 2014) for AI-discovered drug candidates. Initial payment: $115M for exclusive rights to undisclosed pre-human-trial drugs; further payments tied to milestones. This is the third agreement between the companies (after 2023 AI software license and $100M research collaboration in Nov 2025). Insilico's AI pipeline: 28 candidate drugs, ~14 in clinical trials. Process: PandaOmics tool for target identification → Chemistry42 (30 parallel generative models) for molecule design. Key metric: 18 months from target identification to preclinical candidate vs conventional 5-6 years; screens 60-200 compounds vs conventional 200,000-1,000,000. Most advanced drug: Rentosertib for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) — Phase 2a positive results. Garutadustat for inflammatory bowel disease entered Phase 2a January 2026. Caveat: no AI-discovered drug has received regulatory approval yet.
