# August 2026 AI product and funding notes: River AI, Cohere North Micro Vision, Wispr, GLM-5.3

OpenAI enabled a 1 million token context window in Codex for GPT-5.6 Sol. Z.ai unveiled GLM-5.3, claiming coding and security gains. Wonder and Serve Robotics announced robot delivery on the Grubhub marketplace in three cities.
