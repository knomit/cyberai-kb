# Nvidia Ising: open AI models for quantum processor calibration and error correction

Nvidia released the Ising family of open models on April 15, 2026 for quantum computing infrastructure. Two models: (1) Ising Calibration — vision language model that interprets and reacts to quantum processor measurements; autonomous calibration without downtime; (2) Ising Decoding — 3D neural network for real-time error correction; 2.5x faster and 3x more accurate than conventional methods. Jensen Huang: 'AI becomes the control plane—the operating system of quantum machines.' Early partners: Harvard Engineering, Infleqtion, IQM Quantum Computers, Lawrence Berkeley National Lab, UK National Physical Laboratory. Strategy: same playbook as Nvidia's AI model open-sourcing — embed into the foundation of quantum computing before maturity. Quantum seen as potential solution to AI's energy burden and compute scarcity.
