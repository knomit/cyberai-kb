# John Ternus named Apple CEO, succeeding Tim Cook on September 1 2026

Apple announced John Ternus, longtime hardware engineering leader, as its next CEO, succeeding Tim Cook on September 1, 2026. This signals Apple doubling down on hardware, devices, and tightly integrated experiences in the AI era. Apple was dethroned from No. 1 public company as Nvidia and Google surged ahead. Key strategic context: Apple abandoned frontier AI lab ambitions (partnered with Google for Gemini/Siri), Mac mini and Mac Studio are backordered until fall due to AI agent boom, Vision Pro was a wrong bet vs. AR glasses. Ternus must escape the iPhone's gravitational pull and find the next disruptive form factor.
