# DeepSeek open-sourced DSpark speculative decoding

Per The Batch, researchers at Peking University and DeepSeek introduced DSpark, a speculative decoding method using a small draft module whose verification length is dynamically adjusted with server load. Applied to DeepSeek-V4 models, it sped per-user generation by ~57-85% versus DeepSeek's prior MTP-1 drafter without accuracy loss. Checkpoints DeepSeek-V4-Pro-DSpark and DeepSeek-V4-Flash-DSpark were released on Hugging Face under an MIT license.
