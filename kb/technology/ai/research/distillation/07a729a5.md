# Perplexity JOLT: joint teacher-student self-distillation

Perplexity's JOLT is a self-distillation method that jointly trains a model as both teacher and student, adapting feedback to the student's capabilities. It improved performance and training efficiency across mathematical reasoning, coding and agentic tasks (per Perplexity's post).
