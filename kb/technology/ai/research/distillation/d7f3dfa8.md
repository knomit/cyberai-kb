# Semi-OPD: distilling on offline student rollouts can beat on-policy distillation

NVIDIA research finds Semi-OPD (distilling from offline rollouts generated by the initial student) often outperforms on-policy distillation (OPD) in accuracy and training efficiency. OPD is beneficial only when initial teacher and student are highly aligned with high overlap ratios.
