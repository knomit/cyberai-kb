# Google self-training framework: LLMs improve from self-generated questions, answers and reasoning

Google researchers introduced a self-training framework in which foundation models improve using entirely self-generated questions, answers and reasoning traces, guided only by broad topic descriptions (arXiv 2610.32770, via TLDR AI 2026-10-27).
