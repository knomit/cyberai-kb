# NVIDIA Joints, Camera, Action! (JCA) generates video with synchronized 3D rig motion

NVIDIA's JCA generates videos and synchronized 3D motion for arbitrary character rigs from text prompts; supports scene-aware motion, keyframe-based control, and bidirectional conversion between video and rig animation.
