# Google Research 'sleep framework' for LLM continual learning

Per TLDR AI (2026-10-28), Google Research proposed a 'sleep framework' that gives LLMs a periodic lifecycle alternating active interaction with offline memory consolidation, converting short-term context into long-term parameters. Claimed benefits: acquiring new facts without catastrophic forgetting, retaining long-context knowledge up to 10M tokens, and improved complex reasoning (research-reported claims).
