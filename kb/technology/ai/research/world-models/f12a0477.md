# EchoWM is an open omnimodal world model generating synchronized 720p video, sound, music and speech along 6-DoF camera paths

EchoWM (released by JD's open-source group as JoyAI-Echo) is an omnimodal world model that follows continuous 6-DoF camera trajectories while jointly generating 720p video plus environmental sound, music and speech. It supports first- and third-person interaction and uses a combination of progressive and autoregressive training to achieve synchronized long-horizon generation.
