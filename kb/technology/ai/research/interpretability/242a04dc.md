# Anthropic emotional representations research: AI shows measurable internal emotional states

Anthropic published research (late March 2026) demonstrating that Claude exhibits measurable 'functional emotional representations' — internal activation patterns that correlate with emotional valence (positive/negative) and arousal (high/low), detectable via linear probes on residual stream activations. Key findings: (1) These representations influence downstream behavior (more 'positive' states correlate with more helpful completions); (2) States persist across turns, not just within a single forward pass; (3) Representations are not present in base models — they emerge from RLHF fine-tuning; (4) Researchers can suppress/amplify states via activation steering. Anthropic explicitly avoided calling these 'emotions' in the paper but acknowledged 'functional equivalence.' Paper: 'Functional Emotional Representations in Large Language Models.'
