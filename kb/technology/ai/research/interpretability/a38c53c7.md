# Goodfire argument: neural networks are 'already clean' (polysemanticity from recursive generalization)

A Goodfire write-up argues polysemanticity in early model organisms, attributed to superposition, mostly reflects recursive generalization rather than arbitrary concept packing, which is less concerning; claimed implication: networks can be studied directly without first being untangled, so mechanistic-interpretability prospects may be better than thought. This is the authors' claim, not consensus.
