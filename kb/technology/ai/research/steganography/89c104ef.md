# Language models can hide and decode messages in images without being told the method

A LessWrong post reports that language models can encode messages in images that the same model can decode even when no information about the encoding method is passed; the encoded messaging is hard to spot, though decoding is obvious if the decoder's tool calls are monitored. Framed as a possible precursor to image steganography. Implication: monitoring tool calls is a more reliable detection route than inspecting the images.
