# Priming: converting pretrained Transformers to efficient architectures at <1% of training cost

Amazon Science describes 'Priming', which reuses pretrained Transformer weights to build models with more efficient architectures at under 1% of original training cost and up to 2x throughput. It enables cheap exploration of layer type, hybrid ratio and layer placement from a shared strong initialization, and is not tied to a specific model family.
