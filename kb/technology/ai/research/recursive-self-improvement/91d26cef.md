# Dream-RSI closes the recursive self-improvement loop at the exploration layer using replay simulators

Dream-RSI is a recursive self-improvement method built on the premise that progress in RSI hinges on effective exploration and that accumulated discovery history can serve as a replay simulator over an already-realized search space. The loop: continuously collect discovery histories through online exploration, construct replay simulators from them, refine meta-exploration strategies via 'dreaming' inside those simulators, then redeploy the upgraded policy online. The authors report improvements in both discovery effectiveness and efficiency across several settings.
