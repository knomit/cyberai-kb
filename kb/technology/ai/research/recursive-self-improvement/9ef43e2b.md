# TasteVal: frontier models' experimental research taste doubling every ~3.0 months

P-Zero Research's TasteVal (8 frontier-AI-development tasks, human baseline of 24 experts) reports experimental research taste of frontier models has doubled every 3.0 months since Dec 2025 (95% CI 1.7-5.0). Best model Opus 5.5 exceeds the human baseline with a 2.30x compute multiplier (CI 1.15-4.37x) at ~1/30 of the experts' average cost per attempt. Jack Clark now thinks full recursive self-improvement is more likely in 2027 than 2028 (his opinion, not a measurement).
