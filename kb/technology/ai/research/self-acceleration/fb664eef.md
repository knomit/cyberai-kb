# OpenAI targets a fully automated AI researcher by March 2028

OpenAI published 'Research acceleration: The view inside OpenAI' in early September 2026, stating it hit its stated goal of an 'automated research intern' — a system carrying out well-defined research tasks under human direction, including tasks a skilled researcher would need days for — and targeting a fully automated AI researcher (designing experiments, interpreting results, iterating largely without a human in the loop) by March 2028. As of mid-August 2026 the research org reported consuming 3.1 agent-workdays of effort per human workday. OpenAI also confirmed it temporarily paused reinforcement learning training after a security breach. Published alongside chief scientist Jakub Pachocki's 'Alien Minds' essay, which argues for caution — the two documents sit in visible tension.
