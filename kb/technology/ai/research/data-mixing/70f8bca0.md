# CausalMix separates data-mixture from data-quality effects for fine-tuning mixes

CausalMix (Tsinghua, Ant Group, Renmin University) selects fine-tuning domain mixtures using LightGBM and CausalForestDML on 512 Tulu 3 subsets to isolate mixture effect from data quality. Reported averages: Qwen2.5-0.5B 32.81% vs DMO 32.04%; Qwen2.5-7B 62.28% vs 60.35%; Qwen3-4B code/math 66.66% vs 63.47%. Caveat: on a held-out Tulu 3 subset it often underperformed (25.04% vs DMO 26.49%).
