# Nvidia's 4th scaling law: agentic scaling (AI talking to AI, multi-trillion params)

At GTC 2026, Nvidia posed a 4th scaling law: 'agentic scaling' — following pretraining scaling, post-training scaling, and test-time scaling. Agentic scaling involves AI not just talking to humans, but to other AIs, vastly increasing demand for low-latency, large-context inference. Kari Briski, VP of GenAI software at Nvidia: 'The fourth scaling law is not just about one reasoning model. It's about a swarm of agents with subagents. Agents talking to agents.' Claims: multi-agent systems will unlock multi-trillion parameter models, turning daylong requests into hours. Requirements: 15x faster token delivery, 10x larger models. Jensen Huang: 'intelligence is directly correlated to the amount of compute that you have.' Strategic implication: agentic scaling justifies continued GPU demand expansion and positions Nvidia at center of inference infrastructure.
