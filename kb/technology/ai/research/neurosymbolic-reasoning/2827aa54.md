# Metis proposes explicit epistemic-state reasoning core ('The Age of Reasoning')

Startup Metis published a position paper 'The Age of Reasoning' proposing AI architectures with an explicit persistent epistemic state of claims/hypotheses and a reasoning policy/value function guiding steps that reduce uncertainty, with claim status changes licensed by evidence and independent judgment, giving auditable provenance. Position paper, little technical detail, no reported results.
