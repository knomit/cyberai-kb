# Apple's Residual Context Diffusion improves diffusion LLMs by recycling discarded tokens

Apple Machine Learning research introduced Residual Context Diffusion, a module for block-wise diffusion large language models (dLLMs). Instead of discarding non-confident tokens during the remasking/decoding step, it converts those discarded token representations into contextual residuals and reinjects them into the next denoising step, consistently improving frontier dLLM accuracy with minimal extra compute.
