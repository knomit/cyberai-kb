# Meta AI's FLAT encodes images and text as variable-length continuous token sequences

Meta AI introduced FLAT, a method that converts images and text into the same flexible-length sequence of continuous tokens usable for both retrieval and generation. Nested dropout arranges the information from coarse to fine within the sequence, which is what lets a model trade computation for visual detail by varying the number of tokens it uses — the coarse-to-fine ordering is the mechanism, not an incidental detail, because without it truncating the sequence would not degrade gracefully.
