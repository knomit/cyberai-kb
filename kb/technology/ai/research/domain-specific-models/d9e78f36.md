# Thinking Machines: fine-tuned custom models beat frontier models on financial tasks

Research from Thinking Machines ('Learning to Replicate Expert Judgment in Financial Tasks') found that frontier models struggle with relatively simple financial tasks, while custom models fine-tuned on high-quality proprietary datasets labeled by expert investors perform better and are substantially cheaper — pointing toward a future of differentiated, organization-specific models that outperform frontier models on their niche.
