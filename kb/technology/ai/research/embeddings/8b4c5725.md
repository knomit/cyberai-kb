# Perplexity PPLX-EMBED-V2-LATE: multi-vector multimodal embeddings (0.6B and 9B)

Perplexity launched PPLX-EMBED-V2-LATE, multi-vector multimodal embeddings that keep token-level vectors in a shared text-image space, improving retrieval on benchmarks such as ViDoRe V3, in 0.6B and 9B sizes.
