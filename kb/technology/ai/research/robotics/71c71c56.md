# Frontier models' robot-arm control ability doubling about every 54 days

Researchers tested nine multimodal models on six real-arm tasks between July and October 2026 and found performance doubling about every 54 days; best execution rate up 4.4x and lowest cost per subtask down 6.2x. Models are limited mostly by efficiency and precision rather than reasoning. A short-window trend from six tasks, not a long-run law.
