# EXPO-FT demonstrates a stable RL approach toward a universal robotics post-training recipe

A research post argues robotics needs a universal post-training recipe analogous to language models' post-training pipeline to reach reliable autonomous deployment; existing approaches like imitation learning rely on ad hoc intuition rather than structured processes, and RL approaches are typically unstable. The proposed EXPO-FT method demonstrates a stable RL approach, and the authors call for standardized protocols, reward specifications, human feedback systems, and scalable processes to improve robotics reliability.
