# Guide to parallelism strategies for transformer training and their bottlenecks

An interactive guide by Edward Yang covers the main parallelism strategies for training transformers: data parallelism, FSDP (Fully Sharded Data Parallel), tensor parallelism, pipeline parallelism, and expert parallelism. Its focus is on how hardware topology and communication patterns determine the point at which each strategy becomes bottlenecked.
