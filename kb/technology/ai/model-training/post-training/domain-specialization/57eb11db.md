# Harvey post-trained a Kimi K3 base with async RL for long-horizon legal workflows

Legal AI company Harvey post-trained a Kimi K3 base model using asynchronous reinforcement learning inside realistic long-horizon legal environments. It then trained separate capabilities for diligence, review tables, and firm knowledge, which the main system can route to as tools or subagents.

The central claim is that a professional-services model improves by learning the workflow and the harness it operates in — not merely by ingesting more legal text. Caveat: all reported results are vendor-authored and task-specific.
