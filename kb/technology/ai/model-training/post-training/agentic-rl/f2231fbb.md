# TaoLive's Harness-Aware Training teaches a compact 35B model to interpret a changing agent harness

TaoLive introduced Harness-Aware Training, a post-training recipe (not a new model architecture). During supervised fine-tuning and agentic reinforcement learning, the training deliberately varies skill names and wording, tool schemas, prompt structure, and hook behavior. This teaches a compact 35B model to interpret whatever harness it currently receives rather than memorizing one fixed interface.

Caveat: the evaluation is vendor-authored and concentrated in live-commerce scenarios.
