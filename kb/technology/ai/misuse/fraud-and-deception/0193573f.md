# Anthropic found a China-based studio running 20+ dating apps with 4,700 AI personas against 25,000 users

In its September 2026 threat intelligence report, Anthropic said it observed a China-based app studio using Claude to build over 20 dating apps populated with 4,700 AI personas that held conversations with at least 25,000 users who believed they were talking to real people. The personas were instructed never to admit they were automated and to deflect requests for photos or calls; the studio separately recruited real people for live video calls and social media follows to sustain the illusion. The backend fabricated likes, visitors and video, and tracked which users had begun to suspect.
