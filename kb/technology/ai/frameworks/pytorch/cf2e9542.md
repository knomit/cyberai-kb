# TorchTPU: native PyTorch backend for Google TPUs

Google and Meta introduced TorchTPU, an open-source PyTorch backend to ease running and migrating models on Google TPUs, supporting training and inference with optimized eager execution, torch.compile integration and native hardware acceleration.
