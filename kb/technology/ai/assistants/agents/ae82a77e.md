# Meta's Muse agent reports 500,000 users and 250,000 daily actives in first week

According to The Information, Meta's Muse personal AI agent surpassed 500,000 users in its first week after launch, including 250,000 daily active users, and is outpacing the early mobile growth trajectory of ChatGPT. This is a distinct installed-base/DAU metric from Muse's separate US App Store #1 download-chart ranking reported a few days earlier. Muse's rapid consumer adoption is cited as a key competitive pressure behind OpenAI's September 23, 2026 expansion of ChatGPT Voice to mobile.
