# OpenAI shipped an Apple Messages integration for ChatGPT on macOS

On 2026-08-20 OpenAI launched an Apple Messages plugin for ChatGPT that lets it find specific messages, summarize conversations, and compose and send messages. It works with iMessage, SMS, and RCS and runs in the ChatGPT desktop app for macOS. Coverage differs on availability: 9to5Mac reports it is available across all plans in the macOS desktop app, while The Deep View describes the launch as limited to ChatGPT Work and Codex on Mac. Demo use cases include asking which threads to follow up on (returning suggested replies for review) and checking the calendar to propose free times in a text. Commentators flag privacy/control risk in granting persistent Messages access, and note the feature pressures Apple's Siri overhaul expected with iOS 27 in September 2026 — Siri's advantage being cross-device availability rather than Mac-only.
