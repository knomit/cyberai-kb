# Apple Leads WWDC 2026 with Safety, Security, and Child Safety Frameworks Before AI Feature Reveal

At WWDC 2026, Apple did something without precedent at a major AI keynote: it paused before announcing any AI features to explain its safety guidelines, security frameworks, and child safety guardrails. The move was framed as: 'before we put the most powerful technology in your hands and the hands of your kids, here is how we built guardrails.' No comparable AI company has led a major product announcement this way. The Deep View editorial called it 'a breath of fresh air' and suggested it should reset industry expectations.
