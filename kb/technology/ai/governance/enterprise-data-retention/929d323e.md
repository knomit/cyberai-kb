# Anthropic's Enterprise Frontier Safeguards and OpenAI's Private Safety Processing both promise misuse monitoring without vendor-held data, and neither has published the design

MERGED from the full side-by-side record of the two schemes and a thinner record of Anthropic's EFS announcement. The thinner record's two distinct claims — the ZDR-equivalence framing and the false-positive-reduction claim — are preserved in the EFS section below.

In late August/early September 2026 the two labs previewed competing answers to the same enterprise objection, reported side by side in The Batch (2026-09-04).

BACKGROUND: since June 2026, businesses using Claude Fable 5 have been required to let Anthropic retain conversations for 30 DAYS, which broke existing zero-data-retention (ZDR) contracts. Many enterprises declined to adopt Fable 5 as a result, and the ARC Prize Foundation declined to run verified tests rather than expose its private test questions. Anthropic's own August risk report conceded the rule would be unpopular with customers who expect zero retention and could hurt its business IF RIVALS DID NOT MATCH IT.

ANTHROPIC — ENTERPRISE FRONTIER SAFEGUARDS (EFS), announced after a Bloomberg leak and introduced alongside the Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 releases: the 30-day retention requirement STAYS, but the data sits on the CUSTOMER'S OWN INFRASTRUCTURE or a designated third-party provider (Amazon S3, Azure Blob Storage, Google Cloud Storage), with customer-managed encryption keys and audit logs. Anthropic says this gives customers THE SAME PRIVACY RIGHTS AS A ZERO-DATA-RETENTION POLICY, plus safeguards that REDUCE FALSE POSITIVES IN CYBERSECURITY CONTEXTS. Anthropic's automated systems analyze a rolling window of traffic for serious misuse signals — offensive cyber or bio capability development, stolen or leaked credentials — and flags go to the customer WITH NO ANTHROPIC HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED. EFS ships sometime in FALL 2026; until then eligible enterprise customers can use Fable 5 and Fable 5.1 with no Anthropic retention. Under the policy in force today Anthropic keeps Fable 5 and Mythos 5 conversations 30 days on every platform, and content it flags AT ITS OWN DISCRETION can be kept UP TO TWO YEARS and read by approved reviewers through a logged process.

OPENAI — PRIVATE SAFETY PROCESSING (PSP), published one day before the Anthropic news broke: OpenAI reaffirmed that FOR APPROVED BUSINESS CUSTOMERS PLUGGING MODELS INTO THEIR OWN SOFTWARE, its most capable models come with ZDR — prompts and replies are never logged, and staff cannot see them EXCEPT WHERE FEDERAL LAW REQUIRES. PSP scans data held on the customer's servers, or encrypted on OpenAI's with keys OpenAI SAYS its staff does not hold, and returns ONLY A LABEL for the type of activity, not the content.

THE UNRESOLVED QUESTION The Batch presses: "our employees can't see it" is not "our systems can't see it" — to scan data, software the vendor wrote must unlock and read it wherever it sits, and NEITHER COMPANY HAS EXPLAINED HOW. Anthropic's technical staffer Sholto Douglas described the plan on X as monitoring "done via automated systems we provide to you." Neither company has published the criteria by which something counts as a cyberattack or as unsafe, and NEITHER HAS PUBLISHED AN INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE DESIGN; OpenAI promised a PSP technical paper in September 2026. Both cite cyberattacks visible only across many requests — including state-actor espionage and best-of-N jailbreaking — as the justification.

FORESEEABLE MISREADING: moving logs onto customer infrastructure is A CHANGE OF CUSTODY, NOT DELETION — the data still exists and can be compromised or subpoenaed. Whether logs on a customer's servers can be reached in a suit against the AI company is a question neither company has addressed; in the New York Times copyright suit a court ordered OpenAI to preserve chat logs it would normally have deleted, and OpenAI said ZDR customers were unaffected because it never had their data. The ZDR-equivalence and false-positive-reduction claims for EFS are ANTHROPIC'S OWN CHARACTERIZATION of a system that had not shipped at the time of the announcement.
