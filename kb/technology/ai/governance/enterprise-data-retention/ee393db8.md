# Anthropic introduced Enterprise Frontier Safeguards with the 5.1 release

Alongside Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, Anthropic introduced a data-retention system called Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, which it says gives customers the same privacy rights as a zero-data-retention (ZDR) policy, plus safeguards that reduce false positives in cybersecurity contexts.
