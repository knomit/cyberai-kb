# AI Agent Governance Gap: 70% of Enterprises Run Agents Faster Than They Can Govern Them (Gartner)

Gartner's inaugural Market Guide for Guardian Agents confirms that enterprise AI agent adoption is dramatically outpacing governance controls, creating significant security and operational risks.

- **Adoption scale**: ~70% of enterprises already run AI agents in production; another 23% plan deployments in 2026
- **Governance risk**: Gartner states 'enterprise adoption of AI agents is accelerating, outpacing maturity of governance policy controls'
- **Core problem**: Many organizations have no centralized inventory of agents operating in their environment — no visibility into what data they access or what identities they use
- **'Identity dark matter'**: AI agents operate continuously, span multiple applications, acquire permissions opportunistically, and generate activity at machine speed — creating an invisible, unmanaged layer of identity activity
- **Guardian Agents**: A new product category — agents that supervise other AI agents, helping ensure agent actions align with organizational goals and boundaries
- **Risk escalation**: As AI agents become more autonomous in critical workflows, risks of operational failure and noncompliance escalate
