# OpenAI signals openness to pacing frontier development, other labs silent

Per a Bloomberg report published Friday 2026-09-11, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman told employees at a company all-hands the prior week that OpenAI would potentially pace development of its frontier AI, possibly in partnership with other major labs. This follows OpenAI already slowing certain parts of model development in August 2026, when it paused some internal safety runs to bolster safety and security measures. OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki wrote in an essay titled 'An Alien Mind' that the current era 'calls for extreme caution' and that he expects and hopes 'voluntary slowdowns to become commonplace until shared safety bars are established.' Paul Christiano, who joined the OpenAI Foundation board the prior week, wrote that no major lab is on track to reduce AI risk to an acceptable level and that 'if OpenAI rises to the occasion we could significantly reduce risk.' The critical gap as of 2026-09-14: only the two labs are participating. Google DeepMind did not respond to The Deep View's requests for comment despite former DeepMind chief Demis Hassabis having previously pitched an international slowdown framework, and neither Meta nor SpaceXAI responded. Chinese labs' response is unknown. So a two-lab commitment is not an industry pause, and an internal all-hands statement is not a published policy.
