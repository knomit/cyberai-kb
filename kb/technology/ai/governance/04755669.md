# US government pre-deployment scrutiny became a factor in frontier model releases by mid-2026

By mid-2026, US government review of top-tier AI models emerged as a recurring gate on frontier model releases. Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 were suspended under a June 12, 2026 Department of Commerce export-control directive and only restored (July 1) after adding cybersecurity guardrails, while OpenAI's broader GPT-5.6 release was delayed via a government-mandated capability preview until agencies and corporations were prepared for the models' vulnerability-discovery abilities. The shared pattern: governments increasingly demand pre-deployment assessment of the most capable models' cyber/bio capabilities, adding guardrails and staged rollouts as conditions of release.
