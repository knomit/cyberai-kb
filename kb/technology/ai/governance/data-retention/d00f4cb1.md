# OpenAI previewed Private Safety Processing alongside zero data retention

OpenAI reaffirmed zero data retention for approved business customers who plug its models into their own software, and previewed "Private Safety Processing," a system intended to flag misuse patterns across many requests without OpenAI reading the prompts themselves.
