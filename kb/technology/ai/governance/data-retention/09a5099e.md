# Anthropic Enterprise Frontier Safeguards relocates ZDR retention to customer infrastructure

Anthropic will launch "Enterprise Frontier Safeguards" in autumn 2026, requiring zero-data-retention enterprise customers to store the same 30-day retention window of data on their own infrastructure or partner clouds (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Foundry) rather than on Anthropic's servers. Until then, eligible customers can use Claude Fable 5/5.1 with true zero retention. This does NOT mean retention is eliminated — it relocates who holds the data, not whether it exists.
