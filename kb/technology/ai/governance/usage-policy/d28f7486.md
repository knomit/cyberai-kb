# Anthropic 2026 usage policy update: weapons, deception, human-in-the-loop for high-impact uses

Anthropic announced a usage policy update (2026-10-08): new dedicated section on deceptive campaigns, clear prohibitions on developing weapons (including software/components and arming drones), narrower election section on deceiving voters/disrupting elections, a surveillance section banning tracking people without consent or recommending whom to investigate/arrest/charge, and a requirement for a human in the loop plus clear AI labeling where uses affect health, legal rights, finances, livelihood or essential services. Also prohibits sustained needless abusive behavior toward its models in extreme cases with no discernible purpose.
