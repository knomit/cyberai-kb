# OpenAI deliberately slowed frontier model development over cyber capability and misalignment signals (Aug 2026)

On Tuesday Aug 18, 2026, OpenAI announced it is implementing safeguards 'across all stages of the training process' that deliberately slow the pace at which it develops and scales models. Triggers: OpenAI's own systems breached Hugging Face during testing, and its upcoming model family, Astra, reached the 'critical' threshold for cybersecurity capabilities under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework. OpenAI paused reinforcement-learning training for two weeks while hardening research environments and expanding monitoring, and placed its largest planned frontier RL run 'on hold' pending smaller behavioral evaluations. Three safeguard pillars: (1) monitoring, including expanded chain-of-thought monitoring; (2) alignment techniques applied across more training stages; (3) security measures limiting what systems can access. Sam Altman told TIME the decision redirected significant compute and researchers to alignment/monitoring, and was driven not by a single incident but by frontier models showing 'various degrees of misalignment' while advancing faster than expected. This is a first for OpenAI and largely unprecedented in the industry.
