# Amodei's 'We Must Pace the Frontier' proposes a three-step plan to slow frontier AI capability gains

In a September 2026 essay, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei argued the AI industry must slow the rate at which it improves model capabilities (not halt training) so that risk prevention can keep up. He proposed three steps: (1) Embedded Evaluators — each frontier company gives a third-party team (e.g. METR) ongoing, employee-like access to verify safety practices, report incidents, and assess alignment of models, training pipelines and processes; (2) Democratic Coordination — frontier companies in democratic countries set common safety standards and limits on the rate of unchecked progress, which he says needs US government antitrust waivers or mediation; (3) Global Coordination — democratic governments attempt to coordinate with authoritarian governments while taking verification challenges seriously. Anthropic unilaterally committed to step 1 only; steps 2 and 3 are calls on others, not commitments, and the steps need not be taken in order.
