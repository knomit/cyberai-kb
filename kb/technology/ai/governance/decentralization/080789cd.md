# Linux Foundation announces Agent Name Service (ANS) at Confidential Computing Summit

At the Confidential Computing Summit (June 2026, San Francisco), organized by The Linux Foundation with leaders from Google, Microsoft, AMD, Intel and others, participants pushed a 'sovereign AI' / decentralization agenda: encrypting and controlling what agents remember, rooting trust in hardware-based confidential computing (enforced at the chip level), and giving every agent a verified identity and audit trail. The Linux Foundation announced the Agent Name Service (ANS), analogous to DNS, to provide verifiable agent identity. Opaque CEO Aaron Fulkerson (event emcee) argued 'we should never have to pay for AI with our data' and warned that dependence on one or two model providers creates a fragile single-off-switch system. Reported by The Deep View; OpenAI and Anthropic were noted as absent.
