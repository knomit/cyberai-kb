# Anthropic gives METR employee-like access for independent risk evaluation

On 2026-10-15 Anthropic announced it will give a six-person METR team 'employee-like access' (company hardware, office space, internal tools) to assess frontier-model risks; METR will publish its results. Employees can share anything except customer data, attorney-client privileged information, and model-architecture information. Anthropic listed four evaluator requirements: frontier-capability expertise, financial/operational independence, enough access (model and training), and freedom to publish. Reuters (2026-10-13) reported Anthropic and OpenAI are trying to control what evaluators publish, a transparency caveat.
