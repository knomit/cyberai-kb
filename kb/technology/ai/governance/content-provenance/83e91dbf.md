# Anthropic watermarks all Claude-generated content post-Aug 2026 for EU AI Act

Anthropic updated its support documentation to state that all Claude models released after August 2, 2026 automatically watermark both text and files they generate or edit, with files using the C2PA open standard. The measure is to comply with EU AI Act transparency rules requiring AI-generated/edited content to be identifiable. Anthropic plans to extend watermarking to older models and to make text watermarks persist across copy-paste. The standard applies across Anthropic products including Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and Claude Tag. OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, Cohere, Meta and Synthesia have also committed to the EU code of practice on transparency.
