# Anthropic restored Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 after US export control directive

Per The Batch, Anthropic restored customer access to Claude Fable 5 (via Claude API, Claude Code, and other platforms) on July 1, 2026, about three weeks after a June 12 US Department of Commerce export control directive suspended access to Fable 5 and the more powerful Mythos 5. The directive followed Amazon researchers using Fable 5 to obtain cyberattack information; controls were lifted June 30 after Anthropic added guardrails that block some cybersecurity queries and route them to the less capable Claude Opus 4.8.
