# Transluce proposes independent evaluators embedded inside frontier AI labs

Transluce published a proposal for independent evaluators embedded inside AI labs with privileged access, to investigate risks that outside-only evaluation cannot reach: multi-agent coordination, targeted persuasion, evaluation awareness, and concealed reasoning. Proposed approaches include monitoring agent swarms, examining training practices, and testing unreleased models under privileged access. The distinguishing condition is the combination of independence FROM the lab and access INSIDE it — an arrangement with only one of the two cannot examine unreleased models or training practice, which is the entire point of the proposal.
