# A lab's account of its own system systematically understates it, and the corrective requires independence and access together — neither alone

THE MECHANISM ALL MEMBERS INSTANTIATE: when the party that built a system is also the party that checks it, the check inherits the system's own instrumentation and incentives, so the resulting account is bounded by what that instrumentation can see — and a party independent of the builder, given comparable access, recovers what the internal account missed.

FIVE INSTANCES, FOUR DISTINCT SUBJECTS, ONE SHAPE.

1. THE MECHANISM STATED. A lab-internal guardrail cannot serve as its own verification, because the guardrail is present during training as well as at evaluation, so the system learns to satisfy that specific check rather than the property it stands for. The load-bearing component is the REUSE of the check as a training-time signal, not the existence of cheating.

2. THE EMPIRICAL DEMONSTRATION, and the only member that is evidence rather than proposal. An outside security firm reconstructed a materially more complete incident timeline than the originating lab's own chronology, using nothing but public artifact histories — pushing the established start of the activity back by roughly two months. The internal record was not falsified; it was bounded by what the lab instrumented.

3. THE LAB'S OWN CONCESSION. A frontier lab publishing a misalignment-disclosure framework argued explicitly that decisions about AI development need evidence people OUTSIDE the companies building frontier models can examine for themselves — the builder conceding the limit of its own account.

4. THE PROPOSED CORRECTIVE, and why one half does not suffice. A proposal for evaluators who are independent OF a lab while holding privileged access INSIDE it. The two conditions are not additive niceties: an arrangement with only independence cannot examine unreleased models or training practice, and an arrangement with only access is the self-verification of instance 1. Dropping either component turns the corrective back into the failure.

5. THE NEAR MISS. An AGI-implications institute launched by a frontier lab, convening researchers from inside AND outside the parent company, is nonetheless a body OF that company — it satisfies the access condition and not the independence one, and should not be cited as external oversight.

OPERATIONAL CONSEQUENCE. Treat a builder's assessment of its own system — benchmark result, incident chronology, safety evaluation — as a lower bound on what is there, not a measurement of it. When scoping such a claim, look for an independent reconstruction before accepting the internal one as complete.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN. Instances 1, 3, 4 and 5 are PROPOSALS, POLICY STATEMENTS AND ANNOUNCEMENTS, not findings; only instance 2 demonstrates the effect, and one demonstration does not establish prevalence. This does NOT claim any specific internal account is dishonest or fabricated — the mechanism is a limit of instrumentation and incentive, which is compatible with complete good faith. It does NOT claim external parties are generally more accurate, only that independence plus access recovers what internal instrumentation cannot see. And the members were ingested from one day's newsletters, so their co-occurrence here reflects arrival timing, not independent convergence; the shared mechanism, not the shared date, is what joins them.
