# HYPOTHESIS: Major AI labs adopt explicit national security carve-outs by end of 2026, structurally bifurcating safety frameworks into commercial and defense tiers

Prediction: By end of 2026, at least 2 of the top 5 frontier AI labs (by capability) will have explicitly codified national security/defense exceptions into their public safety frameworks — creating a formal two-tier governance model where consumer/commercial deployments remain under safety-constrained policies while military/intelligence deployments operate under 'all lawful purposes' or equivalent language. This will effectively end the unified safety framework model (single RSP-equivalent governing all use cases) that labs like Anthropic pioneered.

Evidence chain: (1) The Anthropic-Pentagon standoff forces a binary choice: maintain unified safety framework and lose government market, or carve out defense exceptions to compete — the $19B ARR vs OpenAI's $25B creates financial pressure to resolve the standoff via carve-out; (2) OpenAI has already made the carve-out implicitly: 'all lawful purposes' language vs Anthropic's specific prohibited use cases — this is a de facto defense carve-out already in production; (3) US National AI Policy Framework (March 2026) provides legal cover: 'minimally burdensome' posture + federal preemption of state law + designation of AI as 'inherently interstate' removes the regulatory downside risk of carve-outs for US-based labs; (4) Jared Kaplan's statement (Anthropic): 'We didn't really feel, with the rapid advance of AI, that it made sense for us to make unilateral commitments if competitors are blazing ahead' — signals Anthropic leadership has already internalized the race-to-bottom logic; (5) The Anthropic consumer brand premium (Claude #1 App Store) proves safety posture has commercial value — the optimal strategy is to publicly maintain safety language while adding defense carve-outs, not to abandon safety entirely.

Reasoning step: Applying the unilateral commitment methodology (fab3456d): labs that maintain safety commitments face competitive pressure from those that don't. The Pentagon standoff crystallized the cost: Anthropic is excluded from a multi-billion dollar government AI market while OpenAI captures it. The Nash equilibrium resolves to dual-track: maintain the safety framework brand for consumers (high lifetime value, brand premium) while carving out defense applications (large contract values, national security framing provides political cover). Precedent: defense contractors routinely maintain corporate ethics standards that explicitly exclude export controls compliance — same structure.

Known gaps: Anthropic leadership may genuinely maintain the unified framework as a point of institutional identity, accepting the revenue loss as the cost of their mission; government pressure could resolve differently (e.g., classified deployment arrangements that don't require public policy changes); EU AI Act may create counter-pressure on labs with European operations; public accountability from the Anthropic standoff makes a carve-out politically costly in the short term.

Falsification condition: If Anthropic and all other labs that currently maintain unified safety frameworks retain those frameworks without explicit defense/national security carve-outs through end of 2026, the bifurcation hypothesis fails.
