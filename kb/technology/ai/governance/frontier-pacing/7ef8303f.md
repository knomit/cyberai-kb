# Dario Amodei's three-part 'We Must Pace the Frontier' framework

On Saturday 2026-09-12, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay titled 'We Must Pace the Frontier,' citing recursive self-improvement, rogue-agent incidents such as the OpenAI–Hugging Face breach, and general lack of understanding of these systems. He proposed a three-part framework intended to make labs 'race to the top' on safety rather than to the bottom on risk: (1) embedded evaluators — frontier AI companies commit to giving internal access to teams of embedded third-party evaluators who verify adherence to safety practices, report incidents and assess alignment; (2) a democratic coalition of frontier labs within democratic countries that coordinates safety standards and limits the rate of unchecked AI progress; (3) global coordination between the US and other democratic governments to attempt to verify compliance with authoritarian governments. Amodei says Anthropic has unilaterally committed to the framework and calls on other labs and governments to do the same. Amodei is explicit that this is NOT a pause: 'pacing does not mean halting model training or technical progress, but ensuring companies take adequate time to align and safeguard their models, and for third party evaluators to confirm this,' and he expects 'progress will still seem fast.' Note the reversal: Anthropic previously argued a unilateral pause by one lab achieves little because 'it would change who the front-runner is, but it would not create the wider deliberative process that is currently missing.'
