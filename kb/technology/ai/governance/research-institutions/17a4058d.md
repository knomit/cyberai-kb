# Google DeepMind launched the DeepMind Institute to study AGI implications

Google DeepMind launched the DeepMind Institute to study the technical and societal implications of AGI across safety, governance, institutions, and human values. It is led by Demis Hassabis, James Manyika, and Shane Legg, and will convene interdisciplinary researchers from both inside and outside Google. The 'inside and outside Google' scope is the distinguishing feature against a purely internal research team, but the Institute remains a Google DeepMind body — it is not an independent third party, and should not be cited as external oversight.
