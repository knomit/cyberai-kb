# Altman backs federal frontier AI safety rules and says OpenAI runs safety cases before major RL runs

Sam Altman stated support for consistent federal safety requirements for frontier AI while urging labs to act before legislation arrives. He said OpenAI now uses safety cases before major reinforcement learning runs, and argued that capability growth should be deliberately paced alongside alignment and monitoring. The operational claim — safety cases gating large RL runs — is the substantive part; the regulatory endorsement is a stated position.
