# DeepSeek V4-Pro and V4-Flash previews (27 April 2026): 1.6T/49B MoE at $3.48/M tokens, with a hybrid attention scheme using 27% of the compute and 10% of the KV memory of V3.2 at 1M tokens

MERGED from the release record and a separate architecture record describing the same preview's attention scheme. Apart, the release record named the mechanism ('hybrid attention combining CSA and HCA') without quantifying it, and the architecture record quantified it without saying which model shipped it.

## THE RELEASE
DeepSeek released previews of DEEPSEEK-V4-PRO and DEEPSEEK-V4-FLASH on 27 APRIL 2026.
- V4-PRO: 1.6 TRILLION total / 49 BILLION ACTIVE parameters; 1 MILLION TOKEN context window; reported to outperform all current open models in reasoning, math, STEM and coding, and to be competitive with GPT-5.4-High and Claude-Opus-4.6-Max.
- V4-FLASH: 284 BILLION total / 13 BILLION ACTIVE parameters; the smaller, faster, cheaper variant.

## THE ATTENTION SCHEME, NAMED AND QUANTIFIED
The new hybrid attention combines COMPRESSED SPARSE ATTENTION (CSA) and HEAVILY COMPRESSED ATTENTION (HCA) for better computational efficiency. Mechanically it ALTERNATES BETWEEN TWO KINDS OF ATTENTION LAYERS: both compress the keys and values the model stores while reading input, but one additionally ATTENDS ONLY TO A SELECTED SUBSET OF TOKENS.
THE MEASURED EFFECT, at 1 MILLION TOKENS OF INPUT: the model uses only 27 PERCENT OF THE COMPUTATION and 10 PERCENT OF THE MEMORY FOR STORED KEYS AND VALUES that DeepSeek-V3.2 required.

## TRAINING
The V4 preview was PRETRAINED ON MORE THAN 32 TRILLION TOKENS, then FINE-TUNED AS 10 SEPARATE DOMAIN SPECIALISTS and MERGED BACK INTO ONE MODEL VIA ON-POLICY DISTILLATION. (The same recipe is recorded for the later V4-Flash-0731 checkpoint at kb/technology/ai/models/llms/deepseek/d631a798.md.)

## PRICING AND POSITIONING
V4-Pro at $3.48 per million tokens, against $25/M for Claude Opus 4.6 and $15/M for GPT-5.4. Despite lower compute cost it achieves comparable benchmark scores. Available at deepseek.com (Expert Mode, Instant Mode) and via API. This continues DeepSeek's philosophy of frontier performance at low cost, putting price pressure on American labs.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN
- These are PREVIEWS, later superseded: see kb/technology/ai/models/deepseek/016631b4.md for the 13 August 2026 V4-Pro-0813 general release (and the load-bearing finding that its Terminal-Bench 2.1 score moves by up to 33 points depending on harness), and kb/technology/ai/models/efficient-architectures/d33fd9a9.md for the September 2026 V4.1-Flash architecture change.
- The 27%/10% efficiency figures are stated AT 1M TOKENS OF INPUT and RELATIVE TO V3.2 — they are not general efficiency claims and not a comparison to any other vendor.
- The capability comparisons against GPT-5.4-High and Claude-Opus-4.6-Max are as-reported, not independently administered, and benchmark placements at this cadence go stale within weeks.
