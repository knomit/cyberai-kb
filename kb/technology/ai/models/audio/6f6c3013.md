# GPT-Realtime-2: Configurable Reasoning Speech-to-Speech Model with 5 Effort Levels (May 2026)

OpenAI introduced three audio models in its Realtime API in May 2026:

GPT-Realtime-2: End-to-end speech-to-speech model with configurable reasoning effort. Key specs:
- Context: 128K token input, 32K output
- Time to first audio: 1.12s (minimal reasoning) to 2.33s (high reasoning)
- 5 reasoning levels: minimal, low (default), medium, high, xhigh
- Supports: parallel tool calls, tool call narration, optional preambles, tone control, function calling, graceful failure handling
- Knowledge cutoff: September 30, 2024
- Pricing: $32/$0.40/$64 per million input/cached/output audio tokens; $4/$0.40/$24 per million text tokens

GPT-Realtime-Translate: Speech-to-speech translation, 70+ input languages, 13 output languages. $0.034/minute.
GPT-Realtime-Whisper: Speech-to-text. $0.017/minute.

Benchmark performance of GPT-Realtime-2:
- Scale AI Audio MultiChallenge Audio Output: #1 at xhigh reasoning (48.45% avg pass rate, vs. GPT-Realtime-1.5 at 34.73%)
- Artificial Analysis Conversational Dynamics: #1 at minimal reasoning (96.1%)
- Artificial Analysis Big Bench Audio at high reasoning: tied with Gemini 3.1 Flash Live Preview (96.6%), behind Step-Audio R1.1 Realtime (97.6%) and Grok Voice Think Fast 1.0 (97.1%)
- τ-Voice agentic tasks: led airline domain (63%), but 3rd overall (39.8%) behind Grok Voice Think Fast 1.0 (52.1%)

Key trade-off: Step-Audio R1.1 Realtime (1.51s) and Grok Voice Think Fast 1.0 (1.25s) are faster despite similar accuracy. Real-time interaction typically requires <500ms latency, which even minimal reasoning GPT-Realtime-2 at 1.12s doesn't achieve. Overall pass rate below 50% even at xhigh reasoning indicates reliable multi-turn spoken dialogue remains unsolved.
