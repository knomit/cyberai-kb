# Anonymous model Ox Alpha processed 26 trillion tokens on OpenCode in four days

OpenCode users processed 26 trillion tokens through Ox Alpha during the anonymous model's first four days, setting an OpenRouter launch record. The model recorded 327,000 unique users and 8,328,244 completed sessions. It is available for free through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, making it trivial to substitute into existing developer workflows. OpenCode's model page does not disclose Ox Alpha's maker, release date, knowledge-cutoff date, or output-limit metadata.
