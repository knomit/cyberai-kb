# Krea 3 image editing model and FLUX 3 Image Klein released

Krea 3 is an image-editing model finishing edits in under eight seconds at $0.06 per image, claimed 4x faster than Nano Banana Pro, combining up to eight references. Black Forest Labs released FLUX 3 Image Klein, a 5.6B-parameter open-weight model with text-to-image, editing, native 4K output and up to 10 reference images.
