# Cohere Parse targets enterprise document parsing at ~1/7 the cost of frontier VLMs

Cohere launched Cohere Parse, a vision-language model built to convert large volumes of complex multimodal enterprise documents into machine-readable data. It runs at $1.50 per 1,000 pages versus roughly $10 per 1,000 pages for hyperscaler models, supports nine languages, and can be deployed in private cloud or on-premise with a small footprint for regulated industries. Cohere positions it as closing the gap between expensive frontier vision models and cheaper but less accurate traditional OCR, which misses tables, diagrams, and visual structure — a bottleneck as enterprises push agents and RAG into workflows.
