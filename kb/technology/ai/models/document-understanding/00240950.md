# Cohere Parse 5 is a 2.3B vision language model priced at $1.50 per 1,000 pages

On 2026-08-27 Cohere released Parse 5 (parse-v5.0), a 2.3-billion-parameter vision language model that converts enterprise documents — PDFs, slides and images — into clean, structured Markdown for downstream indexing, RAG and agentic retrieval. It detects tables and embedded images rather than flattening them the way standard OCR does. Pricing through the Cohere API is $1.50 per 1,000 pages, against roughly $10 per 1,000 pages for frontier vision models from hyperscalers; Cohere claims the best price-performance tradeoff among models it evaluated from OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, Mistral, Microsoft, Google and Amazon, while acknowledging it does not top raw accuracy benchmarks. It covers nine major world languages and can be deployed via Model Vault for single-tenant inference, or on-premise with a small footprint, which matters for regulated industries like finance and healthcare. It is also distributed through Microsoft Foundry and AWS SageMaker. Note the positioning claim is about cost per page at acceptable quality, NOT about being the most accurate document parser available.
