# Google launches Gemini Distillation Service

Google's Gemini Distillation Service lets users train a smaller, more efficient 'student' model using the outputs and reasoning patterns of a larger 'teacher' model, enabling production-grade efficiency with deeper reasoning. It is recommended for high-volume, latency-sensitive applications and complex reasoning tasks. It currently supports gemini-3.1-pro as the teacher model and gemini-2.5-flash as the student model.
