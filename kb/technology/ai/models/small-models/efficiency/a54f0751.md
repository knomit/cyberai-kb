# Pathway's BDH-CQ is a 150-million-parameter small reasoning model positioned against frontier cost curves

Pathway developed BDH-CQ, a small reasoning model with roughly 150 million parameters. It is reported to cost a fraction of OpenAI's most cost-effective model (GPT 5.6 Luna) while demanding substantially less compute and energy — cited as evidence that model-level efficiency, alongside chips, data-center architecture, and cleaner grid supply, is part of how AI's resource crunch gets addressed.
