# Thomson Reuters built a domain LLM family for $40M on Qwen base

Thomson Reuters released "Thomson," a proprietary domain-specific LLM family built on Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (397B parameters, 17B active), trained at a reported $40 million over three months using a 200-billion-token curated mid-training dataset drawn from a 19-trillion-token pool. A 35B open-weights version (Thomson-1.0-Small) ships on Hugging Face for non-commercial use. Thomson-1.0-Large narrowly outperformed GPT-5.4 and Claude Sonnet 5 on completeness and factuality for tax, legal and news content, beating both by roughly 15 points in factuality on open-web content.
