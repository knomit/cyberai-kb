# Qwen-Drive is a vision-language foundation model staged for driving

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen-Drive 1.0, a vision-language foundation model for autonomous driving. It uses a staged training strategy integrating perception, language and planning objectives, with the stated goal of gaining specialized driving competence while retaining general visual understanding and instruction-following — i.e. avoiding the catastrophic-forgetting trade-off typical of narrow fine-tunes. A GPU with 24 GB or more of memory is recommended to run it.
