# Contrastive Language Models (CLM-8B) match larger LLMs at up to 9x lower latency

Contrastive Language Models (CLMs) are a proposed new class of 'System One' model trained with a contrastive learning objective connecting states and actions, rather than next-token prediction. CLM-8B reportedly delivers performance comparable to the classification model Jev across computer-use, gaming, and tool-calling tasks while achieving up to 9x lower latency, and sets a new state-of-the-art on certain agentic coding benchmarks. It is pre-trained on 60M Nemotron Q&A pairs, mid-trained on 30M synthetic hard-negative examples, and post-trained on 1M agentic trajectories.
