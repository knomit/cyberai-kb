# Mistral Voxtral TTS: 3B-Param Open-Weights Text-to-Speech, 9 Languages, 90ms Latency

Mistral launched Voxtral TTS (March 26, 2026): first text-to-speech model from the company, following Voxtral Transcribe 2 speech-to-text. 3B parameters — 3x smaller than industry standard. 9 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese, Italian, Hindi, Arabic. Cross-language input/output. Voice adaptation from 5-second reference. Latency: 90ms from 10-second voice sample + 500 characters. On-device: fits in smartwatch, phone, or laptop. Human evaluations: Voxtral TTS beats ElevenLabs v2.5 Flash on naturalness. Available on Hugging Face (open weights) and Mistral Studio playground. Pierre Stock (VP Science, first employee hired at Mistral): model is optimal for efficiency and conversational capability. Use cases: customer support, sales/marketing, real-time translation, personal voice agents.
