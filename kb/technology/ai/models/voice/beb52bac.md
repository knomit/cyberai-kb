# Microsoft's MAI Realtime full-duplex voice model surfaced in MAI Playground

Microsoft's first native real-time voice model, MAI Realtime, surfaced as a hidden early-access entry in the company's MAI Playground. It is a bidirectional, full-duplex system that can listen and speak simultaneously rather than taking turns, with two available voices described as noticeably more natural than Copilot's current voice mode. It is expected to reach Microsoft Foundry and Copilot voice, with no announced timeline.
