# Black Forest Labs FLUX 3 Action: 7B world-action model tops RoboLab-120 at 42.9%

BFL released FLUX 3 Action, a 7B diffusion-transformer world-action model (camera feeds + text + joint positions to ~32 actions/2s plus predicted video). Ranks first on Nvidia RoboLab-120 at 42.9% over HiDream-O1-Embodied (39.9%), Atomic-WAM (39.6%), OASIS WAM (39.0%); pi0.5 ninth (28.0%), GR00T N1.6 13th (7.2%). Adapted to SO-101 arm with ~200 demos. Weights on Hugging Face under FLUX Kommunity License (commercial use limited to companies under $5M annual revenue). Needs 69GB GPU memory vs 40GB for Cosmos3-Nano-Policy. None of RoboLab's top five yet appear on RoboArena (real robots).
