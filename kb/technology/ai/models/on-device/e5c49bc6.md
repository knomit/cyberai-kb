# Liquid AI launches LFM3 on-device open-weight model family

Per TLDR AI (2026-10-28), Liquid AI launched LFM3, a family of open-weight text, vision, audio, omni and decision models on a shared tri-hybrid backbone, starting around 2B parameters. The 1.7B text model beats its 2.6B predecessor with 35% fewer parameters, fits in about 1 GB at 4-bit, and supports a 256K context window. LFM3-VL-2B adds video input and is reported to match Gemma 4 12B across 62 vision benchmarks; all models fine-tune on a single GPU. Figures are vendor-reported.
