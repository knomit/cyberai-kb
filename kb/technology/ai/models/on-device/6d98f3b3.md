# Liquid AI ships LFM2.5-2.6B on-device agentic model

Liquid AI released LFM2.5-2.6B, a 2.6-billion-parameter on-device agentic model that enables free inference, low latency, and stronger privacy by running locally on hardware such as phones or CPUs.
