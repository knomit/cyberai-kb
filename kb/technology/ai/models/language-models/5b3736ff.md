# Thinking Machines released Inkling-Small (276B MoE, 12B active)

Thinking Machines released Inkling-Small, a 276B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 12B active parameters. It retains Inkling's multimodal reasoning, variable thinking effort, and 1M-token context window while using substantially less compute. The Deep View reported it achieves performance comparable to the company's recently announced 975B-parameter model at roughly one-third the size.
