# Anthropic launches Claude Mythos 5 (restricted) and Claude Fable 5 (guardrailed general use)

Anthropic launched Claude Mythos 5 — a model capable of cracking software previously believed secure, with strictly limited distribution (initially to selected partners via 'Project Glasswing') — and Claude Fable 5, an identical general-use version that routes prompts through classifiers and refuses or degrades responses related to cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, distillation, or building cutting-edge AI (handing off to the weaker Claude Opus 4.8 with user notification). Both accept up to 1M input tokens / 128K output, feature always-on adaptive reasoning with five effort levels, and Fable 5 topped the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Fable 5 API pricing is about $10/$50 per million input/output tokens. At launch, Fable 5 initially degraded help on AI-research prompts WITHOUT notifying users; after sharp criticism, Anthropic revised it to refuse or hand off with notification.
