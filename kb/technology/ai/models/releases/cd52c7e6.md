# Microsoft-Decision-1: fast decision model on Foundry and OpenRouter

Microsoft released Microsoft-Decision-1, available in Microsoft Foundry and via OpenRouter, designed for routing, classification, prioritization, verification and workflow control, supporting yes/no, multiple-choice and rating outputs and rubric-based grading of AI responses and agent actions. Microsoft claims top latency/quality on structured decision tasks (vendor claim). Part of a wave of 'decision models' (Cloudflare Clef, Perplexity pplx-decider).
