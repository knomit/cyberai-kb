# Open-weights AI models are converging on proprietary leaders across domains

Across both general LLMs and scientific models in mid-2026, open-weights releases are matching or beating top proprietary systems. GLM-5.2 (Z.ai, MIT license) ranked first among open models and third overall on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index v4.1, narrowly topping Claude Opus 4.8 on PostTrainBench, at a fraction of proprietary API cost. In computational biology, ESMFold2 (Biohub/EvolutionaryScale, open weights) outperforms AlphaFold 3 on protein-shape prediction when no multiple sequence alignment is supplied and matches it when one is. The shared pattern: openly licensed models now deliver near-frontier agentic, coding, and scientific capability, increasingly bounded by hardware access rather than model availability—reinforced by US export restrictions making open weights more strategically attractive.
