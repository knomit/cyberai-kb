# Recursive Flint: 40B-parameter MoE (2.5B active) local agentic coding model

Recursive released Flint, a mixture-of-experts model built for fast agentic coding on local hardware: 40B total parameters, 2.5B active per token, built from scratch by a small team of people and agents in under four months. Reported speeds: over 125 tokens/s on an M4 Pro MacBook Pro and over 150 tokens/s on a 64GB NVIDIA RTX Spark at short contexts; usable as a daily local coding model without sending data to a remote server.
