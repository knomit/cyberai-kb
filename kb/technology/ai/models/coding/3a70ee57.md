# Poolside's Laguna XS 2.1 is a 33B MoE coding model scoring 63.1% on SWE-bench Multilingual

Poolside introduced Laguna XS 2.1, a 33-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model optimized for agentic coding and long-horizon tasks, reporting a 5.4-point improvement on SWE-bench Multilingual to 63.1%. It offers three quantized checkpoints, is licensed under OpenMDW-1.1, and is available on Hugging Face or via API. Reported via TLDR AI, July 3, 2026.
