# Mind Lab's Macaron-V1 uses five 1B-parameter LoRA experts on GLM-5.1 for continual learning

Mind Lab's Macaron-V1 model was built by attaching five LoRA expert modules, each about one billion parameters, to GLM-5.1; the system dynamically switches to the expert best suited to a given task, and accumulated usage data can be distilled into dedicated experts as a form of continual learning. Mind Lab claims Macaron-V1 surpasses GLM-5.2 on its benchmarks. Reported via TLDR AI / KrASIA, Aug 4, 2026.
