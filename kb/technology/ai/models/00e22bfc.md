# Chinese labs are competing on price, efficiency, and open weights across model categories (Aug 2026)

As of early August 2026, Chinese AI labs are pushing frontier-competitive models that differentiate on low cost, efficiency, and open weights rather than only raw benchmark leadership. Concrete examples surfaced in newsletters: Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max (2.4T params) priced at $2 input / $6 output per million tokens (vs. Fable $10/$50) with open weights planned, placing 4th on Code Arena front-end; MiniMax H3 became the first open model to top an Artificial Analysis AI video ranking; DeepSeek's V4-Flash was reported as the cheapest well-known model to run (~105x cheaper than Claude Fable 5); and Mind Lab's Macaron-V1 (five ~1B LoRA experts on GLM-5.1) claims to surpass GLM-5.2 via continual learning. This does NOT mean Chinese models lead US frontier labs on all benchmarks — the pattern is competition on token economics, open availability, and specialized capability. Sourced from TLDR AI and The Deep View, Aug 2026.
