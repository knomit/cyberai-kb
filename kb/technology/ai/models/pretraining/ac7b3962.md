# Microsoft MAI-Base-2: 2.8T-param MoE with 80B active

Microsoft AI detailed training of MAI-Base-2, a 2.8T-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 80B active parameters that it says surpassed every open-weight model on its held-out pre-training evaluations. The deep dive covers data strategy, compute efficiency and infrastructure, and plans to offer internally trained frontier models to enterprises via Foundry.

Added via The Deep View (2026-10-19): it beat trillion-plus-parameter models from Alibaba, DeepSeek, Moonshot and Xiaomi on held-out evals (not compared with OpenAI/Anthropic/Google models) and is claimed 4-12x more pre-training-FLOPs-efficient than DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base. Microsoft says pre-training uses organic data without distillation from other models. Business Insider reports Mustafa Suleyman told staff an unnamed in-house base model beat OpenAI's GPT 5.6 Sol in private testing using one-quarter of the compute. All claims are Microsoft's own.
