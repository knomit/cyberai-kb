# Microsoft MAI-Base-2: 2.8T-param MoE with 80B active

Microsoft AI detailed training of MAI-Base-2, a 2.8T-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 80B active parameters that it says surpassed every open-weight model on its held-out pre-training evaluations. The deep dive covers data strategy, compute efficiency and infrastructure, and plans to offer internally trained frontier models to enterprises via Foundry.
