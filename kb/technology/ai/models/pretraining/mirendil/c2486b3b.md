# Mirendil-Base: one researcher pretrained a 100B-500B LLM in 3.5 months for under $10M compute

Import AI 478: AI R&D startup Mirendil says Mirendil-Base was built by one researcher in 3.5 months with under $10M compute, from scratch on PyTorch/JAX using only public data. Tiers: Base 100B-500B, Large 500B-2T (~80% trained), XL 2T-10T (~19% trained). Base beats Nemotron-550B-Base, GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base but trails Kimi-K3; Large and XL exceed K3. Mirendil says it is not yet at fully autonomous recursive self-improvement. Company claims, not independently verified.
