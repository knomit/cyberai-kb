# Odyssey-3 is an autoregressive diffusion transformer world model for physical and virtual agents

Odyssey announced Odyssey-3, a foundation world model built on an autoregressive diffusion transformer trained to simulate highly diverse scenarios. Odyssey says the model can control robots, power humanoids, drive vehicles, pilot drones, train other AIs and play video games, and that it has a learned understanding of physics, dynamics, cause-and-effect and human behaviors. The stated goal is physical agents that interface natively with both physical and virtual systems.
