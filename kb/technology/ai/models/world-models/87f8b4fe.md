# World Labs released Atlas, a natively multimodal world model for spatial intelligence

World Labs published Atlas, a world-generation model pretrained from scratch to operate natively on text, images, video and 3D. It fuses all inputs into a shared spatial context and generates what comes next from that context. World Labs reports performance improves with increased training compute, and that the model handles world generation, reconstruction and simulation tasks.
