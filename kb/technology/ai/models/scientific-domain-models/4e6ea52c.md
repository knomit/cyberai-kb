# Periodic Neon beats GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 on FrontierXRD scientific analysis at lower cost

Periodic announced Neon, a model for scientific analysis that outperforms GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 on FrontierXRD, a highly challenging evaluation for scientific analysis, at a lower cost per analysis. Periodic says Neon establishes a Pareto-optimal cost-performance frontier through midtraining and reinforcement learning on laboratory data, and that Neon is already deployed in labs analyzing experiments aimed at better superconductors and magnets. Periodic frames the approach as 'nature is our learning environment' — training signal drawn from real experimental outcomes rather than synthetic or text corpora.
