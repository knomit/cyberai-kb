# GLM-5.3-Flash architecture: hybrid attention and Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections

Z.ai says GLM-5.3-Flash is architected for 'extreme efficiency' and designed for ultra-low-cost inference. It uses a hybrid attention architecture to reduce the cost of serving long-context queries without sacrificing accuracy, and introduces 'Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections,' a technique that improves how information moves between layers of the network to improve scaling efficiency. Z.ai says it is scaling the same recipe to larger frontier models.
