# Qwen4 architecture previewed in Qwen3.8-Flash: 6B active of 125B parameters

Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Flash release previews the Qwen4 architecture, activating roughly 6 billion of 125 billion parameters. Rather than adding more experts, the design attaches about 51 billion parameters as a separate embedding table indexed by two- and three-character text fragments.
