# Mid-2026 AI competition centers on cost-efficiency, not raw capability

Multiple mid-2026 developments (per The Batch issue-365) point to intelligence-per-dollar rather than peak capability as the axis of frontier LLM competition. DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, a small MoE model (13B active/284B total), beat its own larger V4-Pro on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (50 vs 44) at ~$0.03/task purely via re-fine-tuning, and its 3-bit quant runs in 110GB memory. In the same window OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna's price 80% (to $0.20/$1.20 per 1M tokens) and Terra 20%, citing efficiency gains; Google's Gemini 3.6 Flash held 50 Intelligence Index points while cutting cost/task from $0.59 to $0.50; and Thinking Machines' Inkling Small matched ~40 points with <1/3 of its flagship's parameters. NOTE: benchmark parity claims are scoped to Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index at stated reasoning levels; absolute capability leadership still sat with larger models (Kimi K3 at 57). This is a pattern across vendors, not a single result.
