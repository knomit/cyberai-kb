# DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 surpasses DeepSeek-V4-Pro via re-fine-tuning

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 (Jul 2026), an official version of the smaller 'Flash' model in its V4 family that, on an unchanged architecture, was lifted past the larger DeepSeek-V4-Pro purely through a new round of fine-tuning. Architecture: mixture-of-experts transformer, 284B total parameters, 13B active per token (304B with the DSpark speculative-decoding module). Handles up to 1M input tokens and 384K output tokens (~122.7 tok/s). Pretrained on >32 trillion tokens, then fine-tuned by building per-domain specialists (SFT + Group Relative Policy Optimization) and merging them via on-policy distillation. On Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index at max reasoning it scored 50 (up from the April preview's 40 and V4-Pro's 44), tying Gemini 3.6 Flash and just behind GPT-5.6 Luna and GLM-5.2 (51); open-weights leader Kimi K3 scored 57. Cost per Intelligence Index task at max reasoning was ~$0.03. Terminal-Bench 2.1: 82.7% (up ~21 points from preview). Weights are free under MIT license; API price $0.14/$0.0028/$0.28 per million input/cached/output tokens. A 3-bit quantized version runs on a 110GB-memory machine.
