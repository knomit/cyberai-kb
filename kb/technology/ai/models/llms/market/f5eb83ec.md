# Frontier LLM providers cut prices and shipped efficiency updates in late July / early Aug 2026

In a crowded stretch of releases: OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna's price by 80% to $0.20/$1.20 per 1M input/output tokens and GPT-5.6 Terra by 20% to $2/$12, attributing the cuts to efficiency gains (including production inference code optimized by GPT-5.6 Sol). Google introduced Gemini 3.6 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite aimed at speed/cost; Gemini 3.6 Flash held 50 points on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index while its cost per task fell from $0.59 to $0.50, and Artificial Analysis measured both completing tasks in about half the time of predecessors. Thinking Machines' Inkling Small reached 40 on the Intelligence Index with less than a third of its flagship Inkling model's parameters.
