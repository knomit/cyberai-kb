# Late July / early Aug 2026: OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna 80% and Terra 20% citing efficiency gains, amid a wider frontier round of cost-and-speed updates

OPENAI'S CUTS. OpenAI reduced GPT-5.6 Luna (lighter-weight) pricing by 80%, to $0.20 per 1M input tokens and $1.20 per 1M output tokens, and GPT-5.6 Terra (midweight) by 20%, to $2 / $12 per 1M input/output. It attributed the cuts to efficiency gains — including production inference code optimized by GPT-5.6 Sol itself. Sol's API speed was improved in the same round. The changes extended across API usage, Codex, and ChatGPT Work subscriptions.

THE WIDER ROUND. The cuts landed in a crowded stretch of competing cost/speed releases:
- Google introduced Gemini 3.6 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite aimed at speed and cost. Gemini 3.6 Flash held 50 points on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index while its cost per task fell from $0.59 to $0.50, and Artificial Analysis measured both models completing tasks in about half the time of their predecessors.
- Thinking Machines' Inkling Small reached 40 on the Intelligence Index with less than a third of the parameters of its flagship Inkling model.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the efficiency attribution is OpenAI's own stated reason, not an independently verified cost analysis, and the benchmark and cost-per-task figures are as measured/reported by Artificial Analysis and the vendors rather than audited here. This is one reporting window (late July / early August 2026), not a durable price level — a separate, explicitly temporary three-month cut of more than 20% applied to GPT-5.6 Sol and is not part of this round.
