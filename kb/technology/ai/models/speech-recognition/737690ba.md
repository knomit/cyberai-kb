# Google, Meta and Microsoft all shipped speech-to-text models in a 10-day window, all under 4% word error rate

Three speech-to-text releases landed between 2026-08-26 and 2026-09-03, reported together in The Batch 2026-09-11. All three score under 4% on Artificial Analysis' word error rate benchmark, and all three do speaker diarization.

Google Gemini 3.5 Transcribe (released 2026-08-26, via the Gemini API): aimed at producing a clean transcript usable in wider AI workflows. Transcribes over 85 languages, removes filler words such as "uh" and "um", gives word-level timestamps, and detects up to 8 speakers. Independently measured at about 2.6% WER non-streaming and 4.0% streaming. Priced per token for audio in and text out, averaging roughly $0.005/minute (~$0.30/hour) for pre-recorded files and $0.009/minute (~$0.54/hour) for real-time streaming.

Meta Muse Voice Transcribe (released 2026-09-01, from Meta Superintelligence Labs, via the Meta Model API, Meta AI for Mac and Muse Code): designed as a real-time listening layer — transcribe, separate speakers, and detect when a talker starts and stops. Handles over an hour of audio, distinguishes 20+ speakers, natively supports code-switching both across and within sentences, and uses contextual bias to improve accuracy. Trained on 70+ languages with 25+ "extensively verified". 3.1% WER on Artificial Analysis' streaming test — lowest among streaming models — ahead of Gemini 3.5 Transcribe at 4.0%. $0.18 per hour of audio ($3 per 1,000 minutes).

Microsoft MAI-Transcribe-2 (released 2026-09-03, public preview via Azure Speech): a batch engine that does not chase live latency. 60 languages, speaker diarization, word-level timestamps, automatic language identification, strong noisy-environment performance. Lowest WER among non-streaming models, with Microsoft reporting it beat Whisper-Large-V3, GPT-Transcribe, ScribeV2 and Gemini 3.5 Transcribe on FLEURS across 60 languages. $0.10 per hour of audio through the end of 2026.

Foreseeable misreading: the "lowest WER" claims are not in competition with each other — Muse leads the streaming category and MAI-Transcribe-2 leads non-streaming, and those are different products solving different problems. Compare within the streaming/batch category you actually need, and note the ~5.4x price spread ($0.10 to $0.54 per hour) across roughly comparable accuracy.
