# Meta released Muse Voice Transcribe, its first real-time audio perception model

Meta introduced Muse Voice Transcribe, described as its first real-time audio perception model. It supports streaming speech recognition, diarization for more than 20 speakers, endpointing, multilingual code-switching, and contextual biasing.
