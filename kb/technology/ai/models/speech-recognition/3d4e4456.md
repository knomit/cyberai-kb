# Microsoft's MAI-Transcribe-2 prices ASR at 10 cents per audio hour

Microsoft released MAI-Transcribe-2 on 2026-09-03, a speech recognition model with speaker diarization, configurable transcription styles and word-level timestamps, covering 60 languages at 10 cents per hour of audio. Microsoft claims it beats Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, GPT-Transcribe and Whisper V3-Large on accuracy while being faster and cheaper than competing offerings from OpenAI, Google and ElevenLabs. All comparative claims are Microsoft-reported. The strategic read is that Microsoft is building frontier-class models one modality at a time and swapping them into products that previously ran on OpenAI technology.
