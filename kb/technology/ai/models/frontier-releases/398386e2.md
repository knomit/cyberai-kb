# OpenAI GPT-6 Astra launched at $10/$50 per million tokens

On Thursday 2026-09-03, OpenAI unveiled GPT-6 Astra, which it characterizes as a 'generational leap in capability.' Rollout began with a limited set of organizations including those in OpenAI's Daybreak Access program, then to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, API and AWS customers 'over the coming days.' API pricing is $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens — identical to Anthropic's Mythos/Fable 5 and 5.1, placing Astra among the most expensive models on the market. OpenAI argues the sticker price is misleading because Astra uses 'substantially fewer total tokens per task' in multiple scenarios; that efficiency claim is OpenAI's own and was not independently benchmarked at launch. OpenAI claims Astra is 'the world's best computer use model,' its best model for professional work, scientific discovery, mathematics and health research, and 'the best model for software engineering to date,' outranking GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Fable 5.1 on DeepSWE v1.1. OpenAI President Greg Brockman told press 'it's not unreasonable to feel that we are now in the AGI era,' while framing it as a continuum rather than a threshold crossing.
