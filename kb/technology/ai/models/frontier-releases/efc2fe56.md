# Anonymous 'Ox Alpha' stealth model launched on OpenRouter with 1.05M context and free preview

On Thursday (week of 2026-08-17) an anonymous third-party provider released a stealth reasoning model called Ox Alpha on OpenRouter, aimed at coding, sustained agentic work, and production workloads. Reported specifications: a 1.05-million-token context window and a 131,000-token maximum output, offered free in preview for a week with 'near unlimited usage' and a stated capacity of 100 trillion tokens per day. Prompts and outputs are retained by the provider but stated as not currently used for training. Early third-party reports claimed it beat frontier models including Anthropic's Fable 5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol on coding evaluations such as Deep SWE, though no independent validation on formal public leaderboards was available. Stripe CEO Patrick Collison — Stripe is acquiring OpenRouter — called it 'very impressive'. Speculation about its origin included Google Gemini, Microsoft MAI, and Z.ai's GLM family; none confirmed.
