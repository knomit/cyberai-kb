# Gemini 3.7 Flash measured 330 output tokens/second with 13.2s time to first token

Google shipped Gemini 3.7 Flash in August 2026 without disclosing throughput or latency figures, but Artificial Analysis measured 330 output tokens per second — behind only Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite — and a 13.2-second wait to the first token. Its Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index average is 56, up from 52 for its predecessor.
