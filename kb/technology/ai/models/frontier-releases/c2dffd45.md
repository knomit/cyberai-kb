# Google Gemini 3.8 Flash launched at $0.75/$3.75 per million tokens

On Wednesday 2026-09-02, Google unveiled Gemini 3.8 Flash, the third Flash-series release in six weeks, with introductory API pricing of $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens — the same speed and cost as its predecessor Gemini 3.7 Flash. Google describes it as its 'most intelligent workhorse model' with gains in software engineering, agentic tasks and multi-step reasoning; this is Google's own framing, and it used the identical 'most intelligent workhorse model' label for 3.7 Flash weeks earlier, so the phrase is marketing continuity rather than an independent capability measurement. On Google's own published eval table, Gemini 3.8 Flash still trails Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 on knowledge work, long-horizon software engineering, general agentic tasks and computer use, while beating Opus 5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol on domain-specific legal, finance and biology tasks and on agentic terminal coding. The price gap is the clearest edge: Opus 5 lists at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens and GPT-5.6 Sol at $4/$20, making rivals roughly 4x–5x more expensive per token. This does not mean Gemini 3.8 Flash is the strongest model overall — Google has not shipped a heavyweight frontier model since Gemini 3.1 Pro in February 2026.
