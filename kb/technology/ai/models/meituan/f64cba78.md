# Meituan launches LongCat-2.0 1.6T-parameter MoE model

Meituan launched LongCat-2.0, a 1.6 trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model tailored for agentic coding, multi-step workflows and long-context processing. The model was revealed as the engine behind 'Owl Alpha,' a stealth model on OpenRouter that had ranked in the top three by global daily volume.
