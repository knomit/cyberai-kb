# Kimi K3: 2.8T-parameter multimodal model with 1M-token context, open weights July 27

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 features 2.8 trillion parameters, a one-million-token context window, and a multimodal architecture handling text, images, and video in one model. Full open weights are scheduled for July 27, 2026; until then it is usable via Moonshot's API. Kimi K3 is triggering claims it has overtaken leading Anthropic and OpenAI models, but The Deep View notes the lead is in only one benchmark and the model could largely be distilled from US frontier models rather than a research breakthrough. Moonshot itself said K3 sat just below Fable 5 in coding, agents, and frontier software-engineering benchmarks, outranking Anthropic's model only in codebase cleaning and long-horizon engineering.
