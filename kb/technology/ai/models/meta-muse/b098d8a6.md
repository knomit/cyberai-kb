# Meta releases Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full frontier models

On 2026-10-12/13 Meta unveiled Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, its first frontier-class models (successor to Muse Spark), combining 'active memory' (retaining user context across conversations) with 'active focus' (working through hard goals). Muse Wonder will power Meta's Muse agent, rolling out first to subscribers. Muse Wonder Full reportedly beats Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra and Gemini 4 Argon on the Artificial Analysis index but still trails Claude Opus 5.5 on it. Claims are Meta's own and per newsletter reports.
