# Muse Spark Open license terms, local-run footprint and benchmark standing

Per The Batch (2026-10-23): Muse Spark Open weights are under the Muse Model license: commercial and noncommercial use allowed up to $20M revenue, 'Built with Muse Spark Open' display required, separate licenses for others; Wonder models are proprietary with undisclosed architecture and training data. Needs ~1 TB memory at 4-bit (two 512GB Mac Studios). Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index: Wonder Full 56, Wonder 52, Spark Open 39 (prior Muse Spark 1.3: 48); Spark Open outranks Mistral Large 4 and Reflection Beam but trails MiMo-V2.6-Pro (46) and GLM-5.3 (45). Muse Wonder costs $4.52 per index task (fifth highest). Beam, Mistral Large 4 and Nemotron 3.5 Super VL have more permissive licenses.
