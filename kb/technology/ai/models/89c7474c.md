# Diverging open-weights strategies in 2026: Chinese GLM line sustains open-tier leadership as Meta pivots closed

Across early-to-mid 2026 the open-weights landscape bifurcated. Z.ai's GLM line continued iterating as the open-tier frontrunner: GLM-5.1 (April 2026) led SWE-Bench Pro at 58.4% as an open-weights model built for 8-hour autonomous sessions, and successor GLM-5.2 ranked first among open models (third overall) on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index v4.1, trailing only Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 while undercutting them sharply on price (~$1.40/M input tokens). Simultaneously Meta abandoned its role as the leading US open-weights champion, releasing the closed/proprietary Muse Spark (April 2026). The combined pattern: open-weight leadership at the agentic/coding frontier is increasingly carried by Chinese labs (Z.ai/Zhipu) and reinforced by US export restrictions, even as a major US incumbent retreats to proprietary models.
