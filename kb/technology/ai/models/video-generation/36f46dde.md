# Google launches Veo 3.1 Lite: video generation at $0.05/second

Google DeepMind released Veo 3.1 Lite in late March 2026, a lighter, lower-cost version of Veo 3 optimized for high-volume use cases. Pricing: $0.05 per second of generated video (vs Veo 3 at ~$0.35/sec). Max resolution 720p vs Veo 3's 1080p. Latency ~40% lower. Available via Google Cloud Vertex AI. Targets social media content creators and marketing automation pipelines.
