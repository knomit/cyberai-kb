# Google shipped Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash with scene extension and 4K upscaling

Google introduced Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, adding creative controls for extending scenes, interpolating first and last frames, 4K upscaling, and faster video iteration through the Gemini API.
