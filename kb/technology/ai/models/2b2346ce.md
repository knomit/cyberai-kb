# 2026 open-weights agentic race: Chinese GLM line leads, Nvidia counters for US, on a stable ~$1.40/M cost floor

By mid-2026 the open-weights tier had become genuinely competitive at frontier agentic coding, contested mainly between Chinese and US labs. Z.ai's (Zhipu AI) GLM line set the open-tier pace: GLM-5.1 (754B MoE, 40B active, MIT license) led SWE-Bench Pro at 58.4% and was built for 8-hour autonomous sessions; GLM-5.2 extended this to 1M-token context and ranked first among open models on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index v4.1, drawing strong community praise as a 'step change' for open-source agents. On the US side, Nvidia's Nemotron 3 Super 120B (hybrid Mamba-2/MoE, first US open-weights leader since Llama 4) competed on speed (442 t/s) backed by a planned $26B/5-yr open-weights commitment explicitly aimed at keeping developers in the Nvidia ecosystem against Chinese models. Notably, Z.ai held API pricing constant at $1.40/$0.26/$4.40 per M tokens across GLM-5.1 and 5.2, signaling a stabilizing open-weights cost floor far below proprietary leaders.
