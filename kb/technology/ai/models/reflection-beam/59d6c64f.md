# Reflection Beam training recipe and speed/efficiency measurements

Beam (501B MoE, 23B active, Apache 2.0, price $0.70/$2.10 per 1M tokens, knowledge cutoff June 30 2026): pretrained on 23.8T tokens with 6,144 GPUs in under four weeks (filters discarded ~95% of raw web text); RL on ~1M mostly synthetic environments with 10,500 GPUs for four weeks (>100M attempts, asynchronous with some attempts from >1-day-old model versions); RL copy and behavior-guideline copy merged via multi-teacher on-policy distillation. Artificial Analysis: Index 30, 228 tokens/s, 10.1 s latency, 1.4 min per index task (fastest measured), ~26K output tokens per task (fewest among open weights). Reflection claims ~1/4-1/3 of GLM-5.2 reasoning compute (active params x tokens approximation, excludes prompt reading and serving cost).
