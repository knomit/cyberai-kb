# xAI releases Grok 4.6 focused on long-running agent tasks

xAI released Grok 4.6, focused on long-running agent tasks and reportedly matching GPT-5.6 (Sol) on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. It emphasizes turning product ideas into working versions and capabilities like vulnerability patching and AI research. Grok 4.6 is available in Cursor, Grok Build, and via API, with 2x included usage for the first week.
