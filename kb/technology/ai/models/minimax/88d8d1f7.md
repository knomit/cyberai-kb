# MiniMax H3 is first open model to top an AI video ranking

China's MiniMax H3 became the first open model to top an Artificial Analysis AI video ranking, placing first in Video Editing, second in Text-to-Video, and third in Image-to-Video. Reported via TLDR AI / The Decoder, Aug 2026.
