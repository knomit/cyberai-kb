# NextLM's 30B Savant 3.5 beat frontier models on a niche sales-prospecting task at ~1/100th the cost

Marketing and behavioral insights firm NextLM published research on Savant 3.5, a system built on open-source NVIDIA Nemotron models and purpose-built to help salespeople identify the best customers. Savant sits at roughly 30 billion parameters with 3 billion active per input, using Nemotron 3.5 Lightning as a supporting signal to sharpen rankings. On the key metric — share of eventual customers appearing in the model's top 10% of recommendations — Savant placed 24.6% of buyers in the top decile, versus 22.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol, 22.3% for Grok 4.5, 18.1% for Anthropic's Fable 5, and 14.6% for Opus 5 (last). Cost was $0.003–$0.011 per 1,000 customer prospects scored versus $0.26–$5.11 for the frontier-model APIs tested. NextLM founder/CEO Chris Anzalone framed the result as evidence that task-tuned small models can beat the frontier in narrow domains, while acknowledging general models retain advantages in breadth and ease of use.
