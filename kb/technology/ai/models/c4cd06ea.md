# Mid-2026 AI model competition centered on low cost-per-task and open-weights parity

As of late July 2026, frontier AI competition had shifted decisively toward low cost-per-task and open-weights parity. Moonshot's Kimi K3 (2.8T-param MoE, weights promised) ranked first among open models and third overall on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index while undercutting top proprietary models on price, and Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 explicitly used aggressive pricing ($0.26/task; Zuckerberg framing it as an attack on rivals' high margins, subsidized by ad revenue) to compete. Both models, like many launches this cycle, were trained specifically for agentic tool use.
