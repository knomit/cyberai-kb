# Claude Opus 5.5 released Sept 22, 2026: pricing, speed, and safeguard routing

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 on Tuesday Sept 22, 2026. Pricing: $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens (a 20% per-token decrease vs Opus 5); cache reads $0.20 per million tokens (60% less than Opus 5); cache writes $5/million; batch processing $2/$10 per million input/output tokens; Zero Data Retention available. Anthropic says it costs 40% less than Opus 5 on typical workloads (the 40% figure is per-workload, not per-token), generates output more than 30% faster than Opus 5, and matches Claude Fable 5.1 on most work. Input up to 1M tokens (text and images), output up to 128K tokens (300K in Batch API); five reasoning effort levels (low, medium, high, xhigh, max; defaults to high); knowledge cutoff June 2026, identical to Claude Fable/Mythos 5.1's. Safeguard design: most cybersecurity tasks are rerouted to Opus 4.8 and requests flagged by biology classifiers are routed to Opus 5; only organizations vetted through Anthropic's Life Sciences and Cyber verification programs can use Opus 5.5 itself for those tasks. Anthropic called it its strongest model on behavioral alignment (strongest automated behavioral audit result to date), reporting it passed Anthropic's internal quality threshold on knowledge-work tasks (business reports) on 16 of 18 attempts, versus 0 of 18 for both Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Opus 5. 5.5 versions of Sonnet and Haiku were promised 'in the coming weeks' -- the first Haiku-class update since version 4.5 in October 2025. Five-hour usage limits raised on Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans.

INDEPENDENT BENCHMARKS (Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3 and Vals AI Vals Index, both published ~Sept 25 2026): both rank Claude Opus 5.5 #1 overall among all models. On Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index v4.3 (composite of 10 math/science/coding/reasoning evals), Opus 5.5 at max reasoning with fallback scored a weighted 58 -- 7 points above Claude Opus 5 and 5 points above Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra -- topping 6 of 10 component evals (incl. Humanity's Last Exam 61.4%, SciCode 66.9%) and tying a 7th (Terminal-Bench 4.0, 59.6%). Despite lower per-token cost, its cost per benchmark task remains relatively high at $5.98 (vs $7.63 for Claude Fable 5.1, but still below GPT-6 Astra's $3.26) because it uses more tokens per task than earlier Opus models. On Vals AI's Index, Opus 5.5 scores 69.69%, about 3 points ahead of GPT-6 Astra (counting fallbacks as failures did not meaningfully change the score); it also topped Vals' RSI Index, MedScribe, ProofBench v1.1 (a perfect score), VibeCodeBench1-100, ProgramBench, and Terminal-Bench 4.0. Opus 5.5 arrived the same day as OpenAI's GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, both of which it outperforms.
