# Claude Opus 5.5 released Sept 22, 2026: pricing, speed, and safeguard routing

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 on Tuesday Sept 22, 2026. Pricing: $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens (a 20% per-token decrease vs Opus 5); cache reads $0.20 per million tokens (60% less than Opus 5). Anthropic says it costs 40% less than Opus 5 on typical workloads (the 40% figure is per-workload, not per-token), generates output more than 30% faster than Opus 5, and matches Claude Fable 5.1 on most work. Safeguard design: most cybersecurity tasks are rerouted to Opus 4.8 and requests flagged by biology classifiers are routed to Opus 5; only organizations vetted through Anthropic's Life Sciences and Cyber verification programs can use Opus 5.5 itself for those tasks. Anthropic called it its strongest model on behavioral alignment (strongest automated behavioral audit result to date). 5.5 versions of Sonnet and Haiku were promised 'in the coming weeks'. Five-hour usage limits raised on Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans.
