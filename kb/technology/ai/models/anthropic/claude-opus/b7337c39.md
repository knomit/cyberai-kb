# Claude Sonnet 5.5 released: 30% faster than Sonnet 5, same token pricing

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5 on 2026-09-28 (Monday). It runs about 30% faster than Sonnet 5 and uses fewer tokens per task, cutting cost by up to 30% for most tasks despite identical per-token pricing ($2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output, $0.20 per million cache reads). It is a lower-cost complement to Opus 5.5 ($4/$20) and sits close to Opus 5.5 on most benchmarks; it beat both Sonnet 5 and Opus 5.5 on Terminal-Bench 4.0. It is the first Sonnet with the same cyber safeguards as Anthropic's most capable models, and launches with classifiers against reasoning extraction (distillation). A Haiku 5.5 is planned in coming weeks. Not implied: the lower per-task cost applies to 'most tasks', not all.
