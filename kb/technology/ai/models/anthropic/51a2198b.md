# Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 with 20% price cut

On 2026-11-09 Anthropic released Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, claimed to generate tokens 30% faster than 5.1 and priced 20% lower: $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens (down from $10/$50), matching GPT-6 Astra; an optional fast mode generates 2.5x faster at 2x price ($16/$80). Both are the same underlying model: Fable is the public version with cybersecurity and biology safeguards; Mythos is limited to verified organizations in cybersecurity or biomedical research via the Cyber Verification Program or Life Sciences Verification Program. Fable can act as Advisor (smaller models escalate hard problems to it) or Orchestrator (delegating subtasks to smaller models). Fable 5.2 is available on AWS, Azure and Google Cloud. Not meant to imply Mythos is generally available.
