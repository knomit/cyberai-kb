# Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5 (and locked Mythos 5 cyber variant) on June 9, 2026

On June 9, 2026, Anthropic made Claude Fable 5 generally available, calling it its most capable model. Unusually, it shipped one underlying model as two products split by safety classifiers: Fable 5 for the public, and Claude Mythos 5 (same model with cyber safeguards lifted) restricted to vetted cyber defenders and critical-infrastructure operators — which Anthropic calls the strongest cybersecurity model in the world. Fable 5 routes flagged cyber/bio/chem/distillation requests to the weaker Claude Opus 4.8. Both cost $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, under half the price of the earlier Mythos Preview.
