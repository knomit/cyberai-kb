# Anthropic releases Claude Sonnet 5

On 2026-06-30/07-01 Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5, described as its 'most agentic' Claude model, narrowing the gap between efficient and frontier models with performance approaching Opus 4.8 but a 'much lower ability' at cybersecurity tasks. It became the default model for Free and Pro users. Introductory Claude Code/Platform pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, rising to $3/$15 in August 2026. Early testers included Cursor, Rakuten, Lovable and Factory.
