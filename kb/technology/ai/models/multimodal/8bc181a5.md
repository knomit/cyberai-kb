# Nvidia Nemoton 3 Nano Omni: open multimodal omni model for agentic workflows

Nvidia launched Nemoton 3 Nano Omni, an open multimodal model combining vision, speech, and language in a single model to enable agents to skip handoffs between separate models. Architecture: 30B-A3B hybrid mixture-of-experts (same as Nemotron 3 Nano) with added audio and vision encoders. Claims 9x higher throughput than other open omni models; tops 6 leaderboards across complex document intelligence, video, and audio understanding. Can work alongside proprietary cloud models or other Nvidia Nemotron open models. Use cases: computer-use agents navigating GUIs, interpreting documents/charts/screenshots/audio for customer service and workflow monitoring. Available via Hugging Face, OpenRouter, build.nvidia.com as NVIDIA NIM microservice, and through Nvidia Cloud Partners.
