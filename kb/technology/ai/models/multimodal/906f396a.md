# Mage: 4B-parameter multimodal model family (Mage-VL and Mage-Flow)

Mage is a lightweight, research-friendly multimodal model family built with a fixed 4B-parameter budget, compact enough to train, fine-tune, and deploy on modest hardware while remaining competitive with much larger open systems. Mage-VL is a codec-native streaming multimodal foundation model, and Mage-Flow is a native-resolution foundation model for image generation and editing.
