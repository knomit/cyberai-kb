# Qwen3.5: Alibaba's 8-model VLM family; 9B beats gpt-oss-120B, 397B leads vision benchmarks

Alibaba released Qwen3.5 family (March 2026): 8 open-weights vision-language models (397B-0.8B params). Architecture: mixture-of-experts or dense transformer with mixed attention + Gated DeltaNet layers. License: Apache 2.0. Features: tool use, web search, CoT reasoning in 200+ languages; 254K-1M token context; up to 64K output tokens. Key benchmark results: (1) Qwen3.5-9B outperforms gpt-oss-120B (10x bigger) on most language tasks except reasoning/coding; (2) Qwen3.5-397B-A17B outperforms GPT-5.2, Claude 4.5 Opus, and Gemini-3 Pro on 28 of 44 vision benchmarks; (3) Qwen3.5-122B-A10B (MoE activating 10B params/token) generally outperforms dense Qwen3.5-27B. Hosted versions: Qwen3.5-Plus for agentic applications. Notable: technical lead Lin Junyang resigned abruptly ("Bye my beloved qwen" post); 4 team members followed; Alibaba put project under tighter senior supervision.
