# TML-Interaction-Small: real-time multimodal model from Thinking Machines Lab

Thinking Machines Lab (founded by Mira Murati ~Feb 2025) released TML-Interaction-Small for closed research preview (wider release later 2026). 276B parameter MoE transformer (12B active/token), encoder-free early fusion architecture. Processes audio/video/text concurrently via 200ms 'micro-turns'; separate async background model handles reasoning/tool calls. Benchmarks: FD-bench V1 response latency 0.40s vs Gemini-3.1-flash-live 0.57s vs GPT-Realtime-2 1.18s. FD-bench V1.5 interruption handling 77.8 vs GPT-Realtime-2 47.8. Audio MultiChallenge 43.4% APR (GPT-Realtime-2: 48.5%). BigBench Audio 96.5% (background model on). Weaker than GPT-Realtime-2 on reasoning; stronger on interactivity/latency. Competing models: MiniCPM-o 4.5 (9B, OpenBMB, Feb 2026), Gemini 3.1 Flash Live (Google, Mar 2026), Qwen3.5 Omni (Alibaba, Mar 2026), GPT-Realtime-2 (OpenAI, May 2026).
