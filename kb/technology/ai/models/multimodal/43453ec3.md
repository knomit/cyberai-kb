# MiniMax H3 open multimodal model generates 2K video with native stereo audio

MiniMax H3 is an open model with unified context across text, images, video, and audio. It can generate up to 15 seconds of video at 2K resolution with native stereo sound, and performs well at instruction following, text/brand rendering, and video-to-video motion transfer.
