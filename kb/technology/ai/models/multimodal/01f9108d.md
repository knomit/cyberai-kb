# Google Nano Banana 2 - text rendering breakthrough for enterprise image generation

Google unveiled Nano Banana 2 image generation model, powered by real-time web search data. Key improvements: (1) Text rendering — most significant upgrade, solves long-standing AI image flaw where generated text was often gibberish; can also translate localized text within images between languages; (2) 'Real-world accuracy' with improved lighting, textures, details; (3) Creative control: better instruction following, subject/character consistency, production-ready resolutions 512px to 4K; (4) Enterprise use cases: infographics, diagrams, data visualizations, printable materials (billboards, programs), marketing campaigns. Available across Gemini app, Google Search, AI Studio, Google Cloud, Google Ads. Copyright risk: model trained on web data, ongoing copyright infringement suits against AI firms persist.
