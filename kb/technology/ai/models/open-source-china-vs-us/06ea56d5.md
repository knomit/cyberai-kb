# Chinese open models closing the gap with US models (Stanford HAI 2026 AI Index)

According to Stanford University's Human-Centered AI (HAI) institute 2026 AI Index, China's open models — historically lagging US firms' models — have substantially narrowed the gap, competing on cost and efficiency without sacrificing performance. Chinese labs focus largely on open-source and efficient models rather than the ultrapowerful proprietary models pursued by a few leading US labs.
