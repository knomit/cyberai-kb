# Sakana AI's Fugu Ultra v2 routes tasks across a pool of models and recursively calls itself

Fugu Ultra v2 is the higher-performance model in Sakana AI's Fugu family. It uses a language model trained to route tasks across a fixed pool of open and specialized models and to recursively call instances of itself, rather than depending on any individual proprietary frontier model in its pool. It targets answer quality on complex multi-step reasoning, autonomous research and full-stack software development, and supports configurable reasoning effort, function calling, structured outputs, image and PDF input, and built-in web search.
