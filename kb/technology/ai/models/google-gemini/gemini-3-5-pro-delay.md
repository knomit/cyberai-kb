# Gemini 3.5 Pro was reported delayed on 16 July 2026 over coding performance; Alphabet shares fell 4%

THE REPORT. On 16 July 2026, reporting (per CNBC) said Google had delayed its flagship Gemini 3.5 Pro model. It was described as MONTHS BEHIND SCHEDULE due to performance issues — specifically not matching the coding capabilities of rivals Anthropic and OpenAI.

THE MARKET REACTION. Alphabet shares fell 4% the same day.

GOOGLE'S RESPONSE. Google said the model remained in PARTNER TESTING, alongside an upgraded Flash model and other releases, and would be broadly available 'as soon as it's ready.'

CONTEXT THAT MAKES THE 'ALONGSIDE' CONCRETE: five days later, on 21 July 2026, Google launched Gemini 3.6 Flash along with 3.5 Flash-Lite and 3.5 Flash Cyber. The flagship slipped while the lighter workhorse line kept cadence — and Gemini 3.7 Flash followed roughly three weeks after that, on 13 August 2026.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the delay is REPORTED, not confirmed by Google, which characterized the model as in testing rather than delayed. 'Months behind schedule' implies an internal target date that is not stated in any record here. The 4% share move is same-day correlation; no source establishes the report caused it, and nothing here rules out other contributors. The comparison to Anthropic and OpenAI coding capability is the report's characterization, not a benchmark result.
