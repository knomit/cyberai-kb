# Google's Gemini flagship slipped months on coding while the Flash line shipped four times in five months — and the newest Flash leads on exactly the capability the flagship lacked

Between March and August 2026 two Gemini release tracks moved in opposite directions, and the capability at issue was the same on both.

THE FLAGSHIP SLIPPED, ON CODING SPECIFICALLY. Gemini 3.5 Pro was reported on 16 July 2026 as months behind schedule due to performance issues, explicitly not matching the coding capabilities of rivals Anthropic and OpenAI (CNBC). Alphabet shares fell 4% on the report. Google said the model remained in partner testing alongside an upgraded Flash model, and would be broadly available 'as soon as it's ready'. See the merged canonical kb/technology/ai/models/google-gemini/gemini-3-5-pro-delay.md.

THE WORKHORSE LINE SHIPPED FOUR TIMES.
- 4 March 2026: Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, aimed at high-volume developer workloads, lower latency, cost-effective translation and content moderation.
- 10 June 2026: Gemini 3.5 Flash Live Translate, real-time speech-to-speech across 70+ languages (single-source newsletter reporting).
- 21 July 2026: THREE at once — Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber (fine-tuned to find and fix security vulnerabilities). Google reported 3.6 Flash using up to 17% FEWER OUTPUT TOKENS than 3.5 Flash on the Artificial Analysis Index.
- 13 August 2026: Gemini 3.7 Flash, approximately THREE WEEKS after 3.6 Flash, billed as its most intelligent workhorse model FOR CODING AND AGENTS. Reported gains of 10-15 percentage points over 3.6 Flash on both FrontierCode 1.1 Main and DeepSWE v1.1, plus 12 points on the GDP.pdf document benchmark. API prices temporarily cut ~50% through year-end to $0.75/M input and $3.75/M output, the same nominal price as 3.6 Flash. Shipped with updated safeguards against chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear misuse and cyber offenses.

THE OBSERVATION. The capability the flagship was held back for — coding — is the capability the newest workhorse release is positioned and benchmarked on. Two tracks, opposite trajectories, one contested capability, inside five months.

THE CAUSAL READING IS NOT ESTABLISHED. No source states that Google redirected effort from Pro to Flash, that Flash progress explains the Pro delay, or that the two tracks compete for the same resources. The co-occurrence is documented; the explanation is not. Do NOT cite this record for a claim that Google abandoned or deprioritized the flagship.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN:
- Flash coding scores are NOT comparable to the rival models 3.5 Pro was measured against. The +10-15 point figures are Google's own, versus its OWN previous Flash model, on benchmarks Google selected. Nothing here shows Flash closed the gap to Anthropic or OpenAI.
- The 17%-fewer-output-tokens and the coding-point figures measure different things on different indices and must not be combined into a single efficiency or capability trend.
- The ~50% price cut is explicitly TEMPORARY, through year-end, against an unchanged nominal price — it is a promotion, not a price reduction.
- 'Four times in five months' counts announcements, not independent model families; 3.5 Flash-Lite and 3.6 Flash shipped the same day, and the Live Translate entry rests on single-source newsletter reporting at confidence 0.55.
- The delay reporting is 'reportedly' throughout; Google's own statement characterizes it as partner testing rather than a delay.
