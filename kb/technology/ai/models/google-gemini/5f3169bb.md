# Google released Gemini Carbon, tops Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59

On Wednesday 2026-10-28 Google released Gemini Carbon (TLDR calls it Gemini 4 Carbon), a frontier model aimed at security and heavy agentic workloads, built on Argon (the frontier model unveiled late September 2026). Google claims state-of-the-art cybersecurity and long-horizon performance, cut unsupported claims 2x versus Argon, and says it resisted every evaluated prompt-injection attack. Access starts with a subset of cyber defenders, then extends to partners via the Fairwind program before general availability. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v5, updated the same day) Carbon scores 59 versus Claude Opus 5.5 at 58 and GPT-6 Astra at 57; it trails on OSWorld and FrontierSWE. Introductory price: $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output tokens, cached input 95% off. Does NOT mean general availability yet.
