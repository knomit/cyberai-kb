# Gemini 3.7 Flash benchmark gains over 3.6 Flash

Google reported Gemini 3.7 Flash improved coding benchmark scores by 10-15 percentage points versus 3.6 Flash on both FrontierCode 1.1 Main and DeepSWE v1.1, and outperformed 3.6 Flash by 12 percentage points on the GDP.pdf benchmark for processing complex documents. It also claimed better first-pass code accuracy, stronger performance in knowledge-dense fields (finance, law, biosciences), and improved UI development from simple prompts.
