# Google Gemini 3.7 Flash launched with 50% introductory price cut

On Aug 13, 2026, Google announced Gemini 3.7 Flash, billed as its most intelligent 'workhorse' model for coding and agents, released just ~3 weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash. API prices were temporarily cut ~50% through year-end: $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens (same nominal price as 3.6 Flash). It is available via Google Antigravity, the Gemini API (Google AI Studio, Android Studio), enterprise platforms, and integrated into the Gemini Spark agent for AI Pro/Ultra subscribers. Google shipped it with updated safeguards against chemical/biological/radiological/nuclear misuse and cyber offenses.
