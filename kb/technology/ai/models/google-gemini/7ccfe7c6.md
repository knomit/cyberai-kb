# Gemini 3.5 Pro never shipped: team prioritized Flash iteration on agentic coding

Per a thread summarized by TLDR AI, Google had both Flash and Pro in the 3.5 series but saw gaps in agentic capabilities especially coding; the team judged the fastest path was iterating on the smaller Flash model; by the time Flash improved, Argon pre-training was done.
