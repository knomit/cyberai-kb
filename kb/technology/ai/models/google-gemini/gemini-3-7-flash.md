# Google Gemini 3.7 Flash: launch, pricing, and benchmark gains

On Aug 13, 2026, Google announced Gemini 3.7 Flash, billed as its most intelligent 'workhorse' model for coding and agents, released just ~3 weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash. API prices were temporarily cut ~50% through year-end: $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens (same nominal price as 3.6 Flash). It is available via Google Antigravity, the Gemini API (Google AI Studio, Android Studio), enterprise platforms, and integrated into the Gemini Spark agent for AI Pro/Ultra subscribers. Google shipped it with updated safeguards against chemical/biological/radiological/nuclear misuse and cyber offenses. On benchmarks, Google reported 3.7 Flash improved coding scores by 10-15 percentage points versus 3.6 Flash on both FrontierCode 1.1 Main and DeepSWE v1.1, and outperformed 3.6 Flash by 12 percentage points on the GDP.pdf benchmark for processing complex documents. It also claimed better first-pass code accuracy, stronger performance in knowledge-dense fields (finance, law, biosciences), and improved UI development from simple prompts.
