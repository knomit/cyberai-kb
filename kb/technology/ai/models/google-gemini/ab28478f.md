# Google Gemini 3.7 Flash (13 Aug 2026): launch, ~50% introductory price cut, Google-reported coding gains, and Artificial Analysis' independently measured 330 tok/s at 13.2s time-to-first-token

MERGED from two records of the same August 2026 Gemini 3.7 Flash release: one carrying launch, pricing, availability and Google's own benchmark claims; the other carrying Artificial Analysis' independent throughput, latency and Intelligence Index measurements. Keeping vendor-reported and independently-measured figures in one record is the point — the two categories must not be confused.

LAUNCH. On Aug 13, 2026, Google announced Gemini 3.7 Flash, billed as its most intelligent 'workhorse' model for coding and agents, released just ~3 weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash.

PRICING. API prices were TEMPORARILY cut ~50% THROUGH YEAR-END: $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens. The nominal (uncut) price is the same as 3.6 Flash. The temporary/permanent distinction is load-bearing and must survive citation.

AVAILABILITY. Google Antigravity, the Gemini API (Google AI Studio, Android Studio), enterprise platforms, and integrated into the Gemini Spark agent for AI Pro/Ultra subscribers. Google shipped it with updated safeguards against chemical/biological/radiological/nuclear misuse and cyber offenses.

GOOGLE-REPORTED BENCHMARKS. Google reported 3.7 Flash improved coding scores by 10–15 percentage points versus 3.6 Flash on both FrontierCode 1.1 Main and DeepSWE v1.1, and outperformed 3.6 Flash by 12 percentage points on the GDP.pdf benchmark for processing complex documents. It also claimed better first-pass code accuracy, stronger performance in knowledge-dense fields (finance, law, biosciences), and improved UI development from simple prompts.

INDEPENDENTLY MEASURED FIGURES. Google shipped the model WITHOUT disclosing throughput or latency figures. Artificial Analysis measured 330 OUTPUT TOKENS PER SECOND — behind only Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite — and a 13.2-SECOND wait to the first token. Its Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index average is 56, up from 52 for its predecessor.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the coding, document and knowledge-domain gains are GOOGLE'S OWN reported figures, not independently audited; only the throughput, TTFT and Intelligence Index numbers come from Artificial Analysis. The price cut is stated as temporary through year-end and should not be cited as the model's standing price. Benchmark placements at this cadence go stale within weeks.
