# Google launches Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber

On July 21, 2026, Google launched three lighter Gemini models aimed at reducing AI agent cost and latency: Gemini 3.6 Flash (improved coding/knowledge/multimodal), 3.5 Flash-Lite (its fastest, lowest-latency 3.5-class model), and 3.5 Flash Cyber (fine-tuned to find and fix security vulnerabilities). Google reports 3.6 Flash uses up to 17% fewer output tokens than its predecessor 3.5 Flash on the Artificial Analysis Index. 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash-Lite are available in the Gemini app and Gemini API via Google AI Studio; 3.5 Flash-Lite is also rolling out to Google Search.
