# Gemini 4 Argon: Google flagship frontier model (Oct 2026)

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon on 2026-09-30 as its flagship frontier model, claiming frontier performance in software engineering, knowledge work, cybersecurity, legal and finance. Initial rollout is to cyber defenders in Google's Fairwind program and paid API / Google AI Ultra customers; Google is also going through the US government's voluntary pre-release model checks before general availability. Per Google it sets state of the art on DeepSWE v1.1 (ahead of OpenAI Astra, Claude Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5), the Vals Index and LVBench, but trails Astra on FrontierSWE v2 and Terminal-Bench Science, and trails Opus 5.5 on PostTrainBench and Terminal-Bench 4.0. Output limit is 1M tokens (up from 64K). Does NOT mean it leads every benchmark: benchmark claims are Google's own.
