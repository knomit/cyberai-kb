# Meta launched Muse Spark 1.1 and Meta Model API in a price move

Meta launched Muse Spark 1.1, a vision-language model trained for agentic tasks, and opened Meta Model API, its first paid model access. Muse Spark 1.1 tops the MCP Atlas (Scale AI) and JobBench tool-use leaderboards and scored 51 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index (tying GLM-5.2 and GPT-5.6 Luna) at a low $0.26 per task. Priced at $1.25/$0.15/$4.25 per million input/cached/output tokens. Mark Zuckerberg framed the low pricing as an attack on competitors' high-margin economics, leveraging Meta's ad revenue.
