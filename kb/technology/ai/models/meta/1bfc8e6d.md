# Meta Muse Spark: proprietary pivot from open-weights with contemplating multi-agent mode and health leadership

Meta released Muse Spark in mid-April 2026 — first new model in a year and first from Meta's Superintelligence Labs. Major strategic pivot: closed/proprietary, ending Meta's role as leading US open-weights champion. Specs: natively multimodal (text/image/speech in, text out), 262K token context, undisclosed parameter count. Performance: 4th place on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (score 52 in reasoning mode). Key strengths: HealthBench Hard leader (42.8%, vs GPT-5.4 at 40.1%), CharXiv Reasoning leader (86.4%), DeepSearchQA leader (74.8%). Weak in coding (47 on Coding Index). Highly token-efficient: 59M tokens on AI Index vs 158M for Claude Opus 4.6. Three reasoning modes: instant, thinking, contemplating. Contemplating mode: multiple parallel agents propose → refine → aggregate results, achieving better performance at comparable latency. Meta enlisted 1,000+ physicians to curate health training data. Meta previously spent $14.3B for 49% stake in Scale AI (June 2025) and brought in Alexandr Wang as chief AI officer. Developer community concerned about pivot away from open-weights.
