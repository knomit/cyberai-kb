# Meta launched Muse Spark 1.1 and the Meta Model API, pricing it as an attack on rivals' margins

Meta introduced Muse Spark 1.1, a low-cost vision-language / multimodal reasoning model trained for agentic tasks, with improved tool use, coding, computer interaction, and multimodal capabilities. Alongside it Meta opened the Meta Model API, its first paid model access.

Benchmarks and price: Muse Spark 1.1 tops the MCP Atlas (Scale AI) and JobBench tool-use leaderboards and scored 51 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index — tying GLM-5.2 and GPT-5.6 Luna — at a low $0.26 per task. Token pricing is $1.25 / $0.15 / $4.25 per million input / cached / output tokens.

Mark Zuckerberg framed the low pricing as an attack on competitors' high-margin economics, leveraging Meta's ad revenue.
