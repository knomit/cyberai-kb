# Meta launches budget-tier Muse Ember model undercutting Claude Haiku 5.5 on cost per task

On 2026-11-09 Meta launched Muse Ember. Pricing is $0.10 per million input and $0.50 per million output tokens (same base as Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna), with cached input at $0.004 versus $0.01. Artificial Analysis estimates cost per task at $0.14 (Ember), $0.15 (Luna), $0.41 (Haiku 5.5). AutomationBench-AA: Ember 37.8% vs Haiku 5.5 13.9% vs Luna 20.2%. Coding Agent Index-AA: Ember 46, Haiku 5.5 37, Luna 31. Figures are as reported by The Deep View and Meta; early benchmarks.
