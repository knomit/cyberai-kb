# Google releases Gemma 4 open-weight multimodal models

Google's Gemma 4 is a new generation of open-weight multimodal language models with dense and Mixture-of-Experts architectures, parameters ranging from 2.3B to 31B. It adds a thinking mode, long-context efficiency, and a unified encoder-free architecture, achieving performance comparable to larger models across benchmarks. Reported in TLDR AI, July 8 2026.

LICENCE CLAIM IS CONTRADICTED — DO NOT CITE THIS FACT FOR GEMMA 4'S LICENCE. This record originally stated Gemma 4 was 'released under an Apache 2.0 license'. kb/technology/ai/models/open-weights/e266899a.md, which carries 2 sources against this record's 0, states that ALL Gemma 4 variants were released under the GEMMA TERMS OF SERVICE and are explicitly 'not fully open-source'. WHY THE DISTINCTION IS LOAD-BEARING: Apache 2.0 is a permissive open-source licence with no use restrictions; the Gemma Terms of Service impose use restrictions and do not meet the open-source definition. Anyone deciding whether they may use these weights, and how, gets a materially different answer from each. A reader who compresses 'open-weight' into 'open-source' inherits exactly this error.

The architectural and parameter claims above are unaffected by the licence contradiction. Note also that e266899a's variant breakdown (E2B, E4B, 26B MoE, 31B Dense) is more specific than this record's '2.3B to 31B' range, and that the two records disagree on the low end — 2.3B here versus E2B '2B effective parameters' there.
