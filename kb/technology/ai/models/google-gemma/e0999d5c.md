# Google releases Gemma 4 open-weight multimodal models

Google's Gemma 4 is a new generation of open-weight multimodal language models with dense and Mixture-of-Experts architectures, parameters ranging from 2.3B to 31B. It adds a thinking mode, long-context efficiency, and a unified encoder-free architecture, achieving performance comparable to larger models across benchmarks. Released under an Apache 2.0 license. Reported in TLDR AI, July 8 2026.
