# GPT-5.6 pricing and benchmark positioning vs Anthropic

Per The Deep View, GPT-5.6 Sol costs $5 per 1M input tokens and $30 per 1M output tokens, versus $10/$50 for Anthropic's Mythos and Fable. OpenAI's GPT-5.6 models are reported to outperform Anthropic's Mythos and Fable on benchmarks such as Agent's Last Exam. ARC Prize results describe GPT-5.6 Sol as the standout of the family and reportedly the first model to win an ARC-AGI-3 task.
