# GPT-5.6 pricing and benchmark positioning

GPT-5.6 pricing per 1M input/cached/output tokens: Sol $5/$0.50/$30, Terra $2.50/$0.25/$15, Luna $1/$0.10/$6 — versus $10/$50 (input/output) for Anthropic's Mythos and Fable. Cerebras planned to offer Sol at up to 750 tokens/sec starting July 2026. Benchmarks (OpenAI's tests): GPT-5.6 Sol in ultra mode set state of the art on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (multistep command-line coding) at 91.9%; at an unspecified reasoning level Sol scored 88.8%, narrowly beating Anthropic's Claude Mythos 5 (88.0%). GPT-5.6 models are reported to outperform Mythos and Fable on Agent's Last Exam. ARC Prize results describe Sol as the standout of the family and reportedly the first model to win an ARC-AGI-3 task. Reported by The Deep View, The Batch (2026-07-03), and ARC Prize.
