# OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 family (Sol, Terra, Luna)

Around July 9-10, 2026, OpenAI launched GPT-5.6, a family of three models: Sol (flagship, leading intelligence and efficiency), Terra (balanced), and Luna (fastest and least expensive). Sol is positioned for coding, cybersecurity, and science. Per The Deep View, the US government worked with OpenAI to delay the broader release until agencies and corporations were prepared for the model's ability to uncover software vulnerabilities.
