# OpenAI GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, Luna): preview to public launch

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 is a family of three tiers — Sol (flagship), Terra (balanced everyday), and Luna (fast/affordable). It first moved into a narrow preview as the Sol variant became available, reportedly adding a reasoning-effort control slider and an 'ultra' mode for complex tasks, with broad access said to depend on US government review approvals. It then reached public release on Thursday July 9, 2026, expanding preview access globally. Terra is described as competitive with GPT-5.5 while ~2x cheaper, and Luna as the lowest-cost option. Corroborated by TLDR AI (2026-07-08) and TestingCatalog.
