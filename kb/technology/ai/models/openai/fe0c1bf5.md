# OpenAI Ultrafast for GPT-6.1 Sol rolled out: up to 8x faster, $12/$60 per M tokens

OpenAI began rolling out Ultrafast for GPT-6.1 Sol on 2026-10-09 in the API, Codex and ChatGPT Work (TLDR AI). Near-Astra-level intelligence at up to 8x the speed of Sol Standard. API price in Ultrafast mode: $12 per million input tokens and $60 per million output tokens. It is a premium speed tier, not a cheaper one.
