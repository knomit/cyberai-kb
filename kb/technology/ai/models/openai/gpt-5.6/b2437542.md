# OpenAI previews GPT-5.6 family (Sol, Terra, Luna)

OpenAI announced a preview of its GPT-5.6 family of three closed vision-language models: GPT-5.6 Sol (most capable, comparable to Anthropic's Claude 5 Mythos), the mid-tier GPT-5.6 Terra, and the fast, low-cost GPT-5.6 Luna. All take text and images in and produce text out. GPT-5.6 Sol has a max reasoning level and an 'ultra mode' that spawns and coordinates subagents. Reported by The Batch (DeepLearning.AI), 2026-07-03.
