# GPT-5.6 pricing and Terminal-Bench 2.1 performance

GPT-5.6 pricing per 1M input/cached/output tokens: Sol $5/$0.50/$30, Terra $2.50/$0.25/$15, Luna $1/$0.10/$6. Cerebras planned to offer Sol at up to 750 tokens/sec starting July. In OpenAI's tests, GPT-5.6 Sol in ultra mode set the state of the art on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (multistep command-line coding) at 91.9%; at an unspecified reasoning level Sol scored 88.8%, narrowly beating Anthropic's Claude Mythos 5 (88.0%). Reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
