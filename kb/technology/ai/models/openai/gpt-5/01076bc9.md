# OpenAI previews GPT-5.6 with Sol, Terra, and Luna

OpenAI introduced GPT-5.6 Preview, a family of three models named Sol, Terra, and Luna, with Sol positioned as the flagship model. OpenAI limited the GPT-5.6 rollout as the Sol variant became available.
