# OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra mid-tier computer-use model

On 2026-10-26 OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Terra, a mid-tier model aimed at 'fast and capable computer use at scale', competing with Anthropic's Sonnet. OpenAI claims: 15 points higher than GPT-5.6 Terra on Agents' Last Exam (Berkeley RDI + 300 experts) at ~1/3 the cost on medium effort; on par with GPT-6 Astra at 1/10 the price per token; 3x+ faster than GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol; vs Claude Opus 5.5 at max effort over 3x more token-efficient at 1/4 the per-token cost (a previous-generation comparison). Error rate on error-inducing prompts fell from 53.6% (GPT-5.6 Terra) to 16.7% at low effort and 4.3% at max effort. Available to paid ChatGPT Work and Codex users and via API. These are vendor-reported numbers.
