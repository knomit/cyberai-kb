# OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol Ultrafast reaches ~750 tokens/sec, up to 14x speed

In August 2026 OpenAI previewed 'Ultrafast', a GPT-5.6 Sol mode capable of generating up to 750 output tokens per second and operating up to 14x standard processing speed, aiming to deliver real-time performance without switching to a smaller model. Separately, OpenAI's lowest-cost model GPT-5.6 Luna is priced at $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens.
