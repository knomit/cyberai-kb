# OpenAI GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna released Sept 22, 2026 at ~50% lower prices

On Sept 22, 2026 OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna as cheaper, faster counterparts to GPT-6 Astra (which OpenAI still calls its best model). Pricing: GPT-6 Sol $2/M input and $10/M output (vs GPT-5.6 Sol $4/$20); GPT-6 Luna $0.10/M input and $0.50/M output (vs previous generation $0.20/$1.20). OpenAI claims GPT-6 Sol beats Claude Opus 5 on AutomationBench, Agents' Last Exam and DeepSWE v1.1, and that both models have lower rates of circumventing warnings, coding deception and unauthorized agent interactions. Availability at launch: ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users; Free and Go users get GPT-6 Luna in the desktop app only; not yet in traditional chat.
