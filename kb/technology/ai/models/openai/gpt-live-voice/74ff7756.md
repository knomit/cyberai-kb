# GPT-Live-1 benchmark results vs Advanced Voice Mode

In OpenAI's internal tests, GPT-Live-1 at high reasoning scored 84.2% on GPQA (graduate-level science) versus 45.3% for its predecessor AVM. On BrowseComp (agentic web search for hard-to-find facts) GPT-Live-1 at high reasoning scored 75.2% versus 0.7% for AVM. Human raters preferred GPT-Live-1's conversational quality to AVM 75.7% of the time, and preferred GPT-Live-1 mini 69.2% of the time, in matched 5-10 minute conversations. On safety, GPT-Live-1 flagged disallowed content better than AVM: illicit behavior 97% vs 74%, self-harm 96% vs 89%; on adversarial prompts GPT-Live-1 flagged 84% of mental-health queries vs AVM's 57%, and 98% for self-harm vs AVM's 72%. All comparisons were against OpenAI's own predecessor rather than rival voice models.
