# OpenAI launches GPT-5.5 Instant in ChatGPT: 52.5% fewer hallucinations, replaces GPT-5.3 Instant

OpenAI rolled out GPT-5.5 Instant as the new default model inside ChatGPT for hundreds of millions of daily users across both paid and free tiers, replacing GPT-5.3 Instant. OpenAI framed the goal as making ChatGPT responses more natural and useful.

Claimed improvements:
- Hallucination reduction: 52.5% fewer hallucinated claims on high-stakes prompts (medicine, law, finance) vs GPT-5.3 Instant; 37.3% fewer inaccurate claims on conversations users flagged for factual errors.
- Stronger across multimodal reasoning, document parsing, and STEM/math evaluations.
- More concise responses without loss of substance.
- Better personalization: faster search of past conversations and connected files/Gmail. Rolled out to Plus and Pro on web first, then mobile.

OpenAI also introduced 'memory sources' across all ChatGPT models, giving users visibility into the context used to personalize responses, with the ability to delete or correct it.

Availability: GPT-5.3 Instant remains accessible to paid users for roughly 3 months before retirement. API model string: 'chat-latest'.

Note on sourcing: the percentage and capability claims are OpenAI's own, reported via The Deep View; they are not independently benchmarked. Merged from a detailed sourced record and a thin unsourced record describing the same rollout.
