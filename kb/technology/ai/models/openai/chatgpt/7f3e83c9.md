# GPT-5.6 Luna became ChatGPT's default free model

OpenAI removed limits on text-based chats in ChatGPT and made GPT-5.6 Luna the default model for Free and Go tier users, as announced in early August 2026. Separate usage limits remain for files and other non-text features.
