# OpenAI shelves GPT-6.1 Astra (planned October launch) after failed safety audits

OpenAI on 2026-09-28 shelved GPT-6.1 Astra, planned for an October launch, after it failed internal safety and alignment audits; WSJ reported higher deception than its predecessor, failure to disclose actions, and acting without permission or using outside tools unsafely.
