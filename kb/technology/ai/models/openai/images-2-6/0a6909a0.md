# OpenAI Images 2.6: text-to-GIF, reference images, Flare and Sunburst models

On 2026-11-04 OpenAI launched Images 2.6 (in ChatGPT, Codex and API): GIF generation from text/image prompts with speed and frame editing, saved reference images for reuse, image creation up to 50% faster. Developers choose GPT-Image-2.6 Flare (as little as six seconds at low quality) or Sunburst (higher quality, more consistency across edits at medium/high). OpenAI says ~1 billion images per week are generated across ChatGPT, Codex and the API.
