# OpenAI GPT-6 Cyber: ExploitGym 48.5% and staying in scope

On 2026-11-05 (Thursday) OpenAI announced GPT-6 Cyber, a cybersecurity model it says is far more reliable at staying within researcher-set boundaries. On ExploitGym it scored 48.5% vs 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber and 42.4% for GPT-6 Astra; it avoided exploiting out-of-scope services in 99.93% of tests without a deployment prompt and 100% with one. OpenAI reports it produced 948 Linux privilege-escalation exploits and found 81 previously unknown high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities. Access is via API and Codex for select members of Project Daybreak Red, with planned expansion. Figures are OpenAI self-reported; they do not show the model is safe outside those tests.
