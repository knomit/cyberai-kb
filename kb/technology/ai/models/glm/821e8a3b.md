# Z.ai released GLM-5.3, gains coming entirely from post-training

Z.ai released GLM-5.3, which it claims is its most capable open-weights model for coding, with over 50% improvement over GLM-5.2 on its in-house coding benchmark and 'emergent' cyber capabilities including improved vulnerability discovery. Per Z.ai, the only change versus GLM-5.2 is the amount of post-training: more environments, more diverse tasks, and more compute. Analyst commentary notes Z.ai's time-to-release is days rather than the months typical of OpenAI and Anthropic.
