# Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter model marketed as an 'always-on work-mate'

On Sunday Aug 2, 2026, Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter model it calls its biggest and most capable Qwen model, with upgrades in coding, agentic use, and long-horizon tasks. Alibaba's promotional campaign framed it as an 'always-on work-mate' that reclaims users' time (e.g., designing chips for 12 hours while the engineer goes fishing) — a lifestyle framing contrasting with US labs' productivity messaging. On the independent Code Arena front-end web benchmark it placed fourth, behind Claude Opus 5, Kimi K3 Max, and Claude Opus 5 High. Its pricing ($2 input / $6 output per million tokens) is on the cheaper end of the frontier field (vs. Fable at $10 input / $50 output). Alibaba planned to release Qwen3.8-Max's open weights the following week. Reported by The Deep View, Aug 4, 2026.
