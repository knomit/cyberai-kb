# Alibaba released Qwen-3.8, a 27B Apache-2 vision-capable model

Alibaba's Qwen lab released Qwen-3.8, a lightweight 27-billion-parameter, Apache 2.0 licensed, vision-capable LLM that Alibaba says outperforms previous generations particularly in real-world coding and office workflows. Independent review (Simon Willison) rates it excellent but notes it defaults to wildly overthinking problems.
