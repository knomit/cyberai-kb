# August 2026 saw a wave of model releases emphasizing agentic, long-horizon, and coding tasks

In August 2026 multiple AI vendors shipped models explicitly optimized for long-running agent tasks and coding: xAI's Grok 4.6 (long-running agents; reportedly matching GPT-5.6 Sol on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index; in Cursor, Grok Build, API), DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813 (aggressive pricing at $0.435/$0.87 per 1M input/output tokens; reportedly beating Claude Opus 4.8 on Terminal Bench 2.1, Cybergym, DeepSWE, AutomationBench), Alibaba's Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B (coding and long-horizon agent execution, adjustable reasoning_effort), and Microsoft's MAI-Thinking-1 (cost-efficient enterprise reasoning for coding/math/knowledge). Benchmark and pricing claims are as-reported by each vendor/newsletter and not independently verified here. See the individual model facts for exact figures and caveats.
