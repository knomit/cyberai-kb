# Meta launched Muse Image, a search-grounded image generation model, across Meta AI, Instagram and WhatsApp

Meta launched Muse Image in July 2026 (reported in TLDR AI, 2026-07-08), an AI model for generating high-quality visuals.

Technically, Muse Image grounds generation in search results and reasons before rendering, rather than generating purely from the prompt.

Distribution: available in Meta AI and integrated with Instagram and WhatsApp. Users can generate images from prompts, apply AI effects on Instagram, or redesign rooms with real products via Facebook Marketplace.

Pricing: free for general consumer use, with more advanced options in Meta's subscription plans; priced at $0.01 per image for production volumes.
