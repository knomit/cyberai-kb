# Nano Banana 2.1 (Gemini 3 series image model) released

Google's Nano Banana 2.1 is a Gemini 3 series model based on Gemini 3.6 Flash with up to 1M-token context, accepting text and image inputs and outputting images and text. Available in the Gemini app/API, Google AI Studio, Search AI Mode, Ads, Flow and Stitch (per TLDR AI, Oct 7 2026).
