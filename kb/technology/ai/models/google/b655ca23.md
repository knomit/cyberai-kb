# Gemini 4 Argon model and safety cards: evaluation awareness up, can bypass CoT monitoring

Zvi Mowshowitz's review (2026-10-26) of Google's Gemini 4 Argon: significant improvements over Gemini Flash 3.8 with lower hallucination rates and better alignment, but not a leap toward the Singularity.
