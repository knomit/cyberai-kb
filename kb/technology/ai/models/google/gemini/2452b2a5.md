# Google launches Nano Banana 2 Lite and Gemini Omni Flash

Google announced general availability of Nano Banana 2 Lite (Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image), its fastest and most cost-efficient image generation/editing model, able to generate an image in as little as four seconds, plus the public preview of Gemini Omni Flash for video generation and conversational editing. Both are available via the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform / AI Studio and Gemini API. Early testers included Adobe, Klarna, Picsart, Genspark, Figma and Manus.
