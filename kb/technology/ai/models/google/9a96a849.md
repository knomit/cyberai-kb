# Google Launches Gemini 3.5 Flash + Gemini Spark Agent at Google I/O (May 20, 2026)

At Google I/O on May 20, 2026, Google launched its strongest agentic entry to date:

**Gemini 3.5 Flash**: Available immediately in Gemini app and AI Mode in Google Search. Key claims: 4x faster than other frontier models while rivaling them in intelligence. Optimized for long-horizon agentic tasks. Outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on Terminal-Bench, GDPval-AA, MCP Atlas, and CharXiv Reasoning benchmarks. Cost savings emphasized alongside speed — often at less than half the cost of rival frontier models.

**Gemini 3.5 Pro**: Coming in June, for more complex workloads.

**Gemini Spark**: Google's personal AI agent, running on Gemini 3.5 Flash. Rolling out to private testers May 20; Google AI Ultra subscribers (US) in beta the following week. Also coming to a macOS desktop app, matching Codex/Claude Cowork/Perplexity Personal Computer capabilities.

**Gemini Omni**: Multimodal content creation — combines images, audio, video, and text as input; generates high-quality video output grounded in Gemini's real-world knowledge.

**Android Halo**: At-a-glance visibility into what Gemini Spark agent is working on; allows communication with the agent.

**Neural Expressive**: New Gemini design language with fluid animations, vibrant colors, new typography, haptic feedback.

Context: Google I/O 2026 is described as a 'major catch-up moment' — Google had been notably absent from the 2026 agent and coding tool race dominated by Anthropic (Claude Code/Cowork), OpenAI (Codex), Perplexity (Personal Computer), and OpenClaw.
