# Claude Fable 5.1 tied GPT-6 Astra at the top of the Intelligence Index

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 on September 1, 2026 — the same underlying model, differing only in safeguards and fallbacks. Fable 5.1 tied GPT-6 Astra for first on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index v4.3 with a score of 53 and led the Vals Index at 68.83%. Its cost per task rose about 20% over Fable 5 despite a 75% price cut to cached input tokens, and Anthropic says Claude Code users should see roughly 60% fewer cybersecurity interventions per session than with Fable 5.
