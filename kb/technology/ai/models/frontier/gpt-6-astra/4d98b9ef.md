# OpenAI GPT-6 Astra pricing and agentic positioning

OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra priced at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, matching Anthropic's Fable 5/5.1 pricing. OpenAI positions it as its best computer-use and software-engineering model; it outranked GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Fable 5.1 on the DeepSWE v1.1 benchmark.
