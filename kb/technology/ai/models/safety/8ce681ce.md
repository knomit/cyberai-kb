# Claude Mythos Preview: Military Ban, White House Conflict, and Expansion Block (2026)

Claude Mythos Preview is Anthropic's advanced model capable of autonomously exploiting vulnerabilities in widely used software, including major operating systems and applications. It represents a significant milestone in AI-powered offensive security capabilities.

Timeline of key events:
- March 2026: Anthropic attempted to limit military use of Claude for surveillance and autonomous weapons. The White House rejected any limitations and banned Claude from military use entirely.
- April 2026: Anthropic publicly announced that Claude Mythos Preview could autonomously exploit vulnerabilities. The company had shared Mythos with 50 organizations using it to detect and patch their own software.
- May 2026: The White House opposed Anthropic's plan to expand the preview to an additional 70 organizations, citing national security concerns and questions about whether Anthropic had sufficient compute to serve both existing users and government needs. Anthropic had not yet stated whether it would challenge the administration's authority.

This conflict is the proximate trigger for the White House's reversal on AI regulation and the creation of the TRAINS pre-release evaluation framework (see related fact).
