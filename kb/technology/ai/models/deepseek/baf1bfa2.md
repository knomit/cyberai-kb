# DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813-NVFP4 is an NVFP4-quantized MoE model

DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813-NVFP4 is a quantized release of DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813, an autoregressive Mixture-of-Experts language model. It was quantized with NVIDIA Model Optimizer and is licensed for both commercial and non-commercial use, targeting reasoning, agentic, and tool-use workloads.
