# DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813 (13 Aug 2026): 1.6T-parameter MoE launched alongside the open-source Harness v0.1 — its headline Terminal-Bench 2.1 gain is vendor-harness-dependent, and independent harnesses measured 9–33 points lower

MERGED from two records of the same 13 August 2026 launch: one carrying architecture and the vendor-versus-independent benchmark discrepancy, the other carrying the date, pricing, and the paired Harness release. Merging matters here because one record's 'outcompetes Claude Opus 4.8 on Terminal Bench 2.1' sat apart from the other's evidence that Terminal-Bench 2.1 scores for this model move by up to 33 points depending on harness.

THE LAUNCH. On Thursday 13 August 2026 DeepSeek launched two things together: DEEPSEEK-V4-PRO-0813, graduating its flagship from preview, and HARNESS v0.1, an open-source agent harness in developer preview under the MIT license. The pairing positions DeepSeek as an open-source challenger to Anthropic's Claude Code and signals a deliberate push at developers and enterprises. See kb/technology/software/ai-agents/harnesses/1d605de8.md for the harness in detail.

ARCHITECTURE. A mixture-of-experts transformer with 1.6 TRILLION total parameters and 49 BILLION ACTIVE per token (1.7T with the optional DSpark speculative-decoding module), accepting up to 1 MILLION input tokens and emitting up to 384,000 at 78.1 tokens/second, under an MIT license.

PRICING. $0.435 per 1M input tokens and $0.87 per 1M output tokens on DeepSeek's API and chat.

INDEPENDENT INDEX PLACEMENT. It scored 53 on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index at $0.25/task — THIRD among open-weights models, up eight points from the April preview, and one point above DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731.

VENDOR-REPORTED CODING-AGENT GAINS (DeepSeek's own tests, max reasoning, MINIMAL HARNESS): Terminal-Bench 2.1 rose 72.1%→87.9%; DeepSWE 12.8%→62.7%; CyberGym 52.7%→83.3%. DeepSeek also reported it outcompetes Claude Opus 4.8 on Terminal Bench 2.1, CyberGym, DeepSWE and AutomationBench.

THE HARNESS DISCREPANCY — LOAD-BEARING, AND IT QUALIFIES THE CLAIM ABOVE. Independent harnesses measured LOWER Terminal-Bench 2.1 numbers for the same model: Vals AI 54.68 and Artificial Analysis 78.7% (via Terminus 2), against DeepSeek's own 87.9%. That is a spread of up to 33 points on one benchmark for one model, illustrating how much HARNESS CHOICE moves agentic scores. Any 'beats Opus 4.8 on Terminal Bench 2.1' citation must carry which harness produced it.

ADOPTION CONTEXT. DeepSeek was second only to Anthropic in total tokens consumed in July 2026.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the benchmark comparisons against Claude Opus 4.8 are as-reported by DeepSeek and by newsletter coverage, not independently administered. The publication of Harness v0.1 is what makes DeepSeek's own numbers reproducible in principle — agentic performance is a joint function of model and scaffolding — but no source here reports an independent replication using it.
