# Google Lyria 3: music generation with stems, lyrics, and voice cloning

Google DeepMind released Lyria 3 in late March 2026, successor to Lyria 2. New capabilities: (1) Stem separation generation — can generate individual instrument tracks (drums, bass, melody, vocals) as separate audio files; (2) Lyrics-to-song: input lyrics + genre prompt, outputs full produced track; (3) Voice cloning with consent verification — upload 30s reference audio to clone a singing voice (gated with identity verification); (4) Real-time collaborative mode in YouTube Studio. Lyria 3 powers the 'AI Music' tab in YouTube Studio. Max track length extended to 5 minutes (from 2 min in Lyria 2).
