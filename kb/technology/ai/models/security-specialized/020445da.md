# Google shipped Gemini 3.8 Flash plus a restricted Flash Cyber variant

Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash on 2026-09-03, claiming improved coding, agentic and multi-step reasoning performance over 3.7 Flash while holding the same introductory pricing. Alongside it Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, a specialized variant for vulnerability detection and automated patching, distributed only through a restricted defender program rather than general API availability. Benchmark gains are Google-reported.
