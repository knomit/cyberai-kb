# Together AI's tev1-4B-experimental classifier cost only $17 to train

Together AI released tev1-4B-experimental, a Jev-like classifier fine-tuned on top of Qwen3.5 4B, available on Together serverless at $0.042 per million input tokens and $0 per million output tokens. Together AI published the data recipe and a fine-tuning tutorial; the model reportedly cost only $17 to train.
