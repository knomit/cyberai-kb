# Google launches native computer use on Gemini 3.5 Flash

Google launched native computer-use capabilities for Gemini 3.5 Flash, allowing the lightweight model to interact directly with digital desktop interfaces. The model processes continuous screenshots to execute click, scroll, and typing actions across varied software environments.
