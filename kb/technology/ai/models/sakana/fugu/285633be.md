# Fugu-Ultra benchmark results and pricing

Per Sakana's tests, Fugu-Ultra set state of the art on Terminal-Bench 2.1, LiveCodeBench, LiveCodeBench Pro, SWE-Bench Pro, and Humanity's Last Exam; Fugu and Fugu-Ultra both scored 95.5 on GPQA-Diamond (highest recorded); Fugu scored 60.1 on SciCode (best of any available model). Fugu-Ultra pricing (including submodels/agents): $5/$30/$0.50 per 1M input/output/cached tokens, doubled above 272,000-token contexts; subscriptions $20/$100/$200 per month for standard/pro/max. Available outside Europe via Sakana API, OpenRouter, Vercel. Reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
