# Sakana AI releases Fugu and Fugu-Ultra model orchestrators

Tokyo-based Sakana AI released Fugu and Fugu-Ultra, models that delegate tasks to other models and agents under a single API. Fugu targets discrete tasks (basic coding, chat); Fugu-Ultra targets long-running tasks. They call a range of LLMs (open models plus closed models like Claude Opus 4.8, Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT-5.5) and themselves. Fugu was fine-tuned from an undisclosed LLM, then trained with sep-CMA-ES (an evolutionary algorithm) for model selection; Fugu-Ultra uses a 'Conductor' coordinator and was trained with GRPO reinforcement learning. Reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
