# Ant Group released Ling-3.0-flash-Fin, an open-weights finance model

Ant Group released Ling-3.0-flash-Fin, an open-weights model developed with financial institutions for tasks including source checking, valuation spreadsheets, and report writing. Artificial Analysis reported scores of 23 on its Intelligence Index and 24 on its Finance & Accounting Index. Note the two scores are on different indices and should not be quoted as a single number; both are low in absolute terms relative to frontier general models, so the model's case rests on domain fit and open weights rather than on index position.
