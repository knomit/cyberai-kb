# "Ox Alpha" is an anonymously-provided stealth reasoning model routed by OpenRouter

Ox Alpha is a reasoning model aimed at coding, sustained agentic work, and production workloads — long-horizon software engineering, complex reasoning, and workflows combining text with visual context. It is developed and operated by a provider that has chosen to remain anonymous. OpenRouter routes requests to it but is not its developer, owner, or provider.
