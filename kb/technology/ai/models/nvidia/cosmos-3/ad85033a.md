# NVIDIA Cosmos 3: four open 16B world-simulation models

NVIDIA's Cosmos 3 offers four open-weight models (16B trainable parameters each) for interactive world simulation and driving scenes, supporting multi-sensor generation, synchronized observations and photorealistic video from simulator controls.
