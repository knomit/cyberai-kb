# NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe is an open-weight multilingual transcription model

NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe is an open-weight model for multilingual transcription, translation, speaker attribution and audio Q&A, supporting up to 40 languages, and ranked first on several transcription and speaker-attribution leaderboards per NVIDIA. Granary v2 provides reusable data pipelines and fine-tuning options for custom vocabularies.
