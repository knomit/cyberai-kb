# Inkling 2 Small: 594B MoE (17B active), Apache 2.0 weights to follow

Inkling 2 Small (Thinking Machines) is a Mixture-of-Experts model with 594B total / 17B active parameters, image input and variable effort. Initially available via Tinker with closed weights; full weights promised under Apache 2.0 about a week later after external safety testing and red-teaming. Distinct from the earlier Inkling-Small (276B/12B active).
