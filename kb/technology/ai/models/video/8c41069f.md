# Grok Imagine 1.0: Tops Video Arena at $4.20/min vs Sora $30/min, 7x Cheaper

xAI launched Grok Imagine 1.0 video generator (March 2026). Capabilities: Text + images/video in, up to 15 seconds at 1280x720 via API ($4.20/min). Topped Artificial Analysis Video Arena at launch in both text-to-video and image-to-video categories, ahead of Runway Gen-4.5, Kling 2.5 Turbo, Veo 3.1. LM Arena: #1 image-to-video (1400 Elo), #4 text-to-video (1362 Elo). Generation time: 110.1 seconds average. Price vs competitors: Kling 2.5 Turbo $4.20/min (no audio), Veo 3.1 $12/min, OpenAI Sora 2 Pro $30/min. 7x cheaper than Sora at comparable or higher quality. Integrated into X social network for direct sharing. xAI controversy: AI-generated nonconsensual sexualized images including of children persisted after xAI promised fixes. EU investigations, country bans.
