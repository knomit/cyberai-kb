# DeepSeek V4-Pro and V4-Flash: 1M context, $3.48/M tokens, competitive with frontier closed models (Apr 27, 2026)

DeepSeek released previews of DeepSeek-V4-Pro and DeepSeek-V4-Flash on April 27, 2026. V4-Pro: 1.6 trillion total / 49 billion active parameters; new hybrid attention combining Compressed Sparse Attention (CSA) and Heavily Compressed Attention (HCA) for better computational efficiency; 1 million token context window; outperforms all current open models in reasoning, math, STEM, and coding; competitive with GPT-5.4-High and Claude-Opus-4.6-Max. V4-Flash: 284 billion total / 13 billion active parameters; smaller/faster/cheaper variant. Pricing: V4-Pro at $3.48/M tokens vs $25/M for Claude Opus 4.6 and $15/M for GPT-5.4. Despite lower compute cost, achieves comparable benchmark scores. Available at deepseek.com (Expert Mode, Instant Mode) and API. Continuation of DeepSeek's philosophy of frontier performance at low cost — putting price pressure on American labs.
