# Mercury 2.5 outputs 1,107 tok/s as the largest diffusion language model trained

Inception Labs released Mercury 2.5 on 2026-09-09, described as the largest diffusion language model trained to date. Vendor-reported specs: 1,107 output tokens per second on widely available Nvidia GPUs, a 260K-token context window, and quality comparable to cost-optimized frontier models (GPT-5.6 Luna Low, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, Claude Haiku 4.5) — i.e. it targets the cheap tier, not the frontier. Launch pricing was 80% off at $0.04 per million input tokens and $0.15 per million output tokens. The significant claim is that diffusion decoding buys an order-of-magnitude throughput advantage at comparable cheap-tier quality; all figures are Inception-reported.
