# Inception Labs Mercury 2.5 diffusion LM outputs 1,107 tokens/sec

Inception Labs' Mercury 2.5, described as the largest diffusion language model ever trained, outputs 1,107 tokens per second on widely available Nvidia GPUs with a 260K-token context window, launch-priced at $0.04 per million input tokens and $0.15 per million output tokens.
