# Mercury 3 pricing and speed claims: $0.10/M in, $0.35/M out (50% launch discount), 4-8x faster

Per The Deep View (Oct 20, 2026): Inception's Mercury 3 costs $0.10/M input and $0.35/M output tokens at launch pricing (50% off eventual standard price; promo length unstated), equal input price and 35% cheaper output than Haiku 5.5 / GPT-6 Luna. Inception claims 4-8x faster than Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna and GLM-5.3 Flash and better on agentic benchmarks, ~1,000 tok/s, and that Mercury 3 agent swarms boosted model speed 16% via recursive self-improvement. Mercury 3 has 40x the parameters of Mercury 1 (Feb 2025); total undisclosed. Google DeepMind released DiffusionGemma (26B params) in June 2026. Vendor claims.
