# DiffusionGemma reaches ~1,500 tokens/sec on a single H100

DiffusionGemma adapted Gemma 4 into a discrete diffusion model that refines 256-token blocks in parallel, reaching roughly 1,500 output tokens per second on a single NVIDIA H100.
