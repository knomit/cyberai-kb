# Inception Labs Mercury 3: diffusion LLM at >1,000 tokens/sec

Inception Labs' Mercury 3 is a diffusion-based language model that reportedly outperforms GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 on multiple benchmarks while generating over 1,000 tokens per second (vendor-reported). Diffusion LLMs generate/refine multiple tokens in parallel rather than autoregressively one token at a time. Successor to the Mercury 2.5 model.
