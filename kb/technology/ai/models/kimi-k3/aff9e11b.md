# Moonshot releases Kimi K3 weights

Moonshot AI released the model weights and a technical report for Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with native visual understanding and a 1-million-token context window. Its new architecture is claimed to deliver about 2.5x the intelligence per unit of compute. Moonshot is also opening up more of the surrounding stack, including high-performance attention kernels and a MoE component.
