# Tencent Hy4 preview: 770B-parameter open-weight MoE with 1M context

Tencent released Hy4, an open-weight text LLM with 770B total parameters and 49B active parameters, a 1M-token context window, and a 1.56TB footprint on Hugging Face. It ships two reasoning levels: 'high' (on by default) and 'no_think', which disables reasoning.
