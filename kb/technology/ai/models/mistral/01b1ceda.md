# Mistral releases open weights for trillion-parameter Mistral Large 4

Per TLDR AI (2026-10-30) and The Deep View: Mistral actually released the weights of Mistral Large 4 (~1T parameters) on Thursday Oct 29, 2026, for self-hosted deployment and cybersecurity, after a public preview announced in October. Vendor-reported benchmarks: top open model on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, Terminal-Bench 4.0 rising from 28% to 41% (tying GLM-5.3), and a tie with MiMo V2 6-Pro on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index. These are vendor claims, not independent verification.
