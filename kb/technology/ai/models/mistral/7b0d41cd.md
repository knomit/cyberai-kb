# Mistral OCR 4.1 released for complex document parsing

Mistral released OCR 4.1, a vision-multimodal model engineered to ingest, parse, and structure complex document formats. It handles complex tabular layouts and hierarchical document structures and outputs clean machine-readable JSON or Markdown.
