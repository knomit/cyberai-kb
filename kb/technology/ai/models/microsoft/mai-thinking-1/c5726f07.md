# Microsoft builds MAI-Thinking-1 reasoning model from scratch

Microsoft introduced MAI-Thinking-1, its first reasoning language model not distilled or fine-tuned from another developer's model. It is a 1-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model (35B active per token), text in/out up to 256,000 tokens, comparable to Claude Sonnet 4.6. It leads a family of seven MAI models unveiled at Microsoft Build, including MAI-Code-1-Flash (in GitHub Copilot and VS Code). Pretraining used 30T tokens and midtraining 3.55T tokens of primarily human-generated, licensed data (over 50% code), avoiding synthetic data. Reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
