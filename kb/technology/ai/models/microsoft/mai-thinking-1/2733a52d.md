# MAI-Thinking-1 tops AIME 2025 math among mid-tier peers

Per Microsoft's tests, MAI-Thinking-1 is strongest at mathematics: on AIME 2025 it scored 97.0%, topping Claude Sonnet 4.6 (95.6%) and DeepSeek V3.2 (93.1%) but trailing Claude Opus 4.6 (99.8%). It trails peers on graduate-level science and agentic coding; no independent evaluations published yet. Microsoft trained three RL specialist models (STEM reasoning; agentic coding/tool use; helpfulness/safety) then distilled them into one via supervised fine-tuning plus a final RL round. This follows the April 2026 amendment making Microsoft's license to OpenAI models non-exclusive. Reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
