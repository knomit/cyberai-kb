# OpenAI Releases GPT-OSS: Open-Source 120B and 20B Models with Safeguard Variants

OpenAI released GPT-OSS in May 2026, a family of open-source language models including a 120B parameter flagship and a 20B parameter efficient variant, both released under permissive licenses for commercial use. The release also includes Safeguard model variants — fine-tuned versions with enhanced safety alignment and content filtering built-in — intended for deployment in regulated industries. GPT-OSS represents a strategic pivot for OpenAI, which had previously emphasized closed-source models. The release is widely interpreted as a competitive response to Meta's Llama series success in the open-source community and a move to re-engage developer communities that had shifted to open alternatives. The 120B model performance benchmarks against GPT-4o on many tasks while being freely deployable on-premises.
