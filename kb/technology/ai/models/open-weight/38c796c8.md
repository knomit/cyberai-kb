# Mistral Large 4: 1T-parameter open-weight flagship, weights due Oct 27 2026

Mistral launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4 (ML4) on Oct 6 2026: a 1-trillion-parameter open-weight multimodal model aimed at agentic use, trained on 4,000 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs and trained with reinforcement learning rather than only distillation. Weights are scheduled for release on Oct 27 2026; it is served in preview from Mistral data centers in Europe and via API. Mistral claims parity with top closed models and competitiveness with open models 3x its size (vendor claims). Too large for desktop/laptop local use.
