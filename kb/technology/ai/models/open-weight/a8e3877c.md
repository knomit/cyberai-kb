# Google EmbeddingGemma 2 and Reflection Beam: new US open models

Google released EmbeddingGemma 2 (Oct 6 2026): 740M-parameter multimodal embedding model (text, code, images, audio, video in one space; 270M params for text-only), Apache 2.0, built for on-device search/retrieval. Reflection AI released Beam, an open model of 500B+ parameters for coding/reasoning/agentic work, claimed up to 4x cheaper reasoning than larger open models such as GLM-5.2 (vendor claim).
