# Meta Muse Glimmer open-weight agentic model

On 2026-08-11 Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter open-weight agentic model under the Apache 2.0 license, small enough to run locally on a Mac or PC with a single consumer GPU. Meta says it is optimized for always-on local agents, coding, function/tool calling, multi-step reasoning, model evaluation, and multilingual/multimodal input, and reports it outperforms similarly sized models (cited as Google Gemma4-31B and Qwen3.6-27B) on agentic/coding benchmarks. The release accompanied a ~6,500-word Mark Zuckerberg essay, 'The Future is for Everyone,' arguing for decentralized 'personal superintelligence' and a balance of power against AI centralization.
