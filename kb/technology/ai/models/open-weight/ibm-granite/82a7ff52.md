# IBM Granite 4.2 ships dense decoder-only reasoning models at 3B, 8B and 30B trained on 15T tokens

IBM's Granite 4.2 family are dense, decoder-only reasoning LLMs released in 3B, 8B and 30B sizes, trained on 15 trillion tokens using a five-phase strategy that includes a multi-stage reinforcement learning pipeline. They support native tool calling with a THINKING / NON-THINKING switch. The 8B and 30B models learn agentic behavior through RL stages in real environments, targeting capabilities such as code editing and web search.
