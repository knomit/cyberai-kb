# Thinking Machines' Inkling is its first from-scratch model, released under Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face

Thinking Machines released a model called Inkling in July 2026 — the company's first model trained from scratch — with weights available on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license. Notable design choices include its architecture and position encoding, ingesting images and audio without a separately pretrained encoder in front of the model, and a configurable 'thinking effort' setting.
