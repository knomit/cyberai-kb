# Z.ai's GLM-5.3-Flash was the anonymous 'Ox Alpha' model

On August 26, 2026, Chinese AI lab Z.ai unveiled GLM-5.3-Flash and revealed it was the model previously tested anonymously as 'Ox Alpha' on OpenCode and OpenRouter, where it topped leaderboards and went viral. It is the first natively multimodal model in the GLM-5 series: a Mixture-of-Experts model with roughly 320 billion total parameters and about 18 billion active parameters. Z.ai claims it outperforms GLM-5.2 across benchmarks at roughly a tenth of the price, approaches Claude Opus 4.8 on coding and agentic benchmarks, and performs on par with DeepSeek-V4, GPT-5.6 Terra, and Gemini 3.7 Flash on software engineering, multi-step agentic tasks, tool use, and professional work. Z.ai stated all inference traffic was served on Chinese AI chips. Weights are published on Hugging Face and the model powers Z.ai's ZCode coding tool.
