# GLM-5.3: a 753B MoE whose gains came entirely from more post-training of GLM-5.2, tying Kimi K3 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at unchanged pricing

MERGED from three records of the same August 2026 GLM-5.3 launch (two previously merged, plus a third carrying the post-training claim and Z.ai's own benchmark). Every scope qualifier and disclaimer from all inputs is preserved.

ARCHITECTURE. GLM-5.3 is a mixture-of-experts transformer with 753 BILLION TOTAL PARAMETERS and 40 BILLION ACTIVE per token, accepting up to 1 MILLION input tokens and emitting up to 128,000 output tokens at ~90 tokens/second. It offers adjustable reasoning (low/high/max), tool calling, structured output, streaming, and context caching.

HOW IT WAS BUILT — THE LOAD-BEARING CLAIM. Z.ai achieved these gains SOLELY BY FINE-TUNING GLM-5.2 — no new model, no architecture change. Per Z.ai, THE ONLY CHANGE VERSUS GLM-5.2 IS THE AMOUNT OF POST-TRAINING: more environments, more diverse tasks, and more compute.

INDEPENDENT BENCHMARK. At max reasoning it scored 60 points on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index at $0.68 per task, TYING open-weights leader Kimi K3 ($0.84/task) and jumping 7 POINTS over GLM-5.2. It trails Claude Opus 5 (63), GPT-5.6 Sol (61), and Grok 4.6 (61).

VENDOR-REPORTED BENCHMARK. Z.ai claims GLM-5.3 is its most capable open-weights coding model, with OVER 50% IMPROVEMENT over GLM-5.2 on its IN-HOUSE coding benchmark, and 'emergent' cyber capabilities including improved vulnerability discovery. This is Z.ai's own measurement and is not the Artificial Analysis figure above.

RELEASE CADENCE. Analyst commentary notes Z.ai's time-to-release is DAYS rather than the months typical of OpenAI and Anthropic.

API AND PRICING. Available via API in August 2026 at $1.40 per million input tokens and $4.40 per million output tokens — PRICING UNCHANGED FROM GLM-5.2 — so developers get substantially stronger coding and long-horizon agent performance at the same cost. GLM-5.2 supports a 1M-token context window.

WEIGHTS. As of the API-release reporting, Z.ai stated it planned to release the weights openly but had not set a date. See kb/technology/security/governance/open-weights-release/e54ecf14.md, which records that Z.ai subsequently released the weights after roughly two weeks of safety evaluation with vetted security partners, having published GLM-5.3's CyberGym and ExploitBench scores first.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN:
- GLM-5.3 is a DISTINCT MODEL from GLM-5.3-Flash (the ~320B-parameter natively multimodal model previously tested anonymously as 'Ox Alpha'). Do not conflate their parameter counts, benchmarks, or pricing.
- Benchmark placements are as-measured by Artificial Analysis at a cadence that goes stale within weeks.
- The in-house coding benchmark result and the 'emergent cyber capabilities' characterization are VENDOR-REPORTED and not independently audited; only the Intelligence Index placement is third-party.
