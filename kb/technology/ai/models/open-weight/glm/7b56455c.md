# GLM-5.3: a 753B MoE fine-tune of GLM-5.2 that tied Kimi K3 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, priced unchanged from its predecessor

Z.ai's GLM-5.3 is a mixture-of-experts transformer with 753 billion total parameters and 40 billion active per token, accepting up to 1 million input tokens and emitting up to 128,000 output tokens at ~90 tokens/second. It offers adjustable reasoning (low/high/max), tool calling, structured output, streaming, and context caching.

BENCHMARK. At max reasoning it scored 60 points on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index at $0.68 per task, tying open-weights leader Kimi K3 ($0.84/task) and jumping 7 points over GLM-5.2. It trails Claude Opus 5 (63), GPT-5.6 Sol (61), and Grok 4.6 (61).

HOW IT WAS BUILT. Notably, Z.ai achieved this solely by fine-tuning GLM-5.2 rather than training a new model or changing the architecture.

API AND PRICING. Z.ai made GLM-5.3 available via API in August 2026 at $1.40 per million input tokens and $4.40 per million output tokens — pricing unchanged from GLM-5.2 — so developers get substantially stronger coding and long-horizon agent performance at the same cost. GLM-5.2 supports a 1M-token context window.

WEIGHTS. As of the API-release reporting, Z.ai stated it planned to release the model's weights openly but had not set a release date. See kb/technology/security/governance/open-weights-release/e54ecf14.md, which records that Z.ai subsequently released the weights after roughly two weeks of safety evaluation with vetted security partners, having published GLM-5.3's CyberGym and ExploitBench scores first.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: GLM-5.3 is a distinct model from GLM-5.3-Flash (the ~320B-parameter natively multimodal model previously tested anonymously as 'Ox Alpha'); do not conflate their parameter counts, benchmarks, or pricing. Benchmark placements are as-measured by Artificial Analysis at a cadence that goes stale within weeks.
