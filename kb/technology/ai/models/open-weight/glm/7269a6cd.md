# 'Ox Alpha' was Z.ai's GLM-5.3-Flash: the anonymous stealth model, its record adoption, and its August 26, 2026 unmasking

THE STEALTH RELEASE (as recorded before the reveal). Ox Alpha appeared as a reasoning model aimed at coding, sustained agentic work, and production workloads — long-horizon software engineering, complex reasoning, and workflows combining text with visual context. It was developed and operated by a provider that had chosen to remain anonymous; OpenRouter routed requests to it but was not its developer, owner, or provider. OpenCode's model page disclosed neither maker, release date, knowledge-cutoff date, nor output-limit metadata. It was offered free through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, making it trivial to substitute into existing developer workflows.

ADOPTION DURING THE ANONYMOUS PERIOD. OpenCode users processed 26 trillion tokens through Ox Alpha in its first four days, setting an OpenRouter launch record, across 327,000 unique users and 8,328,244 completed sessions.

THE REVEAL. On August 26, 2026 Chinese AI lab Z.ai unveiled GLM-5.3-Flash and stated it was the model previously tested anonymously as Ox Alpha on OpenCode and OpenRouter, where it topped leaderboards and went viral. It is the first natively multimodal model in the GLM-5 series: a Mixture-of-Experts model with roughly 320 billion total parameters and about 18 billion active parameters. Z.ai claims it outperforms GLM-5.2 across benchmarks at roughly a tenth of the price, approaches Claude Opus 4.8 on coding and agentic benchmarks, and performs on par with DeepSeek-V4, GPT-5.6 Terra, and Gemini 3.7 Flash on software engineering, multi-step agentic tasks, tool use, and professional work. Z.ai stated all inference traffic was served on Chinese AI chips. Weights are published on Hugging Face and the model powers Z.ai's ZCode coding tool.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: the benchmark and pricing claims are vendor-reported by Z.ai and were not independently validated on public leaderboards at time of writing. The adoption figures are OpenCode/OpenRouter platform-reported. Separate corpus records tracking mid-2026 'beats frontier' claims as self-reported and unvalidated still apply to the pre-reveal evidence state — the reveal resolved the AUTHORSHIP question, not the independent-validation question.
