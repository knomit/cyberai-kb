# Cisco LLM Security Leaderboard: Anthropic 8/10 Top Spots, Mistral/DeepSeek Bottom

Cisco released its LLM Security Leaderboard (March 2026), based on single- and multi-turn adversarial attacks to elicit harmful responses. Results: Anthropic holds 8 of top 10 spots. #1 Claude Opus 4.5, #2 Sonnet 4.5, #3 Haiku 4.5. OpenAI GPT-5.2 (#7) and GPT-5 Nano (#9). Bottom of leaderboard: Mistral Magistral Small 2509 and Ministral 3 14B Instruct (last two). Also in bottom 10: DeepSeek, Cohere, Qwen, xAI models. Amy Chang (Head of AI Threat Intelligence & Security Research, Cisco): '83% of organizations planned to deploy agentic AI, but only 29% felt ready to do so securely.' Multi-turn conversational attacks specifically tested — better reflect real-world adversarial behavior. Methodology posted publicly. No extra guardrails used. Microsoft provides similar scores but only for internal customers (limited public pressure).
