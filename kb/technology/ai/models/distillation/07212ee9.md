# Fireworks AI releases Ember-1, a distilled Kimi K3 model with 40% fewer tokens

Fireworks AI introduced Ember-1, a specialized model built on Kimi K3 that matches Kimi K3's quality while using 40% fewer tokens. It launched as a Research Preview serving option on Fireworks Serverless alongside the base Kimi K3 model, under a new 'research releases' program that gives developers two-week serverless access to new research models; models with sufficient demand are made permanent.
