# Xiaomi open-sourced MiMo-V2.6 Pro and Flash omnimodal models with RL code and environments

Xiaomi released MiMo-V2.6 Pro and MiMo-V2.6 Flash (Sept 2026) as open omnimodal models able to coordinate agents (e.g., build and visually test 3D scenes, create Blender assets, control a robotic arm from camera feeds, compose music as scores/MIDI). Xiaomi published the technical report, RL training environments and RL framework code. Its paper attributes RL stability at scale to freezing the MoE router and a multi-layer defense against reward hacking, and frames the series as a step on the recursive-self-improvement (RSI) path. Available via AI Studio, MiMo Code, MiMo Desktop, Xiaomi's MiMo API Platform and OpenRouter.
