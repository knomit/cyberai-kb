# Google's TimesFM-3 is a 330M-parameter time-series foundation model for zero-shot multivariate forecasting

Google introduced TimesFM-3, a 330-million-parameter time-series foundation model pretrained on more than 1 trillion time points. It adds zero-shot forecasting across multiple targets and supports both historical and known-future covariates without task-specific fine-tuning.
