# OpenAI Launches GPT-5.5 Instant: 52.5% Fewer Hallucinations, Replaces GPT-5.3 Instant

OpenAI launched GPT-5.5 Instant, the new default model for ChatGPT's hundreds of millions of daily users (replacing GPT-5.3 Instant). Key improvements claimed:

- **Hallucination reduction**: 52.5% fewer hallucinated claims on high-stakes prompts (medicine, law, finance) vs GPT-5.3 Instant; 37.3% fewer inaccurate claims on conversations users flagged for factual errors.
- **Improved capabilities**: Stronger across multimodal reasoning, document parsing, STEM/math evaluations.
- **Tighter responses**: More concise without loss of substance.
- **Better personalization**: Faster search of past conversations and connected files/Gmail. Rolling out to Plus and Pro users on web, coming to mobile.

OpenAI also introduced 'memory sources' across all ChatGPT models — giving users visibility into what context is used to personalize responses, with ability to delete or correct it.

Availability: GPT-5.5 Instant rolling out to all ChatGPT users. GPT-5.3 Instant remains accessible to paid users for ~3 months before retirement. API model string: 'chat-latest'.
