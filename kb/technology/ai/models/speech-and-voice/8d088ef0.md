# Google released Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.5 Transcribe as a developer stack for real-time voice apps

On 2026-09-15 Google released Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.5 Transcribe together as a developer offering for building real-time voice applications, with enhanced voice recognition, transcription and live interaction capabilities aimed at improving accuracy in voice-driven applications. Google separately described Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking as its most advanced live dialogue models to date, with upgrades in intelligence and parallel reasoning.
