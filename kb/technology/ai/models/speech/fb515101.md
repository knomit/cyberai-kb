# Mistral Voxtral 3 speech models and voice agent SDK

Mistral released Voxtral 3: speech-to-text and text-to-speech in 50+ languages, including Small Transcribe 3 (multilingual transcription) and Mini TTS 3 (emotional speech), plus a voice agent SDK combining real-time listening, reasoning and speaking over phone or web.
