# Google released Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, the model behind Pixel's Rambler

On August 26, 2026, Google introduced Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, described as its most precise speech-to-text model, converting raw audio into polished, formatted text. It is the model powering the Rambler dictation feature on Android (via Gboard) and the Gemini app on macOS, with Chrome support coming. It ships as two APIs: gemini-3.5-transcribe-live for continuous bidirectional real-time interaction with sub-second latency, and gemini-3.5-transcribe for pre-recorded audio with speaker attribution and word-level timestamps. It can delegate complex tasks to other Gemini models via function calls, recognizes speaker-specific jargon and spellings, handles disfluencies and self-corrections, and automatically transcribes over 85 languages. Google reports a 70% improvement over its prior transcription model (Chirp 3) on time-to-final-transcription per Artificial Analysis benchmarks, plus gains on the multilingual FLEURS benchmark. Available via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.
