# Microsoft MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming tops Artificial Analysis WER leaderboard; MAI-Voice-2.1 launched

Microsoft unveiled (Thursday 2026-10-01) its first streaming transcription model MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming: 60 languages, text as soon as 100 ms after audio, claimed 2x faster than closest competitors, and topped the Artificial Analysis Word-Error-Rate leaderboard at launch ahead of Grok, Meta and OpenAI models. Also launched MAI-Voice-2.1 (text-to-speech, 23 languages/26 locales) and MAI-Voice-2.1-Flash (latency-optimized). The Deep View frames this as Microsoft's strategy of domain-specific in-house models (MAI-Thinking-1, MAI-Code-1.1-Flash, MAI-Image-2.6). Suno also released Speech (beta), a model generating voice and music in one track. Speed/leaderboard claims are Microsoft's/AA's at launch.
