# Liquid AI releases LFM 2.5 230M non-transformer model

Liquid AI announced Liquid Foundation Models (LFM) 2.5 230M, a 230-million-parameter model built on a non-transformer architecture using state-space and liquid neural network continuous-time formulations. Despite its compact size, Liquid AI says it matches the performance of transformer models roughly three times larger on core edge reasoning and sequence-generation benchmarks.
