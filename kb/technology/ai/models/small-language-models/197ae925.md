# Pathway's BDH-CQ is a 150-million-parameter small reasoning model

The Deep View cites Pathway's BDH-CQ, a 150-million-parameter small reasoning model, as an example of model-level efficiency gains: it reportedly costs a fraction of OpenAI's most cost-effective model (referred to as GPT 5.6 Luna) and demands less compute and energy.
