# OpenAI launches GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna

OpenAI announced GPT-5.6 with a public release on Thursday July 9 2026, expanding preview access globally. The lineup: Sol (flagship), Terra (balanced everyday model with performance competitive to GPT-5.5 while ~2x cheaper), and Luna (fast, affordable, lowest cost). Reported in TLDR AI, July 8 2026.
