# OpenAI preparing GPT-5.6 with three tiers

OpenAI has moved GPT-5.6 into a narrow preview, splitting it into three tiers named Sol, Terra, and Luna. Notable changes include a new reasoning-effort control slider and an 'ultra' mode for complex tasks. Broad access reportedly depends on US government review approvals.
