# OpenAI GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, Luna) preview to public launch

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 was split into three tiers — Sol (flagship), Terra (balanced everyday), and Luna (fast/affordable) — and first moved into a narrow preview that reportedly added a reasoning-effort control slider and an 'ultra' mode for complex tasks, with broad access said to depend on US government review approvals. It then reached public release on Thursday July 9 2026, expanding preview access globally; Terra is described as competitive with GPT-5.5 while ~2x cheaper, and Luna as the lowest-cost option. Corroborated by TLDR AI (2026-07-08) and TestingCatalog.
