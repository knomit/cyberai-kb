# Mid-2026 frontier LLM competition shifted to intelligence-per-dollar and open-weights parity, evidenced across at least six vendors

As of mid-to-late July 2026, frontier AI competition had shifted decisively from peak capability toward low cost-per-task and open-weights parity. Two independently recorded evidence sets converge on the same pattern.

EVIDENCE SET A — open-weights parity and price-as-weapon.
- Moonshot's Kimi K3 (2.8T-parameter MoE, weights promised) ranked first among open models and third overall on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index while undercutting top proprietary models on price.
- Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 explicitly used aggressive pricing at $0.26 per task, with Zuckerberg framing it as an attack on rivals' high margins, subsidized by Meta's ad revenue.
- Both models, like many launches this cycle, were trained specifically for agentic tool use.

EVIDENCE SET B — intelligence-per-dollar across vendors (per The Batch issue-365).
- DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, a small MoE model (13B active / 284B total), beat its own larger V4-Pro on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (50 vs 44) at roughly $0.03 per task, purely via re-fine-tuning; its 3-bit quantization runs in 110GB of memory.
- OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna's price 80% (to $0.20/$1.20 per 1M tokens) and Terra by 20%, citing efficiency gains.
- Google's Gemini 3.6 Flash held 50 Intelligence Index points while cutting cost per task from $0.59 to $0.50.
- Thinking Machines' Inkling Small matched roughly 40 points with under one third of its flagship's parameters.

SCOPE QUALIFIERS — load-bearing, carried from both source records.
- Benchmark parity claims are scoped to Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index at stated reasoning levels; they are not general capability claims.
- Absolute capability leadership still sat with larger models — Kimi K3 at 57 on the Intelligence Index.
- This is a pattern observed across vendors, not a single result, and it captures the pattern only: see the source facts for exact benchmark scores, prices, and their individual caveats.
