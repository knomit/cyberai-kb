# Nvidia Nemotron 3 Ultra: open-weights hybrid mamba-transformer MoE

Nvidia released Nemotron 3 Ultra, a large open-weights LLM built on a hybrid mamba-transformer mixture-of-experts architecture (550B total parameters, 55B active per token) for long-running agentic tasks. It accepts up to 1M-token context, outputs ~183 tokens/sec, and ships with weights, training data, recipes, and RL environments under the OpenMDW-1.1 license (API median price ~$0.60/$2.60 per million input/output tokens). It was pretrained on 20T tokens (15T broad + 5T higher-quality including 173B GitHub code tokens) using 4-bit NVFP4. On Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index it scored 47.7 (NVFP4) / 48.2 (full precision) — highest among U.S. open-weights models (beating Gemma 4 31B at 39.2 and gpt-oss-120b at 33.3) but behind China's Moonshot Kimi K2.6 (53.9). It was roughly 3x faster than comparable open-weights rivals. Nvidia also recently shipped the Vera CPU, RTX Spark PC chip, and Cosmos 3 world model.
