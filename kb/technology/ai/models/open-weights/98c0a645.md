# The stealth model 'Ox Alpha' was revealed to be GLM-5.3 Flash

Z.ai confirmed in August 2026 that Ox Alpha — a multimodal model released in stealth mode that quickly gained popularity on OpenRouter and other platforms — is GLM-5.3 Flash. The company released weights for the 320-billion-parameter model under an MIT license.
