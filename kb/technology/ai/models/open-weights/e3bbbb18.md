# Nvidia Nemotron 3.5 Super VL: open 120B/12B-active multimodal model

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL on 2026-10-15: open multimodal model for agentic enterprise workflows, 120B parameters with 12B active; weights and open datasets on Hugging Face. TLDR adds it scored 26.5 on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index with substantially fewer output tokens than its predecessor, and that Nvidia also introduced Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B and near-lossless NVFP4 quantized weights.
