# Thinking Machines releases Inkling, its first open model (975B MoE)

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling, its first open-weights model: a 975B-parameter mixture-of-experts architecture with 41B active parameters, multimodal reasoning, and a one-million-token context window. The model was made available for customization through Thinking Machines' Tinker product, alongside a smaller preview version.
