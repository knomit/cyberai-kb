# Reflection Beam: 501B MoE (23B active) open-weight model, behind Chinese frontier

Reflection AI announced Beam, a 501B-parameter MoE (23B active) pretrained on 23.8T tokens using 6,144 GB300 NVL72 GPUs for four weeks, then RL on ~10.5K GPUs for four weeks over ~1M environments. Scores: DeepSWE 1.1 44.4 (GLM-5.3: 61), GPQA Diamond 90.5 (91.7), MCP Atlas 78.7 (84.2). Claims comparable reasoning scores to GLM-5.2 with 3-4x less inference compute. Weights and technical report promised later in October 2026.
