# Reflection Beam: 501B sparse MoE (23B active), 23.8T tokens, open weights planned for late October 2026

Reflection AI introduced Beam, a 501B-parameter sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with 23B active parameters for coding, reasoning and agentic workloads, trained on 23.8T pretraining tokens and over 100M reinforcement-learning rollouts; open weights and developer artifacts are planned for later in October 2026 (not yet released at time of the TLDR report).
