# Gemma 4 release: E2B, E4B, 26B MoE, 31B Dense variants

Google released Gemma 4 family in late March 2026: (1) Gemma 4 E2B — 2B effective parameters MoE, runs on-device on high-end smartphones, optimized for Pixel 9 Pro; (2) Gemma 4 E4B — 4B effective MoE, targets edge servers and laptops; (3) Gemma 4 26B MoE — 26B effective parameters (sparse), matches Gemma 3 27B quality at 40% lower compute; (4) Gemma 4 31B Dense — best-in-class for open-weight models at its size, surpasses Llama 4 Scout on MMLU and HumanEval. All variants released under Gemma Terms of Service (not fully open-source). Context window: 128K tokens across all variants.
