# GLM-5.3 is a 753B-parameter MoE that ties Kimi K3 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

Z.ai's GLM-5.3 is a mixture-of-experts transformer with 753 billion total parameters and 40 billion active per token, accepting up to 1 million input tokens and emitting up to 128,000 output tokens at ~90 tokens/second. It offers adjustable reasoning (low/high/max), tool calling, structured output, streaming, and context caching. At max reasoning it scored 60 points on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index at $0.68 per task, tying open-weights leader Kimi K3 ($0.84/task) and jumping 7 points over GLM-5.2. It trails Claude Opus 5 (63), GPT-5.6 Sol (61), and Grok 4.6 (61). Notably, Z.ai achieved this solely by fine-tuning GLM-5.2 rather than training a new model or changing the architecture.
