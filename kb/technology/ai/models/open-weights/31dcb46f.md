# DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813 is a 1.6T-parameter MoE with large coding-agent gains

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813, graduating its flagship from preview. It is a mixture-of-experts transformer with 1.6 trillion total parameters and 49 billion active per token (1.7T with the optional DSpark speculative decoding module), accepting up to 1 million input tokens and emitting up to 384,000 at 78.1 tokens/second, under an MIT license. It scored 53 on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index at $0.25/task — third among open-weights models, up eight points from the April preview, and one point above DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731. In DeepSeek's own tests (max reasoning, minimal harness) coding-agent gains were largest: Terminal-Bench 2.1 rose 72.1%→87.9%, DeepSWE 12.8%→62.7%, and CyberGym 52.7%→83.3%. Independent harnesses measured lower Terminal-Bench 2.1 numbers (Vals AI 54.68; Artificial Analysis 78.7% via Terminus 2), illustrating how much harness choice moves agentic scores.
