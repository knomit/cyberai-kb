# MiniMax M3.1 Flash: ~3x faster inference, 70% lower cost, weights later

MiniMax announced M3.1 Flash (2026-10-19/20), claiming a generational leap in agentic tasks, coding, Computer Use and cybersecurity; online inference is nearly 3x faster and usage cost 70% lower than the prior version. Available via MiniMax API, M Plan and MiniMax Code. Weights are not yet released; MiniMax says they will be released at a later date. The Deep View reports a 1M-token context window and pricing of $0.15/M input, $0.60/M output, under $0.02/M cached input (vendor claims, not independently verified).
