# Moonshot launches Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter multimodal model

Moonshot AI launched Kimi K3 on 16 July 2026: a 2.8-trillion-parameter multimodal model with a 1-million-token context window, optimized for faster long-context decoding and agentic coding. Available through Kimi's products and API, with open weights scheduled for release on 27 July 2026. It is Kimi's most capable model to date.
