# Thinking Labs (Mira Murati) releases Inkling, a US open-weight generalist model

On 15 July 2026, Thinking Labs - the AI startup founded by ex-OpenAI CTO Mira Murati - launched its first model, Inkling, an open-weight generalist model reasoning over text, images, and audio. Specs: Mixture-of-Experts transformer with 975 billion total parameters and 41 billion active; context window up to 1 million tokens; pretrained on 45 trillion tokens of text, images, audio and video. A lighter counterpart, Inkling-Small, has 12 billion active parameters. Fine-tuning is offered via Tinker, the company's training API platform. 'Controllable thinking effort' lets users tune the cost/performance curve. Benchmarks are consistent across specialties but not best-in-class - Chinese open-weight models GLM 5.2, DeepSeek V4 Pro, and Kimi K2.6 reportedly outperform it.
