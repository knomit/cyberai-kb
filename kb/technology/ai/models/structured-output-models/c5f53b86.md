# Typesafe introduced 'System One Models' and Jev, claiming two orders of magnitude speed gain and no hallucination

Typesafe announced Jev, which it positions as a 'System One Model' — a new class of frontier model built to make fast, structured decisions that software can consume directly. Typesafe claims Jev achieves intelligence on System One tasks comparable to existing large language models while being two orders of magnitude faster and more efficient, is optimized for structured outputs, and cannot hallucinate. Jev is available in early access. The 'cannot hallucinate' claim is the vendor's own and rests on constraining outputs to a structured space; it is not an independently verified property.
