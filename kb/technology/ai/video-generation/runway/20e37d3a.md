# Runway debuted Runway Ads, an AI agent for paid ad campaigns

At the Runway AI Summit (Wednesday before 2026-10-04) Runway introduced Runway Ads, a product for generating and optimizing paid ad campaigns. Per co-CEO Cristóbal Valenzuela (interview with The Deep View), it is an agent that creates end-to-end ads, edits and publishes them, and tracks performance; he describes ads as a first stepping stone toward pixel-based agent interaction. Does not mean Runway has abandoned creative/film use cases.
