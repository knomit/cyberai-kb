# Google releases Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash-Lite text-to-speech models

Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS on September 24, 2026. The models let developers design and direct voices via text description and control pacing, dialect, and delivery line-by-line; Flash-Lite targets high-volume use cases like dubbing and voice agents, while Flash can replicate an authorized voice from a 30-second sample. Google describes them as its most expressive audio-generation models yet.
