# Forecasters have systematically underestimated AI benchmark progress

A review of AI progress forecasts found that forecasters have dramatically underestimated AI progress on benchmarks, and predictions about AI adoption and diffusion also tend to lean toward underestimation; there is not yet enough evidence to assess forecasters' track record on predicting the macro-scale economic and societal impacts of AI.
