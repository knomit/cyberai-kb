# Q2D-Web benchmarks first-stage web-search retrievers over 190M documents in ten languages

Perplexity released Q2D-Web, a large-scale benchmark and leaderboard for evaluating first-stage retrieval models on web search: 190 million documents and 69,721 queries across ten languages. It uses three independent sets of relevance judgments to reduce annotation bias, and processes evaluation submissions via subsampling to cut compute cost while preserving model ranking integrity.

The design addresses a specific gap: most retrieval benchmarks are too small to expose how first-stage retrievers behave against web-scale distractor sets. Scope note — it evaluates FIRST-STAGE retrieval specifically, not end-to-end search quality or reranking.
