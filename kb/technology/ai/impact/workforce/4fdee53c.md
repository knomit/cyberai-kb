# CodeSignal survey: 91% of engineers use AI coding tools, 34% say it replaced junior tasks

CodeSignal (technical assessment platform) published March 2026 survey of 2,400 software engineers: 91% use AI coding tools at least weekly (up from 67% in 2024), 58% say AI writes first drafts of most of their code, 34% say AI has fully replaced tasks previously done by junior engineers on their team, 41% say their team hired fewer junior engineers in 2025 vs 2024 due to AI. Top tools: GitHub Copilot (54%), Cursor (38%), Claude Code (31%), Codeium (18%). 67% believe AI will make senior engineers more productive but not reduce senior headcount in next 3 years.
