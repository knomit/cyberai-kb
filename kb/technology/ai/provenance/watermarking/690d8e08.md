# SynthID cited as widely adopted AI-content watermark standard

The Deep View (Oct 2026) reports, citing Google DeepMind's Jeff Chang, that SynthID is one of the most widely adopted standards for detecting AI-generated content, adopted by OpenAI, Anthropic and ElevenLabs. Adoption claim comes from Google DeepMind via the newsletter; not every music model supports it, so absence of a watermark does not prove content is human-made.
