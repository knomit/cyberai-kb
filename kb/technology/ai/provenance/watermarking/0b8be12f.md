# Modulate API detects AI-generated music without watermarks (~95% accurate)

Per The Deep View (Oct 2026), Modulate released in July 2026 an API for detecting AI-generated music that does not rely on watermarks, claimed ~95% accurate. It targets whether vocals/instrumentals were synthesized by an AI model (not merely digitally altered). Rationale from CEO Mike Pappas: not every generation model honors watermark standards such as Google DeepMind's SynthID, and watermarks can be worked around. Accuracy figure is a vendor claim reported by the newsletter.
