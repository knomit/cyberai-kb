# Full Claude Platform on Google Cloud; Agent Skills donated to Agentic AI Foundation

Claude Platform features are now available on Google Cloud with Google Cloud authentication, billing and commitment retirement. Separately Anthropic donated its Agent Skills open standard to the Agentic AI Foundation, placing development under vendor-neutral governance.
