# Agentic AI crosses into consumer hands: Perplexity Computer and Galaxy S26 mark the tipping point

February/March 2026 marks the moment agentic AI left the enterprise/developer sandbox and entered consumer hands. Two simultaneous signals: (1) Perplexity Computer — first practical multi-model agent orchestrator for consumers, routing tasks across 19 specialist models (Claude for coding, Gemini for research, Grok for speed), running for hours in a secure sandbox on a $200/month subscription; (2) Samsung Galaxy S26 — first agentic feature to successfully ship on a consumer smartphone (Gemini task automation), after multiple failed attempts including Rabbit R1. The enabling layer: Nvidia's +75% data center revenue to $62.3B confirms the compute investment required to power these consumer agentic products is scaling rapidly. Pattern: agentic AI is no longer just enterprise software — it is being productized and distributed to mainstream consumers.
