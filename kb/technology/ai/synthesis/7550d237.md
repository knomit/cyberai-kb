# Agentic AI has become the central competitive battleground across every major tech player in 2026

By late April 2026, every major AI and tech company has converged on agentic AI as the primary product and competitive focus. Evidence: OpenAI released GPT-5.5 (built via recursive self-improvement for agentic workflows) and launched ChatGPT Workspace Agents for enterprise teams; Google launched Gemini Enterprise (end-to-end agent platform on Vertex AI) and Workspace Intelligence for contextual agentic workflows; Anthropic launched Claude Design and Claude Code/Mythos; SpaceX/xAI struck a $60B conditional deal with Cursor to compete in coding agents; Adobe launched CX Enterprise for agentic marketing; OpenAI's Codex hit 4M users with 1M added in two weeks. The competition is simultaneously at the model layer (GPT-5.5, GLM-5.1, Mythos), the application layer (Workspace Agents, Gemini Enterprise, Claude Design), and the infrastructure layer (Vast Data, AWS Graviton, Google TPU 8t/8i).
