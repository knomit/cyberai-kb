# The AI productivity paradox: executive optimism masks a worker-level disconnect driven by poor human direction

Multiple converging signals in Apr 2026 reveal a structural gap between AI's promised and realized productivity gains. (1) 92% of executives say AI boosts productivity; 40% of workers say it saves them no time — the 'workslop' epidemic of poorly-directed AI output. (2) Andrew Ng's tiered model shows coding agents accelerate unevenly: frontend dramatically faster, research barely at all. (3) Peter Steinberger (OpenClaw founder): 'The bottleneck is no longer typing. It's thinking.' Human direction and vision remain the binding constraint. (4) Despite 50% of entry-level tech jobs predicted to be wiped out (Dario Amodei), the bottleneck remains human cognitive work: goal-setting, validation, judgment. The paradox: tools are powerful but the human-AI interaction layer is where value is being created or destroyed.
