# NVIDIA releases Alpamayo 2 Super reasoning model for autonomous vehicles

NVIDIA released Alpamayo 2 Super under a commercial license for robotaxis and autonomous vehicles. It is a reasoning model designed to handle rare driving scenarios with inspectable decisions and broad multitask capabilities.
