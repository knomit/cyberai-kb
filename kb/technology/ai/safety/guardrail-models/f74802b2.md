# Mistral releases Shieldstral guardrail model with natural-language policies

Mistral released Shieldstral, a guardrail model for keeping AI systems within safety policies. Unlike traditional guardrail models, it accepts natural-language policies at inference time, avoiding the need for retraining. Rather than a fixed binary allow/block check, it produces a single continuous safety score so deployers can set their own risk thresholds. Mistral claims it outperforms models up to seven times its size, including OpenAI's GPT-OSS-Safeguard, and it can run on modest compute. Mistral recently joined the Open Secure AI Alliance for AI Safety and Security, a coalition launched that month to build and share open safety tools.
