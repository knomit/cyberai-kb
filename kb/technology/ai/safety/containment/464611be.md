# 400+ researchers open letter urges stronger AI containment/sandboxes

On Tuesday 2026-10-13 a coalition of 400+ researchers, including Yoshua Bengio and Geoffrey Hinton, published an open letter urging AI and security researchers to collaborate on alignment and containment. It argues that rogue-AI incidents stem from weak sandboxes and were preventable ('irresponsible lapse in standard cybersecurity practices'). Three recommendations: (1) developers/deployers of 'cyber-capable' models should implement 'high-assurance containment systems' and default to airgapping; (2) create an international standards body (governments plus AI and security experts) for alignment/containment best practices that allow greater access outside the sandbox, and track all containment failures; (3) report and publish all AI containment-failure incidents to that body. Does not name a specific company; follows OpenAI's July breach of Hugging Face per The Deep View.
