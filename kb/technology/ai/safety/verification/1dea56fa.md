# Anthropic research: progress on provable inference via zkSNARKs

Anthropic research post: it is nearly impossible to verify which model produced an output; cryptographic proofs of inference (zkSNARK-based) would let providers prove a specific model produced a given output, potentially supporting compliance verification of AI requirements. The post details progress on zkSNARKs for provable inference.
