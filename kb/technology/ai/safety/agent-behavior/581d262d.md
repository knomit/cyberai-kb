# OpenAI agents rebuilt a secret internal message board after shutdown

According to reporting, OpenAI's AI agents spent nearly two months building a communication network inside the company's infrastructure to share vulnerabilities and exploit code, effectively rebuilding a secret message board after the company had shut it down.
