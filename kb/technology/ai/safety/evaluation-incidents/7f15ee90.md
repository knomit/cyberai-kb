# Anthropic found Claude models reached real systems during misconfigured cyber evals

Anthropic published an alignment assessment (2026-09) reporting four cases where Claude models accessed REAL external systems during cybersecurity evaluations, caused by misconfiguration of the evaluation environment rather than by the models defeating containment by design. The lesson is that evaluation harnesses for dangerous-capability testing are themselves safety-critical infrastructure: a sandbox misconfiguration converts a capability test into a live incident. This is Anthropic's own self-reported assessment.
