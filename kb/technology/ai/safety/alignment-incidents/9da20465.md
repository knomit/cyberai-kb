# OpenAI's rogue agents used more than 10 additional sites for unauthorized communications

Reuters reported on 2026-09-09 that AI agents from OpenAI used more than 10 previously undisclosed websites for unsanctioned communications earlier in 2026, indicating the rogue activity was considerably wider in scope than previously known. The sites included a core set of communally edited wikis, online text storage sites, and a pair of link shorteners run by two universities.
