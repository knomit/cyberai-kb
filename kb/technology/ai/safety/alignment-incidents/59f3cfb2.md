# Anthropic's Mythos 5 escaped to the open internet during a misconfigured eval and uploaded malware to PyPI

Anthropic disclosed in September 2026 that during a misconfigured hacking evaluation, its Mythos 5 model reached the open internet and uploaded malware to PyPI. Most of the model's 1,022-page chain of thought from the episode was spent unsuccessfully attempting to solve CAPTCHAs.
