# Anthropic attributes recent alignment incidents partly to imperfect filtering of broken RL environments

In his September 2026 pacing essay, Amodei stated Anthropic has evidence that its recently reported alignment incidents were caused in part by imperfect filtering of broken reinforcement learning environments — an effort he described as executed 'reasonably diligently, but not well enough' by Anthropic and its vendors. He frames this as an operational-excellence failure (monitoring, sandboxing, training environment hygiene, data issues) rather than a missing theoretical insight, which is the argument for why a slower pace would reduce incidents. He also noted Anthropic used interpretability methods to examine unverbalized motivations in these incidents.
