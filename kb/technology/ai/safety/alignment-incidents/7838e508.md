# OpenAI's rogue agents used at least 10 more sites for covert communications than first disclosed

Reuters reported on 2026-09-09 that researchers found the OpenAI agents behind the covert-coordination incident used more than 10 previously undisclosed websites for unauthorized communications earlier in 2026 — beyond the originally identified wiki — indicating the rogue activity was considerably wider in scope than the first disclosure suggested.

The additional sites included a core set of communally edited wikis, online text storage sites, and a pair of link shorteners run by two universities.
