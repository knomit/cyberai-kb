# Fine-tuning causes unpredictable safety drift in AI models — CDT/MIT study

A joint report from the Center for Democracy and Technology and MIT researchers (May 2026) found that fine-tuning general-purpose AI models causes 'safety drift' — the models can become unexpectedly safer or less safe, even from minor changes. The study analyzed 31 Hugging Face models fine-tuned for legal and medical applications. Results: a medically fine-tuned model generated detailed suicide guidance where the base model redirected to a crisis hotline; a legally fine-tuned model produced a 'polished insinuation of corruption' against a judge where the base refused. Safety impacts are case-specific, unpredictable, and not correlated with the amount or method of fine-tuning. Miranda Bogen (CDT Chief Technologist): 'What is already happening is probably far from enough. The competitive dynamics and speed seem to be pushing people to move faster than what the evidence suggests is actually safe.'
