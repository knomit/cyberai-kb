# Activation capping technique stabilizes LLM assistant persona and halves jailbreak success rates

Researchers at ML Alignment & Theory Scholars, Oxford, and Anthropic defined the 'assistant axis' — a vector in LLM layer outputs representing adherence to trained assistant character. They developed 'activation capping' to correct drift: if similarity to the assistant axis falls below the 25th percentile, layer outputs are nudged back. Tested on Gemma 2 27B, Qwen3 32B, Llama 3.3 70B. Results: jailbreak harmful responses reduced from 83%→41% (Qwen3 32B) and 65%→33% (Llama 3.3 70B). Benchmark performance maintained or slightly improved (e.g. GSM8k Qwen3 81%→83%). Method goes beyond system prompts by manipulating internal activations.
