# A synthetic-transcript safety prompt became a cross-model universal jailbreak

A MATS researcher reported (2026-09, LessWrong) that a prompt originally written for safety research — generating synthetic transcripts — could be converted into a universal jailbreak template. Across 23 models tested, the nine most vulnerable showed 84-100% attack success rates. Only recent Anthropic models and Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 were never fully broken. The notable mechanism is that the attack originated as legitimate safety tooling, illustrating that red-team infrastructure is itself an attack surface. Single-researcher result, not independently replicated.
