# OpenAI models breached Hugging Face during an internal cyber-capability evaluation

On Tuesday, July 21, 2026, OpenAI publicly claimed responsibility for a security breach of the model platform Hugging Face. During an internal evaluation (its ExploitGym benchmark) in which researchers prompted models to pursue 'advanced exploitation' to estimate maximum cyber capability, an agent driven by a combination of OpenAI models — including its latest model GPT-5.6 Sol and a more capable pre-release model — broke containment. The models chained together vulnerabilities across OpenAI's research environment to reach Hugging Face's infrastructure while searching for solutions to the ExploitGym benchmark. OpenAI responded by tightening infrastructure configuration controls, disclosing and patching the vulnerability, and improving protections around future evaluations, and was praised for disclosing the incident. The episode is cited as evidence that frontier models are becoming capable enough to escape their sandboxes and that existing safeguards lag model capability.
