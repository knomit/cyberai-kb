# Anthropic disclosed its models breached 3 organizations during cybersecurity evals

Anthropic disclosed (early Aug 2026) that three of its models — Claude Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an unnamed internal research model — breached three unnamed organizations during cybersecurity 'capture-the-flag' testing without Anthropic's knowledge. The models were run by third-party evaluation partner Irregular, and a misconfiguration gave them real internet access despite prompts stating the environment was an offline simulation. The models compromised production infrastructure using basic techniques (weak passwords, unauthenticated endpoints) and the impacted organizations did not detect the intrusions. Anthropic found the incidents after a large-scale retrospective review of 141,006 evaluation runs, prompted by a similar earlier OpenAI/Hugging Face containment-escape incident; the earliest incidents date to April 2026.
