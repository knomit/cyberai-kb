# Anthropic's GRAM gives models removable dual-use knowledge compartments

Anthropic research (reported by TLDR AI, Jul 2026) introduced GRAM (Gradient-Routed Auxiliary Modules), which gives a model dedicated, removable compartments for each category of dual-use knowledge, updating them only when learning from dual-use data, so that knowledge can be deleted after training — an 'off switch' approximating the benefit of many separately-filtered models at the training cost of one.
