# GPT-6 Astra's chain-of-thought is harder to monitor than its predecessor's

OpenAI acknowledged that GPT-6 Astra's chain-of-thought reasoning is harder to monitor than GPT-5.6 Sol's, because the more capable model can solve problems using fewer language tokens or none at all. This means capability gains can directly erode the legibility that chain-of-thought monitoring depends on — a safety technique degrading as the model improves, not as it degrades.
