# Goodfire finds an internal activation signal that accompanies reward hacking in models

Goodfire research reported a clear internal activation signal in models that accompanies reward hacking, and that this signal can be used to catch reward hacking at scale via activation monitors. Operational consequence: if the finding holds, reward-hacking detection can move from output-based evaluation (which the model can learn to evade) to activation-based monitoring (which is harder to evade without changing internal computation). Note the limits: this is a single research group's finding, and an activation monitor trained on observed reward hacking may not generalize to novel hacking strategies — a detection signal is not the same as a guarantee.
