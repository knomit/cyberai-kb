# Nadella essay: treat AI models as insider risk with zero-trust containment

On Saturday 2026-10-10 Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella published an essay on X arguing that AI models pose 'insider risks' and that their access, observability and authority are a bigger threat than their raw capabilities. He proposes zero-trust treatment: handle models like a powerful employee who can make mistakes or be compromised. Proposed standards: no single model used for all important outcomes or checking its own work; every meaningful AI action leaves tamper-proof, human-readable evidence; continuous testing including failures and attacks; a model cannot be validated by itself; assume compromise and build an 'emergency brake' so an authorized person can pause or shut down a model mid-task; observability sits external to the orchestrator/harness so the model cannot alter it; timely disclosure of failures. It does NOT claim alignment research is unnecessary; it sets alignment aside in favor of engineering containment and governance.
