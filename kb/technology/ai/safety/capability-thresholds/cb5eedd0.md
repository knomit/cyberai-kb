# OpenAI says Astra is its first model crossing the 'Critical' cybersecurity capability threshold

OpenAI says its upcoming model Astra is the first offering to cross its own 'Critical' cybersecurity capability threshold: the model can find previously unknown security flaws and exploit them without step-by-step human guidance. OpenAI plans to release it soon but will restrict access to its cybersecurity capabilities, with further detail promised in the launch System Card. Consequence: this is a self-assessed threshold crossing under OpenAI's own framework, not an independent evaluation.
