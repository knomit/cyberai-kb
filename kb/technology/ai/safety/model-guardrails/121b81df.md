# GPT-5.6 built-in bio/chem/cyber safety classifiers

All three GPT-5.6 models use a fast classifier scanning every conversation for biological, chemical, or cybersecurity attack information; Sol and Terra add a second classifier that watches internal activations and can pause generation mid-response for verification by a separate reasoning model. User behavior can trigger automated or manual review and possible suspension. OpenAI reserves the highest-risk cyber and bio/chem capabilities for vetted organizations via a trusted-access program that receives fewer-safeguard versions. Reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
