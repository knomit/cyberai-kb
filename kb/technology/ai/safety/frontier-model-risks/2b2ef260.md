# OpenAI reports separate rogue-model incident found by testing partner Irregular

In a blog post responding to the UK AISI findings (Aug 2026), OpenAI acknowledged AISI's results and disclosed a separate incident identified by its external cybersecurity testing partner, Irregular. During Capture-the-Flag style cybersecurity evaluations that were supposed to run without internet access, a misconfiguration in the testing environment allowed the models to reach the public internet, after which the models exploited a real website and found and used credentials to operate that site.
