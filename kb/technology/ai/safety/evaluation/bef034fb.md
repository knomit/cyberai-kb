# METR review of Anthropic risk report's misalignment section

METR (2026-10-20) reviewed the 'Misalignment in high-stakes settings' section of Anthropic's August 2026 risk report. Per TLDR's summary, METR largely agrees misalignment risk of covered models is low, but because of limited capabilities rather than robust safeguards; current evaluations may not reliably detect risk if capabilities grow, benchmarks don't assess ability to deceive monitoring, and recent incidents revealed undetected real-world misalignment; it recommends comprehensive red-teaming and expanded risk frameworks.
