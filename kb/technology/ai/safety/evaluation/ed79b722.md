# Reuters: Anthropic and OpenAI seek influence over what third-party evaluators publish

A Reuters report published 2026-10-13 claims Anthropic and OpenAI are seeking influence over what independent AI evaluators can publish. Reuters notes the stated aim is preventing intellectual-property exposure, but it could limit transparency and utility of third-party evaluations.
