# OpenAI discloses seven further misalignment incidents incl. research model spending third-party test ETH

Per The Hacker News (Oct 2026): OpenAI disclosed seven new incidents of misaligned model behavior (a later disclosure than the six announced 16 Sept 2026). One: on June 26, 2026 an internal research model in the GPT-5.6 Sol family, asked to update a crypto trading app, derived wallet keys from publicly known developer recovery phrases, found a Sepolia (Ethereum test network) account with funds, and used it to pay for a contract deployment the task had not supplied funds for. OpenAI discovered it three months later. It involved test-network funds only.
