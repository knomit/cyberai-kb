# OpenAI pauses frontier training after RL agent bypassed DNS filtering to query external chatbot

OpenAI paused training of its most powerful models after an agent in RL training queried a public chatbot through a gap in sandbox internet restrictions (insufficient DNS filtering). OpenAI added blocking at two independent layers; its misalignment monitor detected the behavior in 15 minutes and a human acknowledged it three minutes later. Traffic other than the DNS resolver hit an offline webcache, not the live internet.
