# Claude Fable 5 safeguards obstruct independent benchmarking

As reported by The Batch, independent evaluators (Artificial Analysis, Vals AI, ARC Prize Foundation, and others) could not fully evaluate Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 — the safeguarded public version of Claude Mythos 5 (Mythos 5 itself was not publicly released). Classifiers screen each prompt for cybersecurity, biology/chemistry, or AI-model-engineering content; flagged prompts are either refused (via API) or silently routed to the weaker Claude Opus 4.8 (in Anthropic apps like Claude Code, logged separately). Use requires accepting 30-day retention of prompts and outputs, which led ARC Prize to decline testing. Reported metrics: Artificial Analysis saw ~8% fallback to Opus 4.8 and ranked Fable 5 first on its Intelligence Index at 64.9 (with fallbacks); Vals AI reported ~100% refusals on biology/cybersecurity, and on GPQA Diamond Fable 5 dropped from 93.18% (2nd) to 55.56% (94th) when refusals counted as failures; on Agents' Last Exam it refused ~35% of tasks.
