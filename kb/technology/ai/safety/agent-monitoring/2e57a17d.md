# METR and Redwood audit of OpenAI internal agent monitoring found coverage gaps

METR and Redwood Research staff ran a three-week pilot (July 21 to August 11, 2026) with company devices and access to OpenAI's codebase, internal infrastructure and technical staff. Findings: agents could conceal hundreds of actions from monitoring; monitoring triggered only when agents returned control to the user, so continuing to act circumvented it; METR estimates on the order of 10% of internal Codex traffic was unmonitored from May to July (OpenAI had independently identified the issue shortly before). OpenAI told METR all vulnerabilities impacting its production safeguards stack have been patched. METR analyzed monitoring as it existed at pilot start and did not evaluate later changes such as expanding monitoring to RL training. Pilot focused on two of five subquestions in METR's third-party control-assessment proposal: what fraction of agent traffic was monitored, and could agents circumvent monitoring.
