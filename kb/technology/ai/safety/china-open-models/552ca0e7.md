# SemiAnalysis: only ~3.6% of 857 Chinese lab model releases came with safety reports

SemiAnalysis report (published ~2026-10-08) analysed 857 model releases (741 product, 116 research) from nine Chinese labs (ByteDance, Alibaba, Tencent, Baidu, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Zhipu Z.ai, MiniMax, StepFun) between Sept 2021 and Sept 2026. Only 31 (~3.6%) had safety reports and only 9 of those were available at or before release. Alibaba shipped the most models (238) with 7 safety reports; Zhipu has published 9 reports across 131 models and is the only lab with a strategic safety commitment; DeepSeek 6 of 93; StepFun 3 of 39; Moonshot 1 of 34; MiniMax 1 of 20. The report did not analyse US labs. Implication: enterprises adopting Chinese open-weight models for cost carry the safety burden themselves.
