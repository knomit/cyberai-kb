# Paradigm Research published a browser-based recursive self-improvement simulator

Paradigm Research released the RSI Simulator, a browser-based idle/strategy game that simulates running a frontier AI company through the onset of recursive self-improvement, forcing tradeoffs between investing in researchers versus compute and deciding when to license data. It is intended as an intuition-building tool for reasoning about RSI dynamics.
