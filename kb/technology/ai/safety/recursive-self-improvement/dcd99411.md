# Anthropic reports ~80% of its code authored by Claude, reviving RSI debate

In a mid-2026 blog post, Anthropic stated that about 80% of its code is now authored by Claude (up from under 5% before Claude Code's preview), and that the trend points toward systems that 'design and refine themselves' — thrusting recursive self-improvement (RSI) into public debate. Anthropic measured each engineer contributing ~8x more lines of code in Q2 2026 than in Q1 2023, and rising solve rates for trivial-to-open-ended coding tasks. Anthropic laid out three future scenarios (AI stays below top humans; AI-aided engineering accelerates with humans in control — judged most likely; or AI self-improves) and revived the idea of a temporary global research pause. Reactions ranged from skeptical (Miles Brundage, Arun Rao, compute/data bottleneck arguments) to bullish (OpenAI citing 'early signs of RSI'; Sakana AI launching an RSI Lab).
