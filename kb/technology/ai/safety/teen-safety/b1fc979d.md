# Common Sense Media report: ChatGPT for Teens safeguards fall short; OpenAI disputes

Common Sense Media's Youth AI Safety Institute (report released 2026-10-07) tested 4,000+ prompts on teen-registered (13-17) ChatGPT accounts reviewed by child psychiatrists and claims: no parental alerts in hour-long sensitive conversations, >25% of crisis situations missed for referral, age estimation did not switch adult-registered accounts to teen mode. OpenAI disputes this, saying testing may have ended before parental controls activated, and says teens average under 15 minutes/day, 2% over three consecutive hours. Contested findings, not established fact.
