# Anthropic's Sept 2026 threat report covers Claude misuse disruptions from Dec 2025 to Aug 2026

Anthropic's Threat Intelligence team published a September 2026 report with case studies of operations it identified and disrupted where threat actors attempted to use Claude for malicious activity, covering disruptions between December 2025 and August 2026 and tracing how misuse patterns evolved since prior reports. That absence reflects the observed caseload during the reporting window, not a claim that those model classes are immune.
