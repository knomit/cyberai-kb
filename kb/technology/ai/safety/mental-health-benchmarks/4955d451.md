# OpenAI released MentalHealthBench, a benchmark for AI mental-health support quality

On September 23, 2026, OpenAI unveiled MentalHealthBench, an open benchmark evaluating AI model capabilities in safety, seeking user context, preserving user agency, and providing actionable guidance for mental-health-related conversations. It was built with synthetic conversations spanning severity from non-acute to emergency, evaluated by 80 mental health professionals across 22 countries, 19 languages, and 20 subspecialties across 10 dimensions. In OpenAI's own testing, its Astra model scored highest overall (57.8%), followed by GPT-6 Sol (54%), Luna (50.3%), and Claude Opus 5 (48.1%); all tested models scored relatively better on clinical accuracy, empathy, and reality-testing than on gathering context and supporting user agency.
