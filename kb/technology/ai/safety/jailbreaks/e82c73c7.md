# A synthetic-transcript safety-research prompt became a cross-model universal jailbreak

A MATS researcher reported in September 2026 on LessWrong that a prompt originally written for safety research — generating synthetic transcripts — could be converted into a universal jailbreak template that transfers across vendors. Across 23 models tested, the nine most vulnerable showed 84-100% attack success rates. Only recent Anthropic models and Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 were never fully broken.

The notable mechanism is that the attack originated as legitimate safety tooling: an artifact built for red-teaming generalized into an attack, illustrating that red-team infrastructure is itself an attack surface.

Scope caveat: this is a single-researcher result and has not been independently replicated.
