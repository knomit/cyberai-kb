# Pachocki says capabilities are advancing faster than understanding of them

In the 2026-09-08 Navier-Stokes briefing, OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki said: 'This pace of progress is something to be taken very seriously. Even a week ago, we definitely were not expecting we'd be talking about a solution to Navier-Stokes today. It's a serious moment. We're developing these systems, and their capabilities are advancing faster than our understanding of them in some sense.' He also framed the effort as a stress test of a system OpenAI has been training for general-purpose intelligence. The datapoint worth carrying: OpenAI's own chief scientist reports a one-week forecasting horizon failure on his own lab's system — the people closest to the training run did not anticipate the result. This is a statement about internal predictive capability, not a claim that the model is uncontrolled. It follows Pachocki's statement to The Deep View a week earlier that a pause and cross-lab coordination may be necessary.
