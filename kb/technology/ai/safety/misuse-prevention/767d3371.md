# Anthropic threat report says it blocked bioweapons-adjacent research attempts

On Thursday 2026-09-10, Anthropic published a threat detection report stating it thwarted several instances of users conducting research that could have helped develop biological weapons. The report is Anthropic's own account of misuse it detected and blocked on its own platform; there is no independent verification of the number of attempts, their severity, or how close any came to succeeding. It is evidence that platform-side misuse monitoring is catching CBRN-adjacent queries, not evidence about the base rate of such attempts across the industry.
