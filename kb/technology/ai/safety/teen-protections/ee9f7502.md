# ChatGPT for Teens: new College Planner/study tools and detailed Common Sense Media test findings

OpenAI announced for ChatGPT for Teens (13-17 or age-predicted minors): forthcoming College Planner (US grades 10-12, four-year colleges), flashcards, note-to-quiz tools, Study Mode/Study Hours; teens average under 15 min/day; ~1.2M teens used visualizations and >180K used Study Mode in one week. Common Sense Media's Youth AI Safety Institute rated it 'Unacceptable Risk' after >4,000 prompts (pre-launch Jul 13-Aug 17; post Aug 25-Sep 28): >1 in 4 warranted crisis referrals missed; hotline mentions on suicide/self-harm prompts 88%->77%; depression prompts 63%->3%; follow-up questions after launch 13%; Study Mode bypass finished every assignment; adult accounts never switched to teen mode. OpenAI disputes, citing parental controls possibly not fully activated, and asked for a retest.
