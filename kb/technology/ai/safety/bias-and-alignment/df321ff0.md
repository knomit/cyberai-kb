# Study: LLMs absorb state-media bias, stronger in controlled-media languages

A study by Hannah Waight, Eddie Yang, and colleagues (University of Oregon, Purdue, UC San Diego, NYU, Princeton) found that government-associated media is widespread in LLM training data and shifts model outputs. When prompted in Chinese, Anthropic's Claude 3 Sonnet and OpenAI's GPT-4o expressed more favorable attitudes toward the Chinese government than when prompted in English (judged more favorable to China ~75% of the time on China-related prompts). State media accounted for >40x the document count of Chinese Wikipedia in the CulturaX Chinese web subset, and models near-reproduced the most-repeated state-media strings ~3–5% of the time. Across many languages, countries with stronger media control (per the World Press Freedom Index) showed significantly more favorable bias in their languages versus English.
