# Thinking Machines details a safety methodology for releasing its 'Inkling' open-weight model

AI startup Thinking Machines released the methodology behind its open-weight model 'Inkling': internal evaluations across a broad harm taxonomy (including CBRN and offensive cybersecurity and multimodal tests in 17 languages); external testing by four independent organizations (Scale AI for general misuse, Handshake AI for vulnerable-user interaction, FAR.AI for CBRN/cyber, Apollo Research for loss-of-control); and a fine-tuning study producing 'helpful-only' variants that did not provide new CBRN/cyber uplift. It is exploring pre-training data filtering and staged/iterative deployment of open models.
