# NeuralTrust - AI cybersecurity platform providing LLM firewall layer at MWC 2026

NeuralTrust is an EU-backed cybersecurity platform for enterprise AI providing a firewall-like layer to detect: sensitive data leakage, prompt injections, hallucinations, and other LLM security risks. Founded approximately 2024. Demonstrated at MWC 2026 4YFN startup showcase. Represents the emerging category of security tooling designed specifically for AI/LLM enterprise deployments. Other MWC startup highlights: Ultralytics (open-source computer vision, 125K GitHub stars, 500 clients, 2.5B inferences), Biorce (AI clinical trials, $52M seed round — one of largest in Spanish startup history), Medwise AI (AI search for doctors, co-founder Keith Tsui: 'unwise to build your own model').
