# NewsGuard Audit: Claude Cites Kremlin Propaganda Sources 15% of Time on Pro-Russian Topics (Up from 4%)

NewsGuard audited Anthropic's Claude and found it repeated false claims 15% of the time when asked about pro-Kremlin falsehoods in a 'typical user' voice, citing Russian state-affiliated media every time — up from only 4% previously. Additionally, since the start of the U.S.-Iran war, Claude cited Iranian state-affiliated media in one case when prompted on pro-Iran false claims. NewsGuard: 'Claude has become more vulnerable to state disinformation campaigns in recent months.'
