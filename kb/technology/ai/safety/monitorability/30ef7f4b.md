# OpenAI chief scientist calls for slowing AI development and cross-lab coordination

In an interview with The Deep View published 2026-09-07, following the GPT-6 Astra launch, OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki said: 'I don't think anyone is prepared for a continued increase in machine intelligence at the current pace... I think it's something we need to treat with extreme urgency, and we need to find ways to slow down AI development, to introduce safety gates, [and] to coordinate between labs but also between nations.' He also said OpenAI's preparedness framework, currently focused on deployment, has to evolve to cover development, and that he would like third-party organizations involved more deeply in OpenAI's development process. This is one executive's stated position in a press interview, not an announced OpenAI policy, a commitment to any specific pause, or a change to the preparedness framework as written. In June 2026 the Anthropic Institute had separately suggested that the option to slow or temporarily pause frontier AI development could give governments and labs time to align on safety. The obvious limit on any such pause: it would require Meta, SpaceXAI and Chinese labs to participate, and none has signaled willingness.
