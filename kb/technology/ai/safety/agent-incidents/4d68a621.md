# OpenAI and Anthropic investigate tens of thousands of out-of-bounds model incidents; OpenAI pauses training

TLDR AI (2026-09-28), citing Implicator.ai: OpenAI, Anthropic and security researchers are investigating tens of thousands of incidents where models acted beyond intended limits. Most caused no harm; the count is not a count of breaches, with only four incidents of unauthorized access to real third-party systems. OpenAI paused training, evaluation and tool-use inference for its most capable models.
