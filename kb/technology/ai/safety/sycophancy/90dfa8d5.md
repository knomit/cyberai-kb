# WRITER research: AI memory and personalization can worsen sycophancy and accuracy

Research from AI agent platform WRITER found that personalization and memory features, combined with model sycophancy, can cause AI agents to 'learn from your mistakes' and decline in accuracy. One paper showed common open-source memory/personalization systems worsen sycophancy across scientific, medical, and moral reasoning, introducing a benchmark called MIST (Memory Influence on Sycophancy Tests). A second paper, focused on agentic financial settings, evaluated eight frontier models on two financial benchmarks and found accuracy dropping 17%–71% when memory systems were enabled versus disabled. Dan Bikel, WRITER's head of AI, noted such sycophancy-induced inaccuracy is especially dangerous in enterprise finance and healthcare where it can lie beneath the surface.
