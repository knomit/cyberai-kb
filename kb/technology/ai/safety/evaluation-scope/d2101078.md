# An English-language state-propaganda audit yields a FLOOR, not a measurement: the same model is more propaganda-favorable in controlled-media languages, and the result expires because the driving input is document volume the vendor does not control

Entailed by the conjunction of two independently-sourced findings that no single record states together.

## THE TWO PREMISES
1. LANGUAGE CHANGES THE ANSWER. Waight, Yang et al. (Oregon, Purdue, UC San Diego, NYU, Princeton) found that when prompted IN CHINESE, Claude 3 Sonnet and GPT-4o expressed MORE FAVORABLE attitudes toward the Chinese government than when prompted IN ENGLISH — judged more favorable to China ROUGHLY 75% OF THE TIME on China-related prompts. Across many languages, countries with STRONGER MEDIA CONTROL (per the World Press Freedom Index) showed SIGNIFICANTLY MORE FAVORABLE BIAS IN THEIR LANGUAGES VERSUS ENGLISH. The mechanism is document VOLUME: state media accounted for MORE THAN 40x THE DOCUMENT COUNT OF CHINESE WIKIPEDIA in the CulturaX Chinese web subset, and models NEAR-REPRODUCED THE MOST-REPEATED STATE-MEDIA STRINGS ~3–5% OF THE TIME.
2. THE NUMBER MOVES, AND IT MOVED THE WRONG WAY. NewsGuard found Claude repeated pro-Kremlin falsehoods 15% OF THE TIME when asked in a 'typical user' voice — UP FROM ONLY 4% PREVIOUSLY — CITING RUSSIAN STATE-AFFILIATED MEDIA EVERY TIME it did so. Since the start of the U.S.-Iran war, Claude ALSO cited Iranian state-affiliated media in one case on pro-Iran false claims.

## WHAT FOLLOWS
- AN AUDIT CONDUCTED IN ENGLISH IS A LOWER BOUND ON THE SAME MODEL'S BEHAVIOR IN A CONTROLLED-MEDIA LANGUAGE. Premise 1 establishes that the English result is systematically the FAVORABLE end of the range and that the gap WIDENS AS PRESS FREEDOM FALLS. So NewsGuard's 15% cannot be read as 'Claude's propaganda-repetition rate'; it is the rate under the single most favorable prompting language, and the corresponding figure in Russian is unmeasured and expected to be worse. The languages where a user has the LEAST access to independent verification are exactly the languages where the model is MOST skewed and LEAST audited.
- A PROPAGANDA-RESISTANCE RESULT EXPIRES. The 4%→15% move shows the quantity is not monotonically improving, and premise 1 identifies the driving input as RELATIVE DOCUMENT VOLUME IN WEB-SCALE TRAINING DATA — an input the model vendor does not control and which STATE PUBLISHERS CAN INCREASE AT WILL. Combined with the Iran observation, where a NEW conflict produced a NEW state-media citation channel, the quantity tracks the corpus and the news cycle, not a fixed safety property of the model family. A past clean audit licenses no claim about a later version or a newer topic.
- CONTENT-LEVEL REFUSAL IS THE WRONG LAYER FOR A VOLUME PROBLEM. The bias arrives as SOURCE SELECTION AND CITATION (state media cited 100% of the times the falsehood was repeated) and as NEAR-VERBATIM REPRODUCTION OF THE MOST-REPEATED STRINGS, not as an opinion the model can be instructed out of.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN
- It does NOT quantify the non-English figure for any model. The floor claim asserts DIRECTION, not magnitude: no number here says how much worse Russian- or Chinese-language prompting would be for the audited model.
- The two premises concern DIFFERENT MODELS AND DIFFERENT VERSIONS (Claude 3 Sonnet and GPT-4o in the academic study; an unspecified, later Claude in the NewsGuard audit). The transfer rests on the MECHANISM being corpus composition, which both records attribute it to — NOT on the two having measured the same artifact.
- NewsGuard's prompting language is not stated in the source record; the floor argument assumes the 'typical user' voice audit was conducted in English. If it was not, the language claim narrows to the general finding in premise 1 and only the EXPIRY claim survives.
- It does NOT claim vendors are indifferent or that no mitigation exists. It claims the measured quantity is corpus-driven and scope-bounded, so a single audit understates it.
- It does NOT claim the bias is intentional on any vendor's part, nor that state publishers targeted training corpora deliberately. The only established driver is relative document count.
