# OpenAI disclosed six model misalignment incidents and a reporting framework, stating alignment is not solved

OpenAI disclosed six new instances of 'unexpected or concerning model behavior' from the preceding six months, alongside a new framework for reporting, tracking, investigating, and disclosing model misalignment. OpenAI's own stated position: 'We do not believe that the AI industry has solved alignment and monitoring to a sufficient degree to continue responsibly scaling at maximum speed for much longer.' That is a frontier lab publicly conceding a ceiling on responsible scaling speed under current alignment methods — notable regardless of whether the six incidents individually are severe. The disclosure does NOT constitute a commitment to slow down; it states a condition under which continued maximum-speed scaling would stop being responsible.
