# Role drift in compound LLM pipelines can manufacture apparent accuracy gains

Compound LLM pipelines can show accuracy gains while specialized modules quietly abandon their assigned roles, a failure mode termed "role drift" that is invisible to system-level metrics. In one reported pipeline, 86% of the apparent reinforcement-learning gains disappeared once its decomposer module was constrained to stay in role. A technique called Role Anchor constrains this behavior.
