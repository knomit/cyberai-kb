# AMD Helios emerges as rack-scale rival to Nvidia Vera Rubin with hyperscaler backing

AMD's Helios rack-scale AI system launched as the first major alternative to Nvidia's Vera Rubin for training and running frontier AI models. Helios integrates AMD CPUs, GPUs, Pensando NICs, and ROCm software into one platform. On Monday (week of July 21, 2026) Microsoft signed on to deploy the full-stack Helios on Azure; AMD also lists Meta, OpenAI, Oracle, and Tata as partners. Per the Futurum Group, Helios costs roughly $5–5.5 million versus $3.5–4 million for Vera Rubin, but promises greater operating efficiency. Nvidia has reportedly pre-sold its next 12 months of product; the market is supply-constrained rather than zero-sum.
