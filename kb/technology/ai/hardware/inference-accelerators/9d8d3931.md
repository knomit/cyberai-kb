# Google TPUv7 Ironwood is the first TPU sold for external inference workloads

SemiAnalysis' September 2026 analysis reports Google's TPUv7 Ironwood delivering up to 50% better performance per dollar than Nvidia's B200/B300. The structural change is not the number: Ironwood is the first TPU generation Google is externalizing to compete for other companies' inference workloads, available for outright purchase as well as rental through Google Cloud — previous TPU generations were effectively internal or cloud-rental-only. The analysis argues Google's software engineering depth means the external TPU software stack should mature quickly, which is historically the binding constraint on TPU adoption rather than silicon performance.
