# Google TPUv7 Ironwood is the first TPU sold for external inference workloads

SemiAnalysis' September 2026 analysis reports Google's TPUv7 Ironwood delivering up to 50% better performance per dollar than Nvidia's B200/B300.

THE STRUCTURAL CHANGE IS NOT THE NUMBER: Ironwood is the first TPU generation Google is externalizing to compete for other companies' inference workloads, available for outright PURCHASE as well as rental through Google Cloud. Previous TPU generations were effectively internal or cloud-rental-only, so this reverses Google's historical posture of reserving TPUs for internal and Cloud-managed use.

The analysis argues Google's software engineering depth means the external TPU software stack should mature quickly — historically the binding constraint on TPU adoption has been the software stack rather than silicon performance. That argument is SemiAnalysis' judgement, not a measurement.
