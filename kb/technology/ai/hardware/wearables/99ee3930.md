# Google AI Glasses (Samsung + Warby Parker + Gentle Monster): In-Lens Display, Fall 2026 Launch

At Google I/O May 20, 2026, Google revealed its finished AI smart glasses in partnership with Samsung (hardware), Warby Parker (classic styling), and Gentle Monster (bold/daring styling). Key capabilities:

- **Navigation**: Voice-requested, with turn-by-turn directions in in-lens display; nearby recommendations; order placement
- **Notifications**: Summarized texts and more from paired phone
- **Real-time translation**: Audio matches speaker's voice in target language; text-in-view translation
- **Photo capture**: Hands-free, voice-activated
- **Gemini at the core**: All features powered by Gemini

Competitive positioning: The in-lens display is the key differentiator over Meta Ray-Bans (which lack displays and are primarily valued for hands-free content capture). Google's Android ecosystem enables deep phone integration — maps, translation, notifications, camera — that Meta cannot match.

Timeline: Fall 2026 launch expected. Full product details, pricing, and specifications to come in coming months. Prototypes tested for comfort; privacy remains the main adoption barrier cited by journalists.

Jay Kim (Samsung): 'This intelligent eyewear marks an important step in Samsung's vision for AI. With this new AI form factor, we are further expanding the Galaxy device ecosystem.'
