# Huawei Ascend 910C delivers ~60% of Nvidia H100 inference performance per DeepSeek research

Research from DeepSeek indicates Huawei's Ascend 910C delivers approximately 60% of Nvidia H100 inference performance. Related reporting puts Huawei's DeepSeek all-in-one machine at 60–70% of H100 performance, and the Ascend 910D at ~60% of H100 TRAINING performance at the system level, described as sufficient for domestic needs.

Raw compute comparison (910D vs H100): H100 delivers ~2.8x higher FP16 and ~2.6x higher BF16, but Huawei's dual-chiplet design enables competitive INT8 throughput for inference specifically. The gap is therefore workload-dependent — narrowest on INT8 inference, widest on floating-point training.

CAVEAT: the ~60% figure is against the H100, a 2022-generation Nvidia part. It is not a measure of parity with contemporaneous Nvidia hardware.
