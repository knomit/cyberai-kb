# OpenAI unveils Jalapeño inference chip with Broadcom

OpenAI announced Jalapeño, its first-generation 'intelligence processor' for LLM inference, developed with Broadcom over nine months and marking the first chip in a broader joint compute platform. Designed to limit data movement while balancing compute, memory, and networking, it is intended to work with all LLMs; OpenAI says early testing shows performance-per-watt 'substantially better than current state-of-the-art,' and engineering samples are already running ML workloads including GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark. It follows OpenAI abandoning earlier chip-fab plans in 2024 and its October 2025 Broadcom partnership aiming to deploy 10 gigawatts of custom AI accelerators. President Greg Brockman framed it as part of a full-stack infrastructure strategy to make compute more abundant.
