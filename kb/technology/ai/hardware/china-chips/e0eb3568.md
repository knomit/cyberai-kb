# DeepSeek Huawei-chip training claim gets published benchmarks

A Huawei-led consortium released a technical report documenting full-parameter post-training of a model on Huawei chips, providing benchmarks for DeepSeek's earlier claim of training on Huawei hardware; the report drew skepticism from some observers.
