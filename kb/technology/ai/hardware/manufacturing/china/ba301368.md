# Ascend manufacturing is yield-constrained: SMIC 7nm DUV at ~40-50% yield vs TSMC's ~90% for H100

The Huawei Ascend 910D is fabricated on SMIC's 7nm N+2 DUV process, versus Nvidia's H100 on TSMC 4N (5nm EUV). Estimated Ascend yield is 40–50%, against roughly 90% for the H100. The constraint traces to U.S. export bans on EUV lithography equipment, which SMIC cannot obtain.

SIGNIFICANCE: yield is a volume constraint as much as a performance one. Even if per-chip performance closed, halved yield on a less advanced node caps how much domestic accelerator capacity China can field, and raises effective cost per delivered chip. Analyses focused solely on per-chip throughput parity miss this.

SOURCE QUALITY: yield figures are third-party estimates from analyst commentary, not disclosed by SMIC or Huawei; treat as directional.
