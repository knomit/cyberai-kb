# Study found Google AI Overviews inaccurate ~9% of the time

A study published in April 2026 found that Google's AI Overviews are correct about 91% of the time, with an inaccuracy rate of roughly 9%. Given AI Overviews' scale, that error rate translates to millions of incorrect query responses per day.
