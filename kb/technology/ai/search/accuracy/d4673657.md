# NYT/Oumi study pegs Google AI Overviews at ~90% accuracy

Per an NYT-commissioned analysis by AI startup Oumi (reported by The Deep View, Jul 10, 2026), Google AI Overviews were accurate ~90% of the time across 4,326 queries evaluated on OpenAI's SimpleQA benchmark (Gemini 2: 85%, Gemini 3: 91%). Google processes 5+ trillion searches annually and AI Overviews surpassed 2 billion monthly users (per Sundar Pichai). Google disputed the methodology, arguing SimpleQA contains errors and using one AI to grade another is flawed.
