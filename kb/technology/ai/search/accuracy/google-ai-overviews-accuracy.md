# April 2026 study: Google AI Overviews ~90-91% accurate

An April 2026 analysis (commissioned by The New York Times from AI startup Oumi; also covered by Ars Technica and The Deep View) found Google AI Overviews were accurate about 90-91% of the time, i.e. wrong roughly 9-10% of the time. The study evaluated 4,326 Google searches on OpenAI's SimpleQA benchmark (Gemini 2: 85%, Gemini 3: 91%). Given AI Overviews' scale (Google processes 5+ trillion searches annually and AI Overviews surpassed 2 billion monthly users per Sundar Pichai), that error rate implies millions of incorrect responses. Google disputed the methodology, arguing SimpleQA contains errors and that using one AI to grade another is flawed.
