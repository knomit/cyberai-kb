# Google Flow Music: AI music and production tool; electronic artists are avid users

Seth Forsgren, co-founder and head of product for Google Flow Music (Google's AI music and production tool), told The Deep View (Oct 2026) that some of the most avid users are electronic music artists, and that AI could make music a malleable building block listeners can remix rather than a static piece. Statement is from a company executive, not independent usage data.
