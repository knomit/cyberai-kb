# Cerebras IPO Opens at $350/Share, Closes at $95 Billion Market Cap (May 2026)

Cerebras Systems (ticker: CBRS) went public on NASDAQ in May 2026, with its IPO opening at $350 per share. The company closed its first trading day at a $95 billion market cap, making it one of the most significant AI hardware IPOs to date. Cerebras is known for its wafer-scale AI chips designed specifically for large-scale AI inference and training workloads.
