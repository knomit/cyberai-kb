# Gartner: Global AI Spending to Reach $2.59T in 2026, 47% YoY Growth

Gartner's May 2026 forecast projects global AI spending will reach $2.59 trillion in 2026, representing 47% year-over-year growth. The forecast encompasses AI software, AI-related hardware (GPUs, custom silicon, edge devices), and AI services (consulting, managed AI services, training). Enterprise AI adoption is the primary driver, with industries including financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, and logistics leading investment. Gartner notes that a significant portion of spend is now in inference infrastructure as organizations move from pilot to production deployments at scale. The 47% growth rate substantially exceeds the overall IT spending growth rate, indicating AI is consuming an expanding share of enterprise technology budgets.
