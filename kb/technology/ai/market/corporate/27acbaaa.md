# Meta Lays Off 8,000 Employees Amid AI Transition (May 2026)

Meta announced layoffs of 8,000 employees in May 2026 amid its AI transition — one of the largest single AI-related workforce reductions disclosed by a major tech company. This follows Meta's broader pattern of reallocating headcount from traditional roles toward AI development and AI-powered product teams. Combined with earlier AI researcher departures to Thinking Machines (from xAI/SpaceX), it signals an accelerating reshaping of Big Tech workforce composition driven by AI.
