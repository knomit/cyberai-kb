# Cohere acquires Aleph Alpha in $20 billion deal (April 2026)

Cohere agreed to acquire German AI lab Aleph Alpha in a deal valued at $20 billion (reported April 27, 2026). Aleph Alpha is known for its European sovereign AI positioning and language models. The acquisition consolidates Cohere's enterprise AI position and eliminates a key European frontier AI competitor. Significance: largest European AI company acquisition to date.
