# OpenAI finds task crossover in ChatGPT use

In a report on how AI is expanding what people do at work, OpenAI analyzed 800,000 US user ChatGPT messages and found that workers increasingly use ChatGPT for tasks traditionally associated with other occupations, identifying this 'task crossover' in a high percentage of occupation-specific conversations.
