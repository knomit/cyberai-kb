# Realtime-Bench: best frontier model passes only 36% of real-time tasks under wall-clock conditions

Realtime-Bench (UT Austin, Ohio State, UIUC, NVIDIA, Northwestern, Steadyworks) has 50 interactive tasks: 30 web (travel, e-commerce, service, security) and 20 games. It probes temporal state awareness (RT-Perceive), adaptive deliberation (RT-Deliberate) and time-aware action selection (RT-Act), under wall-clock, paused-clock and token-clock conditions. Per Import AI 479, GPT-6 Astra (high) scored highest on wall clock at 36%, while with a paused clock high-reasoning models reach roughly 70-80%. Implication: latency, not baseline capability, is the main failure mode for real-time agents.
