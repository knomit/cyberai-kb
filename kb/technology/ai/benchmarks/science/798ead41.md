# SciUniverse benchmark: 92 tasks/17 families; best model passes 45.3% of automated-lab tasks

C5R Corp's SciUniverse tests AI operating a mostly automated lab (92 tasks, 17 families). Per Import AI #475: Claude Fable 5.1 (xhigh) leads at 45.3% pass and $40.61/task, then GPT-5 Astra (xhigh) at 32.5%/$52.37, then Claude Opus 5 (xhigh) at 30.5%/$46.31.
