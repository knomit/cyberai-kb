# Remote Labor Index automation rate rose from 2.5% to 16.1% in under 8 months

Researchers at the Center for AI Safety (CAIS) and Scale Labs report that AI success rates on the Remote Labor Index (RLI) — which tests end-to-end completion of economically valuable online freelance projects — rose from 2.5% at launch in October 2025 to 16.1% in July 2026. The July update evaluated three frontier models: GPT-5.5 (6.3%), Claude Opus 4.8 (8.3%), and Fable 5 (16.1%). The authors write the frontier 'has more than quadrupled in under eight months.' RLI covers 3D & CAD, architecture, graphic design, video and animation, audio, data analysis, and web applications. Example tasks include re-creating an engagement ring 3D model with a swapped stone cut, producing a ~60-second 2D animated advertisement to a provided voiceover, and generating a dimensioned floor plan plus photorealistic renders from a scanned cadastral plan.
