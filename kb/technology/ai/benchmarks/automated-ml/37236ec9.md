# Intology's Locus sets PostTrainBench SOTA and beats the human baseline on PostTrainBench+

AI startup Intology's 'Locus' software (which turns LLMs into autonomous ML researchers) scored 44.7% on PostTrainBench — a benchmark measuring how well systems improve an open-weight model above baseline — versus 34.1% for bare Opus 5 and 41.8% for Fable 5, results externally verified by the benchmark authors. PostTrainBench was introduced March 2026 with a then-best of 23.2% (Opus 4.6). On 'PostTrainBench+', which lifts the 10-hour single-GPU limit, Locus reached 51.6% using over 4000 H100 GPU-hours, exceeding the ~51.1% human baseline.
