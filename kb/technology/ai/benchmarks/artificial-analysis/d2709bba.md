# Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 adds coding and agentic science evals

Artificial Analysis released Intelligence Index v5 on 2026-10-28, updating existing benchmarks and adding two new evaluations for coding and agentic science tasks. Nathan Lambert's Interconnects post 'Artificial Analysis v5 and the benchmarker's dilemma' discusses the difficulty of independent model evaluation: building a single number that matches vibe checks, community sentiment and scientific analysis while staying forward-looking, given that independent evaluators influence a trillion-dollar market and the direction of model development.
