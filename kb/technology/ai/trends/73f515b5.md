# Agentic AI is the dominant competitive frontier in mid-2026

As of June 2026, multiple simultaneous developments show agentic AI as the central battleground across the industry: native computer-use models (Google Gemini 3.5 Flash), open agent models praised for coding harnesses (GLM-5.2), agent-orchestration tooling (Orca for parallel coding agents), simulated agent environments/world models (Alibaba Qwen-AgentWorld trained on 10M+ trajectories), domain-specific agents (Perplexity's Computer for Counsel), and litigation over agentic browsing itself (Amazon v. Perplexity's Comet). The convergence indicates competition has shifted from raw model quality toward agents that act in real software and legal environments.
