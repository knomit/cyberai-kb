# US government-gated frontier model access is spurring model orchestration and vendor independence

As of mid-2026, top-tier frontier model launches are increasingly gated by the U.S. government: OpenAI limited GPT-5.6 to ~20 government-approved organizations, and Anthropic's Claude Mythos 5 / Claude Fable 5 were suspended then selectively re-authorized. This access uncertainty, plus disliked data-retention terms, is driving demand for model-orchestration systems that reduce dependence on any single provider or nation — exemplified by Sakana AI's Fugu and Fugu-Ultra, which delegate tasks across many LLMs and match single-model SOTA. In parallel, vendors pursue independence directly: Microsoft built MAI-Thinking-1 from scratch (not distilled from a rival) after its OpenAI license became non-exclusive in April 2026. All reported by The Batch, 2026-07-03.
