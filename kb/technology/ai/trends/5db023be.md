# Mid-2026: AI industry pivots from capability-maxxing to cost-efficiency amid enterprise 'sticker shock'

By mid-2026 the frontier-AI industry visibly shifted from maximizing raw capability and token consumption ('tokenmaxxing') toward cost efficiency, driven by enterprise pushback on ballooning bills. Gartner projected global AI spending near $2.6 trillion in 2026 (+~47% YoY), which The Deep View tied to 'AI sticker shock.' Concrete signals converging in the same week (2026-06-30/07-01): Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 5 delivered near-Opus-4.8 agentic performance at much lower cost ($2/$10 per M tokens introductory), becoming the Free/Pro default; Google's Nano Banana 2 Lite was pitched as its fastest, most cost-efficient image model (≤4s per image); and AI-inference chip startup Etched reached a $5B valuation on the promise of cheaper inference. The pattern: vendors that profited from token-heavy usage are now competing on price/efficiency as compute scarcity and customer cost-sensitivity rise.
