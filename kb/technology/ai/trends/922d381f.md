# Early Aug 2026: AI shift toward smaller, cheaper, on-device and open models

Multiple 2026-08 developments point to a shared industry push toward efficiency and decentralization of AI rather than ever-larger centralized frontier models. Pathway's 150M-parameter post-transformer BDH-CQ approaches frontier-class ARC-AGI-1 reasoning at ~$0.0007/task (roughly 1/11th the per-task cost of OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna Low), framed as 'more intelligence per dollar.' Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30B open-weight (Apache 2.0) agentic model that runs on a single consumer GPU, alongside a Zuckerberg essay advocating decentralized 'personal superintelligence.' OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna pricing ~80% and Anthropic made Claude Sonnet 5 pricing permanent at $2/$10 per M tokens. This does NOT mean large frontier models are obsolete — Pathway's small model still trails on raw accuracy and its 600B-scale claims are unproven; the pattern is about falling cost-per-intelligence and growing local/open options.
