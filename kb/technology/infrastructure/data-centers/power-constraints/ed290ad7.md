# Musk claims ~15GW of 2027 AI compute will be un-energizable in 2027

Elon Musk publicly stated a 'consensus estimate' that roughly 15GW of AI compute produced in 2027 cannot be turned on in 2027. He attributed the bottleneck to site-level infrastructure — transformers, wiring, liquid cooling and chillers, and complex networking — rather than power generation alone. This is an attributed public claim, not a verified measurement.
