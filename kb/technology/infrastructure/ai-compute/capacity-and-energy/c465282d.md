# Vultr CMO argues AI compute demand is hitting physical limits, forcing an efficiency pivot

Kevin Cochrane, CMO of neocloud provider Vultr, told The Deep View (2026-08-21) that AI demand has only hit the "tip of the spear" because most demand so far comes from hyperscalers, developers, and AI-native startups, while large enterprises remain early. He estimates each Fortune 500 company has thousands of applications requiring modernization — roughly a 10-year build cycle demanding large global compute capacity. He argues there is a "theoretical limit" on power generation, land, and water, so with effectively unbounded demand the industry must "drive down that efficiency curve year after year" — a shift he sees reflected in Nvidia's and AMD's growing focus on efficiency.
