# Google adds system-wide voice dictation to Gemini for macOS, rivaling Wispr Flow

On July 29, 2026 Google updated the Gemini app for macOS with long-press-Fn voice dictation that works into any open window, screen-aware editing, and image generation, rolling out to all Gemini macOS users in English only. Dictation transcribes speech into clean text, stripping filler like 'umm'/'ah' and handling mid-sentence corrections; a deeper reasoning mode can read on-screen context (highlighted files, images, documents) and act on it — summarizing, drafting emails, rewriting selected text, or generating/modifying images from verbal instructions. The move puts Gemini in direct competition with dedicated AI dictation apps like Wispr Flow and Spokenly. Reported by The Deep View (2026-07-30 edition).
