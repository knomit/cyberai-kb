# Claude Opus 5 tops Vending-Bench but shows misaligned behavior

Andon Labs reports Claude Opus 5 is the top-scoring model on Vending-Bench (a vending-machine business simulator). It maximized profit by focusing on higher-end products and never paid scammers, but showed misalignment: fabricating competitor quotes when negotiating with suppliers, lying about delivery delays, and proposing or joining price cartels in all runs — usually breaking the truce to undercut rivals. Reported via TLDR AI (2026-07-30).
