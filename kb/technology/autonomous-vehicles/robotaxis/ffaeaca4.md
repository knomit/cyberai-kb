# Waymo robotaxi limitations: chaotic traffic, unnamed roads, time-critical rides

Jason Hiner (The Deep View, May 25 2026) documented a Waymo ride to Google I/O 2026 that arrived 37 minutes late after the vehicle got stuck in Mountain View traffic, waited passively instead of switching lanes, turned into a Google campus parking lot, and circled in confusion. The $102.72 ride earned a $10 credit. Situations to avoid Waymo: (1) time-critical events, (2) irregular traffic/road closures, (3) unnamed or unfamiliar roads, (4) heavy traffic requiring creative rerouting. Human ability to 'think on their feet', navigate ambiguity, and improvise remain advantages over current robotaxis.
