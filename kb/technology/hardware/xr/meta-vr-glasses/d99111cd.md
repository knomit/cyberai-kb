# Meta VR Glasses: 100 g, $1,299, 5K micro-OLED, versus Snap Specs at $2,195

Meta's VR Glasses (announced Sept 2026) fit Quest-class capability into ~100 g glasses (vs 515 g Quest 2) with a 5K Micro-OLED display (37 pixels per degree), eye tracking and hand gestures, but need a pocket computing puck. Priced $1,299; Snap Specs (132-136 g, no puck) cost $2,195 per The Deep View reviewer.
