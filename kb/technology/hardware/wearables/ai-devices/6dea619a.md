# Apple is expected to ship camera-equipped AirPods that provide AI visual context without recording

MacRumors found a video clip in the macOS Tahoe 26.7 release candidate showing a user with camera-equipped AirPods using Visual Intelligence to identify and save a book by its cover. Mark Gurman has reported Apple plans to release AI AirPods, codenamed B798, alongside its foldable and new iPhones in September 2026, supported by the revamped Siri unveiled at WWDC in May 2026. Gurman reports the cameras will not capture/record content but only supply visual context to the AI — a deliberate differentiator from Meta Ray-Ban smart glasses, which drew backlash over unauthorized recording of bystanders. Privacy concerns reportedly caused Apple to postpone its own smart glasses. The RC also contains an error message warning users when hair obstructs the AirPods cameras.
