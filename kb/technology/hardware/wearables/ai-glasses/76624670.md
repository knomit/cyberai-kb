# Brilliant Labs launches open-source, privacy-first Halo AI glasses at $399

Brilliant Labs launched Halo smartglasses (2026-10-27 report): $399, 40 g, 14 hours battery, two microphones, full-colour microOLED in the right eye, Bluetooth 5.3, swappable prescription lenses. Open-sources its software and documents every component. Audio and images are processed locally via the phone and converted to transcripts/descriptions, then raw media is deleted (per CEO Bobak Tavangar). Enterprise option allows integrating company agents.
