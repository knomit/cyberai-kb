# Halliday launches second-generation camera-free smart glasses

Halliday unveiled second-generation smart glasses rebuilt around workplace meetings, deliberately omitting cameras to ease coworker privacy/recording concerns and meet corporate security protocols. Specs: 4-microphone array with up to 2-meter voice recognition, claimed 12 hours battery, under 50 grams, upward-glance display. Features include live captions, real-time translation in 45+ languages, Thread Tracker, Decision Confirmation, and Commitment Check. Priced at $599 with shipping expected September 2026.
