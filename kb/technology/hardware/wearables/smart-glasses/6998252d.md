# Samsung unveiled Gemini-powered AI smart glasses launching fall 2026

At its Unpacked event (covered July 23, 2026), Samsung showed AI smart glasses built around Google Gemini, positioned to compete with Meta Ray-Bans. Frames come via a Warby Parker collaboration (everyday styles) and a bolder Gentle Monster collection. The glasses have one camera, an LED light to indicate recording, open-ear speakers, and microphones, emphasizing content capture, fast Gemini access, and notifications. Samsung, working with Qualcomm, claims about 9 hours of active use per charge (vs. 8 hours for Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2), with a case that recharges them about seven times. Like Meta's audio glasses, they lack in-lens displays. Exact weight/specs were unreleased; launch is planned for fall 2026.
