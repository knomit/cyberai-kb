# Snap Specs launch event: full hardware specifications, Specs Intelligence, and bundle pricing

Snap held the LAUNCH EVENT for its Specs AR glasses on 16 September 2026 — distinct from the June 2026 unveiling recorded separately in this base; public availability is expected in the fall. Hardware as specified at launch: a 51-degree field of view display (equivalent to a 24-inch desktop display or a 115-inch home cinema screen) with 16 million colors; electrochromic lenses shifting from clear to tinted in 10 seconds, with removable inserts supporting single-vision prescriptions from -8 to +2 diopters; two Snapdragon processors, one for computer vision and one for running the lenses; Swiss TR90 polymer frames; an LED that glows when recording, on-device data processing prioritized, and user prompts before accessing sensitive information; and a claimed 4 hours of battery in mixed use. Snap also announced Specs Intelligence, an AI service spanning iPhone, Mac, and the Specs, built to learn user routines and priorities for preemptive assistance such as surfacing relevant information in view; the iOS preview is available to anyone in the US, with a waitlist for the fuller Mac experience. Snap declined to say which AI model powers the experience. PRICING HAS TWO DISTINCT FIGURES that must not be conflated: $2,195 for the glasses alone as unveiled in June, and $2,395 for the bundle of glasses plus the new charging case with cellular connectivity (US users can add Verizon service), which does NOT include the wireless data plan cost.
