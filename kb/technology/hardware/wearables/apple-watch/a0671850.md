# Apple Watch Series 12 S11 chip raises HRV sampling from every 2-4 hours to every 5 minutes

Apple unveiled the Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 in September 2026, both running the new S11 chip. The chip plus new optical and electrical heart sensors power a Health Sensing System that measures heart rate variability every 5 minutes (previously roughly every 2 to 4 hours) and heart rate every 5 seconds (previously attempted every 5 minutes). Machine learning also drives a redesigned pedometer model and a new Readiness score presented as a single 0-10 value labeled Recover, Pace Yourself, Ready, or Go For It; Readiness requires 7 consecutive days of at least 4 hours of sleep before it will produce a score. Apple claims up to 24 hours of battery life despite the added sampling. Notably, Apple markets these as health features rather than branding them under Apple Intelligence. Operational consequence: the sampling-rate jump is the substantive change — a roughly 24x to 48x increase in HRV sampling frequency is what makes stress and recovery inference feasible, independent of any AI branding.
