# Apple Watch S11 adds on-device Audio Intelligence

Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 add an S11 chip with an "Audio Intelligence" suite including Siri Recap (conversation summaries) and Live Rewind (15-second replay transcripts), arriving in beta later in 2026. Apple says this audio is processed in the S11's Secure Exclave and that no recordings are saved.
