# Apple processes Audio Intelligence in the S11 Secure Exclave and deletes audio after processing

Apple's technical privacy claim for the Apple Watch Audio Intelligence features, stated at the September 2026 keynote and reported 2026-09-11: audio for Live Rewind and Siri Recap is captured in the Secure Exclave on the new S11 chip, a region inaccessible to the operating system, apps, the user, and Apple itself, with audio deleted immediately after processing and never recorded or saved. Ron Huang, Apple's VP of sensing and connectivity, framed it as 'Just as Visual Intelligence makes sense of what you see, Audio Intelligence makes sense of what you hear.' Live Rewind is triggered by double-pressing the digital crown and surfaces the last 15 seconds as a saveable transcript. The unresolved issue is social rather than technical: in two-party consent states such as California, Apple says it will always comply with local legal requirements and argues the features are compliant because no audio is recorded — but a user can still capture a note of a conversation the other party believed was private or confidential. Apple has not shipped these features yet: they arrive about a month after announcement and in beta, which The Deep View reads as Apple deliberately gauging reaction before committing. PP Foresight analyst Paolo Pescatore framed the watch as a testbed for a wearables-home-and-new-form-factors roadmap including eventual Apple Glasses.
