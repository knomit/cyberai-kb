# Snap unveils SPECS AR wearable computer at $2,195

Snap unveiled SPECS at the 2026 Augmented World Expo — AR 'wearable computer' glasses positioned to replace screens rather than supplement them. Pre-order $2,195 ($200 refundable deposit), shipping fall 2026 in US/UK/France. Specs: two sizes (47mm/132g, 52mm/136g), liquid-crystal-on-silicon display with 51° field of view (~24-inch desktop equivalent), electrochromic lenses (clear-to-tint in 10s), two Snapdragon processors, ~4 hours mixed-use battery plus charging case for 20 hours total, Swiss TR90 polymer frames. CEO Evan Spiegel calls it 'the beginning of a new era in computing.'
