# Apple's M6 Mac mini and M5 Ultra Mac Studio are positioned explicitly around local AI inference

On 2026-08-25 Apple announced the M6 chip in a new Mac mini and the M5 Ultra in a new Mac Studio, marketing both primarily on AI compute. Apple claims the M6 Mac mini delivers up to 4x faster AI performance, 2x faster storage and graphics, and 40% faster CPU than its predecessor; the M6 has a 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU with Neural Accelerators added to each GPU core for the first time on Mac mini. Mac Studio claims up to 4.3x faster AI performance; the M5 Ultra has an 18-core CPU, up to 40-core GPU with per-core Neural Accelerators, and up to 128GB unified memory. Pricing: Mac mini M6 from $899, Mac mini M5 Pro from $1,699 (each $100 above predecessors), Mac Studio M5 Max from $2,499, Mac Studio M5 Ultra from $5,499; shipping 2026-09-22. The launch followed Mac mini shortages earlier in 2026 driven partly by demand for always-on local agent hosts, compounded by industry-wide memory and storage shortage.
