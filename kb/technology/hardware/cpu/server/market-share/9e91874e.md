# AMD reached 46% of x86 server CPU revenue in Q1 2026

According to Mercury Research, AMD rose to 46% of x86 server CPU revenue and 33% of unit shipments in Q1 2026. This continues a decade-long comeback under CEO Lisa Su, who became CEO in 2014 and in 2017 prioritized re-entering the server CPU market when AMD's server share had fallen to 0%. Nvidia remains dominant in AI accelerators with roughly 90% market share, since those are still mostly GPUs.
