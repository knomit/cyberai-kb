# AMD launches Venice (EPYC 9006) server CPUs for the AI agent era

On July 23, 2026, at its Advancing AI event in San Francisco, AMD unveiled its next-generation 'Venice' server CPUs, officially the EPYC 9006 Series, aimed at AI agents and high-performance computing. CEO Lisa Su said Venice is 'in full production' with the strongest demand AMD has ever seen for a new EPYC generation, and that every major server OEM and cloud provider is on track to begin rolling out in Q4 2026. AMD found the high-end Venice part delivers 2.2x the performance per core of Nvidia's comparable Vera CPU. AMD also offers Helios as a full rack-scale AI system to rival Nvidia's Grace Blackwell and Vera Rubin racks.
