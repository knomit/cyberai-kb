# Nvidia is extending CUDA support to RISC-V CPUs

Presented around Hot Chips 2026: Nvidia is working to extend CUDA support to RISC-V, which would let RISC-V CPUs feed GPU compute. Caveats noted by analysts are that RISC-V's software ecosystem still lags x86-64 and aarch64, and that the vast majority of existing RISC-V hardware will not meet Nvidia's requirements.
