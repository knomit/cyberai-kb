# Meta Reality Labs Tactus: textile tactile and pose-sensing glove for manipulation data

Meta Reality Labs Research published Tactus, a textile-integrated tactile and pose-sensing glove for collecting naturalistic manipulation data: 3D-knit form factor with 1.5mm fingertip stackup, capacitive tactile sensors at manipulation-relevant regions, and IMU-fused pose tracking that stays accurate through hand-object occlusion. Source is the arXiv paper, covered in Import AI 480.
