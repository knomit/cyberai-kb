# CXMT says its 5th-gen DRAM platform reached mass production, on par with Samsung/Micron nodes

ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT), China's largest DRAM maker, said (Sept 2026) its fifth-generation DRAM platform entered mass production with process capability on par with the most advanced mass-produced nodes; it yields at least 50% more dies per wafer than the previous generation. Two products already use it, both 24-gigabit LPDDR5X. This is a company claim.
