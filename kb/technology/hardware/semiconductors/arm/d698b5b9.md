# Arm extended Total Design into physical AI with a robotics vocabulary standard

Arm expanded its Total Design ecosystem into physical AI with 80+ companies including AWS, Hugging Face and Unitree, and unveiled a Robotics Capability Framework to standardize robotics vocabulary. Arm says it shipped 2 billion units into physical-AI use cases over the preceding 12 months, into a market it estimates at roughly $25 billion.
