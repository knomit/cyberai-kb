# NVIDIA expands Jetson Thor line with Blackwell-powered T3000 and T2000

NVIDIA introduced the Blackwell-powered Jetson Thor T3000 and T2000 modules, targeting robotics, visual AI, and agent workloads on compact edge systems. The Jetson Thor platform had already attracted adoption from companies including Amazon Robotics, Boston Dynamics, FANUC, and 1X.
