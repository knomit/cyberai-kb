# Google TPUv7 Ironwood opened to external inference workloads

Google's TPUv7 Ironwood delivers up to 50% better performance-per-dollar than Nvidia's B200/B300 chips and is the first TPU generation Google is opening for external inference workloads, purchasable or rentable via cloud — a change in Google's historical posture of reserving TPUs for internal and Cloud-managed use.
