# Nvidia's Vera Rubin architecture extends its moat beyond the GPU

Nvidia's Vera Rubin architecture adds specialized non-GPU components, notably the Vera CPU for efficient data orchestration. The strategy shifts from producing the fastest processors toward optimizing megascale data-center data flow, indicating the competitive battleground is moving to rack- and datacenter-level infrastructure.
