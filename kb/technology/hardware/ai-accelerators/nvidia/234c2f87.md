# NVIDIA Groq 3 LPX inference accelerator enters full production as a Vera Rubin extension

NVIDIA's Groq 3 LPX AI inference accelerator chip has entered full production. Groq 3 LPX racks are an extension of the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform, targeting ultra-fast token generation for response-sensitive agentic workloads. NVIDIA claims the platform lets agentic AI tasks complete in minutes rather than hours and delivers roughly a 4x boost in response times versus the nearest alternative platform, with the fastest token generation speeds recorded to date.
