# AMD positions Helios rack-scale systems and openness against Nvidia Vera Rubin

In a Deep View Conversations interview, AMD data center GPU general manager Andrew Dieckmann argued frontier AI requires more than GPUs — it demands tightly engineered racks combining GPUs, CPUs, networking, software, cooling, and serviceability. AMD's Helios is its first rack-scale AI system, positioned against Nvidia's Vera Rubin on performance and value, with a Cerebras partnership splitting high-throughput, low-latency inference workloads. Dieckmann frames open hardware, open software, and customer choice as the strategy for loosening Nvidia's grip, and argues responsible deployment matters as these systems become 'intelligence factories' amid community concern over power and water.
