# OpenAI published first test results for Jalapeño, its Broadcom-built inference chip

In August 2026 OpenAI released the first test results for Jalapeño, its new inference chip produced in partnership with Broadcom, reporting promising latency and throughput when running both OpenAI's own and open-weights models. The Batch notes chipmakers must balance raw speed against total concurrent users and power draw, both of which affect inference cost and reliability.
