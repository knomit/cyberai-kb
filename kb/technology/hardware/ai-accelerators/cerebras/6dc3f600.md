# Cerebras announced the CS-4, claiming a widened AI inference speed advantage over Nvidia

Cerebras announced the CS-4, which it says is multiple times faster than its predecessor — a system Cerebras already claimed was more responsive than Nvidia-processor-based systems. As of Aug 19, 2026 the machine was being sampled by a small group of customers, with wider availability planned for Q3. It is a significant step in Cerebras' bid to challenge Nvidia in AI data center hardware.
