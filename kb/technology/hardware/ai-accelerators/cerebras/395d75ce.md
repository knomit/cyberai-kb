# Cerebras splits trillion-parameter models across multiple WSE-3T wafers

A single Cerebras WSE-3T wafer has 44 GB of on-chip memory, while trillion-parameter frontier models need terabytes for weights alone. Cerebras describes sharding a large model across multiple WSE-3T wafers, how models fill on-chip memory, how they map across chips, and how it handles high concurrent requests.
