# Microsoft unveils Maia 300 AI accelerator; large-scale deployment from 2027

Microsoft reportedly planned to unveil the Maia 300 in September 2026. By 2026-10-12 Microsoft announced Maia 300, its third-generation in-house AI accelerator, aimed at reducing reliance on NVIDIA GPUs, claiming 40% better performance per megawatt and planning large-scale (multi-gigawatt Azure Maia system) deployment beginning in 2027. It does not mean the chip is generally deployed now.
