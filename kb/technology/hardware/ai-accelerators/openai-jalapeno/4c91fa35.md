# OpenAI used its own models to design Jalapeño, reaching tapeout in nine months

OpenAI states that AI played a direct role in Jalapeño's development, taking the team from initial design to tapeout in nine months by shortening design, measurement and verification loops and optimizing the chip's arithmetic circuits. The chip was also deliberately architected as a predictable programming target (local tensors, explicit communication, predictable synchronization) so AI could map, place and schedule work on it. Using Codex with GPT-Astra, the team brought three open-weight models that were not in Jalapeño's original production plan to high performance within two months; for selected GPT-OSS attention and mixture-of-experts blocks, AI-generated kernel implementations ran 1.5–1.8x faster than existing human-expert-written ones. OpenAI's head of hardware Richard Ho told media the project, a partnership with Broadcom, began as a concept in late 2024 and that volume would ramp into 2027.
