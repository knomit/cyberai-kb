# OpenAI plans to deploy Jalapeño in its own infrastructure by end of 2026, with Gen 2 and Gen 3 in development

OpenAI says it plans to begin deploying Jalapeño within its own compute infrastructure by the end of 2026, describing it as the first generation of a multigenerational roadmap with Gen 2 'deep in development' and Gen 3 taking shape. OpenAI explicitly states it will continue to widely deploy NVIDIA and other partner accelerators for both training and inference. The architecture keeps prefill (compute-bound prompt processing) and decode (memory-bandwidth-bound token generation) inside one large connected network domain so model state including the KV cache stays local, minimizing data movement — a design aimed at agentic workloads where the prefill/decode balance shifts over a task.
