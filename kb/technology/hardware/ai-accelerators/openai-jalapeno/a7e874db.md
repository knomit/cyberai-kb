# OpenAI's Jalapeño inference chip posts 1.5–1.9x higher throughput per watt than comparison systems

OpenAI published first measured results for Jalapeño, its first custom inference accelerator, on 2026-08-25. Tested on SemiAnalysis's public InferenceX benchmark across GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek R1 670B, and Kimi K2.5 1T, Jalapeño delivered 1.5–1.9x more AI work per watt at peak throughput and 1.7–3.6x lower end-to-end latency than the commercially available comparison systems (NVIDIA GB200 for GPT-OSS, GB300 for the two larger models). For highly interactive workloads the advantage was 2.1–4.1x. Jalapeño is rated at 700 W package TDP but measured sustained power stayed at or below 550 W on the tested workloads; the GB200/GB300 comparators are rated 1,200 W and 1,400 W respectively. OpenAI normalized results by published chip power rating and argues performance per unit of power is the more useful standard than performance per chip. Reported via The Deep View and TLDR AI, 2026-08-26.
