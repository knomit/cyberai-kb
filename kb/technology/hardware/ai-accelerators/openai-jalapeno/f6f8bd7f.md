# OpenAI and Broadcom unveil Jalapeño LLM inference chip

On/around June 2026, OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño, the first accelerator in a planned family of LLM inference chips optimized for performance per watt and rapid deployment. The companies said the processor was designed in nine months with AI-assisted development and is intended for gigawatt-scale data center deployments.
