# Apple unveiled the iPhone Duo foldable at $1,999

Apple unveiled the iPhone Duo, its first foldable iPhone, starting at $1,999 and shipping October 23, 2026, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4, and AirPods 5.
