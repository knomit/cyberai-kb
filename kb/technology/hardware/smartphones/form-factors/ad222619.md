# Honor Robot Phone: a smartphone with a gimbal-mounted robotic camera arm

Honor launched the Robot Phone, a slab smartphone from whose camera socket a robot arm on a gimbal emerges, carrying a 200MP main camera that can track a user's movements to keep shots stable and framed. Honor describes it as an "AI companion": its YOYO assistant wakes to its name with a small dance, has expressive movements such as nodding and shaking, and over 100 on-screen facial expressions. The Deep View's reviewer argued the more durable value is using AI tracking on the gimbal for multimodal AI prompts (e.g. hands-free cooking assistance), though the phone still requires Honor's included kickstand. The device is available only in China and is described as a 1.0 product.
