# Google is reportedly working with AMD on a hybrid TPU with on-package CPU cores

Google is reported to have tapped AMD to help design a next-generation TPU that combines Google's proprietary accelerator technology with on-package general-purpose CPU cores, aimed at CPU-heavy workloads such as reinforcement learning.
