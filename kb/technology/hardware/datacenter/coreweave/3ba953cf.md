# CoreWeave builds 'Valvey' and 'Racky' around Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72

CoreWeave developed two innovations around Nvidia's Vera Rubin NVL72 (a 72-GPU AI supercomputing system): 'Valvey,' a programmable coolant-valve system that monitors pressure/flow/leaks in software to isolate failures and reduce rack downtime, and 'Racky,' a single per-rack controller unifying power, cooling, and environmental sensors. CoreWeave says it provides services to nine of the ten leading model providers.
