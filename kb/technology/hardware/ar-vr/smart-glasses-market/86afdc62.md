# Display-less smart glasses sales surged 167% YoY in Q1 2026

Per IDC data cited by The Deep View, smart glasses without displays surged 167% year-over-year in the first quarter of 2026, with approximately 2.25 million units sold. The broader AR/ER/MR/VR category grew 86% year-over-year. The trend is driven by demand for AI assistants that move beyond a chatbot screen into the real world, with AI seen as the catalyst that gives AR the utility it previously lacked.
