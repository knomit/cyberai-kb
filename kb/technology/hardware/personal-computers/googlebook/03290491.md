# Google launched Googlebooks (Android-based, Gemini-first laptops) on Sept 21, 2026

On Monday Sept 21, 2026 Google launched Googlebooks, a laptop category built on Android while maintaining ChromeOS, with Gemini Intelligence integrated (Magic Pointer, Rambler voice-to-text next to Quick Insert, Create My Widget), Google Antigravity bundled, and a full Linux terminal. Purchases include 12 months of Google AI Pro. Launch partners: HP and Dell (Qualcomm Snapdragon X Elite), Lenovo and Acer (Intel Core Ultra 5 Series 3 Panther Lake), ASUS Googlebook 14 (Intel Core Ultra 5/7 Panther Lake). Pre-orders opened Sept 21; US launch Oct 4, 2026; starting price $899.
