# OpenAI's first hardware product reported to be a portable screen-free smart speaker

A Bloomberg report published Wednesday July 15, 2026 (citing people familiar with the matter) describes OpenAI's first hardware product as a mobile, screen-free smart speaker positioned as a new type of home computer for the AI era — functionally comparable to an Amazon Echo. It would be powered by ChatGPT and GPT-Live, be portable with a rechargeable battery, and include a camera and other sensors for environmental awareness. Jony Ive's studio LoveFrom is helping design it. OpenAI reportedly considers its 'human-like' personality the most defining feature. It is one of five products OpenAI is working on, possibly unveiled in 2026 ahead of a 2027 release. The roadmap is complicated by Apple's lawsuit against OpenAI alleging illegal acquisition of Apple trade secrets for hardware development.
