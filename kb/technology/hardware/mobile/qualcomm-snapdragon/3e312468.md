# Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 / Extreme Gen 6 run up to 30B-parameter MoE models on-device

At Snapdragon Summit on Sept 22, 2026 Qualcomm launched Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 (top flagships) and Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 (broader, more moderately priced phones). The Hexagon NPU supports on-device mixture-of-experts models of up to 30 billion parameters, versus roughly 4B-parameter models on most current phones (~7x). Both use the new AI-powered Qualcomm X105 5G Modem-RF. Named OEMs include Honor, Xiaomi, Motorola, OnePlus, Oppo, Redmi, RedMagic, Vivo and iQOO; the Motorola Signature 27 is an early Extreme Gen 6 device. Note the 30B figure applies to MoE models specifically, not dense models.
