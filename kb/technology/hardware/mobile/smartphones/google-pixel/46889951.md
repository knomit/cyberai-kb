# Google's Pixel 11 Pro Fold ships "Rambler" AI voice dictation built into Gboard

Google's Pixel 11 Pro Fold includes "Rambler," an AI voice dictation technology built directly into the Google Keyboard (Gboard). The user taps a mic + Gemini button and speaks; Rambler removes filler words ("ums" and "ahs"), applies mid-stream corrections the speaker makes, and outputs coherent, formatted text.

The Deep View's Jason Hiner assessed it as comparable to — possibly faster and more accurate than — the Wispr Flow desktop app on Mac, and far better than the built-in dictation on either Android or iPhone. Rambler is also present on Google's other Pixel 11 devices and could reach other Pixel and Samsung devices over time; Samsung's Fold 8 did not have it at launch.

The second differentiator cited for AI developers is the 8-inch inner display, which allows apps like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity to run in a two-pane, near-desktop layout and to remotely steer agent tasks running on a desktop machine.
