# A decade of iPhone camera ML features, 2016 through 2025

A Deep View analysis published 2026-09-08 by Jason Hiner argues iPhone photography is Apple's most successful AI deployment, because ML plus Apple silicon let tiny lenses and sensors produce shots previously reserved for DSLRs — night shots, zoom, macro, full-bokeh portraits. The year-by-year ML/AI feature timeline: 2016 iPhone 7 Plus / iOS 10 — Portrait Mode with ML subject separation, plus people/object/scene recognition powering Memories; 2017 iPhone X — Portrait Lighting and ML front-camera Portrait Mode; 2018 iPhone XS — Smart HDR, ML depth segmentation, Depth Control; 2019 iPhone 11 — Night mode, Deep Fusion, ML Smart HDR; 2020 iPhone 12 Pro — Smart HDR 3, expanded Deep Fusion and Night mode; 2021 iPhone 13 / iOS 15 — Photographic Styles, Smart HDR 4, Cinematic mode, Live Text, Visual Look Up; 2022 iPhone 14 / iOS 16 — Photonic Engine, subject lifting; 2023 iPhone 15 — automatic portrait detection, next-gen Smart HDR; 2024 iPhone 16 / iOS 18 — next-gen Photographic Styles, Clean Up, natural-language search, prompt-generated Memories movies; 2025 iPhone 17 — AI Center Stage for group-selfie reframing, updated Photonic Engine using ML for detail, noise and color. The framing that this is Apple's 'greatest AI success story' is the author's editorial judgment, not a market measurement.
