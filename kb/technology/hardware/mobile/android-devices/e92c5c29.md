# Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold ships Rambler AI dictation in Gboard

The Deep View (2026-08-21) reports Google's Pixel 11 Pro Fold includes "Rambler", an AI voice-dictation feature built into the Google Keyboard (Gboard), invoked via a mic + Gemini button. Rambler removes filler words ("ums and ahs"), applies mid-stream corrections, and outputs coherent, formatted text — functionally comparable to the Wispr Flow desktop app. The device has an 8-inch interior display. Reviewer Jason Hiner argues the combination of Rambler plus the tablet-sized inner screen (for remotely steering agents in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity) makes it the strongest phone for AI developers as of Aug 2026. Rambler also ships on other Pixel 11 devices and may reach additional Pixel/Samsung devices later.
