# Apple iPhone Duo launches at $1,999 with October 23 availability

At its September 9, 2026 event at Apple Park, Apple unveiled the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4, AirPods 5, and — as a 'one more thing' — the iPhone Duo, its first folding phone, competing with foldables from Samsung, Google and Huawei. It starts at $1,999 and ships October 23, 2026. Apple CEO John Ternus framed the goal as 'a larger display that feels as natural and intuitive as iPad.' Deep View editor-in-chief Jason Hiner went hands-on and singled out two things: unusually smooth transitions between closed, open, landscape and portrait modes, ahead of other foldables he has tested; and a large set of form-factor-specific software integrations (video mode, StandBy nightstand alarm clock, seeing both participants and content in Zoom calls, tripod mode for photos). His caveats are explicit: he does not recommend buying at launch, and the Duo does not yet have the best iPhone camera system. His forecast — that most phones look like the iPhone Duo and Galaxy Fold 8 in 3–5 years, by analogy to the Galaxy Note and iPhone 6 Plus — is an individual reviewer's prediction, not data. The AI angle in his argument is that a bigger screen helps every AI workflow: running chatbots, monitoring agents, typing, documents, video calls.
