# Proton Pass adds AI access tokens for controlled, audited agent access to credentials

Proton Pass, an end-to-end-encrypted password manager, added AI access tokens in May 2026. They let a user grant AI agents permissioned access to specific credential items rather than to the whole vault.

Every access is logged, including a required reason-for-access, and tokens carry configurable expiration from 1 hour to 1 year.

The significance is the auditable trail of agent credential use — a control surface that becomes load-bearing as agentic AI becomes mainstream and agents routinely need to authenticate on a user's behalf.
