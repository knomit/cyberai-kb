# LayerX AI Governance & Usage Control Platform — Hands-On Review (THN, May 2026)

The Hacker News published a hands-on review of LayerX Security's AI Governance platform (May 6, 2026). Key capabilities: (1) Full AI discovery — continuously updated inventory of web-based LLMs, AI SaaS, desktop AI apps, developer IDEs, and on-device agent frameworks, with identity correlation across sessions via IdP (Google, Okta, Microsoft Entra); (2) Conversation-level visibility — sees prompts and understands context, unlike network inspection tools that break on encryption; (3) Real-time adaptive guardrails — risk-adaptive policy engine with granular controls: monitor, warn (customizable messages), block, redact sensitive info, or allow controlled bypass; policies applied at the browser/app layer where AI interactions occur; (4) IDE and AI extension coverage — addresses the blind spot of Copilot/Cursor-style tools in dev environments; (5) Unified dashboard — surfaces trends, anomalies, and usage patterns without digging through logs. Architecture: browser-first (extension-based), with optional lightweight endpoint agent for desktop apps, no network architecture changes required. Works on unmanaged devices. Traditional SSE/SASE and endpoint security tools are not effective for AI governance because they can't inspect encrypted AI prompts or understand context.
