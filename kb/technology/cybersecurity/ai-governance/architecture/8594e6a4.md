# Browser-layer AI governance is emerging as the dominant enterprise security architecture for AI (2026)

Traditional enterprise security tools (SSE/SASE, endpoint DLP, network inspection) were not designed for AI interactions and fail in key ways: they cannot see prompts, cannot understand context, and break with encryption or modern app architectures. The architectural response emerging in 2026 is browser-layer enforcement — intercepting AI interactions at the point of consumption (browser extension, IDE plugin) rather than at the network perimeter. This allows prompt-level visibility, real-time policy enforcement, and identity correlation without requiring network architecture changes. LayerX's approach is representative of this pattern. Related to the Gartner finding (May 2026) that 70% of enterprises run AI agents faster than they can govern them.
