# Verizon 2025 DBIR: Median Time to Remediate Edge Device Vulnerabilities is 32 Days

The Verizon 2025 Data Breach Investigations Report (DBIR) found that the median time for organizations to remediate edge device vulnerabilities is 32 days. Combined with Mandiant's M-Trends 2026 finding that the mean time to exploit (MTTE) is now negative seven days — meaning attackers exploit vulnerabilities before patches are available — this creates a structural remediation gap: organizations have roughly 32 days of exposure on average, while attackers need negative time (they may already be in before a patch exists). This gap is one of the most important structural dynamics in enterprise security: known exposure sits in backlogs, and around 70% of breaches are linked to known but unresolved issues.
