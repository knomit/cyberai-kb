# FIRESTARTER Backdoor: Cisco Firepower/ASA Malware That Survives Reboots and Firmware Updates (Sep 2025)

CISA disclosed that an unnamed federal civilian agency's Cisco Firepower device (running ASA software) was compromised in September 2025 with a backdoor called FIRESTARTER. Capabilities: survives reboots, firmware updates, and patches by hooking the LINA process and re-installing itself on receiving termination signals. Hooks into Linux runlevel 6 (reboot), firing persistence routine on every shutdown. Analysis by Eclypsisum: ELF binary that copies itself into memory and registers signal handlers.
