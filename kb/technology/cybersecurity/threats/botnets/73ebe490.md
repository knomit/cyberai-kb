# Kimwolf DDoS-for-Hire Botnet Operator Arrested: Jacob Butler (aka Dort), 23, Ottawa, Canada

The U.S. Department of Justice (DoJ) announced the arrest of Jacob Butler (aka Dort), 23, of Ottawa, Canada, on May 22, 2026, for allegedly operating the Kimwolf distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) botnet as a DDoS-for-hire service.

**Botnet characteristics**: Kimwolf is assessed to be a variant of AISURU, a known IoT botnet family. It targeted devices traditionally 'firewalled' from the rest of the internet — including digital photo frames and web cameras — enslaving them into a botnet used for DDoS-for-hire attacks.

Significance: Law enforcement action against DDoS-as-a-service infrastructure. AISURU-variant botnets represent the continued evolution of IoT device weaponization. The 'firewalled' IoT device targeting is notable — attacking devices that are assumed to be safe because they don't have direct internet-accessible ports.
