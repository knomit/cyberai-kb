# Trapdoor Android Ad Fraud Operation: Malicious SDK Injected Into Legitimate Apps

Trapdoor is a large-scale Android ad fraud operation discovered in May 2026 in which a malicious advertising SDK was injected into otherwise legitimate Android applications. The SDK silently generates fraudulent ad impressions in background services, generating revenue for operators at the expense of advertising networks and brand advertisers. Trapdoor-infected apps collectively had millions of installs across the Google Play Store. The operation is significant because it exploits the trusted distribution channel of the Play Store and legitimate app brands, making detection by end users nearly impossible. Ad fraud at this scale also represents a data privacy risk as the SDK may have auxiliary data collection capabilities.
