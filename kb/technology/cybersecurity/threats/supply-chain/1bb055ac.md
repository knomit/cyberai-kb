# TanStack 'Mini Shai-Hulud' Supply Chain Attack Compromises Two OpenAI Employee Devices (May 2026)

OpenAI disclosed in May 2026 that two of its employee devices in its corporate environment were impacted by the 'Mini Shai-Hulud' supply chain attack on TanStack, a widely used JavaScript library ecosystem. OpenAI stated that no user data, production systems, or intellectual property were compromised. macOS updates were required for affected devices.

Significance: This represents a concrete instance of the AI developer toolchain supply chain attack surface. TanStack is used by a wide range of enterprise development teams, making it a high-value target. The fact that OpenAI — one of the world's most security-conscious AI organizations — was impacted confirms that even top-tier organizations cannot fully insulate their developer environments from supply chain compromise. This is consistent with the broader pattern of attackers targeting AI developer tooling as a primary attack vector.
