# Mini Shai-Hulud Worm: TeamPCP Supply Chain Attack Compromises TanStack, Mistral AI, Guardrails AI, UiPath npm/PyPI Packages (May 2026)

Threat actor 'TeamPCP' (behind recent supply chain attack spree) was linked to the compromise of npm and PyPI packages from TanStack, UiPath, Mistral AI, OpenSearch, and Guardrails AI via a coordinated supply chain worm campaign called 'Mini Shai-Hulud.' The attack targeted widely-used AI and development packages, spreading malicious code across the open-source ecosystem. Checkmarx plugin also compromised. Highlights the growing risk of AI framework supply chain attacks.
