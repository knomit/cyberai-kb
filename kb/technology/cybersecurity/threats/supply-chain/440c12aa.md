# Mini Shai-Hulud Supply Chain Worm: TeamPCP Compromises TanStack, Mistral AI, UiPath, Guardrails AI — Impacts Two OpenAI Employee Devices (May 2026)

Threat actor 'TeamPCP' orchestrated the 'Mini Shai-Hulud' supply chain worm campaign in May 2026, compromising npm and PyPI packages from TanStack, UiPath, Mistral AI, OpenSearch, Guardrails AI, and the Checkmarx plugin. The attack spread malicious code across widely-used AI and developer open-source packages.

Confirmed downstream impact: OpenAI disclosed that two of its corporate employee devices were compromised via the TanStack vector. No user data, production systems, or intellectual property were compromised; macOS updates were required for affected devices.

Significance: The fact that OpenAI — one of the world's most security-conscious AI organizations — was impacted confirms that even top-tier organizations cannot fully insulate their developer environments from supply chain compromise via trusted upstream packages. The attack targeted the exact intersection of AI frameworks (Mistral AI, Guardrails AI) and developer tooling (TanStack, UiPath, node-IPC ecosystem) that represents the highest-leverage attack surface in modern software development. This is a direct, confirmed instance of the AI developer toolchain supply chain attack vector that analysts have identified as the dominant emerging threat surface of 2026.
