# Grafana Breached via TanStack Dependency in Mini Shai-Hulud Supply Chain Wave

Grafana, the widely-used observability and dashboarding platform, was confirmed as a victim of the Mini Shai-Hulud supply chain attack wave in May 2026. The attack vector was Grafana's dependency on a compromised TanStack package. This adds Grafana to the growing list of organizations compromised through the TeamPCP-orchestrated supply chain campaign: TanStack, Mistral AI, UiPath, Guardrails AI, AntV, GitHub Actions, Nx Console, and GitHub itself. Grafana is used extensively in enterprise infrastructure monitoring and cloud operations, meaning the breach potentially exposes sensitive infrastructure telemetry and credentials in production environments.
