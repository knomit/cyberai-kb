# Fast16 Malware: Nuclear Simulation Software Tampering Campaign Targeting Research Institutions

Fast16 is a targeted malware campaign discovered in May 2026 that specifically tampers with nuclear simulation and computational physics software used at research institutions and national laboratories. The malware embeds subtle numerical errors into simulation outputs — introducing calculation drift below the threshold of casual detection — rather than causing obvious system failures. The attack is designed to corrupt research outputs over time, potentially affecting safety calculations, materials modeling, and nuclear reaction simulations without triggering immediate alerts. Fast16 was discovered through anomalous checksum discrepancies in simulation output files at multiple institutions. The campaign is assessed as state-sponsored given the high specificity of the target set and the sophistication of the tamper methodology (plausible deniability through subtle errors vs. obvious sabotage). Attribution is pending but the targeting pattern is consistent with adversaries seeking to undermine Western nuclear research capabilities.
