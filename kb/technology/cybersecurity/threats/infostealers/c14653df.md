# Infostealer Victims: 1 in 4 Has Active Corporate Infrastructure Access; 1 in 6 Gaming Infections Includes VPN/SaaS Credentials

A Flare.io report published in May 2026 found that one in four infostealer victims has active access to corporate infrastructure — including VPN credentials, SaaS sessions, and cloud platforms. Additionally, one in six gaming-related infostealer infections involves a user who also holds active credentials for corporate VPNs, SaaS platforms, or cloud environments. The data establishes that personal device compromises via gaming lures create a direct pipeline to enterprise breach. New and emerging infostealer families documented in May 2026 include Salat, Gremlin, and Reaper (an SHub macOS variant that spoofs Apple, Google, and Microsoft across a multi-stage chain to steal credentials, exfiltrate business files, and establish persistent backdoor access).
