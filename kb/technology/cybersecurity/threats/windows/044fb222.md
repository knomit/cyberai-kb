# Windows Zero-Days: BitLocker Bypass and CTFMON Privilege Escalation (May 2026)

An anonymous cybersecurity researcher who previously disclosed three Microsoft Defender vulnerabilities released two new zero-days: (1) a BitLocker bypass vulnerability, and (2) a privilege escalation vulnerability in Windows Collaborative Translation Framework Monitor (CTFMON). Both remain unpatched as of May 14, 2026.
