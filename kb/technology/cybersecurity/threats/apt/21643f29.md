# China-Linked Threat Actor Targets Azerbaijani Energy Firm via Microsoft Exchange (Dec 2025–Feb 2026)

A China-affiliated threat actor conducted a multi-wave intrusion campaign against an unnamed Azerbaijani oil and gas company between late December 2025 and late February 2026, exploiting Microsoft Exchange. The campaign marks a geographic expansion of the threat actor's targeting into the Caucasus/Central Asia energy sector.
