# MuddyWater (Iranian APT) Uses Chaos Ransomware as False Flag for Espionage (Early 2026)

Iranian state-sponsored group MuddyWater used Chaos ransomware as a cover in a new campaign detected in early 2026. Attack vector: Microsoft Teams social engineering. Operations included reconnaissance, credential harvesting, and data exfiltration, but no actual file-encrypting ransomware was deployed. Victim was added to Chaos ransomware data leak site as cover. Infrastructure and code-signing certificate evidence point to MuddyWater. Rapid7: no evidence MuddyWater is a Chaos affiliate. Tactic: false-flag ransomware attack to mask espionage and complicate attribution.
