# UAT-8616 Attributed to Cisco SD-WAN CVE-2026-20182 Exploitation: Nation-State Pre-Positioning Campaign

Cisco Talos attributed exploitation of CVE-2026-20182 (critical authentication bypass in Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Controller, CVSS 10.0) to UAT-8616, a sophisticated nation-state-adjacent threat actor. Post-compromise actions observed: adding SSH keys, modifying NETCONF configurations, and escalating to root privileges. UAT-8616 used the same post-exploitation playbook against CVE-2026-20127 (an earlier Cisco SD-WAN flaw) earlier in 2026. Rapid7 assessed that SD-WAN controllers are ideal pre-positioning targets for nation-state operators: they sit in the middle of organizational trust relationships, allow persistent access that blends into normal traffic, and enable observation, influence, and lateral pivot at strategic timing. Cisco, Fortinet, and Ivanti are among the most heavily targeted vendors in this pre-positioning campaign wave.
