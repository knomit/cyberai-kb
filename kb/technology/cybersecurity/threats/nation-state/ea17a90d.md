# Mustang Panda Delivers Updated FDMTP Backdoor to APJ Region via DLL Side-Loading

A campaign consistent with Mustang Panda tradecraft — a Chinese state-sponsored APT — was observed in May 2026 targeting the Asia-Pacific and Japan (APJ) region, delivering an updated version of the FDMTP backdoor via DLL side-loading. FDMTP is designed to connect to a remote C2 server, receive commands, profile compromised hosts, and load plugins for: scheduled task management, Windows Registry persistence, and file/command retrieval. The activity has been ongoing since September 2025. Mustang Panda typically targets government entities, NGOs, and diplomatic organizations across the Indo-Pacific.
