# Instructure/Canvas Pays Ransom to ShinyHunters: 3.65TB Breach Disrupts Thousands of Schools (May 2026)

Instructure, parent company of Canvas LMS (widely used in K-12 and higher education), reached a ransom agreement with ShinyHunters in May 2026 after the group breached Instructure's systems and threatened to leak 3.65TB of stolen data. The agreement halted the leak and included: return of stolen data, 'digital confirmation' in the form of shred logs that data was destroyed, assurances that affected institutions would not be individually extorted, and removal of the listing from ShinyHunters' data leak site. Instructure did not disclose the payment amount. The breach disrupted thousands of schools that rely on Canvas. Security experts note that ransom payments provide no reliable guarantee that stolen data was not already copied or shared before the agreement — once data is exfiltrated, shred logs are unverifiable. ShinyHunters is a high-profile, decentralized extortion group responsible for numerous major breaches across multiple sectors.
