# Instructure (Canvas LMS) Pays Ransom to ShinyHunters to Stop 3.65TB Data Leak (May 2026)

Instructure, parent company of Canvas LMS (used widely in education), reached a 'ransom agreement' with the decentralized cybercrime group ShinyHunters after a network breach. ShinyHunters had threatened to leak 3.65TB of stolen data. The agreement halted the leak. Notable as ShinyHunters is a high-profile extortion group responsible for many major breaches.
