# Instructure/Canvas Pays Ransom to ShinyHunters After Breach Disrupts Thousands of Schools

Instructure, developer of the Canvas school information portal, reached a ransom agreement with the ShinyHunters threat group in May 2026 after a breach stole a large amount of data and disrupted thousands of schools. The company did not disclose the payment amount but received 'digital confirmation' in the form of shred logs that the data was destroyed. The agreement included return of stolen data, assurances that affected institutions would not be individually extorted, and removal of the listing from ShinyHunters' data leak site. Security experts note that ransom payments provide no reliable guarantee that stolen data was not already copied or shared. The breach affected the education sector broadly, where Canvas is used by universities and K-12 districts.
