# Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Controller CVE-2026-20182: Max-Severity Auth Bypass Added to CISA KEV

Cisco released patches for CVE-2026-20182, a maximum-severity (CVSS 10.0) authentication bypass flaw in the Catalyst SD-WAN Controller that has been actively exploited in limited attacks to gain admin access. CISA added this vulnerability to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, requiring federal agencies to remediate within their mandated timelines.

SD-WAN controllers are high-value targets because they manage network routing policy across entire enterprise deployments — admin access to an SD-WAN controller provides an attacker with broad network visibility and the ability to redirect, intercept, or disrupt traffic across the organization.
