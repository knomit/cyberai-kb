# Digital.ai: 87% of Applications Attacked Within 24 Hours of AI Model Release Events

Digital.ai's 2026 application security report found that 87% of applications experience active attack attempts within 24 hours of major AI model release events. The correlation suggests that threat actors monitor AI capability announcements as signals to intensify attack campaigns — either because new AI capabilities lower the barrier to crafting exploits, or because organizations distracted by evaluating new models reduce their security vigilance during release windows. The finding has significant operational implications: security operations teams should treat major AI model releases as elevated-threat windows requiring heightened monitoring. Digital.ai tracks application-layer attacks (API abuse, injection, credential stuffing) across a large enterprise customer base.
