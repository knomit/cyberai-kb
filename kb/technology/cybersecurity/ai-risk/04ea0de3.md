# AI Hallucinations Exploiting Human Trust Creating Critical Infrastructure Security Risks (May 2026)

THN analysis: AI hallucinations are introducing serious security risks in critical infrastructure decision-making by exploiting human overtrust in AI outputs. The core issue: when AI models lack certainty, they output confident-sounding but incorrect answers, and humans in high-stakes contexts fail to apply appropriate skepticism. Signals a systemic risk as AI integrates into critical systems.
