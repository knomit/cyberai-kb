# MITRE Fight Fraud Framework (F3): first standardized taxonomy for cyber-enabled financial fraud

MITRE released the Fight Fraud Framework (F3) in April 2026 — described as 'a first-of-its-kind effort to define and standardize the tactics and techniques used in cyber-enabled financial fraud.' Full attack lifecycle covered: Reconnaissance, Resource Development, Initial Access, Defense Evasion, Positioning, Execution, and Monetization. Purpose: help financial institutions understand, detect, and prevent fraud through a shared adversary behavior framework, enabling a shift from reactive response to proactive defense. MITRE: 'Fraud actors often blend traditional cyber techniques with domain-specific fraud tactics, making a unified cyber-fraud framework essential.' Complements ATT&CK for traditional cyberattacks but focuses specifically on financially motivated fraud tradecraft.
