# North Korea steals $285M from Drift Protocol via 6-month social engineering posing as trading firm

North Korean state-linked group stole $285 million from Drift Protocol (DeFi) using a meticulously planned 6-month social engineering operation beginning fall 2025. Method: group posed as a quantitative trading firm at a major crypto conference, built trust through in-person meetings, Telegram coordination, onboarded an Ecosystem Vault with a $1M own-capital deposit. Once exploit hit, chats and malware were 'completely scrubbed.' Elliptic tracking: this is the 18th North Korea-linked crypto theft act in 2026 alone, demonstrating a systematic, industrialized state-level crypto theft program. Pattern: lengthy trust-building before exploitation is becoming the NK standard approach.
