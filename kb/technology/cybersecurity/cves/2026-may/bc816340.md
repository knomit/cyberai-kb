# Microsoft SharePoint RCE CVE-2026-45659 patched; CERT-In mandates 12-hour patch window for internet-facing flaws

Microsoft patched SharePoint RCE vulnerability CVE-2026-45659 (May 26, 2026) — exploitable without specialized conditions across multiple SharePoint Server versions. Separately: India's CERT-In issued new guidelines (May 26, 2026) mandating patching of critical internet-facing vulnerabilities within 12 hours of flagging 'where feasible', explicitly citing AI-assisted attacks as the driver. Context: Verizon DBIR 2026 showed median patch time rose to 43 days; CERT-In's 12-hour mandate represents a dramatic counter-pressure driven by AI-accelerated exploit discovery.
