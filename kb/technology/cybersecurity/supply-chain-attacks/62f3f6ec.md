# DAEMON Tools Supply Chain Attack: Trojanized Installers Since April 8, 2026 (Kaspersky)

Kaspersky disclosed a supply chain attack targeting DAEMON Tools (popular virtual drive software) in which official Windows installers were compromised with malware.

- **Affected versions**: 12.5.0.2421 to 12.5.0.2434 (trojanized since April 8, 2026)
- **Distribution**: Via the legitimate DAEMON Tools website; installers signed with valid digital certificates belonging to DAEMON Tools developers
- **Platform**: Windows only
- **Scale**: Several thousand infection attempts in 100+ countries (Russia, Brazil, Turkey, Spain, Germany, France, Italy, China among most affected)
- **Malware capabilities**: Multi-protocol C2 (HTTP, UDP, TCP, WSS, QUIC, DNS, HTTP/3); injects payloads into legitimate `notepad.exe` and `conhost.exe` processes
- **Attribution**: Evidence points to a Chinese-speaking adversary
- **Context**: Part of a pattern of 2026 supply chain attacks; follows similar breaches in eScan (January), Notepad++ (February), and CPUID (April) 2026
