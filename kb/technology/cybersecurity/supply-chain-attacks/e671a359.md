# Google Android Binary Transparency: Public Ledger to Verify App Authenticity Against Supply Chain Attacks

Google announced expanded Binary Transparency for Android — a public cryptographic ledger ensuring Google apps on Android devices are exactly what Google intended to distribute, providing a verifiable 'Source of Truth' against supply chain tampering.

- **Coverage**: Production Google applications (including Google Play Services, standalone Google apps, and Mainline OS modules)
- **Scope**: All production Android apps released after May 1, 2026 have a corresponding cryptographic entry confirming authenticity
- **Builds on**: Pixel Binary Transparency (introduced October 2021 for Pixel factory images)
- **How it works**: Public, cryptographic log records metadata about official app releases; anyone can verify that the Google software on their Android device is a production version authorized by Google and has not been modified
- **Threat addressed**: Supply chain attacks where malicious actors modify legitimate software before delivery to end users
