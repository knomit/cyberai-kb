# GlassWorm supply chain campaign dismantled by CrowdStrike, Google, and Shadowserver (May 2026)

Part of an ongoing wave of supply chain takedowns in 2026 (alongside Stark Industries/THE.Hosting seizure by FIOD, Fox Tempest by Microsoft, etc.).
