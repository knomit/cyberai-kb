# AI is simultaneously the most powerful offensive and defensive cybersecurity tool, creating a dangerous capability race

The week of Apr 20-26, 2026 crystallized the AI security paradox: Anthropic's Claude Mythos found thousands of zero-days surviving decades of human review (CyberGym score: 83.1) but was restricted to trusted orgs due to misuse risk; OpenAI chose broad deployment with GPT-5.4-Cyber for defenders; LMDeploy's CVE-2026-33626 was exploited within 13 hours of disclosure; PHANTOMPULSE RAT is itself AI-generated with blockchain-based C2. The 'barrier to entry for attackers is collapsing' (SANS Institute). Meanwhile, AI vulnerability discovery can be replicated by smaller structured systems (AISLE/Xint), suggesting the offensive edge of powerful models is temporary but the defensive use case is structurally sound. The patch gap is shrinking faster than defenders can respond.
