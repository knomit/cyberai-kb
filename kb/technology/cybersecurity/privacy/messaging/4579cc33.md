# iOS 26.5: Default End-to-End Encrypted RCS Messaging Between iPhone and Android (May 2026)

Apple released iOS 26.5 beta with end-to-end encryption (E2EE) for Rich Communication Services (RCS) messaging, enabling encrypted cross-platform messaging between iPhone and Android as part of a cross-industry effort to replace SMS. First time E2EE is available by default across the iPhone-Android messaging gap.
