# 80% of top ATT&CK techniques now focus on evasion over destruction — attackers prioritize dwell time

Analysis of 1.1 million malware samples (reported THN, May 27 2026): 80% of the top MITRE ATT&CK techniques now focus on evasion rather than destruction. Attackers have shifted strategy to prioritize dwell time — staying undetected as long as possible — over causing immediate damage. This aligns with the Verizon 2026 DBIR finding that vulnerability exploitation (not credentials) is now the #1 initial access vector, and with AI-powered morphing malware enabling longer-lived evasion.
