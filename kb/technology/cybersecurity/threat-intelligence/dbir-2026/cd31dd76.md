# Verizon 2026 DBIR: vulnerability exploitation overtakes credentials as top initial access vector

Verizon 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report (per Hacker News weekly recap): Vulnerability exploitation (31% of breaches) overtook compromised credentials (13%) as the most common initial access vector for the first time in ~2 decades. Only 26% of CISA KEV-listed critical vulns were fully remediated in 2025 (down from 38% prior year). Median full remediation time rose to 43 days (up from 32). Organizations faced 50% more critical vulnerabilities YoY. Ransomware = 48% of all breaches (up from 44% in 2024). Median ransom payment declined from $150K (2024) to ~$140K (2025).
