# Vect ransomware formalizes partnerships with BreachForums and TeamPCP — industrialized ransomware model

Vect ransomware group formalized partnerships with BreachForums cybercrime marketplace and TeamPCP hacking group. BreachForums members can deploy ransomware; TeamPCP's supply chain attack victims are handed off to Vect. Dataminr described this as 'industrialized ransomware deployment' — lowering barrier to entry, incentivizing members, and exploiting pre-existing breaches. Represents convergence of large-scale credential theft, mature RaaS, and dark web forum mobilization.
