# CISA and Allies Issue Joint Guidance: SBOM Requirements Should Extend to AI System Supply Chains

Multiple government cybersecurity agencies — including CISA and international partners — issued joint guidance in May 2026 requiring that Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) practices be extended to AI systems and their supply chains. The guidance frames SBOMs as an 'ingredients list' for AI software that enables organizations to understand their AI supply chain and make risk-informed decisions. The agencies note that because AI systems are software systems, existing SBOM minimum elements apply, but AI-specific components (model weights, training data provenance, fine-tuning datasets, inference libraries) should also be documented. The guidance arrives as AI model supply chain attacks (e.g., fake Hugging Face repositories, compromised model registries) are demonstrating that AI systems require the same supply chain rigor as traditional software.
