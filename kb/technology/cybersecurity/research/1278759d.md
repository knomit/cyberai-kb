# Fiber optic acoustic eavesdropping: DAS systems can extract private audio through FTTH cables

Researchers from Hong Kong Polytechnic University and Chinese University of Hong Kong published research uncovering a new acoustic side-channel in telecommunications optical fiber. Method: optical fibers are sensitive to acoustic vibrations; attackers with access to one end of an optical fiber can use commercially available Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) systems to remotely monitor sound-induced deformations in the fiber structure and recover the original sound waves. Critical implication: with the proliferation of Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) in modern buildings, this technique enables eavesdropping on private spaces surrounding the other end of a fiber without physical access to the premises. DAS systems are commercially available. No remote code execution or software vulnerability required — purely physical side-channel.
