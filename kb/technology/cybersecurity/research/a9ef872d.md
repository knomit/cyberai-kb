# Mandiant M-Trends 2026: Mean Time to Exploit Now Negative (Exploits Precede Patches)

Mandiant's M-Trends 2026 report found that the mean time to exploit vulnerabilities is now estimated to be negative — meaning attackers are exploiting flaws before patches are released (zero-day exploitation as the norm). Security teams have better visibility than ever but worse rates of confirming fixes actually hold. Key finding: most remediation programs never verify that a fix stayed fixed post-deployment.
