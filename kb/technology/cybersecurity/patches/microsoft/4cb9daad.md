# Microsoft May 2026 Patch Tuesday: 138 Vulnerabilities, 16 AI-Discovered by MDASH System

Microsoft's May 2026 Patch Tuesday (May 13, 2026) addressed 138 security vulnerabilities across its portfolio — 30 rated Critical — including DNS RCE and Netlogon RCE. None were publicly known or under active exploitation at release. Notably, 16 of the 138 fixed flaws were discovered by Microsoft's new MDASH (Multi-model AI-Driven Automated Security Helper), an AI system that uses multiple models to automate vulnerability discovery and remediation at scale. MDASH is in limited testing with select customers. This is the first Patch Tuesday where Microsoft publicly credited an AI system with discovering a substantial portion of the patched vulnerabilities, validating AI-assisted vulnerability research as a production-ready capability alongside human researchers.
