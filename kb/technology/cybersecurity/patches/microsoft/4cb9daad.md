# Microsoft May 2026 Patch Tuesday: 138 Vulnerabilities, 30 Critical Including DNS and Netlogon RCE

Microsoft released patches for 138 security vulnerabilities across its product portfolio on May 13, 2026 (Patch Tuesday). 30 are rated Critical. Notable flaws include DNS RCE and Netlogon RCE vulnerabilities. None of the 138 were listed as publicly known or under active exploitation at time of release. This is the same Patch Tuesday where Microsoft's MDASH AI system was credited with discovering 16 of the fixed flaws.
