# Anthropic Claude Mythos restricted to trusted orgs due to cybersecurity capability concerns

Anthropic's Claude Mythos model was only privately released to a small number of trusted organizations because of concerns it could be exploited by adversaries. Mythos reportedly found thousands of zero-days that survived decades of human review and scored 83.1 on CyberGym cybersecurity reasoning benchmark (vs GPT-5.4 at 66.3, Claude Opus 4.6 at 66.6). Unauthorized access to Mythos was also separately reported. Researchers from AISLE and Xint found that Mythos's vulnerability-discovery results could be replicated with smaller, cheaper models using structured systems.
