# OpenAI launches GPT-5.4-Cyber for digital defenders with lower refusal boundary and binary reverse engineering

OpenAI released GPT-5.4-Cyber, a model designed for digital defenders. It has a lower refusal boundary for legitimate cybersecurity work than standard GPT-5.4 and adds capabilities for advanced defensive workflows including binary reverse engineering. Unlike Anthropic's Mythos (restricted release), OpenAI opted for broad deployment. SANS Institute chief Rob T. Lee noted the AI vulnerability discovery race means 'the barrier to entry for attackers is collapsing' as AI can generate exploits from binary diff analysis.
