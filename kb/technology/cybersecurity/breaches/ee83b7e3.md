# Vercel data breach via Context.ai supply chain compromise — ShinyHunters claims responsibility

Vercel disclosed a security breach originating from the compromise of Context.ai, a third-party AI tool used by a Vercel employee. The attacker used this access to take over the employee's Google Workspace account, gaining access to Vercel environments and non-sensitive environment variables. A Context.ai employee was compromised with Lumma Stealer in Feb 2026. A Context.ai March 2026 AWS incident also exposed OAuth tokens for consumer users. ShinyHunters threat actor claimed responsibility. Classic supply chain escalation pattern: third-party AI tool → employee cloud account → internal environment access.
