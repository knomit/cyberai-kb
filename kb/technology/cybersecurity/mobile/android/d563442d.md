# Android Intrusion Logging: Google Adds Spyware Forensics Feature (May 2026)

Google unveiled a new opt-in Android feature called Intrusion Logging, part of Advanced Protection Mode. The feature stores forensic logs to better analyze sophisticated spyware attacks, enabling post-incident forensics on compromised devices. Designed to help security researchers and targeted individuals identify advanced spyware like Pegasus-class tools.
