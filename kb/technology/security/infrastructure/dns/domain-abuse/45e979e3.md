# Dropcatch domains account for roughly one in five new daily domain registrations

Infoblox coined the term 'dropcatch domains' for expired domains that are re-registered by a new party, inheriting the prior owner's traffic and reputation. In the first half of 2026, about 50,400 dropcatch domains were re-registered daily in generic TLDs like .com alone, rising to roughly 65,000 per day including country-code TLDs — nearly 20% of all daily gTLD and ccTLD registrations, i.e. one in five newly registered domains. At the TLD level .net and .xyz lead dropcatch activity, ahead of .com in third place; other prominent TLDs include .org, .vip, .online, .store, .site, .app and .shop. Median daily dropcatch volumes by registrar: GoDaddy 5,246, Namecheap 4,385, DropCatch.com 3,568.
