# INTERPOL Operation Ramz takes down Sniper Dz phishing-as-a-service platform

Group-IB reported (Thursday, in June 2026) that an INTERPOL-led operation disrupted Sniper Dz, a decade-long phishing-as-a-service (PhaaS) platform. The effort, codenamed Operation Ramz, ran from October 2025 to February 2026 and saw authorities from 13 MENA countries make 201 arrests. Among those arrested was 'Guedz,' the primary developer and administrator of Sniper Dz, by the Algerian National Police; the platform is said to have collected more than 45,000 victim records and had rebranded over the years as Joker Dz, Storm Dz, and Spam Dz. Authorities took down the PhaaS website and seized hardware containing phishing software and scripts.
