# Law enforcement disrupts Amadey and StealC; 27M credentials recovered

A coordinated law-enforcement operation, with private partners Bitdefender, Bitsight, ESET, and Microsoft, took down infrastructure powering the Amadey and StealC malware families. Per Europol, the two-week action identified, flagged, and restricted more than $47 million in criminal-origin cryptocurrency and recovered as many as 27 million stolen login credentials, aiming to disrupt the 'assembly lines' used for ransomware, financial fraud, and attacks on critical infrastructure. It came days after the Operation Endgame action against SocGholish.
