# Law enforcement disrupts Amadey and StealC infostealer infrastructure; 27M credentials recovered, $47M crypto restricted

A coordinated law-enforcement operation, working with private partners Bitdefender, Bitsight, ESET, and Microsoft, took down the infrastructure powering the Amadey and StealC malware families. Per Europol, the two-week action identified, flagged, and restricted more than $47 million in criminal-origin cryptocurrency and recovered as many as 27 million stolen login credentials. Officials framed the target as the 'assembly lines' feeding ransomware, financial fraud, and attacks on critical infrastructure - i.e. the intent was to hit the credential-supply layer that downstream intrusions depend on, rather than any single intrusion set.

It came days after the Operation Endgame action against SocGholish.

Attribution note: the two merged records disagreed on framing. One describes this as a distinct action that followed the SocGholish takedown by days; the other describes Amadey/StealC as having been disrupted 'as part of Operation Endgame'. Both can be true if the Amadey/StealC action sat under the ongoing Operation Endgame umbrella launched in 2024, but the sourcing does not settle it. Treat the Operation Endgame affiliation as probable rather than established.
