# Operation Endgame disrupts SocGholish, cleans ~14,971 WordPress sites

Dutch law enforcement (the Netherlands National High Tech Crime Unit), with counterparts from Canada, Germany, and the U.S., disrupted malicious infrastructure associated with the SocGholish malware operation and cleaned up nearly 15,000 (cited as 14,971) infected WordPress websites. The takedown is part of Operation Endgame, an ongoing international law-enforcement initiative against botnets and criminal infrastructure that was launched in 2024; officials described this as the beginning of further action against SocGholish.
