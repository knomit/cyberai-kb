# Canada's CSIS used first-of-its-kind warrant to clean botnet-infected devices

Canada's Security Intelligence Service (CSIS) used its threat reduction warrant powers for the first time in this manner to remotely alter, degrade, and destroy botnet data on infected devices on Canadian soil - including servers, SOHO routers, and IoT devices (Ring doorbells, security cameras, TVs) - to neutralize two foreign/state-linked botnets. Justice Catherine Kane of the Federal Court granted the warrant on May 1, 2024, renewed it in August 2024, and issued confidential reasons in February 2026; a redacted public version of the ruling was released June 15, 2026.
