# CryptoJS weak RNG ('Ill Bloom') drained ~$5.7M from crypto wallets

Blockchain security firm Coinspect identified CryptoJS.lib.WordArray.random() — a weak random number generator introduced in the JavaScript cryptography library about 12 years ago — as the root cause of the 'Ill Bloom' wallet drains. The function supplied weak entropy to apps that used it to generate recovery phrases. Coinspect's on-chain analysis puts measured theft across two sweeps since late May 2026 at a lower bound of roughly $5.7 million. Five affected apps were confirmed: RRWallet (discontinued, no fix), Bexo Wallet (fixed in 20.1.0), NanChat (fixed in 1.3.0), Bitcoin Libre (fixed in version 4, July 2024), and Milo (discontinued).
