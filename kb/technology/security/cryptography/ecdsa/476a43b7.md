# Anthropic research: ECDSA curve seeds traced to lrand48 seeded with Unix timestamps

Per Anthropic research (via TLDR AI), Claude revealed that the seeds of certain ECDSA curves were derived using a C library's lrand48 random number generator seeded with Unix timestamps, rather than hash algorithms or backdoors.
