# Trump executive order sets 2030/2031 federal post-quantum cryptography deadlines

On June 22, 2026, President Trump signed an executive order setting hard deadlines for federal agencies to migrate high-value/high-impact systems to post-quantum cryptography (PQC): key establishment by December 31, 2030, and digital signatures by December 31, 2031, with national security systems on a separate track. The order targets the 'harvest now, decrypt later' threat and pulls the timeline forward ~4–5 years from the prior 2035 target set by 2022's National Security Memorandum 10. The deadlines align with NIST standards finalized in August 2024 — FIPS 203 (ML-KEM, formerly CRYSTALS-Kyber) for key establishment and FIPS 204/205 for digital signatures.
