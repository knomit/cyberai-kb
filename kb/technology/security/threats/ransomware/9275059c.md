# Play ransomware uses SentinelOne's own uninstaller to disable EDR

GuidePoint Security analyzed a Play (PlayCrypt) double-extortion incident from early 2026: initial access via a compromised SonicWall VPN, then SystemBC deployment, reconnaissance, lateral movement, and exfiltration. Three behaviors are notable for defenders: domain-wide tool staging via the SYSVOL share rather than per-host delivery; EDR removal using the victim's own SentinelOne uninstallation utility rather than a kernel-driver exploit; and recovery of a crash dump from a host where the encryptor failed, providing rare forensic visibility into encryption execution.
