# INC Ransomware is dominant actor exploiting SonicWall SMA 1000 zero-days CVE-2026-15409/15410

Resecurity reported that INC Ransomware has become the 'dominant threat actor' exploiting recently disclosed SonicWall Secure Mobile Access (SMA) 1000 series VPN flaws, accelerating activity since early August 2026. The attacks chain CVE-2026-15409 and CVE-2026-15410 (weaponized as zero-days; patched by SonicWall in mid-July 2026) to achieve arbitrary command execution and device takeover. Rapid7 noted attackers extracted high-value credentials and active session databases. Per Ransomware.Live, the group has claimed 885 victims to date, the most recent listed Aug 2, 2026. Reported by The Hacker News.
