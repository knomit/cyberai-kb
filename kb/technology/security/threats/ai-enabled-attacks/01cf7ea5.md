# Multi-agent AI frameworks executed mass credential harvesting in under six hours

Google's Threat Intelligence Group Q3 2026 report found threat actors used multi-agent AI frameworks to autonomously execute a mass credential-harvesting attack in under six hours after compromising a cloud environment, compressing a timeline that previously required sustained human operator involvement.
