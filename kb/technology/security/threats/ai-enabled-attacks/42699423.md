# IBM: AI-assisted cyberattacks cost enterprises ~$1M more per breach

An IBM study found AI-assisted cyberattacks now cost enterprises about $1 million more per breach, though AI-powered defenses can cut those losses. Adoption of AI in security operations is uneven: three-fourths of surveyed organizations say frontier AI is causing them to rethink agent deployment; more than 50% use AI for threat detection and containment, but only 18% apply agents to vulnerability management.
