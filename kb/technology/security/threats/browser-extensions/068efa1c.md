# 737 malicious Chrome VPN/proxy extensions route traffic through shared SOCKS5 proxies

Security firm Socket found 737 free VPN and proxy Chrome extensions, published across at least 40 Chrome Web Store developer accounts, that mainly target Russian-speaking users seeking access to blocked services and covertly route entire browser sessions through SOCKS5 proxy infrastructure. They accumulated 75,486 installs; 274 impersonate 66 established VPN/privacy brands (including Proton VPN, NordVPN, Surfshark, ExpressVPN, CyberGhost, Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1, and Google's Outline). Researcher Kush Pandya found 520 of 522 in the bulk corpus route traffic through the same SOCKS5 infrastructure.
