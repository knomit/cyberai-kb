# UNK_CondorFiltration: TeamFiltration campaign hit 5,700+ accounts, compromised 7 unmanaged service accounts

THN (2026-09-28) citing Proofpoint: campaign UNK_CondorFiltration targeted 5,700+ accounts across 28 Microsoft 365 tenants (mainly Chilean retail/finance) from 1,487 AWS EC2 IPs in three waves late July–August 2026. It compromised 7 accounts, all unmanaged functional/service accounts with default or unrotated passwords and no MFA. Lesson: non-human identities are an exposure gap.
