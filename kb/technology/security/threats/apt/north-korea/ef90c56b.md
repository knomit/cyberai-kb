# State-sponsored campaign exploited AnySign4PC via hacked Korean sites to drop backdoors

South Korean authorities (KISA) and four security firms disclosed a state-sponsored campaign that compromised trusted domestic websites and abused the locally installed financial-security software AnySign4PC to silently infect visitors with SIGNBT or COPPERHEDGE backdoors — no prompt or user-initiated download required. Affected AnySign4PC versions are 1.1.4.4 through 1.1.4.6, fixed in 1.1.5.0. Attackers also used spear-phishing (fake resumes, recruitment, investment materials) and compromised news, healthcare, education and manufacturing sites. The 'Operation Double Barrel' report describes the actor only as state-sponsored, without formally attributing it to Lazarus.
