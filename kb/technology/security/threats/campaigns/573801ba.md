# PhantomEnigma campaign hijacked 20+ Brazilian government websites for malware delivery

ANY.RUN uncovered an active campaign dubbed PhantomEnigma in which more than 20 Brazilian government websites were hijacked and repurposed as malware delivery channels, putting banks and public agencies at risk. The attack began with fake police-themed documents presented as official communications. Trusted .gov.br links and authenticated emails helped the activity evade detection. ANY.RUN's investigation, built by correlating hundreds of seemingly unrelated sandbox sessions, revealed previously undocumented backdoor behavior, hidden infrastructure relationships, and multiple attack arms.
