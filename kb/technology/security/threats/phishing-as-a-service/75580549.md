# Mirage2FA phishing-as-a-service linked to 4,532 organization domains via Microsoft 365 AiTM

ANY.RUN research on the Mirage2FA campaign (active 2024-2026) found activity potentially linked to 4,532 unique organization email domains, with 48% of targeted email addresses potentially compromised. Mirage2FA is a commercial phishing-as-a-service toolkit that abuses legitimate Microsoft 365 login flows in an adversary-in-the-middle pattern, stealing passwords and session cookies to hijack authenticated M365 sessions and SSO-connected services, bypassing two-factor authentication. The United States accounted for 63.7% of victims, with additional activity in India, Singapore, the UK, Canada, Saudi Arabia and South Africa. Technology, manufacturing and education were the most targeted industries, and researchers uncovered more than 9,000 potential compromise events involving cookie/password theft, SSO logins and 2FA bypass.
