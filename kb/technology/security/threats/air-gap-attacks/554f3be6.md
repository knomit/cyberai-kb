# TrojPix exfiltrates data from air-gapped systems via video cable RF

Researchers at Shandong University demonstrated TrojPix, a covert channel that leaks data from air-gapped computers by subtly modulating on-screen pixels so the video cable radiates a faint radio signal a nearby receiver can decode. In tests it reached a peak throughput of 8.1 Mbps and a range of up to 208 meters (measured separately). TrojPix only works after malware is already present, serving as an exfiltration channel rather than an intrusion method.
