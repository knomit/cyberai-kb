# Refrigeration controllers (Dixell/Copeland XWEB PRO) abused as spam botnet nodes; US agencies warn of attacks

SecurityScorecard found exposed Dixell/Copeland XWEB PRO refrigeration controllers used as proxy exits and spam senders; the SMTP cluster has nine IPs, three confirmed XWEB. US agencies warned industrial refrigeration and cold-chain monitoring systems are actively targeted, risking disruption, fire hazards and inventory loss.
