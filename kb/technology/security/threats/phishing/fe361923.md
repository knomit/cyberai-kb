# Law enforcement dismantles Kratos phishing-as-a-service kit

German (Frankfurt ZIT cybercrime unit and the BKA) and US law enforcement took down the core infrastructure of Kratos, described as one of the world's most widely used criminal phishing kits, pulling 200+ servers offline; Indonesian authorities arrested the alleged developer/operator. Investigators estimate ~1,800 paying customers ran ~15,000 phishing campaigns per month. Kratos was designed to steal the session cookie along with credentials—enough to bypass two-factor authentication into Microsoft 365 accounts. ANY.RUN reverse-engineered the kit, finding two modes: a plain PHP credential harvester and a Node.js reverse proxy relaying logins to Microsoft in real time.
