# CTM360 finds 3,000+ recruitment phishing URLs using Browser-in-the-Browser traps

Cybersecurity firm CTM360 uncovered a large-scale global recruitment-themed phishing campaign spanning over 3,000 URLs that uses fake interview-scheduling pages and Browser-in-the-Browser (BitB) windows to steal Google and Facebook credentials (reported by The Hacker News, Aug 14, 2026).
