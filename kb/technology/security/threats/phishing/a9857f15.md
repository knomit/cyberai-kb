# Microsoft-led coalition takes down EvilTokens device-code phishing service (Storm-2992)

THN (2026-09-28): a law-enforcement/industry coalition led by Microsoft dismantled EvilTokens, arresting two suspected admins, taking down 50+ sites and notifying victims. Microsoft attributes it to Storm-2992; Coinbase said operators were expanding to Gmail and Okta. Its signature technique was device-code phishing: the attacker initiates the device flow and lures the victim into entering the code, granting a session without the password.
