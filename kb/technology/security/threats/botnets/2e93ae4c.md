# Google disrupts x.cpe Android TV-box botnet (1.3M+ devices) and bans Videostrong from certified Android

Late Oct 2026 a Google-led takedown cut command-and-control for x.cpe, a firmware backdoor running as a system app on Android devices (mostly TV boxes). Google says it is highly confident Shenzhen maker Videostrong intentionally built the backdoor and sold criminal access; TrendAI counted 1,472,554 infected devices across 206 models (3 Jun-1 Oct 2026). Google seized domains/servers, took down control domains of over a dozen malware families, banned Videostrong and affiliates from certified Android devices, and is tightening firmware checks. Backdoor remains on devices; owners cannot remove it themselves.
