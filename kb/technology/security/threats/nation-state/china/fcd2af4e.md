# Chinese-speaking APT CL-STA-1062 deploys TinyRCT backdoor in Southeast Asia

Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 attributed a campaign deploying a new custom backdoor called TinyRCT to a Chinese-speaking APT actor it tracks as CL-STA-1062, targeting government entities and critical infrastructure (notably state-owned enterprises in energy and government) in Southeast Asia. Unit 42 says CL-STA-1062 overlaps with UAT-7237, a group first flagged by Cisco Talos in August 2025 against web infrastructure entities in Taiwan, and has been active targeting strategic sectors in East Asia since March 2022. The attackers use a hybrid toolkit including open-source tools such as SoftEther VPN, Mimikatz, and VNT alongside custom malware.
