# FBI/CISA advisory PSA I-062626: Russian intelligence phishes Signal Backup Recovery Keys

On June 26, 2026 the FBI and CISA updated their March 2026 advisory (now PSA I-062626-PSA) warning that Russian Intelligence Services (RIS) actors tracked as UNC5792 and UNC4221 are phishing Signal users to obtain their Signal Backup Recovery Key. Once a victim hands over the key, the attacker can restore the account backup, read private and group message history, and take over the account. The key remains usable even after a new account is created on the same phone number, so the remediation is to generate a new Backup Recovery Key in Signal Settings (which invalidates the old one for future backup downloads). The FBI tied the activity to multiple RIS groups, including FSB officers embedded with the FSB Border Guards.
