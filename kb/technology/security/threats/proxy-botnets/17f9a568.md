# Google disrupts NetNut residential proxy network

Google, in collaboration with the U.S. FBI, Lumen, and other partners, disrupted the NetNut residential proxy network (also known as Popa), building on its January 2026 takedown of IPIDEA. Google disabled accounts and services NetNut used for malware command-and-control and updated Google Play Protect against associated applications.
