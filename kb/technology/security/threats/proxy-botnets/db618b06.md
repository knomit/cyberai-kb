# Google disrupts NetNut residential proxy network spanning ~2 million home devices

Google's Threat Intelligence Group, working with the U.S. FBI, Lumen and other partners, significantly degraded NetNut (also known as Popa), one of the largest networks that turns home devices into rented relays for other people's traffic. The residential proxy network spanned roughly 2 million home devices.

Google disabled accounts and services NetNut used for malware command-and-control and updated Google Play Protect against associated applications. The action built on Google's January 2026 takedown of IPIDEA.

Reported by The Hacker News, July 3, 2026. Note the verb: 'disrupted' and 'degraded', not dismantled — this is a degradation of capability, not a confirmed end of the network.
