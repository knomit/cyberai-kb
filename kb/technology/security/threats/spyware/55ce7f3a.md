# Apple warns users in 110 countries of mercenary spyware targeting

On Thursday (Aug 2026), Apple sent a fresh batch of threat notifications to customers it suspects may have been targeted by mercenary spyware attacks, alerting users across 110 countries (reported by The Hacker News, Aug 14, 2026).
