# US offers up to $10M reward for Zhang Yu, charged over HAFNIUM Exchange attacks

The US State Department Rewards for Justice program offers up to $10 million for information identifying or locating Zhang Yu, a Chinese national charged in connection with the 2021 Microsoft Exchange HAFNIUM attacks. He remains at large; charges are untested in court. The offer matches one made in January 2025.
