# 3AM ransomware hit a US school estate in a hybrid human-AI operation

Bitdefender reports 3AM ransomware compromised a US school estate (first activity Sept 10, 2026) with parts showing a human operator and others behaving like an agentic loop (a model reading each result and retrying with different commands); aside from the encryptor, built on legitimate built-in tools.
