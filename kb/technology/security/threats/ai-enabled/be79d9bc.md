# BOSSAI: single actor ran a 12-agent AI attack mesh against hundreds of orgs in 11 countries

Acronis reports an AI-augmented threat actor, believed to be one individual in Hualien City, Taiwan, used a mesh of 12 AI agents called BOSSAI (combining HexStrike AI, Hermes, CyberStrikeAI, HalberdStrike with Grok, OpenClaw and Claude Code) to target hundreds of organizations in eleven countries (emergency services, banks, universities, military systems, e-commerce, gambling). Agents pulled emergency dispatch records covering seven Taipei districts. Motive unclear, presumably monetization.
