# FortiBleed campaign compromised 80,000+ Fortinet VPN/firewall devices

A large-scale campaign codenamed FortiBleed has systematically targeted and compromised Fortinet FortiGate firewall and SSL VPN gateway devices worldwide. According to SOCRadar (reported in The Hacker News Weekly Recap, June 22 2026), it has run since at least February 2026, with over 80,000 devices identified as having working usernames and passwords, tested by suspected Russian-speaking threat actors using automated tools running around the clock.
