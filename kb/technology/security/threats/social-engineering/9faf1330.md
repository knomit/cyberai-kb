# Russian intelligence used fake support texts to steal Ukrainian messaging accounts

Ukraine's Security Service (SSU), together with the U.S. FBI, uncovered a long-running campaign by Russian intelligence services that used fake technical-support text messages to break into victims' messaging accounts and steal credentials.
