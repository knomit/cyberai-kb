# Email bombing plus Microsoft Quick Assist used as a pre-ransomware access chain

ZeroBEC documented an email-bombing campaign hitting at least 10 users at one organization with ~3,000 messages per day, largely genuine verification and registration confirmations triggered by submitting victim addresses to unrelated public platforms. About 36 hours later, attackers posed as IT help desk over Microsoft Teams; a local-administrator employee granted Microsoft Quick Assist access, enabling deployment of the Xray-core reverse proxy. Attackers then mimicked a Windows security update to trigger a local credential prompt, captured domain credentials, and uploaded artifacts to an external Microsoft Dev Tunnel, followed by domain reconnaissance and attempted NTLM relay against certificate enrollment. The tradecraft overlaps with Aurora ransomware operations.
