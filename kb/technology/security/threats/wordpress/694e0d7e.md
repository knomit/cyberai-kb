# Exposed server reveals WP-SHELLSTORM WordPress backdoor operation

Per The Hacker News, a cybercrime crew left one of its own servers exposed on the internet for three weeks, revealing the WP-SHELLSTORM operation's hacking tools, activity logs, and target lists naming more than 1.4 million WordPress sites being backdoored.
