# FortiBleed campaign compromises 86,644 FortiGate devices; CISA warning

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) urged Fortinet customers with FortiGate appliances to secure against an ongoing campaign codenamed FortiBleed, believed to be the work of Russian-speaking threat actors. As of June 19, 2026, the number of compromised devices stood at 86,644. Per SOCRadar data, generic admin accounts (35%) and built-in Fortinet system accounts (28.3%) made up the majority of compromised credentials, with organization-specific accounts at 36.7% — pointing to widespread failure to rename default accounts or rotate factory credentials.
