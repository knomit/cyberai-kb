# ted backdoor compiled into trojanized HAProxy builds in South Korea

Rapid7 Labs found a previously undocumented Linux backdoor named "ted" compiled directly into trojanized HAProxy builds at two South Korean organizations in the automotive and media sectors, attributed with medium confidence to North Korean state-sponsored actors. Compiling the implant into the binary at build time defeats file-comparison detection against a known-good binary.
