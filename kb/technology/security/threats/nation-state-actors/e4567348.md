# Russian group GTG-20006 used Claude to auto-rebuild its toolkit when detected

Anthropic disrupted a campaign by Russian state-sponsored group GTG-20006, linked to Midnight Blizzard/APT29/Cozy Bear, which used Claude to build an AI-driven workflow that automatically rebuilds and redeploys its toolkit — two Windows implants, a mobile exploitation kit and a credential stealer — whenever detected. Targets included Ukrainian and European military intelligence, diplomatic and defense organizations. Detection-driven rebuild inverts the economics of indicator-based defense.
