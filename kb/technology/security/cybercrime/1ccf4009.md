# Cordial Spider / Snarky Spider: vishing-MFA bypass → SaaS lateral movement via single session

Two cybercrime groups, Cordial Spider and Snarky Spider, are carrying out 'rapid, high-impact attacks' operating almost entirely within SaaS environments, leaving minimal traces. Attack chain: voice calls/SMS/emails direct employees to phishing pages mimicking employer SSO portals → credential capture → remove victim's MFA devices → replace with attacker-controlled devices → delete alert emails. From a single authenticated session, attackers move laterally across entire SaaS ecosystems. Residential proxy networks used to blend as legitimate home user traffic. CrowdStrike: 'This is part of a larger trend of English-speaking ransomware crews that share similar playbooks but are branching off into their own distinct groups.'
