# Finnish police arrest Peter Stokes (Scattered Spider 'Bouquet'), 19, dual US-Estonian citizen

Finnish authorities arrested Peter Stokes, alias 'Bouquet,' aged 19 and a dual US-Estonian citizen, as he attempted to board a flight to Japan (May 2026). US prosecutors charged him as a key member of Scattered Spider, facing multiple counts of wire fraud, conspiracy, and computer intrusion. Scattered Spider is the notorious English-speaking cybercrime group behind major enterprise SaaS attacks.
