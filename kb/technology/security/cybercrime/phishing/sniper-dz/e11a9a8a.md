# INTERPOL Operation Ramz dismantles Sniper Dz phishing-as-a-service platform

An INTERPOL-led operation codenamed Operation Ramz (October 2025–February 2026) disrupted Sniper Dz, a decade-old phishing-as-a-service platform active since at least 2015, per Group-IB (announced Thursday, reported Jun 12, 2026). Authorities from 13 MENA countries made 201 arrests, including 'Guedz', the primary developer/administrator, arrested by the Algerian National Police. The platform had collected more than 45,000 victim records and previously rebranded as Joker Dz, Storm Dz, and Spam Dz. Its website and hardware with phishing scripts were seized.
