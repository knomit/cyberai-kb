# Google sues Chinese smishing network abusing Gemini

Google took legal action against operators of a phishing-as-a-service operation that weaponized Gemini to generate fraudulent phishing pages and run mass SMS phishing (smishing) campaigns impersonating brands (fake brokerage-account alerts, carrier rewards). The turn-key 'Outsider' toolkit costs $88 per week or $200 per month and offers 290+ pre-built templates mimicking legitimate sites to steal passwords, MFA codes, and financial information in real time. Reported in The Hacker News June 2026 Weekly Recap.
