# Google sues Chinese smishing network accused of using Gemini AI for phishing

On Friday June 13, 2026, Google said it is pursuing legal action against a Chinese cybercrime network accused of using Google's Gemini AI agent to send phishing text messages targeting Americans. Google said the network developed and managed a phishing-as-a-service (PhaaS) kit called Outsider, and weaponized Gemini to generate fraudulent phishing pages and deploy mass SMS phishing ('smishing') attacks, often impersonating legitimate brands with lures about 'brokerage account issues' or carrier 'rewards' to steal personal and financial information.
