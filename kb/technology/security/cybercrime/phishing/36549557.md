# Sniper Dz phishing-as-a-service targets MENA users via fake Facebook offers

Group-IB researchers disclosed fraudulent activity targeting Middle East and North Africa (MENA) users via fake Facebook accounts impersonating politicians, public figures, and trusted organizations, promoting fake free mobile internet, financial compensation, and government subsidy offers. Victims clicking embedded links were redirected through intermediary sites to phishing and traffic-monetization infrastructure. Group-IB tied the campaigns to Sniper Dz, a turnkey phishing-as-a-service platform that was taken down in an INTERPOL-led operation the prior month but appears to persist. Reported by The Hacker News, June 2026.
