# Ransomware negotiator sentenced to 70 months for aiding BlackCat

Per The Hacker News, a 41-year-old former ransomware negotiator was sentenced to 70 months (nearly six years) in US prison for conspiring with the now-defunct BlackCat ransomware operators to extort multiple victims.
