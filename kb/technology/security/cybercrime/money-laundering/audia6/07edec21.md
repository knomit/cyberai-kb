# Europol disrupts AudiA6 crypto-laundering service used by ransomware gangs

Europol announced (reported Jun 12, 2026) the disruption of AudiA6, a cryptocurrency laundering service launched in 2021 and used by ransomware gangs and cybercriminal networks. The service is estimated to have laundered more than €336 million (~$389 million) in illicit profits. Its operators are also suspected of running the dark web cybercrime forum Dark2Web. The coordinated action on June 10, 2026 included the arrest of two alleged operators.
