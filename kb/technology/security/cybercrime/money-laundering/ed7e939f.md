# DoJ seizes HuiOne cloud account; Treasury sanctions Prince Group network

The U.S. Department of Justice announced the seizure of a cloud computing account used by subsidiaries of Cambodia-based conglomerate HuiOne Group, while the Treasury unveiled sanctions against nine individuals and 26 entities linked to Prince Group. The subsidiaries allegedly helped launder proceeds of cryptocurrency investment frauds and cyber scams. The seized account hosted backend infrastructure for subsidiaries including HuiOne Guarantee (aka Haowang Guarantee), which ran an illicit Telegram-based marketplace that transacted billions of dollars between 2021 and 2025 selling crimeware tools and stolen data.
