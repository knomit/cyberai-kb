# Anthropic disrupted model distillation attacks from seven China-based AI labs

Anthropic disrupted industrial-scale illicit distillation attacks against Claude originating from seven China-based AI labs, including Alibaba, Moonshot, DeepSeek, Z.ai (Zhipu) and MiniMax. The operations used networks of fake accounts built on stolen credit cards, credentials and API keys to covertly extract Claude's capabilities into their own models.
