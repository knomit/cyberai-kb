# Operation CameraSwarm compromised 14,530+ Dahua devices via credential attacks, 2021 auth bypasses, and P2P relay

Hunt.io disclosed 'Operation CameraSwarm', which compromised more than 14,530 Dahua devices between June 17 and July 22, 2026. Reconstructed from a 407 MB exposed working directory holding 2,616 files across 234 subdirectories (tooling, logs, shell history, campaign records). Confirmed compromises concentrated in Ukraine and Russia. Three attack paths: credential attacks (12,324 unique IPs across 13,229 campaign records); authentication bypass using CVE-2021-33044 and CVE-2021-33045 (1,923 cameras, also configured with a persistent account); and P2P relay (283 cameras identified by serial number, including devices behind NAT). Dahua rates the two 2021 auth-bypass flaws 8.1 CVSS while NVD assigns each 9.8; Dahua says attackers 'bypass device identity authentication by constructing malicious data packets.' Mitigations: apply fixed firmware and disable P2P where not required.
