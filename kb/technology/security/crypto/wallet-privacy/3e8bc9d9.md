# KU Leuven study: 85 browser-extension crypto wallets leak data enabling tracking

Researchers at KU Leuven tested 85 of the most popular crypto wallets that run as browser extensions and found the wallets themselves leak enough information to link and track the people using them, exposing address leaks and cross-site tracking risks. Reported by The Hacker News.
