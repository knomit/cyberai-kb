# Eye Security pushed 2M+ updates to disable abandoned Time4Popcorn updater

Between November 2025 and March 2026 Dutch firm Eye Security pushed more than 2 million updates to machines it did not own to switch off the Updater.exe background updater of Time4Popcorn, an abandoned Popcorn Time copy, which it said could have let an outsider take over machines. Eye found it during a threat hunt when Updater.exe kept contacting an unregistered web address; it registered the domain itself and observed incoming requests. The legal Popcorn Time project from early 2025 is unrelated. Raises legal/ethical questions about unsolicited remote remediation. Reported by The Hacker News 2026-10-24.
