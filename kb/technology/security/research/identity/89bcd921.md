# Varonis TrustSink: rogue external MFA provider captures passwords and survives resets

THN (2026-09-28) citing Varonis: an attacker with high privileges can register a rogue External Authentication Method and place a password page in the legitimate sign-in flow; it captures plaintext passwords while returning a valid signed token. Resetting a captured password does not remove the rogue provider, which captures the replacement.
