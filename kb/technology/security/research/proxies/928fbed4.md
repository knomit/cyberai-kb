# Residential proxy traffic exited through White House network 236 times in 30 days

Layer3 Intel found residential proxy traffic exited via the White House network (AS2650) 236 times from Oct 5 to Nov 3, 2026, via 28 proxy services across six addresses. Synthient linked it to devices running proxy SDKs; the suspected root cause is the Rave streaming app, which bundles 12 proxy SDKs. Data shows exits, not the person behind them.
