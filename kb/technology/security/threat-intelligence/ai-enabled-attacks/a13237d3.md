# Google Threat Intelligence reports AI integrated across attack stages but no fully autonomous pipelines in the wild yet

Google Threat Intelligence Group reported in September 2026 that over the past quarter threat actors moved beyond simple prompt-based LLM interactions to integrate AI capabilities into multiple stages of the attack lifecycle, upgrading script-based automation into highly autonomous systems that reason through complex tasks and make dynamic decisions without human oversight. Critically, GTIG said it 'has not yet observed threat actors deploying fully autonomous pipelines against targets in the wild' — it characterizes agentic AI adoption as 'a gradual maturation of tradecraft', with adversaries using commercial and open-weight models to turn public disclosures and patch delays into working N-day exploit code and progressing toward multi-stage exploit chains.
