# Fortinet products are a concentrated attack surface in 2026

Through the first half of 2026, Fortinet's product line stands out as a concentrated, repeatedly targeted attack surface. The FortiBleed campaign (attributed to Russian-speaking actors, ~86,644 FortiGate firewall/SSL-VPN appliances compromised as of June 19 2026, prompting a CISA warning) was driven largely by unrotated default/built-in credentials (generic admin 35%, built-in Fortinet system accounts 28.3%). In parallel, three FortiSandbox flaws (CVE-2026-39813 path-traversal auth bypass, CVE-2026-39808 and CVE-2026-25089 OS command injection, all CVSS 9.1) were exploited in the wild. The combined signal: internet-facing Fortinet appliances demand priority patching and immediate elimination of default/factory credentials.
