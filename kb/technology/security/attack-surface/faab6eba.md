# Persistent OAuth tokens from AI/productivity tools: major unmanaged attack surface

Every AI tool, workflow automation, and productivity app connected to Google or Microsoft accounts leaves behind persistent OAuth tokens with no expiration date and no automatic cleanup. These represent a permanently open back door for attackers. THN (May 5, 2026) highlights this as one of the most underappreciated attack surfaces in enterprise environments as AI tool adoption accelerates.
