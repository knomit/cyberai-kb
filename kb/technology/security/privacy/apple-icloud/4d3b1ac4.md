# Apple to scan 'Anyone with the Link' iCloud shares for harmful content from version 27.3

Apple (support doc, Nov 3 2026) will check iCloud Photos/Drive items shared via 'Anyone with the Link' for harmful content starting with iOS/iPadOS/macOS/visionOS 27.3. Such sharing disables end-to-end encryption for that item even with Advanced Data Protection on. Does not apply to private, non-link shares.
