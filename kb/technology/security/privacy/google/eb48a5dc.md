# Google privacy policy (effective 2026-10-16) allows ad personalization via AI Search/Gemini cross-service data access

Google updated its privacy policy effective October 16, 2026 to state that if a user allows services such as Search or Gemini Apps to access data from other Google services (Photos, Gmail, Drive), Search may process that data to personalize content, including ads. Google also states it does not show personalized ads based on content from Drive, Gmail or Photos unless the user chooses to make data available for that. This applies only to users who opt in to such cross-service access. Reported by The Hacker News 2026-10-24.
