# AliExpress accused of WebAudio fingerprinting; Firefox says its protections block the technique

Developer Matt Callaghan accused Alibaba's AliExpress of tracking web users by playing sounds through browsers vulnerable to audio fingerprinting. He discovered it after investigating why his Bluetooth headphones stopped playing music when he visited AliExpress: audio from his phone stopped shortly after the homepage loaded, closing the tab fixed it immediately, and muting the tab, Firefox, or Windows did not help despite no visible media on the page. Firefox stated on X that its anti-fingerprinting technology blocks the technique; Mozilla security lead Tom Ritter said the WebAudio constant was made fixed in Firefox 118 three years ago as part of initial Fingerprinting Protection features, eliminating most differences.
