# DigiCert 2026 survey: enterprise AI adoption outrunning security

A DigiCert survey of 1,001 IT and cybersecurity leaders across the US, UK, and Australia (reported July 2026) found nearly 80% of enterprises encountered AI-related security issues in the prior six months. About 50% experienced at least one incident caused by an unauthorized or misconfigured AI agent, and another 28% found AI-related vulnerabilities without an incident. While 90% had discussed AI governance, only ~50% had dedicated budgets and formal governance programs; nearly half lacked full visibility into how AI systems reach outputs. Over six months, 75% of organizations deployed four or more AI-powered systems and 35% deployed more than 10; 86% reported processes to revoke access to compromised AI systems. Science/technology organizations reported the highest incident rate.
