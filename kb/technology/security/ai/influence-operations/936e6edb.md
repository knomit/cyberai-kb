# OpenAI disrupted Russian and Iranian AI-enabled false-front influence operations

OpenAI reported ending two influence operations, one Russian and one Iranian. The Iranian one created personas of seven journalists to pitch outlets and generated comments about the US-Iran war; the Russian one recruited people in Latin America to run an on-the-ground think tank using fake documents and audio scripts. OpenAI said they resembled pre-AI influence operations with AI easing workflows.
