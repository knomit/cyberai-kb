# OpenAI rolling out ChatGPT Lockdown Mode against prompt-injection exfiltration

OpenAI has begun rolling out a 'Lockdown Mode' for eligible personal ChatGPT accounts that limits tools capable of enabling data exfiltration, reducing the risk from prompt-injection attacks. It targets higher-risk users. Reported by The Hacker News, June 8, 2026.
