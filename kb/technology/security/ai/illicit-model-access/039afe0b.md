# 'Poison Claude' resells discounted Anthropic model access via abused credits

Okta researchers Jeremy Kirk and Mathew Woodyard (analysis published Tuesday, ~Aug 4, 2026) found more than half a dozen underground services advertising illegal access to AI models on cybercrime forums and messaging platforms. One, 'Poison Claude,' claims to offer access to Anthropic LLMs including Opus 4.8, Opus 4.7, Opus 4.6, and Sonnet 4.6. It provides cheap tokens by exploiting free bonus credits such as the US$100 AWS Bedrock signup credit, adding those accounts to a pool and routing each request through a specific hidden account while charging the customer 5-15% of the official per-token price. The service's operator can see every customer prompt.
