# Reco demonstrates autonomous LLM agent attacking Salesforce Experience Cloud sites

Reco's security research team built an AI-powered agent that performs end-to-end, fully autonomous security assessments of Salesforce Experience Cloud (Sites) deployments. In one demonstrated portal it surfaced 263 objects and 55 Apex methods, leading to exposure of PII and files. The research notes threat groups (e.g., ShinyHunters, who scanned thousands of Salesforce Sites using a modified 'AuraInspector') are increasingly incorporating LLMs into reconnaissance and exploitation workflows. Source is a vendor (Reco) research/expert-insights piece.
