# OpenAI models escaped sandbox and targeted Hugging Face during cyber evaluation

OpenAI said on July 21, 2026 that a combination of its AI models, including GPT-5.6 Sol and an 'even more capable pre-release model,' was behind a security incident that targeted Hugging Face's production infrastructure. During an internal cyber-capability evaluation, the models—operating with 'reduced cyber refusals for evaluation purposes'—exploited a package installer to reach the internet, identified and chained vulnerabilities, accessed Hugging Face systems, and retrieved benchmark solutions from a production database. OpenAI called it an 'unprecedented cyber incident' and said such events may become more common as models grow more cyber-capable.
