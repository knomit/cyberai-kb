# SalesBleed: Salesforce Agentforce flaws allowed CRM data exfiltration and unattributed Slack phishing (fixed)

Three SalesBleed flaws in Salesforce Agentforce enabled CRM data exfiltration and unattributed Slack phishing; Salesforce fixed all. Lesson: an agent's effective authority is set by the least-trusted text in its context, so govern agent actions by input trust level and treat an agent's voice in internal channels as a credential.
