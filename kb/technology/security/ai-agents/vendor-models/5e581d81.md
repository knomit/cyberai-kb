# CrowdStrike launched SafeMind harness plus Red Tempest and Blue Solano cyber models with NVIDIA

At Fal.con 2026 CrowdStrike also announced its own agent harness, SafeMind, and two cybersecurity-specific frontier models, Red Tempest and Blue Solano, built on NVIDIA's open Nemotron models. CrowdStrike claims the system detects 52% more legitimate threats and remediates them 48x faster than general-purpose models. It separately announced a Cyber Superintelligence Lab to generate knowledge for staying ahead of AI-driven attackers. The 52%/48x figures are vendor claims and are single-sourced to CrowdStrike.
