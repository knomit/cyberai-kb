# Anthropic's September 2026 threat report: nine months of Claude misuse across seven harm areas, including blocked bioweapons-adjacent research — and a scoping note that NO case involved Fable- or Mythos-class models

MERGED from three records of the SAME Anthropic Threat Intelligence report published 10–11 September 2026. One carried the harm-area taxonomy and a case study, one the reporting window and a load-bearing scoping note, one the biological-misuse finding and its verification caveat. All three are preserved.

## SCOPE AND WINDOW
The report presents case studies of operations Anthropic identified and disrupted where threat actors attempted to use Claude for malicious activity, covering DISRUPTIONS BETWEEN DECEMBER 2025 AND AUGUST 2026, and traces how misuse patterns evolved since prior reports. Actors are labelled GENERATIVE THREAT GROUPS (GTGs), spanning STATE-SPONSORED GROUPS, FINANCIALLY MOTIVATED CRIMINALS, COMMERCIAL SPYWARE VENDORS, STATE PROPAGANDA INSTITUTIONS and POLITICALLY MOTIVATED INDIVIDUALS.

## THE SEVEN HARM AREAS
CYBER OPERATIONS, INFLUENCE OPERATIONS, SURVEILLANCE, SCAMS AND FRAUD, BIOLOGICAL MISUSE, CONVENTIONAL WEAPONS DEVELOPMENT, and DISTILLATION.

## THE MODEL-CLASS SCOPING NOTE — load-bearing
A specific note in the report: NONE OF THE DOCUMENTED MISUSE CASES INVOLVED CLAUDE FABLE OR MYTHOS-CLASS MODELS. That absence reflects THE OBSERVED CASELOAD DURING THE REPORTING WINDOW, NOT a claim that those model classes are immune. Do not cite the absence as evidence of safety.

## CASE STUDY — CREDENTIAL HARVESTING AT SCALE
GTG-50014 (aka MeowSHA), a French-speaking operator and suspected ShinyHunters affiliate, ran a distributed credential-harvesting pipeline that MASS-DOWNLOADED 1.8 MILLION DISTINCT ANDROID APKs, scanned them for HARD-CODED SECRETS, and forwarded findings to a Telegram group.

## BIOLOGICAL MISUSE — the finding and its caveat
Anthropic states it THWARTED SEVERAL INSTANCES of users conducting research that could have helped develop BIOLOGICAL WEAPONS. THE CAVEAT IS LOAD-BEARING: this is Anthropic's own account of misuse it detected and blocked on its own platform; there is NO INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION of the number of attempts, their severity, or how close any came to succeeding. It is evidence that PLATFORM-SIDE MISUSE MONITORING IS CATCHING CBRN-ADJACENT QUERIES — not evidence about the base rate of such attempts across the industry.

## ANTHROPIC'S FRAMING
AI is LOWERING THE BAR for conducting these operations rather than creating wholly new capabilities.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN
The whole report is a vendor's account of activity on its own platform, surfaced by its own detection. It says nothing about misuse that evaded detection, and nothing about other providers. Individual case studies documented in the same report are recorded separately — see kb/technology/security/ai-models/distillation-abuse/3abb21ba.md (the seven China-based labs distillation claim) and kb/technology/security/ai-agents/offensive-use/0b0985f2.md (the GTG-20006 / Midnight Blizzard evasion loop).
