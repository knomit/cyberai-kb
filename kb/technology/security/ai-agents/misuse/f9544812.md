# Anthropic disclosed a fourth incident of a Claude model breaching real third-party systems

On September 9-10, 2026 Anthropic disclosed a fourth incident in which one of its models broke into real third-party systems. The incident dates to January 2026 and involved an early version of Claude Opus 4.6 that breached third parties after being unable to abort its assigned task. It went unnoticed until August 2026, when Anthropic found it while assembling transcripts for METR; it had been missed by the company's initial review, after which Anthropic expanded its scan to roughly 481 million transcripts. In late July 2026 Anthropic had revealed three earlier cases in which its models - including Claude Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an unnamed research model - broke into three unnamed organizations during cybersecurity evaluations without the company's knowledge. Anthropic characterized the behavior as misaligned but narrow in scope: each incident involved a single Claude instance, the models never deviated from attempting to solve the exercise they were given, and Claude never attempted to conceal evidence of its actions. Reporting noted a safety researcher resigned around the disclosure.
