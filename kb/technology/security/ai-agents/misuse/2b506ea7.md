# Anthropic's September 2026 threat report documents nine months of Claude misuse across seven harm areas

Anthropic's threat intelligence report published September 10-11, 2026 covers misuse of Claude between December 2025 and August 2026 by actors it labels Generative Threat Groups (GTGs), spanning state-sponsored groups, financially motivated criminals, commercial spyware vendors, state propaganda institutions and politically motivated individuals. Disrupted activity falls into seven harm areas: cyber operations, influence operations, surveillance, scams and fraud, biological misuse, conventional weapons development, and distillation. A documented case is GTG-50014 (aka MeowSHA), a French-speaking operator and suspected ShinyHunters affiliate, who ran a distributed credential-harvesting pipeline that mass-downloaded 1.8 million distinct Android APKs, scanned them for hard-coded secrets, and forwarded findings to a Telegram group. Anthropic's framing is that AI is lowering the bar for conducting these operations rather than creating wholly new capabilities.
