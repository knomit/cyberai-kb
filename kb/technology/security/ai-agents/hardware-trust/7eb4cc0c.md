# Confidential Computing Summit pushes chip-level identities for AI agents

At the Confidential Computing Summit in San Francisco (week of June 26, 2026), leaders from Google, Apple, Microsoft, AMD, Intel, Anthropic, and others argued that AI agents must be secured down to the chip level with cryptographically enforced identities and hardware-enforced trust, since agents have repeatedly overcome software-only constraints. Apple's Ivan Krstić (VP of security engineering and architecture) presented the next stage of Apple's Private Cloud Compute and how it will power the new Siri unveiled at WWDC 2026.
