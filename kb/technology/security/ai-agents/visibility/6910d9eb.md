# Veeam: 70% of orgs say AI workflows touch sensitive data without full oversight

Research from Veeam, cited by The Hacker News (Sept 2026), found that 70% of organizations admit AI agent workflows are already in contact with sensitive corporate data without full oversight in place, and 67% report that IT cannot fully track the autonomous workflows employees are building (shadow AI). This is presented as the visibility gap that needs to be closed before zero-trust controls can be meaningfully enforced on AI agents.
