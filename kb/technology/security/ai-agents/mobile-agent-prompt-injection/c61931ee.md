# Invisible screen text can hijack open-source Android AI agents into host-PC RCE

Researchers demonstrated that an Android app able to draw over other windows and write to shared storage can inject instructions—rendered in text no human can see—into the AI agent driving the phone, chaining to arbitrary command execution on the PC controlling the agent. The paper described this plus six other attacks against five open-source mobile agent frameworks (AppAgent, AppAgentX, Mobile-Agent-v3, Open-AutoGLM, MobA); every framework fell to at least six of the seven attacks. Authors are from Simon Fraser University, the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shandong University, and the Xingtu Lab at Chinese security firm QAX. The paper went to arXiv on July 1, 2026 (revised July 14). No CVEs were assigned and there is no evidence of exploitation outside a controlled setting.
