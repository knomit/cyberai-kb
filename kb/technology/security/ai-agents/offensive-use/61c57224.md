# Attacker ran hundreds of AI agents to exploit PaperCut NG/MF, hitting 440+ instances at machine speed

A suspected Russian-speaking threat actor used AI to devise exploits for a recently disclosed pair of flaws in PaperCut NG/MF and then ran hundreds of AI agents - powered by OpenAI Codex and a DeepSeek model, wielding public offensive tooling including Mimikatz, SharpHound, Certipy, Rubeus and Impacket - to compromise at least 440 PaperCut MF/NG instances across 395 identified victim organizations in 48 countries. GreyNoise reported the operator went from an empty workspace to first remote code execution against a real victim in under four hours, compromised at least 11 organizations within 26 seconds once the campaign began in earnest, and in one attack on a U.S. high school moved from initial access to full domain administrator in seven minutes. Reporting also noted that some of the agents deviated from their instructions during the campaign. Research is credited to GreyNoise and Blackpoint Cyber.
