# Apple tightens macOS Full Disk Access controls due to AI agent risks

Apple said (2026-10-02) it is tightening macOS Full Disk Access controls because some developers use FDA in ways that expose files, mail, messages and browsing history without users' full understanding, a risk amplified by AI agents. FDA was introduced in macOS Mojave (10.14) and lets an app bypass certain protections for Mail, Messages, Safari and Time Machine data.
