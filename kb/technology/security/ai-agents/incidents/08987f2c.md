# Attacker ran Hermes AI agent unattended against Thai Ministry of Finance

An attacker installed a popular AI assistant (Hermes) on a rented server, disabled the setting requiring permission before running risky commands, and pointed it at Thailand's Ministry of Finance for autonomous post-exploitation activity. This illustrates the risk of AI agents run without human-in-the-loop approval. Reported by The Hacker News, July 2026.
