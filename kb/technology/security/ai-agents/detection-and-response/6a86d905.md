# CrowdStrike launched Falcon Guardian for AI agent security at Fal.con 2026

At its Fal.con event in Las Vegas on 2026-09-01, CrowdStrike unveiled Falcon Guardian, an enterprise platform for discovering, monitoring and containing AI agents on a network. It provides automatic discovery of approved and shadow agents with a live inventory, endpoint telemetry capturing the full causal chain of agent actions, per-device agent access control and policy enforcement, and real-time containment of compromised or malicious agents. CrowdStrike positions it as a new product category it calls AIDR (AI detection and response). The product grows out of CrowdStrike's September 2025 acquisition of Pangea.
