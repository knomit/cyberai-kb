# Anthropic unreleased model chained browser and OS flaws into working exploits

Per The Hacker News (2026-10-28): Anthropic's Frontier Red Team reported an unreleased model that found flaws in Chrome, Safari, Android, Windows and Linux and chained them into five lab proof-of-concept exploit chains (e.g. launching Calculator on a stock Mac via Chrome on page load). All flaws were reported and patched; no real-world attacks observed. The capability claim rests on Anthropic's own account; vendor patch credits are verifiable.
