# Five Eyes warns AI-enabled cyberattacks are 'months, not years' away

The Five Eyes cybersecurity alliance (intelligence agencies of the US, UK, New Zealand, Australia and Canada) issued a joint statement urging leaders to 'act now' on AI cyber threats, warning that AI models capable of taking down businesses and governments are mere months away: 'The timeline is not years, it is months.' Frontier models are expected to exceed industry expectations and transform both cyber offense and defense, shrinking the window between vulnerability discovery and exploitation. The alliance urged prioritizing cybersecurity fundamentals: reducing attack surfaces, strong identity controls, fixing legacy-system vulnerabilities, and accelerating patching.
