# Bitdefender 2026: 55.2% of breached orgs pressured to stay quiet

As reported by The Hacker News (Jul 2026), Bitdefender's 2026 cybersecurity assessment found that 55.2% of breached respondents were told to keep the breach quiet, highlighting a governance gap in incident response and breach transparency.
