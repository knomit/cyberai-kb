# Z.ai delayed GLM-5.3's weight release for two weeks of safety evaluation with vetted partners

Rather than the permanent gating OpenAI and Anthropic use (requiring organizations to register before accessing their most exploit-capable models), Z.ai chose a temporary guardrail: it published GLM-5.3's CyberGym and ExploitBench scores and released weights only after roughly two weeks of safety evaluation with vetted security partners. GLM-5.2 used an MIT license; GLM-5.3's license was not announced at launch. This is a distinct third option in the open-weights release debate: disclosure plus a time-boxed embargo rather than permanent access control or unrestricted release.
