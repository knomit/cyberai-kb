# SANS: 78% of security practitioners use AI in their strategy but only 36% have a formal AI risk program

A SANS survey of 536 security professionals found 78% now say AI is part of their cybersecurity strategy, up from 50% the previous year, while just 36% have a formal AI risk program — a widening gap between AI adoption and AI governance in security teams.
