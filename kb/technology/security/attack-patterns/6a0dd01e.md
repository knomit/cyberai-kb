# 84% of cyberattacks misuse legitimate tools — 700K+ incidents analyzed

THN analysis of over 700,000 incidents finds that 84% of attacks involve misuse of legitimate tools (Living-off-the-Land/LotL techniques), reducing detection effectiveness. The predominance of LotL attacks makes signature-based defenses inadequate. This reinforces the attack surface pattern where trusted tooling (OAuth tokens, CI/CD pipelines, Teams, SaaS sessions) is weaponized rather than novel malware.
