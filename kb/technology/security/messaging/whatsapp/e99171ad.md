# WhatsApp begins global username reservations June 2026

On June 29, 2026, WhatsApp announced the start of global username reservations, letting its 3B+ users connect via an optional username instead of sharing phone numbers. It is a privacy feature (no browsable directory; contacts need the exact username), with a username generator and optional username key, and general availability planned later in 2026.
