# Citizen Lab: Russia used Cellebrite on activist Pivovarov's iPhone after sales cutoff

Citizen Lab published findings on June 25, 2026 that Russian authorities used Cellebrite's UFED forensic tools to extract data from the iPhone 12 of detained opposition activist Andrey Pivovarov in June 2021 — roughly three months after Cellebrite said it would stop selling its tools and services to Russia and Belarus. The conclusion rests on traces on the device plus an official Russian government report naming the tool. Pivovarov, who ran the Kremlin-designated 'undesirable' group Open Russia, was detained at St. Petersburg airport on May 31, 2021; investigators searched the extracted data for political contacts and activist organizations.
