# Silver Fox (China): ABCDoor malware via tax-themed phishing in India and Russia

China-based cybercrime group Silver Fox linked (May 4, 2026) to a campaign targeting organizations in Russia and India with new malware 'ABCDoor,' distributed via phishing emails mimicking tax-related communications. Broader China-linked espionage campaign also targets government/defense in South, East, and Southeast Asia, a NATO state, journalists, and activists.
