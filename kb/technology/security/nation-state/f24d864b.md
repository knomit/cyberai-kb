# Chinese Silk Typhoon member extradited to US over COVID research cyberattacks

A Chinese national, Xu Zewei (age 34), accused of being a member of the Silk Typhoon hacking group, was extradited to the US from Italy. Arrested July 2025 by Italian authorities. Charged in connection with cyberattacks on COVID research institutions.
