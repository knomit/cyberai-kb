# 54% of F5 environments expose an actively exploited CVE

Joint SentinelOne and Tenable research found that 54% of environments running F5 products have at least one exposed, actively exploited CVE, versus 25% for Fortinet, and that Citrix customers show the slowest remediation with a 461-day median time to patch. Perimeter device exposure is driven more by organizational patch cadence than by vulnerability count.
