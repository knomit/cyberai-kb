# Mid-2026 threat landscape favors legacy/edge devices and credential abuse

Cybersecurity reporting from June 2026 shows a recurring pattern: attackers prioritize legacy and edge infrastructure plus credential/access abuse over novel exploits. FortiBleed compromised 80,000+ Fortinet FortiGate firewall/SSL-VPN devices via working credentials; AryStinger conscripted 4,300+ aging Realtek-based routers (exploiting decade-old CVEs like CVE-2013-3307) into a recon/proxy network; the Gravity SMTP WordPress flaw (CVE-2026-4020) exposed secrets through an unauthenticated endpoint; and INTERPOL flagged phishing as the most damaging Asia-Pacific cybercrime. Defensive responses are escalating too, exemplified by Canada's CSIS using a first-of-its-kind threat-reduction warrant to remotely clean botnet-infected devices.
