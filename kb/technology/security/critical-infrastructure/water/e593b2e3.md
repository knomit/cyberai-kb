# Coordinated cyberattack hit 30+ Minnesota water systems (July 2026)

A coordinated cyberattack campaign targeted more than 30 water systems in Minnesota on July 26-27, 2026, according to Minnesota IT Services (MNIT). Impact varied by system and the investigation continued to determine operational disruptions; the activity was not officially attributed, though Iranian actors have previously targeted U.S. water facilities. The incident prompted a U.S. government advisory urging critical-infrastructure operators to remove publicly exposed PLCs and other operational technology (OT) from the internet; attackers targeting exposed PLCs have changed passwords to lock out operators.
