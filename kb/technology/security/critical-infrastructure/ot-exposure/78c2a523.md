# Internet-exposed fuel tank gauges in the U.S. fell 56% from a March 2026 peak

BitSight observed a dramatic decline in internet-exposed Automatic Tank Gauge (ATG) systems. From March to June 2026 exposed IP addresses in the U.S. dropped more than 55%, and global exposure fell 49% from the same March peak, with the U.S. accounting for most of the decline. For ten months from June 2025 through March 2026 the U.S. held roughly 4,300–5,300 unique exposed IPs, averaging 4,815 across 2025; April fell 27.6% in a single month and May a further 31.8%.
