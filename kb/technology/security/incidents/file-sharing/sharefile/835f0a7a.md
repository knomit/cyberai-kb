# Progress tells ShareFile customers to shut down Storage Zone Controllers over security threat

Progress Software instructed ShareFile customers to shut down the Windows servers running their Storage Zone Controllers, confirming to The Hacker News that it was responding to a 'credible external security threat.' Progress temporarily disabled access to affected accounts out of caution, said it had no indication of unauthorized access, and did not disclose the nature of the threat or the actor. The order became public when a customer posted Progress's email to Reddit's r/sysadmin on July 10, 2026.
