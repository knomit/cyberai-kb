# GMO Internet Group offers up to $1M bounty after Onamae.com Desktop Cloud breach

Per The Hacker News (2026-10-28): GMO Internet Group offers up to $1 million total for tips leading to arrest or an international wanted notice for attackers who breached Onamae.com Desktop Cloud (Windows desktops for FX trading). GMO refused a ~$50 million (~¥8 billion) extortion demand received Oct 21; attackers posted data Oct 27 and the site was taken down Oct 28. A broker linked unauthorized trades to logins leaked from the service.
