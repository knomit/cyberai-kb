# France tax administration breach via stolen staff passwords undetected ~7 weeks

An attacker using infostealer-stolen passwords of French tax administration (DGFIP) staff took data in June-July 2026, announced the theft on Aug 12, and ANSSI published its report on Sep 23, 2026. Affected: 350,000+ individuals (tax ID, contact details, income reference, withholding rate, message metadata; message content possibly for <250), 250,000+ businesses, and land-registry data on ~435,000 households. ANSSI attributed it to weak login protection, poorly separated networks and monitoring gaps, not sophisticated techniques; one-time email codes were insufficient for the APEX portal. Lesson: MFA strength, network segmentation and monitoring of bulk data extraction.
