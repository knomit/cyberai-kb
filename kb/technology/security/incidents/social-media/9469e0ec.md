# Instagram support-chatbot abuse hijacked ~20,000 accounts

Meta revealed that 20,225 Instagram accounts may have been compromised by abusing its AI-powered High Touch Support (HTS) tool: attackers asked Meta's chatbot to link their own email address to a targeted account, enabling a password reset and takeover, with many high-profile accounts then sold on the dark web. The exploitation was discovered May 31, 2026, and use of the tool was disabled. Separately, a vulnerability in Instagram's web password-reset flow exposed unredacted emails and phone numbers when a username was supplied.
