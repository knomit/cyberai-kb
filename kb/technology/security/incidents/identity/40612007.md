# Azure CLI password-spray compromises 78 Microsoft accounts

Huntress reported a massive automated password-spray attack against Microsoft's Azure CLI: between June 12 and June 26, 2026 the actor made more than 81 million login attempts and compromised at least 78 Microsoft accounts across 64 organizations. Traffic came from an IPv6 range (2a0a:d683::/32) tied to LSHIY LLC (AS32167). Many compromised orgs had Conditional Access enabled; the campaign abused a deprecated OAuth flow (Resource Owner Password Credentials).
