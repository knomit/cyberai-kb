# Salesforce disabled Klue Battlecards integration after Icarus OAuth token abuse

On June 11, 2026, Salesforce disabled the Klue Battlecards app integration within its platform after detecting unusual activity that may have led to unauthorized access to a subset of customer data via the app's connection to Salesforce. Salesforce said the issue was limited to Klue's app connection and did not arise from a vulnerability in the Salesforce platform. An extortion group dubbed 'Icarus' compromised and exfiltrated data from Klue customers, including cybersecurity firm Huntress; copied data included business contacts and price quotes.
