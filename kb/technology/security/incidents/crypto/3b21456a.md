# Web3Auth flaw exploited to drain Keplr Google/Apple-login wallets

An attacker used a Web3Auth security flaw to steal funds from Keplr wallets created via Google or Apple sign-in. Web3Auth then used the attacker's route to move other at-risk wallets' funds into recovery wallets, so an empty balance may mean rescued, not stolen. Wallets made with a recovery phrase or hardware wallet are unaffected; Keplr paused Google/Apple sign-in. Guidance: don't send new funds or re-import the account.
