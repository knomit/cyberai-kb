# LNbits urges all operators to shut down servers amid active exploitation

The open-source Bitcoin Lightning wallet system LNbits told all operators to shut down servers after attacks; one operator lost 2,285,545 satoshis (~0.023 BTC) to new accounts exploiting a bug. The notice followed a narrower warning to disable the Paywall add-on. As of 09:00 UTC Nov 9, 2026 no fixed version was released; developer Ben Arc estimated patches within 24-48 hours.
