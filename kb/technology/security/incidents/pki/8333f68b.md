# DigiCert breach: business system exposed to the internet for 16+ months after a Feb 2025 network change

DigiCert disclosed unauthorized access to a business system exposed to the public internet by a Feb 2025 network-segment move. Between June 27 and July 2, 2026 attackers used a self-registration feature to create a read-only account with access to archived data; names, emails, phones and business addresses were accessed, and in some cases ID documents provided as proof. Account blocked July 2; scope determined in October.
