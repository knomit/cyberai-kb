# Unknown attackers accessed stock exchange senior exec Outlook for 5 months, exfiltrated via Dropbox and OneDrive

Unknown attackers spent at least five months inside the Outlook mailbox of a senior executive at a major global stock exchange, copying the inbox out in small, repeated batches and routing exfiltrated data through Dropbox and OneDrive. The use of legitimate cloud services (Dropbox, OneDrive) for exfiltration made the traffic blend with normal business activity, facilitating long dwell time.
