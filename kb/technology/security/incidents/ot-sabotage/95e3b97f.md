# France attributes October 2026 refrigeration-controller sabotage on ~700 retail sites to Iran's IRGC unit Shahid Kaveh

France said on 2026-11-09 that the IRGC cyber unit Shahid Kaveh attacked Danfoss and Dixell refrigeration controllers at about 700 retail sites on 2026-10-11 via exposed web interfaces, setting unlimited defrost cycles and disabling alarms, causing food losses of tens of millions of euros and ~15-20 fires; Schneider Electric hydro equipment was also targeted. Paris prosecutors seek arrest warrants. CERT-FR advises removing internet exposure, using VPN, and reviewing defrost configuration; EOL devices need replacement, not just factory reset.
