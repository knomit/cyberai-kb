# Berlin state administrative network breach: Rhysida extortion refused

Berlin's state government confirmed in late August 2026 that it is the target of an extortion attempt following the August 2026 compromise of the city's state administrative network, and said it will not meet the extortionists' demands. Forensic work found further data exfiltration from the Senate Department for Mobility, Transport, Climate Protection and Environment, dated between August 7 and August 12, 2026; the department first reported an outflow on August 7 and was cut off from the network on August 14. Berlin published no figure for the volume taken; the only itemized account is the attackers' own leak-site post, indexed August 28, 2026, claiming 5.79 TB of data and personal information on 12,076 individuals. Governing Mayor Kai Wegner stated "The state of Berlin is being blackmailed" after a special Senate session. Der Spiegel named Rhysida as the group, citing the group's darknet leak site and security sources. State criminal police, the public prosecutor and federal security authorities are investigating.
