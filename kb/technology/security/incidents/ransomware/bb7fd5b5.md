# Synology NAS targeted by C3RB3R ransomware exploiting patched DSM flaw (Synology_SA_24_20)

Synology urged DSM updates after ransomware attacks on NAS devices. Exploited flaw not named; Synology points to advisory Synology_SA_24_20 (CVE-2024-10441 RCE CVSS 9.8, CVE-2024-50629, CVE-2024-10445). Ransomware family C3RB3R appends .L0CK3D. Fixed DSM: 7.2.2-72806-1, 7.2.1-69057-6, 7.2-64570-4, 7.1.1-42962-7, 6.2.4-25556-8 or later. Enable automatic updates.
