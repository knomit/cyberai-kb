# Surfshark exposed an internal test server to the internet through a configuration error

Surfshark disclosed that unknown threat actors accessed one of its internal test servers after a configuration error exposed it to the internet. Per Surfshark: 'Due to a human error, an internal test server used by our engineering teams was misconfigured in a way that made it reachable from the internet. It contained parts of the system binaries and internal configurations for certain services. Personal information was never held and accessible from here, VPN traffic and browsing activity are not logged or retained in the first place, and the apps and browser extensions on your devices were not altered in any way.' Discovered 2026-08-31. What was exposed was system binaries and internal service configurations — valuable for attacker reconnaissance even though no user data was present, so 'no personal data' does not mean 'no consequence'.
