# Keystone destroying 18,835 hardware wallets after device verification key stolen

Keystone said on 2026-10-26 that an attacker who accessed its website (discovered Oct 20) obtained the key its hardware wallets use to prove they are genuine. A holder could build counterfeit devices that pass the online authenticity check. Recovery phrases and wallet private keys were not involved. Keystone will destroy 18,835 unsold devices carrying the key and give new devices a new key. Devices received after Oct 20 with the old key should be used only after Keystone support confirms they are genuine; passing authentication alone is not proof.
