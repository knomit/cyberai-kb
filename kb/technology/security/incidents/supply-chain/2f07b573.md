# Polymarket hit by ~$2.7M theft via compromised DebugBear script

Hackers compromised DebugBear (DNS records via its domain registrar), distributing crypto-draining JavaScript that took about $2.7M from 33 deposit wallets in under an hour on Oct 29, 2026; Polymarket briefly suspended trading. In June 2026 Polymarket disclosed another third-party script compromise (~$3M losses). DebugBear says the attack targeted a specific customer and no internal systems compromise was seen.
