# cPGuard update channel compromised on 2 Nov 2026 via billing-app customer account

OPSSHIELD said an attacker used compromised credentials on 2 Nov 2026 02:06 UTC to access its update distribution CDN and push a malicious script through the cPGuard update channel, delivering a backdoor and data-stealing script. Entry was via its billing application (manage.opsshield[.]com) using a customer account the attacker registered.
