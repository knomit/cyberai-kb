# ShinyHunters claimed an FBI breach via an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day (Sept 22, 2026)

On Sept 22, 2026 the extortion group ShinyHunters claimed it breached the FBI and stole data on 'almost ALL FBI Agents' and FBI job applicants, naming Criminal Justice, HR and Medlink services. A spokesperson told The Register the group used a new Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day for RCE and defaced FBIjobs.gov. The FBI said it was aware of claims about unauthorized activity affecting FBIjobs.gov and was investigating. The data-theft scope is the group's claim and was unverified at the time. ShinyHunters cited retaliation for a May 2026 FBI PSA about its Canvas LMS targeting.
