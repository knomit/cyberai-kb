# DoJ corrected QTFY press release: U.S. agencies were targets, not confirmed victims

The U.S. Department of Justice corrected an earlier press statement that had listed NASA, the Federal Reserve, the Department of Energy, the DoJ itself, HHS, NIH, and the U.S. Senate as victims of QTFY computer-intrusion activity. The updated statement lists them instead as 'among the targets of QTFY', with DoJ noting the edits ensure the release accurately reflects the allegations in the affidavit supporting the domain seizures. Reported by Reuters.
