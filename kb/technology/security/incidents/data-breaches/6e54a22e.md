# Denmark CPR register: 8.8M people's data accessed via company's lawful lookup right

Denmark's digitalization ministry said on 2026-10-05 that unauthorized parties accessed names, addresses and CPR numbers of about 8.8M people (living and dead) by abusing a private company's lawful lookup access; large volumes of automated lookups validated CPR numbers. Access was stopped, Datatilsynet notified, police investigating.
