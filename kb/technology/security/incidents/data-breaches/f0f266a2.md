# Thomson Reuters C-Track court software breach exposed court data across 11 US states, the US Virgin Islands and Ontario

Thomson Reuters disclosed on September 2, 2026 that an unauthorized party obtained files from C-Track, the court case management platform sold by its West Publishing Corporation unit. Unauthorized access to the storage location ran from March 1 to June 29, 2026 and was discovered on June 30, 2026 — roughly four months undetected.

Affected jurisdictions are courts in Alabama, Pennsylvania, Kentucky, Montana, Nevada, North Dakota, South Carolina, Tennessee, Ohio, New Hampshire and Wyoming (11 US states), plus the U.S. Virgin Islands and Ontario, Canada.

A subset of records could contain names, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, dates of birth, medical information and health insurance information. Confidential, redacted or sealed court information may also have been affected at some courts.

Thomson Reuters is offering 12 months of Experian IdentityWorks credit monitoring, with enrollment open until December 31, 2026.
