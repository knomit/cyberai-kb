# Binance breach: ~$6.3B affected, attributed to North Korea's TraderTraitor

Per The Hacker News (Oct 30, 2026): Binance disclosed a security incident affecting ~$6.3B of assets, ~$3B frozen (Tether 1.567B USDT, Circle 800M USDC). Initial finding: a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop gave access to a service container used to submit unauthorized withdrawals; no private key leak. Chainalysis estimates ~$5.912B stolen, nearly 4x the next largest heist (Bybit, ~$1.5B, Feb 2025); TRM Labs attributes it to North Korea's TraderTraitor with high confidence. Binance will cover losses from SAFU.
