# Binance disclosure: $6.43B theft chain began with trojanized Homebrew installer and fake recruitment interview

Binance said on 2026-11-03 that the $6.43 billion theft was a multi-stage campaign combining fake recruitment interviews, malicious software downloads, compromised internal AI tooling, endpoint compromise and an advanced transaction-authorisation method. It started in June 2026 when a Wallet DevOps engineer searched for an AI tool and downloaded a trojanized Homebrew installer; two engineers were targeted. Not a single phishing incident. Figure differs from the earlier ~$6.3B entry.
