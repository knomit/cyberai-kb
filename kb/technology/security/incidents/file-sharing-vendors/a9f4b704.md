# Kiteworks urged 9-hour precautionary shutdown after federal threat intel warning

Kiteworks (formerly Accellion), a secure file-sharing vendor whose subsidiaries include Zivver, DRACOON, totemo, ownCloud, WAMNET, Maytech and Bonfy, urged customers to shut down their systems for nine hours over a weekend around Sept 26, 2026 as a precaution, after receiving credible threat intelligence from federal/law-enforcement authorities indicating a possible imminent cyberattack. The company said it had found no evidence that customer systems had actually been compromised, and did not disclose which agency provided the tip or who might be behind the threat.
