# MonsterCloud owner charged with defrauding ransomware victims (~$19M billed)

On 2026-10-07 the US DoJ announced charges against Zohar Pinhasi, owner of Florida firm MonsterCloud, for allegedly claiming proprietary decryption tools while secretly paying ransoms to attackers to obtain decryptors; two counts of wire fraud and one of wire fraud conspiracy, up to 20 years each; headline figure over $19M billed. Allegations, not convictions.
