# Second wave of refrigeration-controller attacks hits European supermarkets (2 Nov 2026), mainly Danfoss devices

Overnight into Mon 2 Nov 2026, attackers remotely altered refrigeration controller settings at supermarkets in Belgium, Sweden, Estonia, Luxembourg, Ireland, Netherlands, Finland, Portugal and Italy (Sicily), following the earlier French wave ~3 weeks before. Danfoss controllers were involved in most cases (CERT-FR names AK-SM and AK-SC; also Copeland xweb and Dixell xweb). Danfoss blames outdated software; in the Netherlands systems were exposed with default passwords. No chain reported more than several dozen affected stores (e.g. Carrefour 15 of 700+); some food was pulled. No group claimed responsibility; Belgium opened a criminal investigation. Similar method does not establish same actors.
