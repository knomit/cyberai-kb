# ccTLD registries .gh, .sl, .as hijacked to obtain unauthorized Google certificates

Google said on 2026-10-06 that attackers compromised three country-code TLD registries (.gh Ghana, .sl Sierra Leone, .as American Samoa) and obtained unauthorized HTTPS certificates for several Google/YouTube domains; Certificate Transparency logs show at least 12 certificates issued 2026-09-22 to 09-27. Google's own systems were not breached. Chrome blocked the certificates via CRLSets and Google worked with the issuing CAs to revoke them. Consequence: any domain under those ccTLDs is exposed to certificate misissuance risk, not only Google's. This does not mean Google was compromised.
