# Progress tells ShareFile customers to shut down Storage Zone Controllers over a credible external security threat

Progress Software instructed ShareFile customers to shut down the Windows servers running their Storage Zone Controllers, confirming to The Hacker News that it was responding to a 'credible external security threat.' Progress temporarily disabled access to affected accounts out of caution and said it had no indication of unauthorized access.

Progress did not disclose the nature of the threat or the actor. The order became public when a customer posted Progress's email to Reddit's r/sysadmin on July 10, 2026. The Hacker News made it their Threat of the Week.

Note the shape: a vendor ordering customers to take production infrastructure offline while declining to say why is a stronger signal than the absence of a CVE would suggest.
