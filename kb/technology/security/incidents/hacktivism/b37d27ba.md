# Syrian Electronic Army hacker Ahmad Umar Agha gets 42 months for AP Twitter hack

Per The Hacker News (Oct 30, 2026): Ahmad Umar Agha ('Th3Pr0') was sentenced to 42 months for a Syrian Electronic Army conspiracy; DOJ (Oct 29) says it was led by Assad's inner circle. His April 23, 2013 hijack of the AP Twitter account posted a false White House attack report, causing a temporary $136B Dow drop.
