# HalluSquatting attack weaponizes AI coding-assistant package hallucinations

As reported by The Hacker News (Jul 9, 2026), researchers disclosed a 'HalluSquatting' attack: because AI coding assistants frequently hallucinate plausible-sounding but non-existent package/tool names, attackers can pre-register those names to trick the assistants into installing attacker-controlled botnet malware.
