# Sophos: AI coding agents trip endpoint detection rules meant for intruders

As reported by The Hacker News (Jul 9, 2026), Sophos analyzed a week of its own endpoint telemetry and found AI coding agents such as Claude Code, Cursor, and OpenAI Codex trigger endpoint detection rules written to catch human attackers, even though the agents are not malicious.
