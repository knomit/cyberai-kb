# Texas AG sued Meta over WhatsApp end-to-end encryption claims

The Texas Attorney General sued Meta alleging WhatsApp does not provide the end-to-end encryption it claims. The suit references a Bloomberg report about the US Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security closing an investigation into allegations that Meta can access encrypted WhatsApp messages. The preliminary BIS finding stated 'there is no limit to the type of WhatsApp message that can be viewed by Meta.' Meta called the allegations 'baseless.'
