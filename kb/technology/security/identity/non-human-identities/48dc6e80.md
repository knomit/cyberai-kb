# Only 21% of organizations have governance controls for non-human identities

JumpCloud's Q3 2026 IT Trends Report found that just 21% of organizations have governance controls in place for non-human identities, leaving deployed-and-abandoned agents ('zombie agents') running with accumulating permissions and no owner or offboarding process.
