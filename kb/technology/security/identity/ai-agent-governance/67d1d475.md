# JumpCloud Q3 2026 IT Trends Report: unreviewed high-risk agent actions doubled to 26% in six months while only 21% of organizations have non-human-identity governance controls

MERGED from two records reporting separate findings of the SAME source document — JumpCloud's Q3 2026 IT Trends Report. They were carried as independent facts, which risked reading two numbers from one survey as two independent corroborations. Both findings and both framings are preserved.

FINDING 1 — UNREVIEWED HIGH-RISK ACTIONS DOUBLED. The proportion of organizations allowing AI agents to take HIGH-RISK ACTIONS WITHOUT HUMAN REVIEW MORE THAN DOUBLED IN SIX MONTHS, rising from 11% to 26%.

FINDING 2 — NON-HUMAN-IDENTITY GOVERNANCE IS RARE. Just 21% OF ORGANIZATIONS have governance controls in place for non-human identities, leaving deployed-and-abandoned agents ('ZOMBIE AGENTS') running with accumulating permissions and no owner and no offboarding process.

THE REPORT'S OWN ARGUMENT. The findings are used to argue that giving an agent an identity is only the START — IT must also control what it can access, what actions it can take, and when a human must intervene.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: both figures come from ONE vendor survey and are a single source, not two. JumpCloud sells identity management, so it has a commercial interest in the governance gap it measures. Neither figure is an independently audited measurement, and the survey's population and sampling frame are not recorded here.
