# Share of AI agents allowed high-risk actions without human review doubled to 26% in six months

JumpCloud's Q3 2026 IT Trends Report found that the proportion of organizations allowing AI agents to take high-risk actions without human review more than doubled in six months, rising from 11% to 26%. The finding is used to argue that giving an agent an identity is only the start — IT must also control what it can access, what actions it can take, and when a human must intervene.
