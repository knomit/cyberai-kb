# A Brevo SAML SSO flaw let attackers take over 138 accounts, exporting contacts from 43 and phishing from 6

In a separate incident from the September 2026 Cloudflare Worker supply chain attack, attackers 'exploited a flaw in the way Brevo handles SAML SSO to gain access to 138 Brevo accounts', per Brevo. Of those 138 accounts, 6 were used to send phishing emails to stored contacts and 43 had their contacts exported. Downstream victims of the resulting phishing included Trezor, CoinTracking, and BitBox.

The three numbers are distinct and must travel together — 138 accessed, 43 exfiltrated, 6 weaponized. Summarizing this as '138 accounts breached and used for phishing' overstates it; summarizing as '6 accounts used for phishing' understates the data loss. Note also that account takeover via the IDENTITY layer (SAML handling) hit a different set of victims than the CDN-layer attack, so remediating one says nothing about the other.
