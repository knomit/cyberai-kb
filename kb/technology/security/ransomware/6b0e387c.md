# 8,800+ ransomware attacks in 2025: 389% YoY increase, WormGPT/FraudGPT cited as enablers

Fortinet recorded 7,831 confirmed ransomware victims globally in 2025 (some reports cite 8,800+), up 389% YoY from ~1,600 in 2024. Crime service kits including WormGPT, FraudGPT, and BruteForceAI cited as key contributors to the increase. Top targeted sectors: manufacturing (1,284), business services (824), retail (682). Geographic concentration: US (3,381), Canada (374), Germany (291). VECT 2.0 RaaS program, which first appeared December 2025, announced every BreachForums registered user becomes an affiliate with access to ransomware, negotiation platform, and leak site.
