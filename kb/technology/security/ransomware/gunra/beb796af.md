# Gunra ransomware exploits Fortinet and Schneider Electric flaws

Cybersecurity and intelligence agencies from South Korea and the U.S. (including CISA) warned of Gunra ransomware attacks against critical-infrastructure sectors worldwide (healthcare, financial services, government, professional/nonprofit services). Attackers gained initial access via internet-facing Schneider Electric PowerLogic P5 (CVE-2024-5559) and Fortinet FortiOS/FortiProxy (CVE-2025-24472), then deployed Gunra in a double-extortion model (data exfiltration plus encryption); victims who do not pay within five to seven days have data leaked. Quote from CISA Acting EAD for Cybersecurity Chris Butera.
