# Cl0p exploits internet-exposed PTC Windchill/FlexPLM for unauthenticated RCE (CVE-2026-12569)

Threat actors linked to the Cl0p ransomware group (aka Chubby Scorpius, FIN11, Graceful Spider, Lace Tempest) are exploiting internet-exposed PTC Windchill and FlexPLM deployments in a new data-extortion campaign, per a coordinated advisory from Ransom-ISAC, eCrime.ch and DEFUSED reported by The Hacker News on July 25, 2026. Attackers chain a pre-authentication information disclosure in the FlexPLM WSDL endpoint with a server-side flaw in the Windchill login servlet to achieve unauthenticated remote code execution and deploy hex-named JSP web shells under /Windchill/login/. The suspected flaw is CVE-2026-12569 (CVSS 9.3). Post-exploitation activity includes filesystem enumeration, staging of engineering/design data, and double-extortion data theft. Targeted sectors include manufacturing, automotive, aerospace, and retail.
