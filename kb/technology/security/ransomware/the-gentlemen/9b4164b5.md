# The Gentlemen ransomware (Storm-2697) claims 478 victims

The Gentlemen ransomware operation, tracked by Microsoft as Storm-2697, has claimed 478 victims to date. Active since March 2025, it began as a closed group and started offering ransomware-as-a-service to affiliates in September 2025. Reported in The Hacker News June 2026 Weekly Recap.
