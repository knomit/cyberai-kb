# Comparitech: Q3 2026 record ransomware quarter, 2,627 incidents

Comparitech: Q3 2026 had 2,627 reported ransomware attacks (+29% vs Q2's 2,030; +61% vs Q3 2025's 1,636), the highest quarterly total to date. Median demand $150,000, average $602,400; most prolific gangs Qilin and The Gentlemen.
