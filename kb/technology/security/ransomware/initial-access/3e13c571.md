# Anubis ransomware affiliates exploit Citrix Bleed 2 (CVE-2025-5777) for initial access

Threat actors associated with the Anubis ransomware operation have been observed exploiting the Citrix Bleed 2 vulnerability (CVE-2025-5777) to obtain initial access, part of a broader trend of ransomware groups turning to Citrix Bleed 2, bring-your-own-vulnerable-driver (BYOVD) techniques, and supply-chain credentials. Reported by The Hacker News, July 3, 2026.
