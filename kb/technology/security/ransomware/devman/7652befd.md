# DevMan RaaS ('Funky Mantis') runs a full-featured affiliate portal

Swiss cybersecurity firm PRODAFT documented the DevMan ransomware-as-a-service operation, which it tracks as Funky Mantis, in a report shared with The Hacker News (July 2026). The centrally administered web portal combines payload/build generation, finance, victim chat, support, victim records, teams, and payout functions, integrating access brokerage with ransomware deployment. Administrators offer country-specific 'networks,' ask whether an affiliate will use personal or program-supplied access, and impose two-to-three-day completion windows. DevMan first emerged in April 2025 as an affiliate for Qilin, DragonForce, Apos, and RansomHub before shifting to its own RaaS operation.
