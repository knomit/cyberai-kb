# Microsoft maps three Salesforce attack paths tied to a year of ShinyHunters activity

Microsoft mapped three Salesforce attack paths linked to a year of activity by the data-extortion group ShinyHunters (and lookalikes). The attackers walked into corporate Salesforce environments without exploiting any flaw in the platform itself, relying instead on social engineering and abuse of legitimate access. Reported by The Hacker News.
