# Proofpoint: GenAI erases behavioral tells in malicious email

Proofpoint (Protect 2026) says generative AI makes malicious emails fluent, personalized and unique, weakening behavior-only detection. FBI IC3 counted 22,000+ AI-related complaints in 2025 with nearly $900M losses, and 24,768 BEC complaints with about $3.05B losses.
