# CrowdStrike: AI harness matters as much as the model in security

On The Deep View Conversations (Fal.Con 2026), CrowdStrike's Adam Meyers said changing the harness around an AI model reduced false positives in vulnerability research without changing the underlying model; CrowdStrike trains red- and blue-team models against each other based on Nvidia Nemotron models and its security data. Discussion also covered the live disruption of the Sality botnet, which operated more than two decades.
