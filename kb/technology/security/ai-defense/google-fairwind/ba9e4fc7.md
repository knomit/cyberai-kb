# Gemini 4 Argon rollout to trusted cyber defenders via Fairwind; guardrail-free version planned

Google is rolling Gemini 4 Argon to trusted cyber defenders through its Fairwind Program and plans a guardrail-free version for them. Argon is described as autonomously discovering, validating and patching critical vulnerabilities, including previously unknown critical flaws in healthcare software used by hospitals.
