# Semperis 2026: 40% of ransomware attacks in past 12 months included threats against staff

Semperis's 2026 ransomware report: 40% of attacks involved psychological or physical threats against staff; in 53% of cases victims said threats were followed through; 24% were swatted; 25% sought psychological/medical treatment; 24% hired personal security.
