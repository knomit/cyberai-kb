# Attacker economics favor repeatable playbooks over novel exploits

THN's analysis argues that threat actors optimize for repeatability and per-attempt cost rather than sophistication: the most common initial access last year was simply to ask the victim. A representative pattern is the fake 'prove you are not a robot' page that silently places a command on the visitor's clipboard and then talks them into running it — a playbook that costs nearly nothing per attempt and can therefore be run at enormous volume.
