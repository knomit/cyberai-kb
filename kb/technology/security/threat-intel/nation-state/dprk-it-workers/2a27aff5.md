# DPRK remote-worker fraud has expanded from IT into healthcare and sales roles

DPRK-linked threat actors have been observed seeking jobs beyond IT, with investigations identifying suspected North Korean workers employed in sales and marketing and in the medical profession. In one February 2026 case, three employees of an Australian healthcare company were flagged after repeatedly connecting via Astrill VPN and IPRoyal Proxy, presenting fraudulently created identity documents, showing similarities between two employees' passports, and submitting electronic bills with word anomalies as proof of residence during onboarding. Recorded Future's Insikt Group observed one cluster linked to PurpleDelta applying to jobs at over 1,100 companies between late 2024 and early 2025, mostly in software/technology, staffing/consulting, and healthcare/biotechnology. The scheme funnels income to Pyongyang's nuclear weapons and ballistic missile programs.
