# Volt Typhoon 'Silent Train' rotating relay network used in zero-day attacks

Chinese state-sponsored Volt Typhoon uses a framework codenamed Silent Train: rapidly rotating chains of compromised SOHO, IoT and enterprise devices serving as relay infrastructure, cleaned up after each operation, used including for zero-day attacks. Findings point to pre-positioning in communications and critical infrastructure relevant to Indo-Pacific crises.
