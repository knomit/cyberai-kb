# Sophos: Aur0ra ransomware actors used Cursor and LLMs, bypassing guardrails with 'red team' framing

Sophos reports Aur0ra ransomware (Gold Ogre) uses AI heavily: an AI-assisted post-exploitation framework built with Cursor, and Cursor's Composer plus models from Anthropic, xAI and Moonshot orchestrated intrusions; guardrails were bypassed by framing activity as authorized red teaming or 'diagnostics testing'. The actors initially used Anthropic almost exclusively.
