# Picus Blue Report 2026: perimeter defenses recover but interior stays weak

Picus Labs' Blue Report 2026, based on more than 338 million real attack simulations across client production environments in H1 2026, found average prevention effectiveness climbed from 62% to 69% (matching its 2024 peak) and logging reached a four-year high of 58%. However the improvement is concentrated at the perimeter: once attackers are inside, defenses invert and are weakest against quiet reconnaissance and credential-theft moves that precede major breaches.
