# Antino backdoor in China-nexus campaign UAT-11587 (Cisco Talos)

Cisco Talos tracks a China-nexus cluster, UAT-11587, deploying a previously undocumented Rust-compiled Windows backdoor codenamed Antino (per researcher Ashley Shen) with host recon, shell/PowerShell execution, file transfer, in-memory shellcode loading and persistence. Targets government and policy organizations in Taiwan, India, the Philippines, Cambodia, Pakistan, Thailand and Myanmar; first detected September 2025 via spear-phishing of Taiwan's academic/think-tank policy community; now 16 entities across eight Asian countries. Headline says C2 abuses Outlook and OneDrive.
