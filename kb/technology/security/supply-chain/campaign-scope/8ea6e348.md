# TeamPCP expanded beyond npm/PyPI to RubyGems and PHP, with named enterprise victims including SAP, Intercom and the European Commission

The Mini Shai-Hulud campaign expanded its registry footprint beyond npm and PyPI to RubyGems and the PHP/Packagist ecosystem. Named organization-level victims reported across the campaign include SAP, Lightning, Intercom, Mistral AI, UiPath, Guardrails AI, Grafana, TanStack, Trivy, LiteLLM, GitHub itself, and the European Commission as a downstream victim.

The campaign has been active since roughly March 2026 (per The Record) or late April 2026 for the 'Mini' fourth-generation variant, and had NOT ceased as of mid-July 2026. It carries a consistent Dune-universe theme, with dead-drop commit branch names drawn from Frank Herbert's Dune.

Note on counting: the named-victim list above is on the order of a dozen organizations. This is the best organization-level enumeration available and is far below the 'thousands of organizations' threshold used in some SolarWinds-scale comparisons.
