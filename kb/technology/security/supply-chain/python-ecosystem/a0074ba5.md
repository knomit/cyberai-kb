# LiteLLM supply chain compromise overlapped almost entirely with earlier Trivy scanner exposure

SOCRadar analysis found 95% of organizations impacted by the 2026 LiteLLM supply chain attack were already exposed via the earlier compromise of the Trivy scanner. Organizations did not need to install LiteLLM directly: the package could arrive transitively through frameworks such as DSPy, MLflow, CrewAI, OpenHands and Arize Phoenix, and its payload executed at Python startup without requiring a LiteLLM import. The incident was attributed to the threat actor TeamPCP.
