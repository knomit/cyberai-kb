# Malicious LiteLLM PyPI releases may have exposed 2,100+ organizations

Two malicious LiteLLM releases sat on PyPI for about 40 minutes in March 2026 carrying credential-stealing code able to harvest cloud keys, SSH keys, Kubernetes tokens, database passwords and other secrets from systems that installed them. Threat-intel firm CloudSEK, from a dataset of roughly 434,000 captured files (tied to the Trivy hack campaign), maps potential exposure to more than 2,500 organizations and published a searchable public lookup. CloudSEK stresses these totals are potential-exposure mappings from captured loot/log files, not a confirmed victim count.
