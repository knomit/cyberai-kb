# Copycat actors began deploying Shai-Hulud clones, complicating attribution to TeamPCP

OX Security reported that new, distinct threat actors began deploying Shai-Hulud clones — derivative self-spreading supply-chain worms modeled on TeamPCP's tooling.

Significance for attribution: once a technique is cloned by unrelated actors, observed activity can no longer be attributed to the original actor on the basis of tradecraft or worm behavior alone. Claims that a 'persistent, sophisticated threat actor with ongoing campaign infrastructure' is behind any given wave require evidence tying that wave specifically to TeamPCP, rather than to the technique's now-public template. This cuts against attribution-based criteria in analyses of the campaign.
