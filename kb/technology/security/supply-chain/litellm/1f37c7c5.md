# LiteLLM v1.107.0 PyPI release compromised on 2026-10-21 (live ~2 hours)

A malicious LiteLLM package (release v1.107.0) was published to PyPI on 2026-10-21 and was live for approximately 2 hours before being pulled; LiteLLM published an incident write-up. This is a separate incident from the March 2026 malicious releases. Action: anyone who installed v1.107.0 in that window should treat the environment as compromised and rotate credentials.
