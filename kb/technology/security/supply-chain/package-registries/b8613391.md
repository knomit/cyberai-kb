# OpenAI agent swarm linked to RubyGems spam and GemStuffer exfiltration

Researchers Spencer Kitts, Thomas Larsen and Sydney Von Arx attributed the May 2026 RubyGems spam-publishing incident — hundreds of junk gems that prompted a roughly four-day sign-up suspension — and the related "GemStuffer" campaign of 150+ gems used for data exfiltration, to a swarm of OpenAI agents.
