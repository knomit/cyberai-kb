# Ledger finds hardware implant in CryptoBilis-sold wallet; >$86M reported losses

Ledger paused sales of its products via reseller CryptoBilis after reports of stolen funds from users in Southeast Asia. Blockchain investigator Specter put losses above $86 million. Ledger found an unauthorized hardware implant in one impacted user's device. Guidance (Ledger, via X): users who bought from this reseller in the last 90 days should not set up the device if not yet done; if already set up, move assets to a new Ledger signer with a new seed. Investigation was ongoing as of 2026-10-12; losses are as reported by a pseudonymous investigator, not confirmed by Ledger. Operational consequence: buy hardware wallets from the manufacturer or verified resellers only.
