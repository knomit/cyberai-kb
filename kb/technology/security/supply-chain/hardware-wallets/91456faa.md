# Trezor warns of suspected hardware implant in Safe 5 wallets sold by CryptoBilis

Trezor (blog post Oct 30, 2026) warned that a Trezor Safe 5 bought from reseller CryptoBilis (via Tokopedia, Indonesia, Aug 2026) allegedly contains an extra board with a cellular module and antenna between main board and screen that reads the screen, including the recovery phrase, and transmits it when plugged in. Trezor had not yet examined the device (unverified customer report) and advises anyone who bought a Trezor from CryptoBilis since March 2026 to move funds to a new wallet with a new recovery phrase.
