# AOMEI Backupper update servers tampered in 5-10 minute windows May-July 2026 to deliver backdoor

Per The Hacker News (Oct 2026), citing Google Threat Intelligence Group, Mandiant and ManageEngine's Threat Analysis Lab: attackers repeatedly altered files AOMEI Backupper fetches from the vendor's own update servers (e.g. down_html.ini), in windows of five to ten minutes between May and July 2026, so any copy checking for updates in a window downloaded and ran a backdoor. AOMEI reports its servers are fixed, but that does not remove the backdoor from machines already hit. AnyViewer runs similar background update checks.
