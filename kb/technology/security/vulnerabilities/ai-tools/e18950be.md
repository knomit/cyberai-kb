# Google Gemini Android: poisoned WhatsApp/Slack/SMS notification hijacks voice assistant via prompt injection

SafeBreach researcher Or Yair disclosed that a single poisoned notification from WhatsApp, Slack, SMS, Signal, Instagram, or Messenger could have hijacked Google Gemini's voice assistant on Android — requiring no malicious app installed, just a hostile notification treated as useful context. Possible outcomes: open connected windows, send a fake message appearing to come from the victim's boss, push the phone into a Zoom call, or quietly poison Gemini's long-term memory. This research follows and extends SafeBreach's earlier 'Invitation Is All You Need' work (malicious Google Calendar invites → Gemini hijack), after which Google hardened Gemini against indirect prompt injection. This new research demonstrates the hardening was incomplete — notification context represents a new unguarded input channel.
