# CISA adds actively exploited N-able N-central flaw CVE-2026-18577 to KEV

On Aug 3, 2026, CISA added CVE-2026-18577 (CVSS 8.2), an authentication-bypass/account-takeover flaw in N-able N-central, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog following active exploitation in the wild. The bug is an incomplete patch of the earlier CVE-2026-18556 (also CVSS 8.2). Successful exploitation grants remote administrative access to N-central servers, letting attackers abuse the built-in Take Control feature to pivot into managed endpoints and deploy persistence. Fixed in version 2026.3 HF1. Reported by The Hacker News.
