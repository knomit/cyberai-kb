# CISA adds VMware ESX CVE-2026-41709 to KEV after Warp Panda exploitation

CISA added Broadcom VMware ESX flaw CVE-2026-41709 (CVSS 2.7, insufficient logging letting an admin-level actor perform operations without being logged) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 2026-10-28 after CrowdStrike reported China-nexus actor Warp Panda used it against the U.S. legal sector. Broadcom fixed it in July 2026 (e.g. ESXi80U3j-25429389, VCF 5.2.4). A low CVSS score does not mean low risk: it reduces audit-trail visibility.
