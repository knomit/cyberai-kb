# Metabase zero-day (CVSS 10.0) exploited in the wild for unauthenticated admin access

Metabase warned that a maximum-severity flaw (CVSS score 10.0) in its business-intelligence and data-visualization software was exploited in the wild as a zero-day, allowing administrative access without authentication. Reported August 2026.
