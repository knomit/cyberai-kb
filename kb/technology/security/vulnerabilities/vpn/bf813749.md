# Palo Alto PAN-OS GlobalProtect flaw CVE-2026-0257 actively exploited

Palo Alto Networks reported active exploitation of CVE-2026-0257 (CVSS 7.8), an authentication bypass flaw in the portal and gateway components of PAN-OS that lets attackers bypass security controls and initiate VPN connections to GlobalProtect portals. Limited in-the-wild exploitation by an unknown actor was first observed on May 17, 2026; no post-access lateral movement had been identified at disclosure. Reported by The Hacker News, June 2026.
