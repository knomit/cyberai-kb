# Unisoc VoLTE video call exploit chain gives full Android kernel access with no vendor fix

SSD Secure Disclosure published on August 17, 2026 the second stage of an exploit chain achieving full Android kernel access on devices running Unisoc modem firmware via a VoLTE video call. Stage one, disclosed March 2026, was remote code execution in the same firmware through a malformed SIP video call. The privilege-escalation flaw is classified CWE-1189 (Improper Isolation of Shared Resources on System-on-a-Chip) with no CVE assigned at publication. It resides in modem firmware shared by at least three Unisoc chipsets — T606 (Motorola E13), T612 (Realme C33) and T7250 (Xiaomi Redmi A5). Researchers confirmed it on a Motorola E13 with a February 2025 patch and a Xiaomi Redmi A5 with a January 2026 patch. Exploitation requires an attacker-controlled private 4G network and a victim who answers the call. SSD says Unisoc did not respond through email or LinkedIn.
