# Alby Hub critical flaw allowed takeover of internet-exposed self-hosted Lightning wallets

Alby warned of a critical flaw in Alby Hub, its self-hosted Bitcoin Lightning wallet that the owner runs on their own computer or server, which could let an attacker take over the wallet and send its funds. Exploitation required that the owner had made the Hub's management interface reachable from the internet; Hubs not exposed externally were not at risk. Versions v1.7.0 through v1.18.5 are affected; v1.19.0 and later are not. Alby said one user had been affected. It withheld technical details of the flaw at disclosure, citing responsible disclosure, and said it would publish them later. Its guidance for anyone still on an older build is two steps in order: first cut off outside access to the Hub's management web interface, then update to the current release, v1.24.0.
