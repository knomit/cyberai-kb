# ShieldCrash is a third-generation Microsoft Defender zero-day, each bypassing the prior fix

A security researcher known as Chaotic Eclipse — since self-identified as Abdelhamid Naceri, a former Microsoft employee who says he was fired in September 2024 for sharing vulnerability information with external parties — released a proof-of-concept for a Microsoft Defender zero-day codenamed ShieldCrash. ShieldCrash is assessed to be a patch bypass for CVE-2026-69414 (CVSS 7.8, 'ShieldBreak'), which was itself a bypass for an earlier Defender flaw called RoguePlanet (CVE-2026-50656). The chain of three successive bypasses is part of an ongoing dispute between the researcher and Microsoft over the company's handling of his bug reports. Naceri was previously credited with CVE-2021-41379 and CVE-2021-24084.
