# Six AirDrop and Quick Share flaws enable crashes and handshake bypass

Researchers disclosed six flaws in Apple AirDrop and Samsung Quick Share (June 2026). All three AirDrop flaws crash 'sharingd', the macOS/iOS background service that also runs AirPlay, Handoff, Universal Clipboard, Continuity Camera and NameDrop, so one crash takes them all down; the simplest needs only a single malformed request to a device set to receive from 'Everyone'. Two Samsung Quick Share flaws let an attacker bypass the secure-session handshake. Apple patched one AirDrop bug (CVE pending, advisory not public); Google paid a bounty and landed a fix for a related Windows flaw.
