# CVE-2026-61500: Rejetto HFS 3.0.0-3.2.0 session forgery via Math.random(), actively exploited

CVE-2026-61500 (CVSS 9.3): Rejetto HFS 3.0.0 through 3.2.0 derives its session-cookie signing key from non-cryptographic Math.random() and leaks outputs of the same generator to unauthenticated clients at login; an attacker can reconstruct generator state, forge an administrator cookie and reach RCE. VulnCheck reports active exploitation attempts.
