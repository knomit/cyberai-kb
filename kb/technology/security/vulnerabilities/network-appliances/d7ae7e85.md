# CISA adds Progress Kemp LoadMaster flaw to KEV catalog after 792 exploit attempts

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) added a critical-severity flaw in Progress Kemp LoadMaster to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, following reports of roughly 792 exploit attempts. Reported August 2026.
