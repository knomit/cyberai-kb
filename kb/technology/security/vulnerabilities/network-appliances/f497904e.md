# CVE-2026-91843: unauthenticated pre-auth root RCE in Check Point Security Management and Log Servers

Check Point disclosed CVE-2026-91843 on 16 September 2026, a stack overflow in the pre-authentication login process of its Security Management Server and Log Servers, rated 9.8 CVSS by Check Point. It allows an attacker without credentials to run code as root over the network. Censys reported the overflow is triggered by a login request carrying a very long username. The vulnerable path runs only through the Trusted Clients setting, which controls which hosts may connect via SmartConsole. Affected branches by Jumbo Hotfix Take: R82.10 at Take 44 or below, R82 at Take 126 or below, R81.20 at Take 166 or below, R81.10 at Take 190 or below, plus end-of-support R81, R80.40, R80.30, R80.20, R80.10 and R80. R82.20 is also vulnerable per Check Point despite not appearing in the CVE record, and Censys reported no Jumbo Hotfix yet protects that branch. Standalone deployments, Log Servers and Multi-Domain servers are vulnerable; hosted Smart-1 Cloud is not. As of 17-18 September 2026 there was no evidence of in-the-wild exploitation, no public PoC, and no KEV listing. Operational consequence: apply the LivePatch fix in sk1000155, and VERIFY installation with `cplp list` rather than assuming automatic updates delivered it — Check Point customers reported delayed automatic rollout for prior fixes. Also restrict Trusted Clients to known hosts and never expose management access to the internet. This does NOT mean automatic updates alone are sufficient.
