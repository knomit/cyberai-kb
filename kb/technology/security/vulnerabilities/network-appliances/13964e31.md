# Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN zero-day CVE-2026-20245 exploited

Per Google-owned Mandiant, an unknown threat actor exploited CVE-2026-20245 (CVSS 7.8) in Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN as a zero-day at least two months before public disclosure. The flaw lets an authenticated local attacker with netadmin privileges execute arbitrary commands with elevated privileges via a crafted file, due to insufficient validation of user-supplied input. The actor used anti-forensic techniques (selectively deleting and restoring artifacts) to evade detection. Cisco acknowledged awareness of exploitation earlier in the month.
