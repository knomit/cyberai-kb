# N-able releases N-central Hotfix 2 amid active exploitation of RMM flaw

N-able released a fresh round of hotfixes (Hotfix 2) for its N-central Remote Monitoring and Management (RMM) product as part of its investigation into ongoing exploitation of a recently disclosed security flaw. Attackers reached managed systems and established persistence. Reported August 2026.
