# FBI/USSS advisory Oct 2026: FortiBleed still active, 86,644 Fortinet credentials

FBI and Secret Service warned Oct 6 2026 that the FortiBleed credential-harvesting campaign against internet-facing FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN gateways remains active. It exploits reused/leaked credentials and legacy SHA-256 password storage; first documented June 2026, it had netted 86,644+ working device credentials across 194 countries as of June 19 2026.
