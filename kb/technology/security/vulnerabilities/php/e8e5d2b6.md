# PHP2Shell: two critical PHP RCE flaws (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619) patched; millions of scan attempts

The Hacker News weekly recap (2026-10-26): the PHP Foundation patched two critical unauthenticated RCE vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 (codename PHP2Shell). Cloudflare saw millions of exploitation attempts after disclosure; no successful exploitation observed yet; some sites went offline as a precaution. Patch PHP promptly even without confirmed exploitation.
