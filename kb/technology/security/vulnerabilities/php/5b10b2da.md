# PHP2Shell: exploit attempt hit Amazon honeypot 4 days before disclosure

Amazon Threat Intelligence reported an attempt to exploit the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities (two PHP core flaws, each rated 9.2) on 2026-10-17 against its MadPot honeypot, from an IP in a commercial anonymization network geolocated to Australia, while the flaws were still under embargo. It is unclear how the actor obtained exploit information. The flaws are under broad in-the-wild exploitation after patches were released. Consequence: embargoed AI-discovered vulnerabilities can leak or be independently found; patch PHP promptly.
