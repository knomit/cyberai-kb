# Check Point patched two critical VPN certificate-handling flaws

Check Point patched two critical VPN certificate-handling flaws it discovered itself: CVE-2026-85102, a certificate trust validation failure enabling unauthenticated RCE on Security Gateways, and CVE-2026-85103, a heap-based buffer overflow. Disclosed and patched September 9, 2026, with no evidence of exploitation.
