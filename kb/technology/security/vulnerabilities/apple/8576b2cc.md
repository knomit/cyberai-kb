# Apple CoreGraphics CVE-2026-86950: public PoC, CISA KEV deadline Oct 2

First public PoC released for CVE-2026-86950, an Apple CoreGraphics flaw triggered by a malicious PDF with a crafted embedded font that crashes unpatched iPhones and Macs. Apple patched Sept 28, crediting Meta Product Security, and said it may have been used in extremely sophisticated attacks on specific targeted individuals on iOS versions before iOS 27. CISA added it to KEV with a federal patch deadline of October 2. WhatsApp PDF checks hint at a possible delivery path (speculative). Follow-up to the earlier patch fact.
