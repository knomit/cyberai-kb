# GitLab and SAP each disclosed maximum-severity flaws in September 2026

The September 2026 trending-CVE cycle included a CVSS 10.0 file-read vulnerability in GitLab (CVE-2026-85706) drawing active interest, and a CVSS 10.0 kernel flaw in SAP (CVE-2026-58240, alongside CVE-2026-44756). Also notable in the same cycle were CVE-2026-82533, a sandbox escape in the DeepSeek Harness AI agent, and in-the-wild exploitation of JFrog Artifactory flaws CVE-2026-42016, CVE-2026-42018 and CVE-2026-82329.
