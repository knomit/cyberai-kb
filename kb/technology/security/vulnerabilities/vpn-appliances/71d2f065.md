# SonicWall patched two exploited SMA 1000 zero-days: CVE-2026-83548 and CVE-2026-83549

SonicWall released updates on/around 2026-09-02 for two Secure Mobile Access (SMA) 1000 series VPN flaws found internally by William Perry and Adam Babis and exploited in zero-day attacks. CVE-2026-83548 (CVSS 10.0) is a pre-authentication SSRF in the Appliance Work Place interface granting unauthenticated access to sensitive functionality. CVE-2026-83549 (CVSS 7.8) is a post-authentication OS command injection in the Appliance Management Console reachable by an authenticated administrator, leading to RCE. SonicWall says it investigated a case indicating active exploitation, suggesting the two are chained. Affected models are 6210, 7210 and 8200v on 12.4.3-03453 and older, and 12.5.0-02835 and older; fixes are 12.4.3-03526 and 12.5.0-02952. Consequence: patching alone is not sufficient — SonicWall advises checking for IoCs and, if found, re-imaging or re-deploying the appliance, changing all user and admin passwords, and resetting TOTP.
