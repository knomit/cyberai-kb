# Apple patches iMessage group-chat flaws CVE-2026-113961 and CVE-2026-113960 plus Brotli CVE-2026-111497

Apple's 2026-11-09 updates (iOS/iPadOS 27.2.1 etc.) fix CVE-2026-113961 (remote attacker could join an iMessage group chat and receive messages) and CVE-2026-113960 (member added could read earlier messages), plus Google Brotli decoder out-of-bounds write CVE-2026-111497, which CISA added to KEV on 2026-10-27, and Bluetooth/AirPlay flaws including no-interaction pairing. Older devices (iPhone XS/XR, iPad 7th gen, some Apple Watches) do not list the iMessage fixes. Notes don't say whether the iMessage flaws were exploited.
