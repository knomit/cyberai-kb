# Zoom annotation flaws allowed zero-click hijacking between meeting participants

Newly disclosed flaws in Zoom's annotation tool (which lets participants draw/type on a shared screen) could let anyone sharing their screen take over watching participants' computers, and vice versa, with no click, download, or prompt and no on-screen indication. Client fixes shipped in June and July 2026 — roughly two months before public disclosure. No exploitation has been reported and none of the three CVE identifiers appear in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Fixed in Zoom Workplace versions before 7.1.5 and 7.0.6 in their respective branches.
