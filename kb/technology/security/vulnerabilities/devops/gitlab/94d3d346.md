# GitLab CVE-2026-19478 (CVSS 9.4) came under active exploitation within days of disclosure

CVE-2026-19478 is a code injection vulnerability in GitLab with a CVSS score of 9.4. It allows an unauthenticated attacker to modify or delete publicly accessible GitLab projects and rewrite their data under certain conditions, without credentials, user interaction, or unusual configuration. GitLab said the issue could be exploited via a GraphQL directive.

Affected versions (Community Edition and Enterprise Edition):
- 18.2 before 18.11.11
- 19.0 before 19.0.8
- 19.1 before 19.1.6
- 19.2 before 19.2.4

Fixes shipped in GitLab CE/EE 19.2.4, 19.1.6, 19.0.8, and 18.11.11.

Exposure-management firm watchTowr told The Hacker News it reproduced the vulnerability within minutes of disclosure, and reported active exploitation in the wild within days of the public advisory.
