# Lava Security: ~2,100 internet-exposed NVIDIA DCGM Exporters leak telemetry of 12,000+ GPUs (CVE-2026-47483)

CVE-2026-47483 (CVSS 8.2) lets unauthenticated attackers exhaust NVIDIA DCGM Exporter profiling endpoints and crash the service. Lava found ~2,100 hosts exposing it unauthenticated (Mar-May 2026), leaking telemetry from 12,000+ GPUs across ~300 organizations (~$100M hardware). Fixed in 4.8.2 or later.
