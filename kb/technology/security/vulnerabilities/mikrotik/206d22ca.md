# CISA KEV adds MikroTik RouterOS CVE-2026-67279 (MikroTrick chain)

CISA added CVE-2026-67279 (CVSS 6.9), an improper-enforcement-of-behavioral-workflow vulnerability in MikroTik RouterOS, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog around Sept 25-26, 2026. It is part of the 'MikroTrick' exploit chain, used to gain full administrative control of internet-exposed, susceptible MikroTik devices. This is a distinct CVE from the CVE-2026-67277 and CVE-2026-86060 MikroTik RouterOS flaws CISA added to KEV in mid-September 2026 after CERT Polska reports.
