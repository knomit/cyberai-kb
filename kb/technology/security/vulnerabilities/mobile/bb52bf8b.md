# Chained OnePlus flaws let a permission-less app root a OnePlus 15; OnePlus disputed researcher's right to disclose

Security researcher Rasmus Moorats chained two unpatched flaws in OnePlus's own software to root a OnePlus 15 running the latest OxygenOS, using a malicious app that requests no special Android permissions; OnePlus told Moorats the same flaws affect many more of its own devices and OPPO's, without specifying which. OnePlus confirmed both flaws in May 2026 but told Moorats in writing that it alone decides when to disclose a flaw publicly, asserted 'the exclusive final right of vulnerability disclosure,' warned that publishing without its permission could create legal liability for him, and said that even after a fix ships, researchers may not publish full technical details on their own. Moorats published his findings in full, including OnePlus's reply, on September 24, 2026, with no fix yet released.
