# CISA adds two Ivanti Connect Secure flaws to KEV; part of exploit chain by UTA0576

On Friday (Oct 30, 2026) CISA added two Ivanti Connect Secure flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after in-the-wild exploitation: CVE-2026-111357 (CVSS 8.8, missing authentication for a critical function, local privilege escalation by an authenticated attacker) and CVE-2026-118527 (CVSS 4.3, predictable identifiers allowing a remote authenticated attacker to upload attacker-controlled files to a known disk location). They are part of a chain first documented by Volexity earlier in October as weaponized together with authentication bypass CVE-2026-104935 (CVSS 8.6) by a previously undocumented actor, UTA0576, yielding unauthenticated root access. Consequence: patch Ivanti Connect Secure; the low CVSS of the file-upload bug understates its role in the chain.
