# WordPress Super Forms and Elementor Pro flaws under mass exploitation

WordPress plugins Super Forms (CVE-2026-14894) and Elementor Pro (CVE-2026-32475) came under active exploitation in September 2026; Wordfence blocked 250,000 and 190,000 attempts respectively. Successful exploitation allows PHP web-shell upload and full site takeover.
