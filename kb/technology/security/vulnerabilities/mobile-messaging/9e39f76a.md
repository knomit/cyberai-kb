# WeWorm: a zero-click WeChat flaw allowed account takeover and self-propagation via unanswered calls

Researchers at Calif disclosed a critical vulnerability in Tencent-owned WeChat enabling a worm dubbed WeWorm that spreads through calls on both Android and iOS without the recipient answering. Exploitation takes seconds and yields full control of the victim's WeChat account — reading and sending messages, making calls, and acting on the victim's behalf — after which the compromised account calls another contact and repeats. Two conditions bound the threat: the attacker must already be on the victim's friends list, and declining the call stops the infection, while answering it or letting it ring allows spread. Tencent shipped fixes on 2026-08-21 (Android 8.0.77, iOS 8.0.76). There is no evidence of exploitation in the wild.
