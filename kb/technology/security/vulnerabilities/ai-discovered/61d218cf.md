# NodeBB patched eight high-severity flaws found by AI pentest agents

Eight security flaws in the NodeBB forum software were disclosed on a Wednesday in July 2026 along with exploit code. Aikido Security rated all eight as high severity and said its AI pentest agents found them during a roughly six-hour review of the codebase, exposing admin access and private chats. Reported by The Hacker News.
