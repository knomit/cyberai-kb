# Default admin:admin password on ForgeRock OpenIDM console exposed two automakers' app logins

Researchers Shubham Shah and Sam Curry found two car brands' mobile apps signed customers in via ForgeRock (now Ping Identity), and their OpenIDM administrator console (Apache Felix) still used a default password, allowing access to any customer account and, for connected cars, remote unlock and engine start. They tested only on their own and consenting owners' US cars in Feb 2026; both systems were fixed about two hours after reporting.
