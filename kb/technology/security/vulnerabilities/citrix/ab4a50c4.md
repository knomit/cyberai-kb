# Citrix NetScaler CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772 actively exploited; CISA patch deadline

THN Weekly Recap (2026-09-28): Citrix patched CVE-2026-88771 (improper input validation allowing unauthenticated arbitrary command execution) and CVE-2026-88772 (RCE or DoS), both under active exploitation. CISA said attackers are exploiting them globally and urged federal agencies to patch by the following Wednesday.
