# Three threat clusters exploited Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center flaws

Cisco Talos identified three separate ransomware and state-sponsored threat clusters exploiting two patched Secure Firewall Management Center flaws: CVE-2026-20079 (CVSS 10.0, unauthenticated authentication bypass leading to root access) and CVE-2026-20316 (CVSS 5.3, unauthenticated login to a low-privilege account). Observed activity includes credential theft and Qilin ransomware deployment.
