# MikroTrick chain hijacks MikroTik RouterOS via internet-exposed SSH with no authentication

CERT Polska coordinated disclosure of six MikroTik RouterOS vulnerabilities and published an attack warning on September 5, 2026. Two of them, CVE-2026-67276 and CVE-2026-86060 (both CVSS 9.2), can be chained — the combination is named MikroTrick — to bypass SSH authentication and obtain full administrative control of any device whose SSH service is reachable from the internet, with no credentials required. CERT Polska dates successful attacks to at least September 2, 2026. Shadowserver counted more than 122,500 MikroTik devices with SSH exposed on the open internet, concentrated in Brazil (11,300), the US (7,100), Indonesia (7,100), the Czech Republic (6,300) and Ukraine (5,100). Patched RouterOS versions are 7.25beta3, 7.24.2, 7.23.4, 7.23.5 (released September 4) and 6.49.21. CERT Polska advises updating and then auditing for unknown users and scripts, since patching alone does not evict an attacker who already has admin.
