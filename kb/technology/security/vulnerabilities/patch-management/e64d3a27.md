# Microsoft patched a record 974 flaws in its September 2026 update

Microsoft's September 2026 security update addressed a record 974 vulnerabilities. Among the flaws flagged as urgent in that cycle were CVE-2026-85880 (Windows Advanced Local Procedure Call, also used in the BlueMoon espionage exploit chain) and CVE-2026-81963.
