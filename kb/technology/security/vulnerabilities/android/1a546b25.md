# Pwn2Own Ireland 2026: three teams hack fully patched Pixel 10; Ikotas Labs wins $300K

On 2026-10-08 three teams broke Google's Pixel 10 at Pwn2Own Ireland (Cork), where targets must be fully patched. Ikotas Labs earned $300,000 (top prize) and overall win. ZDI had not published exploit details as of Oct 9; at least two used a previously known bug (a 'collision', accepted at lower prize).
