# Cloudflare Q3 2026: 18.2M network-layer DDoS attacks mitigated, up from 13.2M in Q2

Cloudflare reported mitigating 18.2 million L3 DDoS attacks and 14.4 trillion HTTP DDoS requests in Q3 2026 (about 8,226 L3 attacks per hour, 197,400 per day, one every 0.44 seconds), versus 13.2M in Q2 and 10.0M in Q1 2026 (about +38% QoQ). Growth is linked to DDoS-for-hire booter/stresser services. Short-burst, low-bandwidth attacks (under 10 minutes and 1 Gbps) surged 208%, while hyper-volumetric attacks declined as a trend.
