# GitHub is cutting public bug bounty payouts and moving top rewards to a VIP tier

Beginning July 27, 2026, GitHub will cut public bug bounty payouts by at least half at every severity level. Critical findings will drop from a range of $20,000-$30,000+ to a fixed $10,000, while the largest rewards move to a permanent, invite-only VIP tier.
