# Early August 2026 saw a cluster of critical vulnerabilities under active in-the-wild exploitation

In early August 2026, multiple independent critical/maximum-severity vulnerabilities were reported as actively exploited in the wild within days of each other: a Metabase zero-day (CVSS 10.0) allowing unauthenticated admin access; a Progress Kemp LoadMaster flaw added to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after ~792 exploit attempts; and an N-able N-central RMM authentication-bypass whose initial fix was incomplete, requiring a further hotfix. This reflects a period of concentrated exploitation pressure on enterprise infrastructure software (BI tools, load balancers, RMM platforms). Note: each disclosure is a separate, unrelated vulnerability in a different product — they are not a single campaign or shared root cause.
