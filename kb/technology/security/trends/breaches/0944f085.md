# Vulnerability exploitation overtook compromised credentials as top breach initial access vector

Per Verizon's 2026 data breach report, vulnerability exploitation overtook compromised credentials as the most common initial access vector for the first time in nearly two decades. 31% of breaches started with vulnerability exploitation (up from 20% in 2024). Credential abuse dropped from 22% to 13%. Only 26% of CISA KEV catalog vulnerabilities were fully remediated by organizations in 2025 (down from 38% prior year). Median remediation time rose to 43 days (up from 32). Ransomware accounted for 48% of all breaches (up from 44%), but median ransom payments declined from $150K to ~$140K.
