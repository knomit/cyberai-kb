# Mid-2026: AI is the central axis of an accelerating cyber offense/defense arms race

By June 2026 AI had become the defining force on both sides of cybersecurity. The Five Eyes alliance warned that frontier AI capable of taking down businesses and governments is 'months, not years' away, shrinking the vulnerability-to-exploit window and forcing defenders toward machine-speed automation and security fundamentals. In parallel, model providers shipped AI defensive tooling: OpenAI expanded Project Daybreak with GPT-5.5-Cyber and a 'patch the planet' open-source remediation effort, while the same class of frontier models (e.g., Anthropic's Mythos) can rapidly find software exploits, illustrating the dual-use dynamic the agencies warned about.
