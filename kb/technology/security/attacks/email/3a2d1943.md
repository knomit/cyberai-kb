# CSS-based attacks can break webmail defenses to steal passwords and tokens

New research shows that CSS embedded in an email can escape its message boundary and interfere with the surrounding webmail interface. Attack chains were demonstrated across Outlook, Gmail, Fastmail, Proton Mail, Yahoo Mail, and AOL Mail, enabling theft of passwords and tokens. Reported by The Hacker News, August 2026.
