# CSS embedded in email can escape its message boundary and attack webmail interfaces across major providers

PortSwigger researcher Gareth Heyes, presenting at Black Hat USA 2026 and reported by The Hacker News in August 2026, showed that CSS inside an email can escape its message boundary and interfere with the surrounding webmail interface.

Attack chains were demonstrated across Outlook, Gmail, Fastmail, Proton Mail, Yahoo Mail and AOL Mail. The techniques can capture passwords, take over third-party accounts, leak tokens, hijack trusted UI actions, and manipulate AI tools that read email — for example spoofing a Microsoft sign-in in Outlook/Firefox, or exfiltrating a Slack token via a Gmail chain following prompt injection.

Scope qualifier: the work is proof-of-concept. Fastmail and Proton Mail fixed some of the reported issues.
