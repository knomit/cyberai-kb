# Cloudflare Adaptive Intelligence tries to flip attacker economics rather than build taller walls

On Monday August 31, 2026, Cloudflare launched Adaptive Intelligence, a continuous detection engine that learns autonomously from live traffic and generates rules on the fly. Cloudflare says it analyzes more than a trillion web visits per day and acts as a 'single, constantly learning brain,' retraining continuously rather than waiting for scheduled rule releases, catching low-and-slow threats such as credential stuffing and scraping, separating humans from malicious intent via behavioral signals from Cloudflare Precursor, and deploying defense upgrades with zero downtime. CTO Dane Knecht framed the design goal explicitly: 'Building taller walls fails when the cost of scaling an attack is effectively zero' — the aim is to make automated attacks slower and more expensive by presenting a moving target instead of a static one.
