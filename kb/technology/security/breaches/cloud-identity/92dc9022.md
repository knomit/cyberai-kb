# 'TheHatman' Azure exfiltration campaign leaked employee databases from major enterprises

A threat actor named TheHatman has flooded cybercrime forums with enterprise employee databases said to have been downloaded directly from organizations' Azure/Entra portals using compromised credentials. Affected companies span IT services, hospitality, telecommunications, retail and logistics, including McDonald's, TCS, Vodafone, HCL Technologies, Kyndryl, Gap, Hexaware and Wyndham Hotels. Hudson Rock assesses the campaign most likely originates from targeted exploitation of infostealer infections rather than a systemic Azure zero-day.
