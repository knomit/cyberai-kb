# SafePal order-tracking plugin flaw exposed data of ~39,798 hardware wallet customers

Hardware wallet maker SafePal disclosed that an authorization flaw in an order-tracking plug-in exposed names, email addresses, shipping addresses, phone numbers, and purchase details of approximately 39,798 customers. Affected customers were notified individually on 2026-08-16 from security@safepal.com. Exposed records did not include seed phrases, private keys, wallet passwords, bank account information, payment card numbers, or government IDs, and SafePal reported no evidence that wallet access or funds were compromised. The affected orders were placed between 2025-03-02 and 2026-04-11, dates describing order placement rather than the exploitable window. SafePal warned of downstream fraud risk (fraudulent calls, emails, refund offers, fake firmware-update requests) because records link a named individual to a home address and a crypto-hardware purchase. Blockchain analytics firm Chainalysis has counted 46 violent crypto "wrench attack" incidents globally through late June with over $30 million stolen.
