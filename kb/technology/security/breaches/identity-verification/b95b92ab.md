# IDScan confirmed a breach tied to 153 million driver's license scans offered for sale

Identity verification firm IDScan.net confirmed that an unauthorized third party may have accessed and/or copied customer information stored within accounts on the IDScan.net cloud platform. The affected data may include full names and driver's license or other government-issued identification numbers. The investigation connected the Louisiana-based company to a database breach exposing scans of 153 million driver's licenses, surfaced after an illicit service called Nexus advertised access to more than 153 million driver's license scans belonging to Canadian and US citizens; that service has since gone offline.
