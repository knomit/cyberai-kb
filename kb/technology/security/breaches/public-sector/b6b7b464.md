# Thomson Reuters C-Track court software breach exposed multi-state court data

Thomson Reuters' West Publishing C-Track court case management platform was breached, with intrusion in March 2026 discovered on June 30, 2026. The breach potentially exposed Social Security numbers, driver's licence numbers, dates of birth and medical or health-insurance data for courts in 11 US states, the US Virgin Islands and Ontario, Canada.
