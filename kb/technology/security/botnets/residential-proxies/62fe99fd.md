# Google disrupts NetNut residential proxy network spanning ~2 million home devices

Google's Threat Intelligence Group, working with the FBI, Lumen, and others, significantly degraded NetNut, one of the largest networks that turns home devices into rented relays for other people's traffic. The residential proxy network spanned roughly 2 million home devices. Reported by The Hacker News, July 3, 2026.
