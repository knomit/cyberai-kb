# China-linked JDY botnet expands to 1,500+ SOHO/IoT devices

Lumen's Black Lotus Labs reported a resurgence and expansion of JDY, a China-nexus state-sponsored covert network now comprising over 1,500 SOHO and IoT devices operating as a centrally controlled high-performance scanner to discover, fingerprint, and map exposed services at scale. JDY was first flagged as a cluster within the KV-botnet in mid-December 2023 and linked to Chinese groups such as Volt Typhoon; operators changed behavior after the U.S. government's KV-botnet takedown in early 2024.
