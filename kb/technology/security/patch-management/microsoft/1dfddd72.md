# Microsoft September 2026 Patch Tuesday fixed a record 974 flaws

Microsoft's September 2026 Patch Tuesday fixed a record 974 vulnerabilities (999 including 25 non-Microsoft CVEs): 723 in Windows, 111 in Office, 62 in SQL, 22 in Developer Tools, with over 110 rated critical and two actively exploited zero-days.
