# 100+ tech companies published a collective cyberdefense letter in August 2026

On Thursday August 27, 2026, a coalition of more than 100 tech companies — including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft — published a letter on OpenAI's blog calling for collective action against AI-enabled cyberattacks. It advances three principles: recognizing the status quo is insufficient, empowering cyber defenders with AI, and mobilizing a global collective response. Recommendations cover enterprises (treat cyber defense as an immediate leadership priority), cybersecurity vendors, governments (coordinate at local/national/international levels, fund response programs), and frontier AI labs (responsible model access, funding and hands-on support for infrastructure defenders, and observability tools that keep models and agents traceable).
