# Greg Brockman called the Hugging Face breach a watershed moment for cybersecurity

In an essay titled "The Defender's Window," OpenAI president Greg Brockman described the AI-driven breach of Hugging Face as a "watershed moment for cybersecurity," writing that organizations he had spoken with knew they needed to fundamentally uplevel their cybersecurity practices with unprecedented speed. The framing is that AI temporarily advantages defenders, and that window should be used decisively.
