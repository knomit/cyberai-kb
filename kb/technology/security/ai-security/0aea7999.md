# Anthropic Cyber Verification Program: three tiers; Glasswing found 129,000+ vulnerabilities

On Oct 6 2026 Anthropic expanded its Cyber Verification Program into three access tiers giving vetted security professionals its most capable models with reduced safeguards/blocking classifiers. Anthropic says Project Glasswing found at least 129,000 verified vulnerabilities Apr-Jul 2026 (33,000+ rated critical/high, likely an undercount) plus 5,500 more via open-source scanning Apr-Oct 2026.
