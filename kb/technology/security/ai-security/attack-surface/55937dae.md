# AI development platforms have become a distinct attack surface for credential theft and cryptomining

VulnCheck reports threat actors have exploited as many as 12 vulnerabilities in the AI stack since 2025, with more than 15,000 successful attempts leveraging CVE-2026-0769, CVE-2025-3248 and CVE-2026-5027. Observed post-exploitation chains include dropping a Python credential harvester, proxy agents and SimpleHelp for remote access; enrolling hosts into an XMR cryptomining botnet; disabling auditd to create a forensic blind spot; and pivoting to scan for further targets. Most vulnerable Langflow hosts sit in the U.S., Germany, Malaysia, Brazil and India. The attraction is that AI dev platforms hold credentials for cloud environments and connected systems.
