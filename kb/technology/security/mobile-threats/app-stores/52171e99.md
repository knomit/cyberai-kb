# Google Play Early Access abused to distribute deceptive apps

Threat actors are abusing Google Play's Early Access program — where apps are not yet publicly reviewable or ratable — to distribute thousands of deceptive Android apps promising money, casino winnings or premium content, including a GTA imitator ("Vice Streets: Open World," package com.gamblechaos.withfriends.game) with over 1 million downloads. Suppressing user reviews removes the signal users rely on to spot fraud.
