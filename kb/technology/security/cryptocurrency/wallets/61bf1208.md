# Coldcard firmware RNG flaw linked to large Bitcoin theft (July 2026)

A firmware flaw in Coldcard hardware wallets was linked to a mass Bitcoin theft. Per Square Engineering, Coldcard firmware contains an RNG integration error causing ngu.random to use MicroPython's deterministic 'Yasmarang' fallback instead of the STM32 hardware RNG, making seed phrases predictable. Galaxy Research mapped a sweep on July 30, 2026 in which an attacker drained 1,196 Bitcoin addresses in 41 minutes, taking 1,082.65 BTC (~$70.2M at the time); The Hacker News' weekly recap cited an estimated $88.6M stolen across thousands of affected wallets.
