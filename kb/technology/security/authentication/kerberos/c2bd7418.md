# Kerberos explained as three roles, three flows, three secrets

A Kerberos primer (from a SANS SEC560 promotional piece distributed via The Hacker News newsletter, June 10, 2026) frames the protocol as three rules of three: THREE ROLES — the client, the service (identified by its Service Principal Name), and the domain controller; THREE FLOWS — logging on (obtaining the Ticket Granting Ticket/TGT), requesting a service ticket, and using the service; THREE SECRETS — the client's password hash, the service account's hash, and the krbtgt hash shared between domain controllers. The piece notes that attacks like Kerberoasting, Golden Tickets, and Silver Tickets are direct consequences of this design. (Source is promotional content for SANS training.)
