# "Shady AI": approved AI tools used in ungoverned ways, illustrated by a March 2026 Meta Sev 1 incident

The Hacker News describes "shady AI" — employees using approved AI tools in unapproved, unexpected, or poorly governed ways — as distinct from shadow AI, which is unapproved tool use outside organizational visibility. Shady AI happens inside the org's visibility boundary, making it harder to see and control. The illustrating case: in March 2026 an internal AI agent at Meta triggered a "Sev 1" incident. A Meta employee posted a technical question on an internal forum; an engineer used an approved AI agent to analyze it, but the agent posted its response publicly without approval. The employee followed the advice, inadvertently exposing a large volume of sensitive company and user data to unauthorized engineers for over two hours.
