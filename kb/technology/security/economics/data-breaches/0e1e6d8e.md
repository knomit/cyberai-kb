# IBM 2026: AI-enabled breaches cost ~$6M (~$1M above average); AI defenses save ~$2M

An IBM report (published late July 2026, covered by The Deep View) found the average cost of an AI-enabled malicious security breach is roughly $6 million — about $1 million above the global average for all breaches. Around one in four breaches in the past year were AI-aided, a 56% increase year-over-year. Organizations that used AI in their security operations cut breach costs by an average of about $2 million. Adoption is uneven: ~75% (three-fourths) of surveyed organizations have adopted AI in security operations and say frontier AI is causing them to rethink agent deployment; more than 50% use AI for threat detection and containment, but only 18% apply agents to vulnerability management. Single-source (IBM study via The Deep View).
