# IBM 2026: average AI-enabled data breach costs about $6 million

The Deep View, citing an IBM report (published late Jul 2026), reported that the average cost of an AI-enabled malicious security breach is roughly $6 million — about $1 million above the global average for all breaches. Around one in four breaches in the past year were aided by AI, a 56% increase year-over-year. Organizations using AI in security operations cut breach costs by an average of about $2 million; ~75% have adopted AI in security ops, but only 18% apply agents to vulnerability management.
