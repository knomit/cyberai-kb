# Published detail did not determine exploitation across three 2026 flaws; precondition breadth tracked it better, though not decisively

This corpus holds several records in which exploitation followed the publication of technical detail or a proof-of-concept, which invites the rule 'publication leads to exploitation'. Three 2026 cases written up by the same research firm show that rule is not what the evidence supports — and the third shows the better rule is not clean either. A LATER DEVELOPMENT, RECORDED BELOW, MAY HAVE OVERTAKEN THE SECOND CASE.

EXPLOITED AT SCALE, NO PRECONDITION. 'wp2shell' chains CVE-2026-63030 and CVE-2026-60137 into pre-authenticated RCE in WordPress Core via the REST batch API. Per the record it 'can be exploited anonymously on a STANDARD WORDPRESS INSTALLATION WITH NO PLUGINS OR SPECIAL CONDITIONS REQUIRED', achieving full site compromise. By 18 July 2026 the flaws had CVE IDs, the full mechanism was published and a working PoC was public; mass exploitation began in the early hours of a Saturday UTC, first using public exploit code to exfiltrate hashed credentials, with RCE following as further details emerged.

NOT EXPLOITED (AS OF JUNE 2026), PRECONDITION PRESENT. CVE-2026-8037 in Progress Kemp LoadMaster is CVSS 9.8 — HIGHER-RATED — and lets an unauthenticated attacker run commands as root, but only 'VIA A CRAFTED API REQUEST WHEN THE API IS ENABLED'. It was reported via ZDI on 15 April 2026, the advisory published 4 June, and watchTowr Labs published a technical exploit write-up on 29 June. As of that reporting: no in-the-wild exploitation reported.

AUGUST 2026 DEVELOPMENT — THIS CASE MAY NO LONGER HOLD. kb/technology/security/vulnerabilities/network-appliances/d7ae7e85.md records that CISA added A CRITICAL PROGRESS KEMP LOADMASTER FLAW to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after roughly 792 exploit attempts (reported August 2026), and kb/technology/security/trends/bb8f5ab8.md carries the same item. CRITICALLY, NEITHER RECORD NAMES THE CVE, so whether the KEV entry is CVE-2026-8037 or a different LoadMaster flaw IS NOT ESTABLISHED IN THIS CORPUS. IF IT IS THE SAME FLAW, the 'not exploited' leg of this record is FALSIFIED and the two-case contrast that motivates the precondition reading collapses to a timing artefact — exploitation arriving roughly two months later than in the WordPress case rather than not at all. ANYONE RELYING ON THE LOADMASTER NEGATIVE MUST RESOLVE THE CVE IDENTITY FIRST. This is exactly the failure mode this record's own caveat named in advance ('absence of evidence ... could reflect a shorter observation window'), which is why it is recorded here rather than quietly dropped.

THE COMPLICATION — EXPLOITED DESPITE A PRECONDITION. CVE-2026-50522 (CVSS 9.8), a deserialization flaw in Microsoft Office SharePoint Server, 'came under active exploitation AFTER A PUBLIC PROOF-OF-CONCEPT' — and it requires 'a network attacker AUTHENTICATED AS AT LEAST A SITE OWNER'. That is a real precondition, and it did not prevent exploitation. It was the third SharePoint Server flaw from the same July 2026 Patch Tuesday to be exploited.

WHAT SURVIVES THE AUGUST DEVELOPMENT. The claim that DISCLOSURE ALONE DOES NOT DETERMINE EXPLOITATION rests on all three cases having published technical detail while their outcomes and timings differed, and that holds whether or not the LoadMaster flaw was later exploited. The claim that PRECONDITION BREADTH ORDERS THE CASES BETTER THAN SEVERITY is WEAKENED if LoadMaster was exploited after all, since the ordering depended on it being the negative case. What remains regardless is the SharePoint lesson: a precondition is not protective in itself, and what matters is HOW MANY REAL TARGETS SATISFY IT. 'The API is enabled' is a deployment choice a minority make; 'someone holds Site Owner' is true in almost every tenant of any size. That quantity is rarely stated in an advisory, which is precisely why the cheap proxies get used instead.

RELATED AND POINTING THE SAME WAY FROM THE OTHER SIDE: kb/technology/security/patterns/7cd2242c.md records August 2026 exploitation of critical enterprise-infrastructure flaws arriving within days of patch, SOMETIMES WITH NO PUBLIC PoC AT ALL. If exploitation can precede or bypass public exploit code entirely, disclosure is neither necessary nor sufficient.

SCOPE AND CAVEATS: THIS IS THREE CASES, one of them a NEGATIVE observation now in doubt. The LoadMaster record carries sources: 0 in this corpus. Exposure counts differ enormously between WordPress, SharePoint Server and a commercial load balancer, so installed-base size is confounded with precondition breadth throughout and this record cannot separate them. WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this does NOT claim publishing technical detail is harmless or that disclosure timing is irrelevant — the corpus separately records exploitation beginning within days, and in some cases hours, of details becoming public. It does not claim preconditions are worthless as a filter. It claims that disclosure is not sufficient and that the population satisfying the precondition is the variable worth estimating — while acknowledging that three cases cannot establish a rate, and that one of the three may since have turned.
