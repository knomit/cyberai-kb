# Text-rendered QR phishing survives image blocking in email clients

Kaspersky detailed a phishing technique in which the attacker builds a scannable QR code out of text characters and markup directly in the email body instead of embedding an image. There is no attachment to open, no embedded picture to decode, and nothing for an image-based or OCR scanner to key off. Because it is markup rather than a remote image, a mail client configured to block images still renders the code, so turning off image loading — a common user precaution against tracking pixels and image-based QR lures — does not defeat this variant. The human reader sees a perfectly scannable QR code.
