# KnowBe4 counted 29,785 Microsoft 365 Direct Send spoofs in July-August 2026

KnowBe4 Threat Lab reported continuing abuse of Microsoft 365 Direct Send in phishing campaigns, counting 29,785 confirmed Direct Send spoofs across July and August 2026. Direct Send was designed to let office printers, scanners and legacy on-premises applications send email without a dedicated account, which also bypasses security gateways; attackers connecting to that same open endpoint can send mail appearing to come from anyone at the organization — HR, accounting, admin or the CEO — because it technically entered through the organization's own infrastructure. Activity clustered Monday to Tuesday during US Eastern business hours, peaking just before noon, dropping, then reaching its highest point around 2 p.m. EST.
