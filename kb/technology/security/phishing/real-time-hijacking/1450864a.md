# Insurance-sector phishing evolves into real-time account hijacking (CTM360)

Research by CTM360, reported by The Hacker News on July 25, 2026, found that phishing operations targeting insurance portals have shifted from harvesting credentials for later use to real-time account hijacking. Instead of collecting usernames and passwords to reuse, attackers now synchronize their activity with the victim, authenticating against the legitimate insurance portal at the moment the victim unknowingly completes the login, so the entire compromise can unfold within a single browsing session. This defeats defenses that rely only on identifying malicious or impersonation domains, and points to a broader need to understand attacker infrastructure and operational workflows rather than just blocklisting sites.
