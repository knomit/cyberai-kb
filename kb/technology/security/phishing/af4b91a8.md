# Microsoft Teams phishing surge in 2026: IT support impersonation + remote access for ransomware

eSentire reports a significant increase in Microsoft Teams-based phishing since early 2026. Threat actors impersonate IT support and help desk personnel to trick users into granting remote access. Attack pattern: email bombing → Teams message from fake IT support offering to resolve the 'issue' → remote access granted → data exfiltration and/or ransomware deployment. Linked to the broader trend of SaaS platform abuse where attackers operate within legitimate environments.
