# Bitget breach update: $387.5M stolen via third-party security product zero-day

Bitget confirmed (2026-09-30) attackers stole $387.5 million from hot and warm wallets (breach Sept 24, 2026) by exploiting a zero-day in third-party security products, per SlowMist, which recovered a customized attacker tool. Attackers obtained high-level internal credentials to issue fraudulent withdrawals. Circle, Tether and NEAR Intents froze about $1.1 million. This supersedes the earlier $351.6M figure and North Korea attribution as reported at that time; the attribution is not restated in this THN item.
