# N0va phishkit targets North American and European organizations by abusing legitimate authentication flows

As reported by The Hacker News in September 2026, a phishing kit called N0va is targeting organizations across North America and Europe with campaigns that impersonate trusted services and abuse legitimate authentication flows, giving threat actors access to victim accounts.
