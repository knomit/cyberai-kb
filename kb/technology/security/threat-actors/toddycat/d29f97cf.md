# ToddyCat-linked Umbrij malware abuses OAuth to read Gmail via Google API

The threat actor known as ToddyCat has been attributed to a new malware called Umbrij, designed to gain surreptitious access to a victim's email correspondence by abusing OAuth tokens to reach Gmail through the Google API. Reported by The Hacker News, July 3, 2026.
