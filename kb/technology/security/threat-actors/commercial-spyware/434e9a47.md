# Pegasus iMessage zero-click infected Serbian student movement member's iPhone

Citizen Lab, working with Serbia's SHARE Foundation, reported that the iPhone of a member of Serbia's student protest movement was infected with NSO Group's Pegasus spyware via an iMessage zero-click exploit requiring no interaction from the target. High-confidence infection indicators span December 2025 to January 2026, which does not rule out additional infections outside that window. Apple addressed the exploited iMessage flaw in iOS 18.4.1. Citizen Lab describes at least 14 people connected to Serbia's student movement, civil society, and political opposition as targeted with advanced spyware since early 2026, ahead of the 2026 election cycle, and also reports a newly built Android spyware functionally similar to NoviSpy but engineered to evade detection.
