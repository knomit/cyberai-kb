# Researchers hired 3 suspected North Korean IT workers via fake crypto startup

Security researchers created a fake cryptocurrency startup, advertised developer jobs, and hired three people they believe were North Korean IT operatives, recording every company-issued virtual machine. Onboarding documents used mismatched identities (e.g. a claimed Pasadena, Texas resident who sent a California driver's license and New York bank account; another with a stolen New York license). One applicant's ID image metadata indicated processing with Google Gemini and carried a Google SynthID watermark. The work references a July 31 joint government alert on North Korean IT-worker infiltration seeking real employee access to source code and internal systems.
