# Jade Sleet breached an India-based IT services firm using FLATROOF and ROOFDECK macOS backdoors

SentinelOne attributed the compromise of an India-based, deliberately 'much smaller' IT services organization to the North Korean threat actor Jade Sleet. The intrusion used Apple macOS backdoors tracked as FLATROOF (aka Gaslight) and ROOFDECK — both previously observed in the March–April 2026 attack on KelpDAO's LayerZero bridge.

Jade Sleet is also tracked as PUKCHONG, Slow Pisces, TraderTraitor, and UNC4899, and has a history of targeting the Web3 sector for cryptocurrency heists; in early 2025 it was tied to the theft of about $1.5 billion from Bybit's cold wallet infrastructure following a supply chain compromise of Safe{Wallet}'s developer environment.

The pattern to carry: Jade Sleet reaches high-value targets by first compromising DEVELOPERS at smaller supplier organizations, so being a small firm is not a reason to assume you are out of scope for a state actor — supplier size is the attraction, not a deterrent. macOS is a primary target platform for this actor, not an afterthought.
