# Citizen Lab: Candiru zero-click spyware reached Al Jazeera devices via ads

Citizen Lab (University of Toronto) reported on 2026-10-15 that Israeli mercenary-spyware vendor Candiru infected Al Jazeera devices through ordinary online ads with no user click; attack code ran when the ad loaded, sometimes out of sight. Citizen Lab calls this the first public technical confirmation of mercenary spyware reaching real devices through targeted ads (previously described only from leaked documents). Spyware was recovered from one device but not the browser/app exploit; the initial step is inferred from attack setup.
