# Bauman University leak exposes Russia's military cyber training pipeline

Leaked Bauman Moscow State Technical University records revealed a long-running program under Department No. 4 that trained roughly 250 career and reserve students in special intelligence, operational information-technical effects, and information-technology protection. Per DomainTools, the curriculum combined offensive and defensive cyber techniques with offensive doctrine for active-measures campaigns and GRU activities, followed by field placements in military units and academies. Department No. 4 is assessed as tied to the GRU, with identified graduates assigned to units associated with APT28 and Sandworm.
