# Threat actor 'Sable Squirrel' spent nearly $7 million acquiring expired domains for a criminal enterprise

Infoblox assesses that a threat actor it tracks as Sable Squirrel has spent close to $7 million on expired (dropcatch) domains, building a criminal enterprise spanning illegal sports streaming, online gambling promotion, and malware infrastructure. Infoblox notes the money buys aged registration history, backlinks, residual traffic, and reputation signals that many defenses still treat as indicators of trustworthiness. Beyond inherited reputation, dropped domains also carry lingering connections: email intended for the original holder, cached search results, inherited web traffic, lingering DNS records, and in some cases a ready-made platform for code injection on already-compromised sites.
