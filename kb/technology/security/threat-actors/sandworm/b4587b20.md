# ESET: UAC-0099 handed access to Sandworm for destructive wiper attack on Ukrainian logistics firm

ESET reports UAC-0099 (Earth Sirrush) gained initial access to a Ukrainian logistics company on July 16, 2025 via spear-phishing (MATCHBOIL downloader to MATCHWOK backdoor), deployed a previously undocumented backdoor MythicRatatoskr with C2 hosted inside the Yggdrasil mesh network, then handed access to Sandworm, which ran a data-wiping attack on September 17, 2025, over two months after foothold.
