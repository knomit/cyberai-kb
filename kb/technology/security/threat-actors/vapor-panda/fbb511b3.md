# Vapor Panda uses AI pentest framework ARTEX against 200+ organizations (CrowdStrike)

CrowdStrike attributed use of ARTEX, a Go-based AI-powered penetration testing framework, to China-based adversary Vapor Panda (formerly VexatiousTemper; overlaps Trend Micro's Earth Estries, active since Feb 2021). Starting Oct 17, 2026 it was used against 200+ organizations: foreign ministries across Latin America, Europe, Asia and the Middle East, a European presidency and PM office, 19 US organizations in pharma, semiconductor, defense, nuclear and finance, and telecoms in the US, Belgium and the Philippines. CrowdStrike says agentic tooling lets a small team widen its target set, not a change of mission.
