# DoJ/FBI disrupted the QScan and QTRouter platforms run by Chinese quartermaster QTFY — and DoJ later CORRECTED its own release: the named US agencies were TARGETS, not confirmed victims

MERGED TO PUT A CORRECTION NEXT TO THE CLAIM IT CORRECTS. One input record reproduced DoJ's original press statement listing US agencies as VICTIMS; a second record held DoJ's subsequent correction changing that to TARGETS; a third covered the FBI side of the same disruption. Left apart, a reader of the first would carry the uncorrected claim. All three are folded in here.

## THE DISRUPTION
On AUGUST 26, 2026 the U.S. Department of Justice announced the disruption of two hacking platforms, QSCAN and QTROUTER, operated by Chinese state-sponsored threat actors to target U.S. critical infrastructure and sensitive networks. The FBI disrupted the infrastructure via domain seizures.

## THE ACTOR
The activity is attributed to a group tracked as QTFY — a TECHNICAL QUARTERMASTER that sold reconnaissance, proxy management and operational routing capabilities to Chinese cyber-espionage operations rather than running intrusions itself. QTFY built and operated the QScan and QTRouter frameworks, used against U.S. critical infrastructure networks, and is employed by the China-based NANJING XINJIUWEI NETWORK TECHNOLOGY COMPANY. Lumen Black Lotus Labs researcher Damon Rouse, who tracked the activity for 18 MONTHS, said the digital quartermaster has been active SINCE MAY 2018 and that Nanjing Xinjiuwei counts both China's MINISTRY OF STATE SECURITY and the PEOPLE'S LIBERATION ARMY among its customers.

## THE CORRECTION — READ THIS BEFORE CITING THE AGENCY LIST
DoJ's original press statement listed NASA, the FEDERAL RESERVE, the DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY, the DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE itself, HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, the NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH and the U.S. SENATE as VICTIMS of QTFY computer-intrusion activity. DoJ SUBSEQUENTLY CORRECTED THE RELEASE to list them instead as 'AMONG THE TARGETS OF QTFY', noting the edits ensure the release accurately reflects THE ALLEGATIONS IN THE AFFIDAVIT supporting the domain seizures. Reported by Reuters.

THE DISTINCTION IS LOAD-BEARING: 'target' records that the actor aimed at an organisation; 'victim' asserts a successful intrusion. The corrected wording is the operative one, and the original victim framing MUST NOT be cited.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN
- The correction does NOT establish that none of the named agencies was compromised — it establishes that the affidavit supports 'target', not that intrusion did not occur.
- The MSS/PLA customer claim and the 2018 start date are Lumen Black Lotus Labs' assessment as reported, not adjudicated findings.
- Disruption of infrastructure is not the same as dismantling the actor; nothing here reports arrests or cessation of the activity.
