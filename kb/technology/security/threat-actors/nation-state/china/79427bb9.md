# DoJ disrupted QScan and QTRouter platforms run by Chinese group QTFY

On August 26, 2026 the U.S. Department of Justice announced the disruption of two hacking platforms, QScan and QTRouter, operated by Chinese state-sponsored threat actors to target U.S. critical infrastructure and sensitive networks. The activity is attributed to a group tracked as QTFY, employed by Nanjing Xinjiuwei Network Technology Company. DoJ named victims including NASA, the Federal Reserve, the Department of Energy, the Department of Justice, Health and Human Services, the National Institutes of Health, and the U.S. Senate. Lumen Black Lotus Labs researcher Damon Rouse, who tracked the activity for 18 months, said the digital quartermaster has been active since May 2018 and that Nanjing Xinjiuwei counts both China's Ministry of State Security and the People's Liberation Army among its customers.
