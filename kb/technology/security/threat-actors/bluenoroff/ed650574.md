# BlueNoroff Zoom/Teams phishing kit profiles crypto wallets before delivering malware

The North Korean threat actor BlueNoroff operates an active phishing kit that impersonates Zoom and Microsoft Teams via typosquatted domains in ClickFix-style social engineering campaigns, according to JUMPSEC research reported by The Hacker News on July 25, 2026. The platform profiles victims' cryptocurrency wallets before malware delivery, enabling selective targeting of high-value victims. It uses compromised trusted industry contacts as the initial access vector to build a self-propagating attack chain via Telegram, combining trust abuse, social engineering, wallet reconnaissance, and malware delivery into a repeatable, operator-driven victim-acquisition pipeline.
