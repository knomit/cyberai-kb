# FBI advisory: Integrity Technology Group hackers stole email via 1,300-script scanning tool

On 2026-10-08 the FBI and agencies of 6 other countries said hackers tied to sanctioned (US, UK) Integrity Technology Group stole email from government, law enforcement, healthcare and religious organizations in Southeast Asia, using a scanner with 1,300+ scripts, password guessing against Microsoft 365/Exchange, and mailbox-copying tools. Active since at least mid-January 2021; advisory gives no theft dates or victim count.
