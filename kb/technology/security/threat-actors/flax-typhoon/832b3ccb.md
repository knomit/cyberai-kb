# FBI/DoJ seize 7 domains disrupting Flax Typhoon tools

FBI and DoJ announced disruption of tools used by China-linked Flax Typhoon (aka Ethereal Panda, RedJuliett), seizing seven domains (c0cc[.]cc, 98aiblog[.]com, 98aicai[.]com, 98aicode[.]com, outlook3650[.]com, youtubecard[.]com, linkedinns[.]net) used to scan and sometimes infiltrate US critical infrastructure. Group is tied to Integrity Technology Group (Beijing); earlier Raptor Train botnet was taken down in a prior US operation.
