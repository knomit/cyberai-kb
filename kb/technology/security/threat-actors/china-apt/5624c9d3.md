# China-linked UAT-7810 expands LapDogs ORB network with LONGLEASH

Cisco Talos attributes the LapDogs Operational Relay Box (ORB) network — first seen June 2025 — to a Chinese APT tracked as UAT-7810, which breaks into internet-facing networking devices and refines bespoke malware (LONGLEASH) to grow the network. ORB infrastructure is handed to secondary China-nexus actors such as UAT-5918, linked to attacks on Taiwan critical infrastructure since at least 2023.
