# Nimbus Manticore deploys NightLedger backdoor

Kaspersky attributed a fresh 2026 campaign to Iranian state-backed group Nimbus Manticore (aka GalaxyGato, Mirage Kitten, Smoke Sandstorm, Subtle Snail, UNC1549). It deploys a previously undocumented Windows backdoor called NightLedger (for reconnaissance, command execution, file operations, process discovery, screenshots) plus two custom WebSocket tunnelers, BridgeHead and ArcBridge, for covert access. Targets span the Middle East, Africa and South Asia: Egypt, government/SMB in Jordan and Tanzania, aviation in Pakistan, telecom in Ethiopia, and financial-sector entities in Burkina Faso.
