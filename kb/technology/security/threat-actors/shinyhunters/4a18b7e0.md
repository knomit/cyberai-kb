# FBI arrests third suspected ShinyHunters co-conspirator tied to FBI jobs portal breach (Oct 2026)

FBI Director Kash Patel said on 2026-10-09 on X that the FBI arrested another suspected ShinyHunters co-conspirator. ShinyHunters said in September 2026 it had breached the FBI's jobs portal and stolen sensitive data on almost all FBI agents and job applicants. The suspect is reportedly a Canadian citizen arrested in Pennsylvania (NYT and CBS, unnamed sources); the FBI has not named the suspect and no charges are public. Patel's post does not say whether the suspect took part in the breach. Do not treat the arrestee as confirmed to have performed the breach.
