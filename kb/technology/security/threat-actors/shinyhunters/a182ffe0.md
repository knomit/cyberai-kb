# Dutch police arrest 24-year-old Amsterdam man in ShinyHunters investigation

Dutch police confirmed a 24-year-old Amsterdam man was arrested in September 2026 in the ShinyHunters investigation, due before Rotterdam District Court on 2026-09-29. Brian Krebs and DataBreaches.Net identified him as Pepijn van der Stap (aka Umbreon), reportedly arrested 2026-09-15 and previously apprehended in 2023.
