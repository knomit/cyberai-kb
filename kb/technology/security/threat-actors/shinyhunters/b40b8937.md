# ShinyHunters: 'Rey' detained in Jordan; FBI breach via unpatched third-party platform; two arrests

Reuters reported ShinyHunters member 'Rey' (Saif al-Din Khader) was detained in Jordan on 2026-09-29 and is cooperating with the FBI; a 24-year-old Amsterdam man (believed Pepijn van der Stap) was also arrested. The FBI said its employee-data breach resulted from a contractor failing to implement a patch on a third-party platform (reported as Oracle PeopleSoft); the FBI removed the Accenture contractor.
