# OceanLotus deploys SPECTRALVIPER in FireAnt supply chain attack on Vietnam investors

ESET attributed two campaigns to OceanLotus (a Vietnam-aligned APT active since 2012) deploying the SPECTRALVIPER backdoor. One was a prolonged espionage operation against a Vietnamese infrastructure and transport construction corporation (mid-2024 to February 2026); the other a supply chain attack via FireAnt Metakit, a software platform popular with Vietnamese stock investors (October 2025 to March 2026). ESET notes a shift toward domestic espionage.
