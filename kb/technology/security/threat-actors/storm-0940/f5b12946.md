# Microsoft: Storm-0940 used ARTEX agentic AI pentest framework in ConnectWise Cloud Backup supply-chain attack

Microsoft attributed the ConnectWise Cloud Backup supply-chain compromise to China state-aligned Storm-0940, which used ARTEX, a Go-based agentic AI penetration-testing framework (made closed-source after earlier abuse against South Korean financial institutions), for scanning, reconnaissance, password spraying and OAuth app abuse at downstream victims at 'machine speed'. Hundreds of organizations across many sectors (aerospace/defense, finance, government, semiconductors, etc.) have been targeted since September 2026.
