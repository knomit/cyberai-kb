# Attackers converge on edge-device vendor ecosystems rather than individual CVEs

A joint SentinelOne and Tenable analysis of two independent datasets found that both nation-state and criminal threat actors focus on edge-device vendors and weak points in the attack surface rather than on specific CVEs, and that the two actor classes independently converge on the same edge infrastructure. Their exposure data is flatter than press coverage implies: Fortinet, the vendor most associated with edge-device attacks in the press, sits mid-pack at 25% container-grain exposure, well behind F5 at 54% and within a 10-point band of Check Point, Ivanti and Citrix. 54% of customer environments running F5 products have at least one exposed, actively exploited CVE. Citrix customers showed the slowest remediation, at a median 461 days time-to-patch. The companies frame this against attacker timelines compressing faster than standard patch cycles, which they attribute in part to frontier AI models narrowing the window between vulnerability discovery and exploitation — treat that causal attribution as the vendors' interpretation rather than a measured finding.
