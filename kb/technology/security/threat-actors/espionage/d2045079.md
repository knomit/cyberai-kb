# Russian group Laundry Bear exploited Zimbra zero-day CVE-2025-66376 to steal mail and 2FA

A Russian state-supported espionage group codenamed Laundry Bear exploited a then-zero-day in Zimbra (CVE-2025-66376) to access Western mailboxes. Zimbra fixed the flaw in November 2025, but it was weaponized in attacks since July 2025 delivering a JavaScript payload called ZimReaper that captures credentials and 2FA codes. Affected: Zimbra Collaboration Suite 10.0 before 10.0.18 and 10.1 before 10.1.13.
