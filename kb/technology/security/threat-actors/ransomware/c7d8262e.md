# FortiBleed credential-theft campaign linked to INC and Lynx ransomware operations

The financially motivated FortiBleed campaign, which harvested verified stolen credentials, has been attributed to the INC and Lynx ransomware operations, indicating the stolen credentials were intended for follow-on intrusions. Reported by The Hacker News, July 2, 2026.
