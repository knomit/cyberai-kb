# Police arrest 16-year-old suspected KillSec ransomware operator

Spanish police (Guardia Civil and Mossos d'Esquadra) arrested a 16-year-old in Alicante on 2026-09-30 as suspected main administrator of the KillSec ransomware group; 3 people were arrested in total (the others in their 20s, in the U.K. and Romania) and the leak site and servers were seized. Operation was led by Hamburg police and prosecutors, announced 2026-10-01. KillSec stole data and threatened to publish it unless victims paid.
