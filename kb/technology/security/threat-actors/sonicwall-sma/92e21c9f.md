# Threat actor UTA0533 exploited SonicWall SMA 1000 VPN zero-days for root access

A previously undocumented threat actor codenamed UTA0533 exploited SonicWall Secure Mobile Access (SMA) 1000 series VPN appliances as zero-days, prior to public disclosure, since June 22, 2026, to gain root access. The activity was uncovered during an incident-response investigation; the impacted organization was not identified.
