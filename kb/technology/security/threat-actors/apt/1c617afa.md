# UAT-11985 targets Taiwan research affiliates with AI-assisted fake event invites and Google AitM phishing

Per The Hacker News citing Cisco Talos (researcher Joey Chen): UAT-11985, first observed mid-2026, runs APT spear-phishing against individuals affiliated with Taiwan research organizations using lures built on legitimate public event themes and impersonating academic/policy institutions, likely with AI-assisted content generation. Victims are sent to an adversary-in-the-middle phishing framework that harvests Google credentials and MFA codes. Consequence: SMS/TOTP MFA does not stop AitM; phishing-resistant MFA is needed.
