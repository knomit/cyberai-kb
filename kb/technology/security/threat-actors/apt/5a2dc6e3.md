# Armored Likho targets governments and power sector with BusySnake stealer

Kaspersky attributed a previously undocumented threat actor named Armored Likho to cyberattacks on government agencies and the electric power sector across Russia, Brazil, and Kazakhstan. Kaspersky says the group blends financially motivated campaigns against individuals with targeted cyber espionage against organizations, using obfuscated modular RATs and infostealers (including a BusySnake stealer) engineered to bypass dynamic analysis, plus tools like Go2Tunnel for remote access and network tunneling.
