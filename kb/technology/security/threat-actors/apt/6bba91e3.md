# Group-IB details China-nexus JadeProx campaign using TriBack Loader

Group-IB codenamed 'JadeProx' a China-linked operation using DLL side-loading to deliver TriBack Loader, which in turn deploys AdaptixC2 and Beagle. Targets included a Vietnamese public hospital's medical imaging system, the Malaysian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and multiple Hong Kong educational institutions. The actor exploits internet-facing systems in Southeast Asia for web shells and uses custom Go-based relay infrastructure hidden behind Alibaba and Cloudflare.
