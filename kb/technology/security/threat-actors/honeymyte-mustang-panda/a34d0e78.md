# DLL side-loading is Mustang Panda actor-level tradecraft, constant across campaigns whose implants and victim sets do not overlap

Two independently reported 2026 Mustang Panda (HoneyMyte) campaigns share their execution primitive while sharing almost nothing else:

- An APJ-region campaign observed May 2026 (ongoing since September 2025) delivered an updated FDMTP backdoor via DLL side-loading; FDMTP profiles hosts and loads plugins for scheduled-task management, registry persistence, and file/command retrieval.
- A Kaspersky-reported campaign deployed CoolClient — as a secondary backdoor following PlugX — by renaming a legitimate signed Sangfor executable to defender.exe and side-loading a malicious libngs.dll from it, with victims in Myanmar, Mongolia, Pakistan and Russia.

Different implants (FDMTP vs CoolClient/PlugX), different reporting vendors, different victim regions, different post-execution capability sets — and the same way of getting code running: a legitimate, signed application loads an attacker-supplied DLL from its own directory. The implant is the variable; the side-load is the constant.

DETECTION CONSEQUENCE: hunting rules keyed to a named implant, its C2, or its plugin set expire with the campaign. Rules keyed to the primitive — a signed vendor binary loading a DLL from a non-canonical path, or resolving an import that ships with no such name in the vendor's own distribution — survive the implant swap that these two campaigns demonstrate the actor performs.

SCOPE AND CAVEATS: the APJ/FDMTP activity is described only as 'consistent with Mustang Panda tradecraft', NOT as confirmed attribution, and its report does not name which legitimate binary was abused — the shared element established here is the side-loading technique, not a shared loader or shared signed vendor. Each campaign rests on a single vendor report. WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this does not claim the two campaigns are the same operation, share infrastructure, or share tooling; and it does not claim side-loading is unique to this actor — it is widely used, so the primitive alone is not attribution. The CoolClient delivery specifics (Sangfor/defender.exe, the Defender exclusion, the scheduled task) are documented for the Myanmar campaign and must not be assumed identical elsewhere.
