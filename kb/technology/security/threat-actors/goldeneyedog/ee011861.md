# DigiCert April 2026 breach attributed to GoldenEyeDog subgroup (code-signing cert theft)

Security firm Expel attributed the April 2026 DigiCert security incident to a threat activity cluster dubbed 'CylindricalCanine', a subgroup of GoldenEyeDog (also known as APT-Q-27, Dragon Breath, and Miuuti Group), a Chinese cybercrime group active since at least 2015 and known for targeting the gambling and gaming sectors with counterfeit websites pushing malware-laced software. In April 2026 the group used its malware to access a support member's device at DigiCert, a code-signing certificate provider, and stole certificates intended for DigiCert customers. Central to its operations is a modified version of Gh0st RAT (aka Farfli). Analysis was shared by Expel researcher Aaron Walton.
