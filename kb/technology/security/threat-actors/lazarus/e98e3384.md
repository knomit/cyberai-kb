# CVE-2026-68820: Lazarus Group exploited a Windows AFD.sys zero-day in Operation Dream Job

North Korea's Lazarus Group was attributed to zero-day exploitation of CVE-2026-68820 (CVSS 7.0), a privilege-escalation flaw in the Windows Ancillary Function Driver for WinSock (AFD.sys), used to gain SYSTEM access. Microsoft patched the flaw in its August 2026 Patch Tuesday updates.

Per Check Point Research, the attacks delivered ForestTiger and a previously unseen backdoor called Troy to defense and aerospace companies in France, Germany, Brazil and India. The activity is part of the long-running 'Operation Dream Job' espionage campaign, which uses fake job offers as the social-engineering lure — including recruiters impersonating Lockheed Martin and Enveil on LinkedIn.

Merged from two records of the same campaign; one supplied the malware names, the other the attributing researcher and the LinkedIn impersonation detail.
