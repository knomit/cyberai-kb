# CVE-2026-68820: Lazarus Group exploited a Windows AFD.sys zero-day in Operation Dream Job

North Korea's Lazarus Group was attributed to zero-day exploitation of CVE-2026-68820 (CVSS 7.0), a privilege escalation flaw in the Windows Ancillary Function Driver for WinSock (AFD.sys) patched in Microsoft's August 2026 Patch Tuesday. The attacks delivered ForestTiger and a previously unseen backdoor called Troy to defense and aerospace companies in France, Germany, Brazil and India, as part of the long-running Operation Dream Job fake-job-offer social engineering campaign.
