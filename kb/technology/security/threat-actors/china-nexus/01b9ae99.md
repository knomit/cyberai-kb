# Armory Castle exploited BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day CVE-2026-102764

The China-nexus espionage group Armory Castle (previously UNC5221 / Warp Panda) exploited a BeyondTrust Remote Support (formerly Bomgar) flaw as a zero-day in late August 2026. CVE-2026-102764 (CVSS 9.3) is improper origin validation in an internal messaging component, allowing a remote attacker to perform file operations on the appliance. BeyondTrust patched SaaS customers as of 2026-10-09; self-hosted customers (versions from 24.3.1 per the article) must patch themselves.
