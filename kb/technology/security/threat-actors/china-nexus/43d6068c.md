# FBI disrupted QTFY, a Chinese cyber-espionage technical quartermaster

The FBI disrupted infrastructure belonging to QTFY, a technical quartermaster that sold reconnaissance, proxy management, and operational routing capabilities to Chinese cyber-espionage operations. QTFY built and operated the QScan and QTRouter frameworks, used against U.S. critical infrastructure networks, and is employed by the China-based Nanjing Xinjiuwei Network Technology Company.
