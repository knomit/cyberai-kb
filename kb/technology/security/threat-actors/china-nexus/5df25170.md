# Fire Ant (UNC3886) pivoted from hypervisors to trusted network infrastructure in 2026

China-linked Fire Ant (aka UNC3886) expanded beyond hypervisors in 2026 to target trusted infrastructure: Cisco IOS XR routers, TACACS servers, authentication systems, and Linux management hosts. Per Sygnia, compromised routers provided covert connectivity, traffic collection, command-output manipulation, and log suppression; long-lived implants across Linux management infrastructure included Medusa rootkit components, custom SSH backdoors, Zabbix-masquerading malware called BridgeAgent, and packet-triggered backdoors. TacTap was used for TACACS credential collection. The actor deliberately manipulated the evidence sources defenders rely on, and used trusted-infrastructure relationships to reach connected third-party and critical-infrastructure environments.
