# VerdantBamboo deploys BSD BRICKSTORM variant on Linux appliances

A China-nexus cyber-espionage group tracked as VerdantBamboo has been observed deploying a BSD variant of the known BRICKSTORM backdoor, alongside two other malware families codenamed PLENET (aka GRIMBOLT) and AGENTPSD, to target Linux systems and appliances. Reported by The Hacker News, June 8, 2026.
