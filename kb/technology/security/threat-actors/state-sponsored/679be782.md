# Three suspected Russian espionage clusters abuse legitimate authentication workflows against Western targets

Google observed three distinct suspected Russian cyber espionage clusters — UNC6293, UNC7005, and UNC5976 — leveraging legitimate authentication flows to target individuals in academia, aerospace and defense, government, and think tanks across Europe, plus academia and think tanks in the U.S. Google described persistent, adaptive phishing campaigns using sophisticated social engineering to compromise personal accounts across multiple platforms. UNC7005 is also attributed to CaptiveCrunch, which targets captive Wi-Fi portals in hotels, conference centers, and airports to redirect users to attacker-controlled infrastructure and steal credentials. Lumen Black Lotus Labs reported the actor likely compromised three Managed Service Providers to conduct the captive portal hijack via a supply chain attack.
