# LightSpy has become a commercialized surveillance platform with router implants in 13+ countries

Arctic Wolf Labs reports the modular LightSpy implant has evolved into a broader surveillance tool used in more than 13 countries and regions including Singapore, Hong Kong, the Netherlands, Pakistan, Japan, China, Malaysia, Germany, the U.S., Thailand, Indonesia, South Korea, Austria and Turkey. Newly reported router-focused capabilities include live router implants in Europe and Africa, which can expose every device on a home or office network and persist across phone replacement, factory reset or OS upgrade. Infrastructure spans 117 servers and 35 domains impersonating Asian electronics manufacturers and router-management services. Customer branding, billing functionality and a demo environment indicate a commercialized platform; it is assessed to be the work of a Chinese contractor after an operator used the admin panel to place a KFC order under their real name and office address.
