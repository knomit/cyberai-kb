# Keeper Security survey: most orgs give AI tools access to critical systems without privileged-access governance

In Keeper Security's RSAC 2026 survey, 46% of respondents said AI-powered tools have access to critical systems and sensitive data, yet 76% said those AI identities are not consistently governed under privileged access policies -- meaning access is often granted to AI agents without the controls normally required for that level of access.
