# Laser attack resets Tangem crypto wallet card passwords

Researchers at Ledger's Donjon security team demonstrated that a precisely timed laser pulse aimed at the chip inside a Tangem crypto wallet card can reset the card's password to an attacker-chosen value, granting full control of the wallet and its funds. The attack requires physical possession of the card, cutting it open (leaving visible damage), and lab equipment Donjon estimates at around $250,000. Tangem cards cannot receive software updates, so no fix is possible; every card already sold carries the flaw.
