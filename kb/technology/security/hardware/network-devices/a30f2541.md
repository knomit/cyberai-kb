# Chinese-made Zbtlink routers ship with a factory backdoor beaconing to C2

Firmware analysis found that routers from Chinese manufacturer Zbtlink contain a factory-shipped backdoor that starts automatically and beacons to Chinese command-and-control infrastructure as often as every 35 seconds, executing commands received from the server. The backdoor was found implanted in at least 20 router models. Zbtlink said the 'remote management component' is used only for after-sales support upon customer request.
