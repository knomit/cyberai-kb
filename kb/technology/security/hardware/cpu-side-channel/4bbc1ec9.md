# New 'Interrupt Injection' attack bypasses Spectre v2 defenses on Intel and AMD CPUs

Researchers (including MIT CSAIL) demonstrated an 'Interrupt Injection' technique that bypasses existing Spectre v2 (branch-target-injection) defenses on modern Intel and AMD CPUs. Those defenses work by wiping or isolating the processor's branch-prediction machinery to remove anything an attacker might have planted; the new attack defeats that protection.
