# MITRE ATT&CK v20 adds AI abuse techniques incl. prompt injection and AI result poisoning

ATT&CK v20 adds nine Enterprise techniques/sub-techniques covering prompt injection, AI-enabled exfiltration, AI result poisoning, attacks on AI-assisted defenses, CAPTCHA evasion and app-specific password theft, plus entries on MFA bypass and water utility attacks; Mobile un-deprecates a technique for delivering malicious apps outside official stores.
