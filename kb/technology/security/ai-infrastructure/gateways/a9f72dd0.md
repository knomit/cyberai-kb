# Nearly 1 in 10 exposed LiteLLM gateways accepted the documented example key

Wiz Research scanned internet-facing LiteLLM AI-gateway servers and found nearly 1 in 10 — 294 of 3,074 scanned in February 2026 — accepted the documented example admin key "sk-1234," exposing model-provider API keys and in some cases cloud IAM credentials. A follow-up August scan found more than 85,000 instances. A credential printed in a quickstart guide becomes a default in production at scale.
