# By August 2026 AI is operationalized on BOTH sides of cybersecurity, and cyber is the one domain with measured AI-driven acceleration

Multiple independent August 2026 sources converge on cybersecurity as the field where AI has most clearly moved from demo to routine operational use — on offense and defense simultaneously.

THE MEASUREMENT: METR (kb/science/meta-science/ai-impact/research-acceleration/34f08e64.md) examined three domains and found MAJOR acceleration ONLY in cyber vulnerabilities — the rate of reported vulnerabilities accelerated dramatically in 2026 vs 2025 both for specific projects (cURL, OpenSSL, Firefox, Microsoft) and aggregate databases (US NVD, OSV). CRITICAL QUALIFIERS from that source, which must not be dropped: mathematics showed only MINOR and hard-to-measure acceleration, and optimization of AI research showed NO MEASURABLE acceleration across seven benchmark areas. The pattern is lumpy differential acceleration, NOT general AI-driven scientific speedup. Also note the METR metric is vulnerabilities REPORTED, which is not the same as vulnerabilities existing or being exploited.

OFFENSE, operational:
- UAT-10147 (kb/technology/security/threat-actors/ai-enabled-cybercrime/da596f8c.md) integrated AI across reconnaissance, exploit refinement, logic troubleshooting, post-exploitation automation, exploit validation, payload generation, persistence, and operational documentation, against a target list of ~170,000 URLs.
- Siemens S7 PLCs (kb/technology/security/ics-ot/plc-attacks/526a379b.md): U.S. agencies stated 'This is not a theoretical risk—it is an active threat', describing AI-generated exploit scripts masquerading as monitoring tools against internet-exposed controllers in water, energy, and manufacturing. QUALIFIER: attribution is unknown, and the observed activity is read access and positioning for FUTURE write operations, not confirmed disruption.
- RedC2 4.0 (kb/technology/security/supply-chain/npm-ecosystem/6c0abb1b.md) is an AI-powered Linux implant delivered via 14 trojanized npm packages.

DEFENSE, operational:
- Google's AVDH (kb/technology/security/ai-assisted-defense/vulnerability-discovery/491a5add.md) produced over 100 TRUE-POSITIVE critical vulnerabilities over 10 months, including Drupal CVE-2026-13242 and CVE-2026-55803; Microsoft's MDASH is described as very similar.
- Anthropic (kb/technology/security/ai-assisted-defense/vendor-programs/026822ba.md) put Claude Mythos 5 behind an asymmetric access design: Claude Enterprise customers can scan codebases and receive suggested patches, and partner-tool users get patches or alerts but CANNOT prompt the model directly, so they cannot ask it to write an exploit. It also announced a $35M Defender Advantage Fund for open-source patching.

THE STRUCTURAL POINT: defenders' AI programs are being deliberately designed as capability-asymmetric — surfacing findings without exposing the exploit-capable model — which is a direct response to the offense side above.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN: this does not establish that AI advantages attackers over defenders or vice versa; the sources document adoption on both sides, not a measured net balance. It also does not mean AI caused the vulnerability-report increase — METR reports correlation in timing and attributes contribution, not proven causation.
