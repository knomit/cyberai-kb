# CrowdStrike CPO: AI agents chain known attack techniques into novel sequences that human-reasoning-based defenses weren't built to anticipate

In a September 2026 interview with The Deep View, CrowdStrike CPO AJ Shipley distinguished two separate effects of AI on cybersecurity: (1) AI lets adversaries enumerate and exploit known vulnerabilities faster and at greater scale (a pace/scale change), and (2) AI agents combine familiar tactics, techniques and procedures (TTPs) into attack chains that are structurally different from those produced by human attacker reasoning. He said the last 20-30 years of security products were built on an intuitive model of how humans (attackers and defenders) reason, so defenses tuned to human-style attack chains must be rebuilt to anticipate AI-agent-style TTP combinations, not just scaled up to handle higher volume.
