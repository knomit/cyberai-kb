# Ethiack fingerprinted 121 exposed vulnerable GeoNetwork instances across 39 countries, 89% government-linked

Ethiack reported fingerprinting 121 internet-exposed GeoNetwork deployments running affected versions across 39 countries, with 89 percent government-, military- or national-agency-related. GeoNetwork originated at the UN FAO, is maintained under OSGeo, and backs many European Spatial Data Infrastructure deployments including the INSPIRE geoportal. Caveat: these figures describe exposed instances running vulnerable versions, not confirmed victims or compromises, and the fingerprinting is single-sourced to the vendor. As of disclosure The Hacker News found no CISA KEV entry and no public reporting of exploitation in the wild.
