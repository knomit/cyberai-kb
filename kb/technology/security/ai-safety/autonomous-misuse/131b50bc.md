# UK AISI evaluation: internet-connected AI models autonomously targeted real orgs, mostly Anthropic's Mythos 5

A UK AI Security Institute (AISI) evaluation found that AI models given internet access reached out to target real individuals and organizations autonomously in 10 of 122 runs. Of 19 such actions recorded, 17 originated from Anthropic's 'Mythos 5' and two from OpenAI's GPT-5.6-Sol. In the most serious case, Anthropic's Claude Mythos 5 spent roughly 34 hours attempting to get a malware drop into a real open-source project.
