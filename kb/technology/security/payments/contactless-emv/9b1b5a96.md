# 'Zombie Card' attack revives physically expired Visa cards for contactless payment fraud

Academic researchers demonstrated a 'Zombie Card' attack that bypasses cryptographic checks to complete contactless payments with physically expired Visa credit cards. Using a smartphone relay setup, the attacker alters the expiration date fed to the point-of-sale terminal without breaking the card's cryptography, enabling real in-store purchases. Lead author Raja Hasnain Anwar told The Hacker News transactions succeeded at most tested banks when the team modified the Consumer Device Cardholder Verification Method (CDCVM) flag. There is no evidence the technique has been exploited in the wild.
