# Microsoft dismantled Fox Tempest — enabler of Rhysida ransomware and malware supply chain

Microsoft cracked down on Fox Tempest, a threat actor that operated upstream in the malware supply chain, enabling Rhysida ransomware, Oyster, Lumma Stealer, and Vidar infections. Fox Tempest ran a fraudulent code-signing service allowing cybercriminals to deploy malware through legitimate-looking signed code.
