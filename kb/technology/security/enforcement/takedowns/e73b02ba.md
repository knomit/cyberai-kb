# FIOD arrested 2, seized 800 servers linked to Stark Industries hosting infrastructure

Netherlands FIOD (Fiscal Intelligence and Investigation Service) arrested two men (57-year-old from Amsterdam, 39-year-old from The Hague) and seized 800 servers from a web hosting company enabling cyber attacks, interference operations, and disinformation. The company is assessed to be related to Stark Industries (EU-sanctioned in May 2025). After sanctions, infrastructure moved to Dutch entity THE.Hosting aka WorkTitans.
