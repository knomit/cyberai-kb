# PNLD breach exposed UK police and government contact details on dark web

The UK Police National Legal Database (PNLD) confirmed a breach in which police, government and customer contact information was compromised and published on the dark web. Exposed data included names, organisations and work email addresses of police officers, police staff, criminal justice professionals, government partners and customers, plus some names/emails of people who submitted questions via 'Ask the Police'. The incident was identified on July 26, 2026; UK guidance warned the data could make phishing targeting named officers more convincing.
