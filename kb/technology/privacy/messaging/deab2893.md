# Apple and Google Enable Cross-Platform E2EE RCS: First Interoperable Encrypted Messaging Between iPhone and Android

Apple and Google rolled out end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) Rich Communication Services (RCS) messaging in beta between iPhone and Android devices in May 2026, closing a long-standing interoperability gap in mainstream mobile messaging. E2EE RCS is available to iPhone users on iOS 26.5 with supported carriers and to Android users on the latest Google Messages. Encrypted conversations are marked with a padlock icon. Wider rollout to iPadOS, macOS, and watchOS is planned for future updates. This represents the first time E2EE is available natively between the two dominant mobile platforms without requiring a third-party app (e.g., Signal, WhatsApp).
